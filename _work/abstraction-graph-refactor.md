## 1. What the page is, after this session

- The page reasons **from the problem side** (world, reasoning benchmarks, games) toward the brain. It is a set of **predictions** about how an ideal bounded agent would internalise, compress and retrieve its world's structure. The predictions may be wrong; evolution may have found cleverer, simpler or more roundabout ways.
- Derivation order: **setup → limits → `L0` features → consequences → `L1` rows → Correspondence (brain)**.
- `L0` is the **first-principles frontier**. It is pushed further over time. A feature is deleted only when it is wrong or genuinely irrelevant. A feature that forces no row stays (horizon, generality ordering stay).
- The requirements table is **where the frontier currently meets the brain**, not a selection criterion on `L0`.
- The brain never adds a row. A region mechanism no row predicts is recorded as a **miss** on this page (§ Correspondence misses), not as a row.
- After pass 4c: **one read primitive** (pattern completion), **one write primitive** (error-driven, at two rates into two stores), control separate; **one register per store** (depth); a factorised relation store and object store with a free variable as a re-pointable link. Eleven rows.
- After pass 5 (applied 18 November 2026): the page carries the eleven-row table with Correspondence and Grade only, Unplaced, Candidates `(brainstorm)`, eleven Correspondence misses, P1–P8, a Reasoning section between Scoring and Understanding (adaptability, the corners table, the loop with row numbers, what to light, what to store), and three `L0` statements under Agent-side attributes (depth moves on error; the rate limit; context is cue, and facts carry it). Still on the page for pass 6: the holding-state store table.
- After pass 4b: **strategy is nodes, not a level above**: what to light is decided by completion score by default and by strategy nodes (predictions about the agent's own derivation) where explicit; stopping is the control primitive's fixed rule. **Context is cue for schemas and content for facts**: the one-shot write stores the whole cue, so facts carry their context and schemas stay clean; the current-facts partition dissolves. The Reasoning section (pass 5) holds the loop and one corners table.
