# Empirical Tensions

Places where sources in the wiki disagree, or where a claim is asserted more strongly than its evidence supports. One row per tension. Updated at every ingest that creates or resolves one.

**Status key:** `LIVE` — unresolved · `LEANING` — evidence favours one side · `BOTH` — both positions hold, in different regimes; the dispute is one of degree rather than of fact, and the row must name the threshold that separates them · `RESOLVED` — settled, kept for the record. The gap table's `OPEN`/`PARTIAL`/`CONTESTED` tokens are **not** valid here; a tension that no evidence bears on is `LIVE`.

**This page is an index.** One line per row; the evidence, sources and status reasoning for each live in its own file under `wiki/tensions/`. Read the index to decide which rows matter, then open only those files.

**Maintenance.** `Status` is a **token field** — one of the tokens in the key above and nothing else; all reasoning belongs in the detail file's `Status` section. `Cited by` counts the concept and entity pages naming the row and is regenerated by `tools/registry-index.py`, which also rebuilds this table from the detail files. A retired row moves to `wiki/tensions/closed/` and leaves the table.
