# Empirical Tensions

Places where sources in the wiki disagree, or where a claim is asserted more strongly than its evidence supports. One row per tension. Updated at every ingest that creates or resolves one.

**Status key:** `LIVE` — unresolved · `LEANING` — evidence favours one side · `RESOLVED` — settled, kept for the record.

| # | Tension | Position A | Position B | Where it bites | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Does the implementation level carry computational content?** | Systems-neuroscience programme: only Marr levels 1–2 transfer; biophysics is substrate detail, biological plausibility is a guide not a requirement (Hassabis et al. 2017) | Neuromorphic / spiking programme: timing codes, energy constraints and one-shot synaptic mechanisms *are* the computation and are lost at the algorithmic level | Decides whether the wiki's target model may be specified purely algorithmically, or must be co-designed with a substrate | `LIVE` — wave 11 (spiking/neuromorphic) will supply the other side |
| T2 | **Are grid codes a general organizer of conceptual knowledge?** | Grid-like codes appear during abstract (non-spatial) categorization and would let state spaces be decomposed for subgoal discovery and hierarchical planning | The epistemic-status note on [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]: the navigation lens is warranted on the metric/embeddable slice only, and untested on genuinely non-embeddable symbolic structure (modular arithmetic, syntactic recursion) | Decides whether "reasoning ≅ navigation" is a finding or a modelling bet — the wiki's single most consequential open question | `LIVE` |
| T3 | **Is backpropagation biologically implausible?** | Classical objections: weight transport (symmetric feedback) and non-locality of the error signal rule it out | Feedback alignment (random fixed backward weights suffice), predictive-coding and energy-based networks approximate backprop with purely local updates, with concrete links to STDP | Decides whether the slow-W meta-graph learner is realizable in neural or neuromorphic hardware | `LEANING` toward B — see [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]] |
| T4 | **Does chain-of-thought help or hurt?** | Ground-truth step-by-step supervision improves accuracy (~10% relative on MATH) and cuts shortcut reliance by 15–41% on adversarial NLI | Model-*generated* CoT at test time decreases accuracy — self-generated intermediate nodes are unreliable graph positions and errors propagate | Decides whether materializing intermediate nodes is an architectural fix or only a prompting artefact | `LEANING` — the two are compatible: CoT selects paths, it does not build reliable nodes ([[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]) |
| T5 | **Is neuroscience inspiration causal or retrofitted?** | Explicit historical record for TD learning, PDP, experience replay and EWC | Attention, memory hops and content-addressable retrieval were plausibly reachable by engineering alone; the biological attribution is often added after the fact | Decides how much weight a "the brain does it this way" argument should carry when choosing between designs | `LIVE` — see the limits section of [[wiki/concepts/neuroscience-ai-transfer.md]] |
