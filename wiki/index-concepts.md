**Coverage note.** Seventy sources ingested. Hassabis et al. 2017 (a survey) built the skeleton, so most pages state a mechanism and its role in the framing without a quantitative result. Geirhos et al. 2020 added the first page carrying an evaluation constraint rather than a mechanism — and its consequence (the intended solution is not identifiable from data alone) now qualifies every other page. Schmidgall et al. 2023 added the *update-rule* layer — the two plasticity pages, plus the wiki's first entity page — and is the first source carrying quantitative comparisons *between* biologically-motivated methods rather than between biology and engineering. Revencu & Csibra 2023 added the *prior* layer: the first page saying what is in the meta-graph before any learning happens, and the first to raise composition-across-modules as a problem distinct from structure discovery. Hutter 2000 added the *ceiling* layer: the first page stating what an optimal learner can and cannot be guaranteed, and the wiki's first proof-backed limit (no optimality certificate exists for any agent whose actions shape its data). Spelke & Kinzler 2007 is the primary source behind the prior layer: it replaces the second-hand inventory with signature limits — the wiki's first *identification criterion* for a prior, and the first evidence that installed priors are defeasible. Butz 2016 added the *substrate* layer — the free-energy rival to the graph framing, stated in full — and with it the wiki's first mechanism for where discrete nodes come from at all. Lake et al. 2017 added the *model-building* layer — the wiki's first three-page ingest, plus its first architecture entity with a one-shot result — and with it the first two evaluation protocols that need no distribution shift (query richness, reward swap), the first quantitative human-vs-machine sample-efficiency table, and the first assignment of a *positive* role to model-free pattern recognition inside a model-building architecture. Richards et al. 2019 added the *specification* layer — the first page saying what one actually writes down (objective, learning rule, architecture), which retro-fits a slot label onto every mechanism already in the wiki and exposes the objective slot as the one both the transfer channel and the `g`/`x` factorization have left empty. Chollet 2019 added the *scoring* layer — intelligence as a conversion rate from priors and experience into skill on unseen tasks, plus the wiki's first benchmark page. LeCun 2022 added the *design* layer: the first source that is a full architecture proposal rather than a survey, a formalism or a critique — [[wiki/concepts/energy-based-models.md]] plus [[wiki/entities/h-jepa.md]], which together fill every slot the wiki had been listing as empty (an objective for abstraction, a mechanism for jumpy planning, a controller for the spotlight, a store with enforced update locality) and supply not one experimental result for any of them. Delétang et al. 2023 added the *measurement* layer: the prediction/compression identity turns the wiki's uncomputable ceiling into a number obtainable for any model on any byte stream, and supplies the first quantitative far-transfer result in the wiki (a text model out-compressing PNG and FLAC) alongside its sharpest counterexample to compression-implies-structure. Choi 2026 added the *dual* of the measurement layer: where every other instrument scores a system against something external (a stream, a task set, a reward), [[wiki/concepts/intelligence-density.md]] scores the system alone — outputs per bit of description, with the verdict carried by the asymptote rather than the value. It arrives with no experiments and one testable architectural consequence (no loop, no knowing), and it is the wiki's first source to argue that provenance is irrelevant to intelligence, which puts it head-on against the scoring layer ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T20). Zhi-Xuan et al. 2022 added no concept page and is the wiki's first *worked instance* rather than a layer: the intuitive-psychology core system written out as a runnable generative model ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]), which turns three previously abstract entries concrete — hierarchical Bayes on [[wiki/concepts/meta-learning.md]] gets an exact conjugate inner loop, the inverse-planning row on [[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]] gets numbers, and the agent-system machine status on [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]] flips from "no deep network has been tried" to a dissociation (baselines at chance exactly where a latent must persist across trials and stay keyed to an agent). It buys all of this with a hand-written environment, which is the whole of the discovery problem restated as engineering cost ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T21). The cross-entropy explainer talk added no new science and one missing *floor*: the wiki had been using surprisal, log-loss, free energy and code length as four names for one quantity without a page that says so, and [[wiki/concepts/divergence-objectives.md]] is that page. Its one non-textbook payload is directional — forward KL (what every predictor here is trained on) has a *specified* mode-covering optimum, so the blurred average across a one-to-many transition is the objective working correctly rather than the model failing, which is the information-theoretic version of the refusal-to-predict argument on [[wiki/concepts/energy-based-models.md]]. Ying et al. 2020 added an *operation* rather than a layer: [[wiki/concepts/subgraph-matching.md]] is the retrieval step — does this stored structure occur here? — that instance binding, event-schema preconditions, core-knowledge entry conditions and analogy had all been assuming without a mechanism, and it is NP-complete when asked exactly. Its payload is that the relation can be moved into the *geometry* of the embedding space (containment as componentwise `≼`), which makes an exponential query a coordinate comparison, satisfies transitivity/anti-symmetry/intersection by construction, and transfers zero-shot from random graphs to chemistry, biology and vision graphs ([[wiki/entities/neuromatch.md]]). It is also the wiki's first source to be undercut by its own numbers: 97.9 AUROC and 49% precision on the same dataset ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T22). Heald et al. 2021 added the *arbitration* layer, and it is the wiki's first source whose payload is a **warning about measurement rather than a mechanism**: [[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]] shows that memory creation, expression and updating all fall out of one posterior over an unbounded, inferred set of contexts, and therefore that a behavioural change decomposes into *proper* learning (a stored state moved) and *apparent* learning (only the weights on unchanged stored states moved) — with savings, anterograde interference and the environmental-consistency effect all landing, under parameter-free simulation, entirely on the second. It closes the retrieval question G37 had opened from the statistical side (a graded posterior over a growing library, complementing [[wiki/concepts/subgraph-matching.md]]'s geometric containment test), supplies the granularity knob and stopping rule [[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]] was missing (`γ`, `κ`, `α` of a sticky hierarchical Dirichlet process), dissolves continual learning's task-boundary assumption, and confirms a novel prediction experimentally (evoked recovery, ΔBIC 394.1 nats over the dual-rate model). Its price is the mirror image of the event-segmentation account's: a principled criterion over contexts whose content is a single scalar, with no composition between them ([[wiki/entities/coin-model.md]], [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T23). Ivanitskiy et al. 2023 added the *instrument* layer's missing half: every scoring page so far measures a system from outside, and [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]] is the first that opens it — linear probes, direct logit attribution and the tuned lens, with the layer index as data. Its payload is a double dissociation found inside one experiment ([[wiki/entities/maze-solving-transformers.md]]): a transformer whose residual stream reconstructs an entire maze from a *single token* at layer 2 (0.83–0.99 per wall) emits paths that cross walls in 13–62% of rollouts while reaching the target ≈100% of the time, and a sibling model solves corridor mazes having acquired no decodable maze representation at all. So a decodable graph is neither necessary nor sufficient for navigating it, which splits the wiki's core framing into two independently failing components for the first time and localises the current deficit in *search* rather than in representation (G10, G5, G15). It also states the instrument's own ceiling: probes need ground-truth labels, so they certify re-representation and can never certify discovery (G17 stands), and the whole case here is correlational — no intervention on the identified adjacency head ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T25). Whittington et al. 2022 added the *model* layer for the cognitive map, and is the first source to move an old gap *down* rather than open a new one: [[wiki/concepts/path-integration.md]] makes path-consistency of `g` a property of the update rule — implemented in continuous attractor networks, oscillator models and trained recurrent networks, with hexagonal symmetry itself traced to a non-negativity constraint — so G3 becomes `PARTIAL` at the cost of a new precondition (the actions must compose, G41). Its second payload is a pair of mirror-image architectures for the same de-aliasing job: [[wiki/entities/cscg.md]] learns any single map fast, locally and with zero transfer, while [[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]] transfers by reusing one structural code across worlds and rebinding it through a fast memory — the wiki's clearest built `p = f(g, x)` — and the two disagree about whether the hippocampus is the map or an index of it ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T28, T29). [[wiki/concepts/successor-representation.md]] adds the third face of the same object: grid-like codes as eigenvectors of a diffusion process, with one basis serving planning, exploration and replay under a diagonal reweighting. It also puts a cost on compositionality for the first time — grid cells warp toward stable rewards and stop warping when tasks vary, so factorisation is a decision nothing controls (G40). Liao & Losonczy 2024 added the *write-rule* layer for the cognitive map, and it is the first source to put a **mechanism under the wiki's own node/edge vocabulary**: behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity potentiates every input active in a seconds-wide window around a dendritic plateau, so one traversal creates one reusable receptive field — a *node*, written in a single shot, over a high-dimensional dendritic basis — while spike-timing-dependent plasticity accumulates the ordered pairwise transitions between such units — the *edges* — and needs many exposures precisely because most observed pairings are spurious. The review's unifying proposal is a **speed–amplitude trade-off** (weight change proportional to the evidence integrated in the event), which makes the two rules two operating points of one principle rather than two mechanisms, and its expressivity claim lands directly on this wiki's framing: concepts plus pairwise sequential relationships suffice to represent *any* graph. Its second payload closes an open problem [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]] had been carrying since the first ingest — what gets replayed — and closes it *against* the machine version: [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]] shows replay suppressing salient-but-idiosyncratic stimuli, upsampling rarely-visited space and preferring remote to imminent trajectories, because consolidating everything overfits. So the consolidation channel now has a specification (weight rehearsal by cross-episode recurrence and inverse visitation, not by reward), the wiki's write-selectivity gap moves to `PARTIAL` (BTSP is licensed by a plateau on a separate pathway; replay culls afterwards), and the interval half of T13 flips — a seconds-wide behavioural plasticity window exists. It is bought with two unexplained teachers: what generates the plateau at the right moment, and whether the inhibitory filter is itself learned ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T30). Chen et al. 2022 added the *replication* layer, and it is the wiki's first source to argue that the cognitive map's machinery is **not localised at all**: [[wiki/concepts/distributed-reference-frames.md]] collects grid, place and head-direction cells recorded in rat primary somatosensory and secondary visual cortex — surviving whisker trimming and darkness, so not a read-out of the sensory input those areas process — alongside grid-like coding of gaze in monkey entorhinal cortex without locomotion and hexadirectional BOLD over concept, word and discrete social-hierarchy spaces in orbitofrontal, ventromedial prefrontal and cingulate cortex. Its architectural payload is that a reference frame is a *replicated cortical primitive* anchored to objects rather than to the world, with recognition as consensus across columns, and its mechanistic payload folds cleanly into [[wiki/concepts/path-integration.md]]: one update rule run once per modality on that modality's own velocity signal (optic flow, proprioception, head movement), which turns the wiki's singular `g` into many concurrent codes with no arbitration rule (G43) and makes the composition-of-integrators question anatomical rather than hypothetical. It is bought at an unusual price — the same paper that argues grids are everywhere states that its own detector is a "relatively arbitrary threshold phenomenon" producing false positives, that BOLD is not spiking, and that **no single-unit grid code in a conceptual space has ever been recorded** ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T37), which is the sharpest qualifier the wiki has on its own biological warrant for a periodic `g`. Schaeffer, Khona & Fiete 2022 added the *licence* layer, and it is the wiki's first source whose payload is an **argument that a result the wiki already relied on does not mean what it says**: [[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]] states the two-way non-identifiability — many losses share a minimum, so matching representations do not reveal the brain's objective; one loss has many minima, so even the correct objective need not yield the brain's solution — and then demonstrates it on the case the wiki leans on hardest. Over >11,000 networks swept across architecture, activation, optimizer, hyperparameters and seed, most path-integrate optimally and almost none acquire grid units under a deliberately lenient one-unit criterion; grids require Difference-of-Softmaxes readout targets (Cartesian, polar, Gaussian and equi-norm difference-of-Gaussians all fail, the last being what three papers' texts report while their code uses DoS), the period is set monotonically by the readout width `σ_E` rather than predicted, only one module ever forms so adjacent-period ratios come out ≈1 against the biological ≈1.4, and grids disappear entirely once the readout population is given place-cell-like multi-field multi-scale heterogeneity while position decoding stays as good. The analytic reason is the sharpest thing in the ingest: gradient descent on a readout reconstruction loss behaves like attractor dynamics whose interaction kernel *is* the readout correlation matrix, so a centre–surround target **is** the pattern-forming Turing instability and the task contributes none of it. Two further payloads: linear-regression "neural predictivity" of neural data correlates with the model's rate-map participation ratio, so a model can win the brain-similarity contest by being higher-dimensional (confirmed independently in macaque vision and human audition), which makes this the wiki's third statement that decodability is a weak constraint on mechanism ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]], T25, T37); and a positive proposal — non-negativity, translation-invariant integration, exponential capacity, intrinsic error correction, whitened information — which is the most concrete candidate content the wiki has for the objective slot it has been listing as empty (G30). It costs the wiki a warrant: [[wiki/concepts/path-integration.md]]'s trained-RNN row is now a demonstration that a periodic `g` is *installable by choice of target*, not that any task selects it, so G3 stays answered by the update rule alone and the biological case rests on recordings plus first-principles attractor theory ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T38, gap G44). Nieh et al. 2021 added the *level* layer: every instrument the wiki holds either scores a system from outside or probes it for a named structure, and [[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]] is the first that asks what shape the activity has before anything is named — a ~5.4-dimensional nonlinear manifold in mouse dorsal CA1 (in a ~450-dimensional state space, requiring 29/40/47 principal components to match a 4/5/6-dimensional nonlinear embedding, so a linear dimensionality count overstates the latent count by 7–8× and the participation-ratio confound behind "neural predictivity" is itself measured against the wrong quantity). Its three payloads are all level-shifts. **(i)** The wiki's first single-unit evidence that a *learned, non-perceptual* variable enters the hippocampal map — 917 CA1 cells with firing fields in an accumulated-evidence × position space, 1.7 fields each, 53% multi-field — with the trial-to-trial *unreliability* of place fields used as the signal that distinguishes a conjunctive map from choice-conditioned splitter cells. **(ii)** The first measurement of gap G40: ~90% of those cells are conjunctive (entangled) while on the manifold position and evidence are two independent gradients (factorised), so factorisation is a property of the *geometry* and not of tuning curves, and intrinsic dimensionality — which drops on a simpler version of the same maze — is the candidate observable for the controller G40 lacks. **(iii)** The measured instance of the reusable-code-plus-pose split [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]] derived twice and never saw: 69–75% of the manifold's geometry transfers *between animals* through a single `SO(5)` rotation — 10 parameters against a 5-dimensional structure — with cross-animal evidence decoding statistically indistinguishable from within-animal, while nothing is matched cell to cell. That last result is direct support for [[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]]'s population-vs-single-unit line, since the projection of the latent onto the population is exactly what varies between conspecifics on the same task, and it yields a new label-free model-comparison instrument (align two systems' manifolds over a symmetry group, report the transferred variance) that is the only one in the wiki able to compare a model to a brain without a probe trained on ground truth ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]). It also dissolves "sequence" as a primitive: 16 088 cell pairs each active in 3.6% of trials are re-detected from 5 latents at TPR 0.87 / FPR 0.14, with manifold path length predicting the elapsed time, which is [[wiki/entities/vector-hash.md]]'s capacity argument arrived at by measurement. The prices are the familiar ones plus one new: the dimension estimate rests on a hand-chosen power-law fit range, nothing is intervened on, the abstract variable is a scalar running count so the non-embeddable slice (G11) is untouched, and the geometry is measured once in a fully trained animal so the discovery half of [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] gets no constraint. Its one genuinely new methodological demand is worth adopting wiki-wide: run the manifold pipeline on the raw *input* stream too, since a low-dimensional structure in the activity means nothing if the input already has it ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T45 gains a third reconciliation — the abstract dimension here rides on the spatial one, carried by the same cells, so the map takes non-perceptual dimensions and has never been asked to do so with navigation removed). Ahmad & Hawkins 2016 added the *implementation* layer, and it is the wiki's first source to make that level carry computational content rather than substrate detail (T1): [[wiki/concepts/sparse-distributed-representations.md]] derives the recognition half of the sparsity account the wiki had only ever priced in capacity — a ~25-synapse random subsample discriminates 10⁶ patterns at a false-positive rate better than 1 in 10⁹ while tolerating 50% corruption of the bits it samples, error falls faster than exponentially in dimension and exponentially in subsample size, and *no* threshold recovers this at 50% density, so sparsity plus high dimensionality are jointly necessary rather than merely efficient. Its second payload is a primitive the wiki lacked: a Boolean-OR union answers set membership in one thresholded dot product, `O(1)` in the number of members, at 4–16 members per slot before mix-and-match errors bite — the Bloom-filter calculation arrived at from dendritic anatomy. [[wiki/concepts/dendritic-computation.md]] carries the unit claim: a pyramidal cell is ~100 independent `θ`-of-`s` coincidence detectors rather than one sum, which makes every unit-count comparison in the wiki ambiguous by two orders of magnitude ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T63), and it is the wiki's only case of a derived scaling law predicting a measured biophysical constant in advance (`θ` between 9 and 20 against an NMDA spike threshold of 8–20). It is bought with three absences the paper states itself: no learning rule, no dendrite-to-soma model, and an assumption of decorrelated activity that is defended by evidence but never extended past the uniform case. Douglas & Martin 2004 added the *wiring* layer, and it is the wiki's first source whose entire payload is a connectome rather than a mechanism, a rule or a score: [[wiki/concepts/canonical-cortical-microcircuit.md]] holds the conserved laminar excitatory graph that every "cortical column" claim in the wiki has been assuming without ever writing down ([[wiki/concepts/distributed-reference-frames.md]]'s voting columns, the uniformity argument that licenses importing a hippocampal motif into a general architecture), together with the invariants that make canonicity a measurement — GABAergic synapses at 10–20% of all synapses from rat somatosensory to human temporal cortex, ~10 smooth-cell types conserved across a 135-million-year divergence, a 2:1 patch-spacing rule, a 25-µm double-bouquet lattice. Four payloads transfer. **(i)** Functional efficacy is decoupled from synapse count: thalamic afferents are <10% of the excitatory synapses in layer 4 and no stronger individually, yet drive cortex, which forces reading input as a *cue selecting a recurrent state* rather than a drive summed into an output — and makes edge magnitude a poor estimator of causal influence in any inferred graph ([[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]). **(ii)** Excitation is *typed*: driver edges set what a target represents, modulator edges set only how strongly, with a morphological correlate for each. **(iii)** Hierarchical depth is a graded measured quantity (the superficial-layer-neuron percentage) that resolves a degeneracy the binary feedforward/feedback rule cannot, and demotes frontal eye field from level 8 to level 4 — an instrument with no analogue for trained models, where depth is read off the architecture rather than measured from the connectivity. **(iv)** The two inhibitory geometries do different jobs — perisomatic cells implement selection over a population, dendrite-targeting cells set what each unit may integrate — and which channels reach a cell is fixed by its layer, a wiring answer to the controller regress [[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]] left open. Its functional model (superficial layers explore interpretations under iterated soft winner-take-all, deep layers commit and then constrain the input) is the only cortex-wide arbitration mechanism in the wiki that names its parts, and is labelled a tentative hypothesis by its own authors with no simulation behind it — which, with the absence of any quantitative connectivity matrix, is the price of the whole ingest ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T65). Lindsey & Litwin-Kumar 2024 added the *valve* layer, and it is the first source to give the consolidation channel a specification a builder can implement: [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]] proposes that the long-term store's plasticity is gated by the short-term store's recall of the proposed update, `Δw_LTM ∝ g(w_STM · w*)`, and shows that this single quantity reduces to prediction accuracy under a supervised rule, reward × decision confidence under a three-factor rule, and stimulus familiarity `x·(Wx)` under an autoassociative rule — three broadcast scalars a circuit already computes, so the gate needs no task label, no boundary detection, no importance estimate and no offline pass. Its analytic payload is a separation result rather than an improvement: recall gating gives *any* underlying synapse model a learnable timescale linear in the number of reinforcements (binary switch alone is invariant, cascade logarithmic, Benna–Fusi linear only with internal states growing exponentially in the target timescale), and under bursty reinforcement — Weibull `k < 1`, the realistic case — it achieves `R^{1/k}`, which is proved unachievable by **any** synapse-local consolidation mechanism. That is the wiki's strongest argument that the fast/slow split must be two *systems with a channel* rather than two timescales inside one synapse (T2). Three further payloads: the optimal coding level `f* ∝ λ` re-derives the sparse-fast/dense-slow asymmetry of [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]] from reliability filtering alone, with no appeal to interference; a non-monotonic gating function generates spacing effects that survive intervening plasticity events, which is the discriminator against the synaptic-saturation account and makes long-interval spacing evidence for the systems mechanism specifically ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T72); and the Drosophila γ1 MBON→α2 dopamine disinhibitory motif (Awata et al. 2019) is a near-exact circuit implementation. It closes the normative half of "what gets replayed" that [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]] had measured without deriving, and detaches the selection criterion from the offline schedule (T34). It is bought with fixed random uncorrelated representations, two modules in one direction, and a threshold that encodes an estimate of environmental reliability nobody says how to obtain. Sun et al. 2023 added the *sign* layer, and it is the first source to argue that the consolidation channel every prior page wanted built is **harmful when run unregulated**: [[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]] formalises systems consolidation as a linear student learning from a sparse-Hopfield notebook's own reactivations — the wiki's first explicit fast→slow transport loop, and it is self-distillation, since the slow learner regresses on targets the fast store reactivates rather than on the world — and shows the optimal number of reactivations is finite and set by the predictability (SNR) of the relation being consolidated. At infinite SNR full transfer is optimal and arises as a *consequence* of optimising generalization; at intermediate SNR the optimum is interior, so partial transfer is the predicted normal case; at low SNR any transport at all drives generalization below chance, and the memory should stay hippocampal forever. One SNR sweep reproduces graded, flat and absent retrograde amnesia, with prior consolidation flattening the gradient (the schema effect), and the theory's determinant is **predictability rather than detail, frequency or salience** — which puts it directly against the recurrence criterion of [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]] ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T81). Its quantitative payload for the two-system architecture is that the notebook's advantage over an optimally-scheduled online student peaks where stored examples ≈ student parameters — the double-descent point, which is simultaneously the worst regime for unregulated overfitting — so replay is most valuable exactly where it is most dangerous. Four interchangeable sources of unpredictability (noise, architecture mismatch, unobserved inputs, the student's own attention) mean predictability is a property of the *pair* and partly of the individual, and every relationship inside one episode gets its own value, making consolidation a decomposition of experiences rather than a triage of them. It is bought with a linear size-matched teacher and student, an SNR that no experiment can measure, no biological mechanism for the regulator, and a stopping rule whose most tractable form (held-out validation) its own authors doubt the brain implements. Wood & Grafman 2003 added the *inventory* layer for the control module, and it is the first source in the wiki to ask whether a controller has contents rather than what it does: [[wiki/concepts/controller-knowledge-vs-process.md]] scores eight theories of prefrontal function (adaptive coding, supervisory attentional system, connectionist, structured event complex, guided activation, somatic marker, temporal organization, working memory) against five criteria, and its three transferable payloads are a reduction (a *process* is a set of representations that stay active, which makes working memory a duration over retrieved long-term items rather than a buffer with an occupancy), a stored unit ([[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]]'s episode plus a goal, a maintenance window that terminates on goal completion, and a storage location fixed by which posterior region supplies its contents), and a plan format the wiki has never implemented (partially ordered event sequences, which interleave without rewriting, against the total orders every sequence model here stores). It also disputes two claims the wiki was carrying: prefrontal cortex responds to *known* actions and overlearned tasks merely shift its activation posteriorly, so practice may relocate a behaviour within the control layer rather than vacating it ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T94, T95, T96, gap G51). Its price is that every argument for the representational side is an argument from consistency — with connectivity, neurophysiology and evolutionary reachability — and not one discriminating experiment, in a review whose winning theory is its own authors'. Known holes are tracked in [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] and [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]].
