**Coverage note.** Five sources ingested. Hassabis et al. 2017 (a survey) built the skeleton, so most pages state a mechanism and its role in the framing without a quantitative result. Geirhos et al. 2020 added the first page carrying an evaluation constraint rather than a mechanism — and its consequence (the intended solution is not identifiable from data alone) now qualifies every other page. Schmidgall et al. 2023 added the *update-rule* layer — the two plasticity pages, plus the wiki's first entity page — and is the first source carrying quantitative comparisons *between* biologically-motivated methods rather than between biology and engineering. Revencu & Csibra 2023 added the *prior* layer: the first page saying what is in the meta-graph before any learning happens, and the first to raise composition-across-modules as a problem distinct from structure discovery. Hutter 2000 added the *ceiling* layer: the first page stating what an optimal learner can and cannot be guaranteed, and the wiki's first proof-backed limit (no optimality certificate exists for any agent whose actions shape its data). Known holes are tracked in [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] and [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]].
