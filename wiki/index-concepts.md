**Coverage note.** Eighteen sources ingested. Hassabis et al. 2017 (a survey) built the skeleton, so most pages state a mechanism and its role in the framing without a quantitative result. Geirhos et al. 2020 added the first page carrying an evaluation constraint rather than a mechanism — and its consequence (the intended solution is not identifiable from data alone) now qualifies every other page. Schmidgall et al. 2023 added the *update-rule* layer — the two plasticity pages, plus the wiki's first entity page — and is the first source carrying quantitative comparisons *between* biologically-motivated methods rather than between biology and engineering. Revencu & Csibra 2023 added the *prior* layer: the first page saying what is in the meta-graph before any learning happens, and the first to raise composition-across-modules as a problem distinct from structure discovery. Hutter 2000 added the *ceiling* layer: the first page stating what an optimal learner can and cannot be guaranteed, and the wiki's first proof-backed limit (no optimality certificate exists for any agent whose actions shape its data). Spelke & Kinzler 2007 is the primary source behind the prior layer: it replaces the second-hand inventory with signature limits — the wiki's first *identification criterion* for a prior, and the first evidence that installed priors are defeasible. Butz 2016 added the *substrate* layer — the free-energy rival to the graph framing, stated in full — and with it the wiki's first mechanism for where discrete nodes come from at all. Lake et al. 2017 added the *model-building* layer — the wiki's first three-page ingest, plus its first architecture entity with a one-shot result — and with it the first two evaluation protocols that need no distribution shift (query richness, reward swap), the first quantitative human-vs-machine sample-efficiency table, and the first assignment of a *positive* role to model-free pattern recognition inside a model-building architecture. Richards et al. 2019 added the *specification* layer — the first page saying what one actually writes down (objective, learning rule, architecture), which retro-fits a slot label onto every mechanism already in the wiki and exposes the objective slot as the one both the transfer channel and the `g`/`x` factorization have left empty. Chollet 2019 added the *scoring* layer — intelligence as a conversion rate from priors and experience into skill on unseen tasks, plus the wiki's first benchmark page. LeCun 2022 added the *design* layer: the first source that is a full architecture proposal rather than a survey, a formalism or a critique — [[wiki/concepts/energy-based-models.md]] plus [[wiki/entities/h-jepa.md]], which together fill every slot the wiki had been listing as empty (an objective for abstraction, a mechanism for jumpy planning, a controller for the spotlight, a store with enforced update locality) and supply not one experimental result for any of them. Delétang et al. 2023 added the *measurement* layer: the prediction/compression identity turns the wiki's uncomputable ceiling into a number obtainable for any model on any byte stream, and supplies the first quantitative far-transfer result in the wiki (a text model out-compressing PNG and FLAC) alongside its sharpest counterexample to compression-implies-structure. Choi 2026 added the *dual* of the measurement layer: where every other instrument scores a system against something external (a stream, a task set, a reward), [[wiki/concepts/intelligence-density.md]] scores the system alone — outputs per bit of description, with the verdict carried by the asymptote rather than the value. It arrives with no experiments and one testable architectural consequence (no loop, no knowing), and it is the wiki's first source to argue that provenance is irrelevant to intelligence, which puts it head-on against the scoring layer ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T20). Zhi-Xuan et al. 2022 added no concept page and is the wiki's first *worked instance* rather than a layer: the intuitive-psychology core system written out as a runnable generative model ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]), which turns three previously abstract entries concrete — hierarchical Bayes on [[wiki/concepts/meta-learning.md]] gets an exact conjugate inner loop, the inverse-planning row on [[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]] gets numbers, and the agent-system machine status on [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]] flips from "no deep network has been tried" to a dissociation (baselines at chance exactly where a latent must persist across trials and stay keyed to an agent). It buys all of this with a hand-written environment, which is the whole of the discovery problem restated as engineering cost ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T21). The cross-entropy explainer talk added no new science and one missing *floor*: the wiki had been using surprisal, log-loss, free energy and code length as four names for one quantity without a page that says so, and [[wiki/concepts/divergence-objectives.md]] is that page. Its one non-textbook payload is directional — forward KL (what every predictor here is trained on) has a *specified* mode-covering optimum, so the blurred average across a one-to-many transition is the objective working correctly rather than the model failing, which is the information-theoretic version of the refusal-to-predict argument on [[wiki/concepts/energy-based-models.md]]. Ying et al. 2020 added an *operation* rather than a layer: [[wiki/concepts/subgraph-matching.md]] is the retrieval step — does this stored structure occur here? — that instance binding, event-schema preconditions, core-knowledge entry conditions and analogy had all been assuming without a mechanism, and it is NP-complete when asked exactly. Its payload is that the relation can be moved into the *geometry* of the embedding space (containment as componentwise `≼`), which makes an exponential query a coordinate comparison, satisfies transitivity/anti-symmetry/intersection by construction, and transfers zero-shot from random graphs to chemistry, biology and vision graphs ([[wiki/entities/neuromatch.md]]). It is also the wiki's first source to be undercut by its own numbers: 97.9 AUROC and 49% precision on the same dataset ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T22). Heald et al. 2021 added the *arbitration* layer, and it is the wiki's first source whose payload is a **warning about measurement rather than a mechanism**: [[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]] shows that memory creation, expression and updating all fall out of one posterior over an unbounded, inferred set of contexts, and therefore that a behavioural change decomposes into *proper* learning (a stored state moved) and *apparent* learning (only the weights on unchanged stored states moved) — with savings, anterograde interference and the environmental-consistency effect all landing, under parameter-free simulation, entirely on the second. It closes the retrieval question G37 had opened from the statistical side (a graded posterior over a growing library, complementing [[wiki/concepts/subgraph-matching.md]]'s geometric containment test), supplies the granularity knob and stopping rule [[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]] was missing (`γ`, `κ`, `α` of a sticky hierarchical Dirichlet process), dissolves continual learning's task-boundary assumption, and confirms a novel prediction experimentally (evoked recovery, ΔBIC 394.1 nats over the dual-rate model). Its price is the mirror image of the event-segmentation account's: a principled criterion over contexts whose content is a single scalar, with no composition between them ([[wiki/entities/coin-model.md]], [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T23). Ivanitskiy et al. 2023 added the *instrument* layer's missing half: every scoring page so far measures a system from outside, and [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]] is the first that opens it — linear probes, direct logit attribution and the tuned lens, with the layer index as data. Its payload is a double dissociation found inside one experiment ([[wiki/entities/maze-solving-transformers.md]]): a transformer whose residual stream reconstructs an entire maze from a *single token* at layer 2 (0.83–0.99 per wall) emits paths that cross walls in 13–62% of rollouts while reaching the target ≈100% of the time, and a sibling model solves corridor mazes having acquired no decodable maze representation at all. So a decodable graph is neither necessary nor sufficient for navigating it, which splits the wiki's core framing into two independently failing components for the first time and localises the current deficit in *search* rather than in representation (G10, G5, G15). It also states the instrument's own ceiling: probes need ground-truth labels, so they certify re-representation and can never certify discovery (G17 stands), and the whole case here is correlational — no intervention on the identified adjacency head ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T25). Known holes are tracked in [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] and [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]].
