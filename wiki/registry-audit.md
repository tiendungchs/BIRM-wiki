> **Note (lint pass, 212 ingests).** **T210, T213 and T214 are one defect, discovered three times.** Each was logged as a disagreement about a benchmark's human number; none of them is. A human baseline is a protocol — pool, attempt budget, aggregation order, exclusion rule, item-pool identity — and each of the three is a different one of those parameters left unstated. The specification, and the wiki's reporting convention, are at [[wiki/concepts/human-baseline.md]]. Rows that are instances of one missing specification should be marked as such rather than re-argued per benchmark.
