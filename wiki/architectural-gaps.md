# Architectural Gaps

What a brain-inspired reasoning model needs and no current architecture supplies. Updated after every ingest that opens or closes a gap, and rewritten at each lint pass.

**Status key:** `OPEN` — no known solution · `PARTIAL` — solved in a restricted setting · `CONTESTED` — candidate solutions exist and disagree.

> **Provenance:** rows G1–G13 were derived from [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] alone; G14–G15 come from the first ingest (Hassabis et al. 2017). Where an ingest has supplied evidence, the `From` column names it. Everything still unmarked records a *mechanism the framing names*, not an architecture the wiki has checked.

| G1 | **Two-level separation exists as a timescale split, not as a factorization** | Meta-graph and instance-graph co-occur in every observation; a flat learner fits the mixture `E_θ[p(obs\|θ)]`, which no individual instance follows — so it cannot transfer at all | The *two learning rates* are instantiated and validated: complementary learning systems (fast hippocampal store + slow cortex + replay) and meta-RL (slow weights training a fast recurrent inner learner). The *factorized code* `p = f(g, x)` is not — no transfer in the historical record moved a factorization. **Where the split is written down by hand it works and is cheap**: a hyperprior over per-agent goal preferences and rationality, with per-agent parameters as the instance level, adapts by an exact Dirichlet–categorical conjugate update in 8 trials and predicts the 9th ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]) — no gradient, no replay. That is evidence the *shape* is right and none that it can be discovered: the shared level was authored, and the environment it sits over is hand-written PDDL | Two-Level Graph Hierarchy; Hardness 1; [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]], [[wiki/concepts/meta-learning.md]], [[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]] | `PARTIAL` |
| G2 | **De-aliasing without a hand-built state space** | The same observation recurs at structurally distinct graph positions; without path-sensitive identity the model merges nodes that must stay distinct | Clone cells, or identity carried by path-integrated `g`; sparse conjunctive coding in the fast store gives distinct codes to repeated observations; **module membership as a free identity tag** — 10-month-olds fail to individuate two entities typed by the same core system (duck/truck) and succeed when they are typed by different ones (ball/doll), so a bank of entry-gated priors de-aliases without path context. **Third free tag:**  an *event* is a **set** of co-active predictive encodings, so two identical observations sitting in different active sets are different events without any path integration. **Fourth answer, and the only one that is a tunable circuit rather than a tag:** expansion recoding through a sparse layer whose transfer function is *steeper than identity* — dentate granule cells separate at the smallest input changes and drive the separated code onto CA3 through detonator synapses, with lesion and NMDA-receptor-knockout data showing the step is necessary for discrimination and dispensable for undemanding conditioning (Yassa & Stark 2011). Its price is that it de-aliases *everything*, including repeats that should have been recognised, so it only works with a controller (G38). **Now built, two ways, with complementary costs:** a clone-structured hidden Markov model whose per-observation clone pool is frozen at 0/1 and whose transitions are learned by expectation-maximisation — local, fast, one environment, zero transfer ([[wiki/entities/cscg.md]]) — and an action-accumulated latent that de-aliases by *position* and transfers, but is slow to acquire ([[wiki/concepts/path-integration.md]], [[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]]). Merging them is stated as the obvious next model and not yet done. **Fifth answer, and the only one that is not a computation at all:** *randomization* — the mossy-fibre projection (~46 inputs per CA3 cell) destroys the input's similarity structure and hands the store a random address, so any two events pick near-orthogonal cell sets with no decision, no threshold and no learned code (Rolls 2013). It is confined to the write path precisely because it cannot recognise a repeat, which is what makes the anatomically separate read port necessary rather than merely convenient. **Sixth answer — randomization moved one stage earlier and made exponential** ([[wiki/entities/vector-hash.md]]): a *fixed random* projection from a prestructured grid code into the store, with any pointwise nonlinearity (rectification suffices; a linear layer fails), makes the store's state set strongly full rank, so every one of exponentially many positions is a distinct, error-correcting address and new environments de-alias against all previous ones by taking a random initial phase. Unlike the mossy-fibre version it *can* be read as well as written, because the address is regenerated by the grid dynamics from a partial cue rather than re-hashed | Hardness 3; [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]], [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]], [[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]], [[wiki/concepts/pattern-separation-completion.md]] | `PARTIAL` |

| G5 | **No joint discover-and-navigate loop** | Hard tasks give no discovery-then-use phase separation: the graph estimate must update *while* it is being searched. | G9 | **W is flat; there is no third tier** | Two levels suffice only while the rewrite is expressible in the base vocabulary. | G10 | **Self-generated intermediate nodes are unreliable** | Multi-hop traversal requires the model to materialize its own intermediate states; when it does, they are not dependable graph positions, so errors compound along the path | **Now measured, and it is not a representation problem.** In maze-solving transformers the graph is verifiably intact — linearly decodable at 0.83–0.99 per wall from one token — while 13–16% of in-distribution rollouts (62% out of distribution for the smaller model) still leave the graph by crossing a wall, and the single worst sub-task everywhere is the *fork decision*, not path-following. | G11 | **No mechanism for the non-embeddable symbolic slice** | The navigation frame is warranted where structure is orderable, continuous, or transition-sampled. | G12 | **No routing policy between structure types** | Given a metric/transition-sampled module and a declarative/relational one, nothing decides which structure goes where. The arbitration is a prerequisite for G11's second mechanism to be usable at all | — . **The row now has a biological existence proof for the *sorting* and still nothing for the *routing*** (Zheng et al. 2024, [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]]): a transition-sampled structure and a taxonomic one over the same objects end up in different, non-overlapping parts of the hippocampal formation, so the brain does place structure types in separate territory — but what reads the right one at query time is unaddressed, and the sorting itself may be a by-product of a resolution gradient (G38) rather than a routing decision | Open Problems; [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G13 | **No tractable system covers all six hardness sources** | AIXI is the only known system that does, and it fails on computability. Every real architecture is a bounded-program approximation that drops whichever sources its search budget cannot reach — and there is no principled account of which to drop | **AIXItl** is the one principled bounded approximation: enumerate every program of length `≤ l̃` with a proof that it never overrates its own expected credit, run them all, act on the highest self-certified value. It dominates every such policy at `O(2^l̃·t̃)` per cycle plus `O(l_P·2^l_P)` setup — so the drop rule is "whatever exceeds `l̃`", which is a budget, not a principle. The transferable part is the *selection criterion* (self-certified value), not the enumeration | Formal Ceiling; [[wiki/entities/aixi.md]] | `PARTIAL` |
| G14 | **No consolidation channel: instance structure never becomes meta structure** | Biology transports one-shot hippocampal bindings into cortical structure by replay during offline periods. Machine replay only stabilizes a *single* learner (decorrelating a training stream); nothing converts what the fast level discovered about one instance into a revision of the meta-graph | Replay/consolidation, named biologically; episodic control uses the fast store for behaviour but has no path back into the slow one. **The biological channel now has a measured selection criterion, and it is not the one machines copied**: replay suppresses salient-but-idiosyncratic stimuli, upsamples rarely-visited space and prefers remote to imminent trajectories, converging on an inductive bias toward *transferable* content — because consolidating everything overfits (Liao & Losonczy 2024; [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]). That gives the channel a specification a builder can implement (weight rehearsal by cross-episode recurrence and inverse visitation, not by reward or TD-error) and leaves the transport itself unbuilt, plus a new sub-problem: seven jobs claim the same replay budget under incompatible sampling policies. **One of those jobs is not transport at all but *construction*:** awake ripples increasingly co-activate a cue and an outcome never experienced together, without the intermediary, only when the chain ends in reward, and with the outcome cell firing *first* — so the offline period also writes edges the agent never traversed, in the direction of credit rather than of use (Barron et al. 2020; [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]). **And the transport has a wiring cost nobody prices** (Rolls 2013): reinstating a cortical pattern requires `C^HBP = C^RC · a_nc/a_CA3` hippocampally-originating afferents per cortical cell — ≥12,000 even in the best case — which makes a monosynaptic read-out impossible and forces a *polysynaptic reverse hierarchy* with gradual fan-out per stage. So the sparser the fast store (better capacity), the more expensive the read-back, a trade-off every retrieval-augmented architecture treats as free. The same source disputes the *schedule* (waking, not sleep — [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T34) and adds a confound: a changed cortical representation after learning is not evidence of transfer, since a changed CA1 code alters cortical firing through fixed connections | [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]], [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G15 | **No control policy over simulation** | Planning is path search, but nothing decides when to roll out, how deep, which branch, or when the answer is good enough. Without it, model-based reasoning is either always-on (unaffordable) or hand-scheduled (not general) | Prefrontal control of hippocampal rollout, proposed biologically; controller-queries-model architectures implement the interface but not the policy. **Active inference supplies the *when* and the *switch*, not the *how deep*:** homeostatic divergence read as free energy both initiates the rollout and sets its target, the epistemic-vs-goal trade-off arbitrates exploration against exploitation on the same quantity, and dropping bottom-up gain (`γ → 0`) with strong biased competition (`β ≪ 0`) is the operational switch from perception into simulation. **A second candidate for the same half:** a rational *arbitration* between model-based and model-free control on a flexibility-vs-speed trade-off, supervised metacognitively, with habitization as the observed shift — which prices planning against its own cached output rather than against a homeostatic drive ([[wiki/concepts/amortized-inference.md]]). No computational account of the metacognitive estimator is given, and neither candidate answers *how deep* | [[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]], [[wiki/concepts/attention.md]], [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]], [[wiki/concepts/amortized-inference.md]] | `PARTIAL` |
| G16 | **The intended graph is not identifiable from data alone** | Shortcut and causal rules are equally consistent with the observations, so no objective computed on one environment can prefer the structural rule. The `g`/`x` factorization G1 asks for therefore cannot be *discovered* — it must be imposed through inductive bias. The complete control surface is four levers (architecture, training data, loss, optimizer) and there is no account of which one buys graph-structured solutions, nor at what cost | Multi-environment invariance where a family is given; cross-entropy's early-stopping-at-first-sufficient-predictor identified as the sharpest loss-side target. **First concrete architecture-lever proposal:** split the predictor into three *typed* channels — top-down (item content, generalising over space), spatial (frame-of-reference mappings between encodings), temporal (change given forces) — so an appearance-only rule is not expressible in the two structural channels. Butz 2016 argues this is *necessary*: free-energy minimisation is indifferent to which encodings develop, so structural priors must be added on top of the objective. Stipulated functionally, not derived, and never implemented **Rival count of the surface:** Richards et al. 2019 give three components (objective, learning rule, architecture) and demote training data to an *anchor* — the components are compressible because they pass through the genome, the environment is not because it is the high-entropy source. If that reading is right, the data lever cannot be part of the design and G16 must be paid for entirely from the other three ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T15)  **Loss lever, formal statement:** cross-entropy is a functional of the predictive distribution `Q` alone, so a shortcut rule and the intended rule inducing the same `Q` have *identical* loss at every point — no amount of data on one environment separates them, and the objective additionally carries an unchosen mode-covering preference that averages across one-to-many transitions ([[wiki/concepts/divergence-objectives.md]]). This closes the loss lever as a candidate answer to G16 rather than filling it | [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]], [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]], [[wiki/concepts/divergence-objectives.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G17 | **No evaluation protocol can certify structure discovery** | i.i.d. testing cannot separate a recovered meta-graph from a correlation holding in the sample, so every "architecture X reasons" claim is unscoreable and the hardness-source scoring table has no valid instrument. Existing o.o.d. benchmarks are domain-specific, hand-built, and must be revised as models learn to game them (the Winograd Schema Challenge was found to contain the shortcuts it was designed to remove) | Controlled-shortcut datasets (Shift-MNIST / biased CelebA / unfair dSprites), where the intended rule is known by construction and the accuracy drop is the measurement; three design conditions for a valid o.o.d. test. Second instrument, from developmental method: **signature limits** — certify an installed prior by a quantitative constant (capacity, Weber ratio, entry threshold) that stays put across scale, retraining and domain shift, and by a **resource-stress probe** (shrink the budget; in biology the learned distinctions degrade first and the core ones last). Diagnostic evidence is in the failure profile, not the success rate ([[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]]). **Two further instruments, and the first that need no distribution shift at all** — hold the trained model fixed and vary the *query*: (i) the **richness protocol** — one representation must answer classify / generate / parse / create-new-concepts / explain, and a shortcut rule is by construction a mapping to one output type ([[wiki/concepts/causal-model-building.md]]); (ii) the **re-goaling protocol** — hold the transition structure fixed, swap the reward function, measure the retraining needed ([[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]]). Both are gameable by multi-task training on the query set, and neither certifies that the abilities come from *one* model. **Fifth instrument, and the first that is a quantity rather than a genre** (Chollet 2019): score `GD = H(Sol^θ_T | TrainSol^opt_{T,C}) / H(Sol^θ_T)` — how far the shortest curriculum-optimal program must be edited to work at evaluation — and run it *developer-aware*, conditioning also on the initial system, so anything the builder injected stops counting as difficulty. This reframes the certification target: the protocol must be run against the developer, not only the system, because skill on any task known in advance is purchasable outright with priors or with data. [[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]] is the instrument built to this spec (private evaluation set, ~3 examples per task, no data generator, enumerated priors). **Sixth instrument — paired violation of expectation** ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]): show one history, then two continuations differing only in the property under test, and score whether the model rates the intended one higher. It needs no labels, no distribution shift and no absolute calibration — only a *relative* judgement between two futures of an identical past — and it is the first protocol in this table that can register a model scoring **below** chance, i.e. having acquired the opposite rule (26.3% on Baby Intuitions Preference). Its limit is that the pair must be authored by hand, so it inherits the developer-awareness problem wholesale. Still `OPEN`: `GD` is defined through Kolmogorov complexity, no approximation is offered, and ARC itself quantifies the generalization difficulty of exactly zero of its tasks (G31). **Seventh instrument, and the first that looks inside the model** (Ivanitskiy et al. 2023): **linear probing** of internal activations against a hypothesised structure — a whole maze reconstructed at 0.83–0.99 per wall from a single token's residual stream at layer 2, with direct logit attribution and the tuned lens locating the components that write it. It answers a question no behavioural protocol reaches (is the structure *there*, and by which layer?) and is structurally unable to answer this row's: probes are trained on ground-truth labels, so they confirm containment of a structure the experimenter already had and cannot certify discovery of one they did not. It also splits the score the other six instruments merge — decodable structure minus behaviourally expressed structure, ~0.95 against 84% valid rollouts on the same model ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]) | [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]], [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]], [[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]], [[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]], [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G18 | **Discovered learning rules do not generalize outside the rule-search restrictions imposed by hand** | Meta-optimized plasticity is the wiki's only route to making slow **W** a *searched* object rather than a parameter blob, but when the rule search space is large and few restrictions are placed on the mechanism, generalization degrades. The freedom that makes rule discovery interesting is the same freedom that breaks it, so the expressiveness needed for structure acquisition is exactly what the method cannot afford | **Variable-shared meta-learning**: rules parameterized by parameter-shared recurrent networks that exchange information locally, which generalize to classification problems unseen during meta-optimization (and analogously for discovered RL algorithms). Restriction by *architecture* rather than by hand-written rule form is the only route shown to survive o.o.d. Which restrictions buy generalization and which merely shrink the space is uncharacterized | [[wiki/concepts/meta-optimized-plasticity.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G19 | **No local rule is selective about what it writes** | Every rule that can write during deployment ([[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]]) is a coactivity/covariance detector. It fires on shortcut-driven coactivity exactly as readily as on structural coactivity, so a fast **M** written by local plasticity accumulates spurious edges at the *instance* level — the same failure as G6, now inside the mechanism the wiki wants for online binding. Metaplasticity gates *how much* a synapse may change, never *whether the change is warranted*. Stated in the specification vocabulary: a rule is *learning* only if its update field has a positive projection onto some objective's gradient, and no local rule here names the objective it improves — so "unselective" may be the milder diagnosis and "orthogonal" the true one | Meta-optimizing the rule so the outer loop's task distribution filters what the rule writes on — i.e. importing the multi-environment identifiability argument into the update rule. Named here, not built. **Biology supplies two partial selectivity mechanisms, at opposite ends of the timescale.** (i) Behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity writes only when a dendritic plateau arrives on a separate anatomical pathway, so *whether to allocate* is a licensing signal rather than a property of the coactivity — but what generates the plateau at the right moment is unknown, which relocates the selection problem rather than solving it. (ii) Offline replay culls: a stimulus that was represented online is actively suppressed from consolidation if it does not recur, with inhibitory plasticity the predicted (unconfirmed) substrate. So selectivity is real in biology and is implemented *outside* the excitatory write rule — once as a gate on it, once as a filter after it (Liao & Losonczy 2024) | [[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]], [[wiki/concepts/meta-optimized-plasticity.md]], [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]] | `PARTIAL` |
| G20 | **No local rule reaches delayed credit *and* stays online** | The two available regimes are mutually exclusive: e-prop is forward-computable but requires a real-time error at each step and is blind to errors delayed beyond individual-neuron timescales; REINFORCE / node perturbation handle arbitrarily delayed sparse reward but are high-variance and give no per-step signal. | G21 | **No mechanism composes the outputs of two specialized modules** | Core knowledge shows a meta-graph can be *pre-installed* — six domain fragments with their own node types, edge vocabularies and constraints — but the fragments are encapsulated and compete for a limited attentional resource, so nothing joins them. Modularity is the wiki's standing architectural stance ([[wiki/concepts/attention.md]]), and it has no binding operation. Worse, if language supplies the common format, then core concepts are a *different kind* from the concepts used as building blocks, so the priors cannot serve as compositional primitives without an unspecified translation layer (the equivocation problem) | Three rivals now. (a) natural-language syntax + compositional semantics (Spelke 2022); (b) an innate language of thought whose format the core systems already speak (Revencu & Csibra 2023); (c) **compose by relaxation** — hold both concepts' predictive encodings active and let mutual constraint satisfaction settle into a joint free-energy minimum, with the relative spatial encoding as the binding term ("a ball lies in a bowl": the two temporal interaction predictions cancel, which *is* the semantics of "lies"). (c) needs no shared symbolic format at all, only that the fragments predict into each other — but the relaxation is intractable in general and is approximated by local adjustment, and low energy is a consistency criterion, not a truth criterion | [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]], [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]] | `CONTESTED` |
| G22 | **Nothing selects which compositions get built** | A composer must be answerable in both directions at once. Driven from outside (corpus, teacher) it **undergenerates** — the primitives are what everyone assumes and therefore rarely says, and many symbols (logical operators, tense/aspect, modals) have no primitive counterpart. Driven from inside it **overgenerates** — nothing explains why a handful of the astronomically many available compositions are ever built. Granting composition at all inherits the combinatorial explosion; it is postponed, not solved. This is gap G4 (vocabulary co-discovery) restated as a *selection* problem rather than an induction problem | **Selection by energy.** If a composition is an attractor, the compositions that are never built are the ones with no low-energy state — "a bowl lies in a ball" fails because a ball affords no interior. The pruner is then internal to the representation rather than external (corpus, teacher), which is what the overgeneration horn needs. Untested, and it prices selection in relaxation compute. **(brainstorm)** A machine is not forced to choose: give it an internal composer and use the external corpus as a *second* pruner over the generated space rather than as its source | [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]], [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G23 | **Machine priors are unconditional; a prior needs an entry test** | A core system applies only to a subset of entities *and* only to a subset of their properties — a pile of sand fails cohesion and never enters the object system. Without an entry test, adding more priors monotonically adds more misapplied constraints, which is why the architecture lever (G16) does not obviously scale by stacking | Core systems' selectivity, stated as a property, with no computational account of what evaluates the entry condition. | G24 | **No principled planning horizon exists, even in the ideal agent** | Path search needs a depth. | G25 | **Optimality cannot be certified for any agent whose actions shape its data** | For passive environments (prediction, classification) the error excess is bounded by a function of `K(µ)` alone. Three things it changes for a builder: capacity is set by the *address space* rather than by the plasticity rule, so it is chosen at design time and is exact; overload degrades resolution rather than identity (the retrieved item stays in the correct Voronoi cell), which is the failure mode a system can detect and act on; and recognition memory falls out of a two-threshold read of mean store activity. ## How gaps are used

A gap is worth recording only if it is *actionable*: it names a mechanism the wiki would adopt if it existed. Gaps that reduce to "the problem is hard" are folded into the open-problems section of the relevant concept page instead.

**Dependency structure.** These are not independent. G16 sits *under* G1: it says the factorization G1 wants is not the kind of thing data can supply, so G1 is a design decision to be paid for rather than a result to be waited for. G17 is the instrument problem for the whole table — without an out-of-distribution test, no gap can be shown closed, because an architecture that assumed the latent variable away is indistinguishable from one that recovered it. G1 is the root — G2 and G3 are the conditions under which its factorization is meaningful, and G9 is what happens when it is applied to itself. G7→G8 and G11→G12 are each a problem followed by the mechanism that would dissolve it. G13 is the accounting frame for all of them: an architecture is scored by which hardness sources it reaches, not by whether it "works". G14 and G15 are the two halves of the write/read loop around the factorization: G14 is how the meta-graph gets updated from experience, G15 is how the graph gets used once it exists. G19 and G20 are the corresponding pair one level down, on the *write rule for fast M*: G19 is what gets written, G20 is when the signal that authorizes the write arrives. G18 is the tax on G9's new candidate answer — self-reference buys the third tier, and generalization is the price. G21–G23 open a column the table did not have: what is in the graph *before* learning. G23 is the condition under which installed priors can be stacked at all; G21 is what they cannot do once installed; G22 is the reason G21's candidate answers do not obviously work. G21 also inverts G4 — where G4 asks how a vocabulary is induced, G21 assumes several are given and asks how they are joined.

**What would close one.** A gap closes when an ingest supplies an architecture that (a) instantiates the named mechanism and (b) is tested on a task where the corresponding latent variable is actually hidden. An architecture that assumes away the latent variable does not count as evidence. Per G17, condition (b) means an **out-of-distribution** test with a well-defined intended solution — an i.i.d. result never closes a gap here.

> **Provenance (second ingest).** Rows G16–G17 come from Geirhos et al. 2020; G6 was revised by the same source from "correlational edges can survive" to "shortcut solutions are the default outcome across every subfield studied".

> **Provenance (third ingest).** Rows G18–G20 come from Schmidgall et al. 2023, which also moved G9 from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` by naming self-referential meta-learning as a candidate third tier. G19 is G6 restated one level down: the same shortcut problem, now in the *write rule for fast M* rather than in the slow learner's weights — which means fixing G6 at the slow level does not fix it at the fast one.

> **Provenance (fifth ingest).** Rows G24–G26 come from Hutter 2000, which also moved G13 from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` by supplying AIXItl. The source changes the table's status in one respect: G25 is the wiki's first gap with a *proof* that its answer does not exist, rather than a report that none has been found. Its dependents are G17 (an o.o.d. certificate cannot be fully general because active-case optimality is uncertifiable) and G13 (the "which hardness sources to drop" question has no principled answer partly because there is no yardstick to score the drop against). G26 sits under G16 as G16 sits under G1: the bias must be paid for (G16), and the strongest bias available does not buy the thing G1 wants (G26).

> **Provenance (fourth ingest).** Rows G21–G23 come from Revencu & Csibra 2023. They shift the table's centre of gravity: G1–G20 all concern what a learner must *acquire*, while these three concern what it must be *given*, and what being given several disconnected priors then costs. G2 also gains a second answer from this source — an entity typed by a different core system is individuated without any path context, so module membership is a free de-aliasing tag.

> **Provenance (sixth ingest).** Spelke & Kinzler 2007, the primary source behind G21–G23, adds no new row but changes three. G17 gains its second candidate instrument (signature limits + the resource-stress probe), which is the first proposal in the table for certifying a *prior* rather than a learned structure. G23 gains a reason rather than just a statement: an ungated geometry prior would be actively wrong on movable objects, so entry tests are correctness machinery, not efficiency machinery. And the source adds a constraint that cuts across G21–G23 — installed priors must be **defeasible** ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T11), since the core principles are false outside the scale of action and are in fact overridden, always via an external symbol system.

> **Provenance (seventh ingest).** Butz 2016 adds G27 and moves three rows off `OPEN`: G5 to `PARTIAL` (a processing loop that is genuinely joint at the instance level, still phase-separated at the meta level) and G15 to `PARTIAL` (homeostatic free energy answers *when to plan*, leaving *how deep* to G24). It is the first source to give G16 a concrete architecture-lever proposal rather than a restatement, and the first to give G21/G22 a composition mechanism that needs no shared symbolic format — composition as relaxation to a joint free-energy minimum, with selection falling out as "no minimum, no composition". G27 is a column the table did not have: G1–G26 all concern the graph's *contents*, G27 concerns whether there are discrete nodes at all. Note the dependency it creates: G27 sits under G2 and G3, since identity and path-consistency are only well-posed once the node set exists.

> **Provenance (eighth ingest).** Lake et al. 2017 adds G28–G29 and changes two rows. G17 gains its first *shift-free* instruments — vary the query, not the data — which matters because every earlier candidate required constructing a distribution shift with a known intended solution, and that construction is the expensive part. G15 gains a second answer to *when to plan* that is priced in the currency of control (flexibility vs. speed, arbitrated metacognitively) rather than of prediction error. G28 is the sharpest single test in the table: it holds the transition structure fixed by construction, so it isolates the *use* half of the framing from the *discovery* half, and it is the reason a model-free system and a model-based one are distinguishable at all after both have converged. G29 sits alongside G16 and G26 as the third independent argument that the target is not reachable by optimising a fixed model on more data — G16 says the data cannot select it, G26 says the strongest bias does not expose it, G29 says the search operator cannot reach it.

> **Provenance (ninth ingest).** Richards et al. 2019 adds G30 and re-labels the table rather than extending it: every existing row can now be sorted into the slot it belongs to (objective / learning rule / architecture), and the sort is what exposes G30 — the wiki's rows cluster in the architecture slot, the objective slot is nearly empty, and it is the only one of the three that is *not directly observable* in brains, so the transfer channel could not have filled it. G16 gains a rival count of the control surface (three components, data demoted to an anchor — T15) and G19 gains a sharper diagnosis: an unselective local rule may not merely be writing the wrong edges but writing *orthogonally to every objective*, which by this source's criterion is change rather than learning.

> **Provenance (tenth ingest).** Chollet 2019 adds G31–G32 and rewrites G17's answer column. The shift it forces is that **evaluation is a design problem with a target quantity**, not a genre of test: every earlier instrument in G17 described what a test should *look like*, and this one says what it should *measure* — and adds the requirement none of them had, that the tasks be unknown to the **developer**, since priors and data are two interchangeable ways of buying skill without generalization. G16 is sharpened in the same move: the four levers are the channels through which a developer pays for the intended rule, and the intelligence formula charges for exactly that payment, so the wiki's control surface and its scoring function are two views of one quantity. G26 gains an antagonist rather than an answer — [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T16 — since the shortest curriculum-consistent program is now argued to be the *wrong* object, not merely an insufficiently structured one.

> **Provenance (eleventh ingest).** LeCun 2022 adds G33–G34 and is the first source in the table to arrive as a *design* rather than as a finding, a formalism or a critique — so it changes the answer column of many rows while closing none, because it reports no experiments. G15's *when to plan* is joined by a mechanism for **what to plan toward** at coarse levels (subgoal = a condition on the lower-level state, scored by a learned cost, with the intermediate vocabulary learned rather than predefined) — and G33 is what that mechanism still assumes. G24's depth question is partly relocated rather than answered: if each level's horizon is set by what its representation can still predict, planning depth is read off the encoder instead of chosen. G30 gains its second candidate objective, and G34 is the reason to distrust it — most terms in a representational objective are anti-degeneracy machinery, not signal. G2 gains a cheap de-aliasing rule (allocate a new memory slot when the query is far from every key) and G5's meta half is untouched, since reconfiguration is not structure learning. G28 is the row this source engages most directly and does not close: a *configurable* world-model engine with a separately-specified cost is exactly the transitions/preferences factorization G28 asks for, stated as a design goal — with no demonstration that swapping the cost yields immediate re-goaling.

> **Provenance (twelfth ingest).** Delétang et al. 2023 adds G35 and changes the *type* of two rows rather than their content. G26 stops being an argument and becomes a measurement: a model can hold the best code length on a modality while demonstrably lacking its structure. G17/G31 gain a cheap partial instrument — compression rate as a function of position in the stream, which needs no authored tasks, no labels and no distribution shift, and is honest only as a *necessary* condition (a flat curve on a structured stream means nothing was found; a steep one does not certify what was). G30's objective slot turns out never to have been empty: autoregressive log-loss *is* minimum code length, and the adjusted rate is the same two-part code the genome-bottleneck argument runs with the design/parameter split moved.

> **Provenance (thirteenth ingest).** Choi 2026 adds G36 and sharpens G26's instrument. It is the wiki's first source arguing intelligence is a property of the *finished arrangement* rather than of the process that built it ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T20), and the first to make recurrence a necessary rather than convenient condition: without a loop no rule can be reused across positions, the domain is pinned to the input dimension, and `ℐ` cannot diverge.

> **Provenance (fourteenth ingest).** Zhi-Xuan et al. 2022 adds no row and supplies the first *worked* answer to three. G1 gains a demonstration that the meta/instance factorization, when authored rather than discovered, makes instance binding an exact closed-form update — which is what the row has been asking for on the acquisition side and cannot get on the discovery side. G23 gains its first implemented near-relative (FEASIBILIZE), gating hypotheses on world state instead of entities on properties. G17 gains a sixth instrument, paired violation of expectation, and with it the table's first evidence of a model scoring *below* chance — a shortcut anti-correlated with the intended rule, which no accuracy-only protocol can distinguish from noise. The source also sharpens what "no structure discovery" costs: the model's environment dynamics are hand-written PDDL, so its win over minimal-bias baselines is unmatched on inputs ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T21), and every gap about *acquiring* a graph is untouched by it.

> **Provenance (sixteenth ingest).** Ying et al. 2020 adds G37 and is the first source in the table whose contribution is an *operation* rather than a mechanism, a bound or a critique: the wiki had discovery and navigation and had been assuming retrieval between them. It also touches three rows without closing any — G34 gains an anti-collapse device that costs nothing (an asymmetric order energy punishes a constant encoder on its own negatives), G32 gains a case where the curriculum is *derived* from the task's compositional structure rather than tuned (+6% AUROC, lower variance), and G17's problem reappears in miniature: a 97.9 AUROC matcher with 49% precision is the ranking-metric version of "the test cannot certify what the model found".

> **Provenance (nineteenth ingest).** Yassa & Stark 2011 adds G38 and changes G2 and G37 in the same move, because it is the wiki's first source to treat de-aliasing and retrieval as *one* quantity rather than two mechanisms: they are the two sides of a single transfer function from input similarity to output similarity, and the interesting object is neither side but the curve's steepness and offset. That reframing turns a design constant into a control problem (G38) and makes the aging literature an engineering result — a store whose bias drifts toward completion does not forget, it returns a neighbour with confidence. It closes nothing: no functional form is given for the controllers, and the review's own caveat is that the input axis of every published curve is uncalibrated.

> **Provenance (twentieth ingest).** Epstein et al. 2017 adds G39 and splits G37 rather than answering it: retrieval and *orientation* are separate operations, and every schema mechanism in the table had been conflating them. It touches two other rows. G15 gains its first candidate for the *which branch* half — graph-theoretic features (degree and closeness centrality of the current node) are represented in posterior and anterior hippocampus before the search, and breadth-first-search cost shows up in lateral prefrontal cortex — so the pruning information a search needs is read off the map rather than computed by the search. G3's periodic-code candidate gains reach (a sixfold entorhinal *and* ventromedial-prefrontal signal over a morphed-stimulus concept plane, with signal strength tracking performance) and a boundary (the social-space result is a vector code, not a grid, and hippocampal coding of discrete transition-defined spaces needs no continuum at all) — so "maps outside space" is better evidenced than "grids outside space".

> **Provenance (twenty-first ingest).** Whittington et al. 2022 adds G40–G41 and is the first source to *downgrade* a row rather than open one: G3, the wiki's oldest architectural hole, moves from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` because path integration makes path-consistency a property of the update rule in implemented models — and immediately opens G41, the precondition it needs (the actions must compose). G2 gains two built mechanisms with mirror-image costs (fast-and-local vs. transferable) and the observation that merging them is straightforward and undone. G40 is the row the wiki had no space for: every page here treats factorisation as free, and the warping of grid cells toward stable rewards is a measured case of a brain choosing *not* to factorise.

> **Provenance (twenty-second ingest).** Rolls 2013 adds G42 and is the first source in the table to price a mechanism in *synapses per neuron* rather than describing it. That changes the type of three rows. G38's cholinergic controller acquires a functional form (one scalar moving recurrent read-out gain down and recurrent learning rate up together) and stops being a named neuromodulator. G14's consolidation channel acquires a cost — `C^HBP = C^RC a_nc/a_CA3`, hence a polysynaptic reverse hierarchy — which inverts an assumption every retrieval-augmented architecture makes: a sparser store is *harder*, not cheaper, to read back. G2 gains a de-aliasing answer that is not a computation at all (random hashing on the write path), and with it the reason the read port must be anatomically separate. The row it does not touch is the one it most exposes: this is the wiki's most quantitatively developed hippocampal model and it has no transfer story whatsoever, so capacity and generalisation are being optimised by different models of the same tissue ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T28).

> **Provenance (Chandra et al. 2023).** Moves G42 from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` — the wiki's first machine-side store with a derived capacity and a *graceful* overload — and in doing so changes the type of two more rows. G2 gains a de-aliasing answer that is randomization *readable in both directions*, because the address is regenerated by frozen dynamics instead of re-hashed. G37's relaxation answer loses its two worst properties (content-dependent basins, linear capacity) and keeps its worst limitation (no similarity structure among addresses). The row it most exposes is G1: this is a complete instance-level store whose shared level is *installed outright* — grid modules, periods, torus topology and the traversal order of a non-spatial episode are all supplied — so it is the cleanest demonstration in the table that capacity and structure discovery are separable problems, and that solving the first buys nothing toward the second. It also lands a negative result the objective slot (G30) should carry: an unconstrained autoencoder of matched size trained end-to-end by backpropagation is *beaten* by the frozen-scaffold architecture, so the inductive bias is not something the optimiser finds on its own.

G27's allocation criterion gains nothing at all — the fitted agent is a fixed-cardinality 2-state sticky hidden Markov model, so the number of contexts is supplied by the experimenter and the paper is evidence for belief maintenance over a known context set only; the wiki should stop reading causal hippocampal results as support for the nonparametric half. G15's arbitration question gains a mechanism note from the wrong direction: the model-free error signal is computed over the inferred belief state, so "model-free" describes the update rule and not the input, and removing the state estimator leaves a competent Q-learner running over an impoverished state space rather than a broken agent ([[wiki/concepts/amortized-inference.md]], [[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]]).

> **Provenance (Zheng et al. 2024).** Opens no row and closes none, but is the first source in the table to report the setting of G40's knob *as measured in a brain* rather than as a stated rule: two relational structures over one item set are held factored, in non-overlapping hippocampal-formation territory, under exactly the conditions ("the components always co-occur") that the rule predicts should favour an entangled code. It also gives G12 its first evidence that structure types are physically sorted at all, while leaving the router unbuilt, and adds [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T51 against the conjunctive-map assumption that T40/T42 and the whole `p = f(g̃ ⊙ x̃)` line rest on.

> **Provenance (Valero et al. 2025).** Opens no row and closes none, but changes the *type* of G38's answer. Every prior candidate set one scalar for the whole structure; recording all four interneuron families simultaneously shows the separation/completion bias is a vector of separately addressable channels, each causally tied to one feature of the place code and each verified by activating that family alone during behaviour. The gap's actuator is therefore built and characterised, and what remains open is strictly the controller above it. It also lands a smaller point on G44 and on [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]: unit *classes* can be recovered from intrinsic activity statistics fitted on a small causally-labelled subset and then validated against properties withheld from the fit — no model in the wiki has a unit taxonomy of any kind.

> **Provenance (Sun et al. 2025).** Adds G46 and changes the evidential status of three rows without moving them. G2's de-aliasing answer is now *watched being built* in a brain rather than argued for — and is shown not to be implied by task success, since long short-term memory networks and transformers solve the same aliased sequence with their states still correlated, which adds a false-positive class to G17 (behavioural success, in or out of distribution, does not certify that a learner split its nodes). G38's separation/completion bias gains the observation that the separating axis is *latent-state* distance rather than input distance and that the bias is position-specific within one animal, which is further evidence against the single-scalar shape. G30's empty objective slot gains a concrete candidate term with a demonstrated necessity: nothing in a prediction loss produces orthogonalization, and it appears only under soft winner-take-all competition or an explicit decorrelation penalty ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T53).

> **Provenance (Qu et al. 2026).** Opens no row and closes none, but changes the evidential status of G39, G14 and G30. **G39** (anchoring a retrieved structure to the present situation) gains its only case where the registration is *inherited rather than recomputed*: four new items inserted into an already-learned conceptual map are reasoned about through the entorhinal grid orientation estimated **before** the insertion, and per-subject agreement between the new items' inferred trajectories and that pre-existing orientation predicts non-local inference accuracy (`r` = 0.21) while being uncorrelated with age — so frame constancy is both the mechanism and a scoreable quantity, and the same regression runs unchanged on any model with a persistent `g` ([[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]]). **G14** (instance structure never becomes meta structure) gains the missing *partial-update* case: what must be protected is selected by role (`g` held fixed, bindings and the metric readout rewritten) rather than by an importance estimate, and the two contributions dissociate — the grid code's contribution to assimilation does not develop with age while the medial-prefrontal distance code's does ([[wiki/concepts/continual-learning.md]]). **G30** (the `g`/`x` factorization occupies only one slot of three) gains its first *external* validation of the architecture bet rather than another instance of it: across 203 developing humans, structural-code strength predicts Raven's matrix-reasoning score and training sessions to criterion, which is the wiki's only measured link between a candidate `g` and the ability the objective slot is supposed to deliver. All of it is cross-sectional and correlational, and the space is a hand-designed 2-D Euclidean plane, so G11's non-embeddable slice is untouched.
