# Architectural Gaps

What a brain-inspired reasoning model needs and no current architecture supplies. Updated after every ingest that opens or closes a gap, and rewritten at each lint pass.

**Status key:** `OPEN` — no known solution · `PARTIAL` — solved in a restricted setting · `CONTESTED` — candidate solutions exist and disagree.

> **Provenance:** rows G1–G13 were derived from [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] alone; G14–G15 come from the first ingest (Hassabis et al. 2017). Where an ingest has supplied evidence, the `From` column names it. Everything still unmarked records a *mechanism the framing names*, not an architecture the wiki has checked.

| G1 | **Two-level separation exists as a timescale split, not as a factorization** | Meta-graph and instance-graph co-occur in every observation; a flat learner fits the mixture `E_θ[p(obs\|θ)]`, which no individual instance follows — so it cannot transfer at all | The *two learning rates* are instantiated and validated: complementary learning systems (fast hippocampal store + slow cortex + replay) and meta-RL (slow weights training a fast recurrent inner learner). | G2 | **De-aliasing without a hand-built state space** | The same observation recurs at structurally distinct graph positions; without path-sensitive identity the model merges nodes that must stay distinct | Clone cells, or identity carried by path-integrated `g`; sparse conjunctive coding in the fast store gives distinct codes to repeated observations; **module membership as a free identity tag** — 10-month-olds fail to individuate two entities typed by the same core system (duck/truck) and succeed when they are typed by different ones (ball/doll), so a bank of entry-gated priors de-aliases without path context. **Third free tag:**  an *event* is a **set** of co-active predictive encodings, so two identical observations sitting in different active sets are different events without any path integration. **Fourth answer, and the only one that is a tunable circuit rather than a tag:** expansion recoding through a sparse layer whose transfer function is *steeper than identity* — dentate granule cells separate at the smallest input changes and drive the separated code onto CA3 through detonator synapses, with lesion and NMDA-receptor-knockout data showing the step is necessary for discrimination and dispensable for undemanding conditioning (Yassa & Stark 2011). Its price is that it de-aliases *everything*, including repeats that should have been recognised, so it only works with a controller (G38). **Now built, two ways, with complementary costs:** a clone-structured hidden Markov model whose per-observation clone pool is frozen at 0/1 and whose transitions are learned by expectation-maximisation — local, fast, one environment, zero transfer ([[wiki/entities/cscg.md]]) — and an action-accumulated latent that de-aliases by *position* and transfers, but is slow to acquire ([[wiki/concepts/path-integration.md]], [[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]]). | G14 | **No consolidation channel: instance structure never becomes meta structure** | Biology transports one-shot hippocampal bindings into cortical structure by replay during offline periods. Machine replay only stabilizes a *single* learner (decorrelating a training stream); nothing converts what the fast level discovered about one instance into a revision of the meta-graph | Replay/consolidation, named biologically; episodic control uses the fast store for behaviour but has no path back into the slow one. **The biological channel now has a measured selection criterion, and it is not the one machines copied**: replay suppresses salient-but-idiosyncratic stimuli, upsamples rarely-visited space and prefers remote to imminent trajectories, converging on an inductive bias toward *transferable* content — because consolidating everything overfits (Liao & Losonczy 2024; [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]). That gives the channel a specification a builder can implement (weight rehearsal by cross-episode recurrence and inverse visitation, not by reward or TD-error) and leaves the transport itself unbuilt, plus a new sub-problem: seven jobs claim the same replay budget under incompatible sampling policies. **One of those jobs is not transport at all but *construction*:** awake ripples increasingly co-activate a cue and an outcome never experienced together, without the intermediary, only when the chain ends in reward, and with the outcome cell firing *first* — so the offline period also writes edges the agent never traversed, in the direction of credit rather than of use (Barron et al. 2020; [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]). **And the transport has a wiring cost nobody prices** (Rolls 2013): reinstating a cortical pattern requires `C^HBP = C^RC · a_nc/a_CA3` hippocampally-originating afferents per cortical cell — ≥12,000 even in the best case — which makes a monosynaptic read-out impossible and forces a *polysynaptic reverse hierarchy* with gradual fan-out per stage. So the sparser the fast store (better capacity), the more expensive the read-back, a trade-off every retrieval-augmented architecture treats as free. The same source disputes the *schedule* (waking, not sleep — [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T34) and adds a confound: a changed cortical representation after learning is not evidence of transfer, since a changed CA1 code alters cortical firing through fixed connections. **A candidate for what the channel carries, one level up from the episode** (Lisman et al. 2018, [[wiki/concepts/memory-allocation-excitability.md]]): an hours-long CREB-dependent excitability tag makes temporally proximal episodes share a cell population *before* consolidation runs, and is proposed (untested) to raise those cells' sharp-wave-ripple participation — so the unit transported would be a tag-glued *cluster* of episodes rather than one episode, which is already an abstraction step, and replay priority becomes a decaying scalar set at encoding rather than a computed quantity. **The valve now has a specification, and it is implementable today** (Lindsey & Litwin-Kumar 2024, [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]]): gate the slow store's write by the fast store's recall of the *proposed update*, `Δw_LTM ∝ g(w_STM · w*)`, which filters one-off experience out of long-term storage because reliable and unreliable updates are indistinguishable at a synapse but separable across a population. The gating argument reduces to one broadcast scalar per event — prediction accuracy, reward × decision confidence, or familiarity, depending on the learning rule — so it needs no task boundary, no importance estimate, no stored exemplars and no offline pass, and it provably beats every synapse-local consolidation mechanism when reinforcement arrives in bursts. What it does **not** supply is the transport itself: the slow store still learns from the same event stream rather than receiving anything the fast store discovered, so the gate answers *which* updates deserve to become structure and leaves *how instance structure becomes meta structure* exactly where it was  **The transport is now built, and it inverts the gap's premise** (Sun et al. 2023, [[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]]): a sparse Hopfield notebook one-shot-binds a random index to the slow learner's activity, settles into that index from random initialisation offline, reinstates the slow learner's input and output layers through return weights, and the slow learner runs gradient descent on *its own reactivated targets* — self-distillation from the fast store, with the environment never re-seen. So instance structure does become meta structure, in a linear model, with no privileged access to the data. What the same model shows is that running this channel unregulated is **harmful**: the optimal number of reactivations is finite and set by the predictability of the relation, and past it generalization degrades, falling below chance in low-SNR environments. For a relation the slow learner cannot model — because of noise, architecture mismatch, unobserved inputs or its own attention — the optimal transport is **zero**, which makes permanent fast-store residence a designed outcome rather than an unfinished consolidation and means the fast store is required at deployment, not only during training. The transport's value peaks where stored examples ≈ slow-learner parameters, the double-descent point that is simultaneously the worst regime for overfitting, so the channel is most valuable exactly where regulating it matters most. What remains missing is the regulator itself: the three estimators that work in the model (held-out validation, maximum-likelihood SNR from input–output covariance, initial learning speed) are either biologically implausible or need a prior linking data statistics to predictability that is deferred to meta-learning, and the criterion disagrees with the recall gate above on recurring-but-unmodellable relations ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T81) **And a constraint on the cargo** (Euston et al. 2012, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]): the cortical learner already represents the context, event and response terms at acquisition — what transfers is only the *association among them*, inside a bounded 0–2 h post-task window, with cortical consolidation then continuing ~2 weeks. That makes the machine version a *partial* write (freeze the representations, replay only into the associative weights) rather than a full-parameter distillation, and it predicts the otherwise odd asymmetry that a cortical lesion costs more at remote than at recent delays without the region being dispensable early ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T97). | [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]], [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]], [[wiki/concepts/memory-allocation-excitability.md]], [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]], [[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]] | `PARTIAL` — the selection criterion is specified and computable, and a transport loop exists in a linear model; what is unbuilt is the regulator that stops it |

| G17 | **No evaluation protocol can certify structure discovery** | i.i.d. testing cannot separate a recovered meta-graph from a correlation holding in the sample, so every "architecture X reasons" claim is unscoreable and the hardness-source scoring table has no valid instrument. Existing o.o.d. benchmarks are domain-specific, hand-built, and must be revised as models learn to game them (the Winograd Schema Challenge was found to contain the shortcuts it was designed to remove) | Controlled-shortcut datasets (Shift-MNIST / biased CelebA / unfair dSprites), where the intended rule is known by construction and the accuracy drop is the measurement; three design conditions for a valid o.o.d. test. Second instrument, from developmental method: **signature limits** — certify an installed prior by a quantitative constant (capacity, Weber ratio, entry threshold) that stays put across scale, retraining and domain shift, and by a **resource-stress probe** (shrink the budget; in biology the learned distinctions degrade first and the core ones last). Diagnostic evidence is in the failure profile, not the success rate ([[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]]). **Two further instruments, and the first that need no distribution shift at all** — hold the trained model fixed and vary the *query*: (i) the **richness protocol** — one representation must answer classify / generate / parse / create-new-concepts / explain, and a shortcut rule is by construction a mapping to one output type ([[wiki/concepts/causal-model-building.md]]); (ii) the **re-goaling protocol** — hold the transition structure fixed, swap the reward function, measure the retraining needed ([[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]]). Both are gameable by multi-task training on the query set, and neither certifies that the abilities come from *one* model. **Fifth instrument, and the first that is a quantity rather than a genre** (Chollet 2019): score `GD = H(Sol^θ_T | TrainSol^opt_{T,C}) / H(Sol^θ_T)` — how far the shortest curriculum-optimal program must be edited to work at evaluation — and run it *developer-aware*, conditioning also on the initial system, so anything the builder injected stops counting as difficulty. This reframes the certification target: the protocol must be run against the developer, not only the system, because skill on any task known in advance is purchasable outright with priors or with data. [[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]] is the instrument built to this spec (private evaluation set, ~3 examples per task, no data generator, enumerated priors). **Sixth instrument — paired violation of expectation** ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]): show one history, then two continuations differing only in the property under test, and score whether the model rates the intended one higher. Its limit is that the pair must be authored by hand, so it inherits the developer-awareness problem wholesale. It answers a question no behavioural protocol reaches (is the structure *there*, and by which layer?) and is structurally unable to answer this row's: probes are trained on ground-truth labels, so they confirm containment of a structure the experimenter already had and cannot certify discovery of one they did not. It also splits the score the other six instruments merge — decodable structure minus behaviourally expressed structure, ~0.95 against 84% valid rollouts on the same model ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]) | [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]], [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]], [[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]], [[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]], [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]] | `OPEN` |

| G19 | **No local rule is selective about what it writes** | Every rule that can write during deployment ([[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]]) is a coactivity/covariance detector. It fires on shortcut-driven coactivity exactly as readily as on structural coactivity, so a fast **M** written by local plasticity accumulates spurious edges at the *instance* level — the same failure as G6, now inside the mechanism the wiki wants for online binding. Metaplasticity gates *how much* a synapse may change, never *whether the change is warranted*. Stated in the specification vocabulary: a rule is *learning* only if its update field has a positive projection onto some objective's gradient, and no local rule here names the objective it improves — so "unselective" may be the milder diagnosis and "orthogonal" the true one | Meta-optimizing the rule so the outer loop's task distribution filters what the rule writes on — i.e. importing the multi-environment identifiability argument into the update rule. Named here, not built. **Biology supplies two partial selectivity mechanisms, at opposite ends of the timescale.** (i) Behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity writes only when a dendritic plateau arrives on a separate anatomical pathway, so *whether to allocate* is a licensing signal rather than a property of the coactivity — but what generates the plateau at the right moment is unknown, which relocates the selection problem rather than solving it. (ii) Offline replay culls: a stimulus that was represented online is actively suppressed from consolidation if it does not recur, with inhibitory plasticity the predicted (unconfirmed) substrate. So selectivity is real in biology and is implemented *outside* the excitatory write rule — once as a gate on it, once as a filter after it (Liao & Losonczy 2024). **A third, purely engineering and older than both** (Albus 1971 via Kanerva 1993, [[wiki/entities/sparse-distributed-memory.md]]): read before you write, and update only if the stored answer is already wrong — `ΔC = g(p̂_u − s_u)/K` applied to the active locations only when `|p̂_u − s_u|` exceeds tolerance. Selectivity here comes from the store's own read-out rather than from a separate licensing pathway, so it needs no plateau, no replay pass and no outer loop; what it does need is a target `p̂_u`, which is exactly what an unsupervised online writer does not have. ## How gaps are used

A gap is worth recording only if it is *actionable*: it names a mechanism the wiki would adopt if it existed. Gaps that reduce to "the problem is hard" are folded into the open-problems section of the relevant concept page instead.

**Dependency structure.** These are not independent. G16 sits *under* G1: it says the factorization G1 wants is not the kind of thing data can supply, so G1 is a design decision to be paid for rather than a result to be waited for. G17 is the instrument problem for the whole table — without an out-of-distribution test, no gap can be shown closed, because an architecture that assumed the latent variable away is indistinguishable from one that recovered it. G1 is the root — G2 and G3 are the conditions under which its factorization is meaningful, and G9 is what happens when it is applied to itself. G7→G8 and G11→G12 are each a problem followed by the mechanism that would dissolve it. G13 is the accounting frame for all of them: an architecture is scored by which hardness sources it reaches, not by whether it "works". G14 and G15 are the two halves of the write/read loop around the factorization: G14 is how the meta-graph gets updated from experience, G15 is how the graph gets used once it exists. G19 and G20 are the corresponding pair one level down, on the *write rule for fast M*: G19 is what gets written, G20 is when the signal that authorizes the write arrives. G18 is the tax on G9's new candidate answer — self-reference buys the third tier, and generalization is the price. G21–G23 open a column the table did not have: what is in the graph *before* learning. G23 is the condition under which installed priors can be stacked at all; G21 is what they cannot do once installed; G22 is the reason G21's candidate answers do not obviously work. G21 also inverts G4 — where G4 asks how a vocabulary is induced, G21 assumes several are given and asks how they are joined.

**What would close one.** A gap closes when an ingest supplies an architecture that (a) instantiates the named mechanism and (b) is tested on a task where the corresponding latent variable is actually hidden. An architecture that assumes away the latent variable does not count as evidence. Per G17, condition (b) means an **out-of-distribution** test with a well-defined intended solution — an i.i.d. result never closes a gap here.

> **Provenance (second ingest).** Rows G16–G17 come from Geirhos et al. 2020; G6 was revised by the same source from "correlational edges can survive" to "shortcut solutions are the default outcome across every subfield studied".

> **Provenance (third ingest).** Rows G18–G20 come from Schmidgall et al. 2023, which also moved G9 from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` by naming self-referential meta-learning as a candidate third tier. G19 is G6 restated one level down: the same shortcut problem, now in the *write rule for fast M* rather than in the slow learner's weights — which means fixing G6 at the slow level does not fix it at the fast one.

> **Provenance (fifth ingest).** Rows G24–G26 come from Hutter 2000, which also moved G13 from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` by supplying AIXItl. The source changes the table's status in one respect: G25 is the wiki's first gap with a *proof* that its answer does not exist, rather than a report that none has been found. Its dependents are G17 (an o.o.d. certificate cannot be fully general because active-case optimality is uncertifiable) and G13 (the "which hardness sources to drop" question has no principled answer partly because there is no yardstick to score the drop against). G26 sits under G16 as G16 sits under G1: the bias must be paid for (G16), and the strongest bias available does not buy the thing G1 wants (G26).

> **Provenance (fourth ingest).** Rows G21–G23 come from Revencu & Csibra 2023. They shift the table's centre of gravity: G1–G20 all concern what a learner must *acquire*, while these three concern what it must be *given*, and what being given several disconnected priors then costs. G2 also gains a second answer from this source — an entity typed by a different core system is individuated without any path context, so module membership is a free de-aliasing tag.

> **Provenance (sixth ingest).** Spelke & Kinzler 2007, the primary source behind G21–G23, adds no new row but changes three. G17 gains its second candidate instrument (signature limits + the resource-stress probe), which is the first proposal in the table for certifying a *prior* rather than a learned structure. G23 gains a reason rather than just a statement: an ungated geometry prior would be actively wrong on movable objects, so entry tests are correctness machinery, not efficiency machinery. And the source adds a constraint that cuts across G21–G23 — installed priors must be **defeasible** ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T11), since the core principles are false outside the scale of action and are in fact overridden, always via an external symbol system.

> **Provenance (seventh ingest).** Butz 2016 adds G27 and moves three rows off `OPEN`: G5 to `PARTIAL` (a processing loop that is genuinely joint at the instance level, still phase-separated at the meta level) and G15 to `PARTIAL` (homeostatic free energy answers *when to plan*, leaving *how deep* to G24). It is the first source to give G16 a concrete architecture-lever proposal rather than a restatement, and the first to give G21/G22 a composition mechanism that needs no shared symbolic format — composition as relaxation to a joint free-energy minimum, with selection falling out as "no minimum, no composition". G27 is a column the table did not have: G1–G26 all concern the graph's *contents*, G27 concerns whether there are discrete nodes at all. Note the dependency it creates: G27 sits under G2 and G3, since identity and path-consistency are only well-posed once the node set exists.

> **Provenance (eighth ingest).** Lake et al. 2017 adds G28–G29 and changes two rows. G17 gains its first *shift-free* instruments — vary the query, not the data — which matters because every earlier candidate required constructing a distribution shift with a known intended solution, and that construction is the expensive part. G15 gains a second answer to *when to plan* that is priced in the currency of control (flexibility vs. speed, arbitrated metacognitively) rather than of prediction error. G28 is the sharpest single test in the table: it holds the transition structure fixed by construction, so it isolates the *use* half of the framing from the *discovery* half, and it is the reason a model-free system and a model-based one are distinguishable at all after both have converged. G29 sits alongside G16 and G26 as the third independent argument that the target is not reachable by optimising a fixed model on more data — G16 says the data cannot select it, G26 says the strongest bias does not expose it, G29 says the search operator cannot reach it.

> **Provenance (ninth ingest).** Richards et al. 2019 adds G30 and re-labels the table rather than extending it: every existing row can now be sorted into the slot it belongs to (objective / learning rule / architecture), and the sort is what exposes G30 — the wiki's rows cluster in the architecture slot, the objective slot is nearly empty, and it is the only one of the three that is *not directly observable* in brains, so the transfer channel could not have filled it. G16 gains a rival count of the control surface (three components, data demoted to an anchor — T15) and G19 gains a sharper diagnosis: an unselective local rule may not merely be writing the wrong edges but writing *orthogonally to every objective*, which by this source's criterion is change rather than learning.

> **Provenance (tenth ingest).** Chollet 2019 adds G31–G32 and rewrites G17's answer column. The shift it forces is that **evaluation is a design problem with a target quantity**, not a genre of test: every earlier instrument in G17 described what a test should *look like*, and this one says what it should *measure* — and adds the requirement none of them had, that the tasks be unknown to the **developer**, since priors and data are two interchangeable ways of buying skill without generalization. G16 is sharpened in the same move: the four levers are the channels through which a developer pays for the intended rule, and the intelligence formula charges for exactly that payment, so the wiki's control surface and its scoring function are two views of one quantity. G26 gains an antagonist rather than an answer — [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T16 — since the shortest curriculum-consistent program is now argued to be the *wrong* object, not merely an insufficiently structured one.

> **Provenance (eleventh ingest).** LeCun 2022 adds G33–G34 and is the first source in the table to arrive as a *design* rather than as a finding, a formalism or a critique — so it changes the answer column of many rows while closing none, because it reports no experiments. G15's *when to plan* is joined by a mechanism for **what to plan toward** at coarse levels (subgoal = a condition on the lower-level state, scored by a learned cost, with the intermediate vocabulary learned rather than predefined) — and G33 is what that mechanism still assumes. G24's depth question is partly relocated rather than answered: if each level's horizon is set by what its representation can still predict, planning depth is read off the encoder instead of chosen. G30 gains its second candidate objective, and G34 is the reason to distrust it — most terms in a representational objective are anti-degeneracy machinery, not signal. G2 gains a cheap de-aliasing rule (allocate a new memory slot when the query is far from every key) and G5's meta half is untouched, since reconfiguration is not structure learning. G28 is the row this source engages most directly and does not close: a *configurable* world-model engine with a separately-specified cost is exactly the transitions/preferences factorization G28 asks for, stated as a design goal — with no demonstration that swapping the cost yields immediate re-goaling.

> **Provenance (twelfth ingest).** Delétang et al. 2023 adds G35 and changes the *type* of two rows rather than their content. G26 stops being an argument and becomes a measurement: a model can hold the best code length on a modality while demonstrably lacking its structure. G17/G31 gain a cheap partial instrument — compression rate as a function of position in the stream, which needs no authored tasks, no labels and no distribution shift, and is honest only as a *necessary* condition (a flat curve on a structured stream means nothing was found; a steep one does not certify what was). G30's objective slot turns out never to have been empty: autoregressive log-loss *is* minimum code length, and the adjusted rate is the same two-part code the genome-bottleneck argument runs with the design/parameter split moved.

> **Provenance (thirteenth ingest).** Choi 2026 adds G36 and sharpens G26's instrument. It is the wiki's first source arguing intelligence is a property of the *finished arrangement* rather than of the process that built it ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T20), and the first to make recurrence a necessary rather than convenient condition: without a loop no rule can be reused across positions, the domain is pinned to the input dimension, and `ℐ` cannot diverge.

> **Provenance (fourteenth ingest).** Zhi-Xuan et al. 2022 adds no row and supplies the first *worked* answer to three. G1 gains a demonstration that the meta/instance factorization, when authored rather than discovered, makes instance binding an exact closed-form update — which is what the row has been asking for on the acquisition side and cannot get on the discovery side. G23 gains its first implemented near-relative (FEASIBILIZE), gating hypotheses on world state instead of entities on properties. G17 gains a sixth instrument, paired violation of expectation, and with it the table's first evidence of a model scoring *below* chance — a shortcut anti-correlated with the intended rule, which no accuracy-only protocol can distinguish from noise. The source also sharpens what "no structure discovery" costs: the model's environment dynamics are hand-written PDDL, so its win over minimal-bias baselines is unmatched on inputs ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T21), and every gap about *acquiring* a graph is untouched by it.

> **Provenance (sixteenth ingest).** Ying et al. 2020 adds G37 and is the first source in the table whose contribution is an *operation* rather than a mechanism, a bound or a critique: the wiki had discovery and navigation and had been assuming retrieval between them. It also touches three rows without closing any — G34 gains an anti-collapse device that costs nothing (an asymmetric order energy punishes a constant encoder on its own negatives), G32 gains a case where the curriculum is *derived* from the task's compositional structure rather than tuned (+6% AUROC, lower variance), and G17's problem reappears in miniature: a 97.9 AUROC matcher with 49% precision is the ranking-metric version of "the test cannot certify what the model found".

> **Provenance (nineteenth ingest).** Yassa & Stark 2011 adds G38 and changes G2 and G37 in the same move, because it is the wiki's first source to treat de-aliasing and retrieval as *one* quantity rather than two mechanisms: they are the two sides of a single transfer function from input similarity to output similarity, and the interesting object is neither side but the curve's steepness and offset. That reframing turns a design constant into a control problem (G38) and makes the aging literature an engineering result — a store whose bias drifts toward completion does not forget, it returns a neighbour with confidence. It closes nothing: no functional form is given for the controllers, and the review's own caveat is that the input axis of every published curve is uncalibrated.

> **Provenance (twentieth ingest).** Epstein et al. 2017 adds G39 and splits G37 rather than answering it: retrieval and *orientation* are separate operations, and every schema mechanism in the table had been conflating them. It touches two other rows. G15 gains its first candidate for the *which branch* half — graph-theoretic features (degree and closeness centrality of the current node) are represented in posterior and anterior hippocampus before the search, and breadth-first-search cost shows up in lateral prefrontal cortex — so the pruning information a search needs is read off the map rather than computed by the search. G3's periodic-code candidate gains reach (a sixfold entorhinal *and* ventromedial-prefrontal signal over a morphed-stimulus concept plane, with signal strength tracking performance) and a boundary (the social-space result is a vector code, not a grid, and hippocampal coding of discrete transition-defined spaces needs no continuum at all) — so "maps outside space" is better evidenced than "grids outside space".

> **Provenance (twenty-first ingest).** Whittington et al. 2022 adds G40–G41 and is the first source to *downgrade* a row rather than open one: G3, the wiki's oldest architectural hole, moves from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` because path integration makes path-consistency a property of the update rule in implemented models — and immediately opens G41, the precondition it needs (the actions must compose). G2 gains two built mechanisms with mirror-image costs (fast-and-local vs. transferable) and the observation that merging them is straightforward and undone. G40 is the row the wiki had no space for: every page here treats factorisation as free, and the warping of grid cells toward stable rewards is a measured case of a brain choosing *not* to factorise.

> **Provenance (twenty-second ingest).** Rolls 2013 adds G42 and is the first source in the table to price a mechanism in *synapses per neuron* rather than describing it. That changes the type of three rows. G38's cholinergic controller acquires a functional form (one scalar moving recurrent read-out gain down and recurrent learning rate up together) and stops being a named neuromodulator. G14's consolidation channel acquires a cost — `C^HBP = C^RC a_nc/a_CA3`, hence a polysynaptic reverse hierarchy — which inverts an assumption every retrieval-augmented architecture makes: a sparser store is *harder*, not cheaper, to read back. G2 gains a de-aliasing answer that is not a computation at all (random hashing on the write path), and with it the reason the read port must be anatomically separate. The row it does not touch is the one it most exposes: this is the wiki's most quantitatively developed hippocampal model and it has no transfer story whatsoever, so capacity and generalisation are being optimised by different models of the same tissue ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T28).

> **Provenance (Chandra et al. 2023).** Moves G42 from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` — the wiki's first machine-side store with a derived capacity and a *graceful* overload — and in doing so changes the type of two more rows. G2 gains a de-aliasing answer that is randomization *readable in both directions*, because the address is regenerated by frozen dynamics instead of re-hashed. G37's relaxation answer loses its two worst properties (content-dependent basins, linear capacity) and keeps its worst limitation (no similarity structure among addresses). The row it most exposes is G1: this is a complete instance-level store whose shared level is *installed outright* — grid modules, periods, torus topology and the traversal order of a non-spatial episode are all supplied — so it is the cleanest demonstration in the table that capacity and structure discovery are separable problems, and that solving the first buys nothing toward the second. It also lands a negative result the objective slot (G30) should carry: an unconstrained autoencoder of matched size trained end-to-end by backpropagation is *beaten* by the frozen-scaffold architecture, so the inductive bias is not something the optimiser finds on its own.


> **Provenance (Li et al. 2026).** Touches G42 from the other side without moving it: [[wiki/entities/mm-tem-hippoformer.md]] is the wiki's first fast store whose *forgetting is learned* — a data-dependent gate `α_t = σ(W_α m_t)` decaying the memory MLP's fast weights, alongside a data-dependent write rate and momentum — which is exactly the decide-when-to-forget policy this row says nothing has, supplied with no capacity estimate to decide it against. So the gap sharpens rather than closes: the machinery for graceful forgetting now exists and is trained end-to-end on task loss, meaning the store's effective capacity is whatever the outer loop happened to find, unmeasured and unreported. The row it most exposes is G30 — the same paper fills its objective slot with *supervised bidirectional retrieval* (`x → g`, `g → g` as explicit auxiliary losses, ablating either one degrades generalisation sharply), which is the first candidate objective in the table that targets the address property of `g` directly rather than a spatial readout of it.

> **Provenance (Seelig & Jayaraman 2015).** Opens no row and closes none, but changes the evidential status of G39 and G43. G39's anchoring operation is now *observed running end to end* in a complete, identified population ([[wiki/entities/fly-central-complex.md]]): a reusable ring code, one arbitrary re-assignable landmark offset, winner-take-all selection when two landmarks alias, and — the part no model in the wiki has — an offset-preserving **reset** rather than a precision-weighted blend when landmark and self-motion conflict, with its own defended time constant. What stays open is exactly what the row says: the conversion from visual features to an offset-free orientation estimate is unknown even here, in the simplest instance anyone has. G43 gains a measurement rather than an anatomical inference — concurrent integrators demonstrably disagree about which cue stream to trust (fly heading follows vision, mouse mEC grids follow physical motion; [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T46) — so arbitration is not an abstraction the wiki invented. It also supplies the first isolated test of the third regime every integrator needs and none is evaluated on: hold the code with *both* input streams removed (>30 s, correct on resumption).

> **Provenance (Constantinescu et al. 2016).** Opens no row. Changes G43 by supplying the first *measured relation between two concurrent frames* rather than another instance of one: entorhinal and ventromedial-prefrontal hexagonal codes over the same conceptual space are aligned to a common grid angle, each stable across sessions more than a week apart. If frames are mutually registered, the arbitration this row asks for collapses from coordinate conversion into weighting — but the same data are equally consistent with a single entorhinal code read out prefrontally, so the row's count question ("how many frames are running") is now measurable and still unmeasured ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T47). It also sharpens G3 and G41 from the evidence side: the abstract space in which a periodic code has been observed is a hand-designed continuous 2D plane whose increments commute by construction, and the authors state as their own open question how such codes behave in conceptual problems not embeddable that way — so the wiki's best abstract-grid result is drawn from exactly the regime where G41 is trivially satisfied.

> **Provenance (Courellis et al. 2024).** Adds G45 and changes the *instrument* column of two rows without moving either. G17's certification problem gains its sharpest negative result yet: in human hippocampus, decoding accuracy for the latent variable was statistically unchanged between sessions with and without one-shot inference, while cross-condition generalization and parallelism rose and then vanished again on error trials — so a protocol that scores decodability would have certified nothing and missed everything ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T25). G40 gains its second measurement and it points the opposite way from the first: entanglement and factorisation are not endpoints of one axis, because shattering dimensionality and CCGP rose *together* in the same population (T50), so a controller that decides "factorise vs entangle" is choosing which directions to make parallel, not how many dimensions to use. G39 is untouched but sharpened by the mechanism: the abstract context direction is bought by a 60% global firing-rate contraction with one dichotomy's separation spared, which is the first candidate answer in the table to *how* a code is made to generalize rather than what it should look like.

> **Provenance (Mishchanchuk et al. 2024).** Opens no row and closes none, but converts G37's statistical answer from a fit to an intervention. The "maintain a posterior over which stored model is generating the current data" answer had rested entirely on behavioural model comparison (COIN) and on simulations against published place-cell data; here ablating ventral CA1 in trained mice moves behaviour from a state-inference strategy to a cached-value one and removes the state-inference signature from accumbens dopamine, while imaging in the same region finds an abstract, cross-generalizing code for the task's latent context. Retrieval is therefore a *localised, necessary* operation rather than a normative description of behaviour. Two rows are sharpened without moving. G27's allocation criterion gains nothing at all — the fitted agent is a fixed-cardinality 2-state sticky hidden Markov model, so the number of contexts is supplied by the experimenter and the paper is evidence for belief maintenance over a known context set only; the wiki should stop reading causal hippocampal results as support for the nonparametric half. G15's arbitration question gains a mechanism note from the wrong direction: the model-free error signal is computed over the inferred belief state, so "model-free" describes the update rule and not the input, and removing the state estimator leaves a competent Q-learner running over an impoverished state space rather than a broken agent ([[wiki/concepts/amortized-inference.md]], [[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]]).

> **Provenance (Zheng et al. 2024).** Opens no row and closes none, but is the first source in the table to report the setting of G40's knob *as measured in a brain* rather than as a stated rule: two relational structures over one item set are held factored, in non-overlapping hippocampal-formation territory, under exactly the conditions ("the components always co-occur") that the rule predicts should favour an entangled code. It also gives G12 its first evidence that structure types are physically sorted at all, while leaving the router unbuilt, and adds [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T51 against the conjunctive-map assumption that T40/T42 and the whole `p = f(g̃ ⊙ x̃)` line rest on.

> **Provenance (Valero et al. 2025).** Opens no row and closes none, but changes the *type* of G38's answer. Every prior candidate set one scalar for the whole structure; recording all four interneuron families simultaneously shows the separation/completion bias is a vector of separately addressable channels, each causally tied to one feature of the place code and each verified by activating that family alone during behaviour. The gap's actuator is therefore built and characterised, and what remains open is strictly the controller above it. It also lands a smaller point on G44 and on [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]: unit *classes* can be recovered from intrinsic activity statistics fitted on a small causally-labelled subset and then validated against properties withheld from the fit — no model in the wiki has a unit taxonomy of any kind.

> **Provenance (Sun et al. 2025).** Adds G46 and changes the evidential status of three rows without moving them. G2's de-aliasing answer is now *watched being built* in a brain rather than argued for — and is shown not to be implied by task success, since long short-term memory networks and transformers solve the same aliased sequence with their states still correlated, which adds a false-positive class to G17 (behavioural success, in or out of distribution, does not certify that a learner split its nodes). G38's separation/completion bias gains the observation that the separating axis is *latent-state* distance rather than input distance and that the bias is position-specific within one animal, which is further evidence against the single-scalar shape. G30's empty objective slot gains a concrete candidate term with a demonstrated necessity: nothing in a prediction loss produces orthogonalization, and it appears only under soft winner-take-all competition or an explicit decorrelation penalty ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T53).

> **Provenance (Qu et al. 2026).** Opens no row and closes none, but changes the evidential status of G39, G14 and G30. **G39** (anchoring a retrieved structure to the present situation) gains its only case where the registration is *inherited rather than recomputed*: four new items inserted into an already-learned conceptual map are reasoned about through the entorhinal grid orientation estimated **before** the insertion, and per-subject agreement between the new items' inferred trajectories and that pre-existing orientation predicts non-local inference accuracy (`r` = 0.21) while being uncorrelated with age — so frame constancy is both the mechanism and a scoreable quantity, and the same regression runs unchanged on any model with a persistent `g` ([[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]]). **G14** (instance structure never becomes meta structure) gains the missing *partial-update* case: what must be protected is selected by role (`g` held fixed, bindings and the metric readout rewritten) rather than by an importance estimate, and the two contributions dissociate — the grid code's contribution to assimilation does not develop with age while the medial-prefrontal distance code's does ([[wiki/concepts/continual-learning.md]]). **G30** (the `g`/`x` factorization occupies only one slot of three) gains its first *external* validation of the architecture bet rather than another instance of it: across 203 developing humans, structural-code strength predicts Raven's matrix-reasoning score and training sessions to criterion, which is the wiki's only measured link between a candidate `g` and the ability the objective slot is supposed to deliver. All of it is cross-sectional and correlational, and the space is a hand-designed 2-D Euclidean plane, so G11's non-embeddable slice is untouched.

> **Provenance (Kanerva 1993).** Opens no row and closes none, but is the oldest source in the table and changes the *type* of three answers. **G42** gains the first capacity model any of these stores had — `τ ≈ 0.10·M`, derived from an explicit signal-to-noise ratio and independent of pattern size — and, more usefully, the first **per-retrieval confidence read-out** (`|s_u|`, each output bit's distance from its vote threshold), which is the observable the row says every machine store lacks. **G38** gains its first *schedule*: the optimal active fraction `p = (2MT)^(−1/3)` falls as the store fills, so the separation/completion set point is load-dependent by derivation rather than by controller, and a machine knows its own load exactly. **G19** gains error-gated writing (CMAC), where the gate is computed from the store's own read rather than delivered by a separate pathway. The same source supplies the counterweight the wiki's random-projection stores need: random addressing is optimal **only for uniform data**, and every known fix — hard addresses drawn from the data distribution, per-location activation radii, per-cue weighted distances — makes the addressing stage data-dependent, which is the one thing [[wiki/entities/vector-hash.md]] and [[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]] both keep random on purpose ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T55).

> **Provenance (Chaudhry et al. 2023).** Opens no row and closes none, but splits **G42** in two: every capacity number in the table so far counts *items*, and this source shows that the number of *chained transitions* a store supports is a different quantity with different scaling and a gap that can diverge with network size — so "how full is the store" and "how far can it roll out" are separate questions, and no other entry answers the second. It also moves the knob: capacity here is bought at the read-out nonlinearity with the code, the sparsity and the learning rule untouched, which is orthogonal to every other lever in the row (fan-in, active fraction, address space) and is the cheapest of them to implement. The row it most exposes is not its own but **G1/G30**: the patterns are supplied, the successor of each is supplied, and the entire content of the result is how many supplied edges survive traversal — the storage side of a graph the source has no way to discover. And it lands a warning the whole table should carry: capacity derived from i.i.d. random patterns can be off not by a constant but *qualitatively*, with a network well inside its predicted capacity recalling nothing at all on correlated real data.

> **Provenance (Douglas & Martin 2004).** Opens no row and closes none, but is the first source to supply *anatomy* for three rows the wiki has only ever argued about functionally. **G43** (arbitration between concurrent reference frames) gains a named circuit rather than a hypothesis: the operation [[wiki/concepts/distributed-reference-frames.md]] calls "voting across columns" is, on this account, soft winner-take-all among superficial pyramidal cells mediated by perisomatic (basket, chandelier) inhibition — propagated between columns by patchy lateral connections with a measured 2:1 spacing-to-diameter geometry, and between areas by layer-5 feedback into the superficial layers of other areas. It is *iterated* rather than one-shot, because the selected cells feed back onto the dendrite-targeting interneurons that set which inputs each pyramid integrates, so the competition and the hypothesis space co-evolve — which is a shape no arbitration proposal in the wiki has. What it does not supply is the objective the competition optimises or any simulation of it; the authors label it a tentative hypothesis. **G12** (no routing policy between structure types) gains a candidate primitive: cortex distinguishes *driver* from *modulator* excitatory edges morphologically, so "which input controls what this unit represents" is decided by edge type rather than by a learned gate. **G17** (no protocol can certify structure discovery) gains an instrument from an unexpected direction — the superficial-layer-neuron percentage makes hierarchical depth a continuous quantity *measured from the connectivity*, and it resolves a degeneracy (enormous numbers of hierarchies satisfy the binary feedforward/feedback rule) that its discrete predecessor could not; the machine analogue, estimating a learned hierarchy from the mixing proportion of two edge types and comparing it against the designed one, does not exist. It also lands a caution on every weight-magnitude reading in the table: with <10% of layer-4 excitatory synapses sufficient to drive cortex through recurrent amplification, an edge's weight is not an estimate of its causal influence.

> **Provenance (Litman 2025).** Opens no row and closes none, but changes what the *learning-rule* slot of **G30** costs. The row's complaint is that a design needs an objective a rule can ascend; the standard reply for two-phase energy-based rules was that they only approximately ascend one (exact as `β → 0`). That reply is withdrawn: the contrastive update is the *exact* gradient of the Helmholtz free-energy difference `J(θ) = A(θ,1) − A(θ,0)` at any finite nudging strength, with no convexity, unique-minimum, weight-symmetry or small-`β` assumption, and its apparent bias is a `T·KL(ρ_1‖ρ_0)` regulariser (Theorem 5.1). So for any architecture whose dynamics minimise an energy, objective *and* learning rule are filled by one choice — G30's slot problem is solved for that family, and what stays open is whether `J` is the objective the factorization needs, since nothing here is sensitive to path-consistency. It also puts a floor under the estimator: the useful signal scales with the phase displacement, and below `β ≈ 10⁻²` the difference the rule measures is smaller than the sampler's own noise, so "take a small enough step to be safe" is not available.

> **Provenance (wave 4 — credit assignment and plasticity: Whittington & Bogacz 2019; Hebbian theory nd; Bengio et al. 2015; Lisman et al. 2018; Gerstner et al. 1996; Scellier & Bengio 2017; Millidge et al. 2020; Shervani-Tabar & Rosenbaum 2023).** Opens no row and closes none — nine sources in a row that changed the *cost* of existing answers rather than the answers. Three effects on the table. **G30** loses its standing excuse: for any architecture whose dynamics minimise an energy, objective and learning rule are one choice, and the contrastive update is the *exact* gradient of `J(θ) = A(θ,1) − A(θ,0)` at any finite nudge ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T69) — so the empty objective slot is empty by omission now, not by obstruction, and what stays open is only whether `J` is sensitive to path-consistency (nothing here is). **G20** (no local rule reaches delayed credit *and* stays online) is unmoved but re-priced: the four models that most convincingly solve non-locality all *assume symmetric feedforward/feedback weights* and rely on their plasticity rule to maintain the symmetry, while the one family that solves weight transport transports the load-bearing part (T3) — and a new cost axis appears that no row was tracking, **inference latency** (predictive coding pays `2L−1` synaptic delays per forward pass forever in exchange for training fast). **G19** (no local rule is selective about what it writes) gains its fourth partial answer from Lisman et al. 2018 — cell-level eligibility as a conjunction of dendritic and somatic evidence — and its sharpest open sub-question, since neither the plateau generator nor the excitability tag's sign is settled (T68). The row this wave most exposes is **G14**: nine sources on how a weight change is computed, none on how a fast-store binding is transported into slow structure.

> **Provenance (lint pass, 67 ingests).** No rows added. [[wiki/concepts/attractor-dynamics.md]] was created to carry the mechanism that G37, G38 and G42 had each been re-deriving separately — relaxation onto weight-defined fixed points, typed by what defines them and by how many there are — and those three rows should be read against it rather than against their individual entity pages. The pass also recorded a shape the table does not express: **G15, G33, G37 and G38 are one gap seen four times** — a characterised mechanism whose single free parameter is exogenous, each with a runtime-computable label-free observable already identified and nothing descending it.

> **Provenance (Wu & Maass 2025, [[wiki/entities/btsp-cam.md]]).** Opens no row and closes none, but supplies the first *implemented* answer to three of them that all reduce to one parameter. **G38** gains a controller of the shape the row has been asking for since it was written: the plateau probability `f_q` is a single scalar, set by a biophysical duration (≈10 s window × ≈0.0005 s⁻¹ plateau rate ⇒ ≈0.005), that moves the store continuously between separation (sparse non-overlapping traces, robust partial-cue recall) and completion (large overlapping traces), and it is a *rate over time* rather than a sparsity constant, so it can be modulated within an episode. What is still missing is the same thing missing from every other candidate — nothing says when it should move. **G19** is untouched and now more sharply so: the gate decides *how much* is written and never *what is worth writing*, and the source shows the uninformativeness is load-bearing (an input-independent gate is exactly what spreads memory load uniformly and stops later items from degrading earlier ones), so write-selectivity and non-interference are in direct competition rather than being two independent desiderata. **G42** gains an analytic theory that predicts recall quality at a given load but still names no `p_max` and no overload behaviour, and it adds a constraint every other capacity row in the table violates: with bounded flippable weights, the store's state depends on the *sequence* of items rather than the set, so set-based capacity theory does not transfer. It also lands on **G37** from an unexpected direction — content-addressable completion is demonstrated with no recurrence and no relaxation at all ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T74) — and opens a competition with [[wiki/concepts/memory-allocation-excitability.md]] over whether allocation should be independent or overlapping (T73).

> **Provenance (Cazalets et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/hag-reservoir.md]]).** Adds no row and moves one. **G29** goes `OPEN` → `PARTIAL`: architecture is searched by a local unsupervised rule whose step is a single synapse, so the lever the row calls the heaviest is pulled algorithmically for the first time in the wiki — within a fixed neuron pool, on an undirected excitatory edge set, and with no objective named for the search to be ascending. The row's cost warning is what changes most: structure search was expected to inherit evolution's slow stochastic hill-climbing, and this one costs a single pass over unlabelled data. Two other rows are touched without moving. **G19** (no local rule is selective about what it writes) gains a sharper form on the side the wiki had not examined — HAG's growth *is* selective (highest correlation among off-homeostasis neurons) while its **erasure is uniformly random**, because the authors have no local criterion predicting the effect of a removal on the population code; so the selectivity deficit is now worse for forgetting than for writing. **G47** (nothing learns the topology of the manifold it represents on) gains its only instance of a recurrent coupling matrix derived from data rather than imposed by translation-invariant wiring — but the object grown is a connectivity graph, not a manifold with a stated topology, and the paper never asks what shape the resulting state space has, so the row stays `OPEN`.

> **Provenance (Mander et al. 2013).** Adds no row and moves none, but changes the *shape* of one. **G14** (no consolidation channel) has been written throughout as a missing mechanism whose specification is a sampling criterion — what to replay, how often. This source measures the channel's throughput in humans and locates its controller outside the store entirely: prefrontal slow-wave amplitude carries the whole between-subject variance in overnight retention, medial prefrontal grey matter mediates the age effect on that amplitude, and hippocampal volume predicts nothing. So a builder implementing G14 owes not only a selection rule but a **rate variable owned by the receiving learner** — degradable independently of the fast store, and priced in oscillation cycles rather than in offline seconds ([[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]]). It also puts an interpretation hazard on the row's main supporting evidence: incomplete transfer is the predicted output of a working regulator *and* of a broken pipe, and no published paradigm separates them ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T82).


> **Provenance (Lebedev et al. 2004).** Adds **G48** and puts a confound under every prefrontal delay-recording result the wiki cites. With an attended and a remembered location dissociated inside one trial, the attentional pointer is the larger delay signal (61% vs. 16% of spatially tuned cells) and the stronger one, and it *grows* as the moment of use approaches — so a sustained, spatially tuned delay signal is not evidence that anything is being maintained ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T88). Two rows are re-priced without moving. **G42** (no fast store knows when it is full) gains a second unmeasured occupant: some of the store's capacity is spent on pointers, so an item count is not the load. **G15/G33** (nothing controls where the next computation is spent) gain their only *observed* controller state — persistent, anticipatory, prefrontal, cleared by relevance before the action that makes the target irrelevant — which supplies the format of the missing policy's output without supplying the policy.

> **Provenance (Lundqvist et al. 2018).** Adds **G49** and gives **G48** its missing half. G48 said a store needs a pointer register as well as a content register; this source shows what the pointer is *for* — in a two-object sequence match with no motor confound at the first test, gamma bursting and object information ramp up in the delay before each test object, **for the item about to be tested only**, and not before an equally predictable sample or before a test object that an earlier mismatch has made irrelevant. So the pointer is a read schedule with a lead time, and it is gated by forthcoming queries rather than by event predictability. Two further rows are touched. **G42** (no fast store knows when it is full) gains a recovery mechanism it had not considered: capacity is freed by an addressed clear (post-trial beta at informative sites) rather than by decay, so occupancy is a controlled variable ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T89). **G15/G33** (nothing controls where the next computation is spent) gain their first *error* analysis: on non-match trials answered "match", the comparison at the test object was computed correctly and the deviation appeared only afterwards, in the read schedule — a control-layer failure with the encoding verified intact.

> **Provenance (DeRosa et al. 2024).** Adds no row and sharpens **G49** on the erase side: the missing primitive is a *family* of at least three (replace / suppress / clear), separable in human cortical representation and cut differently by different subsystems — visual cortex carries only the occupancy bit (held vs. not), somatomotor cortex separates only `clear`, and the frontoparietal control network is alone in keeping all four operations apart. Two rows are touched without moving. **G42** (no fast store knows when it is full) gains the observation that the occupancy bit is represented *separately from* and more cheaply than the operation identity, so "is the store loaded" and "what was just done to it" are different signals with different distributions. **G15/G33** (nothing controls where the next computation is spent) inherit the study's own unresolved question ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T90): whether the operation is implemented by the conjunction of four parallel network codes or by the control network alone with the rest as by-products of top-down drive.

> **Provenance (Gong & Zhang 2024).** Adds no row and gives **G42** its first capacity model for an *attention*-based store — and the first one that is computed for free on every read. Summed row entropy of the causal attention matrix rises with the `N`-back offset exactly as accuracy falls, in a model where the target item never left the context window, so the store was never full: the bound is on selection, not on storage, and a softmax row's fixed unit of probability mass is the mechanism. Two consequences the wiki had no row for: an attention read exposes a per-query confidence scalar nobody gates on, and retrieval precision degrades with *occupancy* by construction in every key-value store here that normalises its scores. **G3/G49 are touched without moving** — the learned solution is retrieval by a fixed structural offset (the addressing mode G3 wants), and it is precisely that mode whose reliability decays monotonically with distance, so "address by structure rather than content" is not free of the interference it was meant to escape ([[wiki/concepts/attention.md]], [[wiki/concepts/working-memory.md]]).

> **Provenance (Heidbreder & Groenewegen 2003, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]).** Adds **G50** and re-scopes two rows the wiki has been writing as if the controller were one module. **G37** (nothing decides which stored structure applies) gains its first evidence that *selection has separate hardware from construction*: ventral prelimbic/infralimbic lesions leave rule acquisition intact and destroy cross-modal shifting, while intramodal reversal survives — so a type-level switch and a value-level switch are dissociable operations, and the row's missing mechanism is a specific circuit rather than a missing step in a general one. **G12** (no routing policy between structure types) gains an anatomical proposal rather than a solution: the two tiers are not interconnected cortically but are chained through the basal ganglia, ventral → accumbens shell → ventral pallido-thalamic → dorsal, so precedence between controllers is carried on the action-selection bus. The source is a 2003 rat review — lesion-based, gradient-not-boundary, no recording of a control state — so every row it touches gains a mechanism to test, not a result to rely on.

> **Provenance (Wood & Grafman 2003, [[wiki/concepts/controller-knowledge-vs-process.md]]).** Adds **G51** and puts a prior question under the whole control layer: *does the controller have parameters at all?* Two rows are re-scoped. **G37** (nothing decides which stored structure applies) gains a candidate answer that is a storage scheme rather than a selection rule — if stored plans are partitioned by content category, by how many events they span and by whether their ordering is committed, then which structure applies is narrowed by *where* the query enters rather than by a search over one undifferentiated library; the price is that the four proposed partition axes have no stated interaction. **G33** (nothing decomposes a task into subgoals) is touched without moving: the structured event complex is a goal-oriented sequence with a maintenance window that ends on goal completion and is interruptible by a supervening goal, which is the first *termination condition* in the wiki that is a property of the stored content rather than a timer or an erase signal — but the framework specifies what is stored and never how it is acquired, so the decomposition is assumed. The row it most sharply qualifies is **G28**/G30's assumption that a plan is a total order: partially ordered stored sequences, claimed here to occupy lateral prefrontal cortex against the medial total-order kind, would interleave without rewriting, and no sequence model in the wiki stores anything but a walk.

> **Provenance (Spedding & Jay 2012, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]).** Adds **G52** and changes the *object* of two rows without moving either. Every gap in this table so far names a missing module, signal or criterion; this one names a missing property of a **connection**, and it arrives with the method that makes it testable — asymmetric disconnection, which severs one edge while leaving both endpoints intact. **G37** (nothing decides which stored structure applies) gains its sharpest intervention: cutting the ventral-hippocampus→prelimbic edge abolishes fear renewal, i.e. destroys the *selection* between two competing memories while leaving both memories expressible, which no lesion of either region achieves. **G50** (the controller cannot set the gain of its own teaching signal) gains a second, opposite instance — here a third region sets the *plasticity gate* on a pathway that is neither of its endpoints, so gain control over learning is distributed across the network rather than owned by the learner or by the environment.

> **Provenance (Preston & Eichenbaum 2013, [[wiki/concepts/schema-assimilation.md]]).** Adds no row and changes three. **G37** (nothing decides which stored structure applies) gains a fourth answer that is not a retrieval mechanism at all: inactivating medial prefrontal cortex leaves dorsal hippocampal retrieval intact but *indiscriminate*, so the biological store returns every candidate the cue touches and a separate controller suppresses the context-inappropriate ones — selection downstream of retrieval, with a failure mode (confident, context-inappropriate output) that none of the row's other answers produces, and a human match in the A–B/A–C intrusion pattern after prefrontal damage. **G38** (nothing sets the separate-vs-integrate knob) gains its first criterion: schema membership rather than input similarity, because new goals learned inside an already-schematised arena are written to the *same* hippocampal cells with similar patterns and differentiate only over subsequent days — the reverse of separate-at-write. **G14** (no consolidation channel) gains a scheduling variable to replace the clock: integration demand (conflict between the fast store's retrieval for the current cue and the new observation) decides whether the slow learner is needed at acquisition or only later, and the episodic detail the read-out requires decides whether the fast store is ever released ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T99). The cost is that "integration demand" is defined by experimental design and never measured within a trial, so all three are reframings awaiting an instrument.

> **Provenance (Sigurdsson & Duvarci 2016, [[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]]).** Adds **G54** and closes half of one row. **G53** (no architecture delivers one signal to two modules by the same wire) gains its motivating measurement: the coupling band that survives cutting the direct wire has to come from a common input, so the collateralised-bus motif is not an anatomical curiosity but the only available explanation for the coordination that remains after the content channel is removed. **G52** (no inter-module connection has state of its own) gains a second state variable and a training signal: the coupling on the hippocampus→prefrontal edge *increases across learning* in parallel with behavioural performance, and its magnitude is the phenotype that tracks working-memory impairment across five disease models — one of which traces the deficit to reduced axonal branching of that specific projection. Neither is a weight in either module.

> **Provenance (Lieberman & Meyer 2018, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]).** Adds no row and changes two. **G17** (no evaluation protocol certifies structure discovery) gains an instrument-level warning it did not have: every probe and selectivity sweep the wiki lists computes `P(activation | condition)`, and a claim about what a component *does* is `P(condition | activation)` — the fix is a head-to-head posterior between the candidate functions on the same activations, with the standing limitation that a function absent from the candidate set cannot win ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]], [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T102). The same source supplies the first quantitative form of the shared-vs-modular question — 17% of a control region is domain-selective at a strong threshold and 88% at a weak one — which is what makes "the controller is partitioned" a statement about degree rather than architecture (T96). **G39** (nothing anchors a retrieved structure to the present situation) gains a candidate substrate and a wider content specification: a ventromedial cluster that is diagnostic of five psychological domains and preferred by none is proposed to hold an integrated **situation model** — spatial, temporal, causal, **evaluative and social** aspects bound together — i.e. one shared slot every domain reads, rather than one anchor per domain. Two consequences the wiki should carry: the evaluative and social dimensions are absent from every schema and event-model formalism here, all of which descend from navigation; and no benchmark in the wiki manipulates *situational demand* as an independent variable, so the operation is untestable in a machine even though its lesion signature is described in humans ([[wiki/concepts/schema-assimilation.md]]).

> **Provenance (Friedman & Robbins 2021, [[wiki/concepts/control-unity-and-diversity.md]]).** Adds **G55** and re-scopes three rows. **G42** (no fast store has a capacity model) gains a warning about the measurement rather than the model: every capacity number in the wiki is read off *one* task, and control tasks are impure by construction — the same constructs correlate at −0.05–0.34 across tasks and at 0.42–0.63 across latent variables, so a model whose task-level control scores look uncorrelated may still have a perfectly unified controller. **G50** (the controller cannot set the gain of its own teaching signal) gains its dose–response shape: each control component has an inverted-U optimum and the optima differ by component, so a controller with one global gain is mis-specified before any learning happens, and self-set gain has to be per-component. **G49** (no store has a relevance-addressed erase) gains a candidate primitive from the opposite direction — one polymorphic braking channel (right inferior frontal gyrus → subthalamic nucleus for actions, → hippocampus via mid-temporal relays for retrievals, → orbitofrontal/amygdala for affect, all sharing a right-frontal beta signature) instead of the store-typed erase family the wiki has been collecting.

> **Provenance (Arnsten et al. 2010, [[wiki/concepts/dynamic-network-connectivity.md]]).** Adds **G56** and moves two rows. **G50** (the controller cannot set the gain of its own teaching signal) keeps its status but gains its first *functional* statement of the loop rather than an anatomical one — prefrontal cortex regulates the firing of the norepinephrine, dopamine and acetylcholine cell groups that in turn set its own functional state ("vicious vs. delicious cycles") — and with it the property a builder most needs: the loop is unstable in *both* directions, so a self-set gain requires a stabiliser of its own, and biology's is a stack of mutually antagonistic chemical brakes rather than a computational one. **G42** (no fast store has a capacity model) gains a locus none of its previous answers has: capacity here is not a ceiling the substrate imposes but a **margin the system chooses**, held in place by a Ca²⁺→K⁺ negative feedback whose job is seizure prevention, and recoverable — blocking the SK channel (apamin) or IP₃-mediated Ca²⁺ release (xestospongin C) *improves* working memory while removing the same brakes genetically lowers seizure threshold. That makes "how full is the store" and "how close is it to instability" the same measurement ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T104), and it is measurable in any recurrent machine store by sweeping its stability regulariser.

> **Provenance (Wang et al. 2018, [[wiki/entities/meta-rl-agent.md]]).** Adds **G57** and moves two rows. **G1** (the two-level split exists as a timescale split, not a factorization) gains its primary source and its sharpest negative result: the split works, in the sense that a frozen-weight inner learner matches Gittins/Thompson/UCB and one-shots novel stimulus pairs — but *neither level is represented anywhere*. The meta-graph is in weights, the instance-graph is in hidden state, and both are recoverable only by principal-component analysis after the fact, so this is the two-level shape achieved with maximal entanglement, the exact opposite of [[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]'s authored hierarchy. **G17** (no evaluation protocol certifies structure discovery) takes damage rather than progress: the two-step task, the wiki's cleanest mechanism-diagnosing protocol, is shown to be passed by a model-free-trained network ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T105), which means behavioural signatures certify the inner loop's profile and not the architecture that produced it — the protocol has to be paired with an intervention on the putative simulator to say anything about mechanism.
