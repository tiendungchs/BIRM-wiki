# Architectural Gaps

What a brain-inspired reasoning model needs and no current architecture supplies. Updated after every ingest that opens or closes a gap, and rewritten at each lint pass.

**Status key:** `OPEN` — no known solution · `PARTIAL` — solved in a restricted setting · `CONTESTED` — candidate solutions exist and disagree.

> **Provenance:** rows G1–G13 were derived from [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] alone; G14–G15 come from the first ingest (Hassabis et al. 2017). Where an ingest has supplied evidence, the `From` column names it. Everything still unmarked records a *mechanism the framing names*, not an architecture the wiki has checked.

| G13 | **No tractable system covers all six hardness sources** | AIXI is the only known system that does, and it fails on computability. | G15 | **No control policy over simulation** | Planning is path search, but nothing decides when to roll out, how deep, which branch, or when the answer is good enough. | G16 | **The intended graph is not identifiable from data alone** | Shortcut and causal rules are equally consistent with the observations, so no objective computed on one environment can prefer the structural rule. The `g`/`x` factorization G1 asks for therefore cannot be *discovered* — it must be imposed through inductive bias. The complete control surface is four levers (architecture, training data, loss, optimizer) and there is no account of which one buys graph-structured solutions, nor at what cost | Multi-environment invariance where a family is given; cross-entropy's early-stopping-at-first-sufficient-predictor identified as the sharpest loss-side target. **First concrete architecture-lever proposal:** split the predictor into three *typed* channels — top-down (item content, generalising over space), spatial (frame-of-reference mappings between encodings), temporal (change given forces) — so an appearance-only rule is not expressible in the two structural channels. Butz 2016 argues this is *necessary*: free-energy minimisation is indifferent to which encodings develop, so structural priors must be added on top of the objective. Stipulated functionally, not derived, and never implemented **Rival count of the surface:** Richards et al. 2019 give three components (objective, learning rule, architecture) and demote training data to an *anchor* — the components are compressible because they pass through the genome, the environment is not because it is the high-entropy source. If that reading is right, the data lever cannot be part of the design and G16 must be paid for entirely from the other three ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T15) | [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]], [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G17 | **No evaluation protocol can certify structure discovery** | i.i.d. testing cannot separate a recovered meta-graph from a correlation holding in the sample, so every "architecture X reasons" claim is unscoreable and the hardness-source scoring table has no valid instrument. Existing o.o.d. benchmarks are domain-specific, hand-built, and must be revised as models learn to game them (the Winograd Schema Challenge was found to contain the shortcuts it was designed to remove) | Controlled-shortcut datasets (Shift-MNIST / biased CelebA / unfair dSprites), where the intended rule is known by construction and the accuracy drop is the measurement; three design conditions for a valid o.o.d. test. Second instrument, from developmental method: **signature limits** — certify an installed prior by a quantitative constant (capacity, Weber ratio, entry threshold) that stays put across scale, retraining and domain shift, and by a **resource-stress probe** (shrink the budget; in biology the learned distinctions degrade first and the core ones last). Diagnostic evidence is in the failure profile, not the success rate ([[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]]). **Two further instruments, and the first that need no distribution shift at all** — hold the trained model fixed and vary the *query*: (i) the **richness protocol** — one representation must answer classify / generate / parse / create-new-concepts / explain, and a shortcut rule is by construction a mapping to one output type ([[wiki/concepts/causal-model-building.md]]); (ii) the **re-goaling protocol** — hold the transition structure fixed, swap the reward function, measure the retraining needed ([[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]]). Both are gameable by multi-task training on the query set, and neither certifies that the abilities come from *one* model. **Fifth instrument, and the first that is a quantity rather than a genre** (Chollet 2019): score `GD = H(Sol^θ_T | TrainSol^opt_{T,C}) / H(Sol^θ_T)` — how far the shortest curriculum-optimal program must be edited to work at evaluation — and run it *developer-aware*, conditioning also on the initial system, so anything the builder injected stops counting as difficulty. This reframes the certification target: the protocol must be run against the developer, not only the system, because skill on any task known in advance is purchasable outright with priors or with data. [[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]] is the instrument built to this spec (private evaluation set, ~3 examples per task, no data generator, enumerated priors). Still `OPEN`: `GD` is defined through Kolmogorov complexity, no approximation is offered, and ARC itself quantifies the generalization difficulty of exactly zero of its tasks (G31) | [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]], [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]], [[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G18 | **Discovered learning rules do not generalize outside the rule-search restrictions imposed by hand** | Meta-optimized plasticity is the wiki's only route to making slow **W** a *searched* object rather than a parameter blob, but when the rule search space is large and few restrictions are placed on the mechanism, generalization degrades. The freedom that makes rule discovery interesting is the same freedom that breaks it, so the expressiveness needed for structure acquisition is exactly what the method cannot afford | **Variable-shared meta-learning**: rules parameterized by parameter-shared recurrent networks that exchange information locally, which generalize to classification problems unseen during meta-optimization (and analogously for discovered RL algorithms). Restriction by *architecture* rather than by hand-written rule form is the only route shown to survive o.o.d. Which restrictions buy generalization and which merely shrink the space is uncharacterized | [[wiki/concepts/meta-optimized-plasticity.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G19 | **No local rule is selective about what it writes** | Every rule that can write during deployment ([[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]]) is a coactivity/covariance detector. It fires on shortcut-driven coactivity exactly as readily as on structural coactivity, so a fast **M** written by local plasticity accumulates spurious edges at the *instance* level — the same failure as G6, now inside the mechanism the wiki wants for online binding. Metaplasticity gates *how much* a synapse may change, never *whether the change is warranted*. Stated in the specification vocabulary: a rule is *learning* only if its update field has a positive projection onto some objective's gradient, and no local rule here names the objective it improves — so "unselective" may be the milder diagnosis and "orthogonal" the true one | Meta-optimizing the rule so the outer loop's task distribution filters what the rule writes on — i.e. importing the multi-environment identifiability argument into the update rule. Named here, not built | [[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]], [[wiki/concepts/meta-optimized-plasticity.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G20 | **No local rule reaches delayed credit *and* stays online** | The two available regimes are mutually exclusive: e-prop is forward-computable but requires a real-time error at each step and is blind to errors delayed beyond individual-neuron timescales; REINFORCE / node perturbation handle arbitrarily delayed sparse reward but are high-variance and give no per-step signal. Multi-hop traversal has precisely the delayed-sparse credit structure, evaluated only at the end of a path, so neither regime fits the wiki's target task | Eligibility traces plus a retroactive neuromodulatory signal — the mechanism both families share and neither exploits fully | [[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]], [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]], [[wiki/entities/spiking-neural-networks.md]] | `OPEN` |
| G21 | **No mechanism composes the outputs of two specialized modules** | Core knowledge shows a meta-graph can be *pre-installed* — six domain fragments with their own node types, edge vocabularies and constraints — but the fragments are encapsulated and compete for a limited attentional resource, so nothing joins them. A content such as "Mary broke the five bottles she liked" needs number, object, agent and social outputs in one representation. Modularity is the wiki's standing architectural stance ([[wiki/concepts/attention.md]]), and it has no binding operation. Worse, if language supplies the common format, then core concepts are a *different kind* from the concepts used as building blocks, so the priors cannot serve as compositional primitives without an unspecified translation layer (the equivocation problem) | Three rivals now. | G22 | **Nothing selects which compositions get built** | A composer must be answerable in both directions at once. Driven from inside it **overgenerates** — nothing explains why a handful of the astronomically many available compositions are ever built. Granting composition at all inherits the combinatorial explosion; it is postponed, not solved. This is gap G4 (vocabulary co-discovery) restated as a *selection* problem rather than an induction problem | **Selection by energy.** If a composition is an attractor, the compositions that are never built are the ones with no low-energy state — "a bowl lies in a ball" fails because a ball affords no interior. | G24 | **No principled planning horizon exists, even in the ideal agent** | Path search needs a depth. | G25 | **Optimality cannot be certified for any agent whose actions shape its data** | For passive environments (prediction, classification) the error excess is bounded by a function of `K(µ)` alone. ## How gaps are used

A gap is worth recording only if it is *actionable*: it names a mechanism the wiki would adopt if it existed. Gaps that reduce to "the problem is hard" are folded into the open-problems section of the relevant concept page instead.

**Dependency structure.** These are not independent. G16 sits *under* G1: it says the factorization G1 wants is not the kind of thing data can supply, so G1 is a design decision to be paid for rather than a result to be waited for. G17 is the instrument problem for the whole table — without an out-of-distribution test, no gap can be shown closed, because an architecture that assumed the latent variable away is indistinguishable from one that recovered it. G1 is the root — G2 and G3 are the conditions under which its factorization is meaningful, and G9 is what happens when it is applied to itself. G7→G8 and G11→G12 are each a problem followed by the mechanism that would dissolve it. G13 is the accounting frame for all of them: an architecture is scored by which hardness sources it reaches, not by whether it "works". G14 and G15 are the two halves of the write/read loop around the factorization: G14 is how the meta-graph gets updated from experience, G15 is how the graph gets used once it exists. G19 and G20 are the corresponding pair one level down, on the *write rule for fast M*: G19 is what gets written, G20 is when the signal that authorizes the write arrives. G18 is the tax on G9's new candidate answer — self-reference buys the third tier, and generalization is the price. G21–G23 open a column the table did not have: what is in the graph *before* learning. G23 is the condition under which installed priors can be stacked at all; G21 is what they cannot do once installed; G22 is the reason G21's candidate answers do not obviously work. G21 also inverts G4 — where G4 asks how a vocabulary is induced, G21 assumes several are given and asks how they are joined.

**What would close one.** A gap closes when an ingest supplies an architecture that (a) instantiates the named mechanism and (b) is tested on a task where the corresponding latent variable is actually hidden. An architecture that assumes away the latent variable does not count as evidence. Per G17, condition (b) means an **out-of-distribution** test with a well-defined intended solution — an i.i.d. result never closes a gap here.

> **Provenance (second ingest).** Rows G16–G17 come from Geirhos et al. 2020; G6 was revised by the same source from "correlational edges can survive" to "shortcut solutions are the default outcome across every subfield studied".

> **Provenance (third ingest).** Rows G18–G20 come from Schmidgall et al. 2023, which also moved G9 from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` by naming self-referential meta-learning as a candidate third tier. G19 is G6 restated one level down: the same shortcut problem, now in the *write rule for fast M* rather than in the slow learner's weights — which means fixing G6 at the slow level does not fix it at the fast one.

> **Provenance (fifth ingest).** Rows G24–G26 come from Hutter 2000, which also moved G13 from `OPEN` to `PARTIAL` by supplying AIXItl. The source changes the table's status in one respect: G25 is the wiki's first gap with a *proof* that its answer does not exist, rather than a report that none has been found. Its dependents are G17 (an o.o.d. certificate cannot be fully general because active-case optimality is uncertifiable) and G13 (the "which hardness sources to drop" question has no principled answer partly because there is no yardstick to score the drop against). G26 sits under G16 as G16 sits under G1: the bias must be paid for (G16), and the strongest bias available does not buy the thing G1 wants (G26).

> **Provenance (fourth ingest).** Rows G21–G23 come from Revencu & Csibra 2023. They shift the table's centre of gravity: G1–G20 all concern what a learner must *acquire*, while these three concern what it must be *given*, and what being given several disconnected priors then costs. G2 also gains a second answer from this source — an entity typed by a different core system is individuated without any path context, so module membership is a free de-aliasing tag.

> **Provenance (sixth ingest).** Spelke & Kinzler 2007, the primary source behind G21–G23, adds no new row but changes three. G17 gains its second candidate instrument (signature limits + the resource-stress probe), which is the first proposal in the table for certifying a *prior* rather than a learned structure. G23 gains a reason rather than just a statement: an ungated geometry prior would be actively wrong on movable objects, so entry tests are correctness machinery, not efficiency machinery. And the source adds a constraint that cuts across G21–G23 — installed priors must be **defeasible** ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T11), since the core principles are false outside the scale of action and are in fact overridden, always via an external symbol system.

> **Provenance (seventh ingest).** Butz 2016 adds G27 and moves three rows off `OPEN`: G5 to `PARTIAL` (a processing loop that is genuinely joint at the instance level, still phase-separated at the meta level) and G15 to `PARTIAL` (homeostatic free energy answers *when to plan*, leaving *how deep* to G24). It is the first source to give G16 a concrete architecture-lever proposal rather than a restatement, and the first to give G21/G22 a composition mechanism that needs no shared symbolic format — composition as relaxation to a joint free-energy minimum, with selection falling out as "no minimum, no composition". G27 is a column the table did not have: G1–G26 all concern the graph's *contents*, G27 concerns whether there are discrete nodes at all. Note the dependency it creates: G27 sits under G2 and G3, since identity and path-consistency are only well-posed once the node set exists.

> **Provenance (eighth ingest).** Lake et al. 2017 adds G28–G29 and changes two rows. G17 gains its first *shift-free* instruments — vary the query, not the data — which matters because every earlier candidate required constructing a distribution shift with a known intended solution, and that construction is the expensive part. G15 gains a second answer to *when to plan* that is priced in the currency of control (flexibility vs. speed, arbitrated metacognitively) rather than of prediction error. G28 is the sharpest single test in the table: it holds the transition structure fixed by construction, so it isolates the *use* half of the framing from the *discovery* half, and it is the reason a model-free system and a model-based one are distinguishable at all after both have converged. G29 sits alongside G16 and G26 as the third independent argument that the target is not reachable by optimising a fixed model on more data — G16 says the data cannot select it, G26 says the strongest bias does not expose it, G29 says the search operator cannot reach it.

> **Provenance (ninth ingest).** Richards et al. 2019 adds G30 and re-labels the table rather than extending it: every existing row can now be sorted into the slot it belongs to (objective / learning rule / architecture), and the sort is what exposes G30 — the wiki's rows cluster in the architecture slot, the objective slot is nearly empty, and it is the only one of the three that is *not directly observable* in brains, so the transfer channel could not have filled it. G16 gains a rival count of the control surface (three components, data demoted to an anchor — T15) and G19 gains a sharper diagnosis: an unselective local rule may not merely be writing the wrong edges but writing *orthogonally to every objective*, which by this source's criterion is change rather than learning.

> **Provenance (tenth ingest).** Chollet 2019 adds G31–G32 and rewrites G17's answer column. The shift it forces is that **evaluation is a design problem with a target quantity**, not a genre of test: every earlier instrument in G17 described what a test should *look like*, and this one says what it should *measure* — and adds the requirement none of them had, that the tasks be unknown to the **developer**, since priors and data are two interchangeable ways of buying skill without generalization. G16 is sharpened in the same move: the four levers are the channels through which a developer pays for the intended rule, and the intelligence formula charges for exactly that payment, so the wiki's control surface and its scoring function are two views of one quantity. G26 gains an antagonist rather than an answer — [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T16 — since the shortest curriculum-consistent program is now argued to be the *wrong* object, not merely an insufficiently structured one.
