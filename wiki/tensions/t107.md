# T107 — Which structure holds the graph that a plan runs on — the hippocampal formation, or the cortical controller?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The prefrontal-graph model is given a non-stub hippocampal component — remapping and forward sweeps included — so its sufficiency claim becomes evidence about locus rather than about a cortical graph alone.

## Position A

**Hippocampal-entorhinal.** The wiki's whole cognitive-map account puts the relational structure in the medial temporal lobe: place and grid codes are the map ([[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]]), the Tolman–Eichenbaum Machine factorises structure from sensory content in entorhinal-like cells ([[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]]), the successor representation is a hippocampal predictive map, and chained retrieval during two-stage inference is abolished by silencing dorsal CA1 (Barron et al. 2020, [[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]]). Prefrontal cortex reads the map and valuates

## Position B

**Prefrontal.** The hippocampal space code is *too redundant and too distributed* to be a compact topological map; it supplies instances (places, routes, episodes) and the cortical network learns the graph over them. Measured along the cascade, population size falls and receptive fields grow from place cells → prefrontal columns, and every unit type the graph-planner needs (state, distance-to-goal, prospective-sequence, strategy-switch) is present in navigating-rat medial prefrontal recordings and recoverable by blind clustering; the hippocampus is modelled as pure localisation input (Martinet et al. 2011, [[wiki/entities/pfc-columnar-planning-model.md]])

## Where it bites

Where a builder puts the adjacency matrix, and what the fast episodic store is *for*. Under A the fast store holds the graph and the controller queries it, so consolidation moves a graph. Under B the fast store holds instances and the controller *abstracts* a graph from them, so consolidation is a change of representational format and the two structures should carry measurably different graphs — the coarse one only in cortex. The two are not fully exclusive (both may hold a graph at different resolutions), and that reconciliation is exactly what the coarse/fine column split predicts: multi-scale codes exist in entorhinal cortex too (Hafting et al. 2005; Kjelstrup et al. 2008), so the open question is whether cortical chunking is redundant with hippocampal scale, or orthogonal to it (chunking by *action structure* rather than by distance)

## Status

`LIVE` — the prefrontal-graph model's hippocampal component is a deliberate stub (no remapping, no forward sweeps), so it cannot be evidence *against* a hippocampal graph; it establishes only that a cortical graph is sufficient for detour behaviour and consistent with prefrontal single-unit data
