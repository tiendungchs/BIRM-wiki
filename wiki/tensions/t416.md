# T416 — Does analogical mapping need explicit higher-order relations (propositions that take propositions as arguments), or do pairwise relations among co-occurring concepts suffice?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Items are built where a higher-order relation is present or absent **with the keyword set, the first-order pairwise relations and the text coherence held equal**. A pairwise-edge mapper ([[wiki/entities/pam.md]]) and a higher-order-structure mapper ([[wiki/entities/sme.md]] or [[wiki/entities/lisa.md]]) are scored against human mapping on those items. A predicts a human systematicity benefit that only the higher-order mapper reproduces. B predicts no benefit once coherence is equated, or one the pairwise mapper also reproduces. A second form: a non-causal higher-order relation (`implies`, `prevents-before`) is shown to raise mapping systematicity as much as `cause` does.

## Position A

**Higher-order structure is what systematicity is.** Structure-mapping defines the preferred interpretation as the one preserving the largest interconnected system of higher-order relations (Gentner 1983; Markman & Gentner 2000, [[wiki/concepts/analogical-mapping.md]]). SME's ranking runs on it: the solar-system → atom interpretation wins at SES 6.03 vs 4.04 because the mass inequality sits under a mappable `CAUSE`. The temperature reading loses because temperature has no higher-order structure in the target. The engine's runtime also falls when content is re-described with more higher-order structure (53 min → 34 s) ([[wiki/entities/sme.md]]). Gentner & Toupin 1986 attribute the benefit of a systematic source story to its richer higher-order `cause` structure. LISA nests propositions as role fillers, and its causal / higher-order links drive which inferences are made in the Lassaline simulations ([[wiki/entities/lisa.md]]). The wiki's own `L0` derivation states that second-order schemas (relations between relations) exist and must be held apart while formed (Width, [[wiki/concepts/abstraction-graph.md]]).

## Position B

**Flat pairwise relations with rich content suffice for mapping; "systematicity" is coherence.** Lu, Ichien & Holyoak 2022 ([[wiki/entities/pam.md]]):
- PAM maps semantic relation networks (concept nodes, learned binary relation-vector edges) and has no higher-order relations at all.
- It reproduces Gentner & Toupin's systematicity × compatibility interaction, and the larger systematicity benefit at age 8–10. The systematic and non-systematic inputs differ only by the keyword `jealous`, which two NLP keyword extractors select from the systematic text.
- It maps 85% of keywords on Turney's 20 cross-domain science analogies and metaphors from keywords alone (humans 88%), and 7/7 core correspondences on the non-isomorphic General → radiation texts.
- The authors' arguments: (i) nearly all evidence for higher-order relations in mapping involves `cause` alone; (ii) direct causation is typically expressed in a single clause, not as a relation between propositions; (iii) syntactically equivalent relations (`temporally prior to`) do not support inference as `cause` does (Lassaline 1996). So `cause` is special pragmatically and through causal-network inference, not through its order. The proposed mechanism is text coherence (Kintsch 1988): any cue that sets up unambiguous relations among several interconnected concept pairs helps mapping.

## Where it bites

Decides whether a mapper's input needs a component that builds propositions over propositions, or only an edge relation between concept pairs. Under A, a machine analogy system needs a parser into nested predicate structure, which no wiki mechanism supplies from raw input (`G4`, `G27`); every working mapper in the wiki is hand-fed such structure. Under B, the input is a pairwise relation readout over concept codes, which is computable today (BART, inner-product relational layers, [[wiki/concepts/relational-bottleneck.md]]). The higher-order machinery is then moved downstream to inference and causal-model transfer (`T192`), not mapping. B also rewrites what "systematicity" means for a builder: a coherence statistic over a pairwise graph, not an order count.

## Status

`LIVE`. B's evidence is one model on the stimulus sets of one laboratory tradition. Its systematicity case rests on a single keyword-selection step, and it concedes propositions for inference and schema induction, so it is a claim about **mapping only**. A's evidence is mostly theoretical plus results from models fed hand-coded higher-order structure. A has never been tested against a pairwise mapper with coherence equated. Neither side has run the discriminating design. **A replication on A's standard materials, not discriminating** (Webb, Holyoak & Lu 2023, [[wiki/entities/digit-matrices.md]]). With Gentner, Rattermann & Forbus 1993's stories in forced choice, 54 undergraduates pick the target sharing the source's **causal** relations in both near (`t(53) = 21.5`) and far (`t(53) = 16.7`) pairs. GPT-3, which has no explicit higher-order relations, does so for near pairs (`p = 0.0039`) and not for far ones (`p = 0.065`). GPT-4 is reported to do both. The higher-order relation is `cause` alone, and the targets also differ in event order and wording, so B's coherence account is not excluded. Status unchanged.
