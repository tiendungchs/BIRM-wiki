# T270 — Does a measure of integration detect a world model?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A measure of aboutness is supplied — B asks for one and leaves it open, so the row currently has no instrument on either side.

## Position A

**Yes — integration is the signature.** The wiki's certification instruments for "this system holds a coherent global representation" are all dynamical: Φ and its surrogates, the perturbational complexity index, the size of the functional-connectivity repertoire against the anatomical matrix, metastable dwell statistics. Anaesthesia collapses all of them together, across three molecularly disjoint agents and four species, and disorders of consciousness order along them (Sitt et al. 2014, `n = 181`; Casali et al. 2013) — so the measures track the presence of the thing ([[wiki/entities/global-neuronal-workspace.md]], [[wiki/concepts/dynamic-repertoire.md]], [[wiki/concepts/metastability.md]])

## Position B

**No — they are blind to aboutness.** A 2-D expander graph has high Φ, small-world connectivity, sparse coding and error-correcting capacity, and hierarchically stacked it could implement predictive processing; it represents nothing. Integration measures are measures of a network's own dynamics, and no dynamical measure distinguishes a model from a resonator. The proposed added condition is representational and cannot be read off the dynamics: coherence with respect to space, time and cause, for a system that also partitions itself from the world by a controllability asymmetry (Safron 2022, [[wiki/entities/integrated-world-modeling-theory.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether the wiki may keep inferring representational content from network statistics. Every claim of the form *this architecture integrates information, therefore it represents the environment* rests on A; if B holds, integration measures are necessary-condition instruments only, and the wiki has **no** instrument for the sufficient condition — which is also what `G92` says from the architecture side. It bears directly on the certification programme ([[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]]): a system could be built to maximise Φ or minimise dynamical predictability and score well while modelling nothing

## Status

`LIVE` — the two sides are compatible if A's measures are read as necessary and not sufficient, which is how B's own author reads them; the dispute is then about whether anything measures the remainder. B supplies no metric — *"to what degrees must these forms of coherence be present in which ways?"* is asked and left open — so B is currently an argument against A's sufficiency rather than a rival instrument, and A retains the practical advantage that its measures are computable on real recordings. Note that A's strongest evidence (anaesthesia, disorders of consciousness) is collected exclusively on systems that *already* have grounded world models, so it cannot discriminate the two positions by construction
