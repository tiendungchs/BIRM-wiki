# T376 — Is apical–basal coupling a global state variable, or a per-column gate that can be aimed?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Coupling is measured at two cortical sites simultaneously while attention is directed to one of them and the arousal state is held fixed — so that a coupling difference *between* areas within one state is either observed or excluded. A positive is selective attention implemented as compartment coupling; a null makes coupling a mode switch and forces selection onto a different mechanism.

## Position A

**A global state variable.** Optogenetic apical stimulation reaches the soma awake and does not under anaesthesia, and the effect replicates across anaesthetics with disjoint molecular targets *and* across frontal, somatosensory and primary visual cortex — one variable, thrown everywhere at once (Suzuki & Larkum 2020; [[wiki/entities/dendritic-integration-theory.md]]). The informative half is a null: apical–basal correlation is unaffected by locomotion or by visual stimulation, so manipulations *within* a state do not move it while the state transition does (Beaulieu-Laroche et al. 2019; Francioni et al. 2019). The named controller — higher-order thalamus acting through metabotropic receptors — is the topology of a broadcast, not of an address

## Position B

**A gate that can be aimed.** Causal modulation of apical compartments in one sensory area, pharmacological or optogenetic, changes detection of one stimulus while the animal stays awake throughout (Takahashi et al. 2016, 2020), and the effect is carried by the specific L5p → higher-order thalamus branch rather than by a global state change. The same cells carry a fast, compartment-specific veto on the same switch — GABA_A on tens of milliseconds and GABA_B on ~0.5 s, with Ca²⁺-channel-suppressing machinery localised to the apical dendrite (Larkum 2013, [[wiki/concepts/apical-amplification.md]], [[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]) — so a per-column control channel demonstrably exists whatever the thalamic one is doing. Aru et al. raise the local reading themselves: if coupling is locally controllable, "the control of coupling could turn out to be a key mechanism of selective attention"

## Where it bites

Whether the import is **one scalar or `N`**. Under A a model gets a mode switch: a single `c` that admits or blocks every module's top-down port, useful as an ablation instrument and as a sleep/wake analogue, and selection has to be bought elsewhere. Under B it gets an addressable routing table over module pairs — the middle option `G93` names — and attention, contextual modulation and conscious access collapse into one mechanism at different spatial scales, which would make [[wiki/concepts/attention.md]] and [[wiki/entities/dendritic-integration-theory.md]] the same page. It also decides what the anaesthesia evidence can support: a globally thrown switch cannot show that the switch is *only* global.

## Status

`LIVE`, and the two sides are not measuring the same variable. A measures **coupling** (does apical input reach the soma?) under a manipulation that is global by construction — anaesthesia changes everything, so uniformity of the result is partly a property of the intervention. B measures **apical activity and its behavioural consequence**, not coupling, so it shows the distal stream is causal for content without showing that its *admission* is what was aimed. Neither experiment can produce the other's negative.

The reconciliation is cheap and untested: **two controllers on one switch** — a slow permissive gain from higher-order thalamus that sets the state, and a fast dendrite-targeting inhibitory veto that does per-column selection within it. That is consistent with every result above, predicts that coupling differences *within* a waking state are inhibitory-interneuron-dependent rather than thalamus-dependent, and is exactly the joint measurement nobody has run. Interacts with `T374` (what the coincidence computes, given that it is allowed to happen) and with `T276` (the same thalamic axons measured at layer 2/3, where the cargo was the sender's arousal state — a state variable, which is evidence for A from a different compartment).
