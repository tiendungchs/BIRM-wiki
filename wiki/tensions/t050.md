# T50 — Is an abstract hippocampal code low-dimensional, or is low dimensionality a red herring?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Dimensionality and expressiveness are measured together in a population where A predicts they trade off — the case A has never measured alongside a shattering-dimensionality statistic.

## Position A

**Low-dimensional.** Abstraction is compression: irrelevant detail is discarded and the conditions collapse onto a small number of orthogonal coding directions, so a disentangled geometry is by construction low-dimensional — the reading the wiki has been carrying since Nieh et al. 2021 (`d ≈ 5` in a ~450-dimensional space), and the reason hippocampal maps are contrasted with decorrelated high-dimensional codes built for memory capacity

## Position B

**Neither — the two axes are independent, and they rose together.** In human hippocampus the emergence of inference brought higher CCGP and parallelism for context *and stimulus* **and** higher shattering dimensionality (0.57 → 0.62), with the parity dichotomy — a pure nonlinear interaction no linear code supplies — becoming decodable, and the *hardest* dichotomies decoded best (Courellis et al. 2024). **And the earlier, stronger version is a rejected null rather than a co-occurrence:** in monkey hippocampus, DLPFC and ACC, shattering dimensionality is > 0.7 before stimulus onset and > 0.88 after, in every area *significantly greater than* that of a perfectly factorised representation — eight condition means at the vertices of a randomly rotated cuboid whose side lengths were fitted to reproduce the observed CCGP for context, value and action. So the disentangled model that accounts for the abstraction is statistically excluded by the expressiveness (Bernardi et al. 2020)

## Where it bites

Whether "disentangle the representation" implies "reduce its dimensionality" in a model. If it does not, then a regulariser that pushes intrinsic dimension down is not an abstraction objective, and the wiki's pattern-separation/decorrelation machinery (high-dimensional, for capacity) and its structural-code machinery (abstract, for transfer) stop being rivals for the same population ([[wiki/concepts/pattern-separation-completion.md]], [[wiki/concepts/abstract-structural-codes.md]], T29)

## Status

`LEANING B` — B is a direct measurement of both quantities in one population against a behavioural contrast, now in two species, and in the monkey case with the factorised model explicitly rejected as a null; A is an inference from geometry, never measured alongside an expressiveness statistic
