# T115 — Does the preparatory attentional template hold the target?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Yes — that is what makes it a template.** Ventral prearcuate cortex holds persistent *object-selective* firing for the cued object through the delay and across saccades, and it is the similarity of each location to *that* code that the priority map computes (Bichot et al. 2015, [[wiki/concepts/priority-map.md]]); every attention and retrieval layer in the wiki likewise sets the query to an encoding of the goal

## Position B

**Not necessarily — and here, not at all.** Over an 8 s delay after a face cue, human cross-task MVPA decodes the *learned associate* (a scene category never shown) in the inferior frontal junction, parietal eye fields and parahippocampal place area, while the cued face is decodable in no template region (only left intraparietal sulcus, `P` = 0.019 uncorrected) and no region carries both; the swap is behaviourally motivated — matched distractor faces are hard to discriminate, the four scenes are not, and the associate stops being used when the target is easy (Zhou & Geng 2025, 2024)

## Where it bites

Whether "what am I looking for" is a function of the goal or a decision with its own utility. Under A a query register is written by the goal encoder and is inspectable as such; under B an architecture needs a proxy-selection sub-policy (predictive validity × detectability), a monitor for proxy failure, and a reload path — and interpretability that reads the query register expecting the goal will find something else

## Status

`LIVE, plausibly reconcilable` — the two are not strictly opposed if the register holds *whatever currently maximises expected guidance value*, which reduces to the target when the target is the most discriminable cue (the monkey studies' condition). What is untested is the crossover: nobody has varied target discriminability and associate validity independently. Two deflations for B: fMRI cannot show the delay-period associate causes the guidance, and the task cannot separate template installation from plain cued associative recall
