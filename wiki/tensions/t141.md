# T141 — Is the brain's discount kernel exponential, or hyperbolic?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The machine construction is run: train an agent with exponential `γ` and refit both kernels to its value units — a hyperbolic neurometric function from an exponential objective would dissolve the row.

## Position A

**Exponential — a settable `γ`.** Every value function in the wiki is `V = E[Σ_k γ^k r]`, the discount is a scalar metaparameter with a proposed chemical carrier (serotonin) and a proposed anatomical substrate (the direct/indirect balance), and the whole closed-loop control story depends on `γ` being one number that multiplies a recursive term (Doya 2002, [[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]])

## Position B

**Hyperbolic.** `SV = A/(1+kD)` fits every subject better than a single exponential (median `R² = 0.95`), consistent with a large animal and human literature, and the *neural* discount function refit to value-region time courses matches the hyperbolic behavioural one with zero mean offset (Kable & Glimcher 2007, [[wiki/concepts/subjective-value.md]])

## Where it bites

Much less than it did. A hyperbolic kernel is **time-inconsistent**: `SV(D)` is not the composition of one-step discounts, preferences between a fixed pair reverse as both approach, and no *single* stationary value function exists for dynamic programming to converge to. That was read as making B incompatible with the wiki's rollout scoring, its TD error ([[wiki/concepts/reward-prediction-error.md]]) and its `γ`-as-metaparameter substrate. **It is not.** `1/(1+kt) = ∫₀¹ w(γ)γᵗ dγ` with `w(γ) = (1/k)γ^{1/k−1}`, and `Q^d_π = ∫₀¹ w(γ)Q^γ_π dγ` for any `d` in that family, so a bank of ordinary Q-learners recombines into the hyperbolic value with every convergence guarantee intact (Fedus et al. 2019, [[wiki/concepts/multi-horizon-value-learning.md]]). What still bites is narrower and now purely architectural: under B a builder needs *several* value heads plus a read-out weighting `w(γ)` rather than one head and one scalar `γ`, and the weighting is an assertion about the environment's hazard distribution that something must set. Under A the behavioural hyperbola must still be an artefact of aggregation, uncertainty about receipt, or a nonlinear utility over amount

## Status

`LIVE` as an empirical question, **defused as an architectural one**: the hazard-prior derivation shows exponential and hyperbolic discounting are not rival algorithms but the same algorithm under different priors over the per-step death rate `λ` — Dirac (A) versus exponential (B) — so A is the special case that asserts zero uncertainty about environmental risk. The remaining disagreement is about which prior the brain holds, not about what machinery it could run. The two literatures also barely touch: A is fitted to trial-by-trial learning in tasks lasting seconds, B to stated preferences over delays of hours to months. The cheap discriminating construction is a machine one — train an agent with exponential `γ` and refit both kernels to its *value units*; if a network optimising `γ^k` returns a hyperbolic neurometric function, B's finding stops being evidence about the algorithm at all
