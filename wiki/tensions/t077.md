# T77 — Is a population's existing latent basis a hard limit on what it can learn, or only a slow one?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Outside-manifold perturbations are trained over multiple days, testing whether the intrinsic manifold expands or reorients on a skill-learning timescale.

## Position A

**A hard limit on any timescale a learner cares about.** Rhesus monkeys learn BCI decoder perturbations that stay inside the fitted 10-D intrinsic manifold within a single session, and fail on perturbations that require new co-modulation patterns — with initial impairment, principal angles, required preferred-direction change, search-space size and hand movement all matched between the two types. The failure produces no washout aftereffect (so no partial learning to detect) and the gap is as large in the final sessions as in the first, across ~2 years of alternating exposure: the animals never learned *how* to learn off-manifold (Sadtler et al. 2014)

## Position B

**Only slow — the manifold is itself learnable, just not in hours.** The same authors' posit: within-manifold learning harnesses fast adaptation, outside-manifold learning engages skill-learning machinery and should benefit from multi-day exposure, with the intrinsic manifold expanding or reorienting. The prior BCI literature they cite reports learning that is "remarkably extensive", and nothing in the study exposed an animal to one outside-manifold mapping for more than a session — alternating types daily is precisely the schedule that would prevent manifold change from accumulating

## Where it bites

Whether a model must be *endowed* with a latent basis spanning the target domain or can grow one. Under A, architecture selection is the dominant term and pretraining chooses the reachable set; under B, basis growth is a second, slower learning process that no wiki architecture implements and that would need its own objective ([[wiki/concepts/manifold-constrained-learning.md]], [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] G47)
