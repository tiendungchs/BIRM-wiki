# T99 — What indexes which module a memory needs — its age, or its overlap with what is already stored?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Integration demand is measured within a trial rather than defined by experimental design, giving B an instrument rather than a reframing.

## Position A

**Age.** The recent/remote framing organizes the whole consolidation literature: the hippocampus holds the mapping at 1–2 days and medial prefrontal cortex holds it at ~30 days (shown to 200), with selective remote-memory lesion deficits and greater remote dendritic spine growth, and a bounded 0–2 h post-task window in which cortical disruption destroys the later memory (Euston et al. 2012, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]], T97)

## Position B

**Integration demand.** Prefrontal involvement tracks how much the new event conflicts with existing associations, not how old it is: in associative-inference, transitive-inference and food-location-schema tasks mPFC damage impairs *acquisition* and blocks inference from the outset, while in single-shock contextual fear — where the animal has no prior model of the box — it is dispensable early and needed only later. Hippocampal duration is set by a second variable, how much episodic detail the read-out requires (flat retrograde gradient for autobiographical memory, consolidation-limited for semanticized memory). Under this reading the recent/remote pattern is what low-integration-demand paradigms produce (Preston & Eichenbaum 2013, [[wiki/concepts/schema-assimilation.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether a machine consolidation schedule is a clock or a signal. Under A, replay after `t` steps with a decay — the current default everywhere in the wiki. Under B the schedule is a function of two online quantities: disagreement between the fast store's retrieval for the current cue and the new observation, and the detail the task's read-out demands. B also explains away A's own worst inconsistency (the same delays giving opposite results across paradigms) as an uncontrolled third variable, which is a stronger position than a tie

## Status

`LEANING` (B: it accounts for A's evidence as a special case, and A has no account of the schema-task results. But the trigger is defined by experimental design and never measured within a trial, so B is currently a reframing without an instrument)
