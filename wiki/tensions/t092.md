# T92 — Is working memory the specialized function of prefrontal cortex?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The claim is restated at a resolution finer than "prefrontal cortex": dorsolateral-spatial and ventral-nonspatial evidence are reconciled or shown to require separate accounts. Compounds with T88.

## Position A

**Yes — maintenance is what the region is for.** The wiki's control layer is built on it: delay-period firing that outlasts the cue is the canonical prefrontal signal, gated prefrontal maintenance is the biological anchor for LSTM-style gating, and [[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]] identifies the store with prefrontal stripes outright ([[wiki/concepts/working-memory.md]])

## Position B

**No — at least not for ventral prefrontal cortex, and not as the *sole* function.** Monkeys with ventral prefrontal removals retain nonspatial visual stimuli for up to 8 s in delayed matching-to-sample (Rushworth et al. 1997a), yet the same region's ablation abolishes arbitrary visuomotor mapping in a task where the instruction cue stays on screen through response selection and execution — near-zero storage demand, near-total deficit. The deficit is also not perseveration: post-lesion the animal repeats a previous incorrect response no more often than it emits any other alternative (Murray et al. 2000)

## Where it bites

Whether a machine control layer should model prefrontal cortex as *a store with a gate* or as *a binder that computes cue × action × outcome conjunctions and happens to hold them* — the second predicts that removing the store's maintenance capacity leaves the binding function intact, which is the opposite of PBWM's ablation profile

## Status

`LIVE` — partly a scope dispute: the maintenance evidence is overwhelmingly dorsolateral and spatial, the falsifying evidence ventral and nonspatial, so "prefrontal cortex" may simply be too coarse a unit for either claim. It compounds with T88, which already says most of the dorsolateral delay signal is an attentional pointer rather than an item
