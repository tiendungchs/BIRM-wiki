# T388 — When a visual capability is missing, is the information absent from the encoder or unreadable by what follows?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The linear probe is run on the *counting and tracing* tasks (BlindTest 4–7), not just the binary and 3-way ones. A predicts the probe fails there too; B predicts it succeeds while the full model stays floored. The features are already extracted and the labels are exact — this is one afternoon of logistic regression.

## Position A

**Absent. The front end does not compute it, and the repair is a better parser.** This is the wiki's standing reading of every perceptual null and the prescription `T215` position B implies. Its direct evidence is the spacing dial: BlindTest accuracy moves from floor to ceiling purely by separating the primitives — ≥96% at `dx = 0.75` on shape counting, 95% at 3 inserted spaces on the circled letter, 0.95–0.99 on path counting at straight-run probability `P = 0.9` ([[wiki/entities/blindtest.md]]). Nothing about the reader changed; the *image* changed. A representation whose usability depends that strongly on inter-primitive clearance is one that has discarded the clearance. The same prescription from the architecture side: [[wiki/concepts/incremental-grouping.md]] fixes exactly this failure by putting a scale hierarchy and a recurrent tracing pathway *in the front end*, and [[wiki/entities/spelkenet.md]] and every fixed parse rule in the wiki assume the parse is something the front end hands downstream

## Position B

**Present and unreadable. The repair is an interface, not a sensor.** A linear probe on *frozen* features, average-pooled over patches, solves BlindTest tasks 1 and 2 at **99.47–100%** both before and after the projection layer, while the full VLMs score **33.14 / 37.78** and **73.21 / 83.63** on the same two tasks. Two encoders, CLIP and SigLIP, and the smallest models in their families were chosen deliberately, so larger encoders retain at least as much. Corroboration with the encoder held exactly fixed: LLaVA-OneVision 0.5B and 72B share an identical 400M SigLIP encoder and differ only in the Qwen2 decoder, and the large decoder substantially outperforms the small one — the decoder is acting as an extended vision encoder (Rahmanzadehgervi et al. 2024). Slow-thinking models add nothing (71.59 vs 72.75; 42.48 vs 54.13) because the extra deliberation runs in token space, i.e. on the far side of the interface

## Where it bites

**What gets built, and where the wiki's perception budget goes.** Under A the work is a better visual front end — a scale hierarchy, a recurrent grouping pathway, a trainable segmenter — and every existing frozen encoder is a dead end. Under B the encoders are already adequate and the missing object is a *routine that queries them at a location*: the narrow read-out channel [[wiki/concepts/visual-routines.md]] derives from the conjunction-search result, arrived at from the opposite direction, and the reason `G75`'s query-relativity requirement cannot be met by any amount of encoder improvement. The two prescriptions are not complementary — they spend the same budget on opposite components, and the field is currently spending it on A.

It also decides how to read [[wiki/entities/blindtest.md]]'s headline and the paper's own title: "VLMs are blind" is a position-A claim that the paper's own probe experiment contradicts.

## Status

`LIVE`, and the row is separable by a measurement that already has its inputs sitting on disk. **The asymmetry that keeps it live:** B's evidence covers two tasks (a binary and a 3-way decision) whose answers are plausibly *linear* in a pooled feature vector; A's evidence covers all seven but is indirect, since a spacing effect is equally predicted by a readout that can only decode well-separated features. Neither side has the decisive datum, which is probe accuracy on the tasks whose answers are counts and connectivity predicates — the ones [[wiki/concepts/visual-routines.md]] argues require an iterated, data-dependent routine that no pooled feature vector and no linear head can hold, in which case **both positions are wrong in the same way**: the information is neither encoded nor merely unread, because it is not a property of any single representation but of a computation nobody runs.

Note that a probe measures *presence*, not *accessibility*, and accessibility is the architectural quantity. A classifier trained on 8,880 in-distribution images with exact supervision is not the same reader as a language model that must address the same information without ever having been given a signal for this decision — so even a clean position-B sweep leaves the interface unbuilt rather than merely unused.
