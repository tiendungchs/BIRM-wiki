# T132 — Is the indirect pathway a veto or an exploration channel?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** One model implements both readings and is fitted to probabilistic-learning behaviour *and* Parkinsonian kinematics — no model in the 19-model census currently does.

## Position A

**A veto.** It carries `NoGo` — action-specific suppression of the alternatives, which must be individually addressable for probabilistic learning to work, and whose removal degrades performance (Frank 2005); the same reading drives the reaching and handwriting models (Bischoff 1998; Contreras-Vidal & Stelmach 1995)

## Position B

**An explorer.** Chakravarthy's group inserts a third regime between `Go` and `NoGo` whose substrate is the indirect pathway: when the value gradient is absent or too weak to learn from, it sets the *stochasticity* of action selection. Dopamine-depleted simulations spend less time in `Go` and more in the other two, reproducing movement undershoot, bradykinesia and increased path variability in reaching, and increased search times in saccadic visual search (Magdoom et al. 2011; Krishnan et al. 2011)

## Where it bites

Decides whether exploration is a hyperparameter or an anatomical channel with its own neuromodulatory control. Every wiki architecture bolts exploration on externally ([[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]]'s `Random Go` at fixed `p`); B makes annealing fall out of the same dopamine signal that sets the selection balance. The readings give opposite lesion predictions — A says a damaged veto makes an agent impulsive, B says it makes the agent deterministic and stuck

## Status

`LIVE` — the two are not formally exclusive (a stochastic veto is a veto), but they are fitted to disjoint data: A to probabilistic-learning and reversal behaviour, B to Parkinsonian motor kinematics, and no model in the 19-model census implements both. **A third position denies the premise:** in the optimal-decision mapping the indirect pathway plays no part in selection at all — the computation needs one inhibitory copy of the salience (direct pathway), one exponentiator (subthalamic nucleus) and one log-compressor (external pallidum), and the D₂ → pallidum projection is excluded from the core model and shown only *not to break it*. If that is right the pathway is free for the learning phase, and both A and B are looking for a selection-time job that the circuit does not need done (Bogacz & Gurney 2007, [[wiki/concepts/evidence-accumulation.md]])
