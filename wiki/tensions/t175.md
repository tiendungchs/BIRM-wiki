# T175 — Once every candidate is a universal approximator, is there any expressivity difference left to measure?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**No — not by any measure that survives the class.** Li, Fong & Tiňo 2024 make three visibly different constrained architectures universal *simultaneously* over time-invariant fading-memory filters, each with one tunable recurrent parameter, and the constructions are explicit ([[wiki/entities/simple-cycle-reservoir.md]], Thms 15/20/21/22). What is left to argue about is sample efficiency, conditioning, the state dimension needed at a given `ε`, and whether the target is inside the class at all — none of which is expressivity.

## Position B

**Yes, and the field already knows universality is the wrong instrument.** Hasani et al. 2021 prove LTC universality (Thm 3) and then write that the theorem "does not yield a concrete measure on where the separation is between different neural network architectures", replacing it with **trajectory length** — the arc length of the PCA-projected hidden trajectory under a circular input — and lower-bounding its growth separately for Neural ODEs, CT-RNNs and LTCs (Thms 4–5). The measure does separate them, by orders of magnitude in places (hard-tanh, width 200: `138 / 121 / 5.4×10⁴`), and it is sensitive to width, weight variance, activation and solver steps ([[wiki/entities/ltc.md]]).

## Where it bites

Every architectural argument in the wiki. If A, the recurrent-structure lever is a no-op inside a stated class and design effort belongs elsewhere (G29). If B, universality is merely a low bar and finer geometric measures are the right tool — which is what most of the wiki's architecture pages implicitly assume without ever naming a measure.

## Status

`LIVE` — and B's instrument has a defect that A does not: LTC's ODE is **stiff**, so a variable-step solver spends up to **133× more integration steps** on it than on a Neural ODE (81.01 vs 0.61 for hard-tanh), while trajectory length is *exponential in step count* for every family. A large fraction of B's separation is bought compute, and the compute-matched comparison is never run. The resolution is cheap and nobody has done it: fix `L` for all families with a fixed-step solver and re-measure. Until then the wiki has **no per-FLOP expressivity measure at all**, which is the actual gap both positions expose. **Update (Hasani et al. 2022, [[wiki/entities/cfc.md]]):** the same group then removed the solver entirely — a CfC evaluates `t` explicitly and costs what a discrete RNN costs — so the `L`-free member of the family now exists and the compute-matched measurement needs no new architecture, only the measure run on it. It has not been run; what *is* known is that the solver-free model matches or beats the stiff one on task accuracy, which is what one would expect if the trajectory-length gap was bought rather than architectural. **A third source declines the question entirely, and that is itself evidence for A**: Gu, Goel & Ré 2022 ([[wiki/entities/s4.md]]) make *no* expressivity claim — no universality theorem, no geometric measure — and instead report an algorithm (`Õ(N+L)` via normal-plus-low-rank + Woodbury + Cauchy kernels) plus benchmarks, then ablate and find the result is carried by an **initialisation**. If the strongest long-range sequence model in the wiki was obtained without any expressivity argument being made, the instrument A says is unavailable was also not needed.
