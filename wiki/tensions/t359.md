# T359 — Is the motivational quantity a gain applied to one value estimate, or a second valuation system with its own associative structure?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** One preparation runs the mesolimbic-gain manipulation and the instrumental-devaluation assay on the same animals in the same session. Deplete or antagonise accumbens dopamine and test, in extinction, (i) instrumental responding for a devalued versus a non-devalued outcome and (ii) the rate of responding overall. A is right if the devaluation *discrimination* is flattened along with the rate — one quantity scaled. B is right if the rate falls while the devalued/non-devalued difference survives intact, and if it survives an accumbens lesion that abolishes Pavlovian–instrumental transfer in the same animals.

## Position A

**A gain on one value estimate.** `wanting(cue, t) = f(V̂(cue), state(t))` — one learned association, one state-dependent multiplier applied at the cue at retrieval ([[wiki/concepts/incentive-salience.md]], Berridge & Robinson 2016; Zhang et al. 2009's closed form). **A is sharper than this row stated it, now that the equation is read.** Zhang et al. write `Ṽ(s_t) = κ·r_t + γ·V̂(s_{t+1})`: the gain is applied to the **primary-reward term only** and is explicitly denied access to `V` and to `Q`, and `V̂` is written by `δ` and by nothing else. So A is not "one value estimate with a knob on it" — it is a two-write-path design in which the modulator can re-rank options at retrieval while being unable to alter anything the learner stores, which is B's firewall achieved with one parameter instead of a second system. Evidence: dopamine depletion abolishes pursuit and consumption while leaving hedonic reactions normal; mesolimbic stimulation quadruples food 'wanting' without raising 'liking'; sensitization amplifies cue-triggered pursuit and not the hedonic estimate. On this reading the architecture is one value function plus one gain register, and `G118`'s two quantities are `V̂` and `κ`.

## Position B

**Two valuation systems, separately stored and separately ablatable.** Cardinal et al. 2002 ([[wiki/concepts/valuation-system-decomposition.md]]): the Pavlovian valuation and the instrumental incentive value are different representations with different update rules and different substrates. Evidence: excitotoxic accumbens lesions leave goal-directed action intact by **both** of Dickinson & Balleine's criteria — sensitivity to contingency degradation and to outcome devaluation (Balleine & Killcross) — and dopamine-receptor antagonists likewise leave the instrumental incentive value alone, while the same manipulations abolish Pavlovian–instrumental transfer, autoshaping and the amphetamine potentiation of conditioned reinforcement. The two valuations also update differently: Pavlovian value tracks motivational state **directly**, instrumental incentive value changes only after the outcome is re-experienced (incentive learning). Pharmacological dissociation of the two valuation processes is reported independently.

**B gains a dissociation *inside* the Pavlovian side, which A's single-gain form cannot produce.** Flagel et al. 2011 ([[wiki/concepts/sign-tracking-and-goal-tracking.md]]): one Pavlovian contingency yields two conditioned responses acquired at matched rates, with a dopamine receptor antagonist during acquisition blocking learning of the cue-directed one and not the outcome-directed one. A gain applied to one Pavlovian value estimate scales pursuit; it does not select *which* conditioned response is learned, and it cannot leave a fully-formed association behind when it is removed (`T404`).

## Where it bites

**Whether `G118`'s second quantity multiplies the agent's action values or runs beside them.** Under A the fix is one extra register: keep `Q`, add a state- and cue-indexed `κ`, and pursuit and preference come apart because `κ` moves. Under B there is no single `Q` to scale — there are two value stores trained by different rules on different admissible signals, their outputs are **summed at a downstream interface** (the accumbens, taking content from the amygdala and cortex and gain from the midbrain), and an architecture that applies a gain to its action values has merged the systems before the interface exists.

The designs differ in what is architecturally denied. A cannot express a manipulation that removes motivational invigoration while leaving a *learned preference ordering that is still sensitive to devaluation* — scaling by `κ` flattens the discrimination and the rate together. B cannot express the clinical case A is built on, where a single sensitized quantity grows and drags pursuit with it, without further saying which of its two systems sensitized. Both are compatible with the hedonic/motivational split; they disagree about whether the motivational term is a *factor* or a *system*.

The evidence classes barely overlap and that is why the row survives. A's is pharmacological and lesion work read through pursuit, consumption and orofacial reactions, with no devaluation test in extinction; B's is lesion work read through the devaluation and contingency-degradation assays, with no hedonic measurement and no sensitization. Neither has run the other's assay on its own preparation.

## Status

`LIVE` — the discriminating design is cheap, uses only assays both literatures already own, and has not been run in one preparation.
