# T336 — Is the analogy integrator a 0.33 cc left frontopolar site, or a right frontal network?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Spec:** `D236`, `D235`, `D243` ([[_brainstorm/birm-spec.md]] §11)  
**Closes when:** The two lesion datasets are analysed by the *same* method — either Urbanski's 27 patients re-run under graph stochastic block modelling, or Mole's 247 re-run under voxel-based lesion-symptom mapping — **or** Mole's cohort reports the matched-control **difference index** (`I39`) rather than raw analogy accuracy, since the left-frontopolar result is a difference-score result and the right-frontal one is not.

## Position A

**A small left frontopolar site, and it is necessary.** Urbanski et al. 2016: 27 focal frontal patients, voxel-based lesion-symptom mapping. Analogy accuracy maps to one **left** rostral prefrontal cluster, MNI −31/51/−3, **0.33 cc**, Brodmann area 47/10; the matched perceptual control maps to no voxel anywhere; deficit severity scales with the number of severed tracts *connecting to that cluster* (`rs = −0.553`) and **no right-hemisphere tract disconnection predicts any deficit in either task**. The analogy-*preserved* patients' lesions overlap in **right** prefrontal cortex. Converging: the only quantitative activation meta-analysis puts the analogy-general peak in left BA 10 at −48/44/−8, family-wise-error corrected, with the right rostrolateral homologue not significant in either content domain (Hobeika et al. 2016). [[wiki/entities/lateral-frontoparietal-network.md]]

## Position B

**A right frontal network, and the left frontopolar result does not replicate.** Mole et al. 2025: **247** prospectively recruited unilateral focal patients (frontal `n = 102`, posterior `n = 74`, 81 matched controls), graph lesion-deficit mapping by layered non-parametric Bayesian stochastic block modelling. On an analogy task *explicitly modelled on Urbanski's*, right frontal patients are worse than left frontal patients (63.19% vs 73.19%, `P < 0.05`), and the inferred network is right middle and inferior frontal gyri with the hierarchy dominated by **caudal** middle frontal gyrus — right, and posterior to the frontal pole. The same cohort's Stroop and phonemic-fluency networks come out strongly **left**, so the lesion sample can detect left-sided effects; and lesion volume predicts nothing. [[wiki/entities/lateral-frontal-pole.md]]

## Where it bites

The wiki's relational supermodule has one location and it is now two locations, on the same task family, with the causal-evidence grade on both sides. The downstream commitments are not cosmetic: Position A's mechanism is **fan-in convergence at a single small integrator** — the dose-response on severed afferents is the wiki's only measurement of `T289`'s *afferent diversity*, and it is a left-hemisphere measurement. Position B has no fan-in claim and proposes instead the **parallel maintenance of several novel representations**, which is a capacity property of a distributed network rather than a property of a convergence point. A builder choosing between "one small expensive integrator most inputs should not reach" and "a wide network whose limit is how many novel items stay active at once" is choosing between different architectures, not different diagrams.

## Status

`LIVE`, and three of the usual dissolvers are already excluded by the sources themselves.

- **Not task format.** Mole's Analogical Reasoning Test is stated to be inspired by Urbanski's: same source-set/two-target-sets layout, same relational-vs-perceptual control logic, same "match on the relation among the elements" instruction.
- **Not verbal/non-verbal lateralisation.** The right-frontal network is recovered on Mole's *entirely verbal* deductive task as well as the visuospatial analogy task.
- **Not general difficulty.** Right frontal patients are worse than left on **both** intra- and cross-dimensional analogy trials, though everyone is worse on cross-dimensional ones; and Urbanski's impaired and preserved groups are indistinguishable on the whole executive battery.

What is **not** excluded, and is where the row will be decided:

1. **Method.** Voxel-based lesion-symptom mapping is mass-univariate and requires ≥3 lesions per voxel; Mole's own argument is that it cannot support strong inference at `n = 27`, and Urbanski notes several frontal areas were unanalysable for exactly that reason. The two studies have never been run through one pipeline. This is a `L0-INSTR` dispute wearing an anatomical costume.
2. **Score definition.** Urbanski's localiser is the **analogy − perceptual-match index**, not analogy accuracy: the index localises the left cluster at `AUC = 0.954` where raw accuracy gives `0.925` and the control arm alone gives nothing (`I39`). Mole ran a perceptual matching baseline and reports it only as an unimpaired group comparison. The index is computable from data already collected.
3. **Which sub-operation.** Mole's own laterality is not uniform across their items: the **Odd-one-out** rule is right-lateralised and the **Progression** rule is not; **indeterminate** deduction is right-lateralised and **determinate** deduction is not. Whatever the right frontal network does, it is not "analogy". If Position A's site is required for relational matching and Position B's network for holding several novel candidate representations in parallel, both results stand and neither task isolates one component — which would make this a `G21` composition question rather than a localisation dispute.
4. **Coverage.** Urbanski's series is frontal-only and 74%-covering; Mole's lobar assignment is a ≥70%-of-lesion rule that no pathology respects. Neither design can report the *absence* of a contribution at a resolution finer than the lobe.

The reconciliation on record — Chau et al. 2025's proposal that Mole's task requires *identifying* an analogy while the frontopolar transform is needed only to *apply* one — addresses the rostro-caudal half and is silent on the laterality half, which is the larger discrepancy. It is also weakened from inside Position A: Urbanski crossed supplied-schema against derived-schema (`AnalogyApply`/`AnalogyFind`) and the frontopolar patients were impaired **equally** on both.
