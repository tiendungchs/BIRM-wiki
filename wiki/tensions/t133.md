# T133 — What does the direct/indirect pathway balance set — the discount factor or the exploration temperature?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**The discount factor.** The indirect pathway supplies the delayed term in the striatal temporal-difference computation, so raising its relative contribution raises the effective `γ`; serotonin from dorsal raphe reaches both striatum and the dopaminergic nuclei and is the proposed knob (Doya 2002, [[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]]).

## Position B

**The exploration temperature.** Chakravarthy's family inserts an *explore* regime between `Go` and `NoGo` whose substrate is the indirect pathway: when the value gradient is flat, that channel sets the stochasticity of action selection (Magdoom et al. 2011; Krishnan et al. 2011, via Helie et al. 2013, [[wiki/entities/basal-ganglia.md]]).

## Where it bites

Two theories read the same one-dimensional anatomical ratio as two different metaparameters, and both cannot be recovered from it without confounding — an agent that lengthened its horizon would be indistinguishable from one that started exploring. It also decides which gap the anatomy answers: G24 (no principled planning horizon) or G61 (exploration is always an external schedule).

## Status

`LIVE` — neither is implemented, and the shared premise is weaker than either: the balance may not be a scalar at all, since both pathways are active during movement and *timing* rather than level predicts choice (T129)
