# T323 — Is a module's integration timescale carried by its individual units, or only by the population?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The across-trial spike-count autocorrelation is fitted **per neuron** in a population with enough trials to make the per-neuron fit identifiable, and the distribution of single-neuron `τ` is reported against the population fit. Tight and centred on the population value → the population statistic is an estimate of a shared per-unit property (A). Broad, multi-modal, or with a population `τ` outside the bulk of it → the areal constant is an emergent property of the ensemble and no unit owns it (B). The same test runs unchanged on a trained network.

## Position A

**A unit owns its time constant.** Every timescale mechanism in the wiki is parameterised this way and several are validated against per-neuron data. [[wiki/concepts/neuronal-parameter-heterogeneity.md]] learns `α^k = [τ^k, γ^k, C^k, u_th^k, u_re^k]` per neuron on a bi-level loop and reports that the learned constants **match recorded distributions for human middle temporal gyrus and mouse V1 layer 4** — a per-neuron `τ` that is both learnable and biologically calibrated. [[wiki/entities/ltc.md]] makes a continuous per-unit rate the whole architecture; [[wiki/concepts/spike-frequency-adaptation.md]]'s DEXAT sets span from per-neuron adaptation constants; [[wiki/entities/ms-ssm.md]] and [[wiki/entities/s4.md]] attach eigenvalues to channels. On this reading the areal number is a population *estimate* of a quantity each unit has, and averaging is just denoising.

## Position B

**Only the ensemble has one.** Murray et al. 2014 fit `A[exp(−kΔ/τ) + B]` at the **population level and say why**: single-neuron autocorrelations are heterogeneous and the clean exponential appears only in the pooled data (Supplementary Fig. 2). The authors state the conclusion explicitly — the intrinsic timescale is *"a characteristic at the population level rather than at the single-neuron level"* and *"does not refer to single-neuron physiology"* ([[wiki/concepts/intrinsic-timescale-measurement.md]]). The mechanism side agrees: in [[wiki/concepts/timescale-hierarchy.md]] the timescales **are the eigenvalues of the coupling matrix**, so they belong to the network operator, and scrambling long-range *weights* while leaving every node's parameters untouched strongly attenuates the spread — a per-unit constant that is unchanged while the timescale changes is not the carrier.

## Where it bites

The parameterisation of every timescale bank, and therefore what `G67`/`G78` are asking for. Under A the design object is a set of numbers, one per unit or per block, and the open question is how to choose or learn them. Under B the design object is a **coupling structure** whose spectrum has the timescales, per-unit constants are a mis-parameterisation that cannot express localisation, and `G67`'s target relocates from "a measure on the unit disc" to "a graph plus a depth field" — which is the relocation [[wiki/concepts/timescale-hierarchy.md]]'s update to `G67` already argued for on other grounds. It also bites the diagnostic: under A you probe units and histogram, under B a per-unit probe reports noise.

**Distinct from T319**, which asks whether an areal timescale is fixed or input-dependent. That row varies the *input* and holds the level of description fixed; this row varies the **level of description** and holds the input fixed. Both can be true at once: a timescale can be an ensemble property that is also input-dependent — indeed B's mechanism (coupling-matrix eigenvalues) predicts exactly that pairing.

## Status

`LIVE`, and the two positions are not yet known to be about the same quantity — which is the cheapest thing to check first. HIFI's per-neuron `τ` is a **membrane/adaptation** constant fitted inside a neuron model; Murray's `τ` is the decay of **across-trial spike-count covariance**, a statistic of the whole recurrent circuit that would be nonzero even with identical membranes. If they are different quantities the wiki has been conflating them, and A's biological validation supports nothing about B's object. Nobody has reported both for one population.
