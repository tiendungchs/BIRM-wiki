# T96 — Is the controller one homogeneous pool or partitioned by content?

**Status:** `BOTH`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The dispute is one of degree with a stated threshold: the criterion separating 17% strong wins from 88% weak ones is justified rather than swept, so the partition claim has a fixed operating point.

## Position A

**Homogeneous.** Prefrontal neurons adaptively code whatever the current task requires, so the same cells serve attention, working memory and control, and functional differences between orbitofrontal and dorsolateral cortex reflect only different balances of response strength rather than category-distinct modules (Duncan's adaptive coding; Miller & Cohen's "no modular discretely localized forms")

## Position B

**Partitioned by content class.** 275 imaging studies show consistent localization differences between functions; social behaviour impairment follows ventromedial damage while mechanistic/reflective impairment follows dorsolateral damage; dissociable networks for emotional vs. non-emotional and social vs. non-social content; emotion-specific neurons in human ventral prefrontal cortex — with the partition predicted to follow *connectivity* to the posterior or subcortical region supplying the content (Wood & Grafman 2003, [[wiki/concepts/controller-knowledge-vs-process.md]]; anatomically echoed by the dorsal/ventral output-port split in [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether one control module suffices or the control layer must be factorized — and if factorized, whether the factorization is chosen by the designer or falls out of which downstream systems each part is wired to

## Status

`BOTH, AT DIFFERENT THRESHOLDS` — the first quantitative arbitration makes the dispute one of degree. Running five content domains head-to-head across the human medial wall, **17%** of voxels prefer one domain over all four rivals at the strong criterion (posterior ≥ 0.60) and **88%** do at the weak one (> 0.50, chance 6%), with one subdivision 99% covered by weak wins and another only 75% (Lieberman & Meyer 2018, [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]). So B is right about the gradients and A is right about the substrate: mostly shared tissue carrying domain-preferring gradients, plus one region shared by construction. It also relocates B's own social pole from ventromedial to *dorso*medial, so the partition is real while the framework's specific assignments are not ([[wiki/concepts/controller-knowledge-vs-process.md]]) . **A third partition, cut on a different axis, is not in the dispute and settles part of it** (Friedman & Robbins 2021, [[wiki/concepts/control-unity-and-diversity.md]]): whatever is true of content, the controller is demonstrably factorized by *operation* — a common component plus updating- and shifting-specific components with separate genetic influences, separate receptor dependences (D1 working memory vs. D2 flexibility) and double-dissociable lesion sites (orbitofrontal reversal vs. ventrolateral BA-12/47 set-shifting; BA-8 conditional selection vs. BA-9/46 monitoring). Pure adaptive coding cannot produce components that a single drug moves in *opposite* directions
