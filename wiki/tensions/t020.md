# T20 — Is intelligence a property of the process that builds a solution, or of the finished arrangement?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A measure scores the construction of a new arrangement for a new domain, which Choi defers and Chollet routes through the uncomputable `GD` — the same object as `G36`.

## Position A

**The process.** Skill is an output artifact carrying none of the intelligence; the formalism splits the system into a program-synthesis engine (which has it) and the skill program it emits (which does not), and scores only the conversion rate from priors and experience into coverage of unseen situations. A hard-coded solver measures its developer, not itself (Chollet 2019, [[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]])

## Position B

**The arrangement.** `ℐ = log₂N/C` measures the arrangement, not its origin: a multiplication algorithm written by hand and a network that learned the same function by gradient descent have identical `ℐ` and both *know*. The gaming channels are closed at the artifact instead — a lookup table has `ℐ → 0` however much data or developer effort produced it, and Blockhead is refuted on storage alone (Choi 2026, [[wiki/concepts/intelligence-density.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what the wiki is trying to build. Under A the target is a synthesis engine and every measurement must control for the developer; under B the target is a compact arrangement covering an unbounded domain and provenance is irrelevant, which makes hand-installed priors ([[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]]) free rather than disqualifying. It also decides whether [[wiki/entities/bayesian-program-learning.md]]'s hand-authored stroke primitives count against it

## Status

`LIVE` — and partly reconcilable: Choi concedes the missing piece is *understanding* (constructing a new arrangement for a new domain) and defers it, which is exactly A's object. The two measure different halves; neither measures both (gap G36)
