# T143 — How much of the world needs to be inside the model at all?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]

## Position A

**Enough to plan on.** The wiki's entire framing: infer the latent graph, then navigate it ([[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]); the richer and more causally faithful the internal model, the more queries it answers.

## Position B

**Less than assumed — the world is its own best model.** Passive dynamic walkers walk with no controller and no model, from body–environment interaction alone; morphological computation makes the body itself part of the information processing, and subsumption architecture got flexible behaviour from no world model whatsoever. Taniguchi et al. 2023 close their survey on exactly this speculation: behaviours may be *emergent* from embodied interaction rather than externalised internal trajectories.

## Where it bites

Sets how much of a reasoning system's competence is allowed to live outside its representation. The wiki has no page for the position and no criterion for which parts of a task are cheaper to offload to dynamics than to model — but note the scope limit: every cited demonstration is sensorimotor, and none of them does anything counterfactual, which is the capability the model is wanted for.
