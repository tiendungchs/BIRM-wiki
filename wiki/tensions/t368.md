# T368 — Is the basolateral→central amygdala projection a valence-general output route or the negative-valence channel?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Projection-identified basolateral→medial-central-nucleus neurons are photoinhibited in an **appetitive** task the central nucleus is already known to be required for — Pavlovian conditioned approach (autoshaping), general Pavlovian–instrumental transfer, or amphetamine potentiation of conditioned reinforcement. A predicts impairment, because the appetitive response needs this edge to reach the brainstem and the midbrain. B predicts sparing or enhancement. A mixed result splits the central nucleus's medial part from the rest of it and the row becomes an anatomy question rather than an architecture one.

## Position A

**A valence-general output route; the axis is representational format.** Cardinal, Parkinson, Hall & Everitt 2002 ([[wiki/entities/amygdala.md]]): the basolateral nucleus reaches the brainstem response nuclei *only* through the central nucleus — conditioned freezing, fear-potentiated startle and conditioned bradycardia are impaired by lesions of **either**, which is the signature of a serial chain. The same central nucleus is required for appetitive autoshaping, for general Pavlovian–instrumental transfer and for amphetamine potentiation of conditioned reinforcement, and central-nucleus-lesioned rats still acquire devaluation-sensitive appetitive conditioned responses. Under A the amygdala's internal split is identity-bearing value (basolateral) versus outcome-blind cue→response links plus global gains (central), and both limbs carry appetitive and aversive learning. Evidence: rat lesion double dissociations across ~10 tasks.

## Position B

**The negative-valence channel.** Namburi et al. 2015 ([[wiki/entities/amygdala.md]]): basolateral neurons projecting to the medial central nucleus **potentiate** after fear conditioning and **depress** after reward conditioning, while the intermingled accumbens-projecting population does the exact reverse; photostimulating them produces real-time place avoidance and will not support self-stimulation; photoinhibiting them during the unconditioned stimulus impairs conditioned freezing **and enhances conditioned reward seeking**. Under B the edge is one half of a signed pair addressed by axon, and valence separation happens *inside* the basolateral nucleus rather than between nuclei. Evidence: mouse projection-identified slice physiology plus optogenetic sufficiency and necessity.

## Where it bites

**Whether an architecture routes by outcome-format or by valence sign.** Under A the module boundary is `value-with-outcome-identity` versus `reflex-link + global gain`, each limb valence-general, and a designer who builds a reward head and a punishment head has mislocated the split — the wiki's current headline for this structure. Under B the negative channel is a channel: a non-negative `head_−` with its own downstream consumer, its own plasticity sign for a shared input, and a measured *interaction* with `head_+` (suppressing one improves the other) that no arrangement in [[wiki/concepts/affective-opponency.md]] contains.

The two are not straightforwardly contradictory and the cheapest reconciliation is anatomical: B's target is the **medial** part of the central nucleus, while A's appetitive results (autoshaping, transfer, amphetamine potentiation) run through the central nucleus → ventral tegmental area → accumbens-core route and may belong to the lateral part. If that holds, both are right and the amygdala carries a format split *and* a valence split on different edges — which is the most expensive design of the three and the one no model here has.

The enhancement result is what keeps the row live. A valence-general output route whose suppression makes appetitive learning **better** is hard to state: A would have to say the route is general but competitive, so removing aversive traffic frees it. That is a testable further claim (the enhancement should then scale with how much aversive traffic is present) and nobody has made it.

## Status

`LIVE` — the discriminating manipulation is one photoinhibition experiment away and uses instruments both labs already have.
