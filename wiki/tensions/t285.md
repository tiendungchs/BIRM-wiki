# T285 — Is there a floor below which mechanistic description is impossible in principle, or only in practice?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `META`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** An operational test decides which side of the boundary a given subnetwork is on, which neither position supplies.

## Position A

**In principle, and the boundary is a property of the task.** A pure memorisation task admits no generalisation from seen to unseen entities, hence has no task-relevant *macrofeatures* by definition, hence every intermediate state of any network solving it is either an irrelevant macrofeature (`first_name_is_george`, preserved to the output by the residual stream) or a weighted sum of per-example microfeatures — which is exactly a lookup table and not a circuit. The DeepMind team's failure to reverse-engineer 5 MLP layers of Pythia 2.8B is therefore the predicted outcome, not a methodological shortfall, and no refinement of the instruments changes it (Rajamanoharan et al. 2023, [[wiki/concepts/memorisation-vs-generalisation.md]])

## Position B

**In practice, and the wiki's own record is against the pessimism.** Every previous "this is not decomposable" verdict in the wiki dissolved under a better-chosen frame: OthelloGPT's world model was declared non-linear at 75% linear-probe accuracy and reached 99.6% once relabelled Mine/Yours ([[wiki/concepts/linear-representation-hypothesis.md]]). Inside the supposedly pure-lookup subnetwork there is a **legible, causal, human-nameable unit** — the baseball neuron L5N6045, 89.3% ROC AUC, loss on baseball players 0.167 → 0.284 under mean ablation, still 83% after projecting out the three directions that would make it a mere signal-booster. And the "pure" classification is unverified: a probe on the summed name tokens alone reaches ~50% against a 33% base rate, so the task is somewhere in "memorisation with rules of thumb", where macrofeatures provably exist

## Where it bites

Decides whether the wiki's interpretability instruments have a domain of validity or an excuse. If A, then [[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]] need a **pre-registration step** — measure held-out-entity inference accuracy before attempting to interpret a subnetwork, and budget for a null — and the design consequence is to route memorised content into an explicit store so that the reasoning machinery stays interpretable (`G98`). If B, a null result remains what it has always been: weak evidence about the interpreter

## Status

`LIVE`. The two positions are compatible on the boundary case and differ on where the boundary lies, and neither side has an operational test for which side a given subnetwork is on. **The asymmetry the baseball neuron reveals is the most likely settlement, and it is stated by neither side:** the argument forbids nameable intermediate states on the **input** side of a lookup (where the only available features are microfeatures of the entity) and says nothing about the **output** side (where `plays_baseball` is a macrofeature of the *answer*). That predicts interpretability of a lookup circuit should succeed downstream of the table and fail upstream of it, monotonically in position — testable on the same subnetwork with the instruments already built, and unrun
