# T281 — Is a neuromodulatory channel unaddressed by construction?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A learning rule for the decoders is exhibited, so an addressed scheme stops costing an unlearned `W_k` per module — the only thing A currently retains.

## Position A

**Yes — that is the whole argument for putting metaparameters on chemistry.** A quantity that regulates every weight must reach everything and address nothing, and the anatomy matches: small brainstem nuclei projecting diffusely to cortex, striatum and cerebellum, so one scalar per knob is the licensed model and specificity is recovered downstream by receptor subtype (Doya 2002, [[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]])

## Position B

**No — the diffuse systems are addressed, at three levels at once.** Basal-forebrain acetylcholine selects a *different function* by projection target (emotional learning via amygdala, spatial memory via dorsal hippocampus, cue encoding via medial prefrontal cortex); dopamine and acetylcholine are distributed non-uniformly across striosome and matrix, with a single cholinergic interneuron locally regulating dopamine release; and the same chemical is delivered either by wiring transmission (one-to-one, precise, transient) or by volume transmission (one-to-many, sustained, diffuse) depending on the projection. Co-release breaks the sender's identity outright — locus-coeruleus neurons release *dopamine* into dorsal hippocampus (Mei et al. 2025)

## Where it bites

Whether "global scalar" is a mechanism or a modelling convenience. If B, the count of control channels is not the count of chemicals, `α`/`β`/`γ`/`δ` cannot be assigned one wire each, and the machine form is a broadcast vector with a per-module decoder rather than four scalars — which is also the cheapest available answer to `G93` (an addressed context channel), and it changes what an ablation of "the dopamine channel" even means. Neither side is contested on the *facts*; A is a claim about the right level of abstraction and B is the anatomy it abstracts from. The discriminating test is behavioural rather than anatomical: a task where two modules need opposite settings of the same metaparameter at the same time, which A cannot express and B can

## Status

`LEANING B` — every measurement is on B's side and A has none of its own; what A retains is that no learning rule exists for the decoders, so an addressed scheme currently costs an unlearned `W_k` per module where A costs nothing, and A's degeneracy complaint (similar behaviour from different modulatory configurations) applies *more* strongly to B, not less
