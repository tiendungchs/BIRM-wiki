# T81 — What determines whether a memory consolidates — that it *recurs*, or that the slow learner can *model* it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The composition is tested: recurrence as the online prefilter on the replay pool, predictability as the offline gate — the two criteria are plausibly composable rather than rival.

## Position A

**Recurrence.** Reliable and unreliable synaptic updates are indistinguishable at one synapse but separable across a population, so the optimal filter gates the long-term write on the fast store's own recall of the proposed update, `Δw_LTM ∝ g(w_STM · w*)` — one broadcast scalar per event (prediction accuracy, reward × confidence, or familiarity), no offline pass, and a proven scaling advantage over any synapse-local mechanism under bursty reinforcement (Lindsey & Litwin-Kumar 2024; [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]])

## Position B

**Predictability.** Consolidation is transport of gradient signal into a slow learner, so its value is the *generalization* it buys: the optimum is monotone only at infinite SNR, interior at intermediate SNR, and zero at low SNR, where continued transport drives generalization below chance. The criterion is whether the relation is modellable *by this student* — which noise, architecture mismatch, unobserved inputs and the student's own attention all reduce interchangeably (Sun et al. 2023; [[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]])

## Where it bites

The two criteria come apart on a specific, buildable case: a **reliably recurring but unmodellable** relation — a nonlinear regularity a linear learner meets daily — passes A's gate at full strength and is refused by B. Sun et al. state the disagreement from their side as a prediction: if the brain uses frequency as its heuristic for predictability, frequent misinformation consolidates over rare accurate testimony, which is precisely the failure mode a recurrence gate has by construction. A machine test is cheap — stream high-frequency/low-reliability against low-frequency/high-reliability relations and ask which is written to slow **W**. They also differ on *what* is regulated: A gates the write event-by-event and needs no offline period, B rations the *number of reactivations* per relation and is naturally offline

## Status

`LIVE`. Not symmetric in scope: A derives its criterion from the statistics of experience with fixed random representations and is agnostic to the learner's capacity; B derives its criterion from the learner's approximation error and is agnostic to how experience arrives. **They are plausibly composable rather than rival** — recurrence as the cheap online prefilter on which updates enter the replay pool, predictability as the stopping rule on how long the pool is replayed **(brainstorm)** — but nobody has run both in one model, and neither supplies the other's missing half

**A third criterion, orthogonal to both** (Klinzing et al. 2019, [[wiki/concepts/sleep-oscillation-nesting.md]]): selection by **where attention was**, carried by the thalamic gating circuit rather than by anything about the memory's content. Sensory-projecting thalamic reticular neurons lock to alpha during waking attention and to spindles during subsequent sleep; spindles are localised where slow oscillations are global; and the slow oscillation's location does not predict the location of the spindle nested in its up-state, so some non-cortical variable addresses the window. This is neither recurrence (A) nor modellability (B) — it is a stale tag set once, at encoding, by the controller's *selection* rather than by the material's statistics, and it is the only one of the three that is computable before the outcome of the episode is known. Explicitly speculative in the source. It composes with A and B in the same way A composes with B (prefilter → gate → address), which is now three stages and no source running even two.

**Where the criterion is *computed* is now measured, and it is not where A or B put it** (Rothschild et al. 2017, [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]). A and B both write their criterion as a function of the fast store's contents — recurrence of an update, modellability of a relation — evaluated before or during the replay event. But pre-ripple auditory-cortical ensemble patterns predict CA1 spiking *during* the ripple (`P = 2.7 × 10⁻⁶`) while pre-ripple CA1 predicts cortex not at all, and the cortical pattern can be set by a sensory cue whose bias outlives the stimulus by 2–14 s. So at least part of the selection is a **receiver-side state variable**, written by intracortical plasticity and settable from outside, and this row's `Closes when` — recurrence as online prefilter, predictability as offline gate — is specified entirely in the wrong module to capture it. This is the third criterion's (attention-tag) *location* claim without its thalamus and without its encoding-time freezing: the cortical bias is re-settable during sleep, which the tag is not. It does not adjudicate A vs B; it says the composition both sides assume needs a third input port.

**A fourth criterion, and the only one that denies the question's premise** (Kumaran, Hassabis & McClelland 2016, [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]]): **significance** — consolidate the statistically unusual event *because* it is unusual, so the slow learner's model is deliberately not a mirror of experience. Unlike A (recurrence) and B (modellability), this criterion is indifferent to whether the relation repeats or whether the student can fit it; its whole content is that a one-off, badly-modelled, high-stakes episode is exactly what must reach cortex. It is computable at encoding from neuromodulatory signals and, unusually, is **revisable afterwards** — molecular stabilization lets a later event raise an earlier episode's replay probability, which none of A, B or the attention-tag criterion permits. It shares the sign of `|δ|`-prioritisation as deployed in machines while having a different justification, and the wiki now has one head-to-head measurement in which both salience-flavoured rules lose to uniform coverage ([[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]]). Filed as its own row because the disagreement is about the *target distribution* rather than about the filter: [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T375.
