# T200 — Is systematicity a property of the architecture or of the training distribution?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Architecture and training distribution are crossed: the meta-learning episode structure applied to the PCFG SET architectures, testing whether the distribution recovers systematicity in all three.

## Position A

**Architecture.** Hupkes et al. 2020 score three standard sequence architectures on PCFG SET — data generated *by* the intended compositional rule, with argument strings never repeated so memorisation is priced out — and get systematicity 0.53 / 0.56 / 0.72 against task accuracies of 0.79 / 0.85 / 0.92, with the differences tracking architectural properties (the Transformer's order-independence, ConvS2S's local composition operator). On this reading the seq2seq family lacks the mechanism, and a compositionally-generated corpus cannot supply it ([[wiki/entities/pcfg-set.md]], G16)

## Position B

**The training distribution.** Lake & Baroni 2023 hold the architecture class fixed — a plain 3-layer seq2seq transformer, 1.4M parameters, no new components — and change only how the signal arrives: 100,000 episodes, each a fresh latent interpretation grammar inferable only from 14 in-context study examples. Result: **100%** exact-match systematic generalization, against **0% in 10/10 runs** for the same architecture trained on the same examples as a static corpus, and ≤0.22% / 0.87% error on SCAN's and COGS's lexical splits ([[wiki/entities/mlc.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides where the wiki spends effort. Position A puts compositionality in the architecture slot, where most of [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] already sits; position B puts it in the objective slot (G30), which is nearly empty and cheap to act on. The partial reconciliation is that neither result contradicts the other — they differ in *presentation*, not in architecture or in data-generating rule — but the reconciliation costs G16 its strongest phrasing, since the intended rule is unidentifiable from a corpus and identifiable from a task stream. What keeps it `LIVE` is that the win is facet-selective: productivity is 0.30–0.50 under A and **exactly 0.000** under B wherever the episode sampler did not vary length, so B has not shown the architecture is unlimited, only that one facet is purchasable
