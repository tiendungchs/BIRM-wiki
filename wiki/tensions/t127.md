# T127 — Is uncertainty itself represented, or only the value of resolving it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A design holds the value of resolving uncertainty fixed while sweeping the amount to resolve, giving the degree terms variance to regress on.

## Position A

**Uncertainty is a first-class represented quantity.** [[wiki/concepts/precision-weighting.md]]'s three-way partition gives precisions their own sufficient statistic `μ^λ`, their own substrate (synaptic gain / neuromodulation / local synchrony) and their own timescale (second-order, minimising the path integral `A = ∫F dt`), separate from states and parameters; the whole attention-is-precision claim, and every learning-rate-as-precision mechanism the wiki imports, requires the magnitude of uncertainty to exist somewhere as a signal (Friston 2009)

## Position B

**Only its value is.** Regressing human source-space induced power on each term of expected free energy separately, the *value of reducing* novelty (rostral middle frontal gyrus, insula, lateral orbitofrontal) and the *value of reducing* variability (medial orbitofrontal cortex) both survive FDR correction at multiple stages, while the **degree** of novelty has no surviving correlate anywhere and the degree of variability likewise. What is carried is uncertainty already multiplied by what resolving it is worth (Zhang et al. 2025, `raw/zhang-2025-novelty-variability-active-inference.md`)

## Where it bites

Decides whether an architecture needs a separate uncertainty register at all. If B holds, `Π` and an epistemic bonus are not two variables read from one number but one variable — and a model that exposes uncertainty magnitude to downstream control is representing something the brain does not. It also bears on [[wiki/concepts/expected-free-energy.md]]'s open question of whether the epistemic term is a *state* of the planner or a *term in its loss*

## Status

`LIVE, weak` — B is a null under low power and the authors say so: the task never varied uncertainty magnitude parametrically, so degree had little variance to regress on. The discriminating experiment is a design that holds the value of resolving fixed while sweeping the amount to resolve
