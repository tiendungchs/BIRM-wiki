# T102 — When forward and reverse inference disagree about a region's function, which wins?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `META`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Reverse inference is run with an instrument stronger than text-mined keyword tags over reported peaks, where its inferential direction is not paid for in construct validity.

## Position A

**Forward.** Meta-analysis over many studies is the field's — and this wiki's — standard evidence for structure–function claims: a region that reliably activates for a task class is taken to serve it, and accumulated study counts are what make the map trustworthy. Almost every localization claim on the wiki's biological pages descends from this instrument

## Position B

**Reverse.** Forward inference computes `P(activity | task)` and the claim needs `P(task | activity)`; going from the first to the second is affirming the consequent. The concrete disagreement: the most social-selective tissue in human medial prefrontal cortex under head-to-head reverse inference appears in *no* social-cognition meta-analysis and not in the same authors' forward map, because signal dropout leaves that location with ~¼ the study count of its dorsal counterpart — forward inference needs accumulated counts, a rate ratio does not (Lieberman & Meyer 2018, [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]])

## Where it bites

Every regional claim the wiki imports from imaging, and every machine selectivity sweep that ranks units by mean activation under a condition — those are forward-inference instruments and inherit the same failure. If B holds, an under-sampled or rarely-active component is systematically invisible to the standard analysis

## Status

`LEANING B` on the logic, which is not in dispute; the empirical caution is that reverse inference here is text-mined keyword tags over reported peaks, so B's instrument is weaker in construct validity exactly where it is stronger in inferential direction. **A second, independent instance with the same sign** (Boly et al. 2017, [[wiki/concepts/constitutive-vs-enabling.md]]): the forward Neurosynth map for *consciousness* includes a frontal cluster, the reverse map erases it, and the peak of that cluster `[−47, 6, 28]` is best predicted by "phonological" (0.76) and "task" (0.63) — while posterior sites invert cleanly (fusiform→faces 0.88, postcentral→touch 0.88, temporo-occipital→visual motion 0.90). The failure mode is now named: **a forward map of a construct can be a map of the paradigm used to measure it**, which is a different error from A's under-sampling case and is not fixed by more studies
