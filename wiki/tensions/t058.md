# T58 — Where does the theta-band structure of hippocampal sequences come from — a rhythm imposed on the network, or a bifurcation inside it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Theta is removed as an input and the network is tested for a Hopf-bifurcation limit cycle at the same frequency, with running-speed independence as the discriminator.

## Position A

**Imposed.** Theta is an external drive that the network is subject to: an 8–10 Hz oscillation added to the input current, whose troughs periodically collapse global inhibition and reset the competition between representations ([[wiki/entities/stp-flickering-cann.md]]), or a clock supplied to schedule inference steps ([[wiki/entities/spiking-tem.md]]). Sequence content is then read out *within* a cycle whose timing the network does not set

## Position B

**Emergent.** No clock anywhere: at intermediate adaptation gain the tracking fixed point loses stability through a Hopf bifurcation, and the resulting limit cycle has frequency `ω = √(2√π αak(1+m)/[ττ_v(J₀+2√π akα)])`, which lands in the theta band for biological parameters and is *independent of running speed*, reproducing theta sweeps and both phase precession and procession from the same solution ([[wiki/entities/adaptive-cann.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether an architecture needs an oscillator module at all. Under A the rhythm is a scheduling signal that a builder must supply and tune; under B it is a symptom of operating near an instability, and its frequency is *diagnostic* of gain rather than settable. The two make opposite predictions under manipulation of adaptation: emergent theta frequency should shift with adaptation strength (as `√(1+m)`) while an imposed rhythm should not, and B additionally predicts that suppressing adaptation abolishes sweeps without abolishing theta in the local field potential
