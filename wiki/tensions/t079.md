# T79 — Where does fast sensorimotor learning physically happen — in the circuit that carries the manifold, or upstream of it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The locus is measured directly — synaptic change in the recording area during within-session learning that preserves the population repertoire.

## Position A

**Upstream: the inputs change, the local network does not.** Within-session BCI learning in M1 preserves the population activity repertoire exactly (repertoire change ≈ 0, `p = 0.55`, `n` = 384; no covariability change along either mapping) while re-associating intents with existing patterns. Connectivity change *within* M1 would be expected to move the repertoire, so the authors read the locus as a change in what M1 receives for a given sensory input — cortical or subcortical, undetermined (Golub et al. 2018)

## Position B

**Locally: fast learning is synaptic change in the recording area.** The default reading of every fast plasticity rule in the wiki — spike-timing-dependent, behavioural-timescale, recall-gated — is that the area whose activity changes is the area whose weights change, and the wiki's fast/slow architecture places the fast store *in* the circuit that does the task ([[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]], [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]]). Repertoire preservation is an inference about connectivity from activity, not a measurement of connectivity; a sufficiently low-rank or readout-restricted local change could also leave the repertoire intact

## Where it bites

Decides where to put the fast learner in an architecture. Under A, fast adaptation is a **routing** module upstream of a frozen representational circuit — the retrieval/key-rebinding shape — and the representational network needs no fast plasticity at all. Under B, the same network holds both timescales and needs a mechanism to keep fast change from corrupting slow structure. The experiment that separates them (record the presumptive M1 input areas during the same perturbation) is unrun ([[wiki/concepts/manifold-constrained-learning.md]])
