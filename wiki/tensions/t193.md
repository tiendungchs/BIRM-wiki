# T193 — Is the developmental relational shift caused by accumulating relational knowledge, or by maturing executive capacity?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Relational knowledge and executive capacity are varied independently in the same children — distractor inhibition and relational-integration load against domain expertise.

## Position A

**Knowledge.** Analogical competence is present from infancy — some analogical ability at 1 year — and what grows is knowledge of the relations being mapped (Goswami 1992, 2001). Adults show the same dependence: domain expertise predicts analogical performance *within* that domain, and experts are more likely than novices to retrieve remote analogs on relational grounds (Novick & Holyoak 1991)

## Position B

**Executive maturation.** Preschoolers' relational responding falls under exactly the two manipulations that load the prefrontal cortex — the presence of a perceptually similar distractor (inhibition) and the requirement to integrate two relations (binding capacity) — and only by 13–14 years, roughly when prefrontal maturation is substantial, is the relational response reliable with both present (Richland, Morrison & Holyoak 2006). Children with autism matched to controls on executive function show comparable trends (Dawson et al. 2007; Morsanyi & Holyoak 2010), and the adult analogues of both manipulations (dual task, anxiety, frontal damage) reproduce the child pattern in healthy adults

## Where it bites

Decides which parameter to scale in a model that reasons better with age or training. Under A the fix is vocabulary — more and better relation types ([[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] G4) — and capacity is a constant. Under B the fix is the binding budget and the inhibition strength, which is a *capacity* parameter no wiki architecture exposes at all ([[wiki/entities/lisa.md]] is the only model that derives one). Holyoak's own reading is that both are real and neither is sufficient, which makes this the rare tension where the discriminating experiment exists — hold relational knowledge fixed and vary load — and has been run only in the adult direction
