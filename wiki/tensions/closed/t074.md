# T74 — Is content-addressable recall necessarily relaxation?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Recurrence is shown to be necessary for something completion alone cannot do — motion, sequences, iterative refinement — or the feedforward bimodal-threshold mechanism is shown to cover those too.

## Position A

**Yes — retrieval *is* the dynamics.** The wiki's attractor stores make completion a descent to a fixed point, and the cue is an initial condition rather than a key ([[wiki/entities/hopfield-network.md]], [[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]], [[wiki/concepts/attractor-dynamics.md]]); the property is derived from symmetric recurrence and a Lyapunov function

## Position B

**No.** A two-layer feedforward binary-weight map returns the stored trace from cues with up to 1/3 of their bits masked, because one-shot gated learning makes each unit's weighted-sum distribution *bimodal* and the threshold sits in the gap — basins created by the learning, not by recurrence, and read in one step instead of ~100 (Wu & Maass 2025). CA1, the modelled area, has almost no recurrent collaterals

## Where it bites

Whether "attractor" in this wiki names a dynamical property or merely a robustness property of a map. If B, every completion result quoted as evidence for recurrent attractor dynamics is also compatible with a feedforward store, and the argument for recurrence has to be made on sequences and search instead of on completion

## Status

`LEANING` toward B for *completion specifically* — the feedforward demonstration is explicit and quantitative, and nothing in it needs the recurrent case to be wrong; what recurrence still uniquely buys (motion, sequences, iterative refinement) is untouched

Retired at the design-ladder pass (step 2): demoted as an implementation or substrate detail. No decision in `_brainstorm/birm-spec.md` §11 or §12 turns on how it resolves, and the finding it records is carried as prose by [[wiki/concepts/attractor-dynamics.md]].
