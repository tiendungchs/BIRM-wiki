# T313 — Do dopamine neurons respond to aversive events and to cues predicting them?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A recording that identifies dopaminergic cells independently of their response profile (cell-type tagging rather than waveform + firing-rate criteria) reports the *fraction* of identified dopamine neurons activated by aversive stimuli and by aversive-predicting cues, in one animal, with appetitive trials interleaved.

## Position A

**No — the phasic signal is appetitive-only.** Aversive stimuli (air puffs to the hand, drops of saline to the mouth) do not evoke the transient activation that juice and food morsels do, and "very few dopamine neurons respond to stimuli that predict aversive outcomes". The record is otherwise strikingly homogeneous: different appetitive stimuli evoke similar responses, and each response occurs in 55–80% of recorded neurons — so the aversive null is not a sampling artefact of a sparse code (Schultz, Dayan & Montague 1997, `raw/schultz-1997-neural-substrate-prediction-reward.md`; Mirenowicz & Schultz 1996 as cited there).

## Position B

**Yes — a substantial fraction increase firing to aversive stimuli and to their predictive cues**, and dopaminergic discharge also covaries with the *variance* of juice rewards (Friston et al. 2012, [[wiki/entities/affordance-active-inference-model.md]]).

## Where it bites

This is a disagreement about the firing record itself, not about its interpretation, and it sits underneath [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T122. Two of the three observations T122's precision reading "gets for free" are the aversive ones; if A's record stands, the precision account is left with the variance result and the mechanistic G-protein-coupled-receptor objection, and loses its firing evidence. If B's record stands, the reward-prediction-error reading needs the predicted quantity widened to something that is *not* reward — at which point `r` in `δ = r + γV(s′) − V(s)` no longer names reward, and every architecture here that wires reward into that slot ([[wiki/concepts/reward-prediction-error.md]]) is wiring the wrong quantity.

**(brainstorm)** The two records are compatible if "dopamine neuron" is not one population — A's criterion is electrophysiological (broad waveform, low tonic rate, recorded in ventral tegmental area and substantia nigra), which selects a set that may be anatomically mixed and functionally split by target. That would make the resolution a *topography* result rather than an adjudication, and would predict that the aversive-responsive subset projects somewhere the appetitive subset does not — testable in the wiki's terms as two broadcast scalars with different address spaces rather than one.
