# T221 — Is expert domain knowledge "crystallized" in the sense that excludes it from an intelligence measure?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** GPQA items are perturbed at the hop level using their shipped reasoning chains (the GSM-Symbolic protocol): falling scores make the competence compositional and defeat the exclusion argument.

## Position A

**Yes — accumulated content, not mechanism.** The ARC framing excludes crystallized ability from the target because it is culturally acquired and not comparable across test-takers, and states the design rule *easy for humans, hard for AI*: a task both humans and machines find hard "isolates only accumulated knowledge", whereas a task humans solve effortlessly and machines cannot isolates a missing mechanism ([[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]]). On that reading a graduate-science benchmark measures the size of a retrievable store, and a retriever should substitute for it

## Position B

**No — what the expert has is not retrievable.** Rein et al. 2023 run the control: PhDs in *other* sciences, paid a $30-per-item bonus to succeed, with unrestricted internet and no time limit (median 30 min, mean 37, top quintile ≥45), reach **34%** against in-domain experts' **65%** and chance 25%. The machine side agrees — a search tool moves GPT-4 by +0.7 points. And the gap runs **opposite** to machine difficulty (chemistry Δ=40.6 is GPT-4's worst domain at 31.8%; biology Δ=23.5 is its best at 58.1%), so "hard for both" is not one quadrant. The six printed items are multi-hop chains whose every hop is individually look-up-able, which locates the gap in *selection and composition* rather than in storage

## Where it bites

Decides whether knowledge-loaded benchmarks are admissible evidence about a reasoning architecture at all, and therefore whether a brain-inspired reasoner should be scored on any of them. If the gap is compositional, GPQA and ARC are measuring overlapping mechanisms under opposite selection rules and the exclusion argument loses its basis; if it is storage, the 34% needs another explanation

## Status

`LIVE` — no experiment separates the two. The decisive one is cheap and unbuilt: perturb GPQA items at the hop level using the reasoning chains already shipped with each question (the GSM-Symbolic protocol, [[wiki/entities/gsm8k.md]]). If scores fall under matched re-instantiation, the competence is compositional and the exclusion argument fails; if they hold, it is a store. Note both sides are also bounded by the same instrument's noise floor — GPQA's own objectivity is 73.6–76.4% ([[wiki/entities/gpqa.md]])
