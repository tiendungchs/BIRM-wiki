# T201 — Is human compositional behaviour a rule system with lapses, or an approximator carrying rule-shaped biases?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Human error structure is predicted rather than absorbed into a lapse rate — a rule system that derives one-to-one and iconic concatenation instead of calling them arbitrary.

## Position A

**A rule system with lapses.** The probabilistic symbolic model infers the gold interpretation grammar from the study examples and translates queries by it; every non-algebraic response is a lapse drawn uniformly, with one fitted lapse rate. This is the position every program-induction and language-of-thought account in the wiki assumes ([[wiki/concepts/program-induction.md]], [[wiki/concepts/language-of-thought.md]]), and it is not obviously wrong here — people do hit 80.7% exact algebraic match, and 72.5% on outputs longer than any studied

## Position B

**An approximator with structured biases.** Human errors are not uniform: one-to-one translation accounts for 24.4% of them, iconic concatenation for 23.3% of function-3 errors, and in a no-example probe 62.1% / 79.3% / 93.1% of participants follow one-to-one, iconic concatenation and mutual exclusivity. A gold-grammar oracle must call all of that arbitrary, and loses to MLC on log-likelihood of human responses; augmenting the oracle with the same three biases ties MLC on the few-shot task and still loses by ≥57 nats on the open-ended one, because a symbolic model that hard-codes three biases cannot fit the fourth thing people do ([[wiki/entities/mlc.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what a brain-inspired reasoner is supposed to reproduce. If B, then a perfectly systematic module is the *wrong* target and the biases are content to be modelled rather than noise to be suppressed — and the wiki gains a mechanism for supplying them (sample them into `p(T)` at a measured rate) that costs no architecture. The caveat is symmetric: MLC was *trained on* an 80/20 algebraic/heuristic mixture calibrated to the human accuracy it is then scored against, so its bias fit is partly fitted rather than predicted, and the Supplementary Information reports it failing on bias nuances it was not optimized for — 98.0% mutual exclusivity against people's 68.2%, iconic concatenation collapsing to 66.7% against 93.9% (T202)
