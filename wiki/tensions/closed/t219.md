# T219 — What is a scaling extrapolation evidence for?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The honest export is adopted — a flat curve licenses "not this method", never "not this ability" — or a case is found where a flat curve did predict an ability ceiling.

## Position A

**For the task's hardness.** Hendrycks et al. 2021 fit a log-linear trend across GPT-2 0.1B→1.5B, project ~10³⁵ parameters for 40% on MATH, and conclude that scale will not deliver mathematical problem solving — the same argument form used for [[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]] and [[wiki/entities/arc-agi-2.md]], where flat or near-zero scores across four orders of magnitude of pretraining are read as evidence about the *ability*

## Position B

**For one algorithm family, and nothing else.** MATH was above 90% within roughly three years, via chain-of-thought, verifier-selected sampling and RL on verifiable rewards — none of which is a parameter count. The curve was measured on answer-only fine-tuned decoders and was silent about a change of inference procedure; the paper's own *conclusion* ("we will need new algorithmic advancements") was correct precisely because its curve was not a forecast. The irony is exact: the intervention that broke the extrapolation is the one the same paper measured as **negative** (T217)

## Where it bites

Every "scale will not solve X" claim in the wiki, and the interpretation of a flat benchmark curve generally. Under B, such a curve is a statement that the *current* method is not converging and carries no information about the discontinuity that a method change can produce — so it can never license an architectural conclusion, only a "not this way"

## Status

`LEANING B` — B is the one case in the wiki where the question is settled by outcome, and it settles it against A. The residual for A: MATH's saturation still required a new algorithm, i.e. the paper's qualitative claim survived its quantitative one, so the honest export is "a flat curve licenses *not this method*, never *not this ability*"

Absorbed into `G17` at the design-ladder pass: the row is evidence that no evaluation protocol can certify structure discovery, not a dispute independent of it, and its finding now sits in [[wiki/gaps/g017.md]] under *Evidence absorbed from the benchmark band*.
