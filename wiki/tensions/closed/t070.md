# T70 — Must a local approximation to backpropagation encode gradients as activity *differences*?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** NGRAD is redefined to accommodate Activation Relaxation, or Activation Relaxation is shown to encode an activity difference after all — its authors state it as an explicit counterexample.

## Position A

**Yes — the NGRAD hypothesis.** Lillicrap et al. 2020 unify the entire family of local credit-assignment schemes by the claim that they all represent gradients implicitly as differences in neural activity, either spatially between populations or compartments (predictive coding error nodes, apical dendrites) or temporally between phases (contrastive Hebbian, equilibrium propagation). This is the wiki's organising taxonomy on [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]] — temporal-error vs. explicit-error, with the choice of *where the difference is taken* driving every architectural cost in the four-model table

## Position B

**No.** Activation Relaxation converges to the exact backpropagated gradients with no layerwise activity difference anywhere: the value units themselves relax onto `∂L/∂x^l` as the equilibrium of `dx^l/dt = −x^l + x^{l+1}·∂x̄^{l+1}/∂x̄^l`, using one neuron type, one parallel backward phase and plain MLP connectivity (Millidge et al. 2020). The authors state it as an explicit counterexample requiring NGRAD to be redefined, while conceding the update can be *read* as a prediction error between a unit's activity and the layer above mapped through backward weights — a difference between two quantities that are never both instantiated as activities

## Where it bites

Whether the wiki's temporal/explicit dichotomy is exhaustive, and therefore whether its cost table is the full menu. Under A a builder must pay one of the two documented prices — extra populations/compartments, or a phase pair with cross-phase storage. Under B there is a third cell where the second quantity is a second *use* of the same units, and the price is storing the forward activation across the value→gradient switch. The two positions may be notational: if "activity difference" is loosened to include a difference between a current activity and a stored one, B collapses into A and NGRAD becomes unfalsifiable — which is why B's real content is the cost table, not the taxonomy
