# T377 — Which structure initiates a coordinated cortico-hippocampal replay event?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Initiation is manipulated rather than cross-correlated — clamp one area's activity during an otherwise normal frame and measure whether the other area's sequence replay survives; or record enough cells in both areas simultaneously to time the two *sequences* (not the two frames) against each other within single events.

## Position A

**Cortex initiates.** Cortical population frames start ~50 ms before hippocampal ones (`P = 2.2 × 10⁻⁸`) and end ~40 ms before them, hippocampus has no reported intrinsic up/down mechanism, and hippocampal interneurons phase-lock to cortical up/down transitions — so the window is opened from outside the store (Ji & Wilson 2007, [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]). The same arm carries content: cortical slow-oscillation up-states drive thalamic spindles that phase-lock ripples, and auditory-cortex patterns precede ripples by ~200 ms and *predict their content* (Rothschild et al. 2017 via Klinzing et al. 2019, [[wiki/concepts/sleep-oscillation-nesting.md]])

## Position B

**The hippocampus initiates the content, inside a window it did not open.** Ji & Wilson's own data trend toward hippocampal *replays* leading cortical ones within a frame (their Supplementary Fig. 10), and they state the direction is not established; hippocampal ripples can directly induce slow oscillations in medial prefrontal cortex, leading the mPFC slow-oscillation–spindle event by ~130 ms; mPFC replay correlates with hippocampal sharp waves directly (Euston et al. 2012, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]). The indexing and complementary-learning-systems accounts both require the store to nominate what is reinstated — under B the cortical frame is permission, not selection

## Where it bites

Where a machine consolidation scheduler lives, and who owns the replay distribution. Under A the receiver both times and selects, so the sampling policy is a state variable of the slow learner and every criterion on [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]] is computed in the wrong place. Under B the store selects and the receiver only gates, which is the architecture every wiki replay implementation already assumes. It also decides whether [[wiki/gaps/g014.md]]'s channel needs a return path for selection requests, or only for cargo

## Status

`LIVE`, and the sharpest datum in it is a **dissociation inside one paper rather than a disagreement between two**: Ji & Wilson measure the *frame* (window) direction as cortex→hippocampus with high significance, and explicitly decline to establish the *replay* (content) direction, which trends the other way in their own supplementary analysis. So "who leads" is two questions, and the evidence currently answers the timing one for A and leaves the content one open — the same split T373 reaches for the cortex-vs-thalamus arm of the same loop. The standing reconciliation in the review literature is a loop with both arms real (cortical events initiate hippocampal replay, which then reinforces ongoing cortical replay), which is compatible with both positions and therefore adjudicates neither; Euston et al. 2012 note directly that some measurements put hippocampal events first and others cortical. Note that the interval analysis that establishes the coordination in the first place is direction-blind by construction — it correlates cross-area spike-timing intervals against the behavioural template, which is symmetric in the two areas
