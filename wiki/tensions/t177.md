# T177 — Does long-range capability come from the architecture or from the initialisation?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The two axes are crossed: HiPPO-initialise a CT-RNN, an LTC and a grown reservoir at matched `n` and see whether the architectural differences survive a shared initialisation.

## Position A

**Architecture.** The wiki's default and the premise of nearly every entity page here: memory horizon is a structural property. [[wiki/entities/ltc.md]] builds an input-dependent rate into the ODE and lower-bounds trajectory length per architecture family; [[wiki/entities/hag-reservoir.md]] grows the recurrent graph and beats every static baseline; [[wiki/entities/simple-cycle-reservoir.md]] proves what a topology can and cannot express; [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] G16 puts the heaviest design lever in the architecture slot.

## Position B

**The initialisation, and it is a generalisation effect rather than a capacity one.** Gu, Goel & Ré 2022 hold architecture, learning rule and objective fixed and vary only the *initial value* of the state matrix `A` on sequential CIFAR-10 at ≤100K parameters: random Gaussian, random diagonal Gaussian, random normal-plus-low-rank, and HiPPO. **With `A` trained, every arm reaches perfect training accuracy and validation accuracies remain separated by over 15%.** Random NPLR — S4's own parameterisation minus HiPPO — "still do not perform well", and the authors state the effectiveness "primarily comes from the HiPPO initialization, not the NPLR parameterization". The predecessor LSSL reports the same swap as 60% → 98% on sequential MNIST ([[wiki/entities/s4.md]]).

## Where it bites

Everything. If B, then (i) architectural arguments about recurrent structure are arguments about a *starting point* that gradient descent neither recovers nor destroys, (ii) the [[wiki/concepts/three-component-framework.md]]'s three slots do not contain the decisive variable, and (iii) the right object of study is the prior on `A` — which for HiPPO is a projection of input history onto an orthogonal polynomial basis, i.e. a **choice of what to compress the past into**, made before any data arrive. It also reframes G29: architecture search would be searching the wrong space.

## Status

`LIVE` — the two positions are not symmetric and the asymmetry is the interesting part. A's evidence is *comparative across architectures*; B's is *within one architecture, across inits*, and no source in the wiki has run both axes at once. The discriminating experiment is cheap and unrun: HiPPO-initialise a CT-RNN, an LTC and a grown reservoir at matched `n`, and see whether the architectural separations A reports survive. If they collapse, B is general; if they persist, the two effects are additive and the wiki needs both. A second caveat on B: the ablation is one task (sequential CIFAR-10) at one scale (100K parameters), with the supporting figures dropped in extraction, so the >15% figure is held from prose. **Update (Karami et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/ms-ssm.md]]) — B is replicated on a second architecture and the variable is refined.** Holding the MS-SSM architecture, learning rule and objective fixed and changing only the *distribution the initial eigenvalues of `A` are drawn from* — Mamba's initialisation vs a scale-stratified draw `diag(A^s) ~ U(−N(S+1−s), −N(S−s)]` that assigns each band a disjoint interval — moves ListOps **57.49 → 63.04** and PTB-XL AUROC **0.928 → 0.939**. Two things follow. (i) B is no longer a single-paper, single-task result: two independent groups, two architectures, two initialisation families, same conclusion. (ii) The object in the fourth slot is not "an initialisation" but a **measure**: HiPPO is one point, and what MS-SSM varies is the *spread* of the draw rather than its identity. The companion number makes the point without any initialisation argument at all — at fixed total state, redistributing it across bands is worth 25.0 ListOps points where doubling the state is worth 4.1 (Mamba 2× state 42.14 vs MS-SSM 63.04 vs Mamba 38.02). So the residual A-position is narrower than it was: capacity is measurably *not* the lever, and what is left for architecture to explain is whatever the wavelet front end contributes beyond the stratified spectrum — which MS-SSM's Table 5 claims is the largest single contribution and reports in a dropped image, so it is unquantified. **Update (Orvieto et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/lru.md]]) — B holds, and the object in the fourth slot shrinks to three scalars.** The strongest form of B was "HiPPO is a good prior on `A`". LRU removes HiPPO and keeps the effect: eigenvalues drawn **uniformly on a ring** `[r_min, r_max]` in the unit disc, with phase restricted to `[0, π/10]` and an input gain `γ = √(1−|λ|²)`, reach Path-X **94.2%** and a six-task Long Range Arena average of **86.3** against S4's 86.09 — the first time the hardest LRA task falls to an unstructured initialisation. The authors state directly that HiPPO theory "should not be thought of as the main source of S4 success", and show by ablation on S4D that the ZOH input multiplier `(exp(ΔÃ)−I)Ã⁻¹B̃` is a **normaliser** (first-order expansion `ΔB̃` matches), that the `Δ` coupling between `A` and `B` is unnecessary, and that S4D-Lin's small `Δ` was silently supplying the phase restriction. **Consequence for this row:** A is not rescued — architecture still does not carry the result — but B's payload is now a **measure over the unit disc parameterised by three swept scalars** (`r_min`, `r_max`, `θ_max`), not a derived operator. That is a much weaker fourth slot than "a projection of history onto an orthogonal polynomial basis, chosen before any data arrive", and it makes the closing experiment cheaper: initialising a CT-RNN, an LTC and a grown reservoir from LRU's ring measure requires no HiPPO machinery at all. It also adds an axis this row never had — LRU's decisive knob is eigenvalue **phase**, and Position B's evidence to date (HiPPO, MS-SSM's stratified bands) varies only **magnitude**.
