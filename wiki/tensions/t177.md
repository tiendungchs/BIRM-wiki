# T177 — Does long-range capability come from the architecture or from the initialisation?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Architecture.** The wiki's default and the premise of nearly every entity page here: memory horizon is a structural property. [[wiki/entities/ltc.md]] builds an input-dependent rate into the ODE and lower-bounds trajectory length per architecture family; [[wiki/entities/hag-reservoir.md]] grows the recurrent graph and beats every static baseline; [[wiki/entities/simple-cycle-reservoir.md]] proves what a topology can and cannot express; [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] G16 puts the heaviest design lever in the architecture slot.

## Position B

**The initialisation, and it is a generalisation effect rather than a capacity one.** Gu, Goel & Ré 2022 hold architecture, learning rule and objective fixed and vary only the *initial value* of the state matrix `A` on sequential CIFAR-10 at ≤100K parameters: random Gaussian, random diagonal Gaussian, random normal-plus-low-rank, and HiPPO. **With `A` trained, every arm reaches perfect training accuracy and validation accuracies remain separated by over 15%.** Random NPLR — S4's own parameterisation minus HiPPO — "still do not perform well", and the authors state the effectiveness "primarily comes from the HiPPO initialization, not the NPLR parameterization". The predecessor LSSL reports the same swap as 60% → 98% on sequential MNIST ([[wiki/entities/s4.md]]).

## Where it bites

Everything. If B, then (i) architectural arguments about recurrent structure are arguments about a *starting point* that gradient descent neither recovers nor destroys, (ii) the [[wiki/concepts/three-component-framework.md]]'s three slots do not contain the decisive variable, and (iii) the right object of study is the prior on `A` — which for HiPPO is a projection of input history onto an orthogonal polynomial basis, i.e. a **choice of what to compress the past into**, made before any data arrive. It also reframes G29: architecture search would be searching the wrong space.

## Status

`LIVE` — the two positions are not symmetric and the asymmetry is the interesting part. A's evidence is *comparative across architectures*; B's is *within one architecture, across inits*, and no source in the wiki has run both axes at once. The discriminating experiment is cheap and unrun: HiPPO-initialise a CT-RNN, an LTC and a grown reservoir at matched `n`, and see whether the architectural separations A reports survive. If they collapse, B is general; if they persist, the two effects are additive and the wiki needs both. A second caveat on B: the ablation is one task (sequential CIFAR-10) at one scale (100K parameters), with the supporting figures dropped in extraction, so the >15% figure is held from prose. **Update (Karami et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/ms-ssm.md]]) — B is replicated on a second architecture and the variable is refined.** Holding the MS-SSM architecture, learning rule and objective fixed and changing only the *distribution the initial eigenvalues of `A` are drawn from* — Mamba's initialisation vs a scale-stratified draw `diag(A^s) ~ U(−N(S+1−s), −N(S−s)]` that assigns each band a disjoint interval — moves ListOps **57.49 → 63.04** and PTB-XL AUROC **0.928 → 0.939**. Two things follow. (i) B is no longer a single-paper, single-task result: two independent groups, two architectures, two initialisation families, same conclusion. (ii) The object in the fourth slot is not "an initialisation" but a **measure**: HiPPO is one point, and what MS-SSM varies is the *spread* of the draw rather than its identity. The companion number makes the point without any initialisation argument at all — at fixed total state, redistributing it across bands is worth 25.0 ListOps points where doubling the state is worth 4.1 (Mamba 2× state 42.14 vs MS-SSM 63.04 vs Mamba 38.02). So the residual A-position is narrower than it was: capacity is measurably *not* the lever, and what is left for architecture to explain is whatever the wavelet front end contributes beyond the stratified spectrum — which MS-SSM's Table 5 claims is the largest single contribution and reports in a dropped image, so it is unquantified.
