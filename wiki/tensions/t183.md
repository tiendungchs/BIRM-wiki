# T183 — Is theory of mind recursive inference, or a fixed point?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Exact rather than sampled nesting is run past depth 1, establishing whether recursion beyond one level buys anything measurable.

## Position A

**Recursive inference.** Because `query` is definable inside the stochastic λ-calculus, a planning query nests inside a goal-inference query with well-defined semantics — inference about inference, arbitrarily deep, and the same construction covers an agent reasoning about another agent's beliefs about a third (Goodman, Gerstenberg & Tenenbaum 2024; Stuhlmüller & Goodman 2014). Being *definable* is the whole argument: no separate mechanism is needed

## Position B

**A fixed point, or not recursive at all.** Two coupled spacetime attractors, each taking the other's inferred plan as its reward input, relax to a mutual best response with no explicit posterior anywhere ([[wiki/entities/spacetime-attractor.md]]). And the wiki's one *measured* inverse-planning model scores 96.0–99.7% on the Baby Intuitions Benchmark at recursion depth **one**, using conjugacy, a 5-point rationality grid and A\* — no nesting, and the tractability comes precisely from refusing it ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether nesting depth is a free architectural parameter or an artefact of a formalism that never has to pay for it. **The cost is now measured, and the answer is neither side's** ([[wiki/entities/autotom.md]], Zhang et al. 2025): recursion is run to **order 4** by replacing the nested posterior with a *single sampled world per level* — draw one state from `b(s)` at level `l` to stand in for level `l−1`, recurse to 0, then run ordinary inverse planning there. That makes depth linear rather than multiplicative, and the price is visible in accuracy rather than in compute: 95.0 → 75.0 → 70.0 → 67.5 → 55.0 across orders 0–4, at ~12K tokens per question against ~6.5K at depth 1. So A's recursion is runnable, B's tractability-by-refusal is not forced — and the amortisation objection lands exactly as stated, since what is computed at depth 4 is a one-sample estimate of the nested query and not the query

## Status

`LIVE` — but the cost question inside it is settled: sampled nesting is cheap and lossy, and nobody has run *exact* nesting past depth 1
