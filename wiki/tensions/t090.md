# T90 — Is a working-memory operation implemented by one controller, or by the conjunction of several subsystems each representing it differently?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Removal success is predicted from each community's pattern separately and jointly, the test the authors propose and the design cannot currently support.

## Position A

**One controller.** The frontoparietal control network is the only one of four cortical communities whose multi-voxel patterns separate all four operations (maintain / replace / suppress / clear), with pairwise classification 0.968–0.999 against 0.53–0.99 elsewhere; on this reading it *issues* the operation and the visual, somatomotor and default-mode patterns are downstream consequences of top-down drive — the machine-natural design, and the one the wiki's control/storage separation already assumes ([[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]], [[wiki/entities/differentiable-neural-computer.md]])

## Position B

**The conjunction.** Each community represents the operation set under a *different* quotient — visual cortex carries held vs. not-held, somatomotor cortex singles out `clear`, the default-mode network adds suppress ≠ clear — and these run in parallel, so the operation may be identified by the joint pattern (maintain = its visual + somatomotor + default-mode + frontoparietal signature) rather than by any one of them. The distinctions are not redundant copies: somatomotor cortex separates `clear` from `suppress` (0.762) better than visual cortex does (0.610), so a subsystem the controller would be "driving" carries a distinction its supposed driver's downstream targets do not share (DeRosa et al. 2024)

## Where it bites

Whether a machine store needs an operation *code* at one site or an operation *signature* distributed over its subsystems — and therefore where a removal can be verified. Under A, log the controller's emitted opcode; under B, no single site's state says which operation ran, and the wiki's whole practice of probing one layer for the control variable is mis-specified (gap G49, [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]])

## Status

`LIVE` — the authors state both readings and the design cannot decide: fMRI, cued operations, no manipulation, and no trial-level link between a community's pattern and whether the item was in fact removed. The discriminating test they propose is predicting removal success (classifier fit on the item itself, Kim et al. 2020) from each community's pattern separately and jointly
