# T162 — Does relational understanding require decoding back to the input space?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The other half of the cheap test is run — a JEPA with a sentence-embedding target against a generative classifier on image-side relational hard negatives, not only text-side.

## Position A

**No — reconstruction is unnecessary and actively harmful.** The JEPA lineage's founding commitment: predict in representation space, never in pixels, because a decoder forces the model to spend capacity on unpredictable detail; the wiki carries this as the argument behind [[wiki/entities/h-jepa.md]], [[wiki/entities/v-jepa-2.md]] and [[wiki/entities/lewm.md]], and [[wiki/concepts/violation-of-expectation.md]] supplies its strongest evidence — at matched protocol a pixel-space predictor (VideoMAEv2) sits at the untrained null where a representation-space predictor scores 98% on IntPhys. **And it now has a controlled within-method measurement** ([[wiki/entities/i-jepa.md]]): identical architecture, masking and dataset, target computed by the EMA encoder versus target computed in pixels, with the pixel arm given 800 epochs against 500 — 66.9 vs **40.7** on ImageNet-1% linear evaluation. It is the only place in the wiki where the commitment is isolated from every co-varying design choice. **Its instrument is now under challenge:** that number is a frozen linear-probe score, and the pixel arm's own paper shows the frozen protocol inverts method rankings — MoCo v3 beats MAE frozen and loses at every non-zero read-out depth ([[wiki/entities/mae.md]], T310). The ablation is still the only controlled one; whether +26.2 survives one fine-tuned block is unmeasured.

## Position B

**Yes, on exactly the ability at issue.** Conditional generative models used as classifiers by Bayes' rule — `p_θ(c|x) ∝ p(c)·p_θ(x|c)`, no retraining — **far outperform** discriminative joint-embedding models like CLIP on Winoground, the benchmark built to test relation and word-order understanding; they also show more shape bias, better human alignment, and better effective robustness (Bordes et al. 2024, [[wiki/concepts/cross-modal-grounding.md]]). The two positions are not evaluated on the same axis: the JEPA evidence is physical plausibility over time, the generative evidence is compositional structure within a frame. **A third data point sits between them and favours B on a narrow axis:** on Clevr object *counting*, the pixel-reconstruction MAE beats the same-scale I-JEPA 90.5 to 86.7, while on depth the two tie at 72.4 — so what latent targets discard is not uniformly irrelevant, and the discarded material includes some the wiki would call structural. **A third, within-method result prices the same claim directly** (He et al. 2022, [[wiki/entities/mae.md]]): replacing the pixel target with its top-96 patch-space PCA coefficients — i.e. keeping exactly the predictable, low-frequency part and deleting what a JEPA argues is wasted capacity — *costs* 0.3 fine-tune / 1.2 linear. On this dataset the unpredictable high-frequency band is a training signal, not a tax. Against it: the same paper's transfer table has MAE beating supervised and contrastive pre-training on COCO (53.3 vs 49.3 AP^box) and ADE20K (53.6 vs 49.1 mIoU) at ViT-L, which is the reconstruction pole winning on dense structural read-outs.

## Where it bites

Whether a reasoning architecture needs a decoder at all, or only for inspection. If B is right, the wiki's whole latent-prediction wave has an untested blind spot exactly where [[wiki/concepts/compositionality.md]] locates the deficit — the relation slot.

## Status

`LIVE` — and **half of the cheap test has now been run** (Chen et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/vl-jepa.md]]): a JEPA whose target is a *sentence embedding* beats CLIP, SigLIP2 and Perception Encoder on the text-only hard-negative triplets of SugarCrepe++ (63.9 vs 58.6) and VISLA (42.9 vs 40.4) from a 300M text encoder against their 537–708M, with the gain concentrated in the **swap** categories that perturb word order while holding the word bag fixed (Swap-Object 42.0 vs 13.5–30.6) and Replace-Relation at a tie. That is a non-generative model winning on the axis B owns — but it is a *text-only* protocol with no image in the loop, so it shows a better text encoder rather than better image↔relation binding, and Winoground and PUG (paired negative *image*) remain unrun by any JEPA. A generative classifier on IntPhys is still unrun. **(brainstorm)** A third reading dissolves it: what reconstruction supplies is not pixels but a *distortion term that prices arrangement* — moving two objects changes the pixels but barely changes a bag-of-objects code, and two arrangements of one scene are almost never each other's minibatch negative — in which case the repair is a paired negative *image*, not a decoder ([[wiki/concepts/energy-based-models.md]]).
