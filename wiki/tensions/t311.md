# T311 — Is non-normality how a contractive linear recurrence holds information longer than its eigenvalues suggest?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A non-normal and a diagonal state matrix are compared at matched spectrum, matched `n` and matched training budget on a task requiring >10⁴-step dependency, with transient amplification measured directly (e.g. `sup_k ‖A^k‖` against `ρ(A)^k`) rather than inferred from accuracy.

## Position A

**Yes.** The state matrices that work are the ones that are maximally non-normal. [[wiki/entities/s4.md]]'s Lemma 3.2: HiPPO's diagonaliser has entries `V_{3i,i} ≈ 2^{4i}`, up to `2^{4N/3}` — its eigendirections are catastrophically non-orthogonal, and its exact diagonalisation is numerically infeasible. A *normal* contraction decays at exactly `λ^k` in every direction; a non-normal one can transiently amplify along non-orthogonal eigendirections with every eigenvalue strictly inside the unit disc. Under this reading, S4's whole `Õ(N+L)` NPLR/Woodbury/Cauchy apparatus exists to *keep* a property that is doing the work, and [[wiki/entities/simple-cycle-reservoir.md]]'s unitary dilation — which buys perfect conditioning at state cost `n' ≈ (N+1)nk` — has thrown away the memory mechanism to get it. Ablation support: S4's own random-*diagonal*-Gaussian arm "does not close the gap" on sequential CIFAR-10.

## Position B

**No — and at matched initialisation spectrum non-normality is a net loss.** [[wiki/entities/lru.md]] runs the controlled version S4 did not. Take the dense real Glorot linear RNN; by the strong circular law (Thm 1) its spectrum is uniform on the unit disc in the wide limit; sample a *diagonal* `Λ` from that same uniform-on-the-disc measure (Lemma 2 with `r_min=0, r_max=1`), so the two arms differ in **non-normality alone and not in spectrum**. Result: sCIFAR **72.2 → 86.5**, ListOps **50.4 → 58.8**, and ~8× faster training. The full diagonal model then reaches Path-X **94.2%** and a six-task Long Range Arena average of **86.3** against S4's 86.09 — 16,384 steps carried by `N` independent scalar complex exponentials, every one of them normal. What replaces the alleged transient-amplification mechanism is two scalars: eigenvalue magnitude near 1, and eigenvalue phase restricted to `[0, π/10]` (Orvieto et al. 2023).

## Where it bites

Whether the wiki's linear-recurrence design variable is a **spectrum** or a **spectrum plus a conditioning number**. If A, then the operative quantity is `κ(V)` and every diagonal SSM in the wiki ([[wiki/entities/ms-ssm.md]], S4D, S5, LRU) is trading capability for tractability, and SCR's dilation route is actively harmful. If B, the design object collapses to a **measure over the unit disc** — two ranges — and the entire NPLR/Woodbury machinery, plus SCR's `(N+1)nk` blow-up, are both solving a problem that does not exist. B also re-prices T174: if the state matrix can be diagonal without loss, "topology" was never a free parameter to argue about, only a spectrum.

## Status

`LEANING` toward B. B is the better-controlled experiment — it holds the initialisation spectrum fixed by construction (strong circular law ⇒ Lemma 2) where A's supporting ablation varies spectrum and normality together, and S4's random-diagonal arm was drawn from a *Gaussian*, not from the matched uniform-on-the-disc measure, so it does not isolate normality either. Two things keep the row open rather than closed. (i) B loses Path-X to S4D by 3.3 points and sCIFAR by 2.5 — the two hardest columns — so a residual non-normal advantage at the extreme is not excluded, only shown to be small. (ii) Neither side measures transient amplification directly; both argue from accuracy. The `sup_k ‖A^k‖ / ρ(A)^k` measurement is cheap on a fitted S4 and has not been run.
