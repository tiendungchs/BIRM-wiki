# T161 — Does a stable signature failure profile identify an *installed* prior?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The one runnable sweep: vary context length and framerate at fixed objective, data and scale, and see whether solidity and collision move. A bottleneck moves; a structural profile does not.

## Position A

**Yes — that is the identification criterion.** A core system is identified not by its content but by a quantitative failure profile held constant across ages, species, cultures and tasks: content can be mimicked by learning, a limit that does not move cannot. The wiki adopted this as G17's second instrument — certify a prior by a constant (capacity, Weber ratio, entry threshold) that survives scale, retraining and domain shift, plus a resource-stress probe (Spelke & Kinzler 2007, [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]])

## Position B

**No — a learned model with no prior reproduces the diagnostic.** V-JEPA has no installed abstraction of any kind and its intuitive-physics profile is sharp and *invariant*: object-intrinsic properties (permanence `g = 9.0`, continuity `g = 11.0`, shape `g = 8.1`) large, object-against-world properties (support, gravity, inertia) moderate, **object–object interaction (solidity, collision) and colour constancy at chance** — and the same partition holds across a 115M→ViT-H scaling range, three pretraining objectives, three corpora and a 100× change in unique-data volume, with its errors *correlated with human errors* in the occluded conditions. What actually pins the profile is architectural resource — a 3–4 s memory window and a framerate — which no scaling or retraining lever touches (Garrido et al. 2025, [[wiki/concepts/violation-of-expectation.md]])

## Where it bites

G17's second instrument, and every "architecture X has prior P" claim it was meant to adjudicate. If a capacity bottleneck produces the same invariance as an installed prior, constancy-under-perturbation is necessary and not sufficient, and the wiki's cheapest certification protocol certifies nothing on its own. It also cuts the other way, at biology: the same argument applies to infants, whose signature limits could likewise index a resource rather than a module

## Status

`LIVE` — and the discriminating experiment is one sweep, already runnable on the ingested model: vary the **resource** the failure could be attributed to (context length, framerate) while holding objective, data and scale fixed. If solidity and collision move, it was a bottleneck; if they do not, the profile survives as evidence of something structural. Nobody has run it on any model or, in the resource-parametric form, on any infant
