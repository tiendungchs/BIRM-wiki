# T24 — Does an adaptation curve measure learning?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

Implicitly assumed throughout the wiki and by the whole few-shot/meta-learning literature: rate of behavioural improvement is the observable that stands in for structure acquisition, and differences in that rate are differences in learning rate (savings, anterograde interference, environmental-consistency effects were all read this way)

## Position B

**No.** Behaviour change decomposes into *proper* learning (a stored state moved) and *apparent* learning (only the posterior weights on unchanged stored states moved). Simulated with parameters fit to *other* experiments, COIN reproduces savings, anterograde interference and the consistency effect with Kalman gains and states essentially unchanged — the entire effect sits in the context probabilities (Heald et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/coin-model.md]])

## Where it bites

Gap G17 and [[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]]: every conversion-rate score read off a learning curve, including in-context-learning curves, confounds acquiring structure with re-weighting stored structure

## Status

`LEANING` toward B in the motor domain — evoked recovery is a confirmed novel prediction (ΔBIC 394.1 nats over the dual-rate model) and the decomposition has not been tested outside sensorimotor adaptation
