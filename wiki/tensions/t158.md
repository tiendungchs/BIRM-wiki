# T158 — When a model is shown to use its world model, does it use it on every input?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The candidate cheap features (`Is-Surrounded-By-Mine`, `Is-Border`) are ablated and end-game accuracy shown to fall — named in the source and not done.

## Position A

**Yes — that is what a causal intervention establishes.** Edit the probed board state and the model's legal-move predictions follow it, on 1,000 test cases and even on boards unreachable by legal play; the representation is therefore on the computational path from input to output (Li et al. 2022; Nanda et al. 2023)

## Position B

**No — the intervention score is an average over cases, and at least two circuits are running.** In the same model, from roughly move 30 onward, the correct legal moves are increasingly often resolved at an *earlier layer* than the correct board state (`MoveFirst`), and board-probe accuracy degrades in end games — while the board intervention still works there (0.112 errors). Reading: late in the game most squares are filled and most empty squares are legal, so legality is predictable from cheap features (`Empty` ∧ local ones) with no board needed, and the cheap circuit finishes first

## Where it bites

Decides what "emergent world model, causally verified" buys. If B holds, a passed intervention test licenses "the model *can* use it" and not "the model's answer came from it" — so a system can be certified to hold and use a latent graph and still route most queries around it, which is [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]] surviving the strongest available internal test. It also makes per-input circuit attribution, not per-dataset probe accuracy, the quantity an architecture argument needs

## Status

`LEANING B` — B's evidence is internal to A's own model and A has no counter-measurement; what would settle it is ablating the candidate cheap features (`Is-Surrounded-By-Mine`, `Is-Border`) and showing end-game accuracy falls, which is named and not done ([[wiki/entities/othellogpt.md]], [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]])
