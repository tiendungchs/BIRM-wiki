# T399 — Is the fall in exploration with age a maturational schedule, or just a sharpening posterior?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A belief-driven exploration rate (uncertainty, ensemble disagreement, or learning progress) is fitted to the child / school-age / adolescent / adult hypothesis-search data and tested on the **domain reversal** — if it reproduces the physical-cool / social-hot crossover at adolescence with no per-domain schedule term, B; if the crossover requires an exogenous per-domain phase offset, A.

## Position A

**A schedule, carried by its own maturational variable.** Gopnik 2020 ([[wiki/concepts/explore-exploit-division-of-labour.md]]): explore and exploit are antagonistic *capacities* with a timed handover, not one selector at two temperatures. The evidence offered is that the profile is (i) cross-species (juvenile mice, great tits, hyenas, capuchins, crows, rodents approaching an informative shock cue), (ii) *causally* tied to prefrontal control rather than to knowledge — interference with prefrontal control areas widens divergent-thinking responses (Thompson-Schill et al. 2009; Chrysikou et al. 2013) — and (iii) **non-monotone by domain**: adolescents stop inferring unlikely *physical* hypotheses like adults, while inferring unlikely *social* hypotheses more than either school-age children or adults (Gopnik et al. 2017). No account in which exploration is a function of accumulated evidence predicts a re-heat in one domain while another cools.

## Position B

**A readout of the belief state, with no schedule anywhere.** Every exploration mechanism the wiki carries derives the rate from what the agent currently holds: value-landscape readouts (Doya 2002; Ishii et al. 2002, [[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]]), world-model ensemble disagreement ([[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]]), learning progress ([[wiki/concepts/learning-progress.md]]), epistemic terms over beliefs ([[wiki/concepts/epistemic-value.md]]). Under all of them, exploration falls because the posterior sharpens — which is Gopnik's own *first* stated reason for explore-first ("the learner is likely to start out knowing less"), before she argues for a maturational division of labour. Domain asymmetry is then a fact about how much evidence each domain has received, and adolescence is the point where the social environment changes rather than where a clock fires.

## Where it bites

Whether a machine needs a maturational variable at all. Under B, `G61` is the whole problem — make the rate an output of the learned selector and nothing else is needed. Under A, `G61` is unclosable as written: no state-indexed readout can produce a re-heat, so the architecture needs `G100`'s per-module phase offsets wired to a *search temperature*, and the explore phase additionally needs its extrinsic objective switched off rather than down-weighted. The two also disagree about what a learning trap is: under B a transient the agent escapes once uncertainty rises, under A a permanent property of the cooled phase, because the committed policy stops generating the observations that would raise that uncertainty.

## Status

`LIVE` — A's domain-reversal datum is the only evidence that discriminates, it is one result in one laboratory, and no B-style model has been fitted to it.
