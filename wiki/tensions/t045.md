# T45 — Is the hippocampal map amodal, or biased to space?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Kumaran's matched design is repeated with a *newly learned* social network, separating the domain effect from the map-versus-memory schedule of T28.

## Position A

**Amodal relational processing.** The relational theory has the hippocampus linking any associations into large frameworks, space being one instance (Cohen & Eichenbaum 1993; Eichenbaum 2004). The wiki's model side assumes this throughout: TEM learns the same structural code for family trees and for 2-D rooms ([[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]]), and the "beyond space" table of [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]] lists concept, word, gaze, social-hierarchy and discrete-transition spaces, including hippocampal *angle* coding of a power × affiliation social space (Tavares et al. 2015). The best-controlled positive is non-spatial, discrete and incidental: bilateral entorhinal adaptation scaling with distance on an implicitly learned object graph, with the graph recoverable by MDS and a matching behavioural RT effect in a separate cohort (Garvert et al. 2017)

## Position B

**Spatially biased.** In the only design that matches relational demand and swaps only the domain — chain-search over the subject's own friends by physical proximity vs by acquaintance, matched on performance, difficulty, reaction time, episodic/emotional/self/imagery ratings — the hippocampus was active for the spatial version only, showed **no** activation for the social version even against a vowel-counting baseline at p < 0.01 uncorrected, and was the *single* region showing the domain × task interaction. Relational demand alone (main effect of task) produced no hippocampal voxels; spatial content alone (non-relational contrast) produced parahippocampal/retrosplenial/parietal but not hippocampal activation. Social navigation instead engaged the mentalising network (medial prefrontal, superior temporal sulcus, temporoparietal junction, temporal poles) (Kumaran & Maguire 2005)

## Where it bites

Decides whether "cognitive map" is the right frame for a general reasoning substrate or a spatial specialisation the wiki has been over-extrapolating. If B, then a model that reuses one map module for spatial and non-spatial relational inference is *not* mirroring the human architecture, and the non-spatial half of abstract reasoning may need a second, differently-organised system — which is what the social-cognition network looks like here. Bites [[wiki/concepts/abstract-structural-codes.md]] (T4, T37) and the transfer claim behind `g`

## Status

`LIVE` — B is one 18-subject fMRI study but the only matched control in the literature the wiki holds; A rests on many unmatched contrasts plus animal recordings. Two unexcluded reconciliations: (i) familiarity — Kumaran's social network was years old while positive social-map results used newly-learned spaces, so this may be the map-vs-memory schedule (T28) rather than a domain effect, though the spatial framework was equally old; Garvert et al. 2017 sharpens this — a one-day-old, non-spatial, discrete graph does drive entorhinal cortex — so recency plus novelty currently predicts a hippocampal-formation map better than spatiality does; (ii) fMRI nulls are weak evidence, and BOLD in a task where the answer is retrieved rather than computed may miss a code that is present ([[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]]); (iii) **the abstract dimension may ride on the spatial one rather than replace it** — mouse dorsal CA1 cells have firing fields in a 2-D accumulated-evidence × position space, but ~90% of them are *conjunctive*, so the non-spatial variable is carried by the same cells that carry space and was accumulated while navigating. That is consistent with both poles at once: the map takes non-perceptual dimensions (A) and it was never asked to do so without space present (B). The experiment T45 needs is the abstract variable with the navigation removed (Nieh et al. 2021, [[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]); (iv) **the recency reading now has a matched-domain positive.** Park et al. 2021 ran a *social* space built inside the experiment (two 1-D rank orders over 16 people, learned on separate days) and recovered hippocampal and entorhinal 2-D distance coding plus a grid-like code for inferred vectors — in the same subjects whose mentalising network (temporoparietal junction, superior temporal sulcus, dorsal medial prefrontal cortex) was engaged and *shared the entorhinal grid orientation*. So position B's regions and position A's regions are not alternatives in a newly learned social space; they are co-aligned. This does not rescue A for over-learned structure, and it does not supply a matched *spatial* control, but it moves the live variable from spatial-vs-social toward old-vs-new and removes the reading in which social relational inference is handled by a second, differently-organised system
