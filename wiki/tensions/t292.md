# T292 — The ape null on shared intentionality: is the representation absent, or present and undeployed?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** An ape is put in a frame where declarative attention-directing pays — no such experiment is yet reported, and the two positions currently share their evidence base.

## Position A

**Absent.** Tomasello & Rakoczy 2007 grant apes everything the competitive paradigms showed — intention-in-action read from perceptible effort (Call et al. 2002), tracking of what a dominant can and did see, individuated by identity across a swap control (Hare et al. 2000, 2001) — and argue the missing thing is not deployed anywhere, in any frame: no declarative pointing or showing in any population, no pretense, no shared symbol used to take a perspective, and no sign that a subordinate treats the dominant's view as its own from a different angle. A motivational gate predicts a frame in which the behaviour appears; for self–other equivalence none has been found

## Position B

**Present and undeployed.** [[wiki/concepts/motivation-representation-synergy.md]] reads the *same* Hare experiments as the precedent case for exactly this error: the twenty-year chimpanzee theory-of-mind negative inverted when the frame changed from cooperation-with-a-human to competition-with-a-conspecific, and within-species tolerance manipulations (dispersed vs. monopolisable reward) move measured cooperation with cognition held fixed. On that reading the declarative-pointing null is a *cooperative-communication* null — the one frame the species is known to fail for motivational reasons — and captive apes do point imperatively to humans, i.e. the act is in the repertoire and its cooperative use is not

## Where it bites

Decides which of two components a builder owes: an equivalence constraint tying perception to action (`G103`), or a disposition term gating deployment (`G102`). They are not substitutes — the first changes what is representable, the second what gets emitted — and the wiki has neither. Sibling of `T291`, which states the general measurement problem; this row is its hardest instance, because here the *same two experiments* are cited by both sides. Note the asymmetry that makes the row decidable in principle: the motivational reading makes a positive prediction (find the frame), the representational reading makes only a negative one Carried by [[wiki/concepts/shared-intentionality.md]], [[wiki/concepts/motivation-representation-synergy.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/emergent-modularity.md]]

## Status

`LIVE` — no experiment yet reported that puts an ape in a frame where declarative attention-directing pays; the two positions currently differ in interpretation of a shared evidence base rather than in the evidence
