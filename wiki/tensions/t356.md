# T356 — Does a homeostatic need drive behaviour by signalling a deficit to be reduced, or by potentiating the positive value of the outcome?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The two candidate populations are put through the *same* two assays in one preparation. (i) Closed-loop place conditioning: A predicts lateral-hypothalamic stimulation conditions place **preference** where arcuate AGRP stimulation conditions **avoidance** — already reported separately, never side by side. (ii) The discriminating one, and it is unrun: progressive-ratio responding for food with the population held stimulated *through* consumption. Under B the responding decays to the floor, most steeply at high effort ratios, because ingestion can no longer lower the signal; under A it stays elevated, because the signal is a multiplier on the outcome's worth and consumption does not consume it. A mixed result — decay in one population and not the other — says the two populations implement different schemes and the general question is malformed.

## Position A

**A potentiator of the outcome's positive value.** The need state raises how good food *is*, and the animal learns and works more because the reinforcer got bigger. Evidence: lateral hypothalamic stimulation elicits avid food consumption, has **positive** valence, and facilitates both Pavlovian and instrumental learning (Margules & Olds 1962; Hoebel & Teitelbaum 1962, as cited by Betley et al. 2015); food deprivation potentiates the reinforcing efficacy of nutritive food. [[wiki/concepts/incentive-salience.md]] is the modern form — `wanting(cue,t) = f(V̂(cue), state(t))`, appetite entering as a **multiplicative state term** on a cue's learned association — and every architecture in the wiki is a degenerate version of A, since `r` is a property of the outcome and the state can only scale it. **A's closed form and its strongest datum** (Zhang et al. 2009): `Ṽ(s_t) = κ·r_t + γV̂(s_{t+1})` with `κ` set by physiology and denied access to `V` and `Q`; rats trained that a tone predicted an *aversive* 3×-seawater salt solution, then made sodium-deficient overnight — a state they had never experienced — showed ventral-pallidum firing to that tone rise to equal or exceed firing to a sucrose cue **on its first presentation, in extinction, with the salt never re-tasted**. A need state therefore re-values a cue with no prediction error available and no re-experience of the outcome, which is the case A is built for and which a deficit-reduction reward has to explain by some other route.

## Position B

**A deficit signal whose reduction is the reinforcement.** The need state is a negative-valence quantity carried by a dedicated population, and preference is conditioned by its *fall*, not by the outcome's worth ([[wiki/concepts/homeostatic-need-signal.md]], Betley et al. 2015). Evidence: AGRP photostimulation devalues a paired flavour and conditions closed-loop place avoidance; AGRP silencing in food-restricted mice conditions flavour **and** place preference with no food present; AGRP activity falls at food-predicting cues before any nutrient and the fall is learned; and — the direct test — holding AGRP stimulation on through consumption, so that eating cannot relieve the signal, drives progressive-ratio responding down to the un-stimulated floor with high-effort ratios hit hardest, while the food itself remains unchanged and *ad libitum* intake is unaltered. The same design appears on a second need: subforniceal NOS1 stimulation elicits drinking and conditions place avoidance.

## Where it bites

**Whether `r` is stored on the outcome or computed from an internal state at consumption time.** Under A the reward function is a lookup scaled by a state register — the wiki's universal design, and `G56`'s gain register is all that is missing. A also makes the row's closing test explicit rather than merely predicting its outcome: a multiplier is not spent by consumption, so under `Ṽ = κ·r + γV̂` an unrelievable need holds progressive-ratio responding *up*, which is the opposite of what was measured. Under B there is no reward function: `r = −Δ(need)` is measured per consumption, which makes devaluation an *intervention on the internal variable* rather than on the outcome, and makes the agent's value ordering change with no learning and no gain change. The two also differ in what is architecturally denied. A cannot express an outcome that becomes worthless while remaining identical and while the agent remains motivated — the progressive-ratio result. B cannot straightforwardly express a reward that has no corresponding regulated variable, which is most of what an artificial agent is trained on.

The evidence classes are asymmetric and that is why the row survives. A's behavioural half is decades of deprivation-potentiation data plus the lateral-hypothalamic self-stimulation literature, none of it recorded from an identified need population, and its one electrophysiological result (Zhang et al. 2009) records a *downstream* structure (ventral pallidum) under a whole-animal depletion rather than any identified need cell; B's is one paper with cell-type-specific manipulation, imaging and a falsification test, on a population A's literature never examined. `G116` bites both: place preference and avoidance are approach/avoid measures, so neither position's valence claim is separated from an action claim by the assays used.

## Status

`LIVE` — the source itself proposes the reconciliation, that negative and positive reinforcement "operate in a concerted push-pull manner" under homeostatic deficit, with hormones (ghrelin, leptin, angiotensin) coordinating both. That is stated and not measured: no experiment in the wiki varies the two contributions independently or says what arbitrates them, so "both" is currently a third unevidenced position rather than a settlement.
