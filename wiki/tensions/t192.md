# T192 — What licenses a candidate analogical inference — its coherence with the target, or a causal model carried over from the source?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Causal structure and coherence are varied independently in one analogical-inference test, since Lassaline 1996 shows they can be made to disagree.

## Position A

**Coherence.** [[wiki/concepts/analogical-mapping.md]]'s acceptance test accepts a copy-with-substitution hypothesis `h` iff `sim(C_h, C_target-episode) > sim(C_h, C_s)` for every mapped sub-element `s` — the hypothesis must fit the whole target better than any of its parts. Computed from the same codes as the mapping, needs no external checker, no causal annotation and no domain knowledge, and it removed every distractor in the Water-Flow/Heat-Flow case for free (Slipchenko & Rachkovskij 2009)

## Position B

**A causal model, and similarity is actively misleading.** Analogical inference strength tracks the source's cause–effect structure, not overall resemblance: a stated *causal* link between two source properties strengthens the transferred inference over a mere conjunction, and weakening the link to "develops before" shrinks the boost (Lassaline 1996); most sharply, when the source shows an effect *despite* a preventive cause, people judge the effect **more** likely in a target that **lacks** the preventer — the inference strengthens while source–target similarity falls (Lee & Holyoak 2008). Holyoak, Lee & Lu 2010 formalise it: the source's causal network plus the mapping is the input to copy-with-substitution, and the output is an elaborated *causal model* of the target, queried afterwards for arbitrary inferences

## Where it bites

Decides what analogical transfer moves and what a model must store per episode. Under A an episode is a bag of relations and inference is a coherence filter — cheap, self-contained, and it will accept a plausible-looking transfer that no causal structure supports while rejecting the preventer case outright. Under B every episode must carry an annotated causal graph, which no wiki mechanism discovers ([[wiki/concepts/causal-model-building.md]], G16) — so B is right about the phenomenon and has no story about where its input comes from, while A runs today on representations that are themselves hand-authored
