# T114 — Does behavioural guidance by a feature imply an attentional gain signal for that feature in sensory cortex?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The deflations are tested: V4 shape tuning resolution at 4–5 dva, and whether the shape match is computed in IT/TEO instead. A null is not a dissociation. Sibling of T279.

## Position A

**Yes, by assumption** — the priority map is `sim(template, stimulus(ℓ))` over the cued features, and feature-match enhancement in V4 is the read-out of that similarity; both cued dimensions should enhance matching items ([[wiki/concepts/priority-map.md]])

## Position B

**No.** In 20-item colour×shape conjunction search, monkeys fixate same-**shape** distractors far above chance (`t` = 21.9 / 11.2, `P` < 10⁻⁹ / 10⁻⁷) yet V4 shows **no significant enhancement** for a same-shape distractor in control *or* inactivation sessions, while the same-**colour** enhancement is present and is abolished by VPA inactivation (Bichot et al. 2019)

## Where it bites

Whether a model can be validated by behavioural guidance curves at all: if shape guides the scan without a shape-match gain at the recorded stage, then the priority map is either computed on a dimension the recorded area does not express, or assembled somewhere other than where the gain is measured — and matching a model's attention weights to fixation statistics would certify the wrong mechanism

## Status

`LIVE` — plausible deflations: V4's shape tuning at 4–5 dva eccentricity may be too coarse for these shapes, the shape match may be computed in IT/TEO instead, or the target-conjunction enhancement may swamp single-feature effects; none is tested here, and a null is not a dissociation **Sibling row (unlinked until the 284-source duplicate audit): T279** asks the same inference — does evidence of feature guidance license a feature-tuned gain signal — from *decoding* evidence rather than from behavioural-guidance evidence. T279's near-random-connectivity model is a mechanism by which T114's negative result would be the general case rather than a shape-specific exception.
