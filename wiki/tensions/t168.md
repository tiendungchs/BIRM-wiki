# T168 — Is collapse-avoidance a property a design *has*, or a state a training run must be *kept in*?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**A property, certified once from the objective.** The wiki's entire treatment of gap G34 catalogues *provisions* — negatives, variance + covariance, distribution matching, an EMA asymmetry, teacher-output normalisation — and ranks them by coefficient count and by whether the loss's minimum can be stated and checked non-collapsed (T164). Nothing in that framing is time-dependent: a design either carries a provision or it does not, and the check is made against the objective, not against a checkpoint.

## Position B

**A state, and the certification does not cover the read-outs the objective is silent about.** DINOv3's ViT-7B trains 1M iterations on one stationary distribution with `L_DINO + L_iBOT + 0.1·L_KoLeo`: no term diverges, no encoder goes constant, patch norms stay stable (the registers work), and the ImageNet linear probe rises **monotonically throughout** — while a linear probe on the *patch* tokens peaks at ~200k iterations and ends **below its own early value**, visible as a steadily rising CLS↔patch cosine similarity. The masked-patch term that DINOv2's ablation showed *buys* dense structure is present and being minimised the whole time and does not *hold* it, because it constrains each patch and not the relations among them. The same decline appears in the ViT-g and, worse, in an independent 7B DINO scaling effort (Siméoni et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/dinov3.md]]). **And a second, non-temporal counterexample to the same certification** ([[wiki/entities/hit-jepa.md]]): in a three-level trajectory JEPA carrying SmoothL1 in representation space *plus* VICReg variance and covariance terms at every level, changing only the channel between levels — upsampled embeddings concatenated downward instead of upsampled attention matrices — degrades retrieval by a factor of **465**, with the objective, the coefficients, the branches and the data untouched. The rank scales linearly with database size, so the true match sits at a fixed ~0.5 percentile: coarse neighbourhood structure survives, within-neighbourhood ordering does not. What the objective certifies is each branch's embedding; what collapsed is a representation *assembled across* branches, which no term scores (Li et al. 2025). **The monitor B implies now exists, and it has not met the failure** (Balestriero & LeCun 2025, [[wiki/entities/lejepa.md]]): a JEPA whose training-loss value is `ρ_s ≈ 0.85`–`0.99` correlated with downstream accuracy across architectures, datasets and budgets is exactly the label-free per-run signal B's monitoring requirement needs — but the correlation is measured against **global** linear probes, which is the read-out that rose monotonically through DINOv3's dense collapse, and its longest run is 100 ImageNet-1k epochs against DINOv3's 1M iterations. So B's requirement has an instrument and no evidence the instrument sees the failure that motivated it.

## Where it bites

Whether an anti-collapse audit is a design review or a monitoring requirement, and what a training run's **stopping rule** is. If B holds: (i) certification must be stated *per read-out*, and the field's default read-out is the one that says train longer; (ii) training horizon becomes a design variable with no coefficient in any objective, alongside I-JEPA's pair sampler (T167) and now the inter-level wiring of a stack (HiT-JEPA) — three of the largest levers in the family are outside the loss; (iii) the fix generalises as a *shape* — a term on the **relations** between elements, anchored to the model's own earlier iterate, which repairs 800k iterations of erosion in 10k and is the wiki's nearest object to a trainable `g`-objective (G30, [[wiki/concepts/abstract-structural-codes.md]]). Home page for this row: [[wiki/concepts/representational-collapse.md]].

## Status

`LEANING B` — the two supporting results share no model family, modality, objective or failure mode, which is what makes A's "certify once from the loss" untenable in general; but each is thin on its own. DINOv3: one model family, one modality, and the degradation is reported as training curves on two benchmarks rather than as a controlled ablation (`w_Gram` is never swept). But the observation is internally controlled — same run, same data, same loss, two probes moving in opposite directions — and it reproduces across two model sizes and one independent scaling effort, which is more support than most rows here carry. A also survives in its narrow reading: nothing *collapsed* in the sense the taxonomy defines, which is exactly the point — the taxonomy's predicate is too coarse to see this. **And the strongest case for B no longer needs a self-supervised objective to suspect** (Akbar 2026, [[wiki/entities/lejepa.md]] §Part 8): a ViT minimising plain supervised cross-entropy — an objective with *no* constant-encoder solution, since the label term prices it — collapses to 20.73% under AdamW with aggressive augmentation, and a covariance-isotropy term restores it to 72.02%. If a run can degenerate under an objective whose minimum is certifiably non-collapsed, collapse-avoidance cannot be a property read off the loss at all. `(tentative)` — collapse is inferred from accuracy, with no spectrum measurement reported.
