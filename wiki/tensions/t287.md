# T287 — Must a compound representation stay decomposable into its constituents?

**Status:** `BOTH`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The separating variable is applied as a design rule: preserve decomposability where meaning is a function of the parts, destroy it where it is not. The row closes when an architecture does both by policy.

## Position A

**Yes — decomposability is the point.** Vector-symbolic binding defines a compound as an *invertible* function of its parts: `bind(role, filler)` can be unbound to recover the filler, superposition keeps a compound similar to the items it contains, and every wiki architecture that manipulates structure ([[wiki/concepts/vector-symbolic-binding.md]], [[wiki/entities/arc-vsa-solver.md]], [[wiki/entities/lisa.md]]) depends on that invertibility to generalise to novel combinations of familiar parts.

## Position B

**No — the model pays to destroy it.** In Pythia 2.8B the input to fact lookup is literally `emb_first(" Michael") + emb_last(" Jordan")` — two vocabulary-wide lookup tables with *disjoint ranges*, so the sum is still fully decomposable and position-tagged at the point it enters the MLPs (Rajamanoharan et al. 2023) — and MLPs 2–6 break that additive structure 60–70% of the way to fully broken where shuffled weights reach only 20–40%. The reason is stated as a requirement, not an accident: a representation that stayed similar to its parts would license `Michael Duncan plays basketball`. The attributes of the compound are then read by directions that have no relation to the constituent tokens (Nanda et al. 2023, [[wiki/concepts/multi-token-embedding.md]]).

## Where it bites

Whether an entity representation should be *built* (composed from parts, invertible, generalising to unseen combinations) or *looked up* (hashed, non-invertible, defined only where a value was written). A reasoner needs both on the same substrate: `Michael Duncan` must be representable as a novel person **and** must not inherit basketball. Nothing in the wiki does both — the VSB side has no mechanism for suppressing false inheritance, and the lookup side has no entry for an entity it never saw.

## Status

`BOTH`, and the separating variable is named: decomposability is wanted where the compound's *meaning* is a function of its parts (a scene, a relation, an ARC transformation) and destructive where it is **not** (a proper name, an arbitrary label, any pure-memorisation mapping — [[wiki/concepts/memorisation-vs-generalisation.md]]). The unrun test is whether one network can hold both regimes at once, i.e. whether a trained model breaks linear structure *more* on names than on compositional phrases at the same depth — the same measurement already in hand, re-run on a compositional control set
