# T243 — Do between-subject differences in a functional connectome measure coupling, or where the regions are?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Areal-feature-based cross-subject registration is applied and the individual-difference results re-run, showing how much survives when topography is absorbed into the warp.

## Position A

**Coupling** — the operative assumption behind every connectome-based individual-difference result, fingerprint, behavioural prediction and clinical biomarker in the literature the wiki draws on: an edge weight is the strength of a connection between two named regions, and its variation across people is variation in that connection.

## Position B

**Substantially topography.** Bijsterbosch et al. 2018, 2019 and Li et al. 2019 (in Bijsterbosch et al. 2021): between-subject differences in the parcellated connectome are *mixed* with between-subject differences in the spatial topography of networks — a parcel boundary in the wrong place for this individual draws its mean time series from partly different tissue, and the difference is reported as connectivity. Substantial at network level; and individual differences in **spatial overlap** of PROFUMO modes predicted behaviour better than differences in temporal correlation. Area size, shape and position are proposed as biomarkers in their own right.

## Where it bites

Every claim in the wiki that reads a connectome difference as a difference in coupling — [[wiki/entities/fcann.md]]'s clinical contrasts most directly. Generally: any architecture that re-parses each episode into its own units before relating them cannot separate *where a node is* from *how strongly it couples* from the edge matrix alone ([[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]])

## Status

`LEANING B` — the mitigation is structural rather than statistical (areal-feature-based cross-subject registration absorbs size/shape/position into the warp, so it stops leaking into the edges), which concedes the confound is real ([[wiki/concepts/node-definition-problem.md]])
