# T252 — Is the direction of large-scale cortical flow fixed by anatomy or set dynamically?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Flow direction is manipulated by task while the connectome is held fixed, showing whether the anatomical instrength gradient can be overridden and by what.

## Position A

**Fixed by anatomy.** White matter changes on a timescale of months to years, the human connectome's instrength gradient (temporal/parietal → frontal/occipital) is significant in every one of 776 subjects and replicates across three further cohorts and parcellations, and in a 1000-region model on that connectome *every* emerging wave follows it across four frequency bands, conduction speeds of 1–10 m/s and five orders of magnitude of coupling gain. Empirically, alpha and theta ECoG waves run parietal/temporal → frontal/occipital at rest *and* during a memory task, i.e. the same direction under two states (Koller et al. 2024, [[wiki/concepts/cortical-traveling-waves.md]])

## Position B

**Set dynamically, and by something else.** Traveling-wave direction changes rapidly with task in several M/EEG studies, resting M/EEG alpha waves travel along the anterior–posterior axis with *varying* direction across studies, and infra-slow fMRI waves in humans and gamma band-limited-power waves in macaque follow the **principal functional connectivity gradient** — a different map from the instrength gradient. In the same modelling paper, a ±0.75 Hz local intrinsic-frequency detuning is sufficient to reverse the direction over unchanged wiring

## Where it bites

Whether a builder can bake routing direction into fixed weights or needs a dynamic controller — and the answer decides which variable is exposed at runtime. The source's own reconciliation is that both are true at different frequencies and timescales: a stable instrength gradient supplies the default, local frequency modulation (a stimulus accelerating a receiving area, or thalamocortical preparation) supplies the override, and which one wins depends on band — instrength-consistent in alpha/theta, functional-gradient-consistent in infra-slow and gamma. That is a hypothesis, not a result: no study measures both gradients and wave direction in the same data, and the ECoG-vs-M/EEG disagreement may be spatial resolution and signal mixing rather than physiology. **The decisive experiment**: compute the instrength gradient and the principal functional gradient in the same subjects and regress band-resolved wave flow potentials against both

## Status

`LIVE`

**An anatomical boundary condition, added at the Markov et al. 2014 ingest** ([[wiki/concepts/counterstream-organization.md]]). The macaque visual connectome fitted by SLN gives the fixed-anatomy position its sharpest form and its own stated limit in one sentence from the authors: *"one could speculate that the functional hierarchy is dynamic and task-dependent, with the structural hierarchy providing boundary values."* That is Position A as a **range** rather than a direction — the wiring sets an interval, the task picks a point inside it. Two measurements make the interval concrete: fitted hierarchical levels carry ±1–2 level confidence intervals, and interareal Granger-causal influence measured during attention is reported to correlate with SLN (Vezoli et al.), i.e. the dynamic estimate tracks the anatomical one on average without being pinned by it. The paper also supplies the mechanism by which a dynamic override could be compartment-selective rather than areal: gamma is supragranular and beta infragranular, and every short-to-medium-range pathway mixes both compartments, so a band-specific change in drive reweights the two counterstreams inside an unchanged anatomical pair. This does not decide the row — the head-to-head on one hierarchy is `raw/bastos-2015-feedforward-feedback-frequency-channels.md`, not yet ingested — but it moves the decisive experiment from "can direction be overridden" to "how wide is the interval the structure leaves open".


**The head-to-head the previous paragraph called for has now been run, on the macaque visual hierarchy, and it splits the answer by area** (Bastos et al. 2015, [[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]]). Directed influence measured per frequency and correlated against the fixed anatomy (SLN, from a different set of 25 animals) yields a functional hierarchy from dynamics alone, and it is compared with the anatomical one across three task epochs of the *same* connectome:

- **Postcue `R = 0.93`, precue `R = 0.91`, prestimulus not significant (`p = 0.2`).** The anatomical hierarchy is recovered when the task is running and is not recovered at rest — so the two do come apart, and the variable that separates them is task state.
- **Posterior areas do not move.** V1 → V2 → V4 → TEO → DP → 7A hold their order in all three epochs.
- **Frontal areas do.** 8L drops in the precue period; both 8L and 8m fall to the bottom of the functional hierarchy before stimulus onset.
- **The named actuator.** Because gamma is supragranular and beta infragranular and every short-to-medium-range pathway mixes both compartments ([[wiki/concepts/counterstream-organization.md]]), driving an area's superficial layers raises its gamma output and moves it *down*; driving its deep layers raises its beta output and moves it *up*. Hierarchical position becomes a per-area scalar set by the laminar balance of drive, with no change of wiring.

This is the row's `Closes when` executed in its general form — direction manipulated by task with the connectome fixed — and the answer is **both, partitioned by position**: anatomy pins the sensory end and leaves the frontal end free. The row stays `LIVE` because the positions above are stated over *traveling-wave* direction and the instrength gradient, and Granger-causal influence is a different observable; the regression of band-resolved wave flow against both gradients in the same subjects is still unrun. What has changed is the shape of the expected answer — not "fixed or dynamic" but **how wide the interval is as a function of depth**, and the prediction that the width grows toward association cortex, which is where [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] `T378` says the anatomical coordinate stops being measurable at all.
