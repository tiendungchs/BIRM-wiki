# T124 — Which epistemic term does the work — uncertainty about the *state*, or uncertainty about the *model*?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** State-uncertainty and model-uncertainty terms are ablated against each other in one implementation — currently only the model-uncertainty side has measurements.

## Position A

**The state.** Both structural results in the wiki score uncertainty about where the agent is or will be: Milosevic et al. 2026 reduce expected free energy to `⟨ℓ,μ⟩ + Φ(μ)` with `Φ = −Σ_t H(ρ_t)`, the negative entropy of the predictive *state* marginal, and explicitly **exclude** parameter-novelty terms from the convex-MDP result; Cooper & Velasquez 2026 identify the objective with a ρ-POMDP whose utility is information gain about a belief over a *fixed hidden state*, and prove the identity only where observation leaves that state unchanged. Both are theorems.

## Position B

**The model.** Decomposing the same objective into entropy corrections separates the observation side into ambiguity (`H[q(y|x,θ)]`, precision of the sensor at reachable states) and novelty (`H[q(y|x)] − H[q(y|x,θ)]`, expected information gain about the parameters `θ`), and ablation puts nearly the whole effect on novelty: with the environment layout as `θ` and one-shot sensing, the full scheme retrieves `98.7%` of good rocks for reward `4.01` while every ablation — including the ambiguity-only heuristic — never probes at all (`0.0%`, reward `1.00`), and Wumpus success is `40.7%` vs. `≤24.0%`. Ambiguity substitutes for novelty *only* when a sensing action persistently changes the observation kernel (Nuijten et al. 2026).

## Where it bites

Whether the wiki's derived exploration drive transfers to structure discovery. A latent-graph agent's unknown *is* a parameter, and its probes are typically one-shot readings that do not alter the sensor — the exact regime where A's covered term is worth nothing and B's excluded term is worth everything. It also means the two clean theoretical results characterise the term with the smallest measured effect: no existence, uniqueness, convergence or weight-calibration result covers parameter novelty.

## Status

`LIVE` — not a contradiction in algebra (the terms are different objects over different distributions) but a direct conflict over which one an implementation should carry; B has the only measurements
