# T166 — Are anti-collapse mechanisms from different families composable?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The localised conflict is tested: batch standardisation inside the loss is removed from Barlow Twins and the predictor/stop-gradient graft repeated, isolating the second cross-branch coupling.

## Position A

**No — they are alternative whole designs.** Adding BYOL's predictor and stop-gradient to Barlow Twins costs **10 points** (71.4 → 61.3; either alone ≈ −1), and the reverse control agrees — BYOL with Barlow Twins' 8192-wide projector drops 74.1 → 72.3. The reading the wiki adopted: an update-rule asymmetry inside a system that already has a well-defined minimum drags the dynamics off it, so a designer picks one design rather than layering safeguards (Zbontar et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/barlow-twins.md]])

## Position B

**Yes, and the graft is mildly positive.** VICReg — same family, same off-diagonal penalty — takes a predictor and stop-gradient *neutrally* (73.2 → 73.2 at 1000 epochs; 68.6 → 67.4 at 100), and its **variance term added to BYOL buys +0.9** (69.3 → 70.2) and to SimSiam +0.2, by raising a representation-level standard deviation that BYOL's tricks leave under-spread. The two mechanisms drive the *same two statistics* — BYOL's embeddings already sit at `1/√d` on the sphere and its representations decorrelate with no term asking them to — so they are two implementations of one target, not rivals (Bardes et al. 2022, [[wiki/entities/vicreg.md]])

**Two grafts from a third pair of loci, and they break the symmetry of the row** (Chen & He 2021, [[wiki/entities/simsiam.md]]): predictor + stop-gradient added to **SimCLR** is inert (66.5 → 66.0; predictor alone 66.4), and predictor added to **SwAV** costs 1.3 (66.5 → 65.2) — while *removing* SwAV's stop-gradient diverges to NaN, because online clustering is already an alternating minimisation and its stop-gradient is structural. So across five host methods the update-rule graft is inert (SimCLR, VICReg), mildly negative (SwAV), or catastrophic (Barlow Twins, −10), and the only host it destroys is the one whose loss is built from **batch-standardised** embeddings — which is the localisation this row's Status already proposed, now supported by two independent non-failures rather than one.

## Where it bites

Decides whether the JEPA lineage's inherited EMA + stop-gradient can be *supplemented* with a certifiable term (cheap: keeps the working system, adds an auditable target) or must be *replaced* (expensive: [[wiki/entities/lewm.md]]'s whole programme). Under B the wiki's certifiability preference costs almost nothing; under A it costs a redesign Home page for this row: [[wiki/concepts/representational-collapse.md]].

## Status

`LEANING B, with the conflict localised` — the two papers differ in one component: Barlow Twins standardises the embeddings along the batch *inside the loss* and VICReg does not, replacing it with an explicit variance hinge. **(brainstorm)** Batch standardisation is a second implicit cross-branch coupling, and stacking it with an EMA/stop-gradient is what costs 10 points — a three-way interaction rather than a two-family incompatibility. The discriminating experiment is one run: Barlow Twins with cross-*covariance* (no standardisation) plus predictor and stop-gradient. Independently, both papers agree the **covariance** term is the piece that does not compose ("optimization with SG and CR is hard": BYOL 70.2 → 69.5, SimSiam 68.1 → 67.6) **A third data point, from a different pair of loci** (Akbar 2026, [[wiki/entities/lejepa.md]] §Part 8): Muon's orthogonalised *updates* and SIGReg's constrained *representation* stack cleanly on a collapsing ViT — 20.73 (AdamW) → 62.44 (Muon alone) → **74.56** (Muon + Weak SIGReg) — and SIGReg is additive again on top of the hand-tuned architecture heuristics (75.87 → 76.98). So the failures of composition in this row are both *update-rule into loss-with-a-minimum*, and provisions acting on genuinely different objects appear to compose.
