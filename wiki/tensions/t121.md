# T121 — Is event-model updating controlled by one signal or by several?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**One.** Every detector in the wiki thresholds a single scalar and emits an untyped boundary: Event Segmentation Theory thresholds prediction error, the long-short-term-memory gate thresholds one accumulated quantity, the sticky hierarchical Dirichlet process thresholds one novel-context responsibility. Parsimony is the argument, and a single trigger is what makes the boundary consumable — one node-creation rule downstream ([[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]], [[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]])

## Position B

**At least two, and probably more.** Prediction error and prediction *uncertainty* (epistemic variance across 32 dropout predictions) each uniquely predict human boundary density despite correlating at r = 0.499 — uncertainty at t = 14.32, error at t = 5.14 (df = 1636) — and they recruit dissociable networks with opposite post-boundary signatures: error → ventrolateral prefrontal shift then widespread pattern stabilisation, uncertainty → dorsal-attention shift with almost none. In the parcels responsive to both, the timecourses still differ significantly. The two together leave ~16% of explainable variance, with latent-cause inference named as a third mechanism. The authors accept the cost explicitly: such an architecture "would be inelegant—but evolution's engineering solutions are sometimes complex and kludgy rather than simple and sleek" (Nguyen et al. 2025, [[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]])

## Where it bites

Gap G27 and everything downstream of it. If B holds, a segmentation module has no single threshold to tune, needs an arbitration policy between triggers that nobody has specified, and should emit **typed** boundaries — `REPLACE` (commit a new node) vs. `INVESTIGATE` (resample, do not commit) — which no wiki architecture consumes

## Status

`LEANING B` — B has the only measurement that separates the two signals rather than assuming they co-vary, and it separates them both behaviourally and anatomically. What A retains: the two triggers are still hand-thresholded, so "two signals" may be two hand-set readouts of one underlying precision-weighted quantity that a properly normative account would recombine (see [[wiki/concepts/expected-free-energy.md]], where `ℓ` and `Φ` are two terms of *one* objective with no free mixing coefficient)
