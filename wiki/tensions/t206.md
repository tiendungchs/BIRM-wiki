# T206 — What does a reported ARC-AGI-2 score denote?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Frontier models are at or past the Grand Prize threshold.** A third-party aggregator (`llm-stats.com`, July 2026, 16 models) reports GPT-5.5 at **0.850**, Gemini 3.1 Pro 0.771, GPT-5.4 0.733, Claude Opus 4.6 0.688 — i.e. the benchmark is effectively solved by closed frontier systems, with an average across all tracked models of 0.451 (`raw/llmstats-2026-arc-agi-2-leaderboard.md`)

## Position B

**The verified open frontier is 24%, and the verified assisted frontier is 54%.** ARC Prize's own measurements: top Kaggle score 24.03% on the private set at $0.20/task after 1,455 teams and 15,154 entries; Gemini 3 Pro baseline **31%** at $0.81/task, rising to 54% only with an application-layer refinement harness at $31/task; scores below 5% not treated as signal at all (`raw/chollet-2025-arc-prize-report.md`, [[wiki/entities/arc-agi-2.md]])

## Where it bites

Every leaderboard row the wiki cites. The aggregator itself reports **0 verified and 13 self-reported** results, and does not state the eval subset (public / semi-private / private), the attempt budget (`pass@1` vs `pass@2`), the inference cost, or whether a harness was in the loop — and the report expects harnesses to be absorbed behind vendor APIs, which erases the model/loop boundary a vendor-reported number implicitly claims. So the two positions may not be measuring different systems so much as *different unstated protocols*, which is the failure mode [[wiki/concepts/external-verification.md]] catalogues for every math leaderboard

## Status

`LIVE`, and it is a reporting-protocol problem before it is an empirical one. Resolvable only from the ARC Prize side: any number not carrying (subset, attempts, $/task, harness yes/no) is uninterpretable and should not be entered in a wiki comparison table. Treat the aggregator row as `(tentative)` throughout
