# T228 — Does model scale reduce or amplify reliance on shortcuts?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The two prompts are crossed in one design: scale swept under few-shot and chain-of-thought with the same items, so neither measurement is a confound for the other.

## Position A

**Amplifies.** On Shortcut Suite ([[wiki/entities/shortcut-suite.md]]) LLaMA2-Chat under few-shot ICL falls monotonically with parameter count on exactly the non-entailment items the heuristic gets wrong — Lexical Overlap `¬E` 75.3 → 48.5 → **3.6** and Subsequence `¬E` 59.5 → 12.4 → **3.1** for 7B → 13B → 70B — while the same models' Standard accuracy *rises* (42.1 → 59.1 → 57.8). The 70B model is a near-perfect constant `entailment` classifier. It is not a one-family artefact: LLaMA3 zero-shot Constituent `¬E` goes 8B **40.1** → 70B **11.1**

## Position B

**Reduces, given the right prompt.** Under chain-of-thought the ordering reverses and LLaMA2-Chat-70B leads its family on most sets (Constituent `¬E` 25.3 against 41.7/34.1 — mixed, but Standard 60.3/70.9 is the family best and the CoT gains are largest at 13B–70B: +40.9 for 13B on Constituent `¬E`). The authors' reading: larger models retain better semantic comprehension and need the prompt to elicit it rather than lacking the competence

## Where it bites

Decides whether shortcut resistance is a capability that scaling buys. If A is the whole story, every i.i.d. scaling curve in the wiki is uninformative about robustness and the fix must go on one of the four levers. If B is, the competence is present and the defect is in *elicitation* — which relocates the problem to [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-control.md]] and makes it a deployment question. The two are not strictly contradictory (they differ in prompt) and that is the finding: **the sign of the scaling effect on robustness is a property of the prompt, so no scaling claim about shortcut reliance is well-posed without one.** The discriminating experiment is unrun and cheap — hold the prompt fixed and sweep scale on the `¬E` arm alone across ≥3 families

## Status

`LIVE` — one benchmark, one paper, and the two positions are not a disagreement about a fact but a pair of measurements taken under different prompts, neither of which controls for the other. Three model families and two vintages support A; B rests on a single CoT column of the same table, is *mixed* on the set where the reversal is largest, and has no ablation separating elicitation from the extra inference-time computation CoT buys. Downgradeable only by the fixed-prompt scale sweep named at left
