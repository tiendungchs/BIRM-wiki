# T69 — Is a finite-nudge contrastive rule a biased approximation of backpropagation, or exact gradient descent on a different objective?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Position B's exact-gradient framework is tested at depth and on generalization, in the regime where A's deficit was measured — currently one hidden layer on Fashion-MNIST.

## Position A

**Biased approximation.** The standard statement of equilibrium propagation: the two-phase update equals the loss gradient only in the limit `β → 0`, and the finite `β` needed for stable learning introduces bias relative to the true gradient — the framing behind the whole "backpropagation-derived local rules generalize worse" result, whose signature (a deficit that does not close by scaling the step size) is exactly the bias branch (Schmidgall et al. 2023; Whittington & Bogacz 2019; Laborieux et al. 2021, whose contribution is *reducing* the estimator bias). **A second, independent argument for small `β`, which is not about bias at all** (Scellier & Bengio 2017): the fully clamped limit `β → ∞` — contrastive Hebbian learning — optimises `E(u^∞) − E(u⁰)`, which can go *negative* when the free and clamped fixed points land in different modes of the energy landscape, and learning then deteriorates (Movellan 1990). Weak clamping is precisely what lets the implicit-function theorem hold `u^β` in the same basin as `u⁰`. On this reading `β` must be small enough to preserve the mode, whatever it does to the gradient

## Position B

**Exact gradient, different objective.** Modelling the state as a Gibbs distribution rather than a minimiser, the contrastive update is the exact gradient of `J(θ) = A(θ,1) − A(θ,0)` for *any* finite `β`, with no convexity, unique-minimum, weight-symmetry or infinitesimal assumption; the apparent bias is `T·KL(ρ_1‖ρ_0)`, a deliberate regulariser (`J = E_{ρ_1}[ℓ] + T·KL(ρ_1‖ρ_0)`, and `J ≤ E_{ρ_0}[ℓ]`). Empirically the small-`β` regime is not safer but **unusable**: Fashion-MNIST, one `tanh` hidden layer, `β = 0.01` stalls at chance (20–30%) while `β = 1` and a path-integral variant reach ~80% tracking backpropagation, and the SNR of `Δs = s_β − s_0` sits below the sampling-noise floor for `β ≲ 10⁻²` (Litman 2025)

## Where it bites

Decides how to score every two-phase local rule, and whether the generalization deficit transfers to them. Under A the rule inherits the deficit and repairs are impossible by averaging; under B it is unbiased for its own objective, all error is sampler variance (fixable by averaging), and the residual question is whether `J` is the objective wanted — which reopens the bias/variance table on [[wiki/concepts/equilibrium-propagation.md]] and on [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]] where contrastive Hebbian learning was listed as unplaced

## Status

`LEANING B` — B's theorems are unconditional given standard regularity, and its empirics invert A's assumption about which regime works. But the demonstration is one hidden layer on Fashion–MNIST at ~80%, no depth scaling and no generalization measurement, so B has not been tested where A's deficit was measured; and B's cosine similarity to `∇L_sup` tops out at ~0.5, which is consistent with `J` and `L_sup` diverging as the KL term grows. The mode-preservation argument is orthogonal to B and survives it: it bounds `β` from *above* for a reason B never addresses, so the two together predict a **window** — `β` large enough to clear the sampler's noise floor, small enough to keep both phases in one basin — rather than a monotone preference either way. Nothing measures where that window sits; B's demonstration is one `tanh` hidden layer, a landscape too simple for mode-crossing to arise, and B's Gibbs framing may dissolve the issue entirely at finite `T` (a distribution has no mode to leave) while reinstating it as `T → 0`
