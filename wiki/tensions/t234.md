# T234 — Is the spiking substrate's advantage expressive power, or unit count?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A learner is shown to converge to something a rate network cannot express at matched unit count — the simulation theorems concern hand-built networks and say nothing about what training finds.

## Position A

**Expressive power.** The wiki's empirical spiking results are stated as things a rate specification *cannot* say: three substrate-only mechanisms are each necessary for a grid code the functional model has no vocabulary for ([[wiki/entities/spiking-tem.md]]), and an arrival-time difference resolved to 20–25 µs has no rate-level description at all ([[wiki/concepts/temporal-coding.md]])

## Position B

**Unit count only.** For spiking neurons with piecewise-linear postsynaptic potentials the function classes coincide: any `s`-gate threshold circuit on real-valued input is simulated by `O(s)` spiking neurons, any continuous `F: [0,1]^n → [0,1]^k` is approximated by a *one-hidden-layer* spiking net, and conversely small sigmoidal nets are simulable by small spiking nets. Every proven separation is in the **number of units** (`Ω(n)`, polynomial, never exponential), not in what is computable (Maass 1997, [[wiki/concepts/circuit-size-separation.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what a spiking result has to demonstrate to count. Under A it suffices to exhibit a representation the rate model lacks; under B that is impossible in principle for type-B neurons and the only admissible evidence is *units-to-competence*, which no benchmark in the wiki reports. It also redirects the T1 argument: if B, "the implementation level carries content" means "the functional specification is realisable but at a size the brain does not have"

## Status

`LIVE`, and the two may not be incompatible — the simulation theorems are about a fixed function computed by a hand-built network, while A's evidence is about what a *learner* converges to under a given architecture, and nothing connects the two. The type-A result is a genuine expressibility separation in the opposite direction: rectangular-pulse spiking neurons cannot compute `x₁ + x₂ ≤ x₃` on `[0,1]³` at any size, which three threshold gates do
