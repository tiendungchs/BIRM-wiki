# T384 — Does retrosplenial cortex hold the recent trace or the remote one?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** One study measures retrosplenial necessity (not activation) for autobiographical or object–scene retrieval at both recent and remote delays in the same subjects with the same task.

## Position A

**Recent.** Human retrosplenial lesions produce retrograde amnesia for **recent** autobiographical events more than remote ones, and fMRI shows greater retrosplenial engagement at recent than remote retrieval — the region is used while a memory is still being assembled from hippocampal output, and drops out as the memory stabilises (Vann, Aggleton & Maguire 2009, [[wiki/entities/retrosplenial-cortex.md]])

## Position B

**Remote — the standard cortical-trace gradient.** Emergent retrosplenial activity accompanies retrieval of **stable** episodic memories while hippocampal activity dominates at encoding and at retrieval of nascent ones; primate retrosplenial cortex activates distinctly for object–scene pairs retrieved a year after encoding versus newly learned ones; retrosplenial activation is preferential for familiar scenes; and muscimol in anterior retrosplenial cortex immediately after training impairs memory 24 h later. The framing is multiple-trace systems consolidation with this region as the cortical trace (Alexander et al. 2023, [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]])

## Where it bites

Which slot this region occupies in a two-store architecture. Under B it is the slow learner's store for spatial and contextual structure, written by the offline channel and read when the fast store is no longer needed — the role [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]] already assigns it for schematized maps. Under A it is a *reader* of the fast store, needed while the fast store is, which makes it part of the retrieval path rather than the destination and predicts the opposite lesion timing. The confound both positions share is the one `T97` names: activation without necessity on B's side, and on A's side a lesion literature that cannot separate "not involved" from "involved and substitutable" in a triad with parallel direct edges

## Status

`LIVE` — the two positions rest on different instruments (human lesion plus fMRI versus primate electrophysiology, rodent pharmacology and scene fMRI) and no study has measured both delays under one manipulation. Note that A's lesion evidence and B's activation evidence are not strictly contradictory: a region could be necessary early and increasingly *active* late, which is exactly the activation-without-necessity pattern `T97` records for medial prefrontal cortex and is the first thing the discriminating experiment should test for
