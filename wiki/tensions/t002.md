# T2 — Is the fast level a separate store or recurrent activity?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The fast level's degrees of freedom and decay timescale are measured in both a separate-store and a recurrent-activity system and shown to differ — or shown identical, which dissolves the row into a change of basis.

## Position A

Complementary learning systems: a second anatomical system with a sparse conjunctive code, coupled by replay

## Position B

Meta-RL: the fast learner is the *activity dynamics* of a network whose weights are fixed — no second store required. Reinforced by Schmidgall et al. 2023: in-context learning changes activations not weights, yet parameter-sharing in a meta-learner yields the *interpretation of activations as weights*, and self-attention's outer/inner products can be cast as learned weight updates that implement gradient descent

## Where it bites

Determines what fast **M** is built from, and whether one-shot binding and fast policy adaptation are the same mechanism

## Status

`LIVE` — but possibly **dissolving**: if activations and weights are interconvertible under parameter sharing, the question is a change of basis, and the real variables are the fast level's degrees of freedom and decay timescale ([[wiki/concepts/meta-optimized-plasticity.md]])
