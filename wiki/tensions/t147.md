# T147 — Does making a learned model inspectable require a discrete bottleneck?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Symbolic extraction is shown to survive a composed multivariate `Φ`, and the extracted equation re-integrated to confirm it reproduces the model it came from.

## Position A

**Yes — verification needs a finite symbol set.** The wiki's standing answer to the verifiability problem of [[wiki/concepts/learned-world-models.md]]: quantise the state into an equivalence class of situations and learn transitions as rules over that alphabet, which is what makes classical planners, PDDL rules and formal proof applicable at all ([[wiki/concepts/affordance-grounded-symbols.md]] — DeepSym, LatPlan, Konidaris et al.). A continuous latent has no object a checker can consume.

## Position B

**No — put the learnable parameters in the *functional form* instead.** A KAN moves the learned degrees of freedom onto per-edge univariate basis functions, so the transition law is a sum of individually plottable curves; symbolic regression on one such activation returned `0.995u(1.003 − u)` against a truth of `r·u(1−u)`, `r = 1`, giving a closed form a human or a solver can check with the state left continuous and no quantisation anywhere (Koenig et al. 2024, [[wiki/entities/kan-ode.md]]). The symbolic step is *optional postprocessing*, so unlike SINDy it does not restrict what can be fitted.

## Where it bites

Decides whether the discretisation problem (G27) is a prerequisite for the reification problem (G8/G11) or an independent one. Under A a builder must solve carving before it can get rules; under B a continuous learner can be symbolised in place, and G27 is only needed for the *state vocabulary*, not for the transition law.

## Status

`LIVE`, and B's evidence is thin in a specific way: the symbolised module was `[1,1,5]` — one node learning one scalar residual inside an otherwise hand-written PDE — so the readability plausibly came from the hand-made factorisation rather than from the KAN, and no result shows extraction surviving a composed multivariate `Φ`. B also never re-integrated the extracted equation to confirm it reproduces the model it replaced, which is the check that would make this a claim about the model rather than about the ground truth
