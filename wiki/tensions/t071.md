# T71 — To repair feedback alignment, should the feedback weights move toward the forward ones, or the forward weights toward the feedback?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The two repairs are run head to head, and their composition analysed: driving `W → Bᵀ` while `B → Wᵀ` is a two-sided pursuit whose fixed point nobody has characterised.

## Position A

**Move the feedback.** Akrout et al. 2019 update `B` by a Hebbian rule that pushes it toward `Wᵀ`; Activation Relaxation needs the same — its backward weights `ψ` must be *learned* (Hebbian), and **fixed random feedback converged lower and tended to diverge** in that framework (Millidge et al. 2020). On this reading a fixed random `B` is a dead end and feedback plasticity is mandatory

## Position B

**Move the forward weights.** Keep `B` fixed and random and add `−θ₂ · e_ℓ e_{ℓ−1}ᵀ` to the forward update: in a linear network `E[e_ℓ e_{ℓ−1}ᵀ ∣ B] ∝ Bᵀ`, so the forward weights are driven onto the transpose of the *random* matrix, closing most of the feedback-alignment/backpropagation gap in the online low-data deep regime where feedback alignment otherwise barely learns (Shervani-Tabar & Rosenbaum 2023). Bonus: it removes a two-iteration transport latency per layer that the feedback-side repairs do not touch

## Where it bites

Decides which side of the pair a builder makes plastic — and therefore what anatomy is required. Under A the cortex needs plastic, error-carrying feedback projections whose rule is separately hyperparameter-sensitive (Kunin et al. 2020); under B feedback can be fixed random wiring, which is the far cheaper and more defensible claim, at the cost of needing a presynaptic error term at every layer (a synthetic `e₀` is stipulated for the input layer)

## Status

`LIVE` — the two have never been run head to head: A's evidence comes from a relaxation framework where the backward weights also carry gradients, B's from a feedforward pseudo-gradient setting at MNIST-class scale. Nor does anything say whether they compose or fight — driving `W → Bᵀ` while `B → Wᵀ` is a two-sided pursuit with no analysis of its fixed point
