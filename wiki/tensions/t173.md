# T173 — Should a learned similarity live in the geometry of the code, or in a comparator on top of it? *(absorbs T184, "should structural similarity live in the code or in the comparator?", merged at the 284-source duplicate audit: one axis, two rows, sharing two of their four citations. T184's sides were labelled in the opposite order; this row uses A = in the code.)*

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Both read-out families are run on both relation types — a transitive reflexive relation and a metric with varying curvature — testing the algebraic-structure discriminator.

## Position A

**In the code.** Encode each item once, compare by a fixed form, and the read-out is free. [[wiki/entities/neuromatch.md]] ablates this directly: the order-embedding hinge `‖max{0, z_q − z_u}‖²` beats an MLP comparator, a neural tensor network and box embeddings on accuracy *and* runs 10× faster (0.03 s vs 0.44 s at query size ≤ 50) — "isolates the geometry as the source of both accuracy and speed". [[wiki/entities/lejepa.md]] gives the normative version: at fixed variance the isotropic Gaussian uniquely minimises linear-, k-NN- and kernel-probe risk, so the prescription is to shape the *space* until the simplest probe is optimal. Every JEPA, VICReg and BYOL read-out in the wiki is a fixed cosine or `ℓ₂`. **And the strong form — that even *structural* properties can be pre-computed into the vectors until a dot product reads them off as surface features:** contextualized HRRs, each filler blended with the typical fillers of the other roles it occupies, reproduce the full MAC/FAC ordering `LS > AN^cm > AN > SS > FA` in 100/100 runs from a single dot product at `n` = 2048, with no alignment search anywhere, and the salience of role alignment re-tunable by re-weighting the probe alone (Plate, NIPS 7)

## Position B

**In the comparator.** [[wiki/entities/learningmatch.md]] sweeps both and finds the opposite: encoder depth "did not have a significant impact on the accuracy", comparator depth did, up to a knee at 5×1024. The head is also given the pair's **raw mean**, not only its difference — because the target match varies drastically with mass in the low-chirp-mass region and negligibly in the high-mass one, and *every fixed score form in the wiki (inner product, cosine, `ℓ₂`, order hinge) is translation-invariant in the embedding*, so none can express a similarity whose scale depends on where the pair sits. [[wiki/concepts/retrieval-capacity.md]]'s cross-encoder result is the same sign: 100% on a task no single-vector model solves at any dimension, and its theorem says a plain dot product over `d`-dimensional keys can address only `(1+1/γ)^d` retrieval sets, so structure-sensitive queries *must* escape the bilinear form. SME is the classical version — search for a consistent one-to-one mapping and let the comparison do the structural work

## Where it bites

Where the parameters of every memory read in the wiki should go, and what a cheap index can be trusted to answer. Under A a store is built once and queried by coordinate test, and G37's retrieval slot is closed; under B the read is `O(n)` head evaluations however good the encoder, and the rank-`d` bound is escaped by moving expressivity into the comparator rather than by raising `d`. **The two routes price differently rather than contradict**: the comparator route un-factorises the score and forfeits `O(1)` per-item retrieval; the code route keeps the index and pays in **dot-product variance** — ordering violations fall 6/100 → 1/100 when `n` goes 2048 → 4096, i.e. the bound is paid in signal-to-noise, not evaded

## Status

`LIVE` — the candidate discriminator is the *algebraic structure of the target relation*, and it is untested: subgraph containment is transitive and reflexive, which is exactly what an order embedding encodes exactly and for free, while a metric with varying curvature has no such property for a geometry to exploit. Nobody has run both read-out families on both relation types, and LearningMatch's own evidence is a depth sweep with no seed variance on one domain `(tentative)`. The code route also has a hard failure the comparator route does not: two entities filling the same role set are indistinguishable. Distinct from T163, which asks whether the *score* may be factorised at all; this asks what form it should take once it is
