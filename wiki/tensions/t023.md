# T23 — What licenses the creation of a new discrete node?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Either criterion — predictive-set change or unbounded-context posterior — is run over the other's representation, and scored against parcel-wise fMRI dissimilarity timecourses as Nguyen et al. 2025 do.

## Position A

**A lasting change in the active set of predictive encodings** — segmentation is derived from the predictive substrate itself, needs no extra module, and covers motion onsets, occlusions, property and affordance changes with one detector. But the detectors on offer are hand-set thresholds or long-short-term-memory gates trained by backpropagation through time, and the clustering objective, granularity control and stopping rule are all unspecified (Butz 2016, [[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]]). **Now split from within** (Nguyen et al. 2025): the "lasting change" is driven by *two* separable quantities, prediction error and prediction uncertainty, which uniquely predict human boundaries (t = 5.14 and 14.32) and recruit dissociable networks — so this position is not one criterion but an unarbitrated pair, see T121

## Position B

**A posterior over an unbounded context set** — a new memory is instantiated with probability equal to the *novel-context responsibility* under a sticky hierarchical Dirichlet process, so the criterion, the granularity (`γ`), the persistence bias (`κ`) and the cross-context pooling (`α`) are one prior, and noise-versus-boundary is a likelihood ratio rather than a threshold. But a node's content is a **single scalar**, and nodes share no structure with one another (Heald et al. 2021, [[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]])

## Where it bites

Gap G27 — whether the discretisation problem is solved by enriching the representation until boundaries fall out, or by putting a principled allocation prior over impoverished units. The two are not obviously combinable: the Bayesian criterion is tractable *because* the state is scalar

## Status

`LIVE` — complementary rather than contradictory on their face, but nobody has run either criterion over the other's representation. **A route to deciding it now exists:** Nguyen et al. 2025 score model-generated boundaries against parcel-wise fMRI pattern-dissimilarity timecourses, and name latent-cause inference (Position B's machinery) as their candidate for the ~16% of explainable variance the prediction-quality triggers miss — so B can be scored on A's own benchmark
