# T237 — Is a delay a more valuable parameter than a weight?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Yes, provably, per parameter.** Maass & Schmitt 1997: `n` programmable **delays** on one type-A spiking neuron carry VC dimension `Θ(n log n)` against `Θ(n)` for `n` programmable weights, and Maass 1997's coincidence-detection witness `CD_n` is computed by a **single** neuron using delays alone with every weight fixed at 1 ([[wiki/concepts/circuit-size-separation.md]]). The wiki opened [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] `G80` on the strength of this — the most expressive variable available, and nothing optimises it

## Position B

**Only under a size constraint.** Mészáros et al. 2025 train delays by exact gradient and measure the benefit directly ([[wiki/concepts/learnable-synaptic-delays.md]]): on SSC and Braille, adding delays to *large* recurrent networks gave **little to no benefit**, and they became "highly beneficial" only as the hidden layer shrank; on SHD, recurrent delays are what let a 128-unit network hold accuracy that a feedforward one loses; on Yin-Yang, weights-and-delays beats weights-alone only when parameter count is unconstrained, while at *matched* parameter count the two are equal. So a delay substitutes for units rather than dominating a weight — the measured exchange is ~5× fewer parameters at equal SHD accuracy, not a higher ceiling

## Where it bites

Whether "train delays" is a general design rule or a compression technique. Position B predicts that the delay's advantage disappears exactly where the wiki's target models live (large networks, over-parameterised), and that its real use is edge and neuromorphic deployment where units are scarce — which is also where the mechanism is cheapest (per-neuron ring buffer, <½ the memory and up to 26× the speed of convolutional delay learning). Note the two positions are not strictly contradictory: VC dimension bounds what a parameter *can* express, and the benchmarks measure what gradient descent *reaches* on tasks the authors themselves say may not require precise delays — so the cleanest test (sound localisation, motion detection) has not been run

## Status

`LEANING` B, untested where it matters
