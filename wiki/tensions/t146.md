# T146 — Does a violation-of-expectation score measure the ability it names?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The remaining two free parameters are closed by stated practice, as Garrido et al. 2025 closed the surprise-moment choice with a randomly-initialised-network null.

## Position A

**Yes — that is the protocol's whole virtue.** Paired plausible/implausible continuations of an identical history need no labels, no distribution shift and no absolute calibration; the score is a relative judgement of exactly the property under test, which is why the wiki lists it as an instrument for G17 ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]).

## Position B

**Not as reported.** The score is not fixed by the protocol but by three free choices made after the fact: (i) *which moment of the error trace counts as surprise* — `max_t` vs `mean_t` moves IRENE's Blocking Barrier from 83.5 to 99.4 and its Multi-Agent from 74.9 to 63.6, and the benchmark authors and a competing group justify opposite choices. **The primary source now states the reason in the open**: BIB uses the maximum because alternatives "like the mean expectedness of steps" *"consistently resulted in lower VOE scores"* (Gandhi et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/bib.md]]) — the parameter was fixed by the scores it produced, at the benchmark's origin, and every BIB number in the wiki inherits it; (ii) *which subtasks are averaged* — two of three Instrumental subtasks are heuristic-solvable, so the average ranks the barrier-ignorant model above the barrier-aware one; (iii) *what the model may be handed* (T21). Alignment with infant looking times is likewise produced here by a model with no agent variable at all (Bortoletto et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/irene.md]]).

## Where it bites

Every BIB, AGENT and IntPhys-style number in the wiki, and G17's sixth instrument: paired VoE remains the cheapest protocol that needs no labels, but it certifies nothing until the error statistic is pre-registered and the shortcut-solvable subset is broken out. Sharpens the general reporting rule already on [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]]: a *drop* in aggregate score can be evidence of structure learning.

## Status

`LIVE, two of four parameters now closed by practice` — Garrido et al. 2025 close (ii) and add the control the protocol never had: `n = 20` **randomly-initialised networks** of the same architecture run through the identical surprise pipeline, with every result reported as a per-property effect size over that null (Welch's t-test, Hedges' `g`) instead of as an accuracy. That is the machine analogue of habituation trials, it costs one forward pass per seed, and it makes a benchmark's low-level bias subtractable rather than arguable. What stays open is (i): `max_t` is still chosen, and still chosen because it scores better. Full treatment on [[wiki/concepts/violation-of-expectation.md]]
