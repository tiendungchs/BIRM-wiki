# T138 — Does the separation/completion bias actually need a controller, or is a well-placed constant enough?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The threshold sweep is re-run under drifting contexts or a degrading encoder: if the tolerant plateau narrows or moves, the constant is not enough.

## Position A

**It needs one, and gap G38 says so.** The bias tracks the behavioural contrast rather than stimulus novelty, is not one number even within one animal at one time (different track positions decorrelate in a fixed order), and the wiki carries four candidate actuators for moving it at runtime — the cholinergic mode bit, the CA3→DG backprojection, BTSP's allocation rate `f_q`, and four separately-addressable interneuron families ([[wiki/concepts/pattern-separation-completion.md]], [[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]], [[wiki/entities/btsp-cam.md]])

## Position B

**A constant is enough on a stationary distribution.** Sweeping the bias exhaustively in a working agent gives a wide tolerant plateau, not a narrow optimum: 43.54% of the threshold space reaches ≥90% accuracy, a contiguous region gives 95.03% ± 2.34%, and the accuracy distribution is negatively skewed (−0.495). The frozen threshold is never adjusted and the agent still beats every learned-representation baseline (Poursiami et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/hami.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides whether G38 is a blocking gap or a refinement. Under A a fast store without a runtime bias controller is incomplete, and building one is on the critical path; under B the design effort belongs elsewhere and the threshold is a one-time calibration. The plateau also changes what evidence would settle it: A's evidence is all *variation* in the biological bias, which is only decisive if the variation buys performance — none of the four candidate actuators has been shown to improve a task score by moving the knob

## Status

`LIVE`. The two positions are not tested on the same thing: the plateau is measured on a stationary observation distribution with four fixed rules and a frozen near-perfect encoder (NMI 0.97–0.9997), which is exactly the regime where a constant should work. The discriminating experiment is the sweep re-run under drifting contexts or a degrading encoder — if the plateau narrows or moves, A wins on its own terms
