# T215 — Is the visual/textual gap on ARC-format tasks a reasoning deficit or a perceptual one?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The parse is supplied as a **ground-truth** object list alongside the image and the tasks re-scored. The *approximate* version — a model-generated natural-language parse — has now been run (Wang et al. 2026, below) and moves the row without closing it: the substituted parse is itself 37–60% wrong, so the reasoning stage has still never been scored on a parse known to be correct.

## Position A

**Reasoning.** Rule correctness itself falls with the modality, not just accuracy: o3 states the intended rule on 57.3% of tasks in text and 40.0% in vision, Claude on 57.5% and 14.4%, Gemini on 46.1% and 23.6% — and intended-rule *coverage* over the 480 tasks drops 85.8→58.5 (o3), 72.1→17.3 (Claude), 67.9→29.3 (Gemini). Something above perception degrades

## Position B

**Perception.** Rule–grid alignment stays 93.6–97.4% in vision, so the models still execute what they state; the separately-scored *visual error* rate (grid dimensions misread, objects misplaced, colour↔integer mis-mapped) is **49.1% / 59.7% / 77.3%**; giving the model Python — i.e. computer-vision libraries to parse the image — is worth +12 to +24 points in vision and ~0 to −5 in text, while extra reasoning effort is worth +9 to +19 in text and ~0 in vision. The Bongard-problem and RAVEN literatures report the same attribution `(tentative — second-hand)`

## Where it bites

What to build. Under A the visual pathway needs a better reasoner; under B it needs a parser, and the reasoning stack is already adequate. It also decides how to read every ARC score in the wiki, since all of them are obtained on the *textual* serialisation, which this result makes the **easier** modality — retiring the wiki's standing conjecture that row-major serialisation of 2-D structure is what floors LLMs on ARC (absorbed into [[wiki/gaps/g017.md]])

## Status

`LEANING B` — B has the direct measurements and A's evidence is not independent of them, since a rule can only be judged intended if the grid was parsed well enough to describe. The discriminating experiment is cheap and unrun: supply the *parse* (an object list) with the image and re-score

**A third locus the row has no column for** ([[wiki/entities/blindtest.md]], Rahmanzadehgervi et al. 2024). Both positions assume the defect is in a *stage* — perception or reasoning — and the paper localises it to the **interface between them**: a linear probe on frozen vision-encoder features solves two BlindTest tasks at 99.47–100% before and after the projection layer, while the same VLMs score 33.14–83.63% on them. Under this reading B's prescription ("it needs a parser") is wrong for the same reason A's is: the parse is computed and not read. That is `T388`, opened as its own row because it is an `L2` signal-flow question the `L0-INSTR` framing here cannot carry.

**Position B's first *constructive* evidence, and it arrives by subtraction** ([[wiki/entities/varc.md]], Hu et al. 2025). Everything either column has held so far is a deficit measurement: what a model gets wrong, and where. VARC is the other sign. Remove the reasoning stack entirely — no language model, no program, no chain of thought, no search, no recurrence, 18M parameters trained from scratch on ARC data alone — hand the grid to a vanilla ViT *as an image with a metric*, and the score goes **up**, to 54.5% single-model and 60.4% ensembled, level with the 60.2% reported average human and above every LLM in the paper's table up to Grok-4-thinking. Under position A this should be impossible: the pathway with the least reasoning capacity in the wiki's whole ARC-1 table scores at the human average. Under B it is the prediction — the perception was the binding constraint, so supplying it natively releases the score without any reasoning being added.

The ablation makes the attribution sharper than the headline does. The gain is decomposed prior by prior on one fixed architecture and data budget: 1D→2D positional embedding, 2×2 patchification on a 64×64 canvas, translation augmentation, scale augmentation — **+27.7 points cumulatively**, of which the canvas-enabled half is +11.5 and scale augmentation alone is +6.2. None of those terms is a reasoning term. They are all statements about how the input is *read*.

Two caveats the row must carry. **(i)** This is not the row's discriminating experiment — no parse is supplied to anything; VARC has no parse at all — so it bounds A rather than closing the row. **(ii)** It is a *different pathway*, not the same models given better perception, so strictly it shows a perception-native architecture can reach human-average on ARC-1, not that the VLM deficit is perceptual. But combined with [[wiki/entities/blindtest.md]] (a large perceptual floor exists, with no rule in the task) the two results bracket the claim from both sides: the floor is real, and removing it is worth the whole gap.

**What it does for this row's `Closes when`.** BlindTest is `T215`'s discriminating experiment run one stage earlier and with the rule removed rather than the parse supplied — the modality is held at vision and the *reasoning demand is set to zero*. The result: 58.07% mean against a 24% chance level and a human 100%, with slow-thinking variants no better (71.59 vs 72.75; 42.48 vs 54.13) and chain-of-thought, 2-shot and meta-prompting all null. A reasoning deficit cannot explain a floor on a task with no rule in it, so position A is now bounded: whatever A explains, it does not explain the perceptual floor, which exists and is large and is measurable without a rule. The row's own experiment — supply the parse as an object list and re-score — remains unrun, and `raw/wang-2026-perception-bottleneck-abstract-reasoning-benchmarks.md` is queued as the source that claims to have run it.

**The within-model version of the modality comparison, and it is a null that bounds position B's prescription** ([[wiki/entities/vdm-i2i.md]], Acuaviva et al. 2025). One VLM (Gemma-3-4B), one task (Sudoku), `n = 1000` fine-tuning samples, three input configurations and nothing else varied: text-only **0.06**, image+text **0.06**, image-only **0.00** (relative accuracy 0.79 / 0.78 / **0.12**). Adding the image is worth exactly nothing and removing the text is fatal, which is this row's modality asymmetry measured inside a single model rather than across a leaderboard. The diagnostic follow-up is the sharper part: trained on the *easier* task of transcribing its own image input back into the textual grid, at `n = 3, 5, 10` the image-only model reproduces training samples verbatim regardless of input — it memorises where it cannot read. Two consequences. **(i)** It is independent confirmation, on a different architecture and a non-ARC grid task, of the visual-error rates position B reports. **(ii)** It bounds B's prescription in the same direction `T388` does: "it needs a parser" cannot be satisfied by bolting a vision encoder onto a language model, because the configuration that has one gains nothing over the configuration that does not. The same paper's positive result is the contrast that makes this legible — a *generative* video backbone on the identical grids beats the language model outright (ARC-AGI 16.75 vs 8.00), so the visual channel is usable and the VLM's is not.

**The row's own experiment, run in its approximate form, and it moves the row without closing it** ([[wiki/concepts/perception-reasoning-dissociation.md]], Wang et al. 2026). Three ARC-style corpora — Mini-ARC, ACRE, Bongard-LOGO — each image independently converted to a natural-language description by a VLM (never a pair, never the task, uniform across the benchmark, image retained alongside), then re-scored. Same model on both sides: **8.05 → 20.13**, **62.00 → 73.00**, **22.00 → 34.50**. Manual attribution of reasoning traces puts **84–89%** of one-stage failures at the two perception steps. On ACRE, swapping a weak describer (LLaVA-1.5) for a strong one (GPT-4o) drives perception errors to **exactly zero**, leaving nine errors all inductive — the only configuration in the wiki where a visual reasoning failure has been reduced to a pure reasoning residue.

Three things this settles and one it does not.

**(i) Position B gains its first *in situ* repair.** Every prior datum for B was a deficit measurement ([[wiki/concepts/rule-level-evaluation.md]]'s visual-error rates, [[wiki/entities/blindtest.md]]'s rule-free floor) or a different pathway ([[wiki/entities/varc.md]]). This is the same models, the same tasks, perception eased and reasoning untouched, and the score moves by a factor on Mini-ARC.

**(ii) It also prices the reasoning term B's prescription assumes away.** With perception errors at zero on ACRE, the hybrid still scores 82.50 against GPT-4o end-to-end at 93.00 — a 10.5-point gap that cannot be perceptual, because the perception stage is shared. And repairing perception *converts* errors rather than deleting them: Mini-ARC inductive errors 4 → 9, deductive 1 → 4. Perception was masking reasoning failures, so "perception is dominant" and "the reasoning stack is already adequate" are not the same claim, and only the first is supported.

**(iii) Setting 2 fails on the one grid-transformation benchmark.** The paper reports strong-describer-plus-weak-reasoner as "mostly close" to the strong model end-to-end: Bongard-LOGO 80.00 vs 78.00 (reversed), ACRE 82.50 vs 93.00, **Mini-ARC 31.54 vs 52.03**. On the ARC-format corpus, strong perception recovers well under half the strong/weak model difference — the opposite of the ordering the abstract implies, and the one benchmark whose format the rest of this row is about.

**What it does not do.** The substituted parse is generated by a VLM, not given: 59.5% of two-stage Mini-ARC errors remain perceptual. And the attribution protocol assigns each failure to the **earliest** step in a declared dependency chain, so every trace where perception *and* induction are wrong is scored perceptual by construction — the 80% headline is an upper bound under the most B-favourable admissible accounting, not a point estimate. `LEANING B` stands, strengthened; `RESOLVED B` needs the oracle parse.

**And the magnitude is the new puzzle.** [[wiki/entities/blindtest.md]] says the perceptual floor is worth most of the gap; [[wiki/entities/varc.md]] says removing the reasoner and supplying a metric on the input reaches 60.4% on ARC-1; supplying a *description* reaches 20.13% on a reduced ARC. If perception were simply absent, a correct description should buy far more than 12 points. The reading that fits all three: what is missing is not a parse but a parse that can be **re-computed under the query** — [[wiki/concepts/visual-routines.md]]'s query-relativity and `G75`, arrived at as a measurement result rather than an argument.
