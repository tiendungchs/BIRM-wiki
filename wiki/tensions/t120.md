# T120 — Is an infinitesimal nudge unusable, or is it the correct regime?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Litman's Fashion-MNIST setup is run on `E_PCN` at `T → 0`, varying the free-phase estimator while holding energy and task fixed — the one comparison that separates the two regimes.

## Position A

**Unusable.** With a Gibbs–Boltzmann state distribution at finite temperature, the perturbation `Δs = s_β − s_0` is indistinguishable from sampling noise for `β ≲ 10⁻²`; on Fashion-MNIST, `β = 0.01` equilibrium propagation sits at 20–30% (near chance) while `β = 1.0` tracks the backpropagation baseline at ~80%, and update–gradient cosine similarity rises monotonically from ~0 at `β ≈ 10⁻³` to ~0.5 at `β = 1`. The infinitesimal limit is not a safer approximation, it is one whose signal is below the noise floor of the sampler that measures it (Litman 2025, [[wiki/concepts/equilibrium-propagation.md]])

## Position B

**Correct, and two orders of magnitude smaller still.** On full ImageNet with a 10-layer convolutional predictive-coding net, top-5 error is **flat across `0.0002 ≤ β ≤ 0.1`** provided `K ≥ 4`, and the whole result (13.23% top-5 against a 12.20% backpropagation baseline) is obtained in that regime. The floor on `β` is named explicitly and it is not statistical: perturbations must survive propagation through the network *given limited machine precision* (Kerjan et al. 2026, [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether `β` is a hyperparameter to search or a quantity fixed by the theory — and, underneath it, whether the free phase is *sampled* or *solved*. Litman's `s_0` is a Monte-Carlo draw from `ρ_0`, so differencing two draws pays a sampling-noise tax that grows as `β` shrinks. Kerjan's free equilibrium is the unique global minimum of `E_PCN` with `E = 0`, reached deterministically in one forward pass, so there is no draw and no tax — only round-off. If that reading is right, the "infinitesimal equilibrium propagation fails" result is a fact about **stochastic** energy-based learners and does not transfer, and every architecture decision that follows from it (finite `β`, path-integral updates, a temperature to tune) is conditional on the free state being sampled rather than computed

## Status

`LIVE` — the likely resolution is BOTH and it is untested. No experiment varies the free-phase *estimator* while holding the energy and the task fixed, which is the one comparison that would settle it. The cheap test: run Litman's Fashion-MNIST setup on `E_PCN` at `T → 0`, where the free phase becomes a forward pass, and see whether the `β = 0.01` failure survives
