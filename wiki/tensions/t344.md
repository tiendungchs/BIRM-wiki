# T344 — What does the layer-5 branch to higher-order thalamus carry: an efference copy, or a composite-object representation?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The higher-cortical target of an L5 thick-tufted → higher-order-thalamus projection is shown to be driven by a signal that tracks the motor command (efference copy) or by one that tracks a learned object arrangement independent of the movement made to acquire it.

## Position A

**Efference copy.** L5 thick-tufted pyramids are the cortex's subcortical motor-output cells; the same axon branches to higher-order thalamic relays that project to hierarchically higher cortex. Guillery & Sherman (2002, 2011) read this as the motor command being copied upward, so what higher cortex receives is a record of the action just issued — the standard forward-model input ([[wiki/concepts/canonical-cortical-microcircuit.md]], [[wiki/entities/cerebellum.md]])

## Position B

**A composite-object representation.** The same cells are **displacement cells**, alternating between two contents: a movement vector (sent subcortically) when the two locations being differenced lie in one object space, and a composite object — "part X at offset d on object Y" — when they lie in two. Higher cortex is therefore receiving a *structural* description, not a motor record, with the two interleaved representations disambiguated at the destination by oscillatory phase or firing pattern (Hawkins et al. 2019, [[wiki/concepts/displacement-codes.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what the feedforward inter-areal channel is *for* in a machine version: a hierarchy whose upward messages are actions (so higher areas learn dynamics and must integrate to recover structure) versus one whose upward messages are already composed part-whole descriptions (so hierarchy is composition and no integration is needed above the first level). It also sets whether one channel can be time-shared at all — position B needs a multiplexing convention that nothing in the wiki has

## Status

`LIVE` — position B is offered by its own authors as "a possible alternate interpretation" of position A's anatomy, with no experiment distinguishing them and no evidence that any cortical projection is time-multiplexed by phase in this way. Position A is itself an interpretation of a branched axon rather than a measurement of what the branch carries
