# T408 — Is the heading integrator's velocity input the vestibular measurement, or a reconstruction from motor prediction plus the exafferent residual?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Heading cells (anterodorsal thalamus or postsubiculum) are recorded during matched active and passive head rotations, in the same animals, while the motor-expectation route is perturbed, either by a resistive torque during its ~40–50-movement recalibration window or by silencing neck-proprioceptive or efference input. A holds if heading tracks the vestibular-nerve signal in every condition. B holds if heading under active motion follows the cancelled-plus-predicted signal: it drifts or mis-scales during the recalibration window, or fails when the efference/proprioceptive route is silenced while passive tuning survives.

## Position A

**The vestibular measurement.** The classic view: angular head velocity from the semicircular canals passes through the vestibular nuclei and the nucleus prepositus / supragenual nucleus to the dorsal tegmental ↔ lateral mammillary ring, which integrates it. Evidence: bilateral labyrinth lesions, canal occlusion or inactivation, and horizontal-canal-deficient mice all abolish heading in anterodorsal thalamus or postsubiculum. Heading cells keep their tuning under passive rotation, when only vestibular cues are available. Every brainstem stage on the route contains angular-head-velocity cells. The wiki is written this way: the lateral mammillary nucleus "turns vestibular input into heading" ([[wiki/entities/mammillary-bodies.md]], [[wiki/concepts/reference-frame-transformation.md]]), and the integrator's `a_t` is a measured self-motion signal ([[wiki/concepts/path-integration.md]]).

## Position B

**A reconstruction: the sensor stage passes on only the unpredicted part.** At the first central stage, vestibular-only neurons are ~70% attenuated during active head movement (monkeys of several species; mice). The cancellation happens only when neck proprioception matches the motor expectation, and it is cerebellum-dependent. The other output classes carry gaze: position-vestibular-pause neurons are suppressed during saccades and pursuit, and the primate nucleus prepositus outputs mostly eye position. So during voluntary behaviour no vestibular-nucleus output carries pure head motion. On the behavioural side, a rat moved passively by cart into a novel room loses stable preferred directions despite a normal vestibular signal, while a rat that walks in keeps them. Something other than the vestibular signal (proprioception, efference copy) carries orientation across. The source's candidate mechanism is context-dependent decoding: the residual ~30% is up-weighted under active motion and/or summed with proprioceptive and efference estimates inside the heading network (Cullen & Taube 2017, [[wiki/entities/head-direction-system.md]]).

## Where it bites

**What rides the integrator's input wire, and therefore where the forward model sits.**

| | Under A | Under B |
|---|---|---|
| Integrator input | The sensor reading | `â_t(efference, proprioception) + e_t` (prediction plus exafferent residual) |
| Forward model | Beside the integrator, used for perception or control | **On the integrator's input path** |
| Effect of a body-dynamics change | None on heading | Transient heading drift or gain error while the forward model recalibrates (~40–50 movements) |
| VR / head-fixed setups | Heading follows whatever vestibular input remains | Heading follows a recalibrated model and may encode the immersed frame |
| Machine consequence | Any integrator can take raw velocity | An integrator fed by a sensor stage that cancels reafference must also be fed the efference prediction, or it integrates only externally caused motion |

It also bites `G92`. Under B the agent/world partition for head motion is computed at the first synapse, and the integrator has to re-add the "agent" half.

## Status

`LIVE`. The source states this as an open conundrum and has no data that decides it. Two qualifications. The cancellation data are mostly monkey and the heading data mostly rat. And the one species difference reported (mouse vestibular-only neurons carry a static neck-position signal unchanged between active and passive motion) offers a partial answer for rodents that does not hold in primates. Heading under active versus passive motion is itself mixed: attenuated passive responses in restrained rats, matched responses in head-fixed anterodorsal recordings.
