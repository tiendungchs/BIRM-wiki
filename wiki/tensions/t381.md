# T381 — Is the medial diencephalon a relay serving the hippocampal memory stream, or a co-equal second stream?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The two candidate streams are lesioned separately with the hippocampal stream cut *downstream* of its diencephalic branch point (so the subiculum's cortical output survives while the fornix/mammillary route is gone, and the converse), and episodic-memory cost is scored on the same task in the same study. A holds if the diencephalic cut costs strictly less. B holds if the two costs are approximately equal and a convergence-cortex lesion costs at least as much as either.

## Position A

**A relay.** The anterior thalamic nuclei and mammillary bodies are downstream enablers for the hippocampus — the Papez-circuit framing, and the one every thalamic page in this wiki is written under: [[wiki/entities/nucleus-reuniens.md]] is "the controller's return path", [[wiki/entities/mediodorsal-thalamus.md]] is a gate whose variable is pooled from the cortex it gates, and [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]] assigns the store and the slow cortical learner the two roles with nothing between them. The supporting data: the diencephalic structures' spatial content is largely hippocampal in origin (subiculum → fornix and → mammillary bodies → anterior thalamus), and complete hippocampal-formation lesions are more disruptive than anterior thalamic lesions on reinforced spatial alternation (Aggleton & O'Mara 2022's own contrary datum).

## Position B

**A co-equal parallel stream.** A hippocampal–cortical stream and a medial-diencephalic–cortical stream run in parallel, partially independent, converging on shared cortical zones where synchronous activity from *both* is what consolidation requires (Aggleton & O'Mara 2022, [[wiki/entities/anterior-thalamic-nuclei.md]]). Supporting: mammillothalamic-tract involvement is the best predictor of memory loss after human thalamic infarct; anterior thalamic lesions abolish spatial-responsive firing in the **subiculum** while sparing CA1, so influence runs store-ward as well; the mammillary bodies and the ascending tegmental input reach the hippocampus through nothing but this route; the two input tracts onto the anterior thalamus carry opposite plasticity rules; and the nuclei support an attentional-set function (paradoxical facilitation of dimension switching) with no hippocampal counterpart.

## Where it bites

**How many long-term-memory modules a reasoning architecture needs, and where the consolidation criterion lives.** Under A there is one store with a bandwidth extension, and the wiki's existing decomposition stands. Under B there are two partially independent write paths into the same cortical target, and consolidation is a **coincidence gate at the destination** — plasticity fires when both streams arrive together — rather than a schedule at the source, which is how `G14` and every replay account in the wiki currently frame it. B also makes "lesion the store, measure the deficit" an invalid instrument for module assignment in this circuit, since the two streams share a source and a target and differ only in path.

## Status

`LIVE` — one review argues B against a century of A, and the decisive comparison (equal-severity lesions of the two paths on one task) is exactly what the review says does not exist. The intermediate position is live too: the streams could be co-equal for *consolidation* and hierarchical for *encoding*.
