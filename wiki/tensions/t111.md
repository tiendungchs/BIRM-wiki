# T111 — Is a control representation a stable property of the tissue, or instantiated by the demand?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The same site is probed under matched distributions differing only in how many tactics are valid, testing whether participation or per-cell rate carries the effect.

## Position A

**Stable, and modulated.** Every representational claim in the wiki reads this way: a region or a layer *has* a code for rule, category, target or task set, and task conditions change how strongly it is expressed — which is what licenses probing a trained model once and reporting what it contains ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]], [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-control.md]])

## Position B

**Instantiated.** In monkey posterior medial prefrontal cortex the *number of neurons representing the task at all* falls significantly (`p < 0.01`, χ²) when the task is retrained so only one response tactic is valid, and returns to its original size when both are restored — same animals, same penetrations, actions still varying, accuracy 99–100%, and no change in pre-SMA or SMA recorded in parallel. Earlier studies found no forelimb-task representation in this territory because their tasks required one tactic; the authors conclude the conventional concept of cortical motor representation "necessitates reconsideration" (Matsuzaka et al. 2012, [[wiki/concepts/policy-abstraction-hierarchy.md]]). A second instance from the same laboratory extends the claim to a *downstream* area and to a *sensory* variable: pre-SMA carries no significant cue-position signal in the mixed-tactic task, though pre-SMA is spatially tuned in reaching tasks with no tactic choice — read as the upstream area having absorbed the binding and never passing the argument down (Awan et al. 2020, [[wiki/concepts/policy-abstraction-hierarchy.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether a null probe result is evidence about the model or about the evaluation distribution — and whether a machine controller should be built as a always-present module whose output is gated, or as a representation that exists only while a decision at its level is live. Under B, "component X does not represent Y" is unfalsifiable without a demand manipulation, and every interpretability sweep in the wiki is missing that control

## Status

`LIVE` — not a clean contradiction, since a population that stops carrying the task may still hold its wiring, and the measure is participation (task-related cells per penetration) rather than per-cell rate. But the reversibility across a two-week retraining cycle is a control the machine literature has no analogue of, and the discriminating experiment is cheap: probe the same site under matched distributions differing only in whether the probed decision is live
