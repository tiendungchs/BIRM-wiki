# T12 — Are the conceptual primitives of infancy installed, or learned from sensorimotor prediction in the first months?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Installed.** Core knowledge is a bank of domain-specific systems present at birth, individuating entities by entry conditions and carrying principles (cohesion, continuity, contact) that no amount of sensorimotor correlation would supply ([[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]])

## Position B

**Learned, but early.** Butz 2016 takes the *same* evidence — newborns anticipating thumb-to-mouth contact, the rooting reflex suppressed for self-touch, goal-directed reaching in the first months — as showing that a progressively accurate predictive model of one's *own body* is already in place, learned in utero. "Innate" conceptual primitives are then the earliest abstractions over that model, and what is genuinely installed is not content but three *typed prediction channels* plus a segmentation bias ([[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what an architecture must ship with. A: domain-specific content and entry gates (gap G23). B: content-free structural biases plus an embodied data stream — which relocates the whole cost onto the data problem, since developmental-scale self-motivated sensorimotor experience is exactly what no robot can supply

## Status

`LIVE, and the discriminating experiment has now been half-run` — Garrido et al. 2025 supply a system with **no** typed channels, no embodiment and no installed content, only prediction in a learned representation space on passive natural video, and it develops the object principles it is tested on: 98% on IntPhys, `g` 8–11 on permanence / continuity / shape constancy over an untrained null, ≥ naive humans on every IntPhys property, at 115M parameters on 128 h of unique video ([[wiki/concepts/violation-of-expectation.md]]). This moves B's burden without discharging it — the result shows a sufficient learned route exists for **two** of the object system's three principles, and the third, **contact**, is the one at chance (solidity, collision). It is also a machine result and not a claim about infants. B's remaining debt is unchanged in kind: nothing shows the human system took this route
