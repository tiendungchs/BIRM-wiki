# T379 — Is the direction of a cortical message set by frequency *order*, or by a fixed set of named channels?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Feedforward theta-band Granger influence is measured with retinal resampling removed — retinal stabilization, a non-visual modality, or a paralyzed preparation. If the ~4 Hz feedforward channel survives, the ordering rule is false and cortex has a slow forward channel; if it disappears with the microsaccades, the ordering rule stands and theta was imported from the eye.

## Position A

**Order.** Predictive coding *derives* the asymmetry rather than fitting it: expectations accumulate prediction errors, so integration low-pass filters going up, while the nonlinear prediction map `f, g` re-creates high frequencies going down — giving `|μ(ω)| ∝ |ξ(ω)|/ω` and the conclusion that **error must express higher frequencies than the estimate that absorbs it** (Bastos et al. 2012, [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]]). This is a statement about the *relative* position of two bands, not about which two, and the framework says nothing about why gamma and beta specifically. It is supported by the laminar spectra (supragranular gamma, infragranular alpha/beta) and by the beta-feedback / gamma-feedforward halves of the measurement below

## Position B

**Named channels.** Correlating per-frequency Granger-influence asymmetry against anatomical SLN across 28 macaque visual area pairs yields **three** narrow bands with fixed direction labels — theta ~4 Hz feedforward, beta 14–18 Hz feedback, gamma 60–80 Hz feedforward — and nothing systematic from 80 to 250 Hz (Bastos et al. 2015, [[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]]). The theta feedforward channel is **slower than the feedback channel**, which no ordering rule permits. The band assignment is also present before the cue and before stimulus onset, i.e. it is a property of the circuit rather than of the stimulus, and interareal coherence in the same data has exactly three peaks — a discrete channel set, not a spectrum with a crossover

## Where it bites

Whether a builder may use a spectral statistic as a **weight-free probe of computational role** — the use [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]] proposes and [[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]] carries as a rule. Under A the probe is general: in any two coupled populations, the one shifted low is the integrator. Under B the probe is a lookup table valid only for cortex, three bands wide, and a model whose units run at arbitrary timescales inherits no direction labels at all. It also decides the routing primitive: an ordering rule gives one continuous axis to modulate, a channel set gives `k` independently gateable carriers on the same edge — which is the cheaper dynamic-routing mechanism and the one that would let a level's depth be retuned without rewiring

## Status

`LIVE`, and cheap to settle because the two positions make opposite predictions about one control condition the source did not run. The source's own escape favours A: theta-rhythmic **microsaccades** move the retina at ~4 Hz and each produces a fresh ascending volley, so the theta channel may be an input artefact rather than a cortical message — and the paper's conditional-Granger analysis (removing influence mediated by any other recorded area) left gamma and beta intact while *degrading* theta, "suggesting the involvement of larger networks for theta". That is exactly the signature [[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]]'s first rule assigns to a relayed clock rather than to direct axonal drive, recovered in visual cortex from an independent dataset — two arguments pointing the same way. But the paper reports the microsaccade exclusion as leaving **gamma** unchanged and does not report it clearing theta, so the artefact reading is stated and not demonstrated. Note that A and B are not fully exclusive: a channel set whose members happen to be ordered would satisfy both, and the dispute is only about whether the theta member is real
