# T83 — Is memory allocation decided by the excitability of neurons just before learning?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The top-down cortical term is tested *inside* the excitability window, where the tag account currently predicts it is unnecessary rather than absent.

## Position A

**Yes — allocation is a local winner-take-all over a decaying excitability tag.** CREB-dependent excitability set by a recent learning event is *sufficient* (KCNQ2/3 dominant-negative → preferential recruitment; Kir2.1 → ~5× less likely to be in the trace; a step-function opsin raising excitability just before conditioning determines which amygdala neurons store the association) and *necessary* (silencing the tagged cells impairs recall). The prior is temporal proximity and nothing else; the rule needs no comparison, no retrieval and no similarity metric (Lisman et al. 2018, [[wiki/concepts/memory-allocation-excitability.md]])

## Position B

**Only inside a window, and it is overruled from cortex outside it.** Inhibiting vmPFC→MEC projections during encoding of a second context 7 d after the first *demotes* the neurons that were most active in the home cage immediately before learning (24% → 18% of the top-10% ensemble) and promotes neurons tagged 7 days earlier, whose CREB tag has long since decayed (15% → 27%) — the opposite of what a pre-learning-excitability competition predicts. Activating the same projection forces separation even at the 5 h interval where local integration normally wins (de Sousa et al. 2026)

## Where it bites

Whether an allocator is a local, content-blind, load-independent competition or a two-term rule with a top-down bias. Under A a machine store allocates by an eligibility vector alone; under B it needs a cross-module path from the slow learner to the fast store's allocator, computed from similarity between the current episode and consolidated content — which no memory-augmented architecture in the wiki has, and which is only available once the slow store has something to compare against (G14, G38). Caveats on B: excitability was never measured directly (activity is the proxy), inhibition and activation are not clean opposites of one variable (both reduce the home-cage fraction), and the effect is absent at 5 h and absent when the two episodes share no features

## Status

`LEANING` — the two are compatible as a *partition of the time axis* (blind-and-cheap inside the tag window, informed-and-cortical outside it), which is the reading both sources' data support; what is untested is whether the top-down term also operates within the window and is simply not needed there
