# T247 — Does a generated representation carry information the source did not, or only re-express it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The operator is estimated from a population and applied where the individual's own task scan exists, measuring how much of the gain is the individual's and how much is the population model's.

## Position A

**Only re-express it, and the gain measures the reader.** The connectome-to-connectome map is a deterministic function `T̂ = f(R)` of one resting scan, so by the data-processing inequality it cannot contain more about that individual than `R` does — yet it lifts fluid-intelligence prediction from `r = 0.076` to `0.180` and matches 300 subjects' worth of raw resting data with 100. On this reading the behaviourally relevant variance was in `R` all along, in directions the downstream linear edge-selection reader could not use, and the operator's whole contribution is a change of basis. What is being measured is the *consumer's* inductive bias, not the representation's content (Yoo et al. 2022, [[wiki/concepts/connectome-state-transformation.md]])

## Position B

**Information is added — from other people.** The operator's coefficients are estimated from 285 other subjects' *task* scans, so the generated connectome is a fusion of one individual's resting graph with a population-level model of what the task does. The data-processing bound applies to the pair (individual scan, fixed function) and says nothing about a function that itself carries hundreds of subjects' task data as parameters; the fact that accuracy keeps climbing with the number of *training* subjects (50→300) while the test individual's data is unchanged is direct evidence the added parameters are doing the work. On this reading the map is an imputation of unmeasured task data, and its failure mode is an individual whose deformation is atypical

## Where it bites

Every generative or transformational stage in the wiki that is scored by a downstream metric: whether the score credits new content or a better-matched basis decides whether to invest in the generator or in the reader. Sharpest where the reader is fixed and linear — connectome-based predictive modelling here, but equally probing classifiers ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]) and any amortised map scored by a task head ([[wiki/concepts/amortized-inference.md]]). The two readings also differ in prediction: (A) says a better reader on raw `R` should close the gap, (B) says it cannot
