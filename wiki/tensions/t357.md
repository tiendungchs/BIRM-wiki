# T357 — Does a negative teaching signal carry its address, or is the address discarded at the first relay?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** One preparation images the claimed **parallel modality pathway** alongside CGRP<sup>PBN</sup> in the same animal across the same stimulus set — pinch, lipopolysaccharide, satiation, novel food, conditioned tone. If a second ascending line separates those stimuli while CGRP<sup>PBN</sup> does not, the division of labour is real and both positions stand at their own levels. If nothing downstream separates them, the forebrain genuinely receives one unaddressed negative scalar and A is a fact about the hypothalamus only. A cheaper partial discriminator: test whether CGRP<sup>PBN</sup> response *shape* (amplitude, duration, target-specific release) predicts stimulus class — if it does, the address survives inside the "scalar" and the row is malformed.

## Position A

**The address is which channel moved, and there is one channel per regulated variable.** Hunger and thirst are carried by separate need-specific populations — arcuate AGRP and subforniceal NOS1 — with different hormonal inputs, different behavioural outputs (subforniceal activation raises water intake and the progressive-ratio breakpoint for water, not for food) and different targets. No tag in the signal, no learned gate: the count of broadcast scalars is set by the count of regulated internal variables (Betley et al. 2015, [[wiki/concepts/homeostatic-need-signal.md]]). This is the wiki's current structural answer to the address problem stated on [[wiki/concepts/reward-prediction-error.md]] — the "approach food when actually thirsty" failure mode.

## Position B

**The address is discarded one synapse up.** The same individual CGRP<sup>PBN</sup> neurons are activated by mechanical, thermal, electrical and chemical cutaneous pain, by visceral lipopolysaccharide malaise, by itch, by meal-termination satiety (arriving from the nucleus of the solitary tract, i.e. the very signal that terminates the feeding A's channel regulates), by a novel food or object, and by a shock-paired tone — 384/384 neurons responding to tail pinch, and every neuron in the feeding assay also pinch-responsive. The population is inhibited as one by food anticipation. Modality and origin are, in the authors' words, "likely" carried by *parallel pathways* — asserted, not recorded (Campos et al. 2018, [[wiki/concepts/general-danger-channel.md]]).

## Where it bites

**Whether a builder may rely on channel identity as the addressing scheme.** Under A, a bank of need-specific scalars is a complete solution: the agent never confuses hunger with thirst because the signals are different wires all the way to the consumer. Under B, that guarantee expires at the first convergence point, and any architecture that puts one downstream of the other has to *re-derive* modality from a second stream that must be built, maintained and aligned in time with the scalar. The two designs differ in what is denied: A's cannot express a general "something is wrong" state that recruits defensive behaviour before the cause is known; B's cannot express need-specific action selection from the broadcast alone.

The positions are not straightforwardly nested, because B's convergence is **not** onto an aversive-only line. A satiety signal and a footshock share cells, and appetitive anticipation drives the line *below* baseline. So B is not "aversive events pooled downstream of value" — it is a single signed axis on which a meal's end and a burn are the same quantity at different magnitudes.

## Status

`LIVE` — both results are cell-type-specific manipulations with imaging, in mouse, on circuits that are directly connected (AGRP inhibits CGRP<sup>PBN</sup>; ablating AGRP neurons disinhibits them and causes anorexia). Neither is disputed as a record. What is disputed is what the forebrain gets, and the paper that would settle it — recording the parallel modality pathway — has not been done.
