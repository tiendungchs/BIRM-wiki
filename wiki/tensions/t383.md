# T383 — Is the egocentric↔allocentric conversion an operation localised to one region, or is "no isolated function" the correct description of that region?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The translation hypothesis is given an operational definition that distinguishes performing a transformation from co-representing its arguments, and a manipulation of retrosplenial cortex is shown to change a downstream frame-dependent code — or the null effect on entorhinal boundary coding is replicated under conditions where the model predicts it must appear.

## Position A

**One region, one operation.** Retrosplenial cortex translates between viewpoint-dependent (parietal, egocentric) and viewpoint-independent (medial-temporal, allocentric) representations by cancelling a rotational offset supplied as an input by the anterodorsal/postsubicular head-direction system, possibly buffering the representations while the conversion runs. The evidence is the anatomy (the only region with early sensory input *and* thalamic heading *and* reciprocal hippocampal/parietal connections), a lesion profile that spares landmark recognition and destroys landmark-to-heading conversion, dysgranular lesions leaving anterodorsal head-direction tuning drifting, and a deficit that only becomes large under cue conflict (Vann, Aggleton & Maguire 2009, [[wiki/entities/retrosplenial-cortex.md]]). **A now exists as running code** ([[wiki/entities/bb-model.md]], Bicanski & Burgess 2018): a bank of 20 gain-field sublayers, each a copy of the allocentric boundary-vector population tuned to one heading, gated one-hot by total head-direction activity and read in both directions on one weight set — which reproduces object-cued recall, the recollection/familiarity dissociation of Papez-circuit lesions, trace cells, viewpoint-specific residual familiarity after hippocampal lesion, mental navigation and preplay-like sequences. Its value to this row is that it makes A **falsifiable**, which the review-level statement was not: the implementation entails that silencing the converter should leave boundary-anchored medial-temporal responses uninstantiated

## Position B

**No isolated function fits.** Retrosplenial anatomy and dynamics are consistent with roles in multiple sensorimotor and cognitive processes and with no single one; what the region supplies is conjunctive tuning across sensory, motor and spatial streams, from which two *non-exclusive* generalisations can be drawn — relating perspectives (of which egocentric↔allocentric is one special case among egocentric→egocentric, allocentric→allocentric and oblique shifts) and prediction with error correction. Three specific costs to A: retrosplenial inactivation has **little effect on boundary-anchored responses in medial entorhinal cortex**, the model's own downstream prediction; conjunctive egocentric–allocentric gain-field-like cells are found in many other structures, so the transformation is distributed and conjunctive coding here is not diagnostic; and "transformation" has no operational definition, so A is ill-posed rather than merely unsupported (Alexander, Place, Starrett, Chrastil & Nitz 2023)

## What the primary under B shows, and why it cuts both ways

Alexander & Nitz 2015 — the recording B's route-centered frame rests on — puts three frames (egocentric turn, route position, room position) in the same 228 retrosplenial cells, multiplicatively nested, with no isolated place fields. For A, this is the gain-field construction measured in the right region and is the closest thing to positive evidence the position has. For B, it is the definitional objection made concrete: the paper's own positive claim is that *downstream* structures could extract any of the three variables or their combinations, i.e. the region supplies a **basis** and the transformation, if any, is the readout's. That makes `Closes when`'s operational definition a demand on the downstream population rather than on retrosplenial tuning — no measurement of cells in this region, at any resolution, can distinguish converting from co-representing.

## A third assignment, from the anatomy, that neither position states

Kravitz et al. 2011 ([[wiki/entities/dorsal-visual-stream.md]]) place the egocentric↔allocentric coordination in **two serial stages, not one**: posterior cingulate (areas 23/31) *and* retrosplenial cortex (29/30), the two intermediate relays of the parieto-medial-temporal pathway, each credited with contributing to the coordination. The supporting datum for the upstream stage is positive and independent of anything in A or B — posterior cingulate units encode saccade-target location in **allocentric coordinates preserved across whole-body rotation** (Dean et al.), and posterior cingulate inactivation impairs following previously learned routes. This costs A its locality without granting B its conclusion: a two-stage conversion is still a conversion, but no single-region manipulation can produce the signature A predicts, which is consistent with the null entorhinal result B leads with. The same review states that the mechanism of the transformation is unidentified at every stage and names measuring the egocentric/allocentric balance *per stage* as the missing experiment — which is `Closes when`'s operational-definition demand arrived at from anatomy.

## Where it bites

Whether a reasoning architecture gets a **converter module** — one block that owns the transform between two representational formats and nothing else, parameterised by an externally computed group element (`G39`'s shape) — or whether frame conversion is a property that has to be distributed across every module that holds a frame ([[wiki/concepts/distributed-reference-frames.md]]'s Prediction 4). The two differ in cost, in failure mode and in what an ablation would show: A predicts both representations survive a converter lesion and only the change of perspective is lost; B predicts no localised ablation produces that signature, which is close to what the entorhinal result reports. The row also carries a measurement problem the wiki generalises past this region: A cannot be falsified until *performing a transformation* is distinguished from *co-representing its arguments*, and the same objection applies to every verb the wiki assigns a module (`anchor`, `gate`, `bind`, `broadcast`)

## Status

`LIVE` — and the addition of an implementation on A's side does not move it, because the implementation's one downstream entailment is the entailment B reports as null; what it changes is that the row can now be settled by a single experiment rather than by reconciling two reviews. B is the later review with more data and holds the one failed entailment; A holds the one lesion signature (landmark recognition intact, heading derivation lost) that B never explains. Neither review is a study, and the two share most of their evidence base while differing on what it licenses, so the row cannot be settled by weighing citations — it needs the definitional repair named in `Closes when` first
