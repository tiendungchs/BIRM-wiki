# T260 — Does the brain have an absolute activity baseline, or only relative ones?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The zero point is validated across sessions and states, not only spatially within one — A's invariant is currently within-session, and B argues from A's own data.

## Position A

**Absolute, and physiologically definable.** The oxygen extraction fraction is spatially near-uniform at awake eyes-closed rest (95% confidence limits ±3%) despite ~4× grey/white variation in blood flow and oxygen consumption, because supply equilibrates to each region's long-run modal demand; so the hemisphere mean is a zero point, and the four canonical task-decrease regions sit at it (ratios 0.98–1.04, all n.s.) — the deactivations are decreases from baseline, not returns from an unnoticed activation (Raichle et al. 2001, [[wiki/concepts/activity-baseline.md]])

## Position B

**Only relative.** The same paper's tables show left area 40 at 0.90 of the hemisphere mean in *both* groups (p = 0.07 and 0.04), a replicated ~10% decrease surviving only the multiple-comparison correction; regional oxygen extraction correlates across two independent groups at `r = 0.89`, where a constant field would correlate at zero; and global oxygen extraction is 0.40 in one group and 0.30 in the other on the same protocol with different scanners. Buckner et al. 2008 make the same reading: *modulated but weakly*, not constant ([[wiki/entities/default-mode-network.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether "deactivation" is a property of a region or of a contrast — and, transferred, whether an interpretability method can report engagement without a control condition. Position B leaves every activation claim in the wiki relative to a chosen baseline task, which is the objection Position A was constructed to escape

## Status

`LEANING B` — B is argued from A's own primary data, and A's invariant is at best within-session and spatial. What survives A is the *strategy* (define a zero from an equilibrium invariant rather than a subtraction) and the two dissociations it delivers (tonic rate ⊥ engagement state; a region's zero is its ecologically ordinary condition, not its no-input condition), neither of which requires the invariant to be exact
