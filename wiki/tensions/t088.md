# T88 — What is prefrontal delay-period activity *about* — the item held, or the place attention is pointed?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Attention is measured independently rather than inferred from task design, and motor preparation to both potential targets excluded, across the dorsolateral/ventrolateral gradient.

## Position A

**The item.** Sustained, spatially tuned firing after a cue disappears is the classic memory trace (Fuster & Alexander 1971; Funahashi et al. 1989), inactivating dorsolateral prefrontal cortex produces "mnemonic scotomas" in localized regions of space (Funahashi et al. 1993a), and antisaccade recordings find more than twice as many cells coding the *stimulus* location as the movement target — read as evidence for a sensory memory. Every maintenance design in [[wiki/concepts/working-memory.md]] takes this reading, T86 and T87 argue only about *how* the item is carried

## Position B

**The pointer.** With the attended and remembered locations dissociated inside one trial, **61%** of spatially tuned dorsolateral prefrontal cells code the attended location and **16%** the remembered one (`I_Att` = 1.84 vs. `I_Rem` = 1.21; 8.8 vs. 5.3 spikes/s preferred-minus-least-preferred), ensemble decoding agrees, tuning to the attended location is *stronger late in the delay* than early with sensory input identical in every coordinate frame, memory tuning is absent at cue onset and develops during the trial, and both locations are equally reward-predictive until the trigger so reward anticipation cannot explain the asymmetry. A's two pillars are reinterpreted: a "mnemonic scotoma" is equally a localized failure to attend, and antisaccade/brightness cues confound the remembered location with the attention-attracting one (Lebedev et al. 2004)

## Where it bites

Everything the wiki reads off prefrontal delay recordings. T86's "sample decodable across the delay" and T87's transient index are both scored on tasks that left attention uncontrolled, so a delay signal cannot be assumed to be about the stored item. For a builder it splits fast **M** in two: a content register and an attentional pointer register, which no wiki architecture separates (gap G48)

## Status

`LEANING B` for *this* task and this region, with three limits the authors state: attention was not independently measured (inferred from task design and a 36 ms reaction-time cost for reoriented saccades); motor preparation to either potential target is not excluded; and the result is regional rather than global — ventrolateral of the principal sulcus the population is 28% attention / 2% memory cells, dorsomedially it is 8% / 9% / 10%, so a recording site chooses the answer. The paper explicitly declines to replace maintenance monolithism with attention monolithism: delay activity also carries rules, categories, task selection and event prediction
