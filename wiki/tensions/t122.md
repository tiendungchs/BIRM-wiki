# T122 — What does phasic dopamine carry — a reward prediction error, or the precision of a prediction error?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** An experiment dissociates teaching signal from gain: dopamine manipulated where the two readings predict opposite effects on learning rate versus incentive salience. A cheaper discriminator now exists on the behavioural side — B predicts that dopamine depletion has a *level-dependent sign* (slower cue-driven reactions from low-level depletion, faster set switching from high-level depletion), which A has no reason to expect from a single teaching signal; the sequence-violation version of it (on/off-medication differences concentrated on trials that break an established sequence, near-absent on in-sequence and on genuinely random trials) is stated in Friston et al. 2012 and untested. Upstream row is T85.

## Position A

**Reward prediction error.** The standard reading the wiki runs on everywhere: `δ = r + γV(s′) − V(s)`, a *teaching signal* delivered to a plasticity rule from outside the network ([[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]]'s PVLV, [[wiki/entities/meta-rl-agent.md]]'s input channel, every temporal-difference model here). Its evidence is the firing record itself, reproduced in detail by two Rescorla–Wagner systems, and it now has a primary source in the wiki that states the whole taxonomy A belongs to — dopamine `δ`, serotonin `γ`, noradrenaline `β`, acetylcholine `α` — together with circuit placements and six control laws for the other three ([[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]], Doya 2002). That sharpens the disagreement: B assigns acetylcholine the *precision of exteroceptive inference* and A assigns it the *learning rate*, so the two accounts now differ on at least two of the four chemicals, not one.

## Position B

**Precision — "the value of prediction error", not "the prediction error on value".** Under the free-energy formulation, value ≡ surprise and expected value ≡ entropy, so the value function is redundant and dopamine's classical *neuromodulatory* action (postsynaptic gain) is read as encoding the precision of proprioceptive/interoceptive prediction errors — i.e. the Rescorla–Wagner **learning rate** and incentive salience, symmetric with acetylcholine doing the same for exteroceptive inference in posterior cortex. Supporting observation: low dopamine ⇒ imprecise priors ⇒ small prediction errors ⇒ **poverty of action** (bradykinesia in Parkinson's disease and under neuroleptics), which an RPE account explains only indirectly (Friston 2009, [[wiki/concepts/precision-weighting.md]]).

**B now has a primary source that runs** ([[wiki/entities/affordance-active-inference-model.md]], Friston et al. 2012), and it supplies three things the 2009 framing did not. (i) A *mechanistic objection to A*: dopamine acts on G-protein-coupled receptors in glutamatergic spines and can only modulate a postsynaptic response, never excite one — so it cannot be the wire carrying an error's content, whatever it correlates with. (ii) Two firing observations that are awkward for A and natural for B: a substantial fraction of dopamine neurons *increase* firing to aversive stimuli and their predictive cues — **a claim A's own primary source flatly denies** (air puffs and saline drops evoke no transient; "very few" neurons respond to aversive-predicting cues; Schultz, Dayan & Montague 1997), so this half of B's evidence is disputed at the level of the record and is now carried separately as [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T313 — and dopamine neurons fire to cues that predict the future *availability of information* about a reward while conveying no information about the reward itself — both are cues portending a predictable sequence, i.e. precision, and neither is a reward error. Dopaminergic discharge also covaries with the *variance* of juice rewards. (iii) A behaving demonstration: with dopamine present only as a fixed postsynaptic gain, one simulated depletion reproduces bradykinesia, perseveration and a set-switching failure, and the syndrome changes qualitatively with the *level* at which the gain is depleted.

## Where it bites

Which slot the signal occupies in an architecture. Position A makes it an input to a *learning rule*; position B makes it a run-time **gain register** inside the *inference dynamics* — the very register gaps G50 and G56 say the wiki lacks. A builder cannot implement both without deciding which one the modulator's set-point is optimising. Both readings are now stated side by side on [[wiki/concepts/reward-prediction-error.md]], together with the licence that keeps this deferrable — the striatal mechanism needs only that the signal move bidirectionally with outcome. The wiki has no experiment separating them, and the source lists the reconciliation of this taxonomy (dopamine = reward error, serotonin = reward timescale, noradrenaline = action randomness, acetylcholine = memory-update speed) with a single precision role as explicitly open.
