# T165 — Is augmentation fragility a consequence of the *contrastive* objective?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The one-run discriminator: Barlow Twins with an EMA target on one branch under crop-only. Recovery toward −13 implicates target richness; staying at −25 implicates the negatives.

## Position A

**Yes — the negatives author the shortcut.** Crops of one image share a colour histogram and histograms differ across images, so a discrimination task on crop-only augmentations is solved by histogram and the representation is never charged for more; SimCLR loses **27.6** points on that ablation where BYOL, whose target is a regression onto a richer representation rather than a discrimination, loses **13.1** and still reaches 59.4%. The wiki carries this as the generalisable statement that *an objective's shortcut is fixed by its noise distribution* (Grill et al. 2020, [[wiki/entities/byol.md]], [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]])

## Position B

**No — a negative-free learner is just as fragile.** Barlow Twins has no negatives, no noise distribution and no discrimination anywhere in its loss, and degrades on the same progressive-removal sweep **like SimCLR** (≈ −25 points at crop-only, Fig. 3) rather than like BYOL. The authors read the same fact as a *feature*: the learned invariances are "better controlled by the specific set of distortions used", where BYOL's are "generic and intriguingly independent" of them (Zbontar et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/barlow-twins.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what the augmentation set is. Under A it is a shortcut-design surface specific to contrastive methods, and the repair is to drop the negatives. Under B the operative variable is whether the objective's *only* source of signal is the distortion — true of both SimCLR and Barlow Twins, false of BYOL, whose EMA target carries features no augmentation demanded — so augmentation design is unavoidable for anything whose loss is a function of two views alone, and BYOL is the outlier needing the explanation. It also strains [[wiki/concepts/divergence-objectives.md]]'s rate–distortion split: all three share `f(X)` and therefore the rate, so that framing predicts equal degradation and does not get it. **And the premise that augmentation design is unavoidable is false**: an image JEPA takes its two views from *masking one image* and needs no distortions at all, reaching 81.1 ImageNet linear evaluation ([[wiki/entities/i-jepa.md]]) — which does not settle A vs B but moves the design surface, since that method's mask sampler is then worth 34 points and is the same kind of unavoidable choice under a different name (T167)
