# T289 — Did the human-specific capacity require a new component, or a re-weighting of components that were already there?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Spec:** `D191`, `D235`, `D236`, `D254` ([[_brainstorm/birm-spec.md]] §11)  
**Closes when:** A behavioural prediction is derived from the anatomical-continuity position, or an anatomical correlate proposed for the behavioural discontinuity — the two evidence types currently cannot meet.

## Position A

**One new operator.** Hauser, Chomsky & Fitch 2002's H3: narrow syntax — recursion plus its two interface mappings — is uniquely human, small, discrete, and plausibly exapted from a non-communicative domain rather than gradually built. The comparative subtraction has a hard boundary in it: tamarins fail `AⁿBⁿ` while mastering `(AB)ⁿ` on identical stimuli, and chimpanzees pay a constant cost per numeral where human children get the whole integer list from the successor function ([[wiki/concepts/discrete-infinity.md]])

## Position B

**No new component.** Sherwood, Subiaul & Zawidzki 2008: almost every human neocortical area has a macaque homologue; total frontal cortex is exactly at the ape scaling prediction and occupies the same cortical fraction as in great apes; the cortical volume increase is **2.75× on 1.25× the neurons** (Shariff 1953), i.e. neuropil not units; the one causal proposal for generalised systematicity is *afferent diversity* into an existing operator (a stronger arcuate-fasciculus projection from middle-temporal semantic areas into an inferior frontal region that does object manipulation and imitation in macaques too); and the human-specific behaviours are derived from a shifted growth schedule (neonatal brain 27% of adult vs. 36% chimpanzee, 70% macaque; fetal growth rate sustained through year 1) acting on domain-general biases ([[wiki/concepts/emergent-modularity.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides how `G70` is closed, and the two prescriptions do not overlap. A says: build one recursive combinator and expose it across domains (`G99`) — no amount of scaling interfaces will produce it. B says: raise the number of distinct representational sources feeding one composition site, and schedule when capacity becomes available, and the capability appears without any new part. **The wiki can afford to test B first**: it is a fan-in sweep on architectures already held, requires no new mechanism, and its null result is exactly the evidence A needs

## Status

`LIVE` — and the two sources are **not symmetric in evidence type**, which is why neither refutes the other. A's evidence is a behavioural discontinuity with no anatomical correlate proposed; B's is an anatomical continuity with no behavioural prediction stated. B additionally concedes its own null is an absence of evidence with a named cause — human/great-ape comparative cytoarchitecture is thin, most volumetric studies run at `n = 1–2` per species, and the one candidate novel human area (dorsal intraparietal, 3-D form from motion) has **no ape data at all**. Two facts sit awkwardly for B in its own source: rapid encephalisation begins at 1.8 Ma while domain-neutral symbolic cognition appears archaeologically only at the *Homo sapiens* speciation, and *Australopithecus garhi* is associated with butchery at 450 cm³. Both say the size variable is not the causal one, which weakens B's mechanism without strengthening A's **The missing evidence type has now arrived from the anatomy side, and it does not resolve the row — it sharpens it** ([[wiki/entities/lateral-frontoparietal-network.md]], Vendetti & Bunge 2014). The proposal is a *graph* change at fixed node inventory: (i) humans show resting-state coupling between rostrolateral prefrontal cortex and mid-inferior-parietal lobule with **no macaque analogue at any threshold**, while dorsolateral seeds give closely matching frontoparietal maps in both species (Mars et al. 2011); (ii) the prefrontal hierarchy is *inverted* — frontopolar cortex sits above dorsolateral in humans and below both dorsolateral and ventrolateral in the macaque structural hierarchy (Goulas et al. 2014); (iii) cortical expansion is concentrated in Brodmann area 10 and realised as neuropil rather than units (fewer, further-spaced layer-III pyramidal cells with more dendrite and spine; Semendeferi et al. 2011, Bianchi et al. 2013), which is B's 2.75×-volume-on-1.25×-neurons datum measured regionally. **Both positions can claim it.** Read as B, this is re-weighting plus one extra afferent — exactly the *afferent diversity* mechanism B names and never instantiates, now with a named tract. Read as A, a connection absent in every nonhuman primate tested and a re-ordered hierarchy is what "one new operator" looks like when the operator is an edge rather than a node. So the row's `Closes when` is satisfied on its second disjunct while the underlying question is untouched — **the closing condition was written as a bridging requirement and turns out not to be a discriminating one.** What would discriminate: whether the capability is present when the edge is present but under-trained. The developmental series on the same page says it is — 7- to 10-year-olds engage the full region set on second-order problems, but engage it *equally* for first-order ones, and what matures is the integrator's *selectivity*, not its presence, which is a re-weighting signature and not a new-component one. **Position B's mechanism now has its first dose-response measurement, and it is on the human side only** (Urbanski et al. 2016, [[wiki/entities/lateral-frontoparietal-network.md]]). *Afferent diversity* — B's named and never-instantiated mechanism — is exactly what the analogy lesion data measure: the site whose damage specifically abolishes analogical reasoning is 0.33 cc of left Brodmann area 10, and severity scales with the **number** of that site's own long-range afferents severed (occipital, temporal, thalamic, fronto-marginal; `rs = −0.553`) rather than with lesion volume or with any single tract. So the human capability does depend on fan-in into one small integrator, as B requires. What it does not do is make the comparison: there is no macaque version of this measurement, and B needs the human fan-in to be *larger*, not merely load-bearing. The result also does not favour A — nothing here looks like a discrete new operator, and the tissue in question is the one whose *connectivity* has no macaque match while its cytoarchitecture does ([[wiki/entities/lateral-frontal-pole.md]]). **A third position arrived with MacLean 2016** ([[wiki/concepts/motivation-representation-synergy.md]]) and is not a variant of either: the human-specific capacity required a change in *neither* the representational components nor their weighting, but in the **motivational** term that decides whether the existing components are deployed — great apes hold perspective taking and use it competitively, cooperative-breeding callitrichids hold the prosocial motivation without the representation, and only the conjunction produces cumulative culture. It shares B's rejection of a new part while denying B's location for the change, and it makes A's discontinuity evidence ambiguous, since every behavioural test cited for a missing operator was administered in one motivational frame (`T291`).
