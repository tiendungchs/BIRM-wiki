# T289 — Did the human-specific capacity require a new component, or a re-weighting of components that were already there?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A behavioural prediction is derived from the anatomical-continuity position, or an anatomical correlate proposed for the behavioural discontinuity — the two evidence types currently cannot meet.

## Position A

**One new operator.** Hauser, Chomsky & Fitch 2002's H3: narrow syntax — recursion plus its two interface mappings — is uniquely human, small, discrete, and plausibly exapted from a non-communicative domain rather than gradually built. The comparative subtraction has a hard boundary in it: tamarins fail `AⁿBⁿ` while mastering `(AB)ⁿ` on identical stimuli, and chimpanzees pay a constant cost per numeral where human children get the whole integer list from the successor function ([[wiki/concepts/discrete-infinity.md]])

## Position B

**No new component.** Sherwood, Subiaul & Zawidzki 2008: almost every human neocortical area has a macaque homologue; total frontal cortex is exactly at the ape scaling prediction and occupies the same cortical fraction as in great apes; the cortical volume increase is **2.75× on 1.25× the neurons** (Shariff 1953), i.e. neuropil not units; the one causal proposal for generalised systematicity is *afferent diversity* into an existing operator (a stronger arcuate-fasciculus projection from middle-temporal semantic areas into an inferior frontal region that does object manipulation and imitation in macaques too); and the human-specific behaviours are derived from a shifted growth schedule (neonatal brain 27% of adult vs. 36% chimpanzee, 70% macaque; fetal growth rate sustained through year 1) acting on domain-general biases ([[wiki/concepts/emergent-modularity.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides how `G70` is closed, and the two prescriptions do not overlap. A says: build one recursive combinator and expose it across domains (`G99`) — no amount of scaling interfaces will produce it. B says: raise the number of distinct representational sources feeding one composition site, and schedule when capacity becomes available, and the capability appears without any new part. **The wiki can afford to test B first**: it is a fan-in sweep on architectures already held, requires no new mechanism, and its null result is exactly the evidence A needs

## Status

`LIVE` — and the two sources are **not symmetric in evidence type**, which is why neither refutes the other. A's evidence is a behavioural discontinuity with no anatomical correlate proposed; B's is an anatomical continuity with no behavioural prediction stated. B additionally concedes its own null is an absence of evidence with a named cause — human/great-ape comparative cytoarchitecture is thin, most volumetric studies run at `n = 1–2` per species, and the one candidate novel human area (dorsal intraparietal, 3-D form from motion) has **no ape data at all**. Two facts sit awkwardly for B in its own source: rapid encephalisation begins at 1.8 Ma while domain-neutral symbolic cognition appears archaeologically only at the *Homo sapiens* speciation, and *Australopithecus garhi* is associated with butchery at 450 cm³. Both say the size variable is not the causal one, which weakens B's mechanism without strengthening A's **A third position arrived with MacLean 2016** ([[wiki/concepts/motivation-representation-synergy.md]]) and is not a variant of either: the human-specific capacity required a change in *neither* the representational components nor their weighting, but in the **motivational** term that decides whether the existing components are deployed — great apes hold perspective taking and use it competitively, cooperative-breeding callitrichids hold the prosocial motivation without the representation, and only the conjunction produces cumulative culture. It shares B's rejection of a new part while denying B's location for the change, and it makes A's discontinuity evidence ambiguous, since every behavioural test cited for a missing operator was administered in one motivational frame (`T291`).
