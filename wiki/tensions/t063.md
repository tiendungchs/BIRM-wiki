# T63 — Is the unit that stores a pattern the neuron or the dendritic segment?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A capacity measurement counts patterns per dendritic segment and per cell in the same preparation, testing whether the somatic threshold arbitrates or an OR over detections does.

## Position A

**The neuron.** Every capacity result the wiki carries counts patterns per *cell* and treats the cell as a thresholded sum over its synapses: `p_max ≈ kC/(a ln(1/a))` with `C` the fan-in per neuron ([[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]]), Hopfield's `0.14N` with `N` neurons, the counters of [[wiki/entities/sparse-distributed-memory.md]] pooled by one output unit. Population geometry is measured the same way, one dimension per recorded cell

## Position B

**The segment.** A pyramidal cell carries ~10⁴ synapses in 100–400-synapse segments, each an independent detector firing on 8–20 co-active clustered synapses, so one cell recognises *hundreds* of patterns and no somatic threshold arbitrates between them; the summing unit is a 20–300-synapse stretch of dendrite and the cell's output is closer to an OR over detections (Ahmad & Hawkins 2016, [[wiki/concepts/dendritic-computation.md]])

## Where it bites

Every unit count and every capacity number in the wiki, in *opposite* directions depending on the mapping: if an artificial unit models a segment, a 10⁴-cell column is 10⁶ units; if it models a cell, artificial networks have ~100× less pattern memory per unit than the biology they are compared against. It also bites credit assignment (a signal must reach a branch, not a cell — [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]]) and interpretability (a "neuron's" tuning curve is a disjunction over ~100 unrelated detectors, which is polysemanticity as an anatomical fact rather than a training artefact). Caveats on B: the paper models no dendrite-to-soma conversion, so "OR over segments" is a conjecture about the output function rather than a result, and its numbers are recognition-error bounds, not storage capacities — the two positions may be measuring different quantities on the same hardware
