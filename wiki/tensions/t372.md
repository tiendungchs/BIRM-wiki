# T372 — When prior knowledge fits, do the controller and the store compete or cooperate?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Schema relatedness is varied **parametrically** within one paradigm (arbitrary → weakly related → strongly congruent) and vmPFC–hippocampal coupling is read at each level. A predicts a monotonic decrease, B a monotonic increase, C (the reconciliation below) an inverted U with the peak at mid-range relatedness. No study has run more than two levels.

## Position A

**Competition (SLIMM — Schema-Linked Interactions between Medial Prefrontal and Medial Temporal regions).** Resonance between input and an active schema shifts memory formation *away* from the medial temporal lobe and onto vmPFC; in congruent situations vmPFC **inhibits** MTL-dependent binding of arbitrary event elements, and novelty (no resonance) releases it. Evidence: schema-congruent encoding raises hits *and* false alarms to perceptually similar lures — reduced pattern separation and reduced recollection; hippocampal–STS/IFG connectivity trades off against mPFC–cortical connectivity; a prior intact narrative gives **weaker** mPFC–MTL coupling during encoding of the sequel and during post-encoding rest (Gilboa & Marlatte 2017, [[wiki/concepts/schema-assimilation.md]]).

## Position B

**Cooperation.** Bidirectional hippocampus–mPFC interaction is *required* for schema-mediated encoding: the hippocampus encodes hierarchical feature overlap while differentiating contexts, relays context-defining information to mPFC where contextual rules are encoded, and mPFC then biases which hippocampal representations are activated. Evidence: in associative inference, vmPFC–hippocampal **coactivation increases** across repetitions of the overlapping pairs and its extent predicts successful expression of the inferred pair; several studies report increased coupling during schema-related encoding and during rest when new material is related to prior knowledge; integration-consistent patterns appear in anterior hippocampus *and* posterior vmPFC, but only when the new overlapping pair is acquired against well-established prior knowledge (Gilboa & Marlatte 2017; Zeithamova et al. 2012, [[wiki/concepts/retrieval-mediated-learning.md]]).

## Where it bites

**The sign of the controller→store edge, as a function of a measurable input.** Every machine reading of the wiki's fast/slow pair takes a side implicitly: a gating architecture (controller suppresses the episodic write when the parametric model already covers the input) is A; a retrieval-augmented architecture where the controller conditions the store's read and the store's output revises the controller is B. They are not stylistic variants — A predicts that making material more schema-congruent should *reduce* episodic write traffic and degrade item distinctiveness, B predicts it should *raise* coupling and improve inference. A also makes the schema's benefit and its distortion the same mechanism (the false-alarm result is A's strongest datum and B has no account of it).

## Status

`LIVE`. The source's own reconciliation is a **nonlinearity rather than a winner**: vmPFC–hippocampal coupling may be weak both when new information is completely arbitrary (no schema to instantiate) and when it is very strongly related (no conflict to resolve), and strongest at **mid-range relatedness**, where overlapping associations must be reinstated and disambiguated. Under that reading A's experiments sit at the congruent end with rich, well-established schemas and B's laboratory paradigms sit in the middle, and the two literatures never overlap on the relatedness axis — which is exactly why no published design can separate them. This is the same unmeasured variable that keeps `T99` at `LEANING` (integration demand defined by design, never within a trial); the inverted-U proposal is the first statement of it that predicts a *shape*, so an instrument for relatedness would settle both rows at once. Note also that A and B are stated over different dependent variables — A over binding and pattern separation at the store, B over coupling and inference — so a study reporting only coupling cannot falsify A.
