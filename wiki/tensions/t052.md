# T52 — Is the hippocampal spatial code carried by the sparse, selective excitatory population, or is it equally present in the dense inhibitory one?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A principle is stated that separates decodability from storage, or interneuron content is manipulated and the spatial code shown to change — the current test is 57 cells in two mice.

## Position A

**Sparse pyramidal cells are the code.** The whole capacity argument runs on sparseness — `p_max ≈ kC/(a ln(1/a))` prices memories in a population with `a ≈ 0.02–0.33`, separation exists to keep those codes non-overlapping, and interneurons enter every model in the wiki as a normalizer that *enforces* the sparseness rather than as a carrier of content (Rolls 2013, [[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]]; [[wiki/entities/spiking-tem.md]], where removing theta inhibition destroys the place code by destroying sparsity)

## Position B

**Position is decodable from interneurons just as well.** All four families show stable position-tuned rate modulation tiling the whole maze, and a CEBRA decoder trained on interneurons only matches one trained on pyramidal cells down-sampled to the same count (`p = 0.63`); leaving out *Vip*, *Id2* or *Pvalb* each raises the error (`p < 0.03`), so the families are not redundant with one another either (Valero et al. 2025, [[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether "population X decodes the variable" licenses "population X is the representation" — the inference every probing result in the wiki makes ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]), and whether a machine store's content must live in its sparse selective units or may also live in the dense control pathway that shapes them

## Status

`LIVE` — the two are not straightforwardly contradictory (decodability is not storage, and a signal that *sets* the code inherits information about it), but the wiki has no principle that separates the two readings, and the test is small: 57 interneurons, five sessions, two mice
