# T126 — Which correction to plain variational inference does the work — the action-entropy planning term, or the epistemic priors?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**The epistemic priors, overwhelmingly.** Ablated on three grid-worlds where the layout is the unknown parameter `θ`: the planning correction `+Σ_t H[q(u_t|x_{t-1})]` is worth `51.9 → 54.5%` on Frozen Lake, while the ambiguity and novelty corrections carry the rest — `0.0% → 98.7%` rock retrieval, `5.5% → 40.7%` on Wumpus. Without novelty a probe that only informs is worthless and the agent walks past it (Nuijten et al. 2026, `raw/nuijten-2026-what-inference-is-active-inference.md`)

## Position B

**The planning term, wherever the horizon is long.** Same group, same year, three different environments: on MiniGrid DoorKey-8×8 at `T=20` the planning correction alone takes success from **4% to 89%** — without it the agent cannot chain find-key → unlock → reach-goal — and adding the full epistemic priors on top changes nothing (89%, "neither help nor hinder"), because the key and door are revealed incidentally by the field of view rather than by a dedicated probe (Nuijten et al. 2026, `raw/nuijten-2026-efe-planning-variational-inference.md`)

## Where it bites

Decides what a builder must implement first. The two corrections are cheap separately and neither implies the other, so the question is which failure a given task will exhibit — inability to *value a detour* (needs epistemic priors) or inability to *coordinate a long sequence* (needs the planning correction)

## Status

`LIVE` — plausibly not a disagreement but an unformalised regime split: epistemic priors pay when uncertainty is concentrated in a latent parameter resolved only by a dedicated action, the planning correction pays when the horizon is long and uncertainty dissolves as a by-product of moving. **No principle predicts in advance which regime a task is in**, and a reasoning agent doing [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] is in both at once
