# T300 — Does added capacity buy robustness beyond what accuracy explains?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** One model family is swept over capacity in a single domain with the post-shift score **differenced against each model's own in-distribution score** (Relative-mCE-style) and the shift held fixed — so that a capacity effect on degradation is separated from a capacity effect on accuracy in both vision and language.

## Position A

**Yes, and monotonically — in vision, on the differenced score.** Hendrycks & Dietterich 2019 ([[wiki/entities/imagenet-c.md]]): feature aggregation and size move Relative mCE 105.0 (ResNet-50) → 88.6 (ResNeXt-50) → **80.1** (ResNeXt-101) and mFR 58.0 → 52.4 → 43.2, while clean error moves only 23.9 → 22.9 → 21.0. The gains "substantially outpace their accuracy gains", so this is not the undifferenced artefact the same paper exposes. The converse holds too: pruning for size (CondenseNet C=G=4 → 8) costs accuracy *and* mCE (80.8 → 84.6)

## Position B

**No — and in the era the field read as robustness progress, the sign is reversed.** The same paper's headline null: from AlexNet through VGG to ResNet-50, `mCE` falls monotonically (100.0 → 76.7) while `Relative mCE` never improves (100.0 → 105.0, with VGG-11 at 123.3) — six years of architecture progress bought no degradation resistance at all. On the language side [[wiki/entities/shortcut-suite.md]] measures the sign as *negative*: LLaMA2-Chat few-shot `¬E` accuracy falls 75.3 → 48.5 → **3.6** for 7B → 13B → 70B, i.e. scale converts a weak model into a constant classifier (T228 Position A)

## Where it bites

Whether capacity is a **fifth lever** on the shortcut problem alongside the four in [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]] — the cheapest one to pull, and the one every scaling programme pulls anyway. If A generalises, robustness is partly purchasable with parameters and the four levers are a way of buying it cheaper; if B does, every i.i.d. scaling curve is uninformative about o.o.d. behaviour and the bias must be installed. The two positions are not yet a contradiction about a fact — they differ in **what is scaled** (width and aggregation within a fixed depth-era design vs. depth-era architecture succession vs. language-model parameter count), in **domain**, and in whether the score was differenced at all, which is precisely why the closing experiment is a differenced sweep rather than another benchmark. Note the asymmetry that makes the comparison hard: B's language evidence is *undifferenced* in exactly the way A's paper warns against, since Standard accuracy rises over the same sweep

## Status

`LIVE` — A rests on one paper, one domain and one architecture family lineage; B's two halves come from different fields and neither was measured against the other's protocol. Nothing yet distinguishes "capacity buys robustness" from "capacity buys robustness in vision under a corruption family authored for 2019 convolutional networks". Upgradeable to `BOTH` if the differenced sweep reproduces A in vision and B in language, which would make the domain the named threshold
