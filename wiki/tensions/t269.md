# T269 — Does prefrontal cortex constitute conscious perceptual content, or only route it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Constitutes.** Perceptual content is decodable from macaque prefrontal cortex during binocular rivalry and flash suppression with no report required (Panagiotaropoulos et al. 2012; Kapoor et al. 2018), and at population resolution the number is now large: **987 units in ventrolateral prefrontal cortex decode the current percept at up to 95% on single, internally generated transitions**, with no report, no change in the dichoptic input, and cross-condition transfers that separately falsify an input code, an epoch code and an eye-movement code (Kapoor et al. 2022, `raw/kapoor-2022-conscious-transitions-pfc.md`); only seen stimuli produce sudden sustained prefrontal activity and false alarms show spontaneous prefrontal ignition (van Vugt et al. 2018); reported-vs-unreported divergence is global and late. Higher-order theories add that a prefrontal second-order representation — a duplicate or a pointer — is what makes a first-order state conscious ([[wiki/entities/global-neuronal-workspace.md]], [[wiki/concepts/ignition.md]])

## Position B

**Only routes, if that.** In the one causal human instrument, focal stimulation of anterolateral and frontopolar prefrontal cortex produces **the lowest rate of reportable content change in the brain** — 0% at the frontal pole against ~67% in unimodal cortex over 1537 sites and 67 patients — and the rare positives (orbitofrontal 17%, anterior cingulate) insert content unrelated to the environment (smells, emotions, motivational states, content-free thoughts) rather than perturbing the ongoing percept, which fusiform stimulation does routinely. Both higher-order versions predict a perturbation of ongoing experience; neither is observed (Raccah, Block & Fox 2021, [[wiki/concepts/perturbation-elicitability.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether a shared-bus architecture's hub *holds* the current content or merely admits and addresses it. Under A the workspace slot is where computation happens and its occupancy is the system's state; under B the slot is an addressing mechanism and the content stays in the modules, which changes what a commit publishes — a representation or a pointer to one. Bears directly on gap `G91` (a gate with no learning rule) and on `T268`

## Status

`LIVE` — the two sides are not measuring the same thing. A is decoding (does the site carry the information?), B is perturbation (does editing the site change the content?), and a dense mixed-selectivity code can pass the first and fail the second for reasons of coding geometry alone, which is B's own proposed mechanism. Three unresolved limits on B: sub-reportable effects are untested (the fix — stimulate during a task and score performance — has not been run), lateral prefrontal transcranial magnetic stimulation and lesions *do* impair perceptual and metacognitive performance (Rounis et al. 2010; Del Cul et al. 2009; Fleming et al. 2014), and the source is written by opponents of A. The reading that satisfies both: prefrontal cortex is the router, so breaking it costs throughput and changes no message. **Sharpened, not resolved, by the unit-resolution recording**: the payload is now measured to be present at 95% in the tissue whose perturbation changes content 0% of the time, so "the router does not hold the message" is no longer available as stated — the message *is* in the router. Three survivors, all untested: the prefrontal copy is used for control rather than constituting the percept (test: stimulate during a task and score performance); the stimulator's spatial kernel cannot write to a code whose opposite preferences sit 0.4 mm apart, which would make elicitation rate a measure of code clustering rather than of content ([[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]); or the two results are in different subregions (macaque ventrolateral inferior convexity vs. human anterolateral/frontopolar) and never met
