# T65 — Do cortical lateral connections link similar units, or deliberately mix dissimilar ones?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A method that resolves which labelled cells a given dendrite actually contacts is applied — tracer data alone cannot decide like-to-like against graded mixing, and the two may hold in different layers.

## Position A

**Like connects to like.** Superficial-layer patches reciprocally link domains with matching functional properties — cytochrome-oxidase blobs to blobs, like eye dominance to like eye dominance, like orientation preference to like (Livingstone & Hubel 1984; Malach et al. 1993; Yoshioka et al. 1996), and in tree shrew the patch distribution extends along the visual-field axis matching the cells' preferred orientation (Bosking et al. 1997). Read as Hebbian clustering, the lateral net is an associative memory over tuning similarity

## Position B

**Mixing is the point.** Patch diameter tracks the basal dendritic spread of the layer-3 pyramids across areas (largest patches, 481 µm, in macaque motor cortex, which has the largest pyramids), so only a cell at a patch's centre samples pure patch input and only a cell mid-gap samples pure non-patch — everything in between receives a graded *mixture*, and the size matching exists to **maximise input diversity** (Malach 1992). Supporting it: like-to-like correlations appear only 400–1000 µm from the injection site, while at short range axons freely innervate functionally diverse territory (Kisvárday et al. 1997; Stettler et al. 2002), and individual cells within one patch each target a *different subset* of the patch set

## Where it bites

Decides what a recurrent lateral layer is *for* in a machine model: an attractor/completion net over similar states, or a mixing stage that manufactures conjunctive selectivity by construction ([[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]). Under B, mixed selectivity is a wiring consequence of dendrite-to-patch scale matching and needs no learning to explain; under A it is evidence of a learned code

## Status

`LIVE` — Douglas & Martin 2004 endorse B and note A's assumption of unbiased dendritic sampling fails in some layer-4 populations (macaque V1, rat barrel cortex), so the two may hold in different layers. Unresolvable from tracer data alone: patch labelling cannot say which of the labelled cells a given dendrite actually contacts
