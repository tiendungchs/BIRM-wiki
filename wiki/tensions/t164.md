# T164 — Must a collapse-avoiding self-supervised learner descend a well-defined objective?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Yes — the anti-collapse provision is a term in the loss.** The wiki's whole treatment of gap G34 is a catalogue of terms: negatives (InfoNCE), variance + covariance (VICReg, Barlow Twins), distribution matching (SIGReg, [[wiki/entities/lewm.md]]). Each is certifiable — you can say what the objective's minimum is and check it is not collapsed — and the wiki's stated preference ordering between them is by *coefficient count*, which presupposes there is an objective to count coefficients in ([[wiki/concepts/energy-based-models.md]]). **And the position is weaker than the wiki has been stating it** (Bardes et al. 2022, [[wiki/entities/vicreg.md]]): VICReg's minimum is non-collapsed for *every* positive coefficient triple, and training collapses anyway at `λ,μ,ν = 1,1,1`, `10,1,1` and `1,10,1`, converging only on the ray `λ = μ ≳ 5, ν = 1`. A term certifies the *target*, not the trajectory — so A's advantage over B is that a designer can say what the system was supposed to reach, not that anyone can check it did. **Barlow Twins is this position at its cleanest and adds a fact that cuts at B directly**: its minimum is stated as a matrix identity (`C = I`), it needs one coefficient that is flat over a 10× range, and adding B's two components *on top of it* costs **10 points** (71.4 → 61.3; stop-gradient alone 70.5, predictor alone 70.2) — so the update-rule asymmetry is not a free extra safeguard but a competing design that pulls the dynamics off a minimum that already exists (Zbontar et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/barlow-twins.md]]) **A's first measured payoff, and it arrives from the same lineage** (Balestriero & LeCun 2025, [[wiki/entities/lejepa.md]]): a certifiable loss whose *value* is monotone in downstream quality certifies the trajectory as well as the target — LeJEPA's training loss correlates with downstream linear-probe accuracy at `ρ_s ≈ 0.85` across architectures, datasets, learning rates and epoch budgets, and `≈0.99` under the rescaling `train_loss / λ^0.4`, giving label-free model selection where B's dynamical systems have no scalar whose value means anything. It also removes B's mechanism from the necessary list: with the embedding distribution constrained directly, the predictor whose removal collapses BYOL and I-JEPA is deletable at no cost and the EMA survives only as evaluation-time weight averaging. What A still cannot do is certify a read-out the loss does not measure (T168).

## Position B

**No — an asymmetry in the update rule suffices, and then there is no objective at all.** BYOL's per-step loss is a regression whose exact global minimum *is* the collapsed constant encoder; it reaches 74.3% ImageNet linear-eval top-1 anyway, because the target branch is updated by an EMA rather than by a gradient. The authors hypothesise that **no** loss `L_{θ,ξ}` exists whose joint gradient descent is BYOL's dynamics, on the GAN analogy. Both components are necessary (removing either collapses at ~0.3%) and both are substitutable by a near-optimal predictor with no EMA at all (52.5% closed-form, 66.5% raised learning rate) — so what prevents collapse is a *rate ratio* between two networks, a quantity appearing in no objective (Grill et al. 2020, [[wiki/entities/byol.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what an anti-collapse audit can check. Under A, checking the objective's minimum suffices; under B, a system can be off-collapse for reasons no inspection of the loss reveals, and every identifiability result the wiki relies on ([[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]], the `N(0,I)`-constrained recovery theorem) is a statement about a *global minimiser* that such a system has no reason to approach Home page for this row: [[wiki/concepts/representational-collapse.md]].

## Status

`LIVE` — B is a demonstration and A is a research programme, so they do not directly conflict on facts, and B has since acquired a second instance in the lineage the wiki cares most about: an image JEPA runs the same zero-coefficient predictor + EMA skeleton to 81.1 ImageNet linear evaluation with the pairs supplied by masking rather than augmentation, so the uncertifiable mechanism is what the whole JEPA family is actually built on ([[wiki/entities/i-jepa.md]]). What is live is the *preference*: B is cheaper (zero anti-collapse coefficients) and A is certifiable, and no source in the wiki has measured what the certifiability buys. The discriminating experiment is cheap and unrun: take one JEPA and train it under both provisions on the same data, then check whether the objective-based one behaves as the recovery theorem predicts on a policy with vanishing conditional action excitation (`ρ_tr → 0`) where the dynamical one has no prediction at all. **B's "rate ratio" now has a literal dial and an interior optimum** (Chen et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/vl-jepa.md]]): with a genuinely separate target network in another modality, a scalar learning-rate multiplier on the target branch is swept, and both endpoints lose — full speed −3.6 classification, **frozen −7.3** — with the optimum at 0.05–0.10. The two regimes the JEPA lineage actually ships (jointly trained/EMA, and frozen) are the two ends of a continuum nobody has been tuning
