# T110 — When a task affords no higher-order structure, does the learner withdraw from the abstract level or keep imposing it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Withdraws, and measurably.** With two rule sets identical in stimuli, instructions, trial count and action space, and differing only in whether a 2nd-order regularity exists in the reward table, the 2nd-order frontal band (prePMd) is engaged above baseline in *both* sets at the beginning of learning and then declines specifically in the flat set by the middle phase (`F(1,19)=4.3, p<.05`) while staying flat in the hierarchical one (`F=.05`) — so within ~120 trials the learner has estimated that no structure exists at that depth and disengaged (Badre et al. 2010, [[wiki/concepts/policy-abstraction-hierarchy.md]])

## Position B

**Keeps imposing it, at a price it does not have to pay.** Subjects build a context→task-set hierarchy on a problem that neither requires nor rewards one, paying six links against four and slower initial learning, and the commitment is durable enough to produce systematic negative transfer when a new context partially matches the imposed rule (Collins & Frank 2013, [[wiki/entities/c-ts-model.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether a machine needs an explicit disengagement rule for an abstraction level, or whether an over-applied hierarchy is a tolerable default. Under A a level carries its own engagement gain driven by whether its hypotheses earn reward, and the wiki's parallel-search story comes with a built-in pruning step; under B the hierarchical prior is unconditional and the cost is paid on every flat problem, which is gap G23's "a prior needs an entry test" in its sharpest form. Partly reconcilable — attenuation of a BOLD signal is not abandonment of a representation, and the two tasks differ in what the flat alternative costs (18 arbitrary edges, which is genuinely hard, versus 4 links, which is not) — so A's decline may be reallocation toward the level that is working rather than a decision that no structure exists

## Status

`LIVE` — the discriminating test is behavioural and unrun: after a flat set is mastered, introduce a 2nd-order regularity and measure how long it takes to be found. A predicts a delay proportional to the disengagement; B predicts none
