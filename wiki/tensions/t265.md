# T265 — Where does the default mode network sit relative to the sensory-fugal hierarchy?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**At its apex, or parallel to it.** Two standing positions: Margulies et al. place the network at the top of the principal macroscale gradient, each of its subregions maximally distant from primary sensory and motor cortex, which is the basis for reading it as the most abstract level of one continuous hierarchy; Buckner & Krienen instead deny it hierarchical constraint altogether — densely interconnected distributed circuits operating *parallel* to the canonical sensory hierarchies ([[wiki/entities/default-mode-network.md]], [[wiki/concepts/broadcast-hierarchy.md]])

## Position B

**It protrudes from the hierarchy.** Paquola et al. 2025, measuring cytoarchitecture (BigBrain), structural communication (navigation efficiency) and signal flow (400-parcel rDCM) on one parcellation: connectivity is organised along the network's *own* data-driven cytoarchitectural axis `E1`, which is neither nested within nor parallel to the sensory-fugal gradient — strong afferent convergence at one end (`r = −0.54` input, `r = −0.60` navigation efficiency), insulation at the other, output balanced across every cortical type. Two hierarchy properties hold (levels; an apex insulated from external input) and one fails (levels do not increasingly intersect toward the top). Internal layout is a **mosaic**, not a gradient: `E1` follows neither subregion boundaries nor any spatial axis

## Where it bites

Whether an architecture's most abstract component is the **top of one stack** — in which case level is an index, coupling runs to `i±1`, and a global revision costs `O(L)` — or a **side-attached system with its own internal level variable**, reading from a filtered set of levels and writing to all of them at once. The second makes "which level is this module at?" a measured property of the module rather than a position in a list, and makes the abstract component's interior *not* a pipeline

## Status

`LEANING B` — B is the only position measured on a single graph with a spin null, and it explains the other two as partial views (an insulated end looks like an apex; a non-gradient interior looks parallel). Three limits keep it from settling: the cytoarchitectural axis is `n = 1` postmortem histology whose sensitivity to superficial-vs-deep profiling the authors concede, with an `n = 8` different-modality (myelin) replication in which the input effect drops to non-significance (`r_avg = −0.26`, `P = 0.101`); navigation efficiency is a routing heuristic over tractography rather than measured flow; and nothing here measures the laminar type of the *connections*, which is what the hierarchy claim is ultimately about
