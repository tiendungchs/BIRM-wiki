# T250 — Does raising a network's global coupling make its decisions faster or slower?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Faster, and for free.** Time-resolved participation coefficient `B` rises with task demand, and the integrated state has higher global efficiency, a *higher* drift rate `v`, a *shorter* non-decision time `t`, and no change in the decision boundary `a` — the profile of a bandwidth increase with nothing traded (Shine et al. 2016, [[wiki/concepts/integration-segregation-balance.md]]).

## Position B

**Slower, and it is a genuine trade.** Mean resting functional connectivity correlates *positively* with correct-response reaction time on the Penn Matrix Reasoning Test (`r = 0.13`, `p = 0.0012`, `N` = 650) at every item difficulty, and the personalised whole-brain models of the slower individuals have lower synaptic amplitude and higher synaptic synchrony — which, driven into a frontoparietal winner-take-all circuit, buys accuracy by lengthening integration time. Higher fluid intelligence is *faster* on simple processing-speed tests and *slower* on the hard matrix items, crossing over at item 8 of 24 (Schirner et al. 2023, [[wiki/concepts/excitation-inhibition-balance.md]]).

## Where it bites

Any architecture that plans to use one global coupling gain as a runtime control needs the sign. The two are not obviously measuring the same thing — a routing statistic during a speeded 2-back versus a mean coupling statistic at rest against an untimed reasoning test — and neither manipulates coupling, so both are correlational. Note also that *neither* mechanism moves a decision threshold: Shine's `a` is null and Schirner's circuit has a fixed one, so whatever the sign, the speed–accuracy point is being set by input statistics rather than by a stopping rule ([[wiki/concepts/evidence-accumulation.md]]). **The decisive experiment**: compute `mean B` and `mean FC` on the same subjects and regress both against the same task's diffusion parameters
