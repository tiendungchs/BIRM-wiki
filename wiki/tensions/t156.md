# T156 — When a compositional model fails out of distribution, is the deficit in the vocabulary or in the search over it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** An instrument replaces primitiveness where no ground-truth vocabulary exists — outside synthetic benchmarks the vocabulary and search hypotheses are currently indistinguishable.

## Position A

**The vocabulary.** Every representational reading in the wiki: a model that fails on unseen compositions lacks the parts, or holds them entangled, and the remedy is a better representation — the entire argument of [[wiki/concepts/compositionality.md]], [[wiki/concepts/program-induction.md]]'s cost 1, and the standing interpretation of a low benchmark score as evidence about what the model *has*.

## Position B

**The search, and the two come apart by a factor of 20.** [[wiki/entities/neo-neural-theorizer.md]] scores its vocabulary independently of its task performance: at 6-step arithmetic programs, action primitiveness is **1.000** (every ground-truth primitive is reproducible by a single learned code) while transferability is **0.019–0.038**. Sampling 1024 programs from the same amortised proposer and majority-voting over those that reproduce the support pair recovers **0.696–0.707** — same weights, same codebook, ~180× compute. Nothing about the representation changed.

## Where it bites

What a benchmark score licenses. Under B, "model X does not compose" is unfalsifiable without a separate coverage measurement, and a large fraction of the wiki's OOD-failure evidence is uninterpretable in the same way. It also relocates the test-time-compute lever: search pays **only** over a factored hypothesis space — the same paper's monolithic baselines are flat in sampling budget and its gradient-refined baseline reaches 0.994 self-fit with 0.000 transfer — so scaling inference is not a general remedy for an amortisation gap but a remedy for a *proposal* gap in a compositional model ([[wiki/concepts/amortized-inference.md]]).

## Status

`LIVE`, and the asymmetry is that only B has an instrument. Primitiveness needs a ground-truth primitive set to score against, which no real domain supplies, so outside synthetic benchmarks the two hypotheses are currently indistinguishable — the open instrument question is what plays the role of primitiveness when nobody knows the true vocabulary
