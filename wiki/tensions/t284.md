# T284 — Does a world model's own predictive uncertainty help or hurt when learning tasks in sequence?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The brainstormed hypothesis is tested: an exploration bonus is consumed by acting on it while a replay priority keeps re-presenting irreducible noise — which predicts the split by environment stochasticity.

## Position A

**Helps, and it is the only boundary-free option.** Latent disagreement (a deep ensemble's variance over next latent features) added as an intrinsic reward raises average performance 0.09 → 0.28 and cuts forgetting 0.37 → 0.13 on 8-task Minihack, and it needs no task-change signal, unlike the `ε`-greedy and entropy schedules every other continual-reinforcement-learning method resets per task (Kessler et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]])

## Position B

**Hurts, in two distinct ways, in the same paper.** (i) As an action bonus on the *easier* 3-task Minigrid it costs 0.26 average performance (0.72 → 0.46). (ii) Used to prioritise *which stored episodes the model trains on* — the identical quantity — it performs at the level of the non-continual baseline, worse than uniform sampling on performance, forgetting and transfer at once. It also fails outright below 10⁵ buffer transitions, where the plain agent still learns

## Where it bites

Decides whether an epistemic term is a general-purpose control signal or a job-specific one. If the same uncertainty estimate cannot be reused for exploration and for replay selection, every expected-free-energy agent that derives both from one quantity is mis-specified, and [[wiki/concepts/epistemic-value.md]]'s ablation literature has been testing the wrong composite. It also decides whether the sign of the exploration bonus's contribution is a function of task difficulty, which no method anneals

## Status

`LIVE`. One paper, one architecture, two benchmarks. **(brainstorm)** The cheapest hypothesis that fits both halves: an exploration bonus is *consumed* by acting on it, whereas a replay priority is persistent and keeps re-presenting whatever the model cannot predict — which in a stochastic environment is irreducible noise. That predicts uncertainty-prioritised replay should be rescued by a decay on the epistemic weight per episode, which is unrun and cheap
