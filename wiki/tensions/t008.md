# T8 — Is compositional machinery innate, or acquired from an external symbolic system?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Acquired.** Infants are endowed only with language-*learning* capacities (sensitivity to speech and prosody, an abstract content-word/function-word distinction); the syntax and compositional semantics of the acquired natural language are what break the modularity of core systems. An innate language of thought is rejected because it overgenerates — the infant could not know which concepts are useful, which apply, or which propositions are true (Spelke 2022)

## Position B

**Innate.** Core-system outputs are already in a language-of-thought format and a general composer sits on top. The language route undergenerates in both directions: core concepts are taken for granted and so rarely uttered, while logical operators, tense/aspect and modals have no core-system counterpart. And the conceded escape hatch — that home-signing deaf children reinvent the needed features of language — makes those features indistinguishable from a pre-linguistic language of thought (Revencu & Csibra 2023)

## Where it bites

Decides whether a reasoning architecture must *contain* a composer or may expect one to emerge from training on a symbolic corpus — i.e. whether a large language model is a candidate answer to gap G21 or only to G22

## Status

`LIVE` — neither side has a criterion that separates productive composition from associative combination, so no evidence currently discriminates them. **A third position, from Hauser, Chomsky & Fitch 2002** ([[wiki/concepts/discrete-infinity.md]]): the composer is *innate* but is one small operator (recursion plus its two interface mappings), domain-general rather than domain-specific, and plausibly not selected for language at all — exapted from navigation, number or social reasoning and later made callable from other domains. This is neither A (which denies an innate composer) nor B (which puts the composer's format inside the core systems), and it dissolves the equivocation problem differently: the fragments need no shared symbolic format because the operator is type-blind on its arguments while the fragments keep their own entry conditions
