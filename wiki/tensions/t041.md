# T41 — Must the grid↔hippocampal weights be learned, or must they be frozen?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Learned.** Rolls 2013 reports that place-like fields form from an entorhinal grid input *only* with Hebbian training in the perforant path — an untrained sparse projection is not enough — and TEM/TEM-t learn the structural-to-hippocampal mapping throughout training, which is what lets the code specialise to the environment family ([[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]], [[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]])

## Position B

**Frozen.** Chandra et al. 2023 report the opposite in both directions: a *fixed random* grid→hippocampal projection plus a learned-once-then-frozen return projection is what produces the exponential fixed-point capacity, and replacing it with statistically matched hippocampal states whose weights are learned bidirectionally **collapses** that capacity; realistic place-cell tuning and the observed cross-environment similarity statistics appear with no training of the projection at all ([[wiki/entities/vector-hash.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides whether the address layer of a machine fast store is a trained encoder or a frozen random hash — i.e. whether the store can specialise its addresses to the domain (needed for similarity-respecting retrieval, G37) or must give that up to keep the capacity (G42)

## Status

`LIVE` — the two sides optimise different quantities on the same weights and neither runs the other's test: no capacity measurement exists for a learned grid→hippocampal map, and no transfer or relational-inference measurement exists for a frozen one. Note they are not strictly contradictory — a frozen projection could suffice for *capacity* while a trained one is required for *place-field tuning of the kind Rolls simulates* — but no experiment separates them
