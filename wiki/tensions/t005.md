# T5 — Was neuroscience causally responsible for the transferred mechanisms?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `META`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A documented case is found where a mechanism was imported from neuroscience before an engineering route to it existed — or the historical record shows the engineering route was always available and the transfer claim is retrospective.

## Position A

Attention, replay, dropout, external memory and elastic weight consolidation are documented imports; TD learning and PDP have explicit historical lineage

## Position B

Several were plausibly reachable by engineering alone and the inspiration was acknowledged only retrospectively — the paper itself says inspiration is often drawn "without explicit acknowledgment"

## Where it bites

Sets how much predictive weight the biology deserves when choosing what to build next; if the record is retrofitted, the "look at the brain" prior is weaker than claimed
