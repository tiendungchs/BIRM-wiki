# T179 — Is short path length between positions why long-range dependencies become learnable?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A content-addressed read is added to an attention-free model, separating the path-length claim (already refuted both ways) from the property no attention-free model in the wiki has.

## Position A

**Yes — it is the founding argument for attention.** [[wiki/entities/transformer.md]] Table 1 makes the whole case on the third column: self-attention connects any two positions in `O(1)` sequential hops where recurrence needs `O(n)` and convolution `O(log_k n)`, on the cited premise (Hochreiter et al. 2001) that the shorter the forward and backward signal paths, the easier the dependency is to learn. Parallelism and the `n < d` cost regime are presented as secondary. The architecture then took machine translation, structured parsing at 40K examples, and the decade.

## Position B

**No.** [[wiki/entities/s4.md]] has `O(L)`-deep information flow through a contractive linear recurrence and a kernel that cannot depend on content, and it carries a 16,384-step dependency at 96.35% (Path-X) where all 11 Transformer variants — every one of them with `O(1)` path length — are at chance, winning every Long Range Arena task by >20 average points. Its own ablation attributes the result to the **initialisation** of `A` (T177), a variable path length does not name. Conversely the `O(1)`-path model fails at 16K.

## Where it bites

Which property to buy when the two are separable. If A, the design target is hop count and quadratic cost is the honest price; if B, the target is the spectrum of the state operator and attention's `O(n²)` buys something else entirely — a read whose *target* is chosen by the current content, which is what an LTI kernel cannot express and what the founding paper never argues for. The wiki's four-cell brainstorm (short paths ± content-dependent reads) exists because 2017 bundled them against RNNs, where they arrive together.

## Status

`LEANING` toward B on the stated claim: short paths are demonstrably neither necessary (S4) nor sufficient (all Transformer variants at chance on Path-X) for a 16,384-step dependency. What is *not* in question is the second, unargued property — no attention-free model in the wiki performs a content-addressed read (Vaswani et al. 2017; Gu et al. 2022). **Update (Orvieto et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/lru.md]]):** B's evidence no longer depends on any structured component. LRU has the same `O(L)`-deep contractive flow and reaches a six-task LRA average of 86.3 (Path-X 94.2%) with a **diagonal** recurrence of `N` independent scalar complex exponentials, `2N` recurrent parameters, and eigenvalues drawn uniformly from a ring — so the property that beats `O(1)` path length at 16,384 steps is a *measure over the unit disc*, not HiPPO and not any graph. This removes the last reading under which S4's structure could be seen as a substitute for short paths, and leaves the content-addressed read as the sole unrefuted function of attention
