# T268 — Is there one commit event or two?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**One, late and global.** The P3 / late-positive component (~300 ms) is the signature of conscious ignition, common across modality and paradigm (masking, attentional blink, psychological refractory period); it vanishes under inattention and in stage 1–2 sleep, and its sudden increase marks threshold crossing ([[wiki/concepts/ignition.md]])

## Position B

**Two, and the earlier one is the perceptual one.** A visual awareness negativity peaking at ~260 ms (duration ~200 ms) is proposed as the earliest electrophysiological correlate of visual awareness, corroborated with MEG; critically, when a stimulus is task-*irrelevant* yet reported as seen, **only** the negativity remains and the P3 does not, which would make the P3 post-perceptual (report, decision, memory encoding)

## Where it bites

Whether an architecture has a single admission gate or a local sensory-level recurrence stage plus a separate global publication stage. Under two gates a system can compute on content it never publishes — which is a different machine from one where computing on something *is* publishing it, and it changes what "the system's current content" means for downstream consumers

## Status

`LIVE` — the source's own reconciliation (the negativity indexes accessibility, the P3 genuine access; usually they succeed one another and jointly index the spread of ignition) is a hypothesis, and it concedes the dispute turns on an unanswerable question: whether task-irrelevant stimuli are truly seen or merely potentially visible
