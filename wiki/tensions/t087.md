# T87 — Is a working memory held at a fixed point, or in a moving trajectory?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The two arms are separated by an imposed mechanism rather than a post-hoc transience index, with the vanilla-RNN control shown to have actually learned the task.

## Position A

**Fixed point.** Sustained delay-period firing is what a recurrent net trained on a delay task converges to on its own — point or line attractors, one per item — and it is the only design that survives an *unknown* delay length by construction, since a state that does not move needs no read-out clock ([[wiki/concepts/attractor-dynamics.md]], [[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]])

## Position B

**Trajectory.** Mouse Neuropixels across >30 regions during olfactory delayed paired association show sequentially peaking, sharply tuned units tiling the delay, which trained vanilla RNNs do not reproduce (transient index far below the animals'). Adding per-unit self-inhibition, sparse recurrence and a sensory/association/motor topology reproduces it — 38.2% vs. 32.6% stimulus-selective units, the same sensory-to-motor selectivity gradient, dPCA trajectories whose velocity does not decay — and the transient arm *beats* the persistent arm at equal parameter count on distractors, 2–6 remembered items and spatial navigation, with higher activity entropy and lower mean squared rate (Liu et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/trnn.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides whether fast **M** should be built to hold a state or to run one: under A the design problem is basin depth and gate timing, under B it is trajectory separation and when to read out. It also decides where capacity comes from — number of distinguishable stable states versus entropy of the activity over the delay

## Status

`LEANING B` on the modelling side, with three caveats the paper states: the two arms are separated *post hoc* by a measured index rather than by an imposed mechanism, the water-maze vanilla RNN scores like a memoryless net and may simply have failed to learn, and the capacity argument reuses the entropy term that is already a component of the transience measure. The variable-delay objection to B is answered but not closed (3–6 s, ~4-point accuracy cost).
