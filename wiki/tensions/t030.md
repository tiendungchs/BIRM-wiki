# T30 — What is replay for — planning the next path, or building a transferable map?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Replay content is manipulated rather than observed: suppress the recurrence-gated component and show the planning benefit survives, or vice versa.

## Position A

**Planning.** Forward replay at choice points resembles the trajectory about to be taken, replay content reflects the imminent plan, and reverse replay supports planning better than forward alone in computational models (Foster 2017; Ólafsdóttir et al. 2018), and a first-principles utility for accessing each memory reproduces the recorded forward/reverse/remote statistics without fitting (Mattar & Daw 2018) — this is the reading that licensed experience replay and prioritised replay in machines. **Prioritised replay's primary source now backs A, but on a different criterion**: Schaul et al. 2016 prioritise by `|δ|` (expected learning progress), not by reward or by imminence, and cite rodent high-TD-error replay rather than reward-replay as the motivation — so the machine artefact A claims descends from a *third* biological finding neither position here is built on ([[wiki/concepts/replay-prioritisation.md]]). **Caveat from the primary source**: the paper this reading is credited with licensing does not make the argument — Mnih et al. 2015 derive replay from gradient decorrelation and behaviour-distribution averaging, sample the buffer **uniformly**, and mention prioritised sweeping only as future work ([[wiki/entities/dqn.md]])

## Position B

**Consolidation, and deliberately anti-planning.** Remote experiences are replayed *more* than the imminent path (Gillespie et al. 2021); replay upsamples the space where least time was spent, reducing the behavioural dwell bias (Grosmark et al. 2021); salient random cues that dominate online representation are *actively suppressed* from ripples (Terada et al. 2022). The proposed principle is a preprogrammed inductive bias toward a map transferable to dissimilar tasks — consolidating everything overfits (Sun et al. 2023; Liao & Losonczy 2024), with the filter's mechanism now measured and its selectivity inducible by implanting a representation (Liao et al. 2024, [[wiki/entities/inhibitory-replay-filter.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what a machine replay buffer should sample. Position A gives reward/TD-error prioritisation, which is what every deployed system uses; position B gives inverse-visitation and cross-episode-recurrence weighting, which nothing uses. ## Status

`LIVE`, but B now has a **normative derivation** where it previously had only recordings: if experience mixes recurring update patterns with one-off ones, gating the long-term write on the fast store's own recall of the update is optimal, and it reproduces "keep what recurs, suppress the idiosyncratic" from first principles — including Terada et al. 2022, where the fixed-location cues *are* the reliable memories (Lindsey & Litwin-Kumar 2024, [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]]). **Position A now has its own normative derivation, and it is older than B's** (Mattar & Daw 2018, [[wiki/concepts/replay-prioritisation.md]]): the utility of accessing any one stored experience is `EVB = Gain × Need` — the policy improvement the resulting backup buys at its target state, times the discounted expected future occupancy of that state — and ordering backups by it reproduces forward replay before a run, reverse replay at reward, remote sleep replay biased to rewarded arms, the *asymmetric* effect of reward increases versus decreases, and the countervailing effects of familiarity, with no parameter fitting. So the *imminent* path dominates for a stated reason: need is the successor-representation row of the current state. Three things keep this from settling the tension. (i) The derivation is about **value** propagation only — replay that trains a generative model or writes a new edge is outside its scope, so it does not touch B's transfer-bias claim on its own ground. (ii) `Gain` is not computable by the agent it is defined for (it presupposes knowing the backup's effect on the policy before performing it), so A's derivation buys a bound, not a mechanism, where B's recall-gate is a mechanism. (iii) It is the *need* term, not a planning premise, that predicts remote and offline replay — which means Gillespie et al. 2021's remote-over-imminent result is a quantitative challenge to A rather than a qualitative refutation of it: A predicts replay tracks expected future occupancy, and whether that is more or less concentrated than observed is a measurement nobody has made against the model. Sun et al. 2023, now ingested, strengthens B on a further axis rather than adjudicating this one: it shows the *amount* of replay is a control variable with an interior optimum set by predictability, and that unregulated reactivation drives generalization below chance — so a buffer tuned for planning value has no term for the harm it does to the map it is consolidating into ([[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]]). Both bodies of evidence are direct recordings and neither is disputed; the review's own resolution is a *schedule* (specific early, generalisable with exposure) rather than an arbitration, and no experiment yet manipulates the two demands against each other in one task. **A staging proposal that could make the two compatible (tentative):** if awake ripples *tag* the just-completed trial and sleep ripples spend the tag, the reward/recency-driven distribution (A) is the selector and the transfer-biased distribution (B) is what the selection seeds, so the two are consecutive stages rather than rivals for one budget (`raw/talk-nd-memory-gating.txt`) **B now has a machine measurement with A's rules run as controls** (Kessler et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]]): across 4- and 8-task continual reinforcement learning, uniform-coverage admission (reservoir sampling, `min(n/t,1)`) beats first-in-first-out and a distance-based coverage rule, while **reward-proportional mini-batch sampling is indistinguishable from uniform** and **uncertainty/surprise-proportional sampling performs at the non-continual baseline** — i.e. both criteria position A licenses are the two that fail. **And the field's foundational deployed rule was already B's.** DQN's uniform sample over the last 10⁶ frames is uniform coverage of a recency window, and it is what produced the 49-game result; prioritisation was added later, by A's reasoning, on top of a rule that had never been justified by either position. Residual for B: the winning rule is uniform over *episodes*, and it forgets like FIFO under deliberate task imbalance, so "even coverage" has not been shown to be implementable over the partition one actually wants covered. **B's central recording is now a causal manipulation rather than an observation** (Liao et al. 2024, [[wiki/entities/inhibitory-replay-filter.md]]). The Terada suppression that anchors B was open to the reading that cue cells are a pre-existing population selected against for some other reason; implanting the representation removes that reading — optogenetically pairing arbitrary pyramidal cells with a *randomly presented* cue makes those cells accumulate ripple suppression, while the identical stimulation *without* a cue makes them gain ripple recruitment, in CA3/CA2 and in CA1. It also supplies B's missing mechanism and its selection statistic (across-episode variance of a representation's co-active partners, accumulated by symmetric spike-timing LTP at `I→E` synapses), with a necessity test: remove inhibitory plasticity and the suppression goes while replay itself stays. What it does **not** do is adjudicate against A — the model is silent on the imminent path, and its own framing is that replay *samples the animal's working model*, which is exactly what a downstream planner would need, so the paper reads A and B as consumer and producer rather than as rivals for one distribution.

**And A's machine wing is now internally split**: `|δ|`-prioritisation wins decisively within one task and loses decisively across a task sequence ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T299), so "prioritise by surprise" cannot be read as a single settled engineering practice supporting A.
