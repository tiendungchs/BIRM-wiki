# T30 — What is replay for — planning the next path, or building a transferable map?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Planning.** Forward replay at choice points resembles the trajectory about to be taken, replay content reflects the imminent plan, and reverse replay supports planning better than forward alone in computational models (Foster 2017; Ólafsdóttir et al. 2018; Mattar & Daw 2018) — this is the reading that licensed experience replay and prioritised replay in machines

## Position B

**Consolidation, and deliberately anti-planning.** Remote experiences are replayed *more* than the imminent path (Gillespie et al. 2021); replay upsamples the space where least time was spent, reducing the behavioural dwell bias (Grosmark et al. 2021); salient random cues that dominate online representation are *actively suppressed* from ripples (Terada et al. 2022). The proposed principle is a preprogrammed inductive bias toward a map transferable to dissimilar tasks — consolidating everything overfits (Sun et al. 2023; Liao & Losonczy 2024)

## Where it bites

Decides what a machine replay buffer should sample. Position A gives reward/TD-error prioritisation, which is what every deployed system uses; position B gives inverse-visitation and cross-episode-recurrence weighting, which nothing uses. It also decides whether gap G14's consolidation channel can share a sampling policy with the planning rollouts of G15, or needs its own

## Status

`LIVE`, but B now has a **normative derivation** where it previously had only recordings: if experience mixes recurring update patterns with one-off ones, gating the long-term write on the fast store's own recall of the update is optimal, and it reproduces "keep what recurs, suppress the idiosyncratic" from first principles — including Terada et al. 2022, where the fixed-location cues *are* the reliable memories (Lindsey & Litwin-Kumar 2024, [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]]). Position A still has no comparable derivation of why the *imminent* path should dominate. Sun et al. 2023, now ingested, strengthens B on a further axis rather than adjudicating this one: it shows the *amount* of replay is a control variable with an interior optimum set by predictability, and that unregulated reactivation drives generalization below chance — so a buffer tuned for planning value has no term for the harm it does to the map it is consolidating into ([[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]]). Both bodies of evidence are direct recordings and neither is disputed; the review's own resolution is a *schedule* (specific early, generalisable with exposure) rather than an arbitration, and no experiment yet manipulates the two demands against each other in one task. **A staging proposal that could make the two compatible (tentative):** if awake ripples *tag* the just-completed trial and sleep ripples spend the tag, the reward/recency-driven distribution (A) is the selector and the transfer-biased distribution (B) is what the selection seeds, so the two are consecutive stages rather than rivals for one budget (`raw/talk-nd-memory-gating.txt`) **B now has a machine measurement with A's rules run as controls** (Kessler et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]]): across 4- and 8-task continual reinforcement learning, uniform-coverage admission (reservoir sampling, `min(n/t,1)`) beats first-in-first-out and a distance-based coverage rule, while **reward-proportional mini-batch sampling is indistinguishable from uniform** and **uncertainty/surprise-proportional sampling performs at the non-continual baseline** — i.e. both criteria position A licenses are the two that fail. Residual for B: the winning rule is uniform over *episodes*, and it forgets like FIFO under deliberate task imbalance, so "even coverage" has not been shown to be implementable over the partition one actually wants covered.
