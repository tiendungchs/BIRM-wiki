# T302 — How deep is a single cortical pyramidal neuron?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The same minimal-network-fitting procedure is run against an explicit two-layer (segment-bank + OR/sum) model of the *same* L5PC, testing whether a 2-layer parameterisation can clear the AUC ≥ 0.9910 bar that a 128-unit single-hidden-layer network cleared only after NMDA removal.

## Position A

**Two layers.** The standing abstraction is a segment bank read out by one somatic stage: Poirazi, Brannon & Mel 2003 fit a CA1 pyramidal cell as a two-layer network, and Ahmad & Hawkins 2016 model the cell as ~100 independent NMDA-spike detectors whose output is closer to an OR over detections than to a weighted sum ([[wiki/concepts/dendritic-computation.md]], [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T63). Both are depth 2, and both are the form every dendritic architecture in the wiki imports

## Position B

**Five to eight.** Fitting the I/O map of a full L5PC compartmental model at 1 ms / single-spike resolution needs a temporally convolutional network of 5–8 layers, 128 channels, 153 ms of history; a thorough search over fully connected architectures of any depth and width failed entirely. Removing the NMDA conductance from the same cell drops the requirement to one hidden layer of 128 units, and a *single basal branch* with 9 NMDA synapses needs only 4 units — so the extra depth is neither the branch detector nor the morphology but the cross-branch, voltage-dependent NMDA interaction that a two-layer model assumes away (Beniaguev et al. 2021, [[wiki/concepts/neuron-complexity-index.md]])

## Where it bites

The conversion factor between artificial units and biological neurons, in the direction opposite to T63. If B, a cortical circuit's computational depth is `~5–8 ×` its anatomical stage count and any "N-layer network vs. N-stage cortex" comparison in the wiki is off by that factor; it also means the segment-bank primitive that [[wiki/entities/dendritic-ann.md]], [[wiki/concepts/sparse-distributed-representations.md]] and [[wiki/entities/thousand-brains-theory.md]] all build on is a *lossy* neuron model whose loss is concentrated exactly where NMDA-mediated interaction between branches lives. Caveats: B is an upper bound found by gradient descent over FCN/TCN architectures only — a recurrent or input-dependent-timescale unit might fit the same map shallowly — and B fits a biophysical *simulation*, while A's detectors rest on measured NMDA spike thresholds
