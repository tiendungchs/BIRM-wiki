# T208 — Does an application-layer harness measure a capability the model has, or one the harness author has?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**The model's.** On ARC-AGI-2 a refinement harness takes a *frozen* Gemini 3 Pro from 31% to 54% at 38× cost, and Claude Opus 4.5 to ~54% with the same harness — the wiki's largest single measurement of what an acceptance test is worth, and its cleanest evidence that the rejector rather than the proposer is binding (G68). The harness is open-source and ARC-Prize-verified, and the ARC reports themselves expect good harness ideas to migrate behind vendor APIs, i.e. to be absorbed as model capability ([[wiki/entities/arc-agi-2.md]])

## Position B

**The author's, on the environments the author saw.** On ARC-AGI-3 the same class of intervention is bimodal: with harnesses built against three public environments and then tested on the full public set, Opus 4.6 goes 0.0% → **97.1%** on a variant of a seen environment class and 0.0% → **0.0%** on an unseen one, same model throughout. The foundation open-sourced a replay-driven harness scoring **100%** on the public set specifically to demonstrate that the number carries no information, and excludes harnesses from the official leaderboard entirely ([[wiki/entities/arc-agi-3.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides whether the wiki's rejector programme (G68) is a general architectural claim or a task-specific engineering one. Both measurements are real and they are not strictly contradictory — version 2's harness was evaluated on tasks its authors had not seen, version 3's transfer test is across *environments* rather than across items — but they license opposite bets. Under A, build the rejector and expect it to generalise; under B, a rejector is only as general as the failure mode it was written against, and the +23 points on version 2 should be re-measured on a set drawn after the harness was frozen. The discriminating experiment is cheap and neither report ran it: freeze one harness, evaluate on two sets differing only in whether the harness author saw their generating process
