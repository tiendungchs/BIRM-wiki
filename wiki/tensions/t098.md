# T98 — Does the episodic store address the controller by delivering content, or by setting its inhibitory gain?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Spec:** `D145`, `D255` ([[_brainstorm/birm-spec.md]] §11)  
**Closes when:** The channel is manipulated with content and gain dissociated — inactivation that removes context without changing prefrontal inhibitory tone, or the reverse.

## Position A

**Content.** The wiki's account of the hippocampus→prefrontal channel is information transfer: ventral/intermediate hippocampal input supplies *context* — which room, which task phase, which rule — and prefrontal cells phase-lock to hippocampal theta with coherence rising at choice points and after rule acquisition, i.e. the channel is read as a data path whose traffic increases when more context is needed (Euston et al. 2012, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]).

## Position B

**Gain.** The measured synaptic effect is short-latency AMPA excitation of prelimbic pyramidal cells **followed by inhibition**, because the same terminals drive GABAergic interneurons. Inactivating ventral hippocampus *lowers* prelimbic interneuron firing and *raises* pyramidal firing, and in extinguished rats raises freezing — so the channel's normal action is to withhold prefrontal output the context does not license, and removing it disinhibits rather than starves the controller (Spedding & Jay 2012).

## Where it bites

Whether a model should route the retrieved context into the controller as an *input feature* (concatenate, attend) or as a *multiplicative/subtractive gate on the controller's output repertoire*. The two make opposite predictions under ablation: cutting a data path degrades performance toward chance, cutting an inhibitory gate produces context-inappropriate but confidently executed responses — which is what the fear-renewal and extinction-recall disconnection results look like.

## Status

`LIVE` — **but a reconciliation is now on the table, and it re-scopes the row** (Eichenbaum 2017, [[wiki/entities/hippocampal-prefrontal-channel.md]]). The ablation signature Position B predicts — context-inappropriate but confidently executed responses — is exactly what removing the *controller* produces: prefrontal-lesioned rats remember today's odour list and falsely recognise previous days' lists, and prefrontal-damaged humans learning `A–B` then `A–C` fail by intruding the original associate. And the circuit model puts the suppression on the **return** leg: the controller projects to perirhinal and lateral entorhinal cortex, which gate the store's input, and prefrontal projections in cortex preferentially target inhibitory neurons. Under that reading content and gain are not two readings of one wire but the two legs of a loop — forward leg delivers context, return leg suppresses — and this row's closing experiment must be run **per-direction**, which no manipulation cited on either side does.
