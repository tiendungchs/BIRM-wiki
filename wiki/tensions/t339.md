# T339 — Does nucleus reuniens carry content between controller and store, or only open the direction?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Reuniens activity is decoded for the trajectory it is supposed to be carrying, at the grain the hippocampal read-out shows — or the relay is driven with a *scrambled* pattern and the store's representation is shown to acquire the specificity anyway, which would settle it for the coordinator.

## Position A

**Content.** Prefrontal cortex → reuniens → septal hippocampus carries a representation of the **future path** during goal-directed behaviour; inactivating the pathway abolishes it (Ito et al. 2015, [[wiki/entities/nucleus-reuniens.md]]). Read this way the relay is the controller's write channel — a goal-conditioned constraint delivered into the store's own coordinate system, which is what `G37` and `G51` need.

## Position B

**Coordination only.** Four results in Eichenbaum 2017 ([[wiki/entities/hippocampal-prefrontal-channel.md]]) point the other way. (i) Reuniens firing rates *differentiate* left- from right-turn routes on the common maze segment but contain **no detailed trajectory information** — enough to bias, not to specify. (ii) **Artificial** optogenetic drive works: net excitation increases and net inhibition decreases context-specific freezing (Xu & Südhof 2013), and a channel whose arbitrary activation improves a memory's specificity is not delivering that memory. (iii) Muscimol inactivation during delay or choice reduces prefrontal phase-locking to hippocampal theta and **eliminates bidirectional functional connectivity** — the measured effect is on the coupling. (iv) Prefrontal→reuniens inactivation spares contextual-fear acquisition and produces **overgeneralisation**, i.e. loss of a discrimination rather than loss of a message.

## Where it bites

The two readings cost different things to build. Under A the relay is a trained module with its own representation and its own capacity — the controller's write bandwidth is a design parameter. Under B it is a low-dimensional switch that opens one direction of an existing edge and carries no payload, which is what the intra-trial direction reversal (hippocampus leads at context entry, prefrontal leads at object sampling, both correct-trials-only) actually requires, and which costs one scalar. B also demotes the collateralised-broadcast reading of `G53`: a bus that carries no content is a clock, and the wiki already has a gap for clocks (`G54`).

## Status

`LIVE` — the two positions rest on the same laboratory and partly the same animals, and the discriminating measurement (decode the relay at the grain of the effect it produces downstream) has not been reported. Note that A's evidence is a *decoding claim about hippocampal representations under a pathway manipulation*, never a demonstration that the prefrontal cortex specified the path — a limitation [[wiki/entities/nucleus-reuniens.md]] already carried before this row existed.
