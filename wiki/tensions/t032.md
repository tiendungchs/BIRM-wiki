# T32 — Is serial order carried by oscillatory phase or by rate-coded time cells?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The generator of rate-coded time cells is measured rather than modelled, discriminating Rolls's three candidates against the phase account.

## Position A

**Phase.** Theta/gamma nesting assigns each item in a sequence to a distinct gamma cycle within a theta cycle, the standard account of order in the hippocampal literature (Lisman & Redish).

**The human evidence for the nesting itself** (Roux & Uhlhaas 2014, `raw/roux-2014-wm-oscillations-alpha-gamma-theta-gamma.md`): theta–gamma phase–amplitude coupling in human hippocampal depth recordings rises with the number of items held (Axmacher et al.) and is stronger on correctly identified items (Holz et al.); delay theta appears preferentially in *sequential* paradigms (Sternberg) and is **replaced by alpha, at different sites**, when the same task holds a simultaneous visual array instead. That substitution is the position's best discriminating handle so far — a rate-coded time-cell chain has no reason to change carrier band with the presentation format — and it comes with a capacity prediction the rate account does not make: `slots = frame period / gamma period`, so sequential material (theta frame) should have the larger span, which it does. It remains coupling measured against *load*, not against *order*: nothing here shows position `i` recovered from phase.

**The direct observation, and it is not hippocampal.** Simultaneous single-unit and local-field recording in monkey prefrontal cortex: spikes evoked by a given stimulus fall at a characteristic point of a **32 Hz** cycle, and when a second item must be held the spikes carrying it occupy a *different* point of the same wave, with ≈3 Hz spike synchronization present alongside (via Knowlton, Hummel & Holyoak 2012). Two items, two phases, in the structure the position requires. This extends the row's Position A beyond the hippocampus and gives it a measurement rather than a model — but it shows *item identity indexed by phase*, not order read off phase, which is the claim still owed. The same arithmetic run forward yields a capacity of 4–6 bindings ([[wiki/concepts/working-memory.md]], [[wiki/entities/lisa.md]]), so under A the span and the order are one mechanism, exactly as under B

## Position B

**Rate.** Different neurons fire at different points within a delay period, and order arises from noise-driven transitions between attractors coupled by slightly asymmetric weights, with adaptation retiring each state — no clock, and the span falls out as `7 ± 2` (Rolls 2013, [[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides whether a machine sequence memory needs an oscillatory scaffold or only a recurrent net with adaptation and noise. Under B, order memory and episodic storage share one network and one learning rule, and stochasticity is load-bearing rather than a nuisance to be annealed away

## Status

`LIVE` — the rate-coded time cells are a direct observation (MacDonald et al. 2011); which mechanism *generates* them is modelled, not measured, and Rolls offers three candidate generators without discriminating them. A now has a direct observation of its own on the other side of the brain (item-specific gamma phase in prefrontal cortex), which removes the asymmetry that made the row look one-sided without deciding it: neither observation is of *order*, and no preparation has both measures at once. A also now has human coupling evidence and a format manipulation that switches the frame band (Roux & Uhlhaas 2014), which the rate account has no mechanism to produce — but it is still coupling-versus-load rather than phase-versus-position
