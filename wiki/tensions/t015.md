# T15 — Is training data a design lever or an anchor?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The wiki stops using the control surface for both questions, or an experiment shows a data change and an architecture change are not interchangeable levers for the same identifiability result.

## Position A

**A lever, co-equal with the other three.** The control surface is architecture, training data, loss and optimizer; the data lever is the one that blocks a specific shortcut *by construction* (adversarial vulnerability, texture bias), and it is the only one whose effect on identifiability is provable — a single environment cannot distinguish a causal edge from a correlational one, an environment *family* can (Geirhos et al. 2020; [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]])

## Position B

**An anchor, not a component.** The designed objects are objective function, learning rule and architecture; the environment is "a crucial consideration to anchor the core components, but not one of the components itself", because the components pass through the genome bottleneck while the environment supplies unbounded information that no compact design can contain (Richards et al. 2019; [[wiki/concepts/three-component-framework.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether curating environment families counts as *building the model* or as *choosing the benchmark* — i.e. whether gap G16 is closed by a data programme or must be paid for in architecture and objective
