# T400 — Does an extrinsic reward *add* to the self-generated one, or suppress its generation?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** One agent carrying both a task reward and a self-generated reward is trained under a salient extrinsic contingency and then has that contingency **removed**, with the self-generated term's magnitude read out directly rather than inferred from behaviour. If the intrinsic term is unchanged through the reward phase and the post-removal engagement returns to baseline, A. If the intrinsic term is attenuated during the reward phase and does **not** recover after removal — engagement below the never-rewarded control — B. The human version of this experiment exists and is the source of the dispute; the machine version needs no new mechanism, only a logged intrinsic term.

## Position A

**One currency, additive.** The rewards sum into a single scalar before the comparison. [[wiki/concepts/subjective-value.md]] is the direct measurement: delayed money, immediate money, gains and losses all land on one axis in the same voxels, discounted before the comparison, with the provenance discarded. [[wiki/concepts/intrinsic-motivation-typology.md]] assumes the same shape structurally — every formula in all three families is a bonus *added* to `r` — and [[wiki/concepts/expected-value-of-control.md]] writes the whole decision as one sum of payoffs minus one cost. Murayama 2022's own headline claim is on this side too ("all rewards are processed on the same hardware"), and it is what makes the common reward network activate for trivia answers and for money alike (Gruber et al. 2014; Lau et al. 2020 — shared striatal activation for curiosity and hunger). Under A the undermining effect is not a signal-flow fact but a behavioural artefact of contrast, expectation or the removal of a now-expected payment.

## Position B

**Prioritised, and the intrinsic term is not generated while the extrinsic one is salient.** Murayama 2022 ([[wiki/concepts/self-boosting-knowledge-acquisition.md]]) argues extrinsic rewards are tangible, evolutionarily prior and carry clear incentive salience, so they are processed preferentially, and their presence *prevents* effective self-generation of the knowledge reward. The evidence is a within-subject neural dissociation rather than a preference: striatal activation is present while participants play an unrewarded game, and is **no longer observed** after performance payment is introduced and then withdrawn (Murayama et al. 2010); preference change under insufficient justification tracks striatal change (Izuma et al. 2010); the undermining effect is larger the more **salient** the reward is made (Amabile et al. 1986; Ross 1975), which is a property of the cue and not of its magnitude. The metacognitive result points the same way: the downward bias in forecasting one's own engagement **disappears when a reward is promised** (Kuratomi et al. 2018), as if the self-generated term were no longer there to be mispredicted.

## Where it bites

Whether an intrinsic reward can be *added* to a task objective at all, which is what every architecture in the wiki does. Under A, mixing is a weighting problem — pick `α`, tune it, and the two terms coexist. Under B, a salient task reward silently zeroes the term the designer just added, and the failure is invisible in the logs because only the sum is recorded; worse, the suppression **outlasts the extrinsic reward**, so a curriculum that uses dense shaping early to bootstrap a hard task destroys the drive it was bootstrapping for. B also makes `G129`'s supply question urgent rather than academic: a store-generated reward that is switched off whenever the task pays has no opportunity to enter its own positive feedback loop. The two positions further disagree about the one applied prescription the framework offers — use extrinsic reward to *start* an agent and then withdraw it — which is coherent under A and self-defeating under B unless the salience is kept low throughout.

## Status

`LIVE` — the human behavioural effect is meta-analytically solid and the neural dissociation is a real within-subject result, but it is one laboratory, the read-out is haemodynamic and undifferentiated, and no experiment separates the three realisations of "prioritised" (attentional capture on the salient cue, gain reduction on the intrinsic channel, a hard gate). No machine agent has ever logged its intrinsic term separately across the introduction and removal of a task reward, which is the cheapest discriminating experiment available and costs one extra scalar in the log.
