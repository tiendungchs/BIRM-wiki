# T240 — Is a resting-state functional network an object, or an artefact of the integration window?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The attractor set's stability under the window length used to estimate `Σ` is reported, which no fcANN result currently does.

## Position A

**An object.** The wiki's operative practice: a group-averaged resting-state functional connectome is a stable enough description of a brain to be used *directly* as a coupling matrix, yielding four attractors that replicate at `r = 0.93` across three sites, scanners and sequences and explain more held-out variance than the data's own leading principal components ([[wiki/entities/fcann.md]]). The resting-state literature's canonical result is likewise a fixed inventory of ~7–10 replicable networks

## Position B

**An artefact of the window.** Deco, Jirsa & McIntosh 2011: functional connectivity recapitulates structural connectivity only at long integration windows; at short windows short-lived configurations appear that the anatomy does not predict, within-subject test–retest reliability of FC is low — and a model built on a realistic skeleton *reproduces that low reliability*, making it a prediction rather than a measurement defect. Their explicit warning is against "holding too strongly to the notion that there are a certain number of functional networks". **Now with the primary numbers** (Honey et al. 2009, [[wiki/concepts/function-to-structure-inference.md]]): within-subject rsFC reliability is `r = 0.38–0.69` across sessions and `r = 0.39–0.61` across two 10-min windows of one scan — and a 998-node model with a literally constant coupling matrix and no scanner reproduces its own rsFC at only `r = 0.69–0.80` across consecutive 8-min windows, because long-range temporal autocorrelation makes the effective sample size far smaller than the frame count. The one anatomical qualifier: pairs *with* a tract are significantly less variable, so the persistent networks are the densely wired ones

## Where it bites

Whether any wiki architecture may be fitted to a long-run correlation matrix. If B, fcANN's landscape is a time-average over a repertoire and its attractors are averages of visited configurations rather than configurations; more generally [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] acquires an unset window parameter on which the recovered graph depends

## Status

`LIVE` — not strictly contradictory: high *group-average* replicability across sites is compatible with low *within-subject* reliability, since averaging is exactly what recovers the anatomy-determined component. The discriminating test is whether fcANN's attractor set is stable under the window length used to estimate `Σ`, which is not reported. Position B has strengthened: the low reliability is now measured in a system where the ground-truth coupling is fixed by construction, which removes the measurement-defect reading entirely
