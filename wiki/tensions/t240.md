# T240 — Is a resting-state functional network an object, or an artefact of the integration window?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The attractor set's stability under the window length used to estimate `Σ` is reported, which no fcANN result currently does.

## Position A

**An object.** The wiki's operative practice: a group-averaged resting-state functional connectome is a stable enough description of a brain to be used *directly* as a coupling matrix, yielding four attractors that replicate at `r = 0.93` across three sites, scanners and sequences and explain more held-out variance than the data's own leading principal components ([[wiki/entities/fcann.md]]). The resting-state literature's canonical result is likewise a fixed inventory of ~7–10 replicable networks. **Now with its own primary evidence** (Laumann et al. 2017, [[wiki/concepts/stationary-surrogate-null.md]]): 10 subjects × 300 min of resting BOLD scored against surrogates matched in covariance, power spectrum and length and stationary by construction. The surrogates reproduce the sliding-window `k`-means "state" inventory at `r = 0.96 ± 0.01` with an identical cluster-validity curve, and the real data's excess multivariate kurtosis is accounted for by head motion (`r = 0.50`, abolished by `FD > 0.2 mm` censoring) and drowsiness (`r = 0.315`), leaving a small residual. The statistic is not blind — it detects an imposed eyes-open→eyes-closed covariance switch and real task/rest alternation (`t(23) = 3.05–5.19`), and continuous rest sits closer to the stationary null than any task condition. Conclusion: **a single correlation structure adequately describes rest**

## Position B

**An artefact of the window.** Deco, Jirsa & McIntosh 2011: functional connectivity recapitulates structural connectivity only at long integration windows; at short windows short-lived configurations appear that the anatomy does not predict, within-subject test–retest reliability of FC is low — and a model built on a realistic skeleton *reproduces that low reliability*, making it a prediction rather than a measurement defect. Their explicit warning is against "holding too strongly to the notion that there are a certain number of functional networks". **Now with the primary numbers** (Honey et al. 2009, [[wiki/concepts/function-to-structure-inference.md]]): within-subject rsFC reliability is `r = 0.38–0.69` across sessions and `r = 0.39–0.61` across two 10-min windows of one scan — and a 998-node model with a literally constant coupling matrix and no scanner reproduces its own rsFC at only `r = 0.69–0.80` across consecutive 8-min windows, because long-range temporal autocorrelation makes the effective sample size far smaller than the frame count. The one anatomical qualifier: pairs *with* a tract are significantly less variable, so the persistent networks are the densely wired ones. **The mechanism survives but the inference from it does not.** Laumann's result agrees with every reliability number quoted here and gives them a different cause: sampling variability on a band-limited, strongly autocorrelated signal, where the effective sample size is far below the frame count. Low reliability is then an estimator property, not evidence that the target moved — so B's *window-dependence* is confirmed while B's *repertoire* reading loses the observable it was carried by. What B retains: the window remains an unset parameter, correlations shorter than ~minutes are uninformative by construction, and nothing here touches non-stationarity at electrophysiological timescales, lag/propagation structure, or spectral non-stationarity at constant covariance, all of which are consistent with the null

## Where it bites

Whether any wiki architecture may be fitted to a long-run correlation matrix. If B, fcANN's landscape is a time-average over a repertoire and its attractors are averages of visited configurations rather than configurations; more generally [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] acquires an unset window parameter on which the recovered graph depends

## Status

`LIVE` — not strictly contradictory: high *group-average* replicability across sites is compatible with low *within-subject* reliability, since averaging is exactly what recovers the anatomy-determined component. The discriminating test is whether fcANN's attractor set is stable under the window length used to estimate `Σ`, which is not reported. Position B has strengthened on the *estimator* and weakened on the *object*: the low reliability is measured in a system where the ground-truth coupling is fixed by construction, which removes the measurement-defect reading — and Laumann et al. 2017 then shows the same sampling variability generates the entire dynamic-state phenomenology out of stationary data, so low reliability no longer licenses "a repertoire is being visited". The tension narrows to its stated closing condition rather than closing: a long-window `Σ` is now the *recommended* estimate rather than a suspect one, but whether fcANN's four attractors are stable under the window length used to estimate `Σ` is still unreported. A second, cheap discriminator is now available and also unrun — compute fcANN's landscape on a covariance-, spectrum- and length-matched stationary surrogate and check that the attractor count and occupancies differ from the real data's
