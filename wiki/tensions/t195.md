# T195 — Is sequential presentation a cost paid against joint training?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Yes — this is the premise of the whole continual-learning literature.** Gradient descent on `L_B` treats parameters load-bearing for A as free, so every family in [[wiki/concepts/continual-learning.md]] exists to recover something of what simultaneous access would have given, and the best outcomes reported are *parity* with joint training rather than an improvement

## Position B

**Not always.** Loading 16 mixed patterns (alternating integer-periodic and parametric-family) into one reservoir subspace-by-subspace gave mean NRMSE **0.094** against **0.19** for loading all 16 simultaneously with scaling and regularisation tuned for the latter. Jaeger states the result is surprising and unexplained. The two homogeneous conditions came out near-parity in the ordinary direction (0.078 vs 0.063 integer-periodic; 0.136 vs 0.131 parametric), so the inversion appears exactly where the pattern set is **heterogeneous** ([[wiki/entities/conceptor.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides whether incremental writing is a constraint to be mitigated or a regulariser to be exploited. The proposed mechanism is visible in the same source's diagnostics: under simultaneous loading a single solution strikes a compromise across all items on equal terms, while under incremental loading item `j` competes only with items `1…j−1` and can be fitted sharply in the space they left — which predicts the advantage should grow with heterogeneity and vanish with homogeneity, which is what was observed

## Status

`LIVE` — one source, one architecture, `N = 100`, 16 items, and the incremental condition required an extra hand-set step (singular values pushed through `tanh(50(2σ−1))`) that the simultaneous condition did not, so the comparison is not clean. The discriminating experiment is cheap and unrun: sweep pattern-set heterogeneity with the rectangularisation applied in both conditions **A second instance, different architecture, different field** (Kessler et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]]): a DreamerV2 agent's average forward transfer is **+0.49** on 3-task Minigrid (forgetting **−0.11**, i.e. it ends *better* on earlier tasks than when it left them), it solves all three tasks continually while the single-task agent struggles on them in 1M steps, and on 8-task Minihack it solves River-Monster-v0 *more* often than the same agent trained on that task alone. Attributed to warm-starting: the world model reconstructs a new environment faster having fitted related ones, and skills from earlier tasks compose. Unlike the conceptor case this is not a heterogeneity effect — the mechanism claimed is shared structure across tasks, which predicts the inversion should *vanish* as tasks become dissimilar, the opposite prediction from position B's existing one. The discriminating experiment is the same in both: sweep task similarity.
