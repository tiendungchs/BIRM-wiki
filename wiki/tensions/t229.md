# T229 — Does making the latent structure legible and editable make a rewriting environment tractable?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** An RL agent is trained inside Baba Is AI and a human baseline collected under a stated protocol — the current evidence is three 2024 models and one product-review anecdote.

## Position A

**Yes — legibility, sparsity, boundedness and meta-stationarity are the conditions, and they are what the lift needs.** [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]'s treatment of hardness source 6 argues that lifting rule-state into the node, `s' = (base_state, rule_config)`, restores stationarity, and that the only obstacle is tractability of the lifted space `(base) × (rule-configs)` — learnable whenever rule-config factorises and rewrites are sparse, legible, bounded and meta-stationary (gap G7). On that reading a domain meeting all five clauses should be the easy case, and G8's reification requirement is discharged for free when the rule is already an object

## Position B

**No — a domain meeting every clause is solved by nobody.** [[wiki/entities/baba-is-ai.md]] renders each rule as three movable word tiles, active iff horizontally aligned: ~15 possible rules, one push per rewrite, the rule written in words inside the observation, and an activation predicate that never changes in any level. GPT-4o reaches **100%** on the same environments when obeying the rules suffices, and GPT-4o / Gemini-1.5-Pro / Gemini-1.5-Flash reach **17.33 / 14.67 / 20.0%** when winning requires composing two edits — failing the held-out `break; make; goto` under every rotation of which plan shape is withheld, with all three primitives demonstrated in context. Adding a *causally inactive* rule lowers accuracy at every rung of the distractor ladder, so even reading the rule state is not free. **And the reference system is not a counterexample**: [[wiki/entities/fluxx.md]] satisfies the same five clauses with a rule-config of a few named slots, face up in English, one rewrite per play and a countable generator, and experienced human players report losing the combined effect at ten to eleven concurrent rules — one of which created a live win condition nobody noticed for several turns `(tentative)`. `|rule_config|` is multiplicative in the live slots whether or not each slot is legible

## Where it bites

Whether G7 is a statement about learnability or only about well-posedness, and where G8's residue lives. The conditions make the problem well-posed; nothing in the wiki searches over rewrites, and the models that parse the rules perfectly never consider editing them — which relocates the gap to the selector (G61) and to subgoal decomposition (G33) rather than to the representation

## Status

`LIVE` — one benchmark, three models of one 2024 vintage, no RL agent trained in the environment, no human baseline, and SD > mean on the hard set; the human-side datum is one anecdote in a product review and carries no protocol
