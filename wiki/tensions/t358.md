# T358 — Does novelty enter valuation as a bonus or as a penalty?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** One agent architecture is run with novelty entering as an additive positive term and, at matched magnitude, as an annealing negative term removed by exposure — on the same tasks, scoring both sample efficiency and the rate of entry into irreversibly bad states. A predicts the two are equivalent up to a constant baseline shift; B predicts they separate exactly on the irreversibility measure, because a penalty the agent has not yet discharged cannot bait it into a novel state. On the biological side: whether any novelty-driven **approach** survives silencing of the danger channel, which the source did not test.

## Position A

**Novelty is a bonus.** Expected free energy adds an explicit parameter-novelty term `D_KL[q(θ|y,x) ‖ q(θ|x)]` with a *positive* sign, and the ablations show it is the term that pays: in environments where no action changes the observation model, every method without it walks straight to the exit and retrieves nothing ([[wiki/concepts/epistemic-value.md]]). Human electroencephalogram fits give novelty-seeking its own fitted precision. Every count-based or curiosity bonus in machine reinforcement learning has the same sign.

## Position B

**Novelty is a penalty that anneals.** First presentation of an unfamiliar palatable food, and of an inedible novel object, activates the same CGRP<sup>PBN</sup> neurons that carry footshock, visceral malaise and itch; the activation recurs after every bite on the first trial. Silencing those neurons with tetanus toxin **attenuates food neophobia** over the first two exposures. With acclimation the response inverts for food — to the pre-consummatory inhibition seen for familiar chow — and consumption latency falls `115.2 ± 16.0 s → 20.4 ± 4.1 s`; for the neutral marble it decays to nothing (Campos et al. 2018, [[wiki/concepts/general-danger-channel.md]]). The drive to sample is then the *relief* of discharging a standing penalty, the same arithmetic as `r = −Δ(need)` on [[wiki/concepts/homeostatic-need-signal.md]] applied to uncertainty.

## Where it bites

**The sign of the exploration term, and what it makes unreachable.** A bonus can be arbitrarily large and pulls the agent toward the unknown, which is why bonus-driven agents walk into unrecoverable states; an annealing penalty pushes the agent away from the unknown and is discharged only by exposure that happens for other reasons, which makes exploration *slow and safe* rather than *fast and reckless*. The two also differ in where the term lives: under A it is computed from a belief over parameters and added at valuation; under B it is a sensory-grade signal on the same line as pain, available to defensive reflexes before any valuation happens.

They are not simple negations of each other. B's signal is *behaviourally* a penalty and is still the thing that gets the animal to sample eventually; A's term is formally a reward and still produces cautious information-gathering. What separates them is the baseline and the failure mode, not the direction of the eventual behaviour.

**A third shape is on the record and neither position predicts it.** Human curiosity is an inverted U over *confidence* — it peaks when the agent has intermediate confidence it already knows the answer and falls both when confidence is high and when the topic is nearly unknown (information-gap theory; Gottlieb & Oudeyer 2018, [[wiki/concepts/information-sampling-vs-search.md]]). A monotone bonus (A) predicts maximal demand at minimal knowledge; an annealing penalty (B) predicts avoidance there and nothing at the peak. The inverted U is what a *learning-progress* term produces, which is a function of neither the novelty of a state nor its familiarity but of the derivative between them — so the closing experiment above should carry a third arm. **That third arm now has a fitted coefficient rather than a hypothesis** ([[wiki/concepts/learning-progress.md]], Ten et al. 2021): in 382 free-choice participants a utility `w_PC·PC + w_LP·LP` beats either term alone in ~72% of individuals, `w_PC` and `w_LP` are uncorrelated, and the *progress* weight is the only one that predicts avoiding an **unlearnable** activity — the state that is maximally novel forever. A monotone bonus (A) parks there permanently; an annealing penalty (B) predicts avoidance of it from the start and cannot explain the ~36.92% of trials instructed participants spend there before leaving. A derivative term predicts both: approach while competence still moves, abandonment once it stops, with the divergence appearing at ~trial 80.
