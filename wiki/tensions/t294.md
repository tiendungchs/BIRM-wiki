# T294 — Is the human developmental lever an *extended* schedule or an *accelerated* one — and does the wiki's `G100` cite the wrong window?

**Status:** `BOTH`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A claim about the human growth schedule names its variable and age band: volume accelerates in year 1, gray-matter gene expression is delayed at ~10 years.

## Position A

**Extension.** [[wiki/concepts/emergent-modularity.md]], from Sherwood, Subiaul & Zawidzki 2008: the human neonatal brain is ~27% of adult size against 36% in chimpanzees and 70% in macaques, postnatal growth continues at the *fetal* rate through the whole first year, and adult neocortex is molecularly held plastic (elevated THBS2/THBS4). On this reading the lever is prolongation of the infant plasticity window, and triadic joint attention at ~9 months is inside it. `G100` was written from this position.

## Position B

**Acceleration on the variable that produced the size, delay on a different variable a decade later.** Somel et al. 2009 flags that the large human brain is attributed to *rapid early postnatal growth rates rather than an extended brain-growth period* (Leigh 2004), and its own measurement finds the neotenic transcriptional shift is (i) **sparse** — ~4% of the prefrontal transcriptome, with a transcriptome-wide shift positively excluded by simulation, (ii) **gray-matter-specific** (`P = 0.0001`; white matter `P > 0.6`), and (iii) **peaking at ~10 years**, not in infancy. Earlier global formulations of neoteny (Bolk) are rejected here on the same grounds they were rejected morphologically.

## Where it bites

Directly on `G100`'s specification. If A, the machine prescription is "run the whole early phase longer" — a global schedule stretch. If B, it is "delay the *commitment* operator in a small, named subset of modules, at one point mid-training, while the rest keeps the ancestral clock" — a sparse per-module phase vector, which is a different and much cheaper object. The two also disagree about **which behaviour the lever explains**: A dates the payoff to the 9-month joint-attention onset, B to adolescence. Nothing measures both variables in one design, and no wiki architecture implements either.

## Status

`BOTH` — they are not contradictory once the variable and the age band are named, and the row exists because the wiki was quoting them as one lever. **Threshold:** *volume* accelerates in year 1; *gray-matter gene expression* is delayed at ~10 years. A claim about the human growth schedule is under-specified until it says which. `LIVE` on the causal question — whether either shift produces the cognitive difference is untested (`DH3`)
