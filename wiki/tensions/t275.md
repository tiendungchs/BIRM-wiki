# T275 — Does suppressing the irrelevant need its own channel, or does it fall out of biasing the relevant?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The excitatory/inhibitory assignment is confirmed synaptically rather than by a fitted coupling-filter sign, and the suppression channel shown addressable independently of the sustaining one.

## Position A

**It falls out.** Guided activation: the controller emits only excitatory bias, and because the controlled systems already run mutual inhibition among their units, raising the matching units suppresses the rest for free — so "enhance the relevant" and "suppress the irrelevant" are one operation and no suppression signal needs an address ([[wiki/concepts/cognitive-control.md]], Miller et al. 2002). The psychometrics agree by omission: no inhibition-specific factor survives a bifactor reparameterisation (T103)

## Position B

**It is a separate population, and the two are doubly dissociable.** In the prefrontal–mediodorsal loop the sustaining and the suppressing signals come from *different thalamic cells* with different excitabilities, different temporal profiles and different cortical targets: transient cells provide excitatory functional input to cue-selective prefrontal cells, persistent cells provide inhibitory input routed through fast-spiking interneurons and couple to those interneurons more strongly (`p = 0.78 × 10⁻²`). Weak optogenetic suppression removes only the excitatory channel — transient prefrontal tuning falls, fast-spiking firing is untouched (`p = 0.81`), out-of-context spiking does not rise; strong suppression removes the inhibitory channel — out-of-context cue-selective spiking rises and fast-spiking firing falls (`p = 0.71 × 10⁻²`). Prefrontal fast-spiking cells themselves inhibit opposite-context cells more than same-context ones, so the suppression is *addressed* by content, not by competition ([[wiki/entities/mediodorsal-thalamus.md]], Rikhye et al. 2018)

## Where it bites

Decides whether a machine controller can be a single additive bias vector into a softmax-normalised substrate, or needs a second, separately gated output whose target set is the complement of the first. It also decides where continual-learning protection comes from: under A, out-of-context weights are protected only insofar as they lose the competition, which is graded and leaky; under B there is a dedicated channel to lesion, and lesioning it costs the earlier task at chance level

## Status

`LEANING B` on directness — B has a double dissociation from two calibrated perturbation strengths in the same animals, A has a mechanism argument plus a null factor. Two caveats keep it open. (i) The excitatory/inhibitory assignment in B is a *fitted coupling filter* sign, not a synaptic measurement; the projection is glutamatergic and the interneuron class is unidentified. (ii) The two positions are not exclusive at different grains — B's addressed channel acts *through* fast-spiking interneurons, i.e. through the very local competition A relies on, so B may be an addressed way of setting A's competition rather than an alternative to it. The discriminating experiment: whether the out-of-context suppression survives when local prefrontal inhibition is blocked. Unrun
