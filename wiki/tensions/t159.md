# T159 — Is a geometric structure that appears only after dimensionality reduction a property of the representation or of the projection?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A steering result is obtained from a direction whose additivity exists only after projection, or shown impossible — projection cannot create additivity, so the discarded remainder must be characterised.

## Position A

**Of the representation.** PCA removes superposition interference and the prompt/format component that every activation carries; what survives is the feature subspace the network's own downstream reads select. The supporting evidence is causal: steering vectors built by projecting the fitted PCA directions *back* into the residual stream move the model's output on 74.3% of trials, so the subspace is at least partly read (Tehenan et al. 2025, [[wiki/concepts/linear-representation-hypothesis.md]]).

## Position B

**Of the pair.** The same relation vectors compose additively at cos 0.993 / 6.0° inside the fitted 3-D subspace and at 0.395 / 66.7° in the raw residual stream they were extracted from; one inverse pair is antipodal projected (0.995) and near-*orthogonal* raw (0.113). Projection is linear, so it cannot create additivity among the projected vectors — it discards a remainder that is large and non-additive, and no measurement says whether that remainder is interference or content. A structure requiring a post-hoc 3-dimensional summary to become visible is a claim about the summary.

## Where it bites

Every "linear / compositional / antipodal geometry" number in the emergent-world-model and steering literatures, most of which report the projected column only. It is T157 one step out: T157 says the *label basis* is a free parameter with more leverage than the decoder class; this says the *subspace* is another one, and unlike the label basis it is fitted from the data rather than guessed, which makes it look objective. Cheap partial resolution: report both columns always, and steer the composed direction (`v_above + v_right`) — if the composite steers as well as its parts, the subspace is the object; if it does not, the remainder was doing work.
