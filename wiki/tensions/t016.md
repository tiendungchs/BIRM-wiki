# T16 — Does the shortest program consistent with the data generalize?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Yes — compression is the provably sufficient bias.** The universal semimeasure dominates every computable hypothesis, convergence is bounded by `K(µ)` alone, and a domain is learnable exactly to the extent it is compressible ([[wiki/concepts/universal-induction.md]], Hutter 2000)

## Position B

**No — policy compression is antagonistic to generalization.** `TrainSol^opt`, the shortest program optimal on the curriculum, by construction discards everything not needed for the *training* situations, including what evaluation needs. Worked case: on `(−0.75, F), (0.15, T)` the shortest fits (`λx: x > 0`) break on `(−0.1, T)` while a nearest-neighbour learner that stored the raw data survives; being ready for future uncertainty costs description length (Chollet 2019)

## Where it bites

Decides whether a minimum-description-length objective is a route to a reasoning model or a trap. It also splits gap G26 in two: compression may fail to *expose* structure (Hutter's version) or fail to *contain* it (Chollet's). **(brainstorm)** Candidate reconciliation: compress the generator over environments (meta-graph), never the policy fitted to one curriculum (instance-graph) — which makes this an argument *for* the two-level factorization. Note the regimes differ — asymptotic passive mixture vs. single finite-horizon program — so both may hold as stated

## Status

`LIVE` — Delétang et al. 2023 supplies A's empirical arm without settling it: models trained on exactly this objective compress modalities they never saw better than the hand-built codecs for those modalities, which is generalization by any reading. It also supplies B's: those same models have no object or 2-D structure for the images they compress best, and once their parameters are charged to the code length they lose to gzip by two orders of magnitude ([[wiki/concepts/prediction-compression-equivalence.md]], gap G35)
