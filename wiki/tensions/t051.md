# T51 — When one item set is embedded in several relational structures, does the map integrate them or hold them in parallel?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Integrate — a conjunctive map.** The wiki's two cleanest multi-dimensional results both find fusion. Park et al. 2021: two 1-D rank orders over 16 people, learned on separate days and never co-presented, produce hippocampal/entorhinal dissimilarity scaling with **2-D Euclidean** distance in the product space, significantly better than with either 1-D order alone, plus a grid-like code over inferred vectors across the plane ([[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]]). Nieh et al. 2021: ~90% of evidence-tuned mouse dorsal-CA1 cells are genuinely **conjunctive** for evidence × position rather than tuned to either alone ([[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]). Qu et al. 2026 replicate the Park result at n = 203 and in a different modality: medial prefrontal cortex codes the **2-D Euclidean** distance between items on an attack × defence rank map significantly better than either 1-D rank (`t₂₀₂` = 2.69), so the two dimensions are combined rather than consulted separately. The theory side assumes fusion throughout — a place cell is `p = f(g̃ ⊙ x̃)`, one code binding structure to content ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T40, T42)

## Position B

**Parallel and anatomically separate.** Zheng et al. 2024 re-analysed Garvert et al. 2017 with link distance, semantic distance and residual distance entered together: both relational structures over the same 12 objects appear in the hippocampal formation in **non-overlapping** clusters (link → bilateral entorhinal, semantic → bilateral hippocampus, semantic posterior to link on the long axis), each effect statistically zero in the other's region of interest (`t₂₂` = −0.25 and 0.54), and residual/perceptual distance nowhere. The authors' conclusion is explicit: the hippocampal formation "does not construct conjunctive maps that integrate similarities across distinct stimulus dimensions"

## Where it bites

Decides the default representation for an entity that participates in more than one structure — one embedding whose metric mixes every known relation, or `k` maps plus a selector. If B, then every wiki architecture that learns a single similarity function per item pair is averaging structures that biology keeps apart, and the missing machinery is a **routing policy over parallel maps** (G12, G40) rather than a better single embedding. If A, the missing machinery is a binding operation and the parallel result is an artefact of the anatomy being asked at fMRI resolution

## Status

`LIVE` — the reconciliation both sides permit is that the **acquisition profile** decides: Park's two dimensions were learned in the same mode, at the same timescale, inside one experiment, and fused; Zheng's differ in mode (implicit vs explicit), timescale (one day vs a lifetime) and consolidation stage, and did not. That predicts a switch, not a fact, and no design has varied the profile while holding content fixed. Two instrument caveats: Zheng's separation cannot distinguish a long-axis **gradient** from two clusters given fMRI spatial autocorrelation (the source says so), and a null in one region of interest at n = 23 is weak evidence of absent code; conversely Park's 2-D-beats-1-D contrast is a model comparison over a *fitted* dissimilarity, which cannot detect a second structure that was never modelled
