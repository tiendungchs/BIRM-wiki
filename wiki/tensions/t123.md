# T123 — Does active inference remove the exploration–exploitation weight, or only fix its value?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The derived `w = 1` nat is tested against a swept weight on the same environments — if the derived value is not optimal, the weight was fixed rather than removed.

## Position A

**Removes it.** The wiki's reading of [[wiki/concepts/expected-free-energy.md]] since the convex-MDP ingest: written over occupancies the objective is `⟨ℓ,μ⟩ + Φ(μ)`, one linear cost plus one convex term with **no mixing coefficient anywhere in the expression** — the balance is the geometry of the objective, and the curiosity bonus `−log ρ_t(s)` is a gradient, not a knob (Milosevic et al. 2026).

## Position B

**Only fixes its value, and the value is a substantive empirical claim.** The ρ-POMDP reduction makes expected free energy *identical* to reward planning plus expected information gain at `w = 1` nat; the authors state outright that EFE "does not eliminate the weight, it derives one". The derived value is reward-near-optimal in 9 / 22 / 32 % of 100 random two-state environments at `H = 1 / 2 / 3`, the success-maximising weight is 20–200× larger on every environment tested, and the advantage vanishes below `γ = 0.99` and reverses at `γ = 0.90` (Cooper & Velasquez 2026).

## Where it bites

Whether the epistemic term needs a calibration argument. Under A it does not, and any implementation may adopt it unexamined; under B the wiki's candidate for the empty objective slot (G30) carries a hyperparameter whose default is defensible only in a named regime — high reward asymmetry (`α ≥ 5`), several observation actions, `H ≥ 2`, near-undiscounted, hidden state preserved by observation. Note the positions are not contradictory in the derivation, only in what follows from it: B accepts A's algebra and denies that a coefficient stops being a coefficient by being derived.

## Status

`LEANING` (to B — it is A's own claim, measured)
