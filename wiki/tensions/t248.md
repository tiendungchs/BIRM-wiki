# T248 — Is the posterior medial structural core an anatomical fact or a tractography artefact?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**A fact.** Six independent graph measures agree on its membership; it appears in all five participants individually; it survives random degradation of the fibre-density matrix; strength and centrality rank consistently across scans (`r² = 0.78` test–retest, `r² = 0.94` left vs right); and 78.9% of DSI fibres land where macaque tracer studies report a connection, with only 6.1% where tracers report absence (Hagmann et al. 2008)

## Position B

**Partly an artefact, and the bias points the same way as the result.** The same authors state that smaller tracts and interhemispheric connections toward *lateral* cortex are likely underrepresented, given resolution limits and anatomical complexity in the centrum semiovale. Missing lateral and cross-hemisphere edges are precisely the edges whose absence inflates the apparent betweenness and k-core resistance of *medial* regions. The tracer comparison bounds false positives (6.1%) and says nothing about false negatives; and the 4%-of-edge-mass interhemispheric figure is itself the quantity most exposed to the bias

## Where it bites

Every wiki page that treats a diffusion-derived connectome as the graph: the skeleton of [[wiki/concepts/dynamic-repertoire.md]], the per-individual weights and delays of [[wiki/entities/virtual-brain-twin.md]], and any architectural argument that reads "put the integrative bottleneck on a small medial core" off the anatomy ([[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] G84). If B, the core is real but its *exclusivity* is overstated, and a lateral parietal/frontal contribution to cross-module routing is being systematically under-credited

## Status

`LIVE` — resolvable only by high-resolution tracer or ex-vivo comparison in the same species; the authors' own proposed test (high-resolution DSI in macaque, validated against CoCoMac at the same parcellation) was unpublished as of the source
