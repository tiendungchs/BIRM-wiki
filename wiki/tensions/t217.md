# T217 — Does conditioning on a self-generated intermediate trace help or hurt?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Per-step reliability is measured as the free variable that sets the sign — the two 2021 ablations differ in direction and differ in steps per problem.

## Position A

**Helps — it is the mechanism, and it was measured in the same year as B.** Cobbe et al. 2021: finetuning GPT-3 6B to emit the final answer *without* intermediate steps drops GSM8K from **20.6% to 5.2%**, a 4× loss from deleting self-generated scratch space at a scale and date comparable to B ([[wiki/entities/gsm8k.md]]). [[wiki/concepts/refinement-loop.md]] names chain-of-thought/latent-space refinement as one of six substrates behind every 2025 top ARC-AGI-2 score, and [[wiki/concepts/external-verification.md]] records the era's headline numbers as a *search over traces* (o1 at 74% greedy → 93% verifier-selected on AIME 2024). Higher reasoning modes buy points; Gemini 3 Deep Think spends 138,000 reasoning tokens on a task the Pro model answers in 96

## Position B

**Hurts, measured on the same model.** Hendrycks et al. 2021: prompting GPT-2 1.5B for a full step-by-step solution before the boxed answer drops MATH accuracy **6.9% → 5.3%**, while *training* on the identical solutions raises it (6.3 → 6.9) and *reading* 99% of a ground-truth one raises it to ~40%. So the trace is valuable as external supervision and as external context, and negative as self-produced context. The authors' mechanism is snowballing: a mistake in the prefix derails the continuation ([[wiki/entities/math-dataset.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether "let it think longer" is an architectural principle or a regime. Under the threshold reading, the intervention has a per-step-reliability precondition `p*` and a system below it is made worse by its own tokens — which predicts that reasoning modes cannot rescue a weak base model, and that the 2021 result reproduces today under temperature or model-size degradation. It also decides whether the rejector is optional: every post-2021 success either raises `p` or escapes the single-trajectory regime by adding a checker

## Status

`LEANING A`, and the row is now a **threshold rather than a disagreement**. Both sides are controlled within-model ablations from 2021 at comparable scale, and they differ in sign: GSM8K (2–8 elementary steps, arithmetic offloaded to a calculator) −4× without the trace, MATH (competition) −1.6 points *with* it. The free variable that reconciles them is per-step reliability `p`, and GSM8K raises `p` by an explicit intervention, which makes the pair the closest thing to a two-point measurement of `p*`. The decisive experiment is still unrun and is now cheap to state: re-run Cobbe's ablation with the calculator annotations disabled and find the crossing
