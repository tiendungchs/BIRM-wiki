# T282 — Does eliminating catastrophic forgetting solve continual learning, or only relocate it?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A second system measures per-component forgetting and router accuracy separately against ground truth — currently CN-DPM is the only such measurement in the wiki.

## Position A

**Solve.** The premise of [[wiki/concepts/continual-learning.md]] and of every family in its table: the damage is B overwriting A, so a method that provably prevents overwriting — freeze prior parameters and allocate new ones — removes the problem, and what remains is a parameter-count bill. Expansion methods are advocated on exactly this ground: "catastrophic forgetting can be eliminated since new information is not overwritten on pre-existing components"

## Position B

**Relocate.** Lee et al. 2020 build that method and dissect it ([[wiki/entities/cn-dpm.md]]): per-component forgetting is 0.0–0.2 points (Split-CIFAR10 88.20 → 88.20, Split-CIFAR100 55.42 → 55.24) and end-to-end accuracy is 45.21% / 20.10%, because the step that decides *which* component applies is right 48.18% of the time at five components and 31.14% at twenty. The authors name expert selection "the main bottleneck". The conserved quantity is not forgetting but **interference between stored things**, which appears at the write when the store is shared and at the read when it is not

## Where it bites

Whether a builder should spend on protection mechanisms or on retrieval mechanisms. If B holds, every row of the continual-learning table that buys non-interference by separation (address-space separation, mask separation, expansion, subspace allocation) has an unbilled retrieval cost that nobody else measures, and the field's headline metric — backward transfer / forgetting — is measuring the half that was solved. The scaling direction is the sharp part: retrieval accuracy *fell* 48% → 31% as the library grew 5 → 20, so the relocated problem gets worse exactly where the method's advantage over replay is largest

## Status

`LIVE` — one system, one measurement, but it is the only measurement of its kind: every other allocate-vs-reuse account in the wiki ([[wiki/entities/coin-model.md]], [[wiki/entities/c-ts-model.md]], [[wiki/entities/hami.md]]) reads retrieval quality off a behavioural fit rather than against ground-truth labels, so nothing else in the wiki can currently be scored on this axis
