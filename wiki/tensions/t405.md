# T405 — Does the habit controller store a cached value, or only how often each action was taken?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Either of two designs is run. (i) **Repetition without reinforcement:** responses are elicited in a context by microstimulation or by instruction, with outcomes equalised across actions, followed by a free-choice test. A is right if no context-specific bias appears. B is right if the bias grows with the number of elicited repetitions and survives devaluation and omission. (ii) **Overtrained omission under matched contingency:** extended training followed by an omission contingency, in a model that also has the source's contingency arbiter. A is right if a model-free cache reproduces the omission-insensitivity without a decaying learning rate. B is right if only a store that never reads the outcome does. The machine version of (i) is free: run an agent whose actions are forced by an external controller for `N` trials under zero reward, then release it and measure the logit bias by `N`.

## Position A

**Cached value (model-free RL).** Habitual control is a model-free learner's common-currency action value, and habitisation is that learner winning the arbitration (Daw, Niv & Dayan 2005). [[wiki/concepts/amortized-inference.md]] builds on this: plan amortization compiles model-based rollouts into the model-free cache (Dyna), and "habitization is the shift". [[wiki/entities/basal-ganglia.md]] places the cache in dorsolateral striatum, trained by the dopaminergic prediction error. Devaluation-insensitivity follows because the cache has no outcome identity.

## Position B

**Value-free action frequency.** Miller, Shenhav & Ludvig 2019 ([[wiki/concepts/value-free-habit.md]]): the habit store is `H(s,a)`, a running average of executed actions with no outcome term. Three arguments:

- **Omission.** Overtrained animals keep pressing when pressing *prevents* reward. A model-free cache should learn that. It is rescued only by an extra assumption, a learning rate that decays in stable environments (Dayan, Kakade & Montague 2000). `H` explains devaluation and omission with one mechanism.
- **Perseveration.** Outcome-independent choice repetition (`β_a > 0` in lagged regressions) appears in rats, monkeys and humans. It is `H` directly, while a value cache cannot produce it.
- **Neural asymmetry.** Goal-directed and habitual control doubly dissociate by lesion. Model-based and model-free signals do not: every candidate model-free region, dopamine included, also carries model-based information.

## Where it bites

**What the fast controller is trained on, and what it is denied.**

| | Under A | Under B |
|---|---|---|
| Teaching signal for the habit store | Reward-prediction error | None. Execution alone |
| Compiling a planner into habit | Value learning from simulated rollouts (Dyna) | Behaviour cloning of the actions the planner actually took |
| What the store can read | Outcome magnitude, in common currency | Nothing about outcomes |
| Habit formation without reward | Impossible | Predicted |

An A-architecture cannot form a habit from unrewarded repetition. A B-architecture cannot use its habit store to rank actions it has never taken. This decides whether [[wiki/concepts/amortized-inference.md]]'s plan amortization writes into a value head or into a policy prior. It also bears on [[wiki/concepts/default-policy-regularization.md]], whose `π₀` is a B-type object with a price attached.

## Status

`LIVE` — B is a simulation paper with qualitative fits. Its one quantitative comparison (the bandit) gives the A agent no perseveration kernel, although standard A formalisations carry one (Lau & Glimcher 2005; Daw et al. 2011). Its interval-vs-ratio result comes from the contingency arbiter and would likely hold for an A-type store under the same arbiter. The omission argument is the strongest point on either side, and neither design above has been run. B also fits its perseveration and overtraining data with `α_H` values five orders of magnitude apart, so B may be two stores rather than one.
