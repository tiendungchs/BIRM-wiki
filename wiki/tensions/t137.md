# T137 — Is the subthalamic nucleus a threshold unit or an exponentiator?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The subthalamic `f–I` relation is measured *in vivo* under synaptic drive, above the <135 Hz range where an exponential and a sigmoid's rising limb are not separable.

## Position A

**A threshold/saturating unit.** Every other model of the circuit assumes an ordinary neural transfer function and treats the nucleus as a fast, diffuse excitatory brake: piecewise-linear in Gurney et al. 2001, sigmoid in Frank 2005, undifferentiated "raise the floor for everything" excitation in Guthrie et al. 2013. On this reading the nucleus contributes a *magnitude* — more conflict, more brake — and nothing depends on the shape of the curve

## Position B

**An exponentiator, exactly.** If the loop implements the multihypothesis sequential probability ratio test, the algorithm *requires* `STN_i = exp(y_i − GP_i)` and `GP_i = Σ − ln Σ`, so the nucleus and the external pallidum jointly compute `ln Σ_j exp(y_j)` — the log-softmax denominator. Exponential fits to pooled published firing-rate data hold up (Hallworth et al. 2003; Wilson et al. 2004), the prediction explains the nucleus's otherwise odd features (non-zero spontaneous rate = `exp(0)`, unusually high attainable rates), and the near-linear pallidal prediction matches Nambu & Llinás 1994 and Kita & Kitai 1991 (Bogacz & Gurney 2007, [[wiki/concepts/evidence-accumulation.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides whether the module is a generic competition or a **normaliser**. Under A the conflict term is a heuristic brake whose functional form is free, and a builder may replace it with any monotone function; under B the form is forced, selection is a threshold on a log-posterior, and the biological argument for putting a softmax denominator in its own subcircuit ([[wiki/entities/basal-ganglia.md]]) stands or falls with the curve. It also decides how much anatomy the selection computation needs: B does not use the indirect pathway at all (T132)

## Status

`LEANING B` on the fits, `LIVE` on their sufficiency — the exponentials are refits of *in vitro* current-injection data collected for other purposes, over a limited rate range (<135 Hz, where an exponential and the rising limb of a sigmoid are hard to separate), with no synaptic input and no behaving animal. The discriminating measurement is the `f–I` relation *in vivo* under synaptic drive, which the source names as its own outstanding test
