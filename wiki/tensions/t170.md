# T170 — When several targets are equally valid, should the predictor represent the modes or should the target space quotient them away?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The two multi-modalities are measured separately: one event under two descriptions versus two events. B's evidence is entirely the first kind and nothing shows the target encoder separates them.

## Position A

**Represent them.** [[wiki/entities/h-jepa.md]] introduces the latent variable `z` for exactly this purpose — the world is multi-modal, a deterministic predictor must average, and averaging is what makes a predictor useless for planning; [[wiki/entities/v-jepa-2.md]] lists "no latent variable, so no multi-modality" as a first-class architectural limitation of its own system, and the pixel-generative branch (Cosmos, [[wiki/entities/adaworld.md]]) buys multi-modality with diffusion.

## Position B

**Quotient them away, in the *target encoder*.** For the same input there are usually several correct answers, and in one-hot token space they are nearly orthogonal (no shared tokens), so a generative model must fit several disjoint high-density regions; a trained text encoder maps them to nearby points, turning the task into one coherent mode in a continuous space. The claim is that most target multi-modality is **surface variability** (word choice, style, paraphrase) rather than genuine alternatives, and that the right place to remove it is the space, not a latent. Measured at matched encoder, data, batch and iterations: 14.8 vs 7.1 CIDEr and 41.0 vs 27.2 top-5 against a token-generative baseline with **twice** the trainable parameters (Chen et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/vl-jepa.md]]).

## Where it bites

Whether a JEPA predictor needs a stochastic latent at all. Under A the multi-modality is handled downstream of the encoder and costs sampling machinery at planning time; under B it is handled *upstream*, by choosing a target encoder whose equivalence classes match the ones the task should ignore — which relocates the decision from the architecture into a component nobody currently derives (VL-JEPA's own ablation (e) shows swapping the Y-Encoder moves read-outs by up to 14 points, so the amount of quotienting is a free parameter).

## Status

`LIVE` — **and the two may be about different multi-modality.** *"The lamp is off"* / *"the room goes dark"* are one event under two descriptions; *"the person went through the left door"* / *"…the right door"* are two events. B's evidence is entirely from the first kind, and nothing in the source shows the Y-Encoder separates them. **(brainstorm)** The testable version: measure the Y-Encoder's distance between paraphrases and between genuine alternatives on the same prompt — if the ratio is near 1 the model is averaging real futures and its own metric cannot see it
