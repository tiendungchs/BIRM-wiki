# T180 — Does verifier-driven test-time search buy reasoning, or only selection?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A verifier-trained policy is measured alone against a matched policy trained without one, at a *fixed* sample budget — every reported comparison varies budget and training regime together.

## Position A

**Selection.** The generator's coverage is already there and the verifier merely picks: o1 on AIME 2024 reads 74% greedy → 83% majority@64 → 93% best-of-1000 with a learned scorer, DeepSeek-Prover-V2 on miniF2F reads ~60% pass@1 → 88.9% pass@8192, and the wiki's own [[wiki/entities/neo-neural-theorizer.md]] goes 0.019–0.038 → ~0.70 at 1024 samples with a *complete* primitive set in hand. On this reading pass@1 measures the policy and everything above it measures the selector, so a reported gain can be bought entirely with inference budget (Raiyan et al. 2026).

## Position B

**Reasoning.** Reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards distils the search back into the policy: the same o1 goes from GPT-4o's 12% to **74% at greedy decoding**, and DeepSeek-R1 elicits self-correction in an open-weights base model from reward signal alone with no supervised traces — so the verifier is training-time scaffolding whose product is a better single forward pass, not a permanent inference-time appendage.

**The oldest instance of the loop supports both halves at once** ([[wiki/entities/alphazero.md]], Silver et al. 2017): search output `π` is distilled into the policy prior `p` continuously, which is Position B's mechanism with an exact verifier; and the trained system's Elo still rises with thinking time *faster* than the alpha-beta engines it beats, which is Position A's claim that the selector keeps paying. The paper reports no raw-network (search-free) playing strength, so it does not adjudicate — but it does show the two effects are not exclusive.

## Where it bites

Whether a brain-inspired reasoner needs a rejector *at run time* or only during learning — i.e. whether [[wiki/concepts/external-verification.md]]'s ladder is an architecture or a curriculum. Also whether the wiki may read a best-of-`k` number as evidence about a representation at all ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T156 is the same question asked of NEO).

## Status

`LIVE`. The decisive experiment is not in the survey: train with a verifier, then measure the resulting policy alone against a matched policy trained without one, at a *fixed* sample budget. Every reported comparison varies the budget and the training regime together.
