# T210 — What does a super-human score on an abstraction benchmark denote?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A generative matrix-reasoning benchmark is scored where the answer set cannot be exploited — the candidates-only control must fall to chance.

## Position A

**Matrix reasoning is solved.** Zhang et al. 2018 measure humans at **84%** on RAVEN's test set, and several groups then report deep networks above that on RAVEN and on its repaired variants I-RAVEN and RAVEN-FAIR; on the original set the trained models here reach 73% (MRNet) and 89% (SCL) `(tentative — all reported second-hand through Odouard & Mitchell 2022)`

## Position B

**The number is a purchase from the generator plus a leak from the answer set.** (i) RAVEN's distractors are the correct answer with one attribute perturbed, so a network trained *only on the eight candidates*, never shown the matrix, exceeds **90%**. (ii) The repairs fix the distractor generator and leave the item generator untouched. (iii) The same two models, on hand-authored items instantiating the *same* concepts they answer correctly i.i.d., fall to **49%/44%** and **62%/68%** — 21–29 points ([[wiki/entities/raven.md]])

## Where it bites

Every leaderboard-derived claim in the wiki, and specifically any comparison of a machine score against a human baseline collected on a *different* item distribution. The two positions are not formally contradictory — both scores are real — but they license opposite readings of the same model, and the discriminating evidence is one-sided: no human baseline was ever collected on the concept variations, so what is established is a large machine drop, not a human/machine gap. Repairs, both cheap and unrun: collect the human baseline on the variation sets, and report per-concept scores rather than a set mean **Root cause, named at the 212-ingest lint pass:** this is not a disagreement about the world but an unspecified reference protocol — see [[wiki/concepts/human-baseline.md]], parameters B1/B3/B5, and note that RAVEN's 84% fails B5 outright (it was never collected on the concept variations).
