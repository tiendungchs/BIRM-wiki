# T25 — Does a decodable internal structure license the claim that the model reasons over it?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Yes — decodability is the evidence.** The emergent-world-model programme reads a successful probe as the model having built and used a representation of the domain: the whole maze reconstructs from a single token's residual stream at layer 2 at 0.83–0.99 per wall, dedicated components (an adjacency head attending to in-context path-length-1 neighbours, a goal-pointing head) operate over it, the tuned lens shows valid-neighbour mass rising from the same layer, and probe accuracy improves in the same grokking-like steps as exact-path accuracy (Ivanitskiy et al. 2023, following Li et al. 2022 on Othello)

## Position B

**No — the same paper breaks both implications.** Representation without competence: the model that reconstructs the maze at ≥0.93 for most cells emits topology-violating paths in 13–16% of rollouts (62% for the smaller model out of distribution) while reaching the target ≈100% of the time. Competence without representation: the corridor-trained model solves its task having never acquired a linearly decodable maze representation. And the whole case is correlational — the authors defer ablation and intervention on the identified heads to future work

## Where it bites

Decides what an "emergent world model" result buys. If decodability neither entails nor is entailed by competence, it scores the *discovery* half of [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] only, and a system can pass it while failing to navigate — which relocates the deficit into search and control (G5, G10, G15) rather than into representation learning

## Status

`LEANING B` — B's evidence is internal to A's own experiment; what would move it back is an intervention result (edit the probe direction, change the path), which exists for Othello and not here. **The named missing evidence now exists.** Nanda et al. 2023 add a probe direction to OthelloGPT's residual stream and its play follows the counterfactual board — 0.10 errors against a 2.72 null, matching gradient-based editing, and holding on boards unreachable by legal play. So for that model A's implication survives its causal test, and the maze case's failure is about *the maze model*, not about the inference. But the same paper opens the successor worry, T158: the verified world model coexists with a cheaper circuit that resolves legal moves earlier in depth, so "the model uses it" is true on average and undetermined per input. A third position also favours neither side: decodability is the wrong quantity. In human hippocampus, context and stimulus *decoding* were statistically unchanged between sessions where participants could and could not perform one-shot inference, while cross-condition generalization and parallelism rose and then collapsed again on error trials — so what licenses the reasoning claim, correlationally, is the representation's **format** (Courellis et al. 2024, [[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]) ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]], [[wiki/entities/maze-solving-transformers.md]])
