# T283 — Can the router that selects among stored structures be a learned discriminative network, or must it be Bayes' rule over generative models?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** CH-HNN's gating accuracy against ground truth is reported, and the two routers run on each other's benchmark.

## Position A

**It must be generative.** Lee et al. 2020 ([[wiki/entities/cn-dpm.md]]) reject a learned gate outright: a router `p(z∣x)` is a classifier over a *growing label set* trained on a non-stationary stream, so it forgets exactly like the learner it routes for and needs the very mechanism it is supposed to provide. Their gate is therefore Bayes' rule over per-expert density models, which is forgetting-free by construction and costs a VAE per expert

## Position B

**It can be discriminative, if its output space does not grow.** Shi et al. 2025 ([[wiki/entities/ch-hnn.md]]) train exactly the rejected object — a feedforward ANN mapping each input to a mask over the learner's units — and it works task-agnostically on 40-episode class-incremental splits. The escape is the objective: supervision is **pairwise** (make `cos(R, R̃)` match `sim(x, x̃)`), so the target is a scalar per sample pair and adding an episode adds no output dimension and no label. Trained incrementally with metaplasticity it *improves* rather than degrades as episodes accumulate

## Where it bites

Whether the read side of a modular memory costs a generative model per module (expensive, and hostage to how discriminable two VAE likelihoods are) or one shared feedforward pass (cheap, amortised, and generalises to episodes never stored — CH-HNN's ImageNet-derived masks transfer to disjoint CIFAR-100 classes). This is the practical form of G37: every schema, expert and core-knowledge library in the wiki needs this router, and the two candidates have opposite cost curves in library size

## Status

`LIVE — not measured head-to-head.` The disagreement is narrower than it looks: both sides avoid the growing-label-set problem, one by making the target generative and one by making it pairwise, and neither has been run on the other's benchmark. The decisive missing number is CH-HNN's **gating accuracy against ground truth**, which it never reports where CN-DPM does (48.18% at five components, 31.14% at twenty) — so B's advantage is currently inferred from end-to-end accuracy and from correlation-matrix agreement
