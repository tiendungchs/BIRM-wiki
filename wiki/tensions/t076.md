# T76 — Does spike-timing potentiation scale up or down with the existing weight?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The barn-owl delay-selection and self-normalisation results are re-derived under the measured multiplicative-down rule, showing whether the modelling assumption is replaceable.

## Position A

**Up — the rule is multiplicative.** `ΔJ_j = ε·J_j·[γ + Σ_f W(t_j^f − t^n)]`: the increment is proportional to the current weight, so the strongest synapses gain the most. This is what makes delay selection *self-reinforcing* rather than diffusive in the barn-owl model, and it is the form assumed wherever the wiki treats spike-timing plasticity as a competitive selection mechanism (Gerstner et al. 1996, [[wiki/concepts/temporal-coding.md]])

## Position B

**Down — potentiation is confined to weak synapses.** Percentage potentiation against log initial excitatory postsynaptic current gives `r = −0.72`, `p = 0.00017`, with significant LTP essentially absent above 500 pA; depression over the same range shows no strength dependence at all (`r = 0.037`, `p = 0.89`). The measured rule is multiplicative-down on the potentiating branch and additive on the depressing one (Bi & Poo 1998)

## Where it bites

Decides whether a spike-timing rule can implement winner-take-all selection by amplitude. Under A, competition ends in a saturated subset and everything else at zero — the mechanism [[wiki/concepts/temporal-coding.md]] uses to tune delays and the mechanism that gives the rule its self-normalization. Under B the winners stop growing first, selection has to be carried entirely by the depressing lobe and by the nonspecific term `γ`, and the stationary weight distribution is heavy-tailed-but-unimodal rather than bimodal — which is the distribution actually measured in cortex (Sammons et al. 2023, [[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]])

## Status

`LEANING B` on the physiology — B is the direct measurement and A is a modelling assumption made two years earlier; but the model's *results* (delay selection, self-normalization) have not been re-derived under B, so what A is doing in that construction is not yet known to be replaceable
