# T95 — Does the control layer store knowledge of its own, or only run procedures over knowledge stored elsewhere?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Representational.** Prefrontal cortex stores memories — goal-oriented event sequences with categories, social rules, event boundaries and grammars — localized by which posterior region supplies their content; lateral prefrontal cortex is argued to derive from motor areas that *store motor programs*, so a gradual evolutionary path to a contentless module does not exist, and a 275-study imaging survey finds consistent regional differentiation between attention, episodic memory, working memory, language and semantic memory (Wood & Grafman 2003, [[wiki/concepts/controller-knowledge-vs-process.md]])

## Position B

**Processing.** The controller is a general algorithm independent of the modality of what it processes: adaptive coding makes prefrontal neurons a temporary, task-specific operating space with little regional specialization, and the supervisory attentional system is a set of priority-setting mechanisms with no knowledge base. Every recording of highly flexible prefrontal tuning across tasks and stimulus domains is evidence for this side

## Where it bites

Whether a machine control module is trained (a schema/plan library with its own parameters, written by the outer loop) or wired (contentless gating over a frozen backbone) — and what a controller ablation should destroy: specific classes of plan, or control in general with contents intact
