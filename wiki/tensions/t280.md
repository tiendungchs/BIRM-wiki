# T280 — Do retrograde amnesia gradients exist?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Gradient flatness is tested against the CLS framework rather than against MMO95's 1995 reading, since CLS is agnostic about hippocampal transience.

## Position A

**Yes — and they are the signature of systems consolidation.** Recent memories are more impaired by hippocampal damage than remote ones, which is the reverse of an ordinary forgetting function and therefore evidence that the cortical copy becomes independent over time; it is the empirical pillar under [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]] and under the whole hippocampus-teaches-cortex channel (McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly 1995)

## Position B

**Often not.** Comprehensive, well-controlled rat fear-conditioning data give gradients that are **flat at every lesion size**, with only a main effect of lesion size on overall memory quality (Sutherland, O'Brien & Lehmann 2008). That is inconsistent with standard consolidation theory *and* with multiple trace theory, which predicts *steeper* gradients for smaller lesions because more traces survive. The CLS authors' own retrospective concedes the evidence is "perhaps less clear now than it seemed to be back in 1995" (O'Reilly et al. 2011)

## Where it bites

Whether the fast store is ever *relieved* of a memory. If gradients are unreliable, the transient-hippocampal-trace reading of consolidation loses its main support and the transformation account — cortex learns its own gist version while the episodic trace stays hippocampal for as long as the memory exists — becomes the default. For a builder that changes the fast store's sizing from a staging buffer to a permanent store, and makes forgetting a *capacity* policy rather than a consolidation by-product ([[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]])

## Status

`LIVE` — and note the two sides may not be in conflict at all: CLS is formally **agnostic** about transience (nothing in the framework requires the hippocampal trace to decay), so a flat gradient falsifies MMO95's 1995 reading rather than CLS itself. The independent modelling route reaches the same place — for a relation the slow learner cannot model, optimal transport is zero and residence is permanent ([[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]], Sun et al. 2023) — which makes the *predicted* gradient shape depend on the predictability of the material, a variable no lesion study manipulates. Rolls 2013's testable corollary is unrun: in a constant restricted environment, no retrograde gradient should be demonstrable at all
