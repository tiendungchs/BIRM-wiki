# T105 — Does model-based *behaviour* license the inference that a model-based *algorithm* is running?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Biological model-based control is classified as emergent or simulating, using the two instruments together — the counterexample stands, but the biological case is undecided.

## Position A

**Yes — that is the point of the protocol.** The two-step task was built to separate the two: after an uncommon-transition reward, a model-free learner repeats the first-stage action and a model-based learner switches, so the stay pattern reads out which algorithm produced the choice; humans and rats show the model-based pattern, and model-based reward-prediction errors appear in ventral striatum (Daw et al. 2011; Miller et al. 2017, via [[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]])

## Position B

**No.** A recurrent network trained *only* by a model-free policy-gradient algorithm, tested with weights frozen, reproduces the model-based stay pattern, the multi-lag regression profile, and reward-prediction errors regressing on a model-based algorithm at `r² = 0.89` against `5.8 × 10⁻⁷` for model-free. Nothing in the trained system simulates a transition model; the behaviour is a property of the emergent inner algorithm, and the outer algorithm that installed it is model-free by construction (Wang et al. 2018, [[wiki/entities/meta-rl-agent.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides what the wiki's cleanest evaluation protocol certifies. Under A the two-step task and the re-goaling test (gap G17) probe mechanism; under B they probe only the *input–output profile of the inner loop*, and an architecture claim needs a second instrument — intervening on the rollout (silencing the simulator, as in the Barron et al. 2020 optogenetic test) rather than scoring the choices. B also weakens the model-free/model-based dichotomy itself: the same recurrent substrate is model-free at the training timescale and model-based at the episode timescale

## Status

`LIVE` — B is a constructive counterexample and stands as such; what is unsettled is whether biological model-based control is of this emergent kind or of the simulating kind, which the two instruments together can distinguish
