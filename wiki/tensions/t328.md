# T328 — Does a per-neuron error need an electrically independent compartment, or is a magnitude residual on coincident events enough?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A credit-assignment model is trained with its error channel restricted to a *scalar gain on the same events the forward pass emits* — shared timing, modulated amplitude — and either matches the segregated-compartment version or fails at a measurable depth.

## Position A

**Independence.** The dendritic-error family requires the apical compartment to hold `δ_l` as a quantity the soma does not report, and the segment literature makes the branch an autonomous detector with its own threshold that no somatic event arbitrates (Sacramento et al. 2018; Ahmad & Hawkins 2016 — [[wiki/concepts/dendritic-computation.md]], [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]]). On this reading the credit channel needs *bandwidth of its own*

## Position B

**Semi-independence suffices.** In vivo, somatic and apical events are near-perfectly coincident in time and differ only in relative magnitude; the entire measured teaching signal — reward, pre-outcome outcome prediction, and the cell-specific sign of the error derivative — lives in that magnitude residual, and abolishing it blocks learning (Francioni et al. 2026, [[wiki/concepts/vectorized-instructive-signals.md]]). The authors present this explicitly as reconciling independent-branch theory with the co-occurrence observed in vivo

## Where it bites

Sets the anatomical price of the wiki's cheapest credit-assignment story. If A, a builder owes a genuinely separate channel per unit — extra cells, extra wiring, or the time-multiplexing everyone is trying to avoid. If B, the channel is a *gain* on the forward event, which costs one scalar per unit per event and no new topology, but bounds the error's information content at whatever a single amplitude can carry. It also bears on T63: B says the segment's autonomy is not what the credit story was buying

## Status

`LIVE` — the in vivo measurement supports B but does not certify it: only ~20% of 466 neurons showed a significant network-predictable residual and the decoders run at 57–63%, so whether the magnitude channel carries enough to instruct a deep network is untested at any depth — and *untestable* in this paradigm as run, since the recorded cells are themselves the output and there is no hidden layer for a propagated error to cross (Kirsanov 2026)
