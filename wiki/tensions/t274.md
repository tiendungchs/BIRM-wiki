# T274 — Is a consciously experienced feature maintained, or recomputed on demand?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Maintained.** The wiki's account of an occupied workspace and of a posterior hot zone both assume that what is experienced is *held*: ignition installs a self-sustaining state in which maintenance and availability are the same thing ([[wiki/concepts/ignition.md]]), and the integrated-information account requires content-specific activity sustained for the whole duration of the experience ([[wiki/entities/integrated-information-theory.md]]). Object identity behaves this way — sustained representation across the trial in posterior iEEG

## Position B

**Recomputed, at least for some dimensions.** Ferrante et al. 2025 (`raw/ferrante-2025-gnwt-iit-adversarial-testing.md`): **face orientation decoded robustly in all three modalities and was sustained in none** — present at onset in posterior cortex, decayed after ~0.5 s, absent in prefrontal cortex — while the stimulus remained on screen and the orientation remained part of the experience. Sustained duration-tracking was found in **25 of 657** posterior electrodes, and only **15% (8/53)** of face-selective electrodes were both selective and sustained

## Where it bites

Whether an architecture may assume that anything it once encoded is still readable. Under A, a single persistent latent serves every downstream consumer and decodability at `t` implies availability at `t+Δ`. Under B, only a privileged subset of dimensions is held and the rest are re-derived from a lower-level sustained code when queried — which needs a *re-derivation path* and a policy for what earns persistence, neither of which any wiki architecture has. It also reframes the sparse maintenance substrate as a design (a small persistence set behind a large transient front end) rather than as a weak measurement

## Status

`LIVE` — the two are not yet separated, because "not decodable at the field scale after 0.5 s" and "not maintained" are the same measurement here; the discriminating experiment is a delayed query on the un-maintained dimension (probe orientation at 1.2 s and score both accuracy and its latency), which would show re-derivation as a cost rather than as an absence
