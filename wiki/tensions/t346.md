# T346 — Is a cortical action→sensory-consequence map stored in the sender's activity, or in the sender→receiver synaptic matrix?

**Status:** `LIVE`
**Level:** `L2`
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]
**Closes when:** One circuit is measured both ways — sender axon tuning *and* bulk (selectivity-bypassing) stimulation of the sender — in the same modality and the same animals, so the two results are not separated by preparation.

## Position A

**In the sender, delivered pre-transformed.** A24b/M2 axons imaged in V1 are *retinotopically biased*: axons in left monocular V1 prefer left turns (0.60 vs 0.40, `p = 4×10⁻¹³`), right V1 right turns (0.64 vs 0.36, `p = 2×10⁻⁵`) — the turns that maximise flow in the field that part of V1 sees; and training in a left–right inverted environment reverses that bias. The motor→sensory coordinate transform is therefore completed upstream, the receiver is handed a prediction in its own basis, and the local comparator only subtracts (Leinweber et al. 2017, [[wiki/entities/a24b-m2-v1-projection.md]])

## Position B

**On the wire, in which receiver cells the projection recruits.** M2 was stimulated **electrically in bulk**, which drives all M2 output in range equally and so bypasses any sender-side selectivity — and auditory-cortical interneurons tuned to the reafferent frequency were still driven more strongly than untuned ones, in aVR-experienced mice and only weakly in aVR-naive ones. The specificity is in the M2→interneuron connectivity, and a week of sensorimotor experience is what aligned it with the tonotopic axis (Schneider et al. 2018, [[wiki/entities/m2-auditory-cortex-filter.md]])

## Where it bites

The module interface, and it is the one choice a machine implementation cannot defer. Under A the forward model is a *computation in the sender* emitting a prediction vector already in the receiver's basis; the wire is a fixed topographic identity map, and learning changes the sender's internal model. Under B the forward model *is the weight matrix on the wire*; the sender may broadcast a content-free state signal (here, "running"), and learning changes the projection's synapses onto the receiver's tuned inhibition. The two make opposite predictions about what silencing the sender's *specificity* — as opposed to its drive — should do, and about where to look for the plasticity ([[wiki/concepts/prediction-error-neurons.md]]'s site-of-plasticity table is written for B and has never been run for A).

Two readings that would dissolve it rather than settle it, both untested: (i) it is a **modality** difference; (ii) it is a difference in *what is predicted* — Position A's case predicts the magnitude and sign of a continuous quantity (flow), Position B's predicts *which channel* on a labelled line will be driven, and an address is exactly the kind of prediction a connectivity matrix can hold while a graded magnitude is not.

## Status

`LIVE` — neither result excludes the other's mechanism in its own preparation. Schneider et al. never recorded M2 axon tuning in auditory cortex, so a sender-side component there is unexcluded (only shown unnecessary for the observed selectivity); Leinweber et al. never ran a selectivity-bypassing stimulation, so a receiver-side component in V1 is unexcluded (only shown unnecessary, since the axons already carry the bias).
