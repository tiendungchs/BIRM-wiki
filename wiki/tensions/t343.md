# T343 — Is the extra-classical surround carried by inter-areal feedback or by within-level lateral connections, and can any dynamical measurement tell?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Spec:** `D258` ([[_brainstorm/birm-spec.md]] §11)  
**Closes when:** Descending input to a sensory area is silenced with its horizontal connections intact (or the converse) in the same preparation, and the residual end-stopping / surround suppression is measured with the stimulus-driven drive held fixed. Position A requires suppression to collapse when feedback alone is cut; Position B requires a substantial fraction to survive.

## Position A

**Feedback.** The suppression is a top-down prediction subtracted inside the lower area. In the model, cutting level-2 → level-1 feedback drops end-stopped error units from 28/32 to 5/32 — an 82% reduction, with the survivors explained by field/stimulus orientation mismatch (Rao & Ballard 1999, [[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]]). The physiological support the paper assembles: inactivating higher visual areas disinhibits surround responses in lower areas; removing cat area 17/18 feedback to the lateral geniculate nucleus strongly reduces end-inhibition; and extra-classical effects in alert monkey V1 layer 2/3 emerge only 80–100 ms after stimulus onset, a latency a feedforward route does not need. The cell-class version is [[wiki/concepts/prediction-error-neurons.md]]'s positive-error circuit: bottom-up excitation against top-down-driven inhibition.

## Position B

**Lateral, and the formalism cannot object.** In the linear case the same residual descent is algebraically rewritable as recurrent lateral inhibition among the units of one level, `ṙ ∝ UᵀI − Wr + …` with `W = UᵀU` (Rao & Ballard 1999, equation 8) — so the feedback term is a *choice of implementation* for a quantity the level can compute from its own horizontal connectivity. Repeated subtraction of neighbours' activity additionally approximates divisive normalization ([[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]), which reproduces extra-classical effects on its own, and V1 suppression from outside the classical receptive field is separately attributed to intra-V1 horizontal connections with no simple arithmetic rule ([[wiki/entities/early-visual-system.md]]). In mouse V1 the inhibition is delivered by a local somatostatin-expressing interneuron population, whose *driver* is what the two positions disagree about.

## Where it bites

`L2`: whether the message that produces a context effect crosses a level boundary at all. It decides what a residual architecture must wire — a descending channel per level pair, or a within-level recurrent matrix `W = UᵀU` that a level can build from its own basis with nothing transported. It also sets what an ablation means: cutting feedback in a model that has both routes removes an effect that the tissue may reconstitute laterally, so the 82% figure is an upper bound on feedback's causal share, not a measurement of it. And it is a **reader-side denial**: with the objective fixed and the dynamics fully observed, an observer still cannot recover which route carried the signal — the complement of [[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]], where the representation is observed and the objective is unrecoverable.

## Status

`LIVE`, and unusually well-posed because the ambiguity is proved rather than suspected: the two wirings are the *same equation*, so no amount of dynamical or response-level data separates them and only a route-selective intervention can. The exact equivalence holds in the linear case; whether it degrades enough under a nonlinearity `f` to become measurable is untested and is the cheapest thing to check in simulation. Bears on [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] **T259** — a level that can substitute its own lateral connections for the level above's feedback is not straightforwardly a link in a chain of command — and on **T278**, whose feedforward null is not threatened by suppression that turns out to be intra-areal.
