# T21 — Does a structured model's win over deep baselines measure the prior, or the state space it was handed?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A model-side ablation is run: the authored agent model kept while the perceptual front-end is degraded to the baselines' input, and vice versa — the two matched-input experiments currently give opposite answers.

## Position A

**The prior.** Structured Bayesian models embed conceptual knowledge of goal-directed agents, so they draw the right inferences from a handful of habituation trials and generalize human-like where minimal-bias learners do not — 96.0–99.7% vs. at-or-below chance on the Baby Intuitions tasks needing cross-trial binding, and the same pattern on the related AGENT benchmark, .96 vs .90 overall and .94 vs .65 under leave-one-type-out (Zhi-Xuan et al. 2022, [[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]; Shu et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/agent-benchmark.md]])

## Position B

**The state space.** The comparison is not matched on inputs: the structured model conditions on noise-free symbolic states with hand-written PDDL dynamics, hand-tuned preprocessing (barriers vanish instantly, trajectories smoothed to diagonals) and three logistic weights fitted on a purpose-built synthetic set, while the baselines work from trajectories or video. The paper concedes only "more upfront conceptual and engineering work" — pricing the difference as effort rather than as information supplied. **The matched-input control now exists and mostly refutes this side**: IRENE reads the same noise-free symbolic JSON, and scores 48.5 on Preference (vs 99.7) and 74.9 on Multi-Agent (vs 99.2) — so the state space buys some of Multi-Agent and none of Preference (Bortoletto et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/irene.md]]). **The perception control answers the opposite way, and it is now primary rather than second-hand.** On AGENT the two baselines are re-run behind a trained Mask R-CNN + ResNet-34 derenderer instead of ground-truth 3-D state, at a mean bounding-box IoU of 0.07: inverse planning falls **.96 → .65** and the neural model **.90 → .51 = chance**. Two-thirds of the structured model's margin over chance is bought by being handed the state, and the ordering it preserves is between two models that are both nearly unusable (Shu et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/agent-benchmark.md]])

## Where it bites

Every future claim that a structured prior beats a learned one. The wiki's rule (G17, closing note of [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]) is that an architecture assuming away the latent variable is not evidence about it — and here the latent variables the baselines fail on (persistent per-agent state) are genuinely hidden from both, while the ones the structured model was handed (object identity, dynamics) are hidden from only one

## Status

`LIVE` — **"matched inputs" names two experiments with opposite answers.** Matched *symbols* (IRENE on BIB) transfer little of the advantage, favouring A; matched *pixels* (the AGENT derenderer) remove most of it, favouring B. Both can be true: the authored agent model is what the neural nets lack, **and** the authored perceptual front-end is what the whole comparison rests on. The experiment nobody has run is a model whose agent hypothesis feeds *back* into the state estimate, so that the two are inferred jointly instead of a fixed reasoner sitting behind a fixed perception module
