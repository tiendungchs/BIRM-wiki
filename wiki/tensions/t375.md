# T375 — Should the consolidated model mirror the environment's statistics, or be deliberately biased away from them?

**Status:** `LIVE`
**Level:** `L2`
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]
**Closes when:** One system is run with both selection rules and the *consolidated* model is read out: measure the implied distribution over states/relations in the slow learner and ask whether it converges on the environment's sampling distribution (A) or on a significance-weighted one (B), with behavioural cost measured on both an unbiased test set and a rare-critical-event test set.

## Position A

**Mirror, after correcting the sampling bias.** Replay's measured selectivity all points at *de-biasing*: it upsamples the regions where least time was spent (Grosmark et al. 2021), prefers remote to imminent trajectories (Gillespie et al. 2021), and **actively suppresses salient-but-idiosyncratic stimuli** from ripples, with the suppression inducible by implanting the representation (Terada et al. 2022; Liao et al. 2024, [[wiki/entities/inhibitory-replay-filter.md]]). The behaviour policy oversamples reward sites and undersamples the rest, so an unfiltered channel would hand the slow learner the policy's occupancy distribution instead of the environment's structure; the filter exists to remove that. The normative version: consolidating everything overfits, and the selection statistic that works is across-episode recurrence, which is a property of the world and not of the agent ([[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]], [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]])

## Position B

**Deliberately bias, toward what matters to the agent.** Kumaran, Hassabis & McClelland 2016 (`raw/kumaran-2016-complementary-learning-systems-updated.md`) propose the fast store's job includes **circumventing the general statistics of the environment**: significant-but-rare experiences (surprising, novel, high reward magnitude of either sign, high information value) are preferentially stabilized *and* preferentially replayed, so a single frightening dog updates the cortical model against a lifetime of friendly ones. Neuromodulatory projections to the hippocampus and molecular stabilization mechanisms let an episode be **retrospectively** reweighted by events occurring after it, which makes replay probability an agent-set variable. The machine arm is measured: biasing the replay buffer toward high reward-prediction-error transitions yields further gains over uniform in a deep Q-network ([[wiki/concepts/replay-prioritisation.md]]), and the rational-memory tradition holds that memory systems are optimized to an organism's goals rather than mirroring the world

## Where it bites

What a builder's consolidation objective *is*. Under A the target is an unbiased estimate of environment structure and every significance signal is a confound to be filtered out; under B the target is an importance-weighted model and the filter in A is discarding exactly the tail the agent needs. The two prescribe opposite buffer rules — inverse-visitation plus cross-episode recurrence (A) against reward/novelty/surprise weighting (B) — and they are the two rules that have actually been run against each other in a continual-learning agent, where **A's rule won and both of B's lost** ([[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]]). It also decides whether the wiki's consolidation stopping rules are complete: `T81`'s recurrence gate and predictability gate are both A-flavoured and neither has a term for an event that will occur once and must never be forgotten

## Status

`LIVE`. Not a restatement of `T30` (planning vs transferable map): both positions here accept that replay is for building the slow learner's model, and disagree about **what distribution that model should approximate**. Three things keep it open.

1. **B's own source names the failure mode and does not bound it.** Post-traumatic stress disorder is offered as the reweighting running away — one aversive experience becoming a dominant representation through repeated reactivation — and the review's first Outstanding Question is literally "under what conditions does hippocampal reweighting result in a *biased* neocortical model of environmental structure?". So B has no regulator, where A's positions come with stopping rules attached.
2. **A's evidence is about salience, B's claim is about significance, and nobody has separated them.** Terada's suppressed cue is salient and *predicts nothing*; B's frightening dog is salient and *predicts a great deal*. A filter keyed to across-episode recurrence suppresses both, which is the case where the two positions make opposite predictions and the case no experiment runs.
3. **They may be composable on different variables.** Weight the *prior* over which relations to model by significance (B) and the *number of reactivations* per relation by predictability (A) — a two-term budget, with B choosing what enters and A choosing how long it stays **(brainstorm)**. Nothing in the wiki runs both terms, and the composition would immediately predict that a rare-but-modellable event consolidates fastest of all, which is testable in the linear model of [[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]] without new machinery.
