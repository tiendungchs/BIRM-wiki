# T264 — Is default-mode anticorrelation a neural phenomenon or a product of global signal regression?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Anticorrelation magnitude is reported with *and* without global signal regression on the same data and regions — no source currently does.

## Position A

**Substantially manufactured.** Global-signal regression mean-centres the correlation distribution and therefore *forces* negative values to exist; the anchor review states the caveat about its own anticorrelation result and leaves the magnitude without GSR unknown (Buckner et al. 2008); the connectome-methods review lists GSR among rejected practices because it removes task-related neural signal and "spuriously inflates anticorrelations" (Bijsterbosch et al. 2021, [[wiki/concepts/node-definition-problem.md]]); and Gao et al. 2013's own check finds all values shift positive without it, with several rest-state core↔salience correlations losing their negative sign

## Position B

**Neural, and GSR is what makes it detectable.** The global signal is a composite of neural and non-neural sources; removal increases anticorrelations by virtue of mean-centering rather than by fabricating structure, and effects that are not reproducible without GSR "may not be analysis artifacts in as much as they reflect neural features whose detection benefits from removal of shared trends" — with a TMS meta-analysis finding subgenual-ACC connectivity↔treatment-response associations carried specifically by patients with large global-signal fluctuations. Recommended practice: report both, especially for anticorrelation research (Tripathi et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/default-mode-network.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether `G90`'s two-mode competition has any measured basis. The internal/external anticorrelation is the wiki's main evidence that the brain runs two mutually suppressing modes at all; if the negative sign is a centering artefact, what remains is a graded reduction in coupling, and an architecture needs a gain rather than an opponent process. Note the positions are not symmetric: side B does not claim GSR is unbiased, only that the bias is a shift whose direction is known, which makes the dispute settleable by reporting both — and almost no source in this wave does

## Status

`LIVE` — no source here reports an anticorrelation magnitude with *and* without GSR on the same data with the same regions; the closest is Gao et al. 2013's statement that state *changes* survive while absolute signs do not, which supports side B for the dynamics and side A for the resting sign
