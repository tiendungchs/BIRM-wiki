# T128 — Are all precisions inferred, or are some structural constants?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The impenetrable-prior claim is tested against a scientific source, and the formalism is given a place to put a frozen gate — or shown not to need one.

## Position A

Precisions are a represented quantity optimised by the same free-energy descent as states and parameters, carried by synaptic gain — attention, salience and learning rate are all one inferred number (Friston 2009, [[wiki/concepts/precision-weighting.md]])

## Position B

At least one prior is **cognitively impenetrable**: in the hollow-mask illusion a correct, confidently held, reportable belief that the mask is concave never revises the percept, which is claimed to be evidence for a prior in older visual circuitry that higher levels cannot write to (`talk-nd-free-energy-principle`, **(tentative)** — an explainer, no experiment)

## Where it bites

Decides whether an architecture may treat every prior's precision as a learnable parameter, or must partition the hierarchy into inferred and frozen gains — and whether "top-down wins" means "the agent's current best belief wins" or "a fixed prior wins"

`LIVE` — B's source is non-scientific and the illusion has scientific literature the wiki has not ingested; the tension is well-posed regardless, since A's formalism has no place to put a frozen gate
