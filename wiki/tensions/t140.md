# T140 — Is delay discounting one non-exponential valuation or two competing exponential systems?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Two *delayed* options are presented, the condition where a purely-immediate system would be silent and the one-system and two-system accounts separate most cleanly.

## Position A

**Two systems.** Hyperbolic-*like* discounting is produced by a steep-`β` system (proposed as ventral striatum, medial prefrontal and posterior cingulate cortex, valuing immediate rewards) working against a patient-`δ` system in lateral prefrontal and posterior parietal cortex; the evidence is that the three regions respond more when an immediate option is on the screen (McClure et al. 2004, via Kable & Glimcher 2007). Behaviourally the two-exponential sum fits choices **as well as** the hyperbola, so choices alone cannot separate the accounts

## Position B

**One system, one non-exponential kernel.** The same three regions' activity varies when only the *delayed* option changes — which the impulsive-system reading forbids — and the discount rate refitted to their time courses is neither steeper than the person's behaviour (`β` requires it: difference median −0.0081, `P = 0.001`) nor shallower (`δ` requires it: +0.0024, `P < 0.0001`), while against behaviour itself the difference is centred on zero (median −0.0009, `P = 0.67`) (Kable & Glimcher 2007, [[wiki/concepts/subjective-value.md]])

## Where it bites

Decides whether a builder needs *two* value heads with different horizons and an arbitrator between them — the natural machine reading of β–δ, and a close relative of the model-based/model-free arbitration of T135 — or one head emitting an already-discounted scalar. Under B the wiki's arbitration machinery is not needed for intertemporal choice at all, and the observed preference reversals must come from the kernel's shape rather than from a contest

## Status

`LEANING B` — B's test is strictly stronger (a mean-offset test, not a contrast), it reproduces in ROIs selected without reference to the discount rate, and it does not challenge A's original finding, only A's interpretation of it. Residual: B never presented two *delayed* options, which is the condition where a purely-immediate system would be silent and the accounts separate most cleanly
