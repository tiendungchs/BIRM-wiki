# T4 — Do abstract structural codes exist in the brain?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A non-spatial abstract code is shown periodic *and* object-invariant *and* relational in one experiment, or the periodic claim is confined to domains with a metric and the general claim retreats to "maps outside space".

## Position A

Grid-like periodic codes appear during abstract categorization in humans; periodic encoding may be a general hallmark of knowledge organization Epstein et al. 2017 adds detail rather than a second report: the same morphed-stimulus result also shows the sixfold signal in ventromedial prefrontal cortex, with its strength tracking task performance

## Position B

The same review states we *lack direct evidence* for codes that are invariant to objects and code relational information, and flags the grid-like claim as requiring much further work. And the nearest independent non-spatial map is *not* periodic — a social power × affiliation space is coded as hippocampal vector *angle* plus posterior-cingulate *magnitude* — so the general claim that survives is "maps outside space", not "grids outside space" ([[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]])

## Where it bites

The wiki's `g` (structural code) has one contested biological anchor; if it fails, the factorization must be justified computationally alone
