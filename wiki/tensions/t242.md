# T242 — Which carrier frequency's power do resting BOLD fluctuations track — and can the question be settled at all?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The question is accepted as unsettleable with fMRI: the haemodynamic transform removes every candidate carrier before fitting, so arbitration needs a non-haemodynamic instrument.

## Position A

**Gamma, positively.** Intracranial and intracortical recordings in monkeys and humans find local BOLD increases accompanied by increases in gamma-band (40–100 Hz) local-field power and in firing rate, with slow (<0.1 Hz, 1/f) envelope fluctuations coherent between distant electrodes and selective for specific resting networks (Logothetis; Leopold; Shmuel & Leopold; Nir et al., in Cabral, Kringelbach & Deco 2014). The metabolic argument supports it: high-frequency oscillations are the expensive ones, and BOLD measures metabolic demand. This is the bridge [[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]] uses to connect a <0.1 Hz human correlation to a 1–100 Hz rodent result.

## Position B

**Alpha/beta, negatively.** Nearly every non-invasive study — simultaneous EEG–fMRI and MEG — finds BOLD *inversely* correlated with alpha and beta (8–32 Hz) power, and recovers RSN spatial maps, including the DMN, from **beta-band amplitude envelopes** via ICA on source-projected MEG (Nikouline; Mantini; Brookes; Hipp; de Pasquale, same review). Frequency-specific hubs differ by band (medial temporal at 4–6 Hz, lateral parietal at 8–23 Hz, sensorimotor at 32–45 Hz), so there is no single carrier to find. A reconciliation exists — gamma and alpha/beta power are themselves anticorrelated and contribute independently to BOLD variance (Scheeringa et al.) — but it dissolves the bridge rather than repairing it, since the sign of the BOLD–power relation then depends on which band dominates locally.

## Where it bites

Every cross-scale claim that leans on a human functional connectome to support an electrophysiological mechanism ([[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]]'s timescale-gap row), and the six whole-brain models on [[wiki/concepts/metastability.md]], three of which posit a gamma carrier and three of which posit no oscillation at all yet fit the same BOLD data.

## Status

`LIVE`, and **structurally so**: the arbitration cannot be done with fMRI. The haemodynamic transform is measured to be a 0.35 Hz low-pass of population rate with a 1.6 s lag (`r = 0.9` against the full Balloon–Windkessel model), so every candidate carrier is removed from the observable before it is fitted; the review's own statement is that settling it requires simultaneous whole-brain electrophysiology and fMRI, which does not exist.
