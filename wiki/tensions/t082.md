# T82 — Is permanent hippocampal dependence an optimal refusal or a transport failure?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Slow-oscillation stimulation is applied with material predictability varied: rescue for high-predictability material only would establish the channel-failure account.

## Position A

**Refusal.** Consolidation is regulated by predictability: for a relation the slow learner cannot model, the optimal transfer is zero, so the memory stays in the fast store forever and the absence of cortical involvement is the *correct* outcome of a working system (Sun et al. 2023, [[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]])

## Position B

**Failure.** The same observable — persistent hippocampal activation at delayed retrieval plus weak hippocampal–prefrontal coupling — is produced by a degraded channel: in older adults it tracks reduced prefrontal slow-wave activity, itself mediated by medial prefrontal grey-matter atrophy, with hippocampal volume and every non-SWA sleep variable predicting nothing (Mander et al. 2013, [[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]])

## Where it bites

Whether the wiki's central consolidation datum — that many memories never leave the fast store — is evidence *for* a regulator or evidence of a lossy pipe. It also decides what a machine system should do when it observes incomplete transfer: under A, nothing (the stopping rule worked); under B, repair the channel. Since predictability is not measurable in any published paradigm, the two are currently confounded in the retrograde-amnesia and ageing literatures both theories postdict

## Status

`LIVE` — separable in principle by manipulating the channel while holding the material fixed: slow-oscillation stimulation should rescue retention for high-predictability material only if B is the operative cause, and should leave low-predictability material untouched under A. Not run. Note the two are not exclusive — a rationed channel and a throttled one compose, and B's mechanism sets the *ceiling* on the budget A rations
