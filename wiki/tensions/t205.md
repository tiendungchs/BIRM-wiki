# T205 — Does ARC-AGI-1 measure abstraction across its whole task set, or only across a residue?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L4`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** The 49 private tasks that fell to 2020 brute force are published and every subsequent method re-scored on the complement.

## Position A

**Roughly half of it does not.** An analysis of all 2020 competition submissions found **49% of the private evaluation set was solved by at least one entry, all of them brute-force DSL search** — before LLMs, at low compute. The report's own verdict is that this fraction "does not carry a useful signal towards AGI", i.e. those tasks have low developer-aware generalization difficulty and are reachable by enumeration over an authored vocabulary ([[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]])

## Position B

**The benchmark's design claims every task requires recombining core-knowledge priors in a way that cannot be prepared for in advance**, and its resistance is evidence the claim holds: unbeaten for five years across a ~50,000× pretraining scale-up, with an $600,000 grand prize unclaimed and a ~30-point gap to the human 97–98%

## Where it bites

Every ARC-AGI-1 score below ~50% reported anywhere in the wiki, including the wiki's own solver pages ([[wiki/entities/arc-vsa-solver.md]] 10.8% on Eval, [[wiki/entities/ilp-arc-synthesizer.md]] 5 hand-picked tasks): if the brute-forceable half is what a sub-50% method is covering, the score prices vocabulary coverage rather than abstraction, and two methods at equal score need not be equally far along. Also bears on the wiki's use of ARC as its G17 instrument — a benchmark can satisfy the whole checklist in [[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]] and still be half-composed of `GD ≈ 0` items, because nothing in the checklist audits *per-task* difficulty

## Status

`LEANING` toward A, and the source concedes it: ARC-AGI-2 was built specifically to fix this, along with the small-private-set and inconsistent-difficulty problems. Resolvable directly — publish which 49 of the 100 private tasks fell to 2020 brute force and re-score every subsequent method on the complement
