# T47 — Are concurrent structural codes independent frames or one code read out in many places?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Independent.** Chen et al. 2022 hold that grid, place and head-direction cells in primary somatosensory and secondary visual cortex arise from a generalized path-integration principle running on each area's *own* self-motion signal — optic flow, proprioception, head movement — and are "complementary yet functionally independent" of the entorhinal integrator; the columnar version makes this thousands of object-anchored frames voting ([[wiki/concepts/distributed-reference-frames.md]])

## Position B

**Registered, or singular.** The only case where two regions' frame orientations have been estimated in one task finds them *aligned*: entorhinal and ventromedial-prefrontal hexagonal codes over the same conceptual bird space share a grid angle (`t21 = 2.18`), each stable across sessions more than a week apart (Constantinescu et al. 2016). Alignment is equally consistent with one entorhinal code propagated to prefrontal cortex, which would delete every fMRI row from the replication evidence

## Where it bites

Decides whether a model needs an arbitration mechanism over many `g` modules (G43) or a single `g` with distributed readouts — and if frames are merely *registered*, arbitration collapses from coordinate conversion to weighting

## Status

`LIVE` — the evidence on each side uses a different instrument (single units for independence, BOLD for alignment) and neither can address the other's claim. Discriminating test: a conflict design where conceptual and spatial axes are rotated relative to one another, asking whether the two angles dissociate
