# T295 — Do jumping spiders belong in the convergence census at all — and does the wiki's only source for them survive inspection?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A refereed anatomical source is obtained: the behavioural datapoint stands, the structural mushroom-body claim should be dropped until one exists.

## Position A

**Yes, they are a census member.** Both unrefereed sources assert it: `raw/chat-nd-convergent-spatial-memory.md` lists salticid mushroom bodies as an independent origin of the expansion→compression motif, and `raw/ihadnoclue-nd-jumping-spiders-spatial-cognition.md` goes further — "spider place cells" recorded in mushroom bodies, stable under visual-cue alteration, functionally analogous to O'Keefe/Moser cells. The *behavioural* half is independently solid: salticid detour routes that lead initially away from out-of-sight prey are a refereed finding (Tarsitano & Jackson; Cross & Jackson)

## Position B

**The structure attribution is wrong, which voids the row as evidence.** Spiders are generally described as **lacking mushroom bodies** — the structure is a mandibulate/insect feature, and the candidate arachnid analogue is the **arcuate body**. The second source is a popular aggregator whose named attributions (a 2021 UMass detour study, a 2022 Cornell place-cell recording, a Dennett quote) could not be traced, and which also asserts mushroom bodies arose "400 Mya independent from the vertebrate hippocampus" — a claim about a structure it has already misassigned

## Where it bites

Bears directly on [[wiki/concepts/convergent-circuit-motifs.md]]'s method, not just on one row. That page's stated failure mode is that convergence only counts when independence is established; this is the adjacent failure — a row can also fail because the *structure* is misidentified, which is cheaper to check and was not checked. The row also happens to be the census's most valuable one if true: a <5×10⁵-neuron planner varies the wiring/metabolic constraint by 10⁵ while holding the capability, which is the only proposed control for that page's substrate confound

## Status

`LEANING` — toward B on the anatomy (the arachnid literature is not ambiguous about mushroom-body absence) and toward A on the behaviour (detour planning in salticids is refereed and independent of these sources). The two halves are separable and should be recorded separately: **keep the behavioural datapoint, drop the structural claim until a refereed arachnid-neuroanatomy source is ingested.** Resolves on one reading of a primary source; flagged as a priority because the wiki currently has *two* mutually-citing unrefereed sources agreeing with each other, which is the failure mode that looks most like corroboration
