# T314 — Should an anti-collapse provision protect the space the loss sees, or the space the read-out sees?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A dimension-contrastive or distribution-matching method is run with its provision applied through a deliberately low-rank discardable head, and either matches the wide-projector version (B) or loses (A) — with both spectra reported, at the embedding *and* at the representation.

## Position A

**The loss's own space is the space to keep full-rank.** Everything the wiki carries on gap G34 measures and enforces non-collapse where the objective is applied: VICReg's variance hinge and covariance penalty act on the embedding, Barlow Twins' cross-correlation matrix is the embedding's, LeJEPA's Epps–Pulley test is on the embedding's random projections, and SimSiam's cheapest collapse monitor is the per-channel std of `z/‖z‖` — `z` being the projector output ([[wiki/concepts/representational-collapse.md]]). Barlow Twins measures **monotone gains in embedding width up to 16384** above a fixed 2048-d representation, with the entropy-estimator reading as its mechanism: more embedding dimensions, better representation ([[wiki/entities/barlow-twins.md]]).

## Position B

**The embedding is collapsed in every one of those runs, and the projector's measured job is to host the collapse.** A trained SimCLR's 128-d embedding covariance has a block of singular values at zero (Jing et al. 2022, [[wiki/entities/directclr.md]]), and the same paper's ablation isolates what the projector contributes: a projector with its singular values **pinned at 1** — full-rank, discarding nothing — scores 52.2 against 51.5 for having no projector at all, while a **fixed low-rank** one scores 62.3 and a fixed low-rank *diagonal* one (DirectCLR, `d₀ = 360` of 2048) scores 62.7, above the trainable linear projector's 61.1. The representation-space spectrum tells the same story from the other side: full-rank whenever a projector is present, collapsed when it is absent. So the quantity that buys downstream performance is the projector's capacity to **throw dimensions away**, and the space that must stay full-rank is the backbone output, which no loss touches.

## Where it bites

Three concrete decisions. (i) **Where a collapse monitor is instrumented.** Both of the wiki's label-free monitors — the rescaled LeJEPA loss and `(L_align, L_uniform)` — are computed on embeddings, i.e. on the space B says is *supposed* to be rank-deficient, which would make a monitor reading of "collapsing" uninformative and a reading of "healthy" possibly a warning. (ii) **Projector width, and the family disagrees by a factor of ~20** — 8192–16384 (Barlow Twins, VICReg) against 360 (DirectCLR) — with no shared account of why either helps. (iii) **The design pattern for any new objective**, including a structural-code objective for `g` (G30): B says a degenerate solution can be *hosted* in a discardable head instead of being priced by a term, which is a cheaper move than every provision in the taxonomy and is untested outside vision contrastive learning. Home page for this row: [[wiki/concepts/representational-collapse.md]].

## Status

`LIVE` — the two positions are measured on the same benchmark and the same backbone, and they are not obviously reconcilable: A's mechanism (a wider embedding estimates the entropy term better) and B's mechanism (a narrow embedding absorbs an unavoidable rank loss) make opposite predictions about projector width and both have ImageNet numbers behind them. Three reasons not to score it yet. **The two are measured on different loss families** — A's width sweep is dimension-contrastive, B's rank sweep is sample-contrastive, and no paper sweeps rank within the other's family, which is exactly the `Closes when`. **B's best configuration is one its own theory does not cover**: the 2-layer *nonlinear* projector wins the paper outright (66.5 vs 62.7), and DirectCLR is admitted to inherit that unexplained mechanism through the backbone's last block and its residual connection — so "the projector is a sequestration device" is established for linear projectors only. **And A has never measured the thing B measures**: no width-sweep in the wiki reports an embedding spectrum, so it is possible that the wide projectors are also rank-deficient and the width is buying something other than rank.
