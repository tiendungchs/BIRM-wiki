# T78 — Is failure to scale evidence against a learning rule's biological plausibility?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Position A

**Yes — behavioural realism is a plausibility criterion.** Humans learn complex tasks unrelated to their evolution, so the brain has a powerful general-purpose learning algorithm; a candidate rule that cannot reach human-level competence on hard tasks is thereby disqualified, and demonstrating good performance at least establishes that *the rule is not the limiting factor*. Adding neuromodulation or evolutionary priors to a model whose learning rule is crippled teaches nothing about the brain (Bartunov et al. 2018, stating the criterion while producing the negative result it condemns)

## Position B

**No — machine-learning benchmark performance is a red herring.** A rule stripped of the varieties of plasticity, neuromodulation, evolutionary priors and architecture the brain actually has may fail for any of those reasons rather than because the rule is wrong; poor ImageNet performance is then a statement about the *missing phenomena*, not about the algorithm. The same paper concedes this reading, and the confounds are real: hyperparameters, initialization and architecture have had a decade of tuning for backpropagation and none for target propagation

## Where it bites

Whether the wiki should read the ImageNet result on [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]] as *falsifying* the target-propagation family or merely as *not yet supporting* it. Under A the programme should be abandoned or redirected to the jobs backpropagation cannot do (T7); under B the negative result is a call for the tuning effort backpropagation already received. It also sets what a plausibility argument must contain: physiological realism alone, or physiological plus behavioural

## Status

`LIVE` — the discriminating experiment is stated but unrun: give one biologically motivated rule the same architecture-and-hyperparameter search effort convolutional backpropagation received, and see whether the gap is search or substance
