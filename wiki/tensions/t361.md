# T361 — Is the lateral habenula a disappointment channel or a general aversion channel?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Lateral-habenula silencing (or recording of projection-identified habenular cells) is reported in one animal across four trial types at matched arousal — reward delivered, reward omitted, aversive event delivered, aversive event omitted — with the dopamine or serotonin response measured downstream in each. A is right if the habenular contribution is confined to the omission cells; B is right if it is present in all bad-outcome cells. A subnucleus split (LHbM versus LHbL) would make both right and is the review's own stated hypothesis.

## Position A

**The negative half of the reward prediction error, specifically.** Hu, Cui & Yang 2020 ([[wiki/entities/lateral-habenula.md]]): lateral-habenula lesion in mice **abolishes** the negative prediction error in ventral tegmental dopamine cells caused by **reward omission** and **leaves intact** the one caused by aversive stimuli. Its proposed input source fits: entopeduncular cells projecting to the habenula are inhibited by reward-predicting cues and excited by reward omission, which is a reward-referenced quantity and not a nociceptive one. On this reading the structure computes `[−δ]⁺` — the rectified negative half of one signed value error — and pain, malaise and threat reach the modulatory nuclei by other routes.

## Position B

**A general aversive-state hub.** The same review: foot shock, tail shock, restraint, lithium-chloride illness, maternal deprivation and social defeat all activate the habenula; 78% of foot-shock-responsive cells are excited; noxious stimuli excite it and morphine blocks that; more than a dozen afferents carry reward omission, pain, thirst, sound, touch, light and social defeat into the same nucleus, and driving almost any of them produces real-time place aversion. Monkey recordings show excitation to punishment and to cues predicting punishment, not only to omission. On this reading the structure is the convergence point at which distinct kinds of bad news become one variable.

**A qualification on A, from A's own primary source.** Matsumoto & Hikosaka 2007 ([[wiki/entities/lateral-habenula.md]]) run a purely appetitive task — reward versus no reward, no aversive event anywhere — and find the habenular reward on–off response declining as the prediction is established, the error signature, but with a **significant residual** difference between unrewarded and rewarded trials (`P = 0.016`) that the simultaneously recorded dopamine neurons do not show. So even inside A's own domain the channel is not `[−δ]⁺`: it carries a rectified outcome term added to the error term. The authors say so themselves — the responses "may not represent pure prediction error".

## Where it bites

**Whether an architecture needs one anti-reward channel or two.** Under A the aversive side of the broadcast system is *not* a second channel at all — it is the rectified negative half of the value channel, carrying no information the value channel lacks, and every non-reward-referenced bad event (pain, illness, threat) needs its own separate wire to the modulatory nuclei. Under B it is one genuinely distinct channel that must **arbitrate** between incommensurable sources — a missed reward and an electric shock arriving on the same variable, with the weights fixed at development.

The two designs differ in what is architecturally denied. A cannot express "this was bad in a way unrelated to anything I predicted"; B cannot keep disappointment and pain separable downstream, because they are the same number by the time any consumer sees them.

It also decides what `T355`'s position B is a channel *of*. That row's aversion channel is the medial-VTA→medial-prefrontal line, driven monosynaptically by this structure. If A holds, the "aversion" channel is in fact fed by a **disappointment** signal, and the reason nobody has recorded it on reward-omission trials becomes the central omission rather than a detail. `G117` inherits the same problem: the two channels a builder is asked to make separately usable may not be value-versus-gain or good-versus-bad but *predicted-versus-unpredicted*.

## Status

`LIVE` — both positions are stated by one review, from disjoint evidence classes, and the review does not reconcile them. A rests on a single lesion dissociation; B on activation and optogenetic-sufficiency data read out almost entirely as real-time place aversion, which `G116` says cannot separate a valence signal from an action signal. The structural escape the review itself proposes — that the medial and lateral subdivisions do different jobs, taking limbic and basal-ganglia input through largely parallel streams — has not been tested, because no manipulation cited reaches subnucleus resolution. A species confound sits on top: the pallidal→habenular projection that supplies A's mechanism is substantially smaller in monkey and cat than in rat, while the prediction-error recordings are in monkey.
