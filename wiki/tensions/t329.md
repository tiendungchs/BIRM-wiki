# T329 — Do pre/post spike pairs contribute to plasticity independently?

**Status:** `LEANING`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** A model result that depends on all-pairs summation — barn-owl delay selection, or the winner-take-all expectation-maximization construction — is re-derived under the suppression rule, showing whether the linear sum is load-bearing or merely convenient.

## Position A

**Independently — sum over all pairs.** Every timing rule in the wiki applies the measured pairwise kernel to each pre/post pair in the two trains and adds the results: `ΔJ_j = ε·J_j·[γ + Σ_f W(t_j^f − t^n)]` for delay selection (Gerstner et al. 1996, [[wiki/concepts/temporal-coding.md]]), and the same linearity underwrites the STDP-plus-winner-take-all reading of the rule as online expectation-maximization (Nessler et al. 2009, 2013). It is an assumption of convenience, never separately tested in any wiki source

## Position B

**No — the first spike dominates and its successors are suppressed.** Triplet and quadruplet stimulation of rat visual cortical slices does not match the independent-sum prediction. The fit requires one efficacy variable per side, `ε ∈ [0,1]`, zeroed by each spike of that neuron and recovering exponentially (`τ_pre ≈ 34 ms`, `τ_post ≈ 75 ms`), multiplying the pairwise kernel: `ΔW = Σ_ij ε_i^pre ε_j^post F(Δt_ij)` (Froemke & Dan 2002, *Nature* 416:433, via Kirsanov 2026 **(tentative)**)

## Where it bites

Any regime where either cell fires faster than ~1 spike per 30–75 ms, which is every regime the wiki's timing models actually run in. Delay selection tunes 200 µs structure by accumulating many pairs per afferent across a phase-locked volley; under B a repeatedly-firing afferent contributes one effective term per ~34 ms instead of one per spike, so the competition that kills mistimed delays is driven by far less evidence than the model assumes. Under B the rule also stops being a train-to-train operator and becomes a **first-spike-order detector** — which changes what code the write mechanism presupposes ([[wiki/concepts/spike-encoding-schemes.md]]) — and adds a rate dependence to the effective kernel with no rate term, since `τ_pre ≠ τ_post`

## Status

`LEANING B` on the physiology — B is the direct measurement and A is an untested convenience, but B is one preparation (rat visual cortical slice) and no wiki result has been re-run under it, so what the linear sum is doing inside those constructions is unknown
