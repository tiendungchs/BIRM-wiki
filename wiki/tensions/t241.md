# T241 — Does the default mode network's activity mean self-referential cognition is occurring?

**Status:** `LIVE`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]]  
**Closes when:** Default-mode activity is dissociated from self-referential process by design rather than by reverse inference — the anaesthetised and sleep cases already show the two come apart.

## Position A

**Yes, in effect** — the standard interpretation the wiki has inherited: DMN regions overlap those active during autobiographical memory and self-judgment, so the network subserves internally-directed, self-referential operations, and its deactivation marks a shift from internal rumination to the external environment

## Position B

**Under-determined.** The DMN persists in anaesthetised non-human primates and in early sleep, when no self-referential process is available, and the inference is a textbook reverse inference (a task activates a region; the region's activity does not imply the task). Deco, Jirsa & McIntosh 2011 propose a dynamical role instead: the DMN is the set of structural hubs — PCC/precuneus being the densest core — and its nodes are the ones that move the whole system into the metastable regime where the repertoire is explored. **Structural confirmation, with one exception** (Hagmann et al. 2008, [[wiki/concepts/connectome-hubs-and-cores.md]]): every *posterior* default-network component — posterior cingulate, precuneus, medial and lateral parietal — is in the cortical structural core by six converging graph measures, and their high resting metabolism is predicted by topology alone, betweenness centrality vs regional cerebral blood flow `r² = 0.49`, with no cognitive content invoked. But **medial prefrontal cortex is the sole default-network component entirely excluded from the core**, so "the default network is the set of structural hubs" is false as stated

## Where it bites

Whether a reasoning architecture needs an "internal mode" module at all, or whether the same anatomy is doing task-independent dynamical work that merely correlates with introspective tasks. It also decides how the wiki reads any future DMN result: as cognitive content, or as hub topology

## Status

`LIVE` — the wave-12 default-mode sources are the test, and the framings are not exclusive: hubs are exactly where an internally-generated state would have to live. The structural data split the network in two, which is the most useful move available — the posterior components are a topological object whose resting cost is explained without cognition, and medial prefrontal cortex contributes from *outside* the integrative bottleneck, which is where a slow evaluative reader would sit. **The field's own anchor review concedes the non-identification** (Buckner, Andrews-Hanna & Schacter 2008, [[wiki/entities/default-mode-network.md]]): going from an active to a passive task changes mental content *and* attentional breadth together, so the internal-mentation account and a **sentinel** account — broad low-level monitoring of the environment when focused attention relaxes — predict the same deactivation map, and one target-detection study (Hahn 2007) found *faster* responses with raised default activity, the opposite of the lapse literature. The review's own two disqualifiers on the dynamical side are the same ones cited above: the intrinsic fluctuations persist in sleep and burst-suppression anaesthesia, and they are slower than one cycle per 10 s, slower than any cognitive event. What it adds *for* the cognitive reading is graded and behavioural rather than anatomical — stimulus-independent thought counts scale ~6× rest-vs-task and ~2× easy-vs-hard with matching activity, individual daydreaming scores correlate with PCC/Rsp activity, and encoding-time default activity predicts which words will be forgotten  **Menon 2023 ([[wiki/entities/default-mode-network.md]]) moves the row toward the cognitive reading without closing it**, on three counts the dynamical account has no answer for: (i) the deactivation is *neuronal* — intracranial high-gamma (76–200 Hz) rises in medial prefrontal and posterior cingulate cortex at rest and is transiently suppressed during spatial attention, word reading and mental arithmetic — so whatever the network is doing is fast neural activity, not a haemodynamic or metabolic property of hub topology; (ii) three nodes can be driven *above* resting baseline by content-specific tasks (self-referential trait judgment in medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate and left angular gyrus; semantic judgment in area PGp), which a pure hub-dynamics account does not predict and which removes the original untestability; (iii) the first causal human datum — **left angular gyrus lesions reduce mind wandering** — attaches one node to one internally generated process. What survives on the dynamical side is that node-level meta-analyses give the nine nodes nine *different* preferential functions, so "self-referential cognition" is not the content of the network even if content is what it carries.
