# G18 — Discovered learning rules do not generalize outside the rule-search restrictions imposed by hand

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L3`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A discovered learning rule generalises to a task family outside the meta-optimisation distribution, with the restriction that buys the generalisation identified by ablation rather than asserted.

## Why it blocks the target

Meta-optimized plasticity is the wiki's only route to making slow **W** a *searched* object rather than a parameter blob, but when the rule search space is large and few restrictions are placed on the mechanism, generalization degrades. The freedom that makes rule discovery interesting is the same freedom that breaks it, so the expressiveness needed for structure acquisition is exactly what the method cannot afford

## Best current answer

**Variable-shared meta-learning**: rules parameterized by parameter-shared recurrent networks that exchange information locally, which generalize to classification problems unseen during meta-optimization (and analogously for discovered RL algorithms). Restriction by *architecture* rather than by hand-written rule form is the only route shown to survive o.o.d. Which restrictions buy generalization and which merely shrink the space is uncharacterized
