# G93 — Context in every wiki architecture is either one global scalar or a hand-supplied task label; none has an *addressed* context channel

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `arrangement`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Why it blocks the target

A gate signal has to reach the right modules and no others. The wiki's two options are both degenerate: [[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]] gives a metaparameter a diffuse broadcast because "a quantity that must reach everything and address nothing is what a broadcast channel is for", and every gated architecture ([[wiki/entities/context-modular-memory-network.md]], [[wiki/concepts/continual-learning.md]]) takes its context index from outside the model. Neither scales to a system where module `A`'s state is relevant to module `B` and irrelevant to module `C`, which is the ordinary case once modules are specialised

## Best current answer

Biology wires the middle option. Each cortical area splits its output by *cell class*: layer-2/3-targeting cortico-cortical cells send fast, tuned content along the direct edge, while layer-5/6 cortico-thalamic cells — measurably more arousal-modulated than their cortico-cortical neighbours — send the area's *state* through higher-order thalamus to a specific downstream area, glutamatergic and target-dependent. Silencing that one thalamic projection deletes the receiver's pupil-linked modulation and leaves its visual responses and its locomotion modulation intact ([[wiki/concepts/transthalamic-context-routing.md]]). The model form is a second output head per module, routed through a low-dimensional bus with a destination table and a bandwidth constraint (slow, untuned, not time-locked). The unpaid parts: nothing says how the destination table is *learned*, the multiplicative effect of the state signal on the content stream was never tested, and the receiver-side integration (layer 1 tuft vs layer 2/3 soma) is unmeasured. **A cheaper second route: address at the receiver rather than at the sender.** The diffuse systems are themselves addressed, and not by a destination table — basal-forebrain acetylcholine selects a function by *projection target* (amygdala / dorsal hippocampus / medial prefrontal cortex), striatal dopamine and acetylcholine differ by *compartment*, the same chemical is delivered by wiring or by volume transmission depending on the projection, and locus-coeruleus neurons release dopamine into hippocampus, so the sender does not even identify the signal (Mei et al. 2025). The model form is one broadcast vector `m(t)` plus a per-module decoder `f_k(W_k·m(t))` → that module's gain, rate, threshold and sign: one small matrix per module, no table, learned end-to-end. Its cost is stated on the same page — with decoders learned, which channel does what is no longer readable, so the scheme is degenerate under behavioural fitting
