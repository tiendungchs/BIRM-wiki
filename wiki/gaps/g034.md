# G34 — Every self-supervised objective's cheapest solution is to represent nothing

**Status:** `PARTIAL`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A self-supervised objective is shown to make collapse unreachable rather than merely penalised — a stated condition on the architecture-objective pair, not an empirical absence of collapse in one training run.

## Why it blocks the target

Representational **collapse** — constant encoders, or a latent with enough capacity to reconstruct any target — satisfies a predictability objective perfectly and carries zero information. It is not a training accident: which architectures can collapse is determined by where the free capacity sits ([[wiki/concepts/representational-collapse.md]], promoted out of [[wiki/concepts/energy-based-models.md]] at the 171-ingest lint), so any objective the wiki proposes for `g` (G30) must be checked against it before anything else. This is G6's shortcut problem in its limit case, with the shortcut being *nothing at all* **And the instrument that would detect the failure rewards it.** The standard vision-language compositionality benchmarks (Winoground, ARO, SUGARCREPE) are binary caption discriminations resolved by `argmax`, which returns the first element on a tie — and a fully collapsed model, assigning both captions one representation, ties every item and scores **100%** where the correct caption is listed first (Bordes et al. 2024, [[wiki/concepts/cross-modal-grounding.md]]). So the field's principal measurement of the property collapse destroys is maximally passed by collapse, and the fix costs one line of scoring code.

## Best current answer

Two families. **Contrastive** — pull up on hallucinated samples; works, but the number of samples needed may grow exponentially with the representation's dimension. **Regularised / volume-minimising** — maximise the encoders' information content and minimise the latent's, so the low-energy region shrink-wraps the data (VICReg, Barlow Twins, whitening, coding-rate reduction). The second is argued to be the only one that scales, and is *dimension*-contrastive rather than *sample*-contrastive. Which latent regulariser to use (discrete, low-rank, sparse, noisy) is stated as unresolved. **A third family, and the first that makes the row's cost measurable** (Maes et al. 2026, [[wiki/entities/lewm.md]]): **distribution matching** — drive the embedding's marginals onto a fixed isotropic Gaussian by projecting onto `M` random unit directions and scoring each with the Epps–Pulley normality statistic, which is equivalent to matching the joint by Cramér–Wold. It removes the EMA teacher and the stop-gradient entirely (neither corresponds to minimising a well-defined objective), trains end-to-end from pixels with **one** effective coefficient against a VICReg-derived objective's six — `O(log n)` bisection instead of an `O(n⁶)` grid — and lowers seed variance. Its cost is that the anti-collapse term becomes a **prior on the latent's geometry**: the target distribution specifies a dimensionality, and in an environment whose intrinsic dimension is far lower the method loses to both baselines, so the row now needs the regulariser matched to the data rather than merely chosen. **The dimension-contrastive family's founding instance, now with its own page and two facts the row was missing** (Zbontar et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/barlow-twins.md]]): `Σ_i (1−C_ii)² + λ Σ_{i≠j} C_ij²` on the cross-correlation matrix of two views' embeddings — Barlow's 1961 factorial-code hypothesis as a loss term. Its minimum is a **stated matrix identity**, it uses **one** coefficient flat over a 10× range (so the row's coefficient-count ordering had a 2021 entry at 1, not 6), and it needs no asymmetry of any kind. First fact: **the anti-collapse provision is partly a normalisation, not a term** — delete the off-diagonal penalty entirely and the model reaches 57.3% rather than collapsing, because `C` is built from embeddings standardised along the *batch* and a constant unit has zero batch variance; delete that standardisation too and it costs 18 points. Second: **the families are not stackable** — adding BYOL's predictor and stop-gradient costs 10 points (71.4 → 61.3), and Barlow Twins' 8192-wide projector costs BYOL 1.8, so a designer picks one whole design rather than layering safeguards. **Both of those facts are qualified by the family's other member** (Bardes et al. 2022, [[wiki/entities/vicreg.md]]), which is the row's primary source and had until now only been cited by name: VICReg applies a **variance hinge + covariance penalty to each branch separately**, and (i) its invariance-only ablation *collapses* where Barlow Twins' reaches 57.3, recovering exactly 57.5 once the variance hinge is restored — so the "normalisation, not a term" locus **is** this term implemented implicitly, and the two halves separate cleanly (variance blocks norm collapse, covariance blocks informational collapse, neither substitutes); (ii) the stacking result does not generalise — VICReg takes the same predictor + stop-gradient graft neutrally and its variance term *improves* BYOL by 0.9, which relocates the 10-point conflict onto Barlow Twins' batch standardisation ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T166); (iii) the row's coefficient-count ordering was quoting the wrong number — VICReg has **one free scale** (`λ = μ`, `ν = 1`), not six, and the six is PLDM's seven-term end-to-end objective, so what SIGReg buys over it is robustness (a flat `λ`) rather than count (VICReg's ray has a collapse boundary a factor of two away); (iv) and the per-branch form is what makes an anti-collapse term work when the two branches have **different weights, architectures or modalities** — image↔caption and waveform↔spectrogram embeddings with no negatives, beating both a contrastive baseline and Barlow Twins by 2–3 points, where the cross-branch cross-correlation loses 4.5. The cost is that certifiability shrinks: VICReg's minimum is non-collapsed at every positive coefficient triple and training collapses over most of that space anyway, so a loss-term provision certifies the target and not the trajectory. **A fourth mechanism that is not a fourth family, because it is not in the loss at all** (Grill et al. 2020, [[wiki/entities/byol.md]]): make the two branches asymmetric — a predictor on the online branch only, and a target branch updated by an EMA of the online weights rather than by a gradient — and a joint-embedding learner whose loss is *exactly minimised by the collapsed solution* reaches 74.3% ImageNet linear-eval top-1 without going there. Both components are necessary (removing either collapses at ~0.3%), and both are substitutable by keeping the predictor near-optimal by other means (closed-form linear regression, 52.5%; raised predictor learning rate, 66.5%), so the operative quantity is a **rate ratio between two networks**, with zero anti-collapse coefficients and zero certifiability: the authors hypothesise no loss exists whose joint gradient descent is these dynamics ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T164). The row's answer column therefore now has two *kinds* of entry, and the wiki's coefficient-count preference ordering only ranks within the first. **And the ordering ranks the smaller lever** (Assran et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/i-jepa.md]]): the fourth mechanism transposed from augmentations to masking gives the wiki's canonical image JEPA — zero anti-collapse coefficients, 81.1 ImageNet linear evaluation — and inside that fixed objective the pair sampler is worth 15.5 → 54.2, where the whole catalogue of terms above spans a few points. Two of the four samplers tested collapse the representation toward the trivial in this row's exact sense (a context uninformative about its target drives the L2 minimiser to a near-constant conditional mean), so collapse has a *data-side* locus none of the five loci above is. **A fifth locus, and a measurement that reprices the whole row** (Oquab et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/dinov2.md]]): the field's most-used self-supervised encoder runs the EMA-teacher skeleton with **no predictor**, and what replaces the asymmetry is a normalisation of the *teacher's output distribution* — running-mean centering, or 3 Sinkhorn–Knopp iterations forcing the batch's assignments over 128k prototypes toward equipartition. Like Barlow Twins' batch standardisation it is invisible to any coefficient count; unlike it, it acts on the target rather than on the loss's inputs. The repricing is the more useful half: DINOv2 carries a **second, separately ablatable spread term** (KoLeo, the Kozachenko–Leonenko nearest-neighbour entropy estimator at weight 0.1 — the non-parametric estimator a contrastive loss implements with negatives and the variance/covariance terms approximate with a Gaussian), and its measured value is **read-out-specific**: −8.3 mAP on instance retrieval when removed, ~0 on segmentation, and 0.5 on the ImageNet linear probe every entry in this row is ranked by. The masked-patch term is the mirror image (−2.9 mIoU segmentation, +0.4 retrieval). So an anti-collapse or spreading provision is not one scalar property that more of is better — it buys a specific *read-out*, and this row's benchmark cannot see the two terms that matter. **A sixth locus, and it changes the row's predicate** (Siméoni et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/dinov3.md]]): scaling that same recipe to a ViT-7B for 1M iterations, the objective is minimised correctly throughout — no flat term, no constant encoder, stable patch norms, ImageNet linear probe rising monotonically — and the *patch*-level linear probe peaks at 200k iterations and ends **below its own early value**, the failure showing as a steadily rising CLS↔patch cosine similarity. So this row's cheapest-solution framing misses a whole failure class: a read-out the objective does not measure can be destroyed by an objective in perfect health, which makes anti-collapse a **state to be monitored per read-out** rather than a property certified once from the loss ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T168). The provision that fixes it is the first here that constrains **second-order structure over a set of tokens within one input** — `L_Gram = ‖X_S X_Sᵀ − X_G X_Gᵀ‖_F²` on ℓ₂-normalised patch features, with the target supplied by an *earlier iterate of the same network* (100k–200k good, 1M harmful) — applied after the damage it repairs it in 1% of the training budget, and its invariance class (anything preserving inner products) makes it the nearest object in the wiki to a trainable objective for the relational half of G30 **A second locus the loss cannot see: the architecture's own information pathways** ([[wiki/entities/hit-jepa.md]]). Three stacked JEPAs, each carrying a variance hinge and a covariance penalty on both its branches, collapse informationally by a factor of 465 when the *inter-level* channel carries upsampled embeddings instead of upsampled attention maps — same loss, same coefficients, same data. The coarse level's features, replicated across positions by the upsampling, are a low-rank block the fine level can lean on to satisfy its own regression while its features go slack, and no per-branch term scores the concatenation. So the provisions catalogued in this row are indexed on *branches*, and a multi-level architecture has cross-branch representations that are nobody's argument. **The distribution-matching family's primary source, and it changes what this row is a list of** (Balestriero & LeCun 2025, [[wiki/entities/lejepa.md]]): the row's entries are provisions chosen for working, and this one starts from *which distribution the embeddings should follow* and proves the answer — at fixed total variance, anisotropy strictly raises linear-probe bias and variance, and the **isotropic Gaussian uniquely minimises** k-NN and kernel-probe integrated squared bias when the downstream task is unknown. Three consequences. (i) **The catalogue is one axis, not five families**: SIGReg with the degenerate test `mean² + (std−1)²` *is* VICReg in the many-slices limit, and with an exact integral it is per-slice kernel MMD — so the loss-term entries differ only in how many moments of the target they pin, with identifiability (finitely many moments do not determine a distribution) pushing up the ladder and gradient stability (moment `k`: gradient norm `O(k)`, variance `O(k² m_{2(k−1)})`) pushing down, and the characteristic function being the one statistic that is bounded at both ends. (ii) **Two entries above become deletable rather than necessary**: with the distribution constrained directly, the predictor that collapses BYOL and I-JEPA when removed costs nothing here (83.93 without vs 83.57 with), the EMA teacher survives only as evaluation-time weight averaging (+~3 ViT, 0 ResNet), and **register tokens are unnecessary** (0–8 registers span 0.7 points), which reattributes DINOv2/v3's instabilities to objective conditioning rather than to ViTs. (iii) **The row acquires the monitor T168 asks for**: LeJEPA's training-loss *value* correlates with downstream linear-probe accuracy at `ρ_s ≈ 0.85`, and `≈0.99` under a `λ^0.4` rescaling, across architectures, datasets, learning rates and budgets — the first label-free ordering of checkpoints in this row, though calibrated on the global read-out that stayed healthy through DINOv3's dense collapse. The standing cost is unchanged and now has a name: the theorem fixes the code's *shape* at a given dimension `K` and is silent on `K`, which is where both measured failures of the family sit ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T169). **And the row's oldest non-term entry turns out to be a dial rather than a switch** (Chen et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/vl-jepa.md]]): in the first JEPA here whose target branch is a *separate network in another modality*, the anti-collapse work is done by InfoNCE's uniformity term while the target encoder is trained at a **scaled-down learning rate**, and sweeping that scalar shows both endpoints lose — jointly trained at full speed −3.6 classification, **frozen −7.3**, optimum at 0.05–0.10. Every design in the row above sits at one endpoint or the other (EMA/joint training, or freezing per T154), so BYOL's "rate ratio between two networks" is a continuous parameter the family has been setting by convention.
