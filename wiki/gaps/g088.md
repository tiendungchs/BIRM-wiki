# G88 — No architecture in the wiki has a direction of information flow that comes from its own weight matrix

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `arrangement`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A trained network's direction of information flow is derived from its own weight matrix rather than from a learned gate, and is shown on a task to route as well as attention at lower parameter cost.

## Why it blocks the target

Every routing decision in the wiki is a learned or gated object — attention logits, a gating network, a controller-written address, a learned adjacency — and each costs parameters, a forward pass, and a training signal. Koller et al. 2024 exhibits the alternative: in a delayed, partially-synchronised network, activity propagates from low- to high-**instrength** nodes, where instrength is the column sum `s_i = Σ_j a_ij` of the weight matrix. No learning, no gate, no controller; the direction is a static scalar field readable off the weights before the network is run, and it directed essentially every wave that emerged across four frequency bands, conduction speeds of 1–10 m/s and five orders of magnitude of coupling gain. The gap is that any recurrent architecture with delayed message passing over a fixed graph *already has* this field and none of them reads it — and the two preconditions are things standard architectures discard: non-uniform instrength (weight normalisation flattens it — the instrength-normalised control produced waves but not one gradient-aligned) and non-zero delays (the zero-delay control likewise: 24% of timepoints carried waves, none directed)

## Best current answer

Partial and untested at the architecture level. The mechanism is characterised in phase-oscillator and Jansen–Rit models on a human connectome, never in a trained network and never against a task. The override is also identified — a ±0.75 Hz local intrinsic-frequency detuning reverses the direction over fixed wiring, which would make local time constants the runtime control variable instead of the weights — but nothing implements it. The minimum experiment is cheap: take any recurrent network with per-edge delays, compute the instrength field, and test whether perturbation responses propagate along it
