# G50 — The controller cannot set the gain of its own teaching signal

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A controller sets the gain of its own teaching signal through a learned edge, and the closed loop is shown to outperform the same architecture receiving that gain from outside.

## Why it blocks the target

Every learning rule in the wiki receives its neuromodulatory term from outside — a reward scalar, a gating signal, a plasticity gate supplied by the environment or by a hand-set schedule. A controller that can only *consume* its teacher cannot decide that the current situation warrants faster learning, cannot down-weight a misleading reward, and cannot arrange its own curriculum, so gaps G32 (nothing designs the experience stream) and G18 (rule search is restricted by hand) both stay closed from the inside

## Best current answer

Anatomy supplies the missing edge and no model implements it: deep layers of ventral prelimbic and infralimbic cortex project to the striatal **patch** compartment and the medial accumbens shell, both of which project *directly* onto dopamine neurons of the ventral tegmental area and substantia nigra pars compacta, and the ventral tier projects to dorsal raphe and locus coeruleus as well — while the dorsal tier does not. The output is **typed by cortical layer**: superficial layers reach the matrix (ordinary action bias), deep layers reach the patch (write to the teaching signal). Norepinephrine adds a second stage — locus coeruleus stimulation raises extracellular dopamine in prelimbic/infralimbic cortex, so the modulators are chained rather than independent. No architecture in the wiki has a controller output typed this way; [[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]] comes closest and its dopamine term is still exogenous **The one normative proposal for a self-setting gain:** if the modulator encodes *precision* rather than a reward error, its set-point is inferred by the same gradient descent on free energy that runs perception and learning — precision is the Rescorla–Wagner learning rate, so the system estimates its own step size from its own residual variance instead of receiving a schedule (Friston 2009, [[wiki/concepts/precision-weighting.md]]). This does not close the gap: it is a proposal with no implementation, and it is in direct conflict with the reward-prediction-error reading the wiki's models actually use (T122). **A second family of self-setting rules, from the reinforcement-learning side and cheaper**: the metaparameters are computed from second-order statistics of the teaching signal itself — the *variance* of the temporal-difference error lowers the discount factor (a long horizon whose value estimates have gone noisy is not affordable), the *sign oscillation* of the same error lowers the learning rate (delta-bar-delta), and the value estimate sets the exploration temperature. Every input is a quantity the agent already computes, and the loop runs within one lifetime in one environment rather than over a task distribution (Doya 2002, [[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]]). Still not closed: six heuristics with no shared objective and nothing guaranteeing the four-channel loop converges, and no implementation of any of them. **A third route, implemented and running on hardware, that answers the row from the outside**: natural e-prop installs a dedicated *learning signal generator* — a second spiking network (800 neurons, 30% adaptive-threshold), co-meta-trained with the learner, whose only output is a per-neuron per-millisecond teaching signal `L_j^t` multiplying the learner's local eligibility traces, and whose *input* is the task's target rather than any loss (Ortner et al. 2025, [[wiki/concepts/analog-in-memory-computing.md]]). One application of `Σ_t L^t ⊙ e^t` writes an entire motor behaviour into a 250-neuron recurrent SNN whose weights sit in analog phase-change memory. This is the first architecture in the wiki where the teaching signal is a *learned, structured, task-conditioned time series* instead of a broadcast scalar — but it does not close the row, it relocates it: the gain is set by an outer loop over `p(T)`, not by the controller, the generator sees the target and never the outcome, and it is 3.2× the size of the network it teaches. The unrun composition the row wants is to feed the generator the outcome as well as the target, so the teacher becomes correctable within a lifetime.
**What the precision proposal now has, and what it still does not**: it has a running agent in which the gain is the only thing dopamine is, with a behavioural syndrome attached to each anatomical placement (Friston et al. 2012, [[wiki/entities/affordance-active-inference-model.md]]) — so the *consumption* side of a self-set teaching gain is demonstrated. It still has no self-setting: those precisions are constants chosen per region by the modeller, and the paper says so explicitly.
