# G1 — Two-level separation exists as a timescale split, not as a factorization

**Status:** `PARTIAL`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A discovered — not hand-written — factorization `p = f(g, x)` transfers a reused `g` to an instance graph absent from training, with the two-rate schedule ablated to show the factorization and not the timescale split carries the transfer.

## Why it blocks the target

Meta-graph and instance-graph co-occur in every observation; a flat learner fits the mixture `E_θ[p(obs|θ)]`, which no individual instance follows — so it cannot transfer at all

## Best current answer

The *two learning rates* are instantiated and validated: complementary learning systems (fast hippocampal store + slow cortex + replay) and meta-RL (slow weights training a fast recurrent inner learner). The *factorized code* `p = f(g, x)` is not — no transfer in the historical record moved a factorization. **Where the split is written down by hand it works and is cheap**: a hyperprior over per-agent goal preferences and rationality, with per-agent parameters as the instance level, adapts by an exact Dirichlet–categorical conjugate update in 8 trials and predicts the 9th ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]) — no gradient, no replay. That is evidence the *shape* is right and none that it can be discovered: the shared level was authored, and the environment it sits over is hand-written PDDL
