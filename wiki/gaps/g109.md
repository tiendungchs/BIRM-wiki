# G109 — No model claiming a knowledge/control split has been scored on the lesion profile that certifies one

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Spec:** `D242` ([[_brainstorm/birm-spec.md]] §11)  
**Closes when:** One architecture is ablated in two places and produces the two profiles in opposite directions — store ablation giving high cross-task item consistency, strong frequency and typicality sensitivity, and no benefit from a partial cue; control ablation giving low cross-task consistency, no frequency sensitivity, largest deficits on the most demanding items, and *cue-following in both directions* (a correct partial cue rescues the item, a misleading one drives the wrong answer).

## Why it blocks the target

The wiki asserts a knowledge/control separation everywhere and never certifies one. [[wiki/concepts/controller-knowledge-vs-process.md]] frames the question and proposes a population-geometry test nobody has run; [[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]] gates a maintained content; [[wiki/concepts/sparse-expert-routing.md]] routes over a shared trunk; every working-memory architecture here splits maintenance from content. The standard evidence offered is that ablating the control path lowers accuracy — which is compatible with the control path simply carrying signal, and so certifies nothing.

Human neuropsychology already supplies the instrument and it is behavioural, item-level and cheap ([[wiki/concepts/controlled-semantic-cognition.md]]). Two lesions to one system give **qualitatively opposite** profiles across ten probes, not two points on a severity axis: a degraded store fails the same items everywhere, tracks familiarity and typicality, and cannot be cued; a broken controller fails inconsistently, ignores frequency, drifts associatively, and is *driven* by whatever partial evidence is supplied — a phonemic cue rescues the item, a misleading cue produces the competitor. The miscue row is the load-bearing one, because it is the only probe whose two lesions give responses of opposite sign rather than different magnitudes, so it cannot be produced by a single capacity variable.

Without this profile, "our model separates representation from control" is an architectural diagram, and the wiki cannot tell a real factorization from a wiring convention — which is `G1`'s two-level separation problem restated at the level of what would count as evidence.

## Best current answer

None in the wiki. The nearest thing is the ablation-accuracy convention, which the argument above disqualifies. The instrument transfers to machines without modification in principle: cross-task consistency is a per-item agreement rate across tasks sharing only the item; frequency sensitivity is a regression of per-item accuracy on training-corpus count; the cue manipulation is a partial prefix or a partial attribute vector supplied at inference, run once correct and once misleading. What is *not* established is whether the profile discriminates in an architecture that was never trained to develop the dissociation — a machine may fail all ten probes in the same direction after either ablation, which would be an informative negative about the instrument rather than about the model, and is why this row sits at `L0-INSTR` rather than `L1`.
