# G56 — No architecture has a run-time gain register on its own connections

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A content-free, seconds-reversible multiplicative gain register sits between the connection and the unit, set independently of the weight, and is shown to buy stability that the weight alone cannot. `G52` is the full four-register version.

## Why it blocks the target

Every model in the wiki fixes connection strength at inference, or makes it a fast *content* store (fast weights, short-term plasticity). Biology carries a third register: a content-free, seconds-reversible multiplicative gate placed between the synapse and the dendrite, whose set-point is a global state scalar (arousal, energy, threat). Without it a system cannot trade its own stability margin for capacity, cannot disconnect an expensive module when the energy is not there, and has no cheap way to switch between a deliberative and a reflexive controller

## Best current answer

The biological mechanism is fully localised — Ca²⁺ and cAMP opening SK, HCN and KCNQ channels inside long, thin dendritic spines, with a narrow spine neck as the electrical bottleneck that makes the shunt per-synapse; α2A adrenergic, D1 dopaminergic and α7 nicotinic receptors set it in opposite directions on *disjoint* spine subsets. Two machine-side facts make this a gap rather than an oversight: the wiki's stability regularisers (spectral-radius clipping, adaptation currents, activity normalisation) are the same tax paid silently and never reported as a capacity number, and the one architecture with a modulator ([[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]]) uses it to gate *what is written*, not to set how hard the store runs. The register now also has a human dose–response handle and an *addressing rule* a builder can copy: exogenous stimulation of the fibre bundle into the control loop raises the gain only where an ensemble is already engaged — induced prefrontal theta rises during a conflict task and not at rest in the same session, so "which module gets amplified" is answered by the module's own activity rather than by a routing decision (Widge et al. 2019, [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-control.md]]) What the register still lacks is a rule for *where to set it*, and the wiki now has exactly one candidate: the gain **is** the precision (inverse variance) of the prediction error the synapse carries, optimised by descent on the same free energy as everything else, and necessarily on a slower timescale than the activity it scales because it minimises the path-integral rather than the instantaneous objective (Friston 2009, [[wiki/concepts/precision-weighting.md]]). Normative rule, no implementation — so the gap stays open on the architecture side while acquiring a set-point. A **second** candidate set-point rule now exists for the same register, from reinforcement learning rather than inference: the noradrenergic gain *is* the inverse temperature of action selection, so it should rise when the value estimate is extreme (near-best performance, or danger) and fall when the action values at the current state are widely spread (Doya 2002; Ishii et al. 2002, [[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]]). Two rival set-points, both computed from quantities the agent has, neither implemented. **A third set-point rule now exists and it *is* implemented, at the price of being learned rather than derived** (Zhao et al. 2022, [[wiki/concepts/cross-paradigm-interface.md]]): train a separate network to emit the register's value directly, supervised not on the task but on **similarity between contexts** — `min ‖S_ta − S_Vth‖² − λ S_ta·S_Vth + µ max(ρ − ‖S_Vth‖₁, 0)`, so similar tasks get similar sparse gates and dissimilar ones get disjoint gates. Over 40 sequential tasks it beats context-dependent gating, elastic weight consolidation and synaptic intelligence, generalises to tasks the emitting network never saw, and keeps improving where single models saturate. Two caveats keep the row open: the register is a **threshold**, so it gates reachability rather than scaling drive, and the whole mechanism runs on a task-similarity function `S_ta` that the paper computes from permutation indices it was handed and calls an open problem in general

**The register has now been *spent* in a behaving model, which sharpens what is still missing** (Friston et al. 2012, [[wiki/entities/affordance-active-inference-model.md]]): a fixed, content-free, D₁-mediated postsynaptic gain, one scalar per region of a two-level sensorimotor hierarchy, is by itself sufficient to produce three dissociable motor syndromes — and depleting it at a *higher* level has the opposite sign on reaction time to depleting it at a lower one. Two lessons the row should carry. First, the payoff a builder gets is real and is not a stability margin: it is the trade-off between how efficiently cues elicit action and how efficiently context is revised, controlled by the *ratio* of gains across levels rather than by any one of them. Second, the precisions there are hand-set constants held fixed for the run, so the demonstration is entirely on the consumption side; the set-point half of this row is untouched, and every candidate rule above remains unimplemented.
