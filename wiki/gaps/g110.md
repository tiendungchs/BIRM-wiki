# G110 — Top-down control is always applied to a module or its output; nothing applies it to the interface that feeds the module

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `arrangement`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Spec:** `D253`, `D255` ([[_brainstorm/birm-spec.md]] §11)  
**Closes when:** A controller's only write into a store is a suppressive signal on the store's *input* gateway module, and the arrangement is shown to beat both output-filtering and query-conditioning on a context-cued retrieval task where the distractors are previously-correct associations.

## Why it blocks the target

Every control arrow in the wiki lands on a module or on what a module returns: an attention weight over retrieved items, a gain on a population, a query vector, a mask on connectivity. The measured circuit does neither. The medial prefrontal cortex has only a weak projection to the medial entorhinal cortex and a strong bidirectional one to **perirhinal and lateral entorhinal cortex** — the structures that gate what enters the hippocampus at all — terminating in their superficial and deep layers respectively, and prefrontal projections in cortex preferentially target inhibitory neurons. So "top-down control of retrieval" is implemented as **narrowing the input the store is allowed to complete from**, upstream of the store, and typed for the *what* stream rather than the *where* stream (Eichenbaum 2017, [[wiki/entities/hippocampal-prefrontal-channel.md]]).

The same row carries a second arrangement fact from the same circuit: **the direction of an edge is re-selected within a single trial.** Dorsal hippocampus leads prefrontal cortex by ~30 ms while a memory is held over a delay and at context entry; prefrontal cortex leads hippocampus by ~30 ms at object sampling and at the choice point — same task, same pair of modules, both directions present only on correct trials, with a third module (nucleus reuniens) whose inactivation eliminates the bidirectional connectivity. No architecture here re-selects which way an edge runs partway through one forward pass.

## Best current answer

**None, and the biology's own version is untested.** The nearest machine constructs each miss the arrangement rather than the mechanism: cross-attention conditions the *query* (control at the reader), retrieval re-ranking filters the *output* (control after the store), and [[wiki/entities/context-modular-memory-network.md]]'s binary mask deletes structures from the store's own dynamics (control *inside* the store). None places the controller's only write on a separate gateway module that both endpoints depend on. What the arrangement is claimed to buy is measured only two synapses downstream: prefrontal inactivation costs dorsal-CA1 conjunctive cells their **object selectivity while leaving spatial specificity exact** (Navawongse & Eichenbaum 2013) — a factorised lesion signature that a global gain or a query-conditioning control cannot produce. Eichenbaum's own stated missing experiments are the two direct tests: prefrontal inactivation should reduce representational specificity *in* perirhinal/lateral entorhinal cortex, and inactivating those areas should reduce dorsal-CA1 stimulus specificity.

## From

Eichenbaum 2017, [[wiki/entities/hippocampal-prefrontal-channel.md]]. Distinct from `G52` (an edge with its own state) and `G53` (a third module licensing a pair's edge): both of those keep the control arrow on the *edge*, while this row moves it onto a module that is neither endpoint and sits in the signal path.
