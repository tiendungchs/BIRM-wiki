# G116 — No task in the wiki's neuromodulator literature orthogonalizes valence against action, so no result there distinguishes a value signal from an action signal

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L0-INSTR`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** One result the wiki relies on for the identity of a broadcast modulatory signal is reported on a design that crosses `Go`/`No-Go` with `reward`/`punishment` in all four cells — or the confound is shown not to apply to a stated class of claims, with the class stated.

## Why it blocks the target

Reward is collected by approach and distant punishment is avoided by inhibition, in the world *and* in the substrate ([[wiki/concepts/affective-opponency.md]]; direct/D₁/Go coupled to reward, indirect/D₂/No-Go to punishment in [[wiki/entities/basal-ganglia.md]]). Standard paradigms therefore populate only the two congruent cells. In those cells a signal that tracks valence and a signal that tracks required action are numerically identical, and the wiki's central dispute over what phasic dopamine carries (`T122`, `T85`) is conducted almost entirely on such data.

The confound is not hypothetical: the same review that names it also shows dopamine is required for active *avoidance* — punishment plus Go — which is the cell where the two readings first separate, and reports dopamine efflux rising under aversive stimuli. Under an absolute-valence reading those are anomalies; under an action reading they are the rule. Worse, the origin of the valence axis is claimed to *move* with the current threat or reward prediction, so "the same outcome" is not the same quantity across conditions and even a four-cell design must hold the baseline fixed or report it.

Independent support from a different evidence class: the same mesolimbic circuitry that generates incentive salience is reported to run in a second mode generating an active-coping *fearful* salience — attention-riveting with a threatening aspect (Richard & Berridge 2011, via [[wiki/concepts/incentive-salience.md]]). One circuit producing both desire and dread is an *effect* variable by construction, which is this row's claim reached without any four-cell design.

A second instrument failure rides along: lesion, depletion and dietary manipulations of a modulator move the **tonic** level, while the disputed claims are about **phasic** coding, and autoreceptor feedback plus inverted-U dose–response can flip the sign of the inferred effect.

## Best current answer

**None run in the wiki.** Named partial instances, all cited by Boureau & Dayan 2010 and none ingested here: Crockett et al. 2009 (acute tryptophan depletion abolishes punishment-induced inhibition in humans — the closest thing to a crossed design in this literature), plus two then-unpublished studies explicitly designed to orthogonalize action and valence (Guitart-Masip et al. 2010; Huys et al. 2010). Acquiring the published forms of those is the cheapest way to move this row.

The machine-side version costs nothing and nobody has run it: score any agent with a Go/No-Go gating architecture on all four cells at matched difficulty. This page's prior predicts a measurable deficit on `reward × No-Go` and `punishment × Go` specifically, which is a falsifiable signature of the coupling rather than of the task.
