# G52 — No inter-module connection in the wiki has state of its own

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** One inter-module connection carries weight, gain, writability and operating point as four separately settable state variables, and a task is shown to need at least three of them at once. `G56` is the gain register alone.

## Why it blocks the target

Every architecture here treats the link between two modules as fixed wiring or as one weight matrix trained by the same rule as everything else. The hippocampus→prefrontal projection carries four separable state variables at once: a **weight** (bidirectionally plastic — potentiation, depression *and* depotentiation, NMDA- and protein-kinase-A-dependent), a **gain** set at the terminal by dopamine D1 and measurable as hippocampal–prefrontal coherence, a **writability** flag set by a *third* region (basolateral-amygdala high-frequency activity blocks subsequent potentiation), and a global **operating point** displaced for hours-to-weeks by stress and restorable pharmacologically. The behavioural memory sits in the edge: extinction training potentiates the pathway, and depotentiating it afterwards abolishes extinction recall without touching either endpoint. Nothing in the wiki has an edge that can be locked, gain-scaled, or globally de-tuned independently of the modules it connects — nor an evaluation that severs an edge rather than ablating a node (Spedding & Jay 2012, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]])

## Best current answer

**None.** The four state variables are separable and separately manipulable in the biology — weight (bidirectionally plastic), gain (dopamine at the terminal), writability (an amygdala metaplastic lock), and a stress-set operating point that displaces the whole tuple for hours to weeks. The nearest machine construct is a gated skip connection with a learned scalar, which gives that scalar no plasticity of its own, no external lock and no global mode switch. Cheapest test: in a two-module model with a learned interface, let a third module emit a binary write-mask on the interface's plasticity and measure whether continual-learning interference falls ([[wiki/entities/hippocampal-prefrontal-channel.md]]). **The row is no longer empty on the machine side** ([[wiki/concepts/cross-paradigm-interface.md]], Zhao et al. 2022): a hybrid unit is an interface module holding its own parameters `W, H, F, Q`, and one of its three sanctioned training placements is *modelled separately, under an independent optimisation goal* — the CLEVRER collision detector is a 1-D UNet with two MLP heads fitted on event labels while both endpoint networks train on theirs. That gives the edge a **weight** and an **objective** of its own, which is more than a gated skip connection has; it still gives it no writability lock, no third-party gain and no global operating point, and the paper reports no experiment isolating the interface's learnability from the hybrid architecture around it
