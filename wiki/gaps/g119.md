# G119 — No architecture has a reinforcer that is admissible to one of its learning systems and inadmissible to another

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `arrangement`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** One system has two learning mechanisms and at least two teaching signals, with a stated and enforced admissibility relation between them — signal `s₁` updates mechanism `m₁` and provably cannot update `m₂` — and the restriction is shown to be load-bearing: removing it degrades performance rather than merely changing it.

## Why it blocks the target

Every broadcast signal in the wiki reaches every consumer. `δ` trains the critic and the actor ([[wiki/concepts/reward-prediction-error.md]]); in [[wiki/entities/meta-rl-agent.md]] the same scalar additionally enters the recurrent network as an input; [[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]]'s per-stripe gate `δ_j = snr_j · δ` restricts *which units* hear the signal but not *which learning mechanism* consumes it. Fungibility is assumed everywhere and stated nowhere.

Biology denies it directly. Betley et al. 2015 ([[wiki/concepts/homeostatic-need-signal.md]]) find that AGRP neuron activity is a fully competent **Pavlovian** reinforcer — it conditions flavour preference bidirectionally, conditions place preference with no food present, and supports closed-loop place avoidance — and every attempt to use it as an **instrumental** reinforcer failed: mice would not learn to lever-press or nose-poke either to terminate stimulation when fed or to silence the neurons when food-restricted. The same signal simultaneously *energizes* instrumental performance on an already-learned contingency, so it is not that the signal fails to reach the instrumental system; it reaches it as a modulator and not as a teacher. The authors state the asymmetry as a result and give no mechanism for it.

**A second evidence class, on the response side.** Cardinal et al. 2002 ([[wiki/concepts/valuation-system-decomposition.md]]) report the same non-fungibility with the roles swapped — not a reinforcer inadmissible to a rule, but a *response* inadmissible to one: chicks in Hershberger's looking-glass runway never learn to run **away** from food in order to reach it, rats cannot learn to withhold approach to a visual CS in order to be rewarded (Bussey et al.), and rodents are near-impossible to train to scratch themselves for reward. In each case the response is a fully competent Pavlovian CR and the instrumental contingency over it is unlearnable, with the reinforcer, the sensory situation and the motor capacity all held constant. This is the instrumental counterpart of the *preparedness* prior already listed on [[wiki/concepts/affective-opponency.md]], and it shows the admissibility relation has two arguments, not one: `(signal, rule)` and `(response, rule)`.

**A third evidence class, and it is the cleanest: the same signal, two learning rules, one task.** Flagel et al. 2011 ([[wiki/concepts/sign-tracking-and-goal-tracking.md]]) give rats one Pavlovian contingency and get two conditioned responses, acquired **at the same rate**. A dopamine receptor antagonist administered throughout acquisition, with the conditioned response then probed drug-free, abolishes learning of the cue-directed response and leaves learning of the outcome-directed response completely intact — while *suppressing performance of both* during the drug sessions. Here the reinforcer, the stimulus, the schedule, the motor repertoire and the species are held constant and the only thing that varies is which learning product is being written; the signal reaches both rules (it demonstrably modulates both behaviours) and teaches only one. That is the admissibility relation this row asks for, measured, with the address confound closed by the fact that both products are learned in the same animal on the same trials.

**Counter-evidence on the same projection, and it cuts the other way.** Sharpe et al. 2017 ([[wiki/concepts/state-prediction-error.md]]) show the dopamine transient is sufficient and necessary for a learning product with no value in it at all — a cue→cue link acquired with no reward in the session, obeying blocking, and devaluation-sensitive at test. So the signal that the third evidence class finds inadmissible to the goal-tracker's predictive learning is, in another preparation, *required* for predictive learning of exactly that kind. Either admissibility is set by something finer than signal identity (timing, terminal field, the rule's own window), or one of the two nulls is a dose artefact (`T404`). Whichever way it resolves, a builder cannot read the admissibility relation off the signal's chemical identity.

What the missing restriction denies a builder is any principled account of **which controller owns a given valuation**. [[wiki/concepts/incentive-salience.md]] already names the case that matters — cue-bound 'wanting' opposing a declarative goal, with the cue-bound side winning — and treats it as an arbitration problem between two controllers reading one value. If reinforcers are non-fungible, the more basic fact is that the two controllers were never trained on the same signal, and an architecture that trains them both on one `δ` has removed the distinction before arbitration is reached.

## Best current answer

**None, and the nearest constructs restrict the wrong dimension.**

- `G19`/`G52` and [[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]]'s `snr_j · δ` gate address *address* — which synapses a signal may move. This row is about *type* — which learning rule may consume it, with the address left alone.
- [[wiki/concepts/broadcast-channel-decomposition.md]] and [[wiki/entities/ventral-tegmental-area.md]] split one projection into channels with different targets. That is again address, and it makes the restriction anatomical: a channel is inadmissible to a mechanism only because its axons do not arrive. Whether *that* is the whole story here is untested, since nobody has traced where the AGRP need signal's teaching effect is consumed.
- [[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]] gives four chemicals four jobs, but each job is global — no chemical is barred from a consumer.

The cheap machine version needs no neuroscience. Give an agent a Pavlovian state-value learner and an instrumental action-value learner sharing one environment, deliver two teaching signals, and low-pass one of them at increasing time constants. The prediction this row is built on is that state-value learning survives a time constant at which action-contingent learning has already broken, because cue-outcome association tolerates a trace and single-action credit does not — which would make admissibility a *derived* property of a signal's bandwidth rather than a wire that has to be omitted. If both break together, the biological asymmetry has some other cause and this row needs restating.
