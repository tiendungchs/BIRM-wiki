# G17 — No evaluation protocol can certify structure discovery

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** An instrument certifies that a *discovered* meta-graph, not a sample correlation, was recovered — and does so on a domain it was not designed for. `I1`–`I16` at [[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]] are the candidates; one must survive a negative control that fakes structure.

## Why it blocks the target

i.i.d. testing cannot separate a recovered meta-graph from a correlation holding in the sample, so every "architecture X reasons" claim is unscoreable and the hardness-source scoring table has no valid instrument. Existing o.o.d. benchmarks are domain-specific, hand-built, and must be revised as models learn to game them (the Winograd Schema Challenge was found to contain the shortcuts it was designed to remove)

## Best current answer

**Sixteen instruments, none sufficient, now inventoried at [[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]]** (split out of this cell at the 212-ingest lint pass, at 20,336 characters and with colliding ordinals; the canonical numbering is `I1`–`I16` there). The shape of the answer, which is what this row now carries: instruments are cheap in proportion to how little they need — the two that need nothing but the model itself are the **support/query split** (`I8`: induce structure from one pair, execute it on a second from the same mechanism; the *gap* between 0.994 self-fit and 0.000 transfer is the measurement) and the **consistency score restricted to items the model got wrong** (`I9`: 0.90 → **0.34** across three architectures) — while the only instrument that is a *quantity* rather than a genre, developer-aware generalization difficulty `GD` (`I5`), has no approximation and is computed for exactly zero tasks (G31). Ten failure modes are shared across the inventory rather than belonging to any instrument, and three of them close candidate answers rather than qualifying them: **developer-blindness is a depleting stock** (`F1`: ~10,000 reported scores against 100 private tasks over five years), **item-level blindness does not imply distributional blindness and the two clauses conflict with difficulty calibration** (`F2`, absorbed below), and **`GD ≈ 0` items are unaudited because difficulty is asserted per-benchmark and never measured per-task** (`F3`: 49% of ARC-AGI-1's private set fell to blind brute-force search in 2020, absorbed below). Two conclusions the inventory now supports and no single instrument stated: **an honest instrument returns a facet vector, not a number** — reached from the behaviour side (0.92 i.i.d. accuracy against 0.72/0.50/0.54 systematicity/productivity/localism) and from the task side (a concept-variation score that *rises* 10 points on one concept and falls 11 on another) — and **the certification target is the developer, not the system**, since skill on any task known in advance is purchasable outright. What this leaves the row: the honest current answer is that G17 is a **maintenance schedule rather than a protocol** — benchmark authorship run as a [[wiki/concepts/refinement-loop.md]] against the field, stopping when authoring tasks easy for humans and hard for AI becomes impossible — which is a different kind of answer from the one the row asks for, and the only one with five years of evidence behind it. **The first instrument whose output is a *dial* rather than a reading** (`I32`, [[wiki/entities/cfq.md]]): construct the train/test split by optimising a **computed** divergence over derivation subgraphs — maximise compound divergence `D_C` while holding atom divergence `D_A ≤ 0.02` as a control — and accuracy becomes monotone in `D_C` at `R² = 0.81–0.88` for three architectures on two datasets, against `R² = 0.11–0.22` for the length ratios the field splits on. This is a partial answer to the row's own complaint that existing o.o.d. benchmarks are "domain-specific, hand-built": the shift magnitude is now **comparable across split methods and across datasets**, published splits can be re-scored retrospectively (the field's default "query pattern" split is `D_C = 0.008`; SCAN's `add primitive` splits violate their own atom-matching control at `D_A` = 0.07–0.08), and the shift is certified *unobservable* — train and test samples at `D_C = 0.694` cannot be told apart by eye. What it does **not** do is close the row: it measures the size of a distribution shift, not whether the recovered object is the intended structure, and its atom granularity is the benchmark author's choice, so every divergence is relative to a chosen factorisation (`G16` reappearing inside the instrument). It also names a leakage channel `F1`/`F2` miss — **hyperparameters tuned on an o.o.d. validation set optimise for one known compound divergence**, leaving no trace in the reported score, so developer-blindness must cover the tuning protocol and no benchmark in the wiki reports where its baselines were tuned. Whether `D_C` is the difficulty or an average over facets that come apart is [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T312. **A defect the inventory exposed that is upstream of every instrument** ([[wiki/concepts/human-baseline.md]], `F10`): the reference system is unspecified. A human baseline is a protocol — pool, attempt budget, aggregation order, exclusion rule, item-pool identity — and the same raw data yields **73% against ~91%** on identical items and participants and **60% against 99%** on one benchmark (both absorbed below). Three tensions the wiki logged separately are one missing specification.

## Evidence absorbed from the benchmark band

Sixteen rows the wiki had logged as separate `LIVE` tensions (`T205`–`T227`, at the design-ladder pass) were evidence *for* this row rather than independent disputes; their findings are below and the rows are retired to `wiki/tensions/closed/`. Eight of the band were kept because they carry a dispute this row does not make: `T204` (algorithm- versus compute-limited frontier), `T208` (whether a rejector generalises past its author's environments, `G68`), `T211` (core knowledge as architectural ingredient or scoring control), `T215` (perception versus reasoning as the locus of the modality gap), `T218` (a grader's false-negative rate as an optimisation pressure under RLVR), `T220` (whether inference budget is a dial a builder can turn up), `T224` (what an external correct trace buys as conditioning input) and `T228` (whether shortcut resistance is a capability scaling buys).

- **Half of ARC-AGI-1 is `GD ≈ 0`.** 49% of the private eval set fell to 2020 brute-force DSL search, before LLMs and at low compute, so any sub-50% score prices vocabulary coverage rather than abstraction ([[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]], `F3`)
- **A reported score denotes nothing without (subset, attempts, $/task, harness yes/no).** An aggregator puts GPT-5.5 at 0.850 on ARC-AGI-2 (0 verified, 13 self-reported) while ARC Prize's own verified open frontier is 24.03% at $0.20/task, and 54% only with a $31/task refinement harness (`raw/llmstats-2026-arc-agi-2-leaderboard.md`, [[wiki/entities/arc-agi-2.md]])
- **Difficulty calibration is itself the leakage channel.** ARC-AGI-2's goal 6 requires public, semi-private and private subsets drawn from distributions matched to ≤1pp mean human accuracy; the 2025 report then attributes private-set overfitting to exactly that i.i.d. property (`F2`, [[wiki/entities/arc-agi-2.md]])
- **An action-only efficiency metric scores unbounded deliberation as maximally efficient.** ARC-AGI-3 counts environment-changing actions and explicitly excludes reasoning steps, tool calls and retries, so the search the metric penalises moves inside the turn where it is free ([[wiki/entities/arc-agi-3.md]])
- **A super-human matrix-reasoning score is bought from the generator plus the answer set.** A network trained only on RAVEN's eight candidates and never shown the matrix exceeds 90%; the same models fall 21–29 points on hand-authored items instantiating the concepts they answer correctly i.i.d. (Odouard & Mitchell 2022, [[wiki/entities/raven.md]])
- **A score on either presentation is a joint measure of perception and mapping.** Frontier models score 40–60 points *worse* on the image than on the row-major textual serialisation of the same ConceptARC tasks (o3 75.6% textual against 29.2% visual with tools), retiring the wiki's standing conjecture that serialising 2-D structure is what floors LLMs (Beger et al. 2025, [[wiki/concepts/rule-level-evaluation.md]])
- **Three ARC-AGI-1 "human baselines" are three estimands**, not three measurements of one quantity: union over testers 97–99%, per-subject mean on 40 *training* tasks 83.8% (Johnson et al. 2021), crowd mean on the eval split 60.2% — and none is per-subject first-attempt accuracy on the eval split, the only quantity commensurable with a machine `pass@2` ([[wiki/concepts/human-baseline.md]], `B3`)
- **The same ConceptARC study yields 91% and 73% on the same 480 items** — three attempts and a per-concept mean against first-attempt per-item accuracy — and only the second is commensurable with a machine `pass@1`, which no source pairs it with (Beger et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/conceptarc.md]], `B2`)
- **Paid human adversaries find the model *class's* blind spots, so adversarial authorship is a usable instrument.** Against an ensemble of differently-seeded adversary models the base model scores 19.3 and 20.4 on ANLI rounds 2–3 against a 33.3 chance level — below chance, not at it — and the data lifts an independently authored numerical-reasoning stress test by +18.5 (Nie et al. 2020, [[wiki/entities/anli.md]])
- **A flat scaling curve licenses "not this method", never "not this ability".** MATH's log-linear fit projected ~10³⁵ parameters for 40%; the benchmark passed 90% within about three years through chain-of-thought, verifier-selected sampling and RL on verifiable rewards, none of which is a parameter count (Hendrycks et al. 2021, [[wiki/entities/math-dataset.md]])
- **The crystallized-knowledge exclusion has no measured basis**, so knowledge-loaded benchmarks are not disqualified as evidence about a reasoning architecture: non-domain PhDs with unrestricted internet, no time limit and a $30-per-item bonus reach 34% on GPQA against in-domain experts' 65%, and a search tool moves GPT-4 by +0.7 points — the gap is in selection and composition, not in a retrievable store (Rein et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/gpqa.md]])
- **Contamination resistance and external auditability trade directly against each other.** FrontierMath's permanently private set has no distributional-leakage route in and a ~10% self-estimated critical error rate nobody outside the authoring organisation can check; HLE's published set is what makes its 15.4% knowable at all (`F12`). The third position is a benchmark as a renewable *process* — printed non-digitised sourcing, all items published, residual leakage measured as `n`-gram `δ`, construction pipeline open-sourced — at the price of no anchor subset across editions ([[wiki/entities/frontiermath.md]], [[wiki/entities/hle.md]], [[wiki/entities/olymmath.md]])
- **Answer extraction is not language-neutral, so a cross-lingual score difference is a joint measurement of model and harness.** Extraction failures are disproportionately frequent among incorrect Chinese responses on the same 582k released trajectories that carry the Wilcoxon result (Sun et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/olymmath.md]])
- **A label-noise floor above every reported score preserves the ranking and destroys the gaps.** Repairing 1,143 of HLE's 2,500 items moves seven frontier models by +7.78 to +11.50, halves adjacent gaps (5.10 → 2.40), and on the defective quarter alone compresses a 2.5× spread to 1.22× with the top rank changing hands (Zhai et al. 2026, [[wiki/entities/hle-verified.md]])
- **A benchmark's error rate is undefined until the audit protocol is specified** — 15.4% under multi-reviewer-with-rebuttal against ~26% scoring-relevant under a component-wise three-valued audit of all 2,500 items, and 18% → 25% on one subset from dropping the rebuttal alone. A model-in-the-loop repair also spends the developer-blindness of the items it repairs ([[wiki/entities/hle.md]], [[wiki/entities/hle-verified.md]], `F12`)
- **A near-zero train−test familiarity disparity is the signature of both-splits-leaked as readily as of neither.** ChatGLM3-6B reads clean at `δ_train-test = 0.55` while sitting at ~3× LLaMA-7B's absolute familiarity, and Aquila2-34B — documented as having ingested the entire GSM8K test set — returns zero suspicious items because it learned the content under a different answer delimiter (Xu et al. 2024, [[wiki/concepts/benchmark-contamination.md]], `F14`)
