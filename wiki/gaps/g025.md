# G25 — Optimality cannot be certified for any agent whose actions shape its data

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L0`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A guarantee is stated for an active agent that is neither a separability-class assumption nor a re-definition of the loss — or the wiki accepts the relativisation and the row is reclassified as settled-by-restriction.

## Why it blocks the target

For passive environments (prediction, classification) the error excess is bounded by a function of `K(µ)` alone. For active ones **no such bound exists for any policy** — two constructions prove it: *Heaven & Hell* (an early irreversible choice, so every unbiased policy scores zero in some member of the class) and *the needle* (`≐2^K(µ)` candidate actions must be tried one at a time, making the only achievable bound vacuous in the regime the agent operates in). This is not a defect of a particular learner: it means gap G17's instrument problem has a formal floor — a certificate is unavailable in principle, not merely unbuilt

## Best current answer

Relativize guarantees to **separability classes** ([[wiki/entities/aixi.md]]): passive / factorizable / stationary / Markovian / uniform / forgetful / farsighted / asymptotically learnable. The weakest workable notion is **asymptotic learnability** — count each divergence from the informed policy once, on the agent's own history, instead of charging it the lost credit. **A second relativization, from active inference:** the deploy→refit→replan loop is a *performative* problem — the reward `ℓ_π` and kernel `P_π` depend on the policy only because the model is refit to that policy's data — and under a support floor on a tabular model class the performative mirror operator has a fixed point by Brouwer, i.e. a policy already optimal for the model its own deployment produces (Milosevic et al. 2026). This replaces "no certificate" with "existence yes, reachability unknown", and localises the obstruction to one quantity: a **uniform lower bound on state coverage** under every deployed policy. With coverage, the loop is a one-way cascade of two contractions; without it the refit target moves and the cascade can close into an unstable feedback loop. The epistemic term pushes mass onto under-visited states but certifies no floor
