# G129 — Every intrinsic reward in the wiki is exhausted by learning; none reads the structure of the agent's own store, so none can grow

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L1`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** One agent's intrinsic reward is computed as a **query over the structure of its own knowledge store** — minimally, the count of frontier edges from held to unheld items — and the resulting profile is shown to differ from every exhaustible bonus: (a) on a large-`N` domain the per-step intrinsic reward **rises** over the first half of acquisition and falls thereafter, while matched novelty / prediction-error / learning-progress bonuses fall monotonically from the start; and (b) the same agent on a small, self-contained domain reproduces the monotone-decreasing profile, so the non-monotonicity is a property of domain size relative to what is held rather than a tuned schedule.

## Why it blocks the target

Every self-generated reward here is spent down by the learning it drives:

| Mechanism | Intrinsic reward | Profile as the agent learns |
|---|---|---|
| Count / pseudo-count bonuses ([[wiki/concepts/intrinsic-motivation-typology.md]], UM) | `r ∝ 1/P(e)` | falls — visited states stop being rare |
| Predictive novelty ([[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]], NM) | prediction error or ensemble disagreement | falls — the model fits |
| [[wiki/concepts/learning-progress.md]] | `d/dt` performance | falls — peaks mid-difficulty per activity, zero at mastery |
| Competence progress (CPM) | `Δ` achievement at a self-set goal | falls at mastery |
| [[wiki/concepts/empowerment.md]] | channel capacity actuators → sensors | fixed by the dynamics; does not grow with knowledge at all |
| [[wiki/concepts/eigenoption-discovery.md]] | `rᵉ(s,s′) = eᵀ(φ(s′) − φ(s))` | fixed function of the Laplacian eigenvector |

**The common form is that the reward's argument is the agent's *error or experience count*, and both are consumed.** Nothing in the wiki takes the store itself as the argument. Murayama 2022 ([[wiki/concepts/self-boosting-knowledge-acquisition.md]]) supplies the alternative with a derivation: define a gap as an edge from a learned node to an unlearned one, and the expected number of gaps after acquiring `K` of `N` pieces is

```
Gaps(K) = α · K · (N − K)
```

which is an inverted parabola peaking at `K = N/2`, rising over the entire practical range whenever `N` is large. Replicated under two other gap definitions and network models — topological voids over empirical knowledge networks (Sizemore et al. 2018), semantic networks from linear-algebra texts (Christianson et al. 2020), and small-world simulation (`N = 100`).

**What the missing reward denies a builder is an agent whose curiosity survives competence.** Every architecture here exhausts its own drive: the better it learns a domain, the less reason it has to stay in it, so long-horizon engagement must be supplied from outside by a curriculum, a task distribution or a reward schedule. The biological profile is the opposite — expertise is the *precondition* for sustained interest, and it is domain-indexed (prior football knowledge predicts curiosity about football facts and cooking knowledge does not; Witherby & Carpenter 2021).

Two further properties come with the store-reading version and no mechanism here has either:

1. **A sign that depends on domain size, not on a hyperparameter.** The same reward function gives the classical decaying curve on a small self-contained domain and a rising curve on a large one. Every wiki bonus needs an annealing schedule to change its profile; this one changes because `N` changed.
2. **Valuation by connectable surface.** An unheld item's worth is its expected degree *into* the store (Path B), which is a structural priority computable with no predictor, no episode clock and no achievement measure — the cheapest unimplemented intrinsic reward in the wiki for any agent that holds an addressable relational memory.

## Best current answer

**None reads the store.** The nearest constructs move adjacent quantities:

- [[wiki/concepts/learning-progress.md]] is the one mechanism with a non-monotone profile, and the non-monotonicity is *across activities at one time* (mid-difficulty activities win) rather than *within one domain over time*; within an activity it still decays to zero at mastery. The two are discriminable by the design in `Closes when`.
- [[wiki/concepts/schema-assimilation.md]] has exactly the right object — a store whose overlap with a new item is measured, with the one-trial encoding advantage to show the overlap is read — and no reward is computed from it.
- [[wiki/concepts/curriculum-learning.md]] orders material by difficulty or by progress, never by boundary structure; "the unheld item with the most edges into the store" is a third generator that has never been run against either.
- `G125` asks that a manufactured reward *decay* when unbacked, and `G128` that a cost term be *writable* by history. This row is the third face of the same missing write path: the benefit's **supply**, which in biology is manufactured by the agent's own accumulation.

The cheap machine version is a query, not a mechanism. For any agent holding an explicit graph, retrieval index or key–value memory, mark items held vs unheld and emit `r ∝ |{(u,v) : u held, v unheld}|` — or credit an acquisition by the number of new frontier edges it opens minus those it closes, which is the derivation's per-step term `α(N − 2k + 1)`. Then check (i) the rising limb on a large-`N` corpus, (ii) the collapse to the decaying profile on a small one, and (iii) whether engagement time is domain-indexed in the way the human data are.

## Status

`OPEN` — the derivation is three lines, the profile it predicts is opposite in sign to every bonus in the wiki over the first half of a large domain, the measurement that would run it needs no new mechanism for any agent with an addressable store, and nobody has run it. The caveat keeping this `L1` rather than `L0`: the source is a framework paper that declines to formalise, the derivation assumes acquisition order is random where real learners choose, and gap *existence* is not gap *awareness*. Adjacent rows: `G128` (the cost term's missing write port — the same conditioning on the agent's own history, on the other side of the subtraction), `G125` (an internally generated reward with no update rule — that row asks the value to fall when unbacked, this one asks the supply to rise with the store), `G72` (inferring what counts as success — frontier expansion is a success criterion generated by the store's structure), `G33` (which decomposition, and at what ratio), `T400` (whether the intrinsic term sums with the extrinsic one or is suppressed by it — orthogonal: the supply question is the same on either routing).
