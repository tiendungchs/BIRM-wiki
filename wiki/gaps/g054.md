# G54 — No architecture distinguishes a channel that carries content from one that only carries timing

**Status:** `PARTIAL`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** Content transport and temporal coordination are carried on separate channels, and the two are shown to be separately ablatable in a machine — cutting timing without cutting content, as the biology does.

## Why it blocks the target

Every inter-module link in the wiki does both jobs at once: it transports a representation *and* it is the only thing aligning the two modules in time. The biology separates them and they survive different lesions — cutting the direct hippocampus→prefrontal axons abolishes gamma coupling (content) and leaves theta coupling (coordination) intact, the same result holding for entorhinal→CA1 — so an architecture with one link per module pair cannot express the case where two modules are coordinated by a third and exchange nothing, or exchange content while free-running. It also means a measured correlation between two modules is not evidence they are connected.

## Best current answer

Split each inter-module connection into a content channel whose gain is the afferent drive and a coordination channel broadcast by a third module (medial septum, midline thalamus, or the collateralising relay of [[wiki/entities/nucleus-reuniens.md]]); no wiki architecture implements the second; **the nearest thing built, and it is a partial pass** — [[wiki/entities/ms-ssm.md]] holds a frozen bank of `S+2` memory horizons fixed at initialisation and lets a content-derived row vector `E_t = Linear_E(x_t)` pick a *mixture over* them, so the signal that sets the horizon is not the signal being stored and cannot alter any horizon, which is the case [[wiki/entities/cfc.md]] named as its missing test and [[wiki/entities/ltc.md]] cannot express. Its ablation states the design rule: the selector must read the **raw** stream, not a band's own representation, because a band-limited channel cannot judge its own relevance. What is still missing is the *inter-module* version — MS-SSM's timing channel is internal to one layer and there is no third module broadcasting it

**Two additions from the wiki's best-characterised edge, and neither closes the row** (Shin & Jadhav 2016, [[wiki/entities/hippocampal-prefrontal-channel.md]]). First, the channel types are separated not only by band but by **behavioural epoch** — theta during locomotion, sharp-wave ripples during immobility, mutually exclusive in time — and the selector is running speed, a global state scalar computed by neither endpoint. That is a cheaper mechanism than the row's proposed third broadcasting module: a state variable both endpoints already observe can select the channel without any of them negotiating, and no wiki architecture has even that. Second, the offline channel's read rule is measured and it is **two-sided**: ripple-coordinated reactivation *excites* prefrontal cells whose representation matches the replayed sequence and *suppresses* those whose does not, with eligibility set by which pairs were coactive in the *theta* mode earlier in the session. So the content channel here is not an additive delivery but a match-gated contrast operation on the receiver — a third channel type the row's content/timing dichotomy does not cover. **What still blocks closure**: no mode has ever been perturbed *as a mode*. Every causal result cuts the anatomy or silences a region for a block of time; the discriminating design (`I41`, [[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]]) — real-time detection of the pattern gating a perturbation to only those events — has been run within the hippocampus and never on an inter-regional coupling.

**A cortical target where the separation is visible in one region's own numbers** (Alexander et al. 2023, [[wiki/entities/retrosplenial-cortex.md]]). Retrosplenial local-field theta is largely *coherent* with CA1 pyramidal-layer theta, and ~35% of dysgranular and ~65% of granular cells are phase-modulated by it — yet only **~5%** of retrosplenial cells fire rhythmically across a continuous stream of theta cycles. A region can therefore be fully entrained by a rhythm it does not reproduce in its spiking: the shared clock rides the field potential and the local content code stays non-rhythmic, which is this row's split observed rather than engineered. Two further details make the coordination channel *directional* rather than merely shared: retrosplenial gamma bursts are phase-shifted relative to the matching CA1 frequencies, and a subset of retrosplenial and subicular cells (including egocentric boundary vector cells) spike at **early** theta phases, ahead of the rest of the sequence — the position a source of top-down constraint would occupy. What is still missing for the row is the machine version: no architecture here has a timing input that modulates when a module's units are readable without contributing to what they encode.

**One structure in which the content wire and the timing wire are anatomically separate and each has its own lesion** (Vann & Aggleton 2004, [[wiki/entities/mammillary-bodies.md]]). The mammillary bodies send everything to the anterior thalamus on one tract, in two parallel sub-populations. The lateral nucleus carries **heading**, a represented variable, to `AD`. Bilateral lesions of it abolish `AD` head-direction tuning, and hippocampal lesions leave that tuning intact. The medial nucleus carries **hippocampal theta**, a rhythm with no represented variable, to `AV`, where ~75% of cells are theta-locked. Septal inactivation removes that theta and leaves the content channel's source untouched. So this is the row's "content channel plus coordination channel", sent from one sender to one receiver on separate wires and separable by lesion. It improves on the previous entries in one respect. Here the timing channel is a **dedicated relay** that exports the store's clock to a consumer, not a field potential the consumer happens to share. Three limits. (i) The segregation is complete for heading and **leaky** for theta (a few theta cells in `LMN` and `AD`). (ii) Nobody has shown that cutting the theta relay alone, with the direct hippocampal theta path into the thalamus intact, changes the receiver; the source calls the relay possibly redundant. (iii) It is biology. The row's `Closes when` asks for a machine.

**Where the two wires merge** (Jankowski et al. 2013, [[wiki/entities/anterior-thalamic-nuclei.md]]). The heading and theta systems stay segregated from the tegmentum through the mammillary bodies. One synapse later, 39% of `AV` head-direction cells fire their heading only in theta-paced spike trains ("head-direction-by-theta" cells, Tsanov et al. 2011a). So the biology separates content from clock on the wire and **multiplies them in the receiver**: `h(heading) · c(theta phase)`. The same conjunction appears in pre-/parasubiculum. Two consequences for the row. (i) Separate ablatability holds only upstream of the merge. Downstream of `AV`, nothing can remove timing without removing content, so the row's `Closes when` describes a property of a *segment* of a pathway, not of a pathway. (ii) The merge gives the machine version a concrete form: a receiver that gates content units by a broadcast phase, so that its own output is readable only in one phase window. Still biology, so `Closes when` is unmet.
