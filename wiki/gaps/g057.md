# G57 — No architecture makes its teaching signal one of its own inputs

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** The same scalar drives a plasticity update and enters the state as an observation in one system, with the two roles independently ablatable and both shown to be load-bearing.

## Why it blocks the target

Every learning signal in the wiki is consumed by a plasticity rule and nowhere else: a reward scalar, a prediction error, a gating term. Biology may double it up — feeding the dopaminergic reward-prediction error into the prefrontal network *as an observation* reproduces the same six behavioural results as feeding raw reward, and it changes what an intervention means: optogenetically blocking or inducing dopamine then shifts behaviour **with synaptic weights held constant**, because the manipulation edits the inner learner's input rather than its parameters (Wang et al. 2018). No model here can be perturbed that way, so no model can express the distinction between "the teacher changed my weights" and "the teacher told me something"

## Best current answer

Partial, and only in one direction: a reward-prediction error injected as an input reproduces bandit, reversal, two-step and Harlow performance, and reinterprets optogenetic dopamine manipulation as an *activity* effect rather than a plasticity effect. What is missing everywhere is the pairing — a system in which the same scalar simultaneously drives the update and enters the state, and in which the two roles can be lesioned apart
