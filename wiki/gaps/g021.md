# G21 — No mechanism composes the outputs of two specialized modules

**Status:** `CONTESTED`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** Two encapsulated modules with different node types and edge vocabularies produce a joint output on an input neither can handle alone, with the composer learned rather than hand-wired, and the three rival accounts (syntax, language of thought, relaxation) discriminated by the result.

## Why it blocks the target

Core knowledge shows a meta-graph can be *pre-installed* — six domain fragments with their own node types, edge vocabularies and constraints — but the fragments are encapsulated and compete for a limited attentional resource, so nothing joins them. A content such as "Mary broke the five bottles she liked" needs number, object, agent and social outputs in one representation. Modularity is the wiki's standing architectural stance ([[wiki/concepts/attention.md]]), and it has no binding operation. Worse, if language supplies the common format, then core concepts are a *different kind* from the concepts used as building blocks, so the priors cannot serve as compositional primitives without an unspecified translation layer (the equivocation problem)

## Best current answer

Three rivals now. (a) natural-language syntax + compositional semantics (Spelke 2022); (b) an innate language of thought whose format the core systems already speak (Revencu & Csibra 2023); (c) **compose by relaxation** — hold both concepts' predictive encodings active and let mutual constraint satisfaction settle into a joint free-energy minimum, with the relative spatial encoding as the binding term ("a ball lies in a bowl": the two temporal interaction predictions cancel, which *is* the semantics of "lies"). (c) needs no shared symbolic format at all, only that the fragments predict into each other — but the relaxation is intractable in general and is approximated by local adjustment, and low energy is a consistency criterion, not a truth criterion **A benchmark now targets this row directly** ([[wiki/entities/arc-agi-2.md]]): *multi-rule compositional reasoning* tasks apply several rules **simultaneously and interactively** (crop to a frame, rescale loose objects, insert each into the hole of matching shape), and *contextual rule application* tasks gate a transformation on a cue elsewhere in the grid, which requires composing a transformation rule with a **selection** rule. The measured consequence: systems that reliably discover one global rule fail here, and the 2024 ARC-AGI-1 winner carries over at 2.5%. **A fourth rival, and the only one implemented and measured** (Andreas et al. 2016, [[wiki/entities/neural-module-networks.md]]): compose through a **declared common type** — every module emits and consumes an unnormalised attention map, so composition is legal exactly when the signatures agree, checkable before anything runs. It is neither syntax (a), nor a language of thought (b), nor relaxation (c): there is no shared symbolic format and no energy to minimise, only an interface contract. It buys depth extrapolation that no learned composer here achieves (withholding all 6-module layouts *raises* 6-module accuracy, 85.2 → 89.7). Two reasons it does not close the row: the composer is hand-wired (a dependency parser), which the `Closes when` explicitly excludes; and the modules are *not* encapsulated in the core-knowledge sense — they are trained jointly on one objective in one format, so nothing here shows two independently-acquired vocabularies being joined.
