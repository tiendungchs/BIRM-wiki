# G21 — No mechanism composes the outputs of two specialized modules

**Status:** `CONTESTED`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Spec:** `D80`, `D137`, `D183`, `D208`, `D235`, `D240`, `D244`, `D254` ([[_brainstorm/birm-spec.md]] §11)  
**Closes when:** Two encapsulated modules with different node types and edge vocabularies produce a joint output on an input neither can handle alone, with the composer learned rather than hand-wired, and the three rival accounts (syntax, language of thought, relaxation) discriminated by the result.

## Why it blocks the target

Core knowledge shows a meta-graph can be *pre-installed* — six domain fragments with their own node types, edge vocabularies and constraints — but the fragments are encapsulated and compete for a limited attentional resource, so nothing joins them. A content such as "Mary broke the five bottles she liked" needs number, object, agent and social outputs in one representation. Modularity is the wiki's standing architectural stance ([[wiki/concepts/attention.md]]), and it has no binding operation. Worse, if language supplies the common format, then core concepts are a *different kind* from the concepts used as building blocks, so the priors cannot serve as compositional primitives without an unspecified translation layer (the equivocation problem)

## Best current answer

Three rivals now. (a) natural-language syntax + compositional semantics (Spelke 2022); (b) an innate language of thought whose format the core systems already speak (Revencu & Csibra 2023); (c) **compose by relaxation** — hold both concepts' predictive encodings active and let mutual constraint satisfaction settle into a joint free-energy minimum, with the relative spatial encoding as the binding term ("a ball lies in a bowl": the two temporal interaction predictions cancel, which *is* the semantics of "lies"). (c) needs no shared symbolic format at all, only that the fragments predict into each other — but the relaxation is intractable in general and is approximated by local adjustment, and low energy is a consistency criterion, not a truth criterion **A benchmark now targets this row directly** ([[wiki/entities/arc-agi-2.md]]): *multi-rule compositional reasoning* tasks apply several rules **simultaneously and interactively** (crop to a frame, rescale loose objects, insert each into the hole of matching shape), and *contextual rule application* tasks gate a transformation on a cue elsewhere in the grid, which requires composing a transformation rule with a **selection** rule. The measured consequence: systems that reliably discover one global rule fail here, and the 2024 ARC-AGI-1 winner carries over at 2.5%. **A fourth rival, and the only one implemented and measured** (Andreas et al. 2016, [[wiki/entities/neural-module-networks.md]]): compose through a **declared common type** — every module emits and consumes an unnormalised attention map, so composition is legal exactly when the signatures agree, checkable before anything runs. It is neither syntax (a), nor a language of thought (b), nor relaxation (c): there is no shared symbolic format and no energy to minimise, only an interface contract. It buys depth extrapolation that no learned composer here achieves (withholding all 6-module layouts *raises* 6-module accuracy, 85.2 → 89.7). Two reasons it does not close the row: the composer is hand-wired (a dependency parser), which the `Closes when` explicitly excludes; and the modules are *not* encapsulated in the core-knowledge sense — they are trained jointly on one objective in one format, so nothing here shows two independently-acquired vocabularies being joined. **A fifth rival, and the only one whose composer is a *transform* rather than a binder** (Chau et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/lateral-frontal-pole.md]]): do not join the modules' outputs at all — project each into a **shared low-dimensional feature basis** first, and let an older, simpler region sum the projections. The human lateral frontal pole is proposed to run several such projections in parallel with filter weights set top-down by mid-dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, and to route the resulting feature branches to posterior cingulate cortex when they describe one option and to anterior cingulate cortex when they describe alternatives. Composition is then legal because everything has been rewritten into one basis, which is (d)'s interface contract with the contract *learned and task-conditioned* rather than declared. The evidence is a filter-count ablation — network↔region representational similarity falls monotonically as four decomposition filters are cut toward one, so the plurality of heads is doing the work — plus a double dissociation against ventromedial prefrontal cortex on whether the decomposition is automatic. It does not close the row for the reason (d) does not: the projected sources are not independently-acquired encapsulated vocabularies but attributes of one stimulus set, and no result here shows two module outputs of *different node type* surviving the projection with their structure intact. **The composer-as-fan-in reading now has a dose-response measurement, and it is the only quantitative one on this row** (Urbanski et al. 2016, [[wiki/entities/lateral-frontoparietal-network.md]]). In 27 focal frontal-lesion patients, the site whose damage specifically impairs analogy is **0.33 cc** of left Brodmann area 10/47, and the severity is predicted not by lesion volume but by *how many of that site's own afferent tracts were cut* — occipital (inferior fronto-occipital fasciculus), temporal (uncinate), thalamic (anterior thalamic radiation), fronto-marginal — `rs = −0.553`, `P = 0.003`, with the count of severed tracts **not** connected to the site predicting nothing while itself correlating with lesion volume. That is composition-by-convergence stated as a measurable: the composer is small, its identity is its afferent set, and no single feed is sufficient to break it. It does not close the row, for the reason the others do not — the converging feeds are sensory streams into one task, not two independently-acquired encapsulated vocabularies — but it converts (d)'s and (e)'s interface story into a prediction the wiki can test on a model: cut afferents into a candidate composition site one at a time and the deficit should be graded in the *number* cut, not in which one. **And the fan-in is now measured as *switchable* rather than inferred** (Wendelken, Chung & Bunge 2012, [[wiki/entities/lateral-frontoparietal-network.md]]): one 177-voxel left rostrolateral seed, defined as the intersection of the semantic and visuospatial second-order clusters so that it is domain-general by construction, couples to **left middle temporal gyrus** when the relations being composed are semantic and to **right dorsolateral prefrontal cortex** when they are visuospatial — same subjects, same trials, same seed, partners set by content. That is (d)'s and (e)'s interface contract with the *binding* held fixed and the *fan-in* re-pointed, which is the only form of composer on this row whose flexibility does not require a new module per content type. It does not close the row and it adds a new liability: in the same data the two domains' peaks are offset *within* the seed region, so the site may be a tiled family of fixed-input composers rather than one re-pointable composer, and only the second is a composer in this row's sense (`T338`).
