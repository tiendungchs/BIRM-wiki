# G66 — No sampler draws training pairs with respect to the quantity being learned

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A sampler draws training pairs from the volume element induced by the target quantity, replacing tuned augmentation or hand-swept spreads, and the derived density beats the tuned one. Special case of `G32`.

## Why it blocks the target

Every learner in the wiki that trains on *pairs* — every joint-embedding method, every learned similarity, every contrastive store — fixes its pair distribution by a convention (uniform over a parameterisation, a crop/blur augmentation policy, `k`-hop BFS from a random anchor) chosen before the target relation is known. Over a continuous manifold that choice *is* the coverage: uniform in `λ₀` starves the high-mass region, uniform in `(m₁,m₂)` starves the low-mass one, and no chart covers both, so the amortiser's failures are regional and a test set drawn from the same sampler cannot see them. The principled measure is the volume element induced by the target metric itself, which nobody computes

## Best current answer

Hand-tuning. [[wiki/entities/learningmatch.md]] adds a "diffused" sampler — a second template drawn at three hand-swept standard-deviation combinations, chosen "to identify which combination produced the largest number of match values close to 1" — which supplies 60% of the training pairs (1.5M of 2.5M) and is the closest thing in the wiki to metric-aware sampling; it is still three tuned scalars. [[wiki/entities/neuromatch.md]]'s curriculum (grow query radius on plateau) is the structural analogue, worth +6%. **The row's sharpest measurement is now at the *episode* level rather than the pair level** ([[wiki/entities/mlc.md]]): a meta-training sampler that resamples word→meaning assignments buys systematicity outright (0% → 100% with the architecture unchanged) and buys *nothing* on the facet it never varies — 100% error on SCAN's length split and three COGS structural types, against 77.8% length extrapolation in the task whose episodes did vary length. Validation episodes drawn from the same sampler (new grammars, deduplicated modulo rule-order and symbol permutations) pass, so the hole is invisible from inside; the sampler decides *which kind* of generalization the system has at all. **The same hole recurs one level down, at the arguments of an installed bias**: the sampler that buys mutual exclusivity at the human marginal rate buys it *unconditionally*, so the model applies it in 98.0% of samples where people apply it in 68.2% and grade it by counter-evidence and pool size — and recovering the gradient required both re-optimization and a new input channel for the conditioning variable (Lake & Baroni 2023 SI-2, [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T202)
