# G86 — Every architecture in the wiki tunes its stopping rule and its memory separately, and nothing couples them

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** One run-time-settable scalar couples a stopping rule to memory robustness in a trained architecture, with the accuracy half of the trade measured on behaviour rather than simulated.

## Why it blocks the target

A reasoning system needs *paired* settings, not independent ones: while it is integrating evidence slowly on a hard sub-problem, its intermediate results must survive interference; while it is answering fast, they should be cheap to overwrite. The wiki's decision machinery sets a threshold ([[wiki/concepts/evidence-accumulation.md]]) and its stores set a write gate or a decay constant ([[wiki/concepts/working-memory.md]], [[wiki/concepts/spike-frequency-adaptation.md]]), and no page links the two. Tuned apart, the two failure modes compound — a fast configuration commits early *and* has already lost the partial result it should have re-used — and the compound failure is invisible to any benchmark that scores only the final answer

## Best current answer

One scalar does both, in the right direction: the amplitude of the synaptic drive shared by a frontoparietal decision circuit and its persistent-activity store. Lowering it lengthens evidence integration and raises accuracy, and simultaneously raises the induction *and* disruption thresholds of the memory, so the deliberating configuration is also the hard-to-clobber one. Upstream it is set by a per-edge long-range excitation/feedforward-inhibition ratio, which is fittable from an observable correlation statistic ([[wiki/concepts/excitation-inhibition-balance.md]])

## From

Schirner, Deco & Ritter 2023

## Status

`OPEN` — the coupling is demonstrated in a biophysical simulation and in no architecture. Two things are missing before it is importable: nothing sets the ratio *at run time* (it is fitted offline over 60 h of simulated biological time and then frozen, so it is a trait where the phenomenon it explains is a state), and the accuracy half of the trade is simulated rather than measured — mean functional connectivity predicts reaction time (`r = 0.13`) and has no relationship at all to intelligence-test score (`r = 0.02`, `p = 0.69`)
