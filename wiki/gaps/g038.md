# G38 — Nothing sets the separation/completion bias, and it is not a constant

**Status:** `PARTIAL`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Level:** `L2`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** The allocate-vs-reuse threshold is set at runtime by a signal the system computes, and the controlled version beats every fixed threshold across a range of input statistics the fixed setting cannot span.

## Why it blocks the target

Every fast store in the wiki fixes its own allocate-vs-reuse threshold at design time (sparsity level, `α` of a Dirichlet process, a nearest-neighbour radius). Biology treats it as a *runtime variable*, and the failure mode of getting it wrong is not forgetting but **confident over-generalization**: a store biased toward completion returns a neighbour instead of reporting a miss. Neurocognitive aging is the natural experiment — perforant-path degradation shifts the balance toward the CA3 recurrent network, producing representational rigidity that correlates with behavioural discrimination deficits in both rats and humans **Sibling of `G42`**, which asks the same store the capacity question this row asks the bias question: nothing knows what the bias should be and nothing knows when the store is full, and the two are the same missing capacity model read from two sides.

## Best current answer

**Two candidate control architectures, neither characterised as a function.** (i) *External and global*: cholinergic input from the medial septum switching the structure between storage (separation) and recall (completion) modes — a mode bit, the type of gating policy that has transferred well before ([[wiki/concepts/neuroscience-ai-transfer.md]]). (ii) *Internal and closed-loop*: hilar mossy cells (↑ separation) and perforant-path-targeting HIPP interneurons (↓ separation) driven by the CA3→DG backprojection, so how well the output stage completed the current cue sets how hard the input stage separates the next one. (ii) is the one a machine could adopt directly, because completion error is measurable at runtime without labels. **(iii) now with a functional form, from the same cholinergic candidate** (Rolls 2013, [[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]]): acetylcholine *reduces the efficacy of the CA3→CA3 recurrent synapses* while facilitating LTP in them, so one scalar simultaneously suppresses the recurrent read-out (stopping completion from dominating) and licenses the recurrent write. Directly copyable: tie recurrent read-out gain and recurrent learning rate to one variable, inversely. What is still missing: no objective the setting optimises, no rule for *when* the scalar should move, and no demonstration in any architecture. **Two more routes to the same knob, neither needing a neuromodulator** (Sanders et al. 2020, [[wiki/entities/hidden-state-inference-remapping.md]]): `α` could be *learned* from the complexity of past experience — an animal that has needed many states should adopt a larger `α` — and it could be *spatially graded*, with the dorsoventral gradient in place-field size read as one inference run in parallel at several `α`, so two observations share a state at one end of the axis and not at the other, giving partial sharing of learning rather than an all-or-nothing decision. That last is the only proposal in the wiki for running the allocate-vs-reuse decision at several resolutions **simultaneously**. Both are untested. The same source also supplies an unlabelled runtime estimate of the knob's setting: population *heterogeneity* peaks when the posterior is maximally uncertain. **And the knob is now at least measurable without labels or unit resolution** (Bakker et al. 2008): the lure bias score `(1st − Lure)/(1st − Repeat)` places any site on the separation↔completion axis from mean activity over three stimulus conditions (human CA3/DG 0.15, every other medial-temporal region 0.59–0.83), which a machine can compute per layer at runtime from self-generated novel/repeat/near-duplicate triplets. That converts the gap from "no observable" to "observable but uncontrolled" — an error signal exists, nothing yet descends it, and the estimator is blind wherever mean activity is normalised constant. **A third control architecture, and the first with an actuator verified per feature** (Valero et al. 2025, [[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]): the bias is not one knob but a small *vector*, driven by four genetically distinct interneuron families acting on the same excitatory population at spike timescale with no weight change — context generalization tracks *Sst*, selectivity tracks *Pvalb*/*Sst*/*Id2*, stability tracks *Pvalb*, space-rate information tracks *Vip* (by disinhibition), and activating one family alone during behaviour moves the named place-field feature, with the perturbation matrix correlating with the coupling-based correlational one (`p = 0.007`). Two things it adds that the neuromodulatory and closed-loop candidates cannot: *per-feature* resolution, so generalization and selectivity are independently addressable rather than yoked to one sparsity number; and a **routing rule that is not hand-set** — a channel generalizes across contexts when it is densely innervated by the excitatory population and splits them when sparsely innervated, so the assignment of channel to feature is a wiring property experience sets. What it still does not supply is the controller that drives the four channels, so the regress moves back one level rather than closing. **One channel now has its controller, and it is the wiki's first complete chain** (de Sousa et al. 2026, [[wiki/concepts/memory-allocation-excitability.md]]): for the episode-level integrate-vs-separate decision, the actuator is NDNF⁺ neurogliaform cells in the stratum lacunosum moleculare of CA1 and the driver is a vmPFC→MEC projection whose activity is high exactly in the cell of the 2×2 where separation is required (different contexts, 7 d apart) and low otherwise. It is bidirectional — inhibit and dCA1 ensemble overlap goes ~15% → ~25% with behavioural linking appearing; activate and it goes ~24% → ~12%, overriding local integration even at 5 h — with the number of active cells unchanged, so it moves *which* units are written, not how many. Four properties the other candidates lack: the controller is **outside the controlled circuit**, its input is a **similarity comparison against consolidated cortical content** rather than a local error, it acts **only after systems consolidation has matured the trace** (inert at 5 h, load-bearing at 7 d), and it is applied in **CA1** rather than DG/CA3. What it still does not give: the function vmPFC computes to set its own gain, so the regress moves to cortex rather than closing; and it is demonstrably engaged only for episodes that share features (no effect for context vs home cage, or context vs a different hippocampal task). **A fifth answer, and the only one that is a *schedule* rather than a controller** (Kanerva 1993, [[wiki/entities/sparse-distributed-memory.md]]): in a store whose separation is produced by expansion plus a threshold, the signal-to-noise-optimal fraction of active units has a closed form, `p = (2MT)^(−1/3)`, so the correct bias **falls as the store fills** and does so on a known exponent. Every other candidate treats the set point as fixed and hunts for the machinery that tracks it; this one says the set point moves with load, and a machine store knows its own `T` exactly — no error signal, no neuromodulator, one line. Its limits are stated in the same source: the derivation assumes uniform random data, the optimum shifts up ~2× for noisy retrieval cues and *down* for clustered data, and for real (clustered) data the fix is not a different `p` but data-dependent addressing (hard addresses drawn from the data distribution, or a per-location radius `H_m`), which is the same knob turned per unit rather than globally. The source also supplies biology's runtime version at a set point: Golgi-cell feedback holding 500–5,000 of 200,000 granule cells active regardless of input activity **And the first machine store that makes it a runtime variable at all** (Graves et al. 2016, [[wiki/entities/differentiable-neural-computer.md]]): the write weighting is `w^w = g^w [g^a a_t + (1 − g^a) c^w]`, so one scalar emitted by the controller each step interpolates between allocating the least-used location (separate) and editing the content-matched one (complete), with a usage vector as the novelty signal and free gates that release a location once it has been read. It is the existence proof that the knob can be *learned and dynamic* rather than set at design time — and it is the wrong kind of learning for this row: `g^a` is trained by backpropagation through time against task loss, so it is not driven by completion error, cannot be set without a differentiable objective over the whole episode, and carries no runtime estimate of whether the current setting is right. **And the knob now has a *wiring* correlate rather than a neuromodulatory or a fitted one** (Collins & Frank 2013, [[wiki/entities/c-ts-model.md]]): parametrically varying the context→prefrontal projection between fully random and one-to-one moves the recovered Dirichlet `α` (`r = 0.76, p < 2×10⁻⁴`) and loads on no other parameter — a random projection lets several contexts converge on one abstract state (reuse), a topographic one forces a fresh state per context (separate). This is the first entry in the row where the separation/completion setting is a readable property of a weight matrix rather than a scalar someone chose, which makes it in principle experience-settable; what is still absent is anything that *sets* it, since the fitted `α` showed no individual differences at all (mean 4.5) while the structural priors did.  **The price of a hand-set threshold, measured** ([[wiki/entities/cn-dpm.md]], Lee et al. 2020). The gap's own example — "`α` of a Dirichlet process" — run end to end: on Split-MNIST `log α` = −600 yields 3.20 components and **54.04%**, `log α` = −400 yields 5.00 and **93.23%**, `log α` = 80 yields 14.4 and 93.54% at 2.7× the parameters. A 200-nat error in one scalar costs 39 accuracy points, and `α` is tuned per scenario *so that exactly the known number of tasks is produced* — the task count refused at training time re-enters through hyperparameter selection. The authors' only defence is a stationarity assumption (an `α` fitted on letters a–m should transfer to n–z because the between-task discrepancy is similar), with no mechanism that estimates or adapts it. A partial after-the-fact remedy does exist and is cheap: set `α` too high, then merge components whose per-datum log-likelihood vectors have cosine similarity > 0.9 (7 experts → 5, accuracy 87.07 → 86.01), which audits the store for redundancy with no labels and no held-out data.

## From

[[wiki/concepts/pattern-separation-completion.md]] (Yassa & Stark 2011); the runtime version of the constant that [[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]] fits and [[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]] leaves as a threshold; [[wiki/entities/hidden-state-inference-remapping.md]]  **A third setting of the knob, computed rather than controlled** (Ahmad & Hawkins 2016, [[wiki/concepts/sparse-distributed-representations.md]]): for a subsampling detector the bias *is* `θ/s`, and sweeping `n` from 10⁴ to 2×10⁵, activity from 0.5% to 3% and `s` from 20 to 50 leaves the error-minimising `θ` in a narrow band (9–20) across the whole space — so this knob is unusually insensitive to load, unlike SDM's `p ∝ (MT)^(−1/3)`. It also adds a hard precondition the controller cannot fix: at 50% density no `θ` gives robust recognition at any dimension, so the sparsity of the code must be regulated before the threshold is worth setting ([[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]). [[wiki/entities/differentiable-neural-computer.md]]; Collins & Frank 2013  **A sixth answer, and the only one where the decision is a *logical* operation on what is already stored** ([[wiki/entities/conceptor.md]], Jaeger 2014): with each stored item held as a soft subspace, what is new about a candidate is the Boolean difference `C^{new} \\ A = C^{new} ∧ ¬A` against the union of everything held, and it is written into the complement `¬A`. There is no threshold anywhere — the allocate/reuse split is *computed* per item from the geometry of the existing store, and it returns a graded answer (how much of the candidate is new) rather than a decision. Its limits are the same as its home: it needs the subspaces to be near-projectors, and it prices novelty in state-space directions, which is a statement about the *code* rather than about the world. [[wiki/entities/cn-dpm.md]] (Lee et al. 2020). **And a learned run-time version of the bias itself** ([[wiki/entities/ch-hnn.md]]): the overlap between the codes two inputs recruit is trained to equal the perceptual similarity of the inputs (`cos(R,R̃) → sim(x,x̃)`), with a separate term fixing the density at `ρ ≈ 1/n` — so separation-vs-completion is neither a constant nor a threshold but a *fitted function of the input pair*, decided per sample. It inherits the source of the similarity measure as its own open question: CLIP feature statistics where the episodes are naturally correlated, a hand-set 0/1 matrix where they are not
