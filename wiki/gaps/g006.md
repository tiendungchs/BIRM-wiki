# G6 — Spurious edges survive training — and are the default, not the exception

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** A learner separates causal from correlational edges without being handed the environment partition, and the separation holds on an out-of-distribution test where the shortcut is inverted.

## Why it blocks the target

Correlational edges work in-distribution and fail out of it. Documented across computer vision, language, reinforcement learning and algorithmic decision-making as one phenomenon; shortcut opportunities do *not* dilute as datasets scale by orders of magnitude, so "more data" is not a route. Invariance across environments is the stated fix, but it presupposes an environment partition the model is not given

## Best current answer

Invariant causal edge discovery; explicit intermediate-node traversal; domain generalisation/randomisation where several environments *are* given **The prestructured-scaffold family makes this maximal by construction** ([[wiki/entities/gcq.md]]): a bump on a torus can move in all four directions from every state, so a world model built on an installed manifold has *every* edge and no representation of a wall, a blocked corridor or an unreachable state. Environment adjacency survives only in the decoder's pixels, and a planner that scores latent distance has nothing to stop it descending into an obstacle. This is the same hole [[wiki/entities/spacetime-attractor.md]] fills for the discrete case with a learned transition *superset* inhibited by present sensory input — the wiki's only mechanism for a map that knows which of its edges are unavailable here.

**Formalised, with the assumption made explicit and priced** ([[wiki/concepts/environment-invariance.md]], Arjovsky et al. 2019): the target is a representation whose optimal classifier is the same in every environment, enforceable by a gradient-norm penalty at a fixed dummy classifier (IRMv1). Given `d − r + d/r` environments in linear general position, invariance across the training family is provably equivalent to invariance across all environments, and the invariant predictor is exactly the one using only the direct causal parents of the target. On Colored MNIST — where the colour/label correlation *reverses* at test — ERM scores 17.1% (below chance) and IRM 66.9% against a 73.0% grayscale oracle. **This does not close the row**: IRM is handed the environment partition, and the paper states that splitting a dataset on an arbitrary conditioning variable can manufacture spurious correlations and destroy the invariance of interest. It also fixes the row's price: the separation costs environment diversity linear in the parameter count of the representation, and the guarantee holds only for linear representations. A second negative result worth keeping — *robustness is not invariance*: a min-max objective over training environments is algebraically a weighted ERM (Prop. 2), so it interpolates between environments and never extrapolates

**The one candidate that does not need the partition** ([[wiki/concepts/independent-causal-mechanisms.md]], Schölkopf et al. 2021). The Sparse Mechanism Shift hypothesis — small distribution changes affect a sparse subset of factors in the *causal* factorization and many or all factors in any other — turns sparsity-of-change into a **score over candidate factorizations**, evaluable on an unlabelled non-stationary stream. Where invariance asks *given environments, find the stable representation*, this asks *given a representation, how many factors did that shift touch*. It has been used as a supervision signal in restricted settings and is reported theoretically sufficient for disentanglement given suitable training data (Lachapelle et al. 2022). **It does not close the row either, and the reason is circularity**: which factors are recoverable depends on which interventions can be observed, so a representation can score sparse merely because the available shifts were narrow — and no estimator of "sparseness of shift" exists for a learned model at scale. The complementary half is a mechanism that detects *that* a shift occurred ([[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]]); nothing in the wiki wires the two together
