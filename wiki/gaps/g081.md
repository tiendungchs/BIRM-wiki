# G81 — No graph-discovery mechanism in the wiki estimates edges through an observation model

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `part`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Why it blocks the target

Every discovery mechanism here — thresholded co-fluctuation ([[wiki/entities/hag-reservoir.md]]), inverse covariance ([[wiki/entities/fcann.md]]), attention as soft adjacency, transition counting — reads edges off a signal it treats as the latent state itself. Where the observable is a filtered, delayed, nonlinearly transformed and *per-node differently transformed* function of the state, those estimators do not degrade gracefully: the transform's regional variability is what makes lag-based direction estimates fail outright in fMRI. An agent that must discover structure from a sensory stream is in the harder case by default, and the wiki has no page in which the emission process is a fitted component

## Best current answer

The three-part generative template — neuronal model, observation model, optimiser — with the observation model an explicit swappable part (the Balloon haemodynamic chain, shared verbatim across families), and edges recovered by inverting the composite rather than by measuring the output. Direction, sign and within-node recurrence all become available at that moment, and none of them are available before it

## From

Li & Yap 2022; Honey et al. 2009

## Status

`OPEN` — solved in neuroimaging only because the emission process is known *a priori* from physiology; nothing here estimates an unknown observation model jointly with the graph, and the identifiability of doing so is untested. **The cost of not doing it is now measured**: reading edges off the co-statistic directly gives ≈6% precision at 80% recall on a 3%-dense graph, and ≈28% even when the generating couplings are exact, because the observable is a distance-weighted *path sum* `Σ_k w_ik w_kj` rather than the adjacency matrix — so the missing component is specifically a deconvolution from path-closed co-statistics back to edges, under a sparsity prior ([[wiki/concepts/function-to-structure-inference.md]])
