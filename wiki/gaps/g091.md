# G91 — The wiki has no gate that learns what deserves to be broadcast, and no architecture in it has a discrete commit at all

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Kind:** `arrangement`  
**Registry:** [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]  
**Closes when:** _unset — see [[wiki/priority-tasks.md]]_

## Why it blocks the target

A shared bus buys `O(n)` inter-module communication in place of `O(n²)` wiring, but only if something decides *which* content occupies it — and in every biological account that decision is thresholded, exclusive and dateable ([[wiki/concepts/ignition.md]]): one coalition takes the bus ~200–300 ms after input, suppresses its competitors, and holds itself by recurrence. Two things follow that nothing here supplies. **(a) No commit.** Every architecture in the wiki mixes continuously — attention softmaxes, precision scales, gating multiplies — so no intermediate result is ever *published* and there is no marker for "step `k` is finished", which is what serial computation over parallel modules requires. **(b) No learning rule for the gate.** The framework that names the mechanism concedes it: nothing says how the threshold is set, how a coalition becomes ignitable, or how credit reaches an admission decision whose payoff arrives many steps later. The gate is also the compute-allocation signal (workspace engagement rises on novel tasks, effortful execution and after errors), so getting it wrong costs on both axes

## Best current answer

Two partial answers, from different levels. **The threshold itself is not a free parameter**: in macaque V1/V4/dlPFC the signal-detection criterion is the bifurcation point of the recurrent cascade and the bias term is pre-stimulus gain, so it can be *set* by resting state rather than fitted (van Vugt et al. 2018), and the window in which a reciprocal network ignites at all is the balanced-amplification window ([[wiki/concepts/excitation-inhibition-balance.md]]). **The learning rule exists for a smaller store**: [[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]] trains a Go/NoGo write gate by reinforcement across delayed consequences, and its ablation result (no dopamine modulation → 0% of networks learn any task) says an ungated store is useless — applying it here would make "what is worth broadcasting" a learned policy over coalitions and predict content-specific ignition thresholds, which this source reports as untested. **A third answer removes the need to learn a threshold at all**, at the cost of an unguaranteed loop: if the modules are coupled at their latents so that inference across them is iterated message passing, then the commit criterion is *convergence* — publish the joint MAP estimate when the messages stop changing — which is content-dependent by construction, gives ambiguous inputs longer latencies for free, and makes non-convergence a usable novelty signal ([[wiki/entities/integrated-world-modeling-theory.md]], [[wiki/concepts/loopy-belief-propagation.md]]). It also answers (a) in principle, since an argmax over a converged posterior is a genuine publication rather than a mixture — but loopy message passing has no convergence guarantee, cortex affords only a handful of rounds per commit at realistic delays, and nothing in the proposal is implemented. Neither answer addresses (a): a trained gate on a continuous mixture is still a mixture. The one architectural consequence already priced is the failure mode — a spontaneous supra-threshold cascade is a false alarm indistinguishable downstream from a hit, so a system that gets its commit this way hallucinates by the mechanism it perceives by, and the only repair is at the propagation stage (`d′`), not at the gate **Half of (a) is now measured in biology, and it arrives with a new unknown** (Dwarakanath et al. 2023, [[wiki/concepts/cortical-state-bistability.md]]): a prefrontal commit with a linear amplitude ramp *and* a non-linear jump in recruited sites, a visible sub-threshold case (piecemeal perception — a switch that starts and fails, with the state antagonism absent), and a noise floor an order of magnitude below it. What it adds to the row is that the releasing event is **content-free** and leads the content change by ~300 ms, so "commit" and "which content" are separately schedulable — and that the gate free-runs with no input, which means a builder owes not only a threshold rule but a *period*. The handover itself needs no learned gate at all: opposite low-frequency phase locking (169.2° for the challenger, −147.6° for the incumbent, from ~750 ms out) moves excitability between populations with one scalar per unit and no weight change, which is the cheapest scheduler in the wiki and still has no rule for assigning the scalar. **A third thing the gate specification lacks, added by the preregistered test: *scope over content dimensions*.** Ferrante et al. 2025 (`raw/ferrante-2025-gnwt-iit-adversarial-testing.md`) found prefrontal cortex carrying stimulus **category** across task at ~70% while **identity** was absent for every category and **orientation** supported the null, in the same tissue and instrument — so what reaches the bus is a *subset of the dimensions of one content*, not merely one content among competitors. A learned admission gate therefore needs a per-dimension policy, and no wiki architecture has one ([[wiki/entities/integrated-information-theory.md]], [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T272, T274).
