## Why it blocks the target

Architectures are routinely justified by reconstruction, prediction or variance-explained against neural or task data, and the implicit premise is that fidelity buys function. Vohryzek et al. 2024 is the wiki's first direct test of that premise and it fails: four anatomical bases differ significantly in how well they reconstruct long-range functional connectivity and 47 task-activation maps, and the reconstructions from *all four* predict fluid intelligence and processing speed equally, with the prediction driven by brain state rather than by basis. If the fidelity→function link is not automatic, then every wiki page that ranks mechanisms by reconstruction error is ranking them on an axis with no demonstrated behavioural consequence

## Best current answer

None. The decoupling is reported, not explained. The two candidate readings are not separated anywhere: (i) the behavioural read-out (two HCP scores) is too coarse to resolve basis differences, or (ii) the differences sit in modes that carry no task-relevant variance. Distinguishing them needs a behavioural measure whose difficulty is graded, which [[wiki/concepts/integration-segregation-balance.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/excitation-inhibition-balance.md]] have and this benchmark does not
