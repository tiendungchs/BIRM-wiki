## The Central Thesis

**A reasoning model is a two-level graph learner: a slow level that acquires the *structure* a family of environments shares, and a fast level that binds one instance of it in a handful of observations — with the two related by a factorization `p = f(g, x)` of structural position from content. The wiki's position is that every component of this is buildable except the factorization, the interfaces between modules, and relations held as objects; that the factorization cannot be learned from data by any objective (it must be paid for in architecture); and that no instrument currently exists to certify it was obtained.**

Four independent arguments converge on the "cannot be learned" half, and they are the wiki's most load-bearing result because they arrive from different fields:

| Argument | Statement | Source of the argument |
|---|---|---|
| **Identifiability** | Shortcut and structural rules are equally consistent with any single environment, so no objective computed on one environment prefers the structural one (G16) | Shortcut learning ([[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]]) |
| **Insufficiency of the strongest bias** | The simplicity prior — provably sufficient for prediction — identifies a *short program*, not a factorization of it; the shortest program's internal variables need not correspond to the graph (G26) | Universal induction ([[wiki/concepts/universal-induction.md]]) |
| **Unreachability by the search operator** | Gradient descent optimises weights inside a fixed structure; the ingredients wanted are structural, and no algorithmic procedure searches over them (G29) | Causal model building ([[wiki/concepts/causal-model-building.md]]) |
| **Empty objective slot** | A design is an objective + a learning rule + an architecture; no quantity is known that is maximised when `g` is path-consistent and minimised when it is content-contaminated, so there is nothing for a rule to ascend (G30) | Three-component framework ([[wiki/concepts/three-component-framework.md]]) |

And a measured counterexample closes the loop: a 70 B model compresses ImageNet patches better than PNG while having no object, depth or occlusion structure at all — world-class code length with none of the graph recovered.

**The corollary the wiki operates under:** since the target must be imposed rather than discovered, biology's value is as a *prior over architectures* — a library of mechanisms that were selected for solving this problem under a sample budget — and the research programme is to work out which of them are load-bearing and at what price.

**The amendment the control-layer literature forces.** The thesis is about *representations*, and thirty-five sources on working memory and prefrontal cortex were about something else: what reads them, what writes them, and what runs between them. The wiki's inventory of representations is now much better than its inventory of interfaces, and the gap table has moved accordingly — of the fourteen gaps opened in that stretch (G47–G60), eleven are about a **read, an edge, or a level boundary**, and none is about a code. The corollary is that "paid for in architecture" was under-read: the architecture that has to be paid for is not only the code's format, it is the protocol by which one module addresses another.

**The amendment the world-model literature forces, and it is a harder one.** The four arguments above say no objective *prefers* the factorization. Self-supervised representation learning supplies the sharper version: **the objective slot has a degenerate solution that every candidate for it satisfies.** A constant structural code is path-consistent trivially — commutativity holds when every path lands on the same point — so G34 (representational collapse) is not a neighbouring row in the gap table but a **filter every candidate for G30 must pass first**, and the self-supervised literature is the wiki's only quantitative body of evidence on how a learner finds the cheapest rule its objective admits ([[wiki/concepts/representational-collapse.md]]). Three further narrowings arrive with it, and together they change what "certified by construction" can mean:

| Narrowing | Statement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Target, not trajectory | A loss whose minimum is provably non-collapsed still collapses over most of its coefficient space | [[wiki/entities/vicreg.md]] |
| Per read-out, not global | One encoder, one correctly-minimised objective, 1M iterations: the image-level probe rises monotonically while the patch-level probe ends *below its own early value* | [[wiki/entities/dinov3.md]] |
| Possibly not from the loss at all | A supervised cross-entropy with **no** degenerate minimum collapses anyway (20.73% top-1) and is repaired by a covariance term, which makes the whole anti-collapse apparatus a *drift control* on the optimiser rather than a shaping of the objective's minimum | [[wiki/entities/lejepa.md]] (Akbar 2026) |

And the largest lever of all is not in the objective: holding loss, architecture and dataset fixed and varying only **which pairs are sampled** moves ImageNet-1% linear evaluation from 15.5 to 54.2 ([[wiki/entities/i-jepa.md]]) — more than the entire spread across the four anti-collapse families the wiki spent thirteen ingests cataloguing.

**The amendment the comparative literature forces, and it changes what "a reasoning model" means.** The thesis as stated specifies a learner at the level every animal taxon examined already reaches: discrete enduring representations, one-shot update, compositionality and featural systematicity are all delivered by a two-level graph learner, and nothing in the comparative record puts any taxon above that line ([[wiki/concepts/relational-reinterpretation.md]]). The four properties claimed human-only rest on one further operation, treating a relation as an object whose roles stay addressable while bound. The wiki now holds preservation-during-binding — [[wiki/entities/esbn.md]] by never forming a compound, [[wiki/concepts/activity-slots.md]] with a linear algebra left on the bound code — but in every candidate the role is a *slot*, never a type that can be reasoned about apart from its filler (G104 `PARTIAL`, G99; T293 on whether an invertible code with scheduled unbinding suffices). The "everything else is buildable" clause above is therefore too strong by at least two components, and the anatomy agrees: the one structural novelty in the human relational network is an edge with no macaque analogue, not a region ([[wiki/entities/lateral-frontoparietal-network.md]]), and whether that edge is a new operator or a re-weighting is live (T289). Two consequences. A world model supplies simulation, not causality: its edges are the environment's generative steps only if the factorization was obtained, which is the certification problem again ([[wiki/concepts/causal-model-building.md]]). And recognising a stored structure inside a new situation, subgraph matching, is assumed by every schema mechanism here and has no gap row ([[wiki/concepts/subgraph-matching.md]]).

**The amendment the cortical-channel literature forces: the interface is partly inside the unit, and the level is not given.** The thesis treats levels and modules as primitives and the protocol between them as the unbuilt part. Waves 22–23 measure that protocol at sub-module grain. Association between streams is a site in one pyramidal cell, with its coupling written by a third structure ([[wiki/concepts/apical-amplification.md]], `T376`). The store's read port is partitioned by cell type, developmentally ([[wiki/entities/subiculum.md]]). A plasticity rule belongs to a fibre of origin, not to a target. And the hierarchy coordinate a "level" presupposes is reproducible only in the sensory streams (`T378` `LIVE`). "Paid for in architecture" therefore includes cell types and their gates (section 24).

## Master Problem Framing: Latent Graph Discovery

**Infer the structure of a relational graph from observations, then navigate it — where the graph is never given and must be recovered from partial, aliased, or sequential evidence.** Full statement, taxonomy and the six sources of hardness: [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]].

Four amendments the ingests have forced on the framing itself, all of which sharpen it rather than replace it:

- **The node set is assumed, not given** (G27). Every element of the graph formalisation presupposes discretised experience; a continuous sensorimotor stream supplies none. The two candidate discretisers — [[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]] (has the representation, no criterion) and [[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]] (has a principled criterion, nodes that are single scalars) — are exact complements and have never been combined.
- **The manifold is assumed too** (G47). Every continuous attractor in the wiki has its topology built in by translation-invariant recurrence — a ring for heading, a torus for grid modules, a plane for a concept space — so the space the graph is *embedded in* is a designer statement made before any data arrive, and the prestructured stores make it deliberately more rigid because that is what buys convex basins and exponential capacity.
- **One node set can carry several graphs at once.** The same 12 objects carry a learned transition graph and a lifetime semantic taxonomy, and the hippocampal formation holds both in non-overlapping territory with no conjunctive code. "The" graph is a modelling assumption.
- **The traversal is not atomic.** A single step is time-multiplexed inside itself: which excitatory source drives the code is handed over mid-node by two different inhibitory channels (entorhinal prediction on entry, recurrent retrieval on exit), so "estimate the graph" and "read the stored transition" are *phases within one step* rather than separable stages ([[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]).
- **The measured biological estimate is weaker than the topology and stronger than the experience.** It is a traffic-weighted predictive metric (successor-representation-like), acquired incidentally with no reward or task; it is *symmetric* even where the transitions were not; and it contains composed edges the agent never traversed, written offline during awake ripples, in the direction of credit rather than of use.

## Current Best Understanding

### 1. The two-timescale split is solved; the factorization is not

Fast/slow separation arrives independently from biology ([[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]]) and from optimization ([[wiki/concepts/meta-learning.md]]), and both are instantiated in working machines. The factorized *code* is not: where the split works it was **written down by hand** — a hyperprior with per-agent parameters adapts by exact conjugate update in 8 trials ([[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]]); a store with exponential capacity and provably convex basins has its grid modules, periods and torus topology all installed ([[wiki/entities/vector-hash.md]]). The cleanest statement available: **capacity and structure discovery are separable problems, and solving the first buys nothing toward the second.**

### 2. Structural codes exist, and their preconditions are now known

`g` has a mechanism — path integration accumulates action displacements so equal total displacement lands on the same code *by construction*, in four model families and three animal phyla ([[wiki/concepts/path-integration.md]], [[wiki/concepts/abstract-structural-codes.md]]). It has an external validation: across 203 developing humans, structural-code strength predicts matrix-reasoning score and training-to-criterion. And it has three hard preconditions the wiki now states explicitly: the domain's **actions must compose** (G41 — a bare social network has no closure and the mechanism is simply unavailable), the space must be embeddable (G11), and the manifold's topology must be known in advance (G47). The abstract-domain evidence is drawn almost entirely from hand-designed continuous 2-D planes, i.e. exactly the regime where all three hold trivially — so "maps outside space" is well evidenced and "grids outside space" is not ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T4, T44).

### 3. Relaxation is the wiki's most reusable primitive

[[wiki/concepts/attractor-dynamics.md]] — settling onto weight-defined fixed points — serves retrieval, de-aliasing, maintenance, sequence generation and path integration with one operation, at a cost independent of library size. Its regimes are typed, its capacity is closed-form, and its overload behaviour is a *design choice* rather than a fact. It is also the only family in the wiki with a self-test that needs no data. The control literature added the strongest version of the claim: a **plan can be a fixed point rather than a search** — one attractor population per future timestep, so the whole action sequence settles at once at a cost linear in horizon ([[wiki/entities/spacetime-attractor.md]]) — and the normative literature added the generative counterpart, where a hierarchical dynamical model is a cascade of attractors in which the manifold says *which sequence* and the state says *where in it* ([[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]]). What relaxation still does not supply is edges, a certificate, or similarity structure over what it stores.

### 4. Memory has numbers; the rest of the architecture does not

The fast store is the one component with derived capacities (`p_max ≈ k·C/(a ln(1/a))`), a derived sparsity schedule, a per-retrieval confidence read-out (`\|s_u\|`), a separate *sequence* capacity that scales differently from item capacity, a priced consolidation channel, and now a capacity that is **conditional on an externally supplied context mask** (≈7× the Hopfield limit under random neuronal gating, ≈30–40× under a post-hoc sign-test refinement — but only if the mask is free). No other part of any architecture here is quantified to that standard.

### 5. The unbuilt pieces are policies over representations — and, more specifically, interfaces

The wiki's open gaps cluster with striking regularity: the *mechanisms* exist and the thing that **sets their one free parameter** does not. When to roll out and how broadly (G15); allocate vs. reuse (G38); which stored structure applies (G37); which subgoal to plan toward (G33). The control layer kept the shape and moved its location — from the middle of a module to its boundary:

| Boundary | What biology does that no architecture here does | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| **The read** | Content is *made ready* several hundred ms before it is queried, for the specific item about to be queried; the read is cancelled when an earlier result makes it unnecessary; and erasure is addressed by relevance rather than by age or usage | G49 |
| **The query** | The template register holds whatever currently maximises expected guidance value — a learned *associate* when the target is hard to discriminate, with the target itself decodable nowhere. The query is chosen, not `encode(goal)` | G60 |
| **The pointer** | What the controller is currently pointing at is a different register from what it is holding, and a delay signal can be a pointer with no item behind it | G48 |
| **The edge** | An inter-module connection carries four separable state variables — weight, terminal gain, writability licensed by a *third* module, and a stress-set operating point — and it multiplexes content (gamma) and timing (theta) as separately lesionable channels | G52, G53, G54 |
| **The level boundary** | A lower level receives the resolved output and the protocol and is architecturally denied the observation that produced them, so the bypass mapping feudal architectures always find is unavailable by wiring rather than by loss | G59 |
| **The controller's own supply** | The control layer projects onto the neuromodulatory cell groups that set its own gain, and the teaching signal is fed back in as an *observation* as well as consumed by a plasticity rule | G50, G57 |
| **The controller's own extent** | The population carrying a control level exists only while that level has a live choice; it shrinks under single-tactic retraining and returns when the choice is restored | G58 |

**The sharpest single lesson for a builder, updated: the missing pieces are not representations, and not even policies over representations — they are protocols between modules.** Every one of the rows above is cheap to implement, and only one has even a partial machine instance (G54, [[wiki/entities/ms-ssm.md]]'s content-selected horizon bank).

### 6. The controller is factorized on three orthogonal axes at once

The control layer's most transferable structural result, because the three cuts are independent and compose into one specification:

| Axis | Partition | Evidence that it is real |
|---|---|---|
| **By operation** | One common component + updating-specific + shifting-specific, and **no** inhibition-specific component | Separate genetic influences, separate receptor dependences (D1 working memory vs. D2 flexibility), double-dissociable lesion sites ([[wiki/concepts/control-unity-and-diversity.md]]) |
| **By output port** | Which code a cell carries is selected by where it projects, and is invisible in the firing rate; two projection classes in the same tissue are indistinguishable by rate and opposite in geometry | Projection-typed recording in mouse medial wall ([[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]], [[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]) |
| **By order of abstraction** | Rostro-caudal levels, searched **in parallel from the first trial** and pruned by reward, each engaged only while it has a live decision | Order-specific frontal activation, tactic-selective medial premotor populations that vanish under single-tactic retraining ([[wiki/concepts/policy-abstraction-hierarchy.md]]) |

Wiki architectures sit at two extremes — one monolithic controller, or `n` fully independent ones — and none has the middle: shared machinery plus operation-specific parameters (G55).

### 7. Credit assignment is no longer the blocker, and the objective slot has its first real candidates

Equilibrium propagation's ([[wiki/concepts/equilibrium-propagation.md]]) contrastive update is the **exact** gradient of a Helmholtz free-energy difference at any finite nudge ([[wiki/tensions/closed/t069.md]], retired as L3 detail; carried on [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]]), so for any architecture whose dynamics minimise an energy, objective and learning rule are filled by one choice. Two results from the normative literature tighten this. First, **the objective is a precision ratio and backpropagation is one setting of one scalar**: the predictive-coding objective interpolates continuously to the backpropagation gradient as `Σ^(0) → ∞`, and that limit is *anti-biological* (it drives error-node activity to zero, which spikes cannot encode) while the usable setting costs nothing measurable (T119). Second, **expected free energy decomposes into a linear reward plus one convex term, and curiosity is that term's gradient** — so exploration stops being a tuned bonus and becomes a derived quantity, with the whole planning problem falling into the convex-MDP class ([[wiki/concepts/expected-free-energy.md]]). Neither has been run against path-consistency, which is what G30 actually asks for. What survives from the credit-assignment literature is the sharper split: the family that solves *non-locality* assumes symmetric feedback weights, and the family that solves *weight transport* transports the load-bearing part (T3).

### 8. Measurement is still the binding constraint, and everything since has made it worse

Candidate instruments for certifying structure discovery (G17) — forty-seven in the current catalogue — and not one that works; the inventory, and the fifteen failure modes they share, are now [[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]] rather than a cell in the gap table. The control layer added a result that undercuts the most-used class outright: a meta-reinforcement-learning agent trained only by model-free updates reproduces the two-step task's canonical model-based behavioural signature, so **model-based behaviour does not license the inference that a model-based algorithm is running** (T105). Combined with the standing finding that probing confirms containment of a structure the experimenter already had, and the proof that no optimality certificate exists for any agent whose actions shape its data (G25), the position is: **behavioural signatures, probes and i.i.d. scores have each now been shown to admit a counterexample, and nothing has replaced them.** Two new instruments did arrive, both from the biology side and both unrun on machines: *learning-trajectory* matching (the order in which a learner disambiguates its aliased states, G46) and *profile* scoring (a family of unrelated-looking failures as the signature of one degraded channel).

### 9. The normative objectives finally have parts

Predictive coding and active inference had been carried as framings; twenty-two sources turned them into objects with separable, separately-testable components.

| Result | Content |
|---|---|
| **The objective is a precision ratio** | The predictive-coding objective interpolates continuously to the backpropagation gradient as `Σ^(0) → ∞`, and that limit is *anti-biological* (it drives error-node activity to zero) while the usable setting costs nothing measurable (T119). Precision is not a tuning constant but a **represented quantity** with its own substrate and timescale, which collapses attention, salience, value and learning rate into one number read in four places ([[wiki/concepts/precision-weighting.md]]) |
| **Expected free energy is reward plus exactly one convex term** | Written over state–action occupancies, planning falls into the **convex-MDP** class: an existence proof and an `O(1/K)` rate where relaxation on an energy landscape has neither, and curiosity becomes the gradient of the convex term rather than a tuned bonus ([[wiki/concepts/expected-free-energy.md]]) |
| **…and its epistemic term has two opposite readings** | With `EV` stored as explicit matrices, flattening the *likelihood* makes it fall (correct) and shifting the *prior* under fixed likelihood makes it fall too (degenerate) — so the decomposition every active-inference source builds on is sign-ambiguous depending on which distribution is moving ([[wiki/entities/deep-active-inference-agent.md]]) |
| **Task competence and model quality move in opposite directions** | On one ablation ladder the HMM taking uniformly random actions learns the dynamics almost perfectly and scores at chance; the reward-maximising variant solves the task and predicts worse. **Random play is the best data-collection policy for model learning and the worst for reward, and nothing in the objective arbitrates** |
| **Hyperparameters have the topology of neuromodulators** | Every hyperparameter is *global* by construction — it regulates how many ordinary parameters change — which is exactly the signature of a diffuse unaddressed broadcast, and the design move is to compute each set-point from second-order statistics of the learner's own signals ([[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]]) |
| **An option is a compiled path reified as one edge** | Which is the graph-level statement of temporal abstraction, and it is the wiki's only mechanism that *adds* a node's worth of structure rather than refining what is there ([[wiki/concepts/temporal-abstraction-options.md]]) |

The discount kernel was the unresolved item, and it is now defused architecturally: a hyperbola is a mixture of exponentials, so a bank of `γ` heads on one encoder recovers it with no new learning rule ([[wiki/concepts/multi-horizon-value-learning.md]]). What stays live is empirical — which hazard prior the brain holds (T141, [[wiki/concepts/subjective-value.md]]).

### 10. The representation layer became measurable — and the measurements were mostly negative

Twenty-eight sources on world models and self-supervised learning. The positives are real: an action-conditioned latent model plans on a *measured* energy surface that is smooth and locally convex around the goal ([[wiki/entities/v-jepa-2.md]]); an edge-label vocabulary is induced at scale for the first time ([[wiki/entities/adaworld.md]], moving G4 to `PARTIAL`); a probed board state is **causally** edited and the model's predictions follow, closing the wiki's oldest acceptance test at the cost of one vector addition ([[wiki/entities/othellogpt.md]]). But the pattern across this literature is that each instrument acquired a counterexample as soon as it was held:

| Instrument | The counterexample the world-model literature supplied |
|---|---|
| **Prediction quality** | Model quality and task competence anti-correlate on a controlled ladder (above), and a world model's loss says nothing about what a planner can do with it (G62) |
| **Linear probing** | A null probe may be a wrong label *basis* rather than an absent feature (T157), and a verified causal direction can coexist with a cheaper circuit the model actually uses (T158) |
| **Geometric structure** | Additivity of spatial relations holds only after projection onto a fitted 3-D subspace, so a structure visible after dimensionality reduction may be a property of the projection (T159) |
| **Violation-of-expectation scores** | Real, but an untrained-network null is a floor for a *read-out*, not evidence that untrained activations are structureless ([[wiki/entities/byol.md]]) |
| **Compositionality benchmarks** | `argmax`-scored caption discriminations return **100%** to a fully collapsed model — the field's principal measurement of the property collapse destroys is maximally passed by collapse |
| **Composition coverage** | A model scoring 0.994 on fitting the phenomena scores **0.000** on the composition its primitives should generate — fit and structure are separable measurements, demonstrated inside one protocol ([[wiki/entities/neo-neural-theorizer.md]]) |

Two structural results from the same literature that are not about measurement and should outlast it. **Retrieval has a dimension bound**: a store read by ranking `⟨q, v_i⟩` cannot be asked for arbitrary subsets — `C(n,k) ≤ (1 + 1/γ)^d` — so "embed everything once and answer queries by coordinate comparison", the cheapest answer to G37, has a ceiling that is a property of the *factorisation*, not of the training ([[wiki/concepts/retrieval-capacity.md]]). And **the behaviour policy is a load-bearing parameter of world-model estimation**: identifiability of the learned dynamics depends on the policy that gathered the data, and every mechanism the wiki has for improving an agent destroys the excitation condition the guarantee needs (G63).

### 11. Sequence capacity does not come from the recurrence — a negative result

Seven sources on sequence architectures, and the load-bearing one is an impossibility rather than a design. **Inside the fading-memory class, recurrent topology carries no expressive power**: a simple cycle reservoir — one ring, one weight, one aperiodic sign pattern — is universal for that class ([[wiki/entities/simple-cycle-reservoir.md]]), so the elaborate untrained connectomes the reservoir literature relies on are not buying expressivity, they are buying a richer *basis* for a linear readout ([[wiki/concepts/autonomous-pattern-generation.md]]). The same boundary is reached from the generation side: a contractive reservoir kept alive by a periodic driver emits an unbounded output at the price of being a function of clock phase alone, so it can run one fixed cycle forever and cannot run a path chosen at run time. What a reasoning-capable sequence model must add is therefore stated sharply and negatively — a variable that survives a cycle, i.e. non-fading state, which is by definition outside the class.

Three consequences for the rest of the wiki. (i) **Timescale is always a decay constant** — `λ`, `τ`, `Δ` — i.e. a property of how fast state is forgotten; a *driver* sets timescale by what is re-injected, costs one scalar per unit, and is a signal rather than a parameter, hence gateable at run time. Nothing sets how many of either a model needs (G67). (ii) The continuous-time line ([[wiki/entities/ltc.md]], [[wiki/entities/cfc.md]]) and the structured state-space line ([[wiki/entities/s4.md]], [[wiki/entities/ms-ssm.md]]) are the same object under two parameterisations, and their differences are computational — an `Ω(L+N)` bound met, a closed form replacing an ODE solve — not representational. (iii) [[wiki/entities/conceptor.md]] is the first machine store in the wiki that **knows when it is full**, reading occupancy off its own contents (`q = Σs_i(A^j)/N`) and defining novelty as a Boolean difference against what is already held; that moves G42 to `PARTIAL` and supplies the only threshold-free candidate for G38.

### 12. The cost of analogy was in the wrong place, and "compositional" is five properties

Twelve sources so far on analogy and program induction. The headline correction: **the wiki priced alignment as the bottleneck and the human data say retrieval is**. Once a source is cued, transfer runs at ~86% whether the source is near or remote; the entire distance penalty sits in *finding* the source — 88% retrieve a same-domain source against 12% for a remote one ([[wiki/concepts/analogical-mapping.md]]). That relocates G37 from the `O(n⁴)`–`O(n!)` mapper to the cue, and the analogy literature supplies the first candidate of the right shape: MAC/FAC's cheap stage is derived from the expensive stage's *own definition* — a content-vector dot product that analytically over-estimates the number of match hypotheses the exact algorithm would generate — so its error is **one-sided** and it never drops a candidate ([[wiki/entities/macfac.md]]). That is a soundness argument rather than an error estimate, which is a certificate type the wiki's five amortisation modes did not have.

Second, **compositionality decomposed**: systematicity, productivity, substitutivity, localism and overgeneralisation are separately failable, and on data generated *by* the intended rule, task accuracies of 0.79–0.92 sit against productivity of 0.30–0.50 ([[wiki/entities/pcfg-set.md]]). This is the strongest form of the shortcut result in the wiki — the training data contain no non-compositional phenomena and the model still finds something else — and it says i.i.d. accuracy is not a weak measurement of structure but a blind one. Third, the same lesson arrives as a number on ARC: full demonstration-consistency is passed four to eleven times more often than it is right (48.8% of tasks get a program reproducing every demonstration; 10.8% solve the query, [[wiki/entities/arc-vsa-solver.md]]), so *fit* and *structure* are now separable measurements in three independent protocols. Fourth, a new gap with an uncomfortable human precedent: **a transferred inference is not tagged as one** — subjects "recognise" never-presented analogical inferences as having been stated at twice the base rate, a week later (G71) — which is retrieval-augmented generation's failure mode, measured in the one working system available.

### 13. The measurement problem is inventoried, and its hardest failure is about the developer rather than the model

Twenty-seven sources on benchmarks — the ARC-AGI family end to end, the concept-variation and adversarial instruments, and the mathematics and graduate-science sets. That sweep was undertaken because section 8 named measurement as the binding constraint and the wiki was scoring architectures against instruments it had never read. Four things came back.

**(i) The instruments are now a catalogue rather than a list of hopes, and the catalogue's structure is a cost ordering.** Forty-seven protocols, `I1`–`I47` at [[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]], ordered by what each needs, over fifteen shared failure modes `F1`–`F15`. The two that need nothing but the model itself turn out to be the sharpest: the **support/query split** (induce structure from one observation pair, execute it on a second from the same mechanism; the *gap* between 0.994 self-fit and 0.000 transfer is the measurement) and the **consistency score restricted to items the model got wrong** — identical *incorrect* outputs under a meaning-preserving substitution cannot both be explained by being correct, and that restriction takes the best of three architectures from 0.90 to **0.34**. Neither needs labels, distribution shift, a probe, or a human.

**(ii) The failure modes are shared, and the three hardest are statements about the builder, not the model.** Developer-blindness is a **depleting stock** — ARC-AGI-1's 100 private tasks absorbed on the order of 10,000 reported scores over five years. Item-level blindness does **not** imply distributional blindness, and the difficulty calibration that makes subset scores comparable is exactly what guarantees the leak (G17). And **`GD ≈ 0` items go unaudited**: 49% of that private set fell to blind brute-force search in 2020, before LLMs, at low compute (G17), invisible to a protocol that asserts difficulty per-benchmark and never measures it per-task. The honest reading is that G17 may be a **maintenance schedule rather than a protocol** — benchmark authorship run as a refinement loop against the field, stopping when authoring tasks easy for humans and hard for AI becomes impossible.

**(iii) An honest instrument returns a facet vector, and this was reached independently from two directions.** From behaviour: 0.92 i.i.d. accuracy coexisting with 0.72 systematicity, 0.50 productivity, 0.54 localism on data generated *by* the intended rule. From the task side: a concept-variation score that **rises** 10 points on one concept while falling 11 on another, so every single-number benchmark score in this wiki is a mean over a concept mixture with unmeasured per-concept variance. Any single number, including the good ones, is under-determined — which retroactively qualifies every score in sections 9–12.

**(iv) The denominator was never specified, and three tensions the wiki had logged separately are one defect.** A human baseline is a protocol — pool, attempt budget, aggregation order, exclusion rule, item-pool identity — not a number, and the same raw data yields **73% against ~91%** on identical items and participants, and **60% against 99%** on one benchmark ([[wiki/concepts/human-baseline.md]], G17). Two consequences that matter beyond bookkeeping. The design rule *easy for humans, hard for AI* is a claim about a **per-item** baseline, and the diagnostic regime it selects is **humans-at-ceiling** — a benchmark hard enough to floor every solver destroys the ordering it exists to produce (two programs 2 points apart on ARC are 23 apart on ConceptARC). And **aggregate parity is compatible with an orthogonal mechanism**: a model sitting at the human aggregate (.90 vs .91) correlates **0.06** with the human item-type profile and scores *below chance* on the one item type authored to catch its heuristic. For a wiki whose premise is that the biological solution is worth importing, the evidence that an import worked is a **matched failure profile**, not a matched accuracy — and that measurement is unavailable on the wiki's central benchmarks, because they report aggregates only.

**(v) One strand of it is not about measurement at all: three rule-rewriting domains now bracket the hardness the wiki had only named.** [[wiki/entities/baba-is-ai.md]], [[wiki/entities/fluxx.md]] and [[wiki/entities/nomic.md]] are the same idea at three depths, and reading them in order changes what gap **G7** is asking. Baba renders each rule as movable word tiles inside the image and satisfies every tractability clause the wiki demands of the `s' = (base_state, rule_config)` lift — factorised, sparse, legible, bounded, meta-stationary — and models that score **100%** when obeying the rules suffices score **14.7–20.0%** when winning requires editing them, never once considering an edit. Fluxx meets the same clauses more cheaply still, with a *countable* rewrite generator (a fixed deck), and the reference system — an experienced human — loses the combined effect at ten or eleven concurrent face-up rules, one of which was a live win condition nobody noticed `(tentative)`. So the clauses buy well-posedness, not smallness (T229), and they split the problem in two: where the solver owns the rewrites this is **search**, where other agents own them it is **filtering**. Nomic supplies the tier above both, and the surprise is that it is cheap: self-amendment does not need a third rewrite-graph level, only a **mutability boolean on each rule-node** plus one gated operator that flips it, with the meta-tower left implicit because a rule-change can transmute the transmutation rule. What it charges for is everything around that — a precedence order that must *coincide* with the entrenchment order or entrenchment protects nothing, a liveness invariant on the system's own rewrite capacity, and a defined outcome for undecidability (made terminal and winning) once self-application is licensed rather than banned. It also supplies the decidable conflict operator **G8** was missing, since ordinal priority over rule-nodes is computed from metadata and never needs a semantic `contradicts` test, and the only recipe the wiki has for a non-stationary objective: entrench the goal's *form* and expose its parameters. None of the three has a solver, the third has no implementation and **no measurements at all**, and the honest open question is whether a system whose rewrite machinery is itself rewritable has any learnable generator left — or whether the generator was never in the environment but in the other agents (T230).

**(vi) Five further sources move the audit upstream of the score, and each finds the instrument's guarantee resting on something nobody checked.** *Was the item withheld at all?* — [[wiki/concepts/benchmark-contamination.md]] supplies the primary source for the paraphrase-differential `δ_train − δ_test` the wiki had been carrying second-hand, and with it the detector's sign structure: **a clean model and a model that saw both splits are the same point**, so a low reading is uninformative in exactly the direction that matters, and only the triple `(δ_train, δ_test, δ_train-test)` distinguishes them. *Was the item ever correct?* — [[wiki/entities/hle-verified.md]] audits all 2,500 items of [[wiki/entities/hle.md]] and returns **668 gold (26.7%), 1,143 repaired, 689 indeterminate**, which makes it the wiki's first *post-release repair* instrument: every other entry in the inventory admits or scores items, none fixes them. Its own circularity is uncontrolled — a model-assisted audit of a model-gated benchmark — and that is the honest reading of HLE's construction too, since HLE is [[wiki/entities/anli.md]]'s adversarial loop with the human adversary replaced by an automated model-in-the-loop gate. *Was the instrument ever built for this?* — [[wiki/entities/aime.md]] is a 1983 human **selection** contest adopted wholesale, quoted second-hand on eight pages before being read once: 15 items, not the 30 the machine convention pools, and an integer 000–999 answer giving `P(correct | no work) = 0.1%` — the wiki's earliest anti-shortcut bound, and a *format rule* rather than an audit. It is also the only human baseline here re-collected annually on a fresh item set at population scale, and it satisfies the humans-at-ceiling placement discipline by accident, because a selection device must rank its takers. *And was the shortcut ever removed?* — [[wiki/entities/shortcut-suite.md]] builds its perturbation so that the label is preserved **by proof** (`q ∧ ⊤ ≡ q`) rather than by a rater, and returns the most uncomfortable scaling result here: under few-shot in-context learning, accuracy on precisely the items the heuristic gets wrong falls monotonically with parameter count (75.3 → 48.5 → **3.6**), and reverses under chain-of-thought. The sign of the scaling effect on robustness is a property of the prompt (T228).

The uncomfortable summary: measurement did not stop being the binding constraint. It became legible, which is a different thing, and the legibility mostly documents why the constraint binds.



### 14. The substrate argument finally has a price list — and three of its four holes are in the *specification*, not the hardware

Nine sources on spiking and neuromorphic implementation, undertaken because T1 — *does the implementation level carry computational content?* — had been `LEANING B` on two results and no theory. That literature supplies the theory, and it cuts both ways.

**(i) The separation is real, quantified, and smaller than the wiki was treating it as.** A single spiking neuron computes coincidence detection and element distinctness that provably cost `Ω(n)` sigmoidal hidden units — **≥1663** at a 10,000-synapse fan-in ([[wiki/concepts/circuit-size-separation.md]]). But for piecewise-linear postsynaptic potentials the two classes are *mutually simulable* in `O(s)` units, so every proven separation is in the **number of units**, never in what is computable. That converts B from an inexpressibility claim into a **density** claim (T234), and it changes what a spiking result has to show: not a representation the rate model lacks, but *units-to-competence* — reported once in the wiki (equal SHD accuracy at ~5× fewer parameters, [[wiki/concepts/learnable-synaptic-delays.md]]) and meaningful only with the weight budget stated, since width and parameter count separate by up to 7.8× at matched accuracy ([[wiki/concepts/width-versus-parameter-count.md]]). The same theorem supplies the one design rule that transfers immediately: `n` programmable **delays** carry VC dimension `Θ(n log n)` against `n` weights' `Θ(n)`, so a delay is a strictly more expressive parameter than a weight at equal count.

**(ii) The headline accuracies were mostly not evidence about spiking learning.** The literature's comparison table splits into two anti-correlated tracks: convert a backpropagation-trained rate network to spikes and you get the only results past MNIST, with **nothing learned in spikes**; train genuinely in spikes and nothing exceeds MNIST (T231). A later spike-native result expires half of that: spike-native heterogeneous training now beats converted and surrogate-gradient baselines on CIFAR-10/100, ImageNet and the neuromorphic sets at 4–8× fewer time steps. So "the rate specification was sufficient" survives as a claim about *those tasks*, and no longer as an argument that nothing else works.

**(iii) Three specification holes opened, and they are upstream of every spiking page the wiki already had.** `G76` — **a temporally coded output has no loss function**: every architecture here that argues timing carries content can read a timing code and none can be trained toward one. `G77` — **every high-performing temporal code needs a time origin nothing supplies**: time-to-first-spike measures latency from stimulus onset, phase coding against a reference oscillation, binary codes against a clock, and the brain does not know when the stimulus began. `G78` — **nothing sets a slow variable's time constant, and memory span *is* that time constant**, which is found by grid search and is not even monotone (raising `τ_b2` from 300 to 500 ms converges *faster*). Each of the three is a hole in what one writes down, not in what one builds it from.

**(iv) Two of the three narrowed inside the same literature, and the narrowing reversed a standing reading.** Against `G76`, the wiki had held that convolving a spike train into a continuous function before measuring error reintroduces exactly the rate-blindness the timing code exists to avoid. The direct measurement says the opposite: a **filtered** error reaches capacity `α_m = 0.14` at 1 ms precision against the Victor–Purpura Chronotron's `0.15`, and still holds ≈0.07 at **0.2 ms**, while it is the *unsmoothed* rule that collapses — because an instantaneous error's attractor sits at the postsynaptic-potential peak, a weight that is independent of the target time (T236). Smoothing is not the concession; it is what makes the loss's fixed point the right one. Against `G78`, per-neuron time constants can be **learned** rather than swept, by a bi-level program on orthogonal sub-training sets ([[wiki/concepts/neuronal-parameter-heterogeneity.md]]) — but the loop is still offline and per-task, so the row narrows without closing: the run-time question, *how long must this partial path survive*, is still answered at design time.

**(v) The transferable structural idea here is not about spikes at all.** [[wiki/concepts/cross-paradigm-interface.md]] treats the boundary between two representational languages as a **module with its own parameters and its own objective** (`Y = Q·F·H·W(X)`), rather than as a conversion or an arrow. That is the first inter-module connection in the wiki with state of its own, and it lands directly on the G52/G21 cluster the gap table calls its cheapest: if module boundaries are trainable objects, the wiki's habit of drawing them as arrows is a modelling error rather than a simplification (T235). It is asserted and not ablated — no learnable-interface-versus-fixed-converter comparison at matched architecture exists — which makes it the most actionable unrun experiment in this section.

The summary: the substrate camp's strongest claim is now its most precisely bounded one. Spiking buys **units and energy**, provably; it buys expressiveness only where the postsynaptic potential is not piecewise-linear; and the reason the wiki's spiking pages could not be scored against its rate pages was never the hardware — it was that nobody had written down the loss, the clock or the time constant.

### 15. The object of study moves from the module to the graph — and six of its fifteen gaps are properties no module can have

Thirty-seven sources on large-scale brain organisation, in five clusters: whole-brain network models and the structure-to-function map, connectome topology, the default-mode network, the consciousness theories and the adversarial collaboration that tested them, higher-order thalamus, and the visual front end. Sections 1–14 all ask what a *component* must compute. This is the first body of sources that asks what the *arrangement* computes, and the wiki's architecture turns out to have almost no answers at that level — not because they are hard, but because nothing had ever been asked to have them.

**(i) The wiki's architectures have a graph, and not one of them can read it.** Every page here draws modules and arrows. The network-neuroscience literature says the arrangement of those arrows is itself a computational object with measurable properties: a structural core of densely interlinked posterior medial and parietal regions ([[wiki/concepts/connectome-hubs-and-cores.md]]), small-worldness as a *property* a graph is found to have rather than a construction one builds ([[wiki/concepts/small-world-topology.md]]), and a one-dimensional integration/segregation family the brain traverses **in seconds** during a task ([[wiki/concepts/integration-segregation-balance.md]]). That last result is the one with no counterpart anywhere in the wiki: every architecture here has a *fixed* graph, so topology is a design-time constant, and the biology treats it as a **demand-graded control variable** (`G85`). A module that does not know its own topological position also cannot be assigned the job of being the crossing point between two subgraphs (`G84`) — a role the connectome fills with identifiable cells and the wiki fills with an arrow.

**(ii) The arrow is at least two channels, and the wiki has been drawing one.** Every direct cortico-cortical edge `A → B` is mirrored by a disynaptic `A → higher-order thalamus → B`, and the two carry different cargo: the direct edge drives, the transthalamic edge delivers **state and context** ([[wiki/concepts/transthalamic-context-routing.md]]). Measured on both edges at once, the afferent with the *highest event rate* is the one causally irrelevant to the target's sensory responses. This lands on the wiki's weakest representational commitment: context in every architecture here is either a single global scalar (a neuromodulator level) or a hand-supplied task label, and neither is **addressed** — neither says *which* downstream population the context is for (`G93`). It is also the network-scale continuation of the neuromorphic layer's one transferable structural idea, [[wiki/concepts/cross-paradigm-interface.md]]: that gave the interface its parameters, this gives the edge a **cargo type**.

**(iii) Structure predicts function; function does not recover structure — and that is a theorem about interpretability, not about brains.** The asymmetry is quantitative and one-way ([[wiki/concepts/function-to-structure-inference.md]]): a connectome plus conduction delays predicts resting functional connectivity at useful accuracy, and no amount of functional connectivity recovers the connectome, because a structural graph does not specify a functional network — it specifies a **manifold** of them, which the ongoing dynamics wanders ([[wiki/concepts/dynamic-repertoire.md]], [[wiki/concepts/metastability.md]]). The transthalamic result above is the worked counterexample: co-activation would have inverted the driving and the modulatory edge. The wiki has read structure off activity throughout — probes, representational similarity, co-activation of units — and this is the first source that prices that move. Its architectural form is `G88`: **no architecture in the wiki has a direction of information flow that comes from its own weight matrix** rather than from an experimenter's diagram. [[wiki/concepts/effective-connectivity.md]] is the instrument that would fix it and it is not free — it estimates the parameters of an assumed generative model, so it buys directedness with a model one must already believe.

**(iv) Two whole operating modes are missing, and both are missing for the same reason: every system here is input-driven by construction.** The default-mode network is a network whose activity is *highest* when nothing is asked ([[wiki/entities/default-mode-network.md]]), and it competes with the input-driven mode rather than idling behind it — so an architecture needs both an internally generated mode and an **arbitrator** between them, and the wiki has neither (`G90`). The second is the discrete commit. Ignition is a sudden, self-amplifying, network-wide reverberation at ~200–300 ms that either happens or does not ([[wiki/concepts/ignition.md]]); no architecture in the wiki has a thresholded all-or-none broadcast at all, and none has a **learned gate deciding what deserves one** (`G91`). Both gaps are about *when to stop feeding forward*, and both are invisible to a wiki whose systems compute a function of their input by construction.

**(v) The predictive stack the wiki has assumed since section 9 is the wrong shape.** Standard predictive coding wires level `i` to `i−1` and `i+1` only, so the top level reaches the bottom by relay. The measured cortical hierarchy is an **apex that broadcasts across levels** — selective in, diffuse out ([[wiki/concepts/broadcast-hierarchy.md]]) — which is a different architecture, not a detail of one, and it is what makes a workspace cheap: `O(n)` inter-module communication through a shared bus rather than `O(n²)` through pairwise edges. The wiki now carries both readings as a live tension, because the instrument that establishes the asymmetry (regression dynamic causal modelling at 400 parcels) buys its scale by being linear with a fixed haemodynamic response, so what it recovers is a directed-influence ordering and not a causal one.

**(vi) The visual sources supply the wiki's first written-down success criterion, and the first honest measurement discipline for one.** [[wiki/concepts/manifold-untangling.md]] states what a good representation *is* in a form that can be scored: all images of one object under identity-preserving transformations form a manifold, and the job of a sensory cascade is to make those manifolds flat and linearly separable — tested by a **fixed-complexity reader on held-out transformations** rather than held-out samples. That is a strictly stronger protocol than the linear probes every joint-embedding entity page in the wiki reports, and it re-prices them. [[wiki/concepts/predictive-adequacy.md]] supplies the companion rule: a description of a component is adequate when it predicts responses to *arbitrary* inputs, as a fraction of explainable variance — under which most of what the field calls a model of V1 is a model of the stimuli someone chose. Two implementation gaps fall out. `G94`: nothing in the wiki adapts a unit's **time constant** or pools gain **locally** — normalisation here is a global, offline, once-per-dataset operation, where the biology runs it as a continuous local computation. `G95`: every self-supervised system in the wiki has its invariance group *declared* by a hand-written augmentation list, while the biology's untangling discovers which directions are nuisance **from the input's own statistics**. And [[wiki/concepts/random-feedback-addressing.md]] closes a mechanism the wiki had long assumed without naming: a controller of mixed-selectivity units can deliver feature-specific top-down feedback **through near-random connections**, so targeted attention needs no targeted wiring.

**(vii) The methodological finding is that a baseline is a claim.** Every "activation" in imaging is a *difference against a control state*, which means a reported decrease may be a property of the control rather than of the task ([[wiki/concepts/activity-baseline.md]]) — the exact error that made the default-mode network look like deactivation for a decade. This generalises without modification to interpretability: an ablation, a patch and a steering vector are all differences against a chosen baseline, and the wiki's mechanistic pages inherit the problem whole. Its causal twin is [[wiki/concepts/perturbation-elicitability.md]]: stimulate a site and the probability that reportable content changes is **not uniform across cortex** — null across dozens of patients in anteromedial prefrontal cortex while reliable in orbitofrontal and cingulate — so a null perturbation result is a fact about *where you stimulated* before it is a fact about what is represented there.

**Which of `G81`–`G95` are properties of the graph rather than of any module: `G84`** (no module knows its topological position), **`G85`** (none reads its topology as a control variable), **`G88`** (none derives flow direction from its own weights), **`G90`** (none has an internal mode or an arbitrator), **`G91`** (none has a discrete commit or a gate on it), **`G93`** (none has an addressed context channel). Six of fifteen. The remaining nine — `G81`–`G83`, `G86`, `G87`, `G89`, `G92`, `G94`, `G95` — are module-level or measurement-level and could in principle be closed by building one better part. The six cannot: **there is no component one can add to a wiki architecture that fixes them, because each is a property of the arrangement**, and the wiki has never had a page whose subject was the arrangement. That is the actual result, and it is the reason section 15 could not be written as a list of new mechanisms.

The summary: the wiki now knows that its architecture diagrams are under-specified in a way no amount of module-level work reaches — the arrows carry one cargo where the biology carries two, the graph is fixed where the biology grades it by demand, direction of flow is drawn by hand where it should fall out of the weights, and nothing in it ever stops taking input. Against that, this literature supplies the first success criterion the wiki can actually score (untangling on held-out transformations) and the first two rules for not fooling itself while doing so (predictive adequacy, and the baseline).

### 16. In continual learning and in interpretability alike, the unsolved part is the *read*, not the write

This section pairs two literatures that look unrelated — continual learning and mechanistic interpretability — and they returned the same shape of answer. In each, the operation the field has been optimising (what to store, how it got stored) is either solved or shown not to matter, and the residue sits entirely in *getting the right thing back out*.

**(i) Eliminating forgetting does not solve continual learning; it relocates the problem.** [[wiki/entities/cn-dpm.md]] is the wiki's first worked expansion-family learner and the first source in it that measures its own retrieval accuracy against ground truth. Forgetting is **0.0 points** — a frozen expert per component cannot be overwritten — and the gate that decides which expert to invoke scores **48.18% at five components and 31.14% at twenty**. The write is exact and the read degrades with the very capacity growth that made the write exact (`T282`). This is the same slot as `G37` (nothing decides which stored structure applies to the current situation), now with the first number attached to it, and it is why `P12` — re-pricing `G37` against the retrieval result — moves from a lint observation to a measured debt.

**(ii) In sequence, every replay-selection rule the field deploys is beaten by uniform sampling.** [[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]] is the wiki's first ranking of replay-selection rules run with the prioritisation baselines as controls, and it goes against every deployed system: **reservoir (uniform coverage) sampling wins, reward-weighted sampling gains nothing over uniform, and uncertainty/surprise-weighted sampling performs at the non-continual baseline.** The write-side selection rule — the thing a decade of prioritised-replay work optimises — buys nothing here. The sign flips against single-task Atari, where surprise-weighted replay is worth ~2× learning speed ([[wiki/concepts/replay-prioritisation.md]]), and nothing has isolated which of objective, statistic or refresh schedule carries the flip. The sharp form (formerly `T284`, retired as L3 detail and carried on [[wiki/entities/continual-dreamer.md]]): **the same model-disagreement quantity is a working exploration reward and a failing replay priority**, so "surprise" is not one signal used two ways but two different quantities sharing a name.

**(iii) A correct mechanistic description can fail to exist, and this is the wiki's first argument for that.** [[wiki/concepts/memorisation-vs-generalisation.md]] makes it a property of the *task*: where knowledge of the training pairs confers no advantage on unseen entities, there are no macrofeatures, so every intermediate state is either irrelevant or an arbitrary weighted sum of per-example microfeatures, and the failure to find a circuit is not an interpreter's failure (`T285`). This is the third member of the wiki's evaluation-epistemology set — [[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]] asks whether the system found the intended structure, [[wiki/concepts/predictive-adequacy.md]] asks whether our description is right, and this asks whether a description of the wanted kind is *available at all*.

**(iv) What the uninterpretable layers produce is measurable even where how they produce it is not.** [[wiki/concepts/multi-token-embedding.md]] takes the positive half: the first 10–20% of layers mint an *entity* from the last few tokens, from which attributes are recoverable as linear projections — a token-embedding table one level up the compositional ladder, with the same structured outputs and the same uninterpretable interior. Its transferable product is not the circuit but the **effective model**: a simplification ladder that replaces each component with the simplest stand-in and reports the accuracy paid at every rung (100% → 98% → ~90% → 94% → 85%). That is a faithfulness protocol the wiki can apply to any of its own architectures, and it is the first instrument in the wiki that prices an interpretation rather than asserting one. It also delivers the first machine measurement of learned write-side hashing (`T286`), which is the machine twin of the addressing question on [[wiki/concepts/random-feedback-addressing.md]].

**(v) The biological answer to (i) and (ii) is a write gate that nobody has to design.** [[wiki/concepts/encoding-retrieval-alternation.md]] is the one new mechanism page here: a store cannot write and read simultaneously, and the hippocampus does not resolve this strategically but by alternating at 4–8 Hz — which makes the two half-cycles the *minus* and *plus* phases of an error-driven rule. An already-known item then produces a null update, so redundancy costs no capacity **without an importance estimate, a task boundary, or an exemplar buffer** — the three things every machine continual learner in the wiki requires. [[wiki/entities/ch-hnn.md]] closes the machine-side version of the same oracle: its task-similarity function is computed from the input rather than from permutation indices, so the task label disappears at inference.

The summary: continual learning and interpretability are the same problem seen from two ends. Storage is not the constraint in either — retrieval is, and neither field measures it.

### 17. Comparative cognition is the wiki's first *outside* coordinate system — and its result is that the human-specific difference is not a new part

Every section above compares architectures to each other or to one brain. The comparative literature supplies an outside axis: given two related solutions to the same problem, what actually differs. Its central finding is negative and it constrains the whole wiki.

**(i) The subtraction inventory comes back nearly empty on the part list.** [[wiki/concepts/emergent-modularity.md]] carries it: **no new cortical areas**, frontal cortex sitting **exactly at the ape scaling prediction**, and **2.75× cortical volume on 1.25× the neurons** — the extra volume is neuropil, which is connections rather than units. The wiki has no lever for that: `G101` now has machine sweeps at fixed unit count ([[wiki/entities/rigl.md]], [[wiki/entities/sparse-evolutionary-training.md]]): extra edges per unit buy i.i.d. accuracy with diminishing returns — above ~20% density mostly training speed — and at matched weights spending the budget on units wins. Whether that is what a higher synapse-per-unit ratio *buys* for structure is the open half (section 28).

**(ii) That negative forces the organising controversy here, `T289`: a new component, or a re-weighting of components already present?** The wiki now holds all three answers with pages behind them, which is why the row is `LIVE` rather than decided:

| Position | Page | What it would take to build |
|---|---|---|
| A new **operator** | [[wiki/concepts/discrete-infinity.md]], [[wiki/concepts/relational-reinterpretation.md]] | `G99` — call a module's *operation* on an argument from outside its domain. Every transfer mechanism in the wiki moves representations; none exposes an operator |
| A re-weighted **schedule** | [[wiki/concepts/emergent-modularity.md]], [[wiki/concepts/developmental-heterochrony.md]] | `G100` — a growth schedule. Two architectures here now grow ([[wiki/entities/progressive-neural-networks.md]], [[wiki/entities/ligo.md]]), but each grows at an experimenter-chosen moment, so none owns *when*; and LiGO's payoff is 45% of compute with zero competence (`T354`) |
| A **motivational** second factor | [[wiki/concepts/motivation-representation-synergy.md]] | `G102` — a state in which a system holds a capability and declines to deploy it. `expressed competence ≈ representation × motivation`, and the wiki has only ever modelled the first term |

**(iii) The schedule lever stopped being a metaphor.** [[wiki/concepts/developmental-heterochrony.md]] measures it: a scalar per-gene time-warp `Δ` against a trajectory whose *shape* is conserved from mouse to human, delayed in **~4% of the prefrontal transcriptome**, gray-matter-specific, peaking around **10 years**. So `G100` is not "train it slower" — it is **a vector of per-module phase offsets over a shared trajectory**, which is a different and much cheaper thing to implement. It also supplies the test that separates *schedule* differences from *mechanism* differences, and `T294` asks whether the wiki's own `G100` cites the wrong developmental window.

**(iv) "Symbolic" became a checklist, and the wiki's own binding machinery scores at the animal level on it.** [[wiki/concepts/relational-reinterpretation.md]] decomposes it into eight separately-testable properties, **four of which every taxon examined has** — discrete enduring representations, single-exposure atomistic update, functional compositionality, featural systematicity. The four claimed human-only rest on one operation: the compound must **preserve the identity of its constituents while bound**, so a role stays addressable as a type distinct from its current filler. That is `G104`, and it is a direct hit on [[wiki/concepts/vector-symbolic-binding.md]]: in the wiki's VSA codes, role and filler stop being separately addressable the moment they are bound. [[wiki/entities/lisa.md]] delivers the property with synchrony and does not scale; [[wiki/entities/esbn.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/activity-slots.md]] deliver it without a compound and do scale, but hold the role as a slot, never as a type. `T293` is the live question of whether an invertible conjunctive code with a scheduled unbinding step is enough, or whether the constituents must genuinely persist.

**(v) A measurement result that re-prices the wiki's entire benchmark half.** `T291`: when a system fails a capability test, has the capability been measured, or only its *deployment under one frame*? The comparative literature reaches this through paired frames — the same task posed cooperatively and competitively — and gets opposite answers from the same animals. ### 24. The cortical channel has measured parts, and its rulers stop where the wiki needs them

Twenty-two sources across two waves: how cortex computes, how it talks to the hippocampus, and the posterior exit from the hippocampal formation. Section 5 said the missing pieces are protocols between modules. This literature measures the protocol, and finds the *module* is the ill-defined part.

**(i) The unit is the cell class, not the layer.** Connectivity follows genetically defined classes (IT/PT/CT) under causal transcription-factor manipulation; a layer is a proxy for a type label that fails wherever it holds two classes ([[wiki/concepts/canonical-cortical-microcircuit.md]], `T371` `LEANING` B).

**(ii) The junction sits inside one cell, and a third party throws it.**

| Result | Page | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Coincident basal + apical-tuft input triggers a burst: `burst = coupling × AND(apical, basal)` | [[wiki/concepts/apical-amplification.md]] | `T374` `LIVE` — a matched prediction bursts the cell here, silences it under predictive coding |

**(iii) The rulers.** SLN gives a continuous depth with ±1–2 level confidence intervals over 339 pathways, and the anatomical argument for a feedforward-driver / feedback-modulator asymmetry falls away ([[wiki/concepts/counterstream-organization.md]]). A dynamics-only hierarchy matches it at `R = 0.93`, with direction carried as frequency — theta/gamma ascending, beta descending ([[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]], `T379` `LIVE`). Both rulers fail on the same frontal areas, whose functional depth moves with task (`T378` `LIVE`). **Depth is defined for the sensory streams and may be a task variable above them.**

**(iv) The cortico-hippocampal channel is a duty-cycled loop.** Pre-ripple cortex predicts ripple content and the ripple predicts cortex after it (`T377` `LEANING` A). Slow-oscillation/spindle/ripple nesting opens the window, and the sign flips when no spindle nests ([[wiki/concepts/sleep-oscillation-nesting.md]], `T373` `LIVE`, `G14` `PARTIAL`).

**(v) The exit is typed by wiring.** Subicular output classes are discrete; distal-class silencing abolishes encoding of a new goal and spares retrieval of the old ([[wiki/entities/subiculum.md]], `G122` `OPEN`). The anterior thalamus may be a second stream rather than a relay (`T381` `LIVE`), and its plasticity rule belongs to the fibre of origin, a register `G52` (`OPEN`) has no slot for ([[wiki/entities/anterior-thalamic-nuclei.md]]). The frame converter is a gated bank of `K` copies with the argmax deleted ([[wiki/entities/bb-model.md]], [[wiki/concepts/reference-frame-transformation.md]]) — the first implementation for `G39` and `G110` (both `OPEN`). A product code makes frame disagreement unrepresentable (`G43` `OPEN`, `T383` `LIVE`).

The summary: the protocol has parts with numbers — a two-port cell, a third-party coupling, a frequency-coded direction, a duty cycle, a typed exit. What dissolved is the box the wiki draws them between. Its unit is a cell class, and its level is only measured where the wiki needs it least.

### 25. The parse is a read assembled per task, not a setting — and on ARC the floor it imposes is perceptual, measurable, and worth most of the gap

Eleven sources on how the input is carved before any rule is sought. The wiki had treated the parse as a design-time constant (`G75` `OPEN`, the parse-specific case of `G73` `OPEN`). This literature says it is computed on demand, and it supplies the first measurements of what getting it wrong costs.

**(i) Where objects live now has five answers, and only one is a menu** (`T151` `LIVE`):

| Account | The object is… | What it prices | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Menu | one of six parse hypotheses, selected jointly with the rule | nothing — a fixed finite list | [[wiki/entities/arc-vsa-solver.md]] |
| Program | whatever a routine's bounded activation reaches; routines are compiled per goal from five fixed operations | an open space of parses, with no implementation | [[wiki/concepts/visual-routines.md]] |
| Pointer | what one of 4–5 sticky indices holds onto across change | how many arguments a relation can bind at once | [[wiki/concepts/visual-indices.md]] |
| Tag | a transient activity label spread over the base representation | time, linear in distance travelled through the object | [[wiki/concepts/incremental-grouping.md]] |
| None | — (per-pixel classifier, no object anywhere) | nothing, and scores 60.4% on ARC-AGI-1 against a reported human 60.2% | [[wiki/entities/varc.md]] |

The tag is the first of these that has been built. Two of Ullman's operations turn out to be one operation at four scales, learned from binary reward. The resulting circuit generalises from ≤7 px curves to 30 px curves at 100% and transfers to region parsing ([[wiki/entities/multiscale-tracing-network.md]]). The pointer reading opens `T387` (`LIVE`): is a pointer assigned by the stimulus, or bound by the program that needs a variable?

**(ii) The ARC perception floor stopped being an inference** (`T215` `LEANING B`). With no rule in the task, VLMs average 58.07% where humans score 100%, and chain-of-thought does not help ([[wiki/entities/blindtest.md]]). VARC's gain decomposes into four input-reading priors worth +27.7 points cumulatively, and none of them is a reasoning term. Re-describing each image in text moves the same model from 8.05 to 20.13 on Mini-ARC ([[wiki/concepts/perception-reasoning-dissociation.md]]). Two limits keep the row from `RESOLVED`. With perception errors at zero, a 10.5-point reasoning gap remains on ACRE. On Mini-ARC a strong describer recovers well under half the strong/weak model difference (31.54 vs 52.03).

**(iii) The deficit sits in the read, not the encoder** (`T388` `LIVE`). A linear probe on frozen vision features solves two BlindTest tasks at 99.47–100%, so the parse is computed and then not read. This is the interface problem of §5, arriving from vision.

**(iv) The right frame is task-dependent, not globally better** ([[wiki/entities/vdm-i2i.md]], `G73`). On the same ConceptARC concepts, an image frame and a text frame have anticorrelated profiles: Count 0.83 image vs 0.17 text, Order 0.07 vs 0.27.

**(v) The biology says the representation is built per step, and cheaply.**

| Result | Page | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Carrying an item costs more than looking again: up to 18 fixations per pattern under a speed instruction; 2–3 bound variables; the learner runs on 16 hand-chosen bits | [[wiki/concepts/active-vision.md]] | `G73`, `G74` `OPEN` |
| The grain of a number code follows from its log axis; the carving switches among three regimes by set size and density; a distractor overwrites the prefrontal copy and spares the parietal one | [[wiki/concepts/numerosity.md]] | `G4` `PARTIAL`, `G27` `CONTESTED`, `T385` `LIVE` |
| Imagery and perception sum on one cortical sheet, with no mode switch and no arbitrator | [[wiki/concepts/mental-imagery.md]] | `G90` `OPEN`, `T386` `BOTH` |
| Vision reaches the map by a third, parieto-medial-temporal pathway; every frame in it is physical-spatial | [[wiki/entities/dorsal-visual-stream.md]] | `G39` `OPEN` |

The summary: a parse is a program over a small fixed operation set, run per task and per step. The cheapest working system skips the parse entirely and still matches the human average. On ARC, most of what the wiki scored as reasoning failure is perception, and much of that perception is computed but never read.

### 26. A store's discreteness is a property of what was written, not of the network that holds it

Thirteen sources on associative stores and the attractor claims behind them, half of them primary records for results the wiki had been carrying through reviews. Section 4 said memory is the one component with numbers; this body asks what those numbers are numbers *of*, and the answer is three times "not the network".

**(i) Capacity is a choice of frame.** A degree-`n` energy takes `0.14N` to `α_n N^{n−1}` with code, state space and write rule untouched ([[wiki/entities/dense-associative-memory.md]]) — and the "`n`-body synapse" that made this look unbiological is an eliminated hidden layer ([[wiki/entities/two-body-dense-associative-memory.md]]) or eliminated astrocytic Ca²⁺ ([[wiki/entities/neuron-astrocyte-associative-memory.md]], `K^max ∼ N³` at `∼N²` compute units). Counted per parameter, nothing has moved since 1982: the best slot store is `C ≈ 1.0N` under a sequenced gate and `0.16N` under a random one ([[wiki/entities/three-factor-key-value-memory.md]]). What the line bought is **overload behaviour** — graded instead of a cliff — not density ([[wiki/concepts/associative-memory.md]]).

**(ii) Discreteness is set by anchor separation at the first write.** The ledger of state-switching results:

| Result | Reading | Row |
|---|---|---|
| Same morph probe, same circuit: abrupt coherent jump after two-location training, linear rate-only change after one-location training | Hysteresis measures how far apart the stored states were *written*, not whether the network has a state set; the steepest jump was in CA1, which has almost no recurrence ([[wiki/concepts/attractor-identification.md]]) | `T394` `LIVE`, `T389` `LIVE` |
| Under a cue switch CA3 commits to one chart per theta cycle; mixed cycles **1.25%**, below a unit shuffle | The read is one member, never a blend — and it is re-decided ~8 Hz on unchanged input ([[wiki/concepts/attractor-dynamics.md]]) | `G123` `OPEN` |
| Dentate output under graded morph moves gradually; under cue conflict it holds at 45° and breaks by 90° | Slope on content change, threshold on reference-frame conflict — one input-similarity axis cannot express the separator | `T393` `LIVE` |
| A trained linear layer *is* a key-value store keyed on its inputs with its own error signals as values | Continual training may destroy the **address**, not the trace ([[wiki/concepts/key-value-memory.md]]) | `T392` `LIVE` |

**(iii) Low dimensionality is not an attractor.** Of four state-space criteria only invariance is defining; localisation, flow-back and isometry are all reproducible by low-rank feedforward input ([[wiki/concepts/attractor-identification.md]]). The criteria pass where measured topologically — a ring with one `H1` and no `H2` in head direction, a twisted torus in 6/6 grid modules ([[wiki/concepts/topological-latent-decoding.md]]) — and the place code is the stock example that fails (`T389`).

**(iv) A manifold code is limited by its input, not its units.** The REM heading ring diffuses **20–50×** faster than independent per-neuron noise predicts, accounted for by manifold-aligned input noise of the same order as the waking velocity drive: the wire that updates the code is the wire that corrupts it, and every robustness number in the wiki is reported against the perturbation that cannot move it (`G124` `OPEN`).

**(v) Attention is mostly not retrieval.** Softmax attention is one step of a continuous modern Hopfield store ([[wiki/entities/continuous-modern-hopfield-network.md]]), yet trained heads mostly pool, lower ones replaceable by input-independent kernels at a 95.5× parameter cut — and heads that do separate stop learning, because a one-hot read zeroes the gradient that placed the keys (`T390` `LIVE`).

The summary: capacity, discreteness and noise floor are each a property of a frame, a write history or an input channel. A builder choosing "an attractor store" has chosen none of them yet; the decisions that matter are where anchors are placed at first write, whether the read is re-run (`G123`), and what noise the update channel carries (`G124`).

### 27. A manufactured want has a history, an opponent, a balance, a price and a supply — and every self-generated reward in the wiki has none of the five

Sixteen sources on how a want is built: conditioned reinforcement, tokens, the price of effort, and the option-discovery architectures that pay themselves. Every intrinsic reward the wiki holds is a fixed function of state or a decaying function of experience; the animal version of each has an update rule.

| Property | Biology | Every wiki mechanism | Row |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Extinguishes when claimed** | Conditioned value is lost when primary reward stops following it ([[wiki/concepts/conditioned-reinforcement.md]]) | constant pseudo-reward, fixed eigenvector reward | `G125` `OPEN` |
| **Trains its own suppressor** | Second-order conditioning *is* a feature-negative discrimination: excitation fast, inhibition slower and ultimately stronger, net pull an inverted U in training ([[wiki/concepts/higher-order-conditioning.md]]) | no opponent term | `G126` `OPEN`, `T395` `LIVE` |
| **Held as a balance** | Tokens are earned, made spendable and redeemed on three schedules; choice follows the redemption schedule (preference reversal, 3/3 subjects) ([[wiki/concepts/token-reinforcement.md]]) | spent the step it is earned | `G127` `OPEN` |
| **Cost has a write port** | Effort is a price revealed by choice ([[wiki/concepts/effort-based-decision-making.md]]); paired with reward, effort acquires secondary value that transfers across tasks ([[wiki/concepts/learned-industriousness.md]]) | fixed function of load | `G128` `OPEN`, `T397` `T398` `LIVE` |
| **Grows with the store** | Reward emitted at the known/unknown boundary: supply `αK(N − K)` rises over the first half of a large domain ([[wiki/concepts/self-boosting-knowledge-acquisition.md]]) | exhausted by learning | `G129` `OPEN` |

Three further results re-price machinery already here. **Predictor ≠ incentive**: one pairing yields a cue that is approached for itself or a cue that only predicts, and dopamine antagonism blocks acquisition of the first only ([[wiki/concepts/sign-tracking-and-goal-tracking.md]], `T404` `LIVE`). **Dopamine also writes valueless links**: the same transient carries a state prediction error where no reward exists ([[wiki/concepts/state-prediction-error.md]]). **Exploration is a life-history phase**, a subsidised high-temperature period handed over to exploit machinery that suppresses it, not a knob ([[wiki/concepts/explore-exploit-division-of-labour.md]], `T399` `LIVE`). And the machine architectures each fail at the junction the biology prices: option-critic needs three additions its return objective does not motivate ([[wiki/entities/option-critic.md]]); DIAYN holds skill count only by fixing the prior ([[wiki/entities/diayn.md]]); Go-Explore wins by a stored frontier, not a decomposition (`T402` `LIVE`); asymmetric self-play needs hand-written legality predicates and may teach through the solutions it ships (`T403` `LIVE`).

The summary: **no self-generated motivational quantity in the wiki has an update rule.** The five rows are cheap — a second learned term, a counter, a redemption contingency — and they are the concrete form of `G72` (`OPEN`) and `G102` (`OPEN`): an agent cannot decline to deploy a capability while its wants have no state to decline with.

### 28. A connection is priced by what its reader can do with it, and a measurement by what the reader ignores

Twenty-three sources on edges, groups and instruments. One result recurs: an edge, a hyperedge or a decodable direction adds nothing unless a downstream reader is built to use it, and every instrument here measured the object, not the reader.

**(i) Edge density is measured on both sides, and neither side says what it buys** (`G101`, `OPEN`).

| Side | Result | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Primates, 25 species | Synapse density flat (mean 256 M/mm³). Synapses per neuron rise with brain mass, 671 → 5,081. Humans on the primate line | [[wiki/concepts/cellular-scaling-rules.md]] |
| Human individuals | Thicker L2/L3 is larger cells at lower density, not more neurons, and tracks IQ. Correlational, second-hand | [[wiki/entities/human-supragranular-pyramidal-neuron.md]] |
| One cell, 24 models | Dendritic area plus longest internal branch predict I/O complexity (`R²` = 0.81). Buys complexity, not accuracy | [[wiki/concepts/neuron-complexity-index.md]] |
| ResNet-50, width fixed | Monotone accuracy with diminishing returns. At every matched weight count, units beat edges per unit (+2.5 / +3.1 at 1×) | [[wiki/entities/rigl.md]] |
| CNN top layers, width fixed | Learned placement beats random placement at equal density (90.02 vs 88.26%) | [[wiki/entities/sparse-evolutionary-training.md]] |

The ratio rises *with* unit count across species, so nature never varied edges at fixed units. The machine sweeps do that, but only on i.i.d. accuracy, never on the `G70`/`G21` failures the row exists for. Whether RigL already meets `Closes when` is flagged on the gap for the curator.

The primate baseline under `G101` is older than the table above. Brain mass scales as `N^a`, and the exponent says whether a lineage bought units or wiring per unit: primates add units at near-invariant density (cortex `a = 1.077`), while a 10× unit increase costs a rodent brain 35× the mass against 11× for a primate ([[wiki/concepts/cellular-scaling-rules.md]], `T301` `LIVE`). The long-range wire scales sub-isometrically: callosal cross-section grows as cortical surface^0.88, so the human callosum serves 211 cm² of cortex per cm² against 90 in galago ([[wiki/concepts/connectivity-scaling-bottleneck.md]], `G84` `OPEN`, `T290` `LIVE`). Wiring inside one cell buys depth: a layer-5 pyramidal neuron needs a 5–8-layer network to fit, and one hidden layer once NMDA is removed ([[wiki/concepts/neuron-complexity-index.md]], `T302` `LIVE`).

**(ii) A group is invisible to a pairwise estimator, and every hypergraph learner here is pairwise** (`G105`, `PARTIAL`). An `n`-bit xor has every pairwise mutual information at zero, so covariance, attention and transition-count estimators return the empty graph ([[wiki/concepts/o-information.md]]). A pairwise relation learner scores 100.0 on binary relations and 35.6 / 41.5 on ternary ones ([[wiki/entities/esbn.md]]). The best-known hypergraph network is exactly a graph convolution on the clique projection ([[wiki/entities/hgnn.md]]), and the one estimator that searches for groups proposes only cliques of an observed pairwise graph ([[wiki/entities/bayesian-hypergraph-reconstruction.md]]). The governing rule: a group term matters only to a reader that is nonlinear in the group ([[wiki/concepts/higher-order-interactions.md]]).

**(iii) Decodable is not used, and used is not complete** (`T25`, `LEANING B`; `T158`, `LEANING`). Amnesic probing erases a property and scores the frozen reader: probe accuracy does not predict use (Spearman 0.085), phrase boundaries decode ~25 points above majority and their erasure *helps*, and named entities decode 7.4 points above majority yet their erasure costs 10.9 points against 1.4 for a random control ([[wiki/concepts/amnesic-probing.md]]). Causal scrubbing tests a whole hypothesis by resampling everything it calls interchangeable: three claims recover 93%, 88% and 84% of loss alone and 72% together, and the test can pass a hypothesis that omits an inhibitory circuit ([[wiki/concepts/causal-scrubbing.md]]).

**(iv) The interface between hierarchy levels has the same shape** (`T406`, `LIVE`). On one agent, raw-state subgoals score 3.02 on Ant Gather against 0.03 for learned-representation goals ([[wiki/entities/hiro.md]]). A learned goal space works when an action-conditioned predictive objective trains it and fails when the RL gradient does, with a return-loss bound on the coarsening ([[wiki/entities/near-optimal-goal-representation.md]]). The variable that decides is *what trains the interface*, which neither position named.

**(v) Re-reading is measured once and gated by a hand-set criterion.** [[wiki/entities/ircot.md]] keys each retrieval on the reasoning sentence just generated. At a matched 15-paragraph budget, recall rises 3.5–22.6 points over a single question-based read, and generated-chain factual errors fall 40–50%. This is the wiki's first measured multi-pass read, but it is not the revision `G123` (`OPEN`) asks for, because no earlier read is ever withdrawn. [[wiki/entities/self-rag.md]] makes the number of reads a model output. On PopQA its own retrieve decision scores 28.3; a hand-set threshold on the same token scores 45.5. The gate's ranking is usable, but its criterion is not, which leaves the price of a read external (`G107` `OPEN`, `G15` `PARTIAL`).

The summary: the wiki can now count edges, groups and decodable directions, and in each case the count was the wrong quantity. What a connection buys is set by its reader.

### 29. The Papez loop is two wires from one source, and the heading integrator's input is a reconstruction, not a sensor

Five sources on the hippocampal–diencephalic–cingulate network and the heading system. Every page here had drawn this circuit as a relay. The anatomy says otherwise in three places.

**(i) The routes split at the cell of origin, so the relay question is per channel** (`T381`, `LIVE`). Deep, middle and distal subicular cells project to anterior thalamus, mammillary bodies and retrosplenial cortex respectively, and no tract lesion isolates the cortical route ([[wiki/entities/hippocampal-diencephalic-cingulate-network.md]]). The mammillary stage carries two variables. Heading is generated below the store and survives hippocampal lesions. Theta comes from the store ([[wiki/entities/mammillary-bodies.md]]).

**(ii) Content and clock travel on separate wires and multiply in the receiver** (`G54`, `PARTIAL`). Lateral mammillary → `AD` carries heading. Medial mammillary → `AV` carries theta, where ~75% of cells are theta-locked. One synapse later, 39% of `AV` heading cells fire only in theta-paced trains ([[wiki/entities/anterior-thalamic-nuclei.md]]). So `h(heading)·c(phase)`: separate ablatability is a property of a *segment* of a pathway, upstream of the merge.

**(iii) The integrator's velocity input is not the sensor reading** (`T408`, `LIVE`; `G92`, `OPEN`).

| Stage | Measurement | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Vestibular nuclei | Vestibular-only neurons ~70% attenuated during self-generated motion, only when neck proprioception confirms the motor expectation. Recalibrated in ~40–50 movements after a torque change | [[wiki/entities/head-direction-system.md]] |
| Thalamic perception route | Active motion ~80% cancelled; a neck command with the head blocked changes nothing | same |
| Vestibulocerebellum | Recodes head rotation to rotation about gravity; active and passive differ (`p` = 0.0021), kinematics confounded | same |
| Ring, no motion | Direction held; contrast relaxes over ~4 s to about half | same |

Biology computes the agent/world partition at the first central synapse, per variable and by wiring, and passes both halves on. That is `G92`'s statistic hard-coded, not derived. A third mechanism neither position states, a gated *selector* between sources, predicts all-or-none errors where a sum predicts graded ones.

**(iv) The anchor is delivered from below the store** (`G39`, `OPEN`). The heading the transform bank consumes comes from a one-way chain the hippocampus cannot write to. Gravity, read by a dedicated sensor, removes one rotation axis before any landmark is used. Tilted mice keep tuning within a session and lose it between sessions, so anchoring has a within-episode and a cross-episode half ([[wiki/gaps/g039.md]]).

The summary: the wiki's "relay" arrows were each two things — two routes, two wires, two halves of self-motion — and the anchor every retrieval needs is computed outside the store that retrieves.

### 30. The automatic store is taught by the action, not the outcome, and the novelty gate sees nodes, not edges

**(i) A value-free teacher for the habit store, measured** ([[wiki/concepts/action-prediction-error.md]]). Tail-of-striatum dopamine carries `δ_APE = a − A(s)`, unaffected by reward size or omission, and shrinks as the action becomes predictable. Stimulation at choice time builds a bias, while stimulation at outcome time does nothing. Three rows move:

| Row | Move | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `T405` | `LIVE` → `LEANING B` | The store carrying expert choice has a teacher with no outcome term. No devaluation or omission test, so not closed |
| `G51` | `OPEN` → `PARTIAL` | A named transfer operation: online behaviour cloning on an efference copy, with the source's weights decaying. Solved between two striatal learners on 2 × 2 choice, not out of the control layer |
| `T131` | `LEANING A` → `LIVE` | Inactivating the late striatal site impairs experts. Sequences may distil into cortex while stimulus→response maps stay in striatum (untested) |

The design rule needs nothing striatal: any controller that emits an efference copy, paired with a store trained on `a − p`, transfers by the same rule. The open part is whether prefrontal control emits the copy.

**(ii) Recognition is two signals, and the cheap one lives outside the store** (`T407`, `LIVE`; [[wiki/concepts/recollection-familiarity.md]], [[wiki/entities/perirhinal-cortex.md]]). Perirhinal familiarity is a one-trial repetition decrement readable at ~75 ms, as fast as identity. The reverse case now exists: a resection sparing the hippocampus left recollection normal and familiarity impaired under bias-corrected ROC. It is one patient with a larger-than-perirhinal lesion, so the row stays open. The architectural consequence is the transferable part. Familiarity reports an **unseen node**, and the hippocampus reports an **unseen arrangement of seen nodes**. These are two update triggers, allocate a node or revise an edge, and every machine novelty signal in the wiki merges them.

**(iii) The great-ape check on the unit axis.** Gorilla and orangutan cerebella sit on the primate line, and the human cortex is over-predicted by 31% in neurons, a modest per-unit excess far below the 2.75× volume figure section 17 relied on ([[wiki/concepts/cellular-scaling-rules.md]], `T301` `LIVE`, `G101`).

The summary: the automatic store needs no reward, only the action and a copy of it, and the novelty signal that should trigger a write is two signals that need two different writes.

Meanwhile a parse-free classifier reaches the human ARC-1 average, so the row must also say what a constructed parse buys that a metric on the input does not |
| 17 | **G125–G129 — no self-generated reward has an update rule** | Extinction on claim, an opponent, a held balance, a writable cost, a supply that grows with the store: biology supplies all five, every intrinsic reward here has none. It is the motivational half of `G102` and the only route to `G72` that does not go through a designer-written reward |
| 18 | **G19/T328 — nothing computes the address of a teaching signal** | Every update-rule source in section 21 says the address is load-bearing and the magnitude is not. The in vivo measurement shows addressed delivery in a paradigm with no hidden layer, so where the address comes from is untested. Machine credit assignment gets it free from `Wᵀ`, and every substitute that falsifies the routing fails at ImageNet |
| 19 | **G68/G89 — nothing rejects, and the one-float calibrator repairs only the bias half** | The rejector literature supplied a metric (`meta-d′/d′`), a guaranteed abstainer and a calibrator. The calibrator is rank-preserving, so the discrimination half of `G89` is untouched. Raw max-softmax is the baseline to beat on closed sets and is anti-correlated with correctness on open ones (`T320`) |
| 20 | **G123/G124 — a store reads once, and its noise floor is set by its input wire** | CA3 re-decides one chart per theta cycle on unchanged input; the heading ring's drift is 20–50× what unit noise predicts. Both say robustness and retrieval are properties of the read schedule and the update channel, which no store here parameterises |

## Promising Directions

- **Give one interface state and see what happens.** The cheapest experiment in the wiki: a two-module model with a learned interface, plus a third module emitting a binary write-mask on that interface's plasticity. Measure continual-learning interference. It costs a mask, and it is the only row of G52's table that does not need an architecture change.
- **Build the level boundary as a narrowing, not as a concatenation.** Hand level `k−1` the abstract variable and the resolved output, and architecturally deny it the observation. Non-bypassability by wiring rather than by auxiliary loss — the standard feudal failure mode removed by construction (G59).
- **Merge the two de-aliasing mechanisms.** A clone-structured hidden Markov model de-aliases fast, locally and without transfer ([[wiki/entities/cscg.md]]); path integration de-aliases by position and transfers but is slow to acquire ([[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]]). Named as the obvious next model by more than one source and never done.
- **Price `g` and `x` on separate budgets.** The nearest available shape for G30: minimise description length *subject to* the code being reusable across instances of a family, plus a path-commutativity residual computable from trajectories alone.
- **Score the learning trajectory, not the endpoint.** On one aliased task a clone model, a softmax recurrent network and a spiking Hebbian network all reach the endpoint mouse CA1 reaches, and only the *order* of disambiguation distinguishes them (G46). This is an instrument the wiki can build today and it needs no distribution shift.
- **Tie recurrent read-out gain and recurrent learning rate to one variable, inversely.** Directly copyable from the cholinergic mechanism; makes the separation/completion knob a single scalar with a functional form.
- **Take the replay filter seriously as a curriculum generator.** Biology's offline pass upsamples rarely-visited states and prefers remote to imminent trajectories — an inductive bias toward *transferable* content, implementable today, orthogonal to everything machine replay does.
- **Steepen the read-out nonlinearity before touching anything else.** Sequence capacity moves from linear to polynomial or exponential through one scalar, with code, sparsity and learning rule untouched — the cheapest capacity lever in the wiki.
- **Build the description channel** (G45). A verbal statement of a task's latent structure produced a hippocampal geometry indistinguishable from one discovered through experience. No architecture has a channel by which a description *redirects what the learner factorises*; the cheap test is specified and unrun.
- **Constrain relations, not placements.** Every anti-collapse provision but one constrains where a *single* code sits; Gram anchoring constrains the matrix of pairwise similarities among codes within one input, and it was invented to fix a failure the other five could not see. That is the shape everything in the latent-graph framing wants, and it exists as a two-line loss ([[wiki/entities/dinov3.md]]).
- **Run the pair sampler as a designed object.** It is the biggest measured lever in that literature and nobody reports it as a parameter. The cheap version: sample `(x, y)` pairs to maximise a *conditional-excitation* statistic rather than by a fixed masking heuristic, which also attacks G63 from the data side.
- **Score the world model with a planner attached** (G62). That whole literature trains on prediction loss and reports prediction loss; the one quantity anyone actually wants is regret of a planner running on the model, and it is computable in any environment the model was fitted from.
- **Compute a metaparameter's set-point from the learner's own second-order statistics** rather than tuning it. That is the one design move the normative literature hands over intact, it applies to every hyperparameter by construction, and the striatal case shows one modulator setting a gain *and* a learning rate at once, which no architecture here does (G64).
- **Run the `γ_effective` readout.** Fit a discount kernel to a trained agent's choices, fit a second to a unit's activations, test both covariance and zero mean offset. If an exponential-`γ` agent's value units return a hyperbolic neurometric function, the human hyperbola stops being evidence about the algorithm (T141); the multi-horizon bank already shows a hyperbola needs no new learning rule.
- **Ablate the interface, not the architecture.** Every hybrid spiking result beats a single-paradigm baseline and none compares a *learnable* interface module against a fixed converter at matched architecture (T235). That one control decides whether module boundaries are trainable objects — the whole G52/G21 cluster — and it costs a second run.
- **Report units-to-competence, not accuracy.** If the spiking substrate's proven advantage is unit count rather than expressive power ([[wiki/concepts/circuit-size-separation.md]]), then the only admissible evidence for it is the number of units a given competence costs, and the wiki has one such number (delays: equal accuracy at ~5× fewer parameters), which must be read with its weight budget ([[wiki/concepts/width-versus-parameter-count.md]]). It is derivable from runs that already exist.
- **Make delays the learnable parameter.** `n` delays carry VC dimension `Θ(n log n)` against `n` weights' `Θ(n)`, and an exact gradient for them now exists ([[wiki/concepts/learnable-synaptic-delays.md]]); no non-spiking architecture in the wiki uses it.
- **Instrument the read before optimising the write.** The two results in section 16 point the same way: forgetting can be driven to zero while the gate that decides which expert to invoke scores 31%, and every prioritised replay rule loses to uniform sampling once tasks arrive in sequence. Log *which* stored structure was retrieved and score that separately from what was stored — the cheapest version of `P16`, now with a number to beat.
- **Price every interpretation on a simplification ladder.** [[wiki/concepts/multi-token-embedding.md]]'s effective-model protocol — replace each component with the simplest stand-in, re-run end to end, report the accuracy paid at each rung — is directly applicable to the wiki's own entity pages and is the first instrument here that costs an interpretation instead of asserting it.
- **Make the write gate fall out of the learning rule.** [[wiki/concepts/encoding-retrieval-alternation.md]]: alternate read and write phases fast enough that an already-known item produces a null update. Redundancy then costs no capacity with no importance estimate, no task boundary and no exemplar buffer — the three things every machine continual learner in the wiki needs.
- **Give one architecture a growth schedule** (`G100`). The comparative literature measures the shape it should have: a *vector of per-module phase offsets* over a trajectory whose form is held fixed, sparse (~4% of components), not a global slowdown. Cheaper than any new module; growth itself now exists ([[wiki/entities/ligo.md]]'s learned per-layer blend), but every growth event is placed by the experimenter, so the schedule is still untried.
- **Score capability nulls under two frames, not one** (`T291`). Every null result on every benchmark here was collected under a single framing; the comparative literature gets opposite answers from the same subjects under cooperative versus competitive framings. A second frame is the cheapest re-analysis in the wiki and it re-prices the whole benchmark half.
- **Select biological mechanisms by independent re-invention count** ([[wiki/concepts/convergent-circuit-motifs.md]]). A motif arrived at separately by mollusc, arthropod, annelid and vertebrate is a repeated experiment rather than one sample — the first selection criterion the transfer channel has ever had.
- **Run the router control `T296` names.** Learned vs. hash vs. random routing at matched compute, on a task where the correct expert for a token depends on context. It decides whether any routing result in the wiki is evidence of decomposition at all; [[wiki/entities/rims.md]] is the architecture to run it on.
- **Split the confidence test by response format** (`T320`). Score max-softmax selective risk on the multiple-choice slice of [[wiki/entities/hle.md]] or on [[wiki/entities/gpqa.md]] against the free-generation slice of the same model. If label-set closedness is the boundary, abstention is a candidate-set construction problem, not a monitor problem.
- **Run the recombination test on released disentanglement models** (`T326`). Locatello's seven datasets and models are released; nobody has scored them on factor combinations absent from training, which is the only claim the factorization premise makes.
- **Put the error two synapses from the reward** (`T328`). Re-run the addressed-teaching-signal design with the decoder on a population downstream of the imaged cells. A residual that still flips sign with *indirect* causal role is credit crossing a hop, and that is the first in vivo test of depth ([[wiki/concepts/vectorized-instructive-signals.md]]).
- **Supply the oracle parse and re-score** (`T215`). It is the row's own discriminating experiment, and it is still unrun. The VLM-described approximation already moves Mini-ARC from 8.05 to 20.13, and only an oracle object list separates `LEANING B` from `RESOLVED B`.
- **Give a subgoal reward an opponent and a balance** (`G126`, `G127`). Two separately-learned opposite-signed terms with different time constants plus a token counter with its own redemption contingency; the test is the inverted U in training amount and the redemption-schedule preference reversal, both specified in the gap rows.
- **Run the forced-action habit test** (`T405`). Force an agent's actions for `N` trials under zero reward, release it, and measure the logit bias by `N`. A cached-value store predicts no bias; an action-frequency store predicts one that grows with `N`.
- **Split novelty into node novelty and edge novelty** (`T407`). A one-shot item habituation trace beside the store reports unseen nodes; the store's mismatch on known parts reports unseen arrangements. Route the first to allocation and the second to edge revision ([[wiki/concepts/recollection-familiarity.md]]).
- **Propose hyperedges by synergy, not by clique membership** (`G105`). The Bayesian hypergraph sampler already searches with calibrated per-group probabilities; seed its factors from `Ω < 0` triples instead of cliques of a pairwise graph, so a zero-pairwise-signal group can be proposed at all.
- **Energy-based framing as the unifying language.** An edge label and a latent variable are the same free variable; path search is `argmin` over a sequence of them; equilibrium propagation then supplies objective and learning rule in one choice; and the precision ratio makes the choice between that and backpropagation a single scalar.

## Major Controversies

The full list is [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] (360 rows, `grep -cE '^\| T[0-9]+ \|' wiki/empirical-tensions.md`). The load-bearing ones:

| # | Question | Status |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | **Does the implementation level carry computational content?** | `LEANING B`. Rebuilding a rate model in spiking neurons made three mechanisms *inexpressible in the original specification* necessary for its central representation (grid emergence 59.6% → 0.00% without neuromodulatory gain); barn-owl coincidence detection reaches 20–25 µs precision from 250 µs potentials |
| T234 | **Is the spiking substrate's advantage expressive power, or unit count?** | `LIVE`, and it caps T1 from inside T1's own camp: for piecewise-linear postsynaptic potentials, spiking and sigmoidal circuits are mutually simulable in `O(s)` units, so every proven separation is in units (`Ω(n)`, ~1663 at realistic fan-in), never in computability. Under this reading a spiking result must show units-to-competence, which nothing in the wiki reports |
| T236 | **Does smoothing a spike train before measuring error destroy the timing code?** | `LEANING B`, and it reverses a reading the wiki held for a long stretch: the filtered error matches the edit-distance loss at 1 ms and holds at 0.2 ms, while the *unsmoothed* rule is the one that collapses — its attractor is the postsynaptic-potential peak, which is independent of the target time |
| T14 | **Are structured representations real, or emergent descriptions of sub-symbolic processes?** | `LIVE` — and unresolvable by construction: the two sides make identical predictions on any single trained task and differ only on transfer, re-goaling and query richness, i.e. exactly the instruments G17 says do not exist |
