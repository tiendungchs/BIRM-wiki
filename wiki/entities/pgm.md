# PGM and WReN — a Raven-style benchmark whose train/test split is a controlled variable, and the relation-comparison network built for it

**Procedurally Generated Matrices (PGM): the same 3×3-matrix-plus-8-candidates format as [[wiki/entities/raven.md]], but generated from an explicit symbolic structure `S = {[r, o, a]}` (relation, object, attribute) so that *what differs between train and test* can be specified at the level of the abstraction rather than at the level of pixels. Eight such regimes were run against one architecture, the Wild Relation Network (WReN), and they split cleanly in two: recombining familiar `[r, o, a]` triples costs 20 points, applying a familiar relation to an unfamiliar attribute or an out-of-range value costs 45–52 and lands at or below the answer-set baseline.**

> **Provenance.** Barrett, Hill, Santoro, Morcos & Lillicrap 2018, *Measuring abstract reasoning in neural networks* (`raw/barrett-2018-measuring-abstract-reasoning.md`, DeepMind, ICML). All numbers are the paper's own. The appendix (dataset statistics, per-question-type tables, hyper-parameters) is not in the ingested text, so figures cited there are absent here rather than approximated.

## The generator

| Primitive set | Elements |
|---|---|
| Relations `R` | progression, XOR, OR, AND, **consistent union** (the three panels draw elements from one common set; panel order irrelevant) |
| Objects `O` | shape, line |
| Attributes `A` | size, type, colour, position, number |

A matrix's abstract content is a set of triples `S = {[r, o, a]}` with `1 ≤ |S| ≤ 4` — e.g. `[progression, shape, colour]` is "greyscale intensity of the shapes increases along rows". Generation: (1) sample 1–4 triples; (2) sample values `v ∈ V` for each attribute in `S` obeying its relation; (3) sample values for attributes *not* in `S` such that **no further relation is induced**; (4) render to 80×80 pixels. Step 3 has two settings: **distracting** (off-structure attributes vary freely) or constant. Distractors cost the model 15.7 points (62.6% with, 78.3% without) — i.e. roughly a quarter of the achieved performance is spent on deciding *which* attributes carry the rule.

**The design move worth carrying.** Because the abstraction is an explicit symbolic object *before* it is pixels, a train/test split can be defined over `S` — hold out a triple, a pair of triples, a pair of attributes, half of a value range. This is what [[wiki/entities/raven.md]]'s grammar and [[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]]'s hand-authoring both fail to give: generalisation stops being one word and becomes eight separately-scored quantities.

## Models, neutral split

| Model | Test (%) | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **WReN** | **62.6** | CNN per panel → panel embeddings → Relation Network over the 8 context panels + one candidate → scalar score; 8 independent passes, softmax over the scores |
| Wild-ResNet | 48.0 | Same per-candidate scoring structure, ResNet instead of the Relation Network |
| ResNet-50 | 42.0 | All 16 panels as input feature maps |
| LSTM | 35.8 | Panels embedded by a small CNN, position-tagged, read sequentially |
| CNN + MLP | 33.0 | 4-layer CNN over the 16 panels |
| **Context-blind ResNet** | **22.4** | ResNet-50 shown the **8 candidates only**, never the matrix (chance = 12.5%) |

WReN's score for candidate `k`: `s_k = f_φ(Σ_{i,j} g_θ(x_i, x_j))` over `X_k = {x_1…x_8} ∪ {c_k}` — `g_θ` an MLP on *pairs* of panel embeddings, `f_φ` an MLP on their sum. Two properties do the work, and the paper separates them: **pairwise relation comparison** and **per-candidate scoring with shared weights**. Wild-ResNet has the second and not the first, and gives up 14.6 points, so the relational bottleneck is the load-bearing half.

**The two baselines that frame every other number here.** Chance is 12.5%; the *answer-set-only* score is 22.4%. So PGM leaks through the candidate set too — 10 points of it — but an order of magnitude less than the original RAVEN's >90% context-blind result, because distractors are not generated by one-attribute perturbation of the answer. The lesson is that a multiple-choice benchmark's floor must be *measured*, not assumed at chance ([[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]]).

## The eight regimes — the core result

WReN, ordered by generalisation error at `β = 0` (no auxiliary loss). `Diff` = test − validation, i.e. the cost of the held-out abstraction with task difficulty partly held fixed.

| Regime | What is held out | Val (%) | Test (%) | Diff | Test with `β = 10` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral | nothing (pixel-level disjoint only) | 63.0 | 62.6 | −0.6 | **76.9** |
| Interpolation | odd-indexed values of colour/size | 79.0 | 64.4 | −14.6 | 67.4 |
| **H.O. Attribute Pairs** | 4 of 20 attribute pairs | 46.7 | 27.2 | −19.5 | **51.7** |
| **H.O. Triple Pairs** | 40 of 400 triple pairs | 63.9 | 41.9 | −22.0 | **56.3** |
| H.O. Triples | 7 of 29 triples | 63.4 | 19.0 | −44.4 | 20.1 |
| H.O. line-type | the attribute `type` on lines | 59.5 | 14.4 | −45.1 | 16.4 |
| H.O. shape-colour | the attribute `colour` on shapes | 59.1 | 12.5 | −46.6 | 13.0 |
| **Extrapolation** | upper half of the colour/size value range | 69.3 | 17.2 | −52.1 | 15.5 |

Absolute scores are not comparable across regimes (test sets differ in underlying difficulty), which is why `Diff` is the column to read.

**The split is sharp and it is the paper's export.**

- **Novel *combinations* of familiar triples generalise.** Held-out triple pairs and attribute pairs sit 20 points down but far above the 22.4% blind baseline — the model composed `[r,o,a]` units it had seen separately, rather than memorising each `S` as an atom. This is systematicity, passed, at a measurable discount.
- **Novel *constituents* do not.** Held-out triples (19.0), held-out line-type (14.4) and held-out shape-colour (12.5) are at or below the answer-set baseline: **the model cannot induce an unfamiliar triple from its familiar components, and cannot carry a known relation onto an attribute it never saw that relation on.** A human who can XOR the colour of lines has no trouble XOR-ing the colour of shapes; WReN is at chance.
- **Extrapolation is the worst regime of the eight (−52.1).** Interpolation and extrapolation pose *identical abstract structure*; only the numeric range differs. So the failure is not in the relation representation at all — it is perceptual, a refusal to encode inputs outside the trained range. Abstraction and the front-end that feeds it fail separately and the benchmark can tell them apart.

## Auxiliary symbolic supervision — the one lever that moved anything

Each PGM's structure is encoded as a 12-bit **meta-target** — `(shape, line, colour, number, position, size, type, progression, XOR, OR, AND, consistent-union)` — by OR-ing the binary encodings of its triples. Predicted through sigmoid units, `L_total = L_target + β·L_meta-target`.

| Effect | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Neutral split | 62.6 → 76.9 (**+13.9**) |
| Held-out triple *pairs* | 41.9 → **56.3** |
| Held-out attribute *pairs* | 27.2 → **51.7** (nearly doubled) |
| Held-out triples / attributes / extrapolation | +1.1 / +2.0 / +0.5 / **−1.7** — i.e. nothing |
| Accuracy when the meta-target prediction was right vs wrong | **87.4% vs 34.8%** |
| Same split on the *relation* bit alone | **86.8% vs 32.1%** (object bit: 78.2 vs 62.2; attribute bit: 79.5 vs 49.0) |
| Prediction certainty (mean \|p − 0.5\|) vs accuracy | Monotone — the meta-target confidence is a usable confidence estimate for the *answer* |

Three readings:

1. **Supervising the explanation buys composition, not extension.** Every regime the auxiliary loss helps is a recombination regime; every regime it fails to help requires a constituent the training set never contained. Pressure to decode representations into discrete symbols makes existing pieces recombinable and does not manufacture pieces.
2. **The relation bit is the bottleneck.** A 54-point accuracy swing on getting the relation right, against 16 for the object type, localises the difficulty to the relation slot — the same localisation the caption-network failures give qualitatively ([[wiki/concepts/compositionality.md]]).
3. **This is a 2018 precedent for process supervision**, and the wiki should date it as such: [[wiki/concepts/rule-level-evaluation.md]] proposes supervising the stated rule rather than the answer as a *next step*; PGM ran the experiment with the rule reduced to a 12-bit vector, measured +13.9 points, and mapped exactly where the gain stops. A free by-product is that the meta-target is a **read-out of what the model thinks the matrix is about** — an interpretability channel and a calibration signal obtained from the training target itself, not from a probe ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]).

## Per-question-type profile

| Contrast | Scores |
|---|---|
| 1 triple vs 2–4 | Single-triple easier; **3 triples harder than 4** (more relations, but also more evidence per hypothesis) |
| OR vs XOR (single triple) | 64.7 vs 53.2 |
| Lines vs shapes | 78.3 vs 46.2 |
| shape-number vs shape-size | **80.1 vs 26.4** |

The 54-point shape-number/shape-size gap is a *perceptual* gradient, not a relational one: the same relations, carried by an attribute whose values are harder to discriminate. Together with the extrapolation result this makes PGM the wiki's cleanest case that **an abstract-reasoning score is a product of a perceptual factor and a relational factor**, and that a single accuracy number reports neither — the same factorisation [[wiki/concepts/rule-level-evaluation.md]] recovers on ConceptARC seven years later by asking for the rule.

## Comparison

| | PGM | [[wiki/entities/raven.md]] (original) | [[wiki/entities/conceptarc.md]] | [[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Items from | Symbolic structure `S` → renderer | Hierarchical image grammar | Hand-authored around 16 concepts | Hand-authored |
| Answer | 8 candidates | 8 candidates | Constructed | Constructed |
| Context-blind baseline | **22.4%** (chance 12.5) | **>90%** | n/a | n/a |
| Train/test relation | **8 declared regimes over the abstraction** | i.i.d. | Disjoint concepts, i.i.d. within | Disjoint tasks |
| Human baseline | **none collected** | 84% | ~91% per-concept | ~75–85% |
| Training-set size assumed | Hundreds of thousands | 42,000 | none (few-shot) | none (few-shot) |

**The limitation the authors state themselves.** The model's world is a handful of relations over finite attribute sets, seen hundreds of thousands of times; a human taking an RPM transfers from everyday life. So PGM measures *within-domain* generalisation across declared axes, and its transfer results say nothing about the acquisition problem [[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]] prices. No human baseline was collected on PGM at all, which makes every "notably weak" verdict here machine-relative.

## Limitations

- **No human baseline**, on any regime — the paper's comparisons to humans are argued, not measured.
- **The candidate set still leaks 10 points** above chance.
- **One architecture across the eight regimes.** The regime profile is WReN's; nothing establishes it as a property of the benchmark.
- **The regimes are chosen, not exhaustive** ("in no way exhaustive"). The set covers value-range shift and combination novelty; it has no regime for a novel *relation*, no length/complexity extrapolation beyond `|S| = 4`, and no compositional-depth axis.
- **Generalising the method to natural data is proposed and untested** (train on black horses and flying seagulls, test on white horses and nesting sparrows).

## Connections

- **[[wiki/entities/raven.md]]** — the sibling generator and the contrast that prices multiple-choice design: same format, but PGM's distractors are not one-attribute perturbations of the answer, so the context-blind score falls from >90% to 22.4% — and PGM adds the axis RAVEN lacks entirely, a train/test split declared over the abstraction rather than over pixels.
- **[[wiki/concepts/compositionality.md]]** — the facet decomposition measured on a visual benchmark and on one architecture: systematicity passes at a 20-point discount (held-out triple/attribute pairs) while the constituent-novelty and value-range regimes collapse to the blind baseline, which places the failure at vocabulary extension rather than at recombination.
- **[[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]]** — supplies the surprisingly-strong-baseline instrument as a *reported column* rather than an afterthought: a context-blind ResNet at 22.4% sets the floor every regime score must be read against, and three of the eight regimes fail to clear it.
- **[[wiki/concepts/abstract-structural-codes.md]]** — the negative result stated in that page's vocabulary: WReN holds a relation bound to the object-attribute channel it was trained through (XOR-on-line-colour does not become XOR-on-shape-colour), so the relation is stored as content indexed by channel rather than as a content-invariant structure.
- **[[wiki/concepts/rule-level-evaluation.md]]** — the 2018 precedent for supervising the abstraction instead of the answer: the 12-bit meta-target is a machine-readable stand-in for the stated rule, worth +13.9 points on the neutral split, and the 87.4%-vs-34.8% accuracy split on whether that rule was predicted correctly is the same factorisation of a score into finding-the-rule and applying-it.
- **[[wiki/concepts/analogical-mapping.md]]** — WReN is the mapping problem solved by exhaustive pairwise comparison: `g_θ` over all panel pairs and a sum, with no alignment, no consistency constraint and no candidate structure — and it beats a ResNet by 20 points while failing exactly where a structural mapper would be expected to succeed, on carrying a known relation to new objects.
- **[[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]** — a probe that costs nothing extra because it *is* the training target: the meta-target says which relations, objects and attributes the model believes are present, its confidence predicts answer accuracy, and it is supervised rather than decoded — the opposite end of that page's spectrum from a post-hoc linear probe.
- **[[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]]** — the case the theory prices as near-zero generalisation difficulty and that measures it anyway: an unlimited public generator plus hundreds of thousands of examples buys 62.6%, and every regime that steps outside the generator's sampled combinations costs 20–52 points.
- **[[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]]** — the opposite design point on every axis (constructed answers, hand-authored tasks, no training set) and the one PGM can inform: ARC has no mechanism for declaring *which* abstraction the test set withholds, which is the quantity PGM makes into eight numbers.
- **[[wiki/entities/conceptarc.md]]** — the same instrument reached from the hand-authored side: concept groups × variations is a held-out-attribute regime authored rather than generated, with a human baseline PGM never collected and without PGM's ability to specify the held-out abstraction formally.
- **[[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]** — the benchmark's latent variable named exactly: the edge *label* is the triple `[r, o, a]` and the edge *vocabulary* `R × O × A` is finite, public and fully sampled in training, so the eight regimes are a controlled experiment on what happens when a label is composed from seen constituents (works) versus when a constituent itself is withheld (fails to the blind baseline) — the vocabulary-extension row of that page's taxonomy, measured.
- **[[wiki/entities/anli.md]]** — the exact converse protocol, and the pairing worth building: PGM names the withheld abstraction formally (a `[r,o,a]` triple) and can say nothing about how hard any individual item is; ANLI measures per-item difficulty during authoring (tries, seconds, model-wrong) and cannot name what its test set withholds, because the set is defined extensionally as whatever the model got wrong.
- **[[wiki/entities/agent-benchmark.md]]** — the same split-over-the-structure design reached independently in the intuitive-psychology domain: four scenarios × 13 authored trial types, with leave-one-type-out, leave-one-scenario-out, train-on-one-type and train-on-one-scenario as the four regimes — the analogue of this page's held-out `[relation, object, attribute]` triples, and with the same result that a learned model's i.i.d. score collapses under them (.90 → .65 / .71).
- **[[wiki/entities/bib.md]]** — the third split-over-the-structure design in the wiki and the most extreme: a single canonical split in which every evaluation task is the conjunction of two training tasks, so there is no held-out *cell* to report, only a held-out composition.
- **[[wiki/entities/math-dataset.md]]** — the wiki's other benchmark shipping a symbolic trace alongside the answer, with the same signature: supervising the derivation helps in-distribution (+13.9 here, ~10% relative there) and buys nothing on the held-out abstraction — while MATH adds the two conditions PGM cannot test, since its traces are also available to *read* and to *generate*.
- **[[wiki/entities/gsm8k.md]]** — the word-problem analogue of this page's held-out triples: GSM-Plus's reversing-operation perturbation turns a given into the unknown, asking whether the *relation* was represented or only the forward computation — the same discrimination, obtained by editing one item instead of by partitioning the generator.
- **[[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]]** — supplies instrument `I11`, the only one that names the withheld abstraction formally, and the dissociation showing that a single o.o.d. number is under-determined even when its shift is valid.
- **[[wiki/concepts/human-baseline.md]]** — the null case: no human baseline collected on any regime, which makes every "notably weak" verdict on this benchmark machine-relative by the authors' own admission.
- **[[wiki/entities/baba-is-ai.md]]** — the held-out-abstraction design transposed from generated relational items to an acted-upon environment: what is withheld is a *sequence of rewrites* rather than a `[relation, object, attribute]` triple, and it is rotated over which of four plan shapes is held out.
- **[[wiki/entities/imagenet-c.md]]** — the same declared-shift discipline where no symbolic generator exists: the shift family is 15 hand-authored image corruptions with a held-out set for method selection, so requirement 1 is satisfied by authorship rather than by construction — and it supplies the differencing (`F15`) that this page's regime table, reported against a blind baseline but not against each model's own neutral-split score, does not do.
- **[[wiki/entities/scan.md]]** — the sequence-domain match for this page's recombine-versus-extend boundary: a modifier carried onto a verb seen only in isolation collapses to 1.2%, which is the same cliff as a relation carried onto an unseen attribute, one level down from recombination.
- **[[wiki/entities/cfq.md]]** — the same design move (split declared over an explicit symbolic structure) with the shift magnitude actually computed, which is what this page's eight fixed regimes lack: they cannot be ordered by how far out of distribution each one is.
