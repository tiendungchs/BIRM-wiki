# Integrated Information Theory — Consciousness as a Network's Power Over Itself

**Consciousness is the intrinsic ability of a neuronal network to influence itself, quantified as the maximally irreducible integrated information (Φ) supported by a network *in a state*. Two consequences make it an architecture claim rather than a metaphysics: the substrate is whichever subset of units maximises Φ — anatomically, a posterior temporo–parieto–occipital "hot zone", with prefrontal cortex not necessary — and the *state of that network, its activity together with its connectivity, specifies both the degree and the content of experience*. That second clause is the one a builder can test, and the one the wiki's only preregistered test of the theory breaks.**

> **Primary source (265th ingest).** Ferrante, Gorska-Klimowska, Henin, … Dehaene, Koch, Tononi, Pitts, Mudrik & Melloni (COGITATE consortium) 2025, *Adversarial testing of global neuronal workspace and integrated information theories of consciousness*, Nature, doi:10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1 (`raw/ferrante-2025-gnwt-iit-adversarial-testing.md`). **`n = 256` humans**: fMRI (120), MEG (102), iEEG (34), two or three independent laboratories per modality, predictions and pass/fail criteria preregistered with both sets of theory proponents, all analysis run by theory-neutral teams, and an optimisation third / replication two-thirds split for the non-invasive arms. This is the study the wiki previously held only second-hand through the framework proponents' replies ([[wiki/entities/global-neuronal-workspace.md]]).

## What is actually on trial

| Level | Content | Testable here? |
|---|---|---|
| **Mathematical core** | Φ: maximally irreducible cause–effect power, computed from the intrinsic perspective of the system on itself | **No.** Intractable at this scale, and the theory holds that what an *extrinsic* observer can decode is not its currency — which is why the consortium scored the decoding prediction as **non-critical for the theory** |
| **Biological siting** | The maximum-Φ complex sits in posterior cortex; prefrontal cortex contributes nothing necessary | Yes |
| **Dynamical signature** | Content is *actively maintained*, by sustained activity **and** sustained short-range connectivity, for as long as the experience lasts | Yes — and this is where it fails |

**The escape hatch is stated in the source and should be scored.** Because only the biological implementation is testable, a failure can be absorbed by moving the implementation while keeping the core. The same source names the price: predictions 1 (posterior content) and 2 (sustained timing) are **shared with recurrent processing and local recurrency theories**, so those are not diagnostic of Φ at all; only prediction 3 (sustained intra-posterior connectivity) is distinctive — and it is the one that failed.

## The posterior siting has a warrant that predates Φ

> Boly, Massimini, Tsuchiya, Postle, Koch & Tononi 2017 (`raw/boly-2017-ncc-front-or-back.md`) — a review by four proponents plus two outsiders, assembling the clinical case for the hot zone independently of the theory's mathematics. The taxonomy it uses is on [[wiki/concepts/constitutive-vs-enabling.md]].

| Evidence class | Front | Back |
|---|---|---|
| **Necessity (lesion)** | Bilateral frontal lobectomy leaves consciousness intact (Brickner's Patient A unnoticed by two neurologists for an hour; Patient KM's IQ rose after near-complete bilateral prefrontal resection). Traumatic frontal lesions occur in ~half of acute traumatic vegetative states and **do not predict outcome** | Traumatic posterior corpus-callosum lesions in **98%** of patients still vegetative at 1 year; **214-fold** risk of permanent vegetative state; same lesions predict permanent coma after cardiac arrest |
| **Content loss (lesion)** | Essentially none specific; frontal injury raises the threshold for 16 ms masked stimuli without abolishing the experience — a modulator signature | Prosopagnosia, achromatopsia, form agnosia, simultanagnosia, loss of somatosensory percepts and of motor awareness |
| **Within-state, no-task** | Frontal deactivation during vivid imagery in meditators | Posterior parieto-occipital hot zone separates dreaming from dreamless NREM; an online posterior EEG marker predicts dreaming at **85%** |
| **Reverse inference** | The frontal cluster in the consciousness map disappears; its peak is best predicted by "task" and "phonological" | Fusiform→faces `p = 0.88`, postcentral→touch `p = 0.88`, and the like |

**Why this matters for scoring the preregistered test.** Prediction 1 passed but was scored non-critical *by the consortium*, on the grounds that extrinsic decodability is not Φ's currency. The clinical ledger is a different instrument reaching the same siting by **necessity and constitution** rather than by decoding, which is the one direction the theory does not disavow — so the anatomical half of the theory is better supported than the preregistered test alone suggests, while the distinctive dynamical half (prediction 3) remains the one that failed. The standing counter-pressure is that a lesion ledger built on nulls in prefrontal cortex is subject to the same instrument objection as a decoding null there ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T269, T272).

## The three preregistered predictions and what came back

| # | Prediction | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Conscious content is maximal in posterior cortex; adding prefrontal activity should **not improve** decoding (it may add noise) | Category decoded at **>95%** (iEEG posterior, cross-task generalisation), orientation at ~95% (pseudotrial aggregation), ~75% fMRI searchlight across striate, extrastriate, ventral temporal and intraparietal cortex. Adding prefrontal regions of interest to a posterior decoder did **not** improve and sometimes reduced accuracy — decisively: `BF₀₁ = 1.94 × 10⁴` (iEEG, faces–objects), `1.91 × 10⁵` (letters–false fonts), `1,205` (orientation) | **Passed** (non-critical) |
| **2** | Content-specific activity in the posterior hot zone is **sustained for the stimulus duration** | Duration-tracking found, but in **25 of 657** posterior electrodes: 12 category-independent (occipital pole), 13 category-specific (lateral fusiform). Only **15% (8/53)** of face-selective electrodes were *both* selective and sustained — the rest were transient onset responses. Cross-temporal representational similarity confirmed sustained category and object-identity representation posteriorly. **But orientation was not sustained anywhere**: decodable in all three modalities, decayed after ~0.5 s | **Passed on the letter of the criterion, challenged on content** — a consciously experienced feature is decodable and not maintained |
| **3** | **Sustained gamma-band connectivity within posterior cortex**, between category-selective areas and V1/V2, throughout the experience | Preregistered pairwise phase consistency: synchrony was present but **early, brief (<0.75 s), confined to 2–25 Hz**, and mostly explained by the stimulus-evoked response. Gamma-band sustained connectivity absent, `BF₀₁ = 1.15–4.9` for the null; MEG all `BF₀₁ > 3` | **Failed — the consortium's most direct challenge to the theory** |

**Why prediction 3 is the load-bearing one.** The theory's claim is that *the state of the network — activity **and** connectivity — specifies the degree and content of consciousness*. Content was present and, at a sparse set of sites, sustained; the connectivity that is supposed to constitute it was not. **The standing caveat, stated by the consortium:** V1/V2 was sampled by only **12 iEEG electrodes** against **472** in prefrontal cortex, so the strongest negative result in the study rests on the study's thinnest coverage.

## What a builder takes from this, independent of the consciousness question

| Result | Design consequence |
|---|---|
| **A posterior decoder is not improved by adding the frontal population**, at `BF₀₁ ≈ 10⁴` | Two populations can both carry a variable while the second is **informationally redundant** to any read-out. Redundancy of this strength is a positive architectural finding: the frontal copy is not a refinement of the sensory one, which is what a *router* (or a control copy) predicts and what an elaborating stage does not — see T269 |
| **Maintenance lives in ~4% of the recorded sites**, anatomically apart (lateral fusiform) from the transient category detectors (striate, extrastriate, ventral) | **(brainstorm)** Detection and maintenance may be different *units*, not different phases of one unit. Every wiki architecture that reads a latent gives the same layer both jobs; this suggests a small dedicated persistence set fed by a large transient front end, which also explains why the sustained substrate looks "sparse" rather than absent |
| **Content is decodable but not maintained** (orientation) | Decodability at time `t` licenses nothing about availability at `t + Δ`. An architecture that assumes anything it once encoded is still readable is assuming a maintenance mechanism it has not built — and the alternative, that orientation is **recomputed on demand** from a sustained lower-level code, is a genuinely different machine ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T274) |
| **A representation persisted while its connectivity did not** | Persistent content does not require persistent recurrent traffic. That is the empirical opening for activity-silent maintenance in short-term synaptic state ([[wiki/concepts/working-memory.md]]) — with the caution that the measurement here is of *synchrony*, not of traffic |
| **The theory's core is declared unmeasurable by probe** | A certification criterion defined on the *intrinsic* perspective cannot be confirmed or refuted by any decoder, including a perfect one. For a machine, this is not a courtesy: it means the quantity cannot be used as a training signal, a monitor, or an acceptance test. See [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T270 |

## The methodology, which is the transferable part

| Device | What it buys |
|---|---|
| **Preregistered pass/fail criteria agreed by both adversaries before data** | Removes the post-hoc renaming of predictions — the failure mode the framework's replies exhibit in mild form |
| **Three modalities, cross-compensating** (fMRI spatial, MEG whole-brain temporal, iEEG resolution) | A null cannot be attributed to the chosen instrument, which is how single-modality theory tests had been dismissed |
| **Optimisation third → replication two-thirds** | Analysis choices are fitted and then tested on held-out participants — the neuroscience form of a train/test split, and rare |
| **Theory-neutral analysts, proponents excluded from acquisition and analysis** | Confirmation bias is removed by role, not by intention |
| **Differential prediction weighting + "lenient falsificationism"** (evidence for *any* tested feature blocks a failure verdict) | Explicit, and explicitly inadequate: the consortium ends by demanding a **quantitative framework for integrating evidence across analyses and modalities, weighted by prediction centrality and measurement error**, and notes none exists |

**(brainstorm) The same demand applies to the wiki's own tension ledger.** [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] scores positions in prose and returns `LIVE`/`LEANING` by judgement. The missing object here and there is the same: a rule mapping (prediction centrality × instrument sensitivity × sample consistency) onto a verdict. Until it exists, "challenged" is a rhetorical rather than a computed state.

- **[[wiki/entities/integrated-world-modeling-theory.md]]** — the synthesis that keeps Φ as a *necessary but insufficient* condition: integration must additionally be coherent in space, time and cause for an embodied controller, the counterexample being a high-Φ expander graph that models nothing.
- **[[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]]** — the mechanism this theory stakes its distinctive prediction on, and the page where the failure is quantified: the observed posterior synchrony was low-frequency, brief and largely evoked-driven, and phase-based versus amplitude-based metrics returned different verdicts on the same data.
- **[[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]** — the read-out defence that cuts both ways: the posterior decoding scored as this theory's positive evidence is exactly what a columnar code gives a coarse instrument, and it succeeds even for non-conscious stimuli, so decodability tracks code geometry rather than content presence.
- **[[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]** — the instrument this theory disavows: it declares extrinsically decodable information not to be its currency, which makes the decoding prediction non-critical for it and, more sharply, makes the whole probe family unable to bear on its core.
- **[[wiki/concepts/working-memory.md]]** — where the maintenance failure lands: content was sustained at ~4% of posterior sites while the connectivity supposed to constitute it was not, which is the empirical opening for activity-silent, synaptic maintenance rather than sustained recurrent traffic.
- **[[wiki/concepts/ignition.md]]** — the rival account of the same commit: a discrete threshold crossing that publishes content, against this theory's claim that no separate admission event is needed because the maximally integrated complex *is* the experience.
- **[[wiki/concepts/dynamic-repertoire.md]]** — where the theory's surrogates do work: Φ-like integration measures collapse together under three molecularly disjoint anaesthetics and order disorders of consciousness, which is the strongest evidence the framework has and is about *degree*, not content.
- **[[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]** — the connection to the wiki's master framing: this theory locates the graph in the substrate (what the network can do to itself) rather than in what the network is *about*, so it certifies a representation's integration while remaining blind to whether the represented graph is the right one.
- **[[wiki/concepts/constitutive-vs-enabling.md]]** — where the posterior siting comes from independently of Φ: a lesion and stimulation ledger (98% of persistent vegetative states carry posterior callosal damage; bilateral frontal lobectomy leaves consciousness intact) assembled by four of this theory's own proponents, so the anatomy claim can be scored without the mathematics.
