**The finding of this pass is that two numbers the wiki reports about itself were wrong, and neither was reachable by any check it had.**

1. **The source count.** The overview has said "N sources ingested" since wave 0, and N was being extrapolated from `INGEST` commits. That undercounts, because a bundled ingest files several sources under one commit — the real total from `_work/ingest-queue.md`, the only complete record, is **244**, not 232. Wave 8 was short by 1 and **wave 10 by 14**. Every per-wave figure quoted in [[wiki/overview.md]] since the wave-10 sweep began was therefore low, including the one used to argue the wave had achieved its purpose.
2. **A tension had no status.** T228 was written with five cells in a six-column table. No escaping rule catches that; the S4 check rewritten at the last pass — count unescaped pipes per row against the separator's width — catches it exactly, and it fired for the first time here. **This is the case that justifies the rewrite:** three of the four rows it flagged were ordinary `\|` escapes, and the fourth was a `LIVE` empirical tension that had been sitting in the table unmarked for eight ingests because the defect was a *missing cell* rather than a malformed one.

Nothing was discarded, and this is recorded as an unverified step rather than a clean one.
