Four things changed at this pass:

1. **G17's answer column was a concept page trapped in a table cell, and it had silently broken.** At **20,336 characters** it was 4.1× the next-largest cell in the wiki, and it had accreted its fifteen evaluation instruments one per ingest with *prose* ordinals — which collided: two different protocols were numbered "eighth" (the support/query split and the consistency score), two "seventh" (linear probing and the distributional-familiarity failure mode). They are now [[wiki/concepts/certification-instruments.md]] under a canonical `I1`–`I15`, ordered by what each instrument *needs*, with the ten failure modes stated once as `F1`–`F10` shared across the inventory rather than as clauses attached to whichever instrument happened to expose them. The failure modes are what the cell could not express, and they are the half worth having: three of them are statements about what the *builder* knew and are not measurable from the model at all.
2. **The wiki's largest homeless concept was its denominator.** [[wiki/concepts/human-baseline.md]]. Twelve benchmark pages each report, argue for, or explicitly decline a human number, and no page owned the question — so three empirical tensions (**T210, T213, T214**) were logged independently as disagreements about three benchmarks when they are one missing specification: a human baseline is a *protocol* (pool, attempt budget, aggregation order, exclusion rule, item-pool identity), and each tension is a different one of those five left unstated. The page also separates the four different jobs the baseline is asked to do — solvability certificate, difficulty calibration, control, target profile — of which only the second is a denominator.
3. **[[wiki/overview.md]] was two waves stale for the third consecutive pass**, at 190 sources and no wave-10 section. Brought to 212 with section 13; section 8's "seven instruments" corrected to fifteen; Key Open Problem 2 rewritten. S5 has now been written, violated, rewritten and violated again — see S5 below, which changes the rule rather than restating it.
4. **The concept index's coverage note is the wiki's largest source-ordered paragraph**, at 31,067 characters on one line, still opening "Seventy sources ingested" at ingest 212. Flagged as L9; the false count is fixed, the structure is not.
