What to read or write next. Derived from [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]], from open problems on concept pages, and from lint passes. Reordered whenever a gap opens or closes.

**Status (lint pass, 128 ingests).** Waves 0–6 are complete; wave 7 (world models / self-supervised) is 3 of 26 in. 158 of the 304 files in `raw/` are still unread. This pass was a *short* one — three ingests since the last lint rather than fifty-eight — and the wiki's structural health reflects that: no orphans, no unindexed pages, no broken links, and **5** asymmetric `Connections` edges out of 2265 (against 49 at the last pass). The audit therefore spent its budget on the carried structural debt instead of on repair.

Four things changed at this pass:

1. **Two carried items are closed.** [[wiki/concepts/equilibrium-propagation.md]] is split out of [[wiki/concepts/energy-based-models.md]] (L3, carried across three passes), and [[wiki/concepts/reward-prediction-error.md]] now exists (named at the last pass inside P1 and never built). Both were implicit concepts by the strict test: eq-prop was ~49% of its host page with three primary sources of its own, and `δ` was in use on 38 pages with four mutually incompatible readings and no page stating them side by side.
2. **The theta question is settled by decision, not by a page.** L5 asked whether theta is a concept or a measurement detail. It is a measurement detail: every load-bearing theta claim in the wiki is a claim about what a theta-band *correlation licenses*, which is what [[wiki/concepts/inter-areal-synchrony.md]] is about. That page is now marked as the canonical home for band claims and L5 is closed.
3. **One new gap, and it is cheap.** G64 — no architecture here lets one modulator set a gain and a learning rate at once, which is what the striatum demonstrably does. It joins the run of gaps that are *building* tasks rather than acquisition tasks.
4. **The machine-side deficit is unchanged and is now the dominant one.** Wave 7 is barely started, waves 8 (sequence architectures), 9 (symbolic layer) and 10 (benchmarks, 41 files) are untouched, and every one of them sits in `raw/` needing no curation.
