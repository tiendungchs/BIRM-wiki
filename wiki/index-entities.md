Also carries the function that wiring computes — (context, event) → most adaptive response — and the recent/remote timeline showing that what consolidates into the controller is the *mapping*, not the terms; and the hippocampal input **edge** as an object with its own state — bidirectionally plastic, D1-gain-controlled, amygdala-locked, stress-detuned, and severable by asymmetric disconnection (gap G52); plus the phase dissociation between the edge (needed at encoding) and the region (needed at retrieval). **Also the human arm**: medial Brodmann areas 9/10/11 partition by *content domain* — social dorsally, self and value anteromedially, value and emotion ventrally — with only 17% of the tissue domain-selective at a strong threshold and one non-selective ventral cluster proposed to hold an integrated situation model. **And the monkey arm**: a posterior medial region individuated functionally rather than architectonically, coding which *response tactic* is running and carrying the task only while more than one tactic is live |
| [[wiki/entities/nucleus-reuniens.md]] | Midline thalamic nucleus carrying the indirect medial-prefrontal ↔ hippocampal route, with single neurons collateralising to both structures | The return arrow the direct anatomy denies: the controller reaches the episodic store only through this relay, which delivers a *goal-conditioned future path* rather than a retrieval query, and whose lesion reproduces a hippocampal–prefrontal disconnection with both endpoints intact |
