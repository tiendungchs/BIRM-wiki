# Certification Instruments

**The inventory of protocols proposed for answering "did this system discover the structure, or fit a correlation?" — fifteen of them, none sufficient, and the record of *why* each fails is more informative than any of them.**

This page is the answer half of gap **G17** (*no evaluation protocol can certify structure discovery*), split out of the gap table at the lint pass at 212 ingests. G17 states the problem; this page holds the accumulated attempts. The split was forced by size — the row had reached 20,336 characters and its instrument ordinals had silently collided (two protocols numbered "eighth", two "seventh") — but it earns its place independently: the failure modes below are shared across instruments and were not statable while each was a clause in one cell.

**The numbering here (I1–I15) is canonical and replaces the ordinals previously used inside G17.** They are ordered by what the instrument needs, cheapest first — the ordering is the page's main claim, because an instrument's cost determines whether it gets run.

---

## What certification would have to mean

The target is not accuracy. i.i.d. testing cannot separate a recovered meta-graph from a correlation that holds in the sample, so a score is evidence about the sample and nothing else. Certification means licensing a claim of the form *the system's behaviour is generated by the intended structure*, which requires an instrument that can come back **negative on a system with a high score**.

Three requirements every instrument is measured against here:

1. **A well-defined intended solution** — something the score can be attributable *to*. Generated benchmarks satisfy this by construction (the generator's own rules); hand-authored ones satisfy it by author declaration; natural data does not satisfy it at all.
2. **Developer-blindness** — the system's builder must not have known the test. And this is a *depleting stock*, not a property (F1 below).
3. **Non-gameability by the obvious training move** — every instrument that presents a fixed query set can be trained on.

## The inventory

| # | Instrument | What it needs | What it returns | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I1** | **Controlled-shortcut datasets** — Shift-MNIST, biased CelebA, unfair dSprites: the intended rule is known by construction and the shortcut is inserted deliberately | A synthetic generator with a planted confound | Accuracy drop when the confound is removed | [[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]] |
| **I2** | **Signature limits + resource-stress probe** — certify an installed prior by a quantitative constant (capacity, Weber ratio, entry threshold) that stays put across scale, retraining and domain shift; then shrink the budget, since in biology learned distinctions degrade first and core ones last | Nothing but repeated runs | A constant, or its absence. **Diagnostic evidence is in the failure profile, not the success rate** | [[wiki/concepts/core-knowledge.md]] |
| **I3** | **Richness protocol** — one representation must answer classify / generate / parse / create-new-concepts / explain; a shortcut rule is by construction a mapping to *one* output type | Multiple task heads on one model | Which output types the representation supports | [[wiki/concepts/causal-model-building.md]] |
| **I4** | **Re-goaling protocol** — hold the transition structure fixed, swap the reward function, measure the retraining needed | An environment with separable dynamics and reward | Retraining cost as a proxy for model reuse | [[wiki/concepts/simulation-based-planning.md]] |
| **I5** | **Generalization difficulty `GD`** — `GD = H(Sol^θ_T \| TrainSol^opt_{T,C}) / H(Sol^θ_T)`: how far the shortest curriculum-optimal program must be edited to work at evaluation, run **developer-aware** so anything the builder injected stops counting as difficulty | Kolmogorov complexity | A quantity rather than a genre — the only one here | Chollet 2019; [[wiki/concepts/skill-acquisition-efficiency.md]] |
| **I6** | **Paired violation of expectation** — one history, two continuations differing only in the property under test; score whether the intended one is rated higher | Hand-authored contrast pairs | A *relative* judgement needing no labels, no shift, no absolute calibration. **The only instrument that can register a score below chance**, i.e. the opposite rule acquired (26.3% on Baby Intuitions Preference) | [[wiki/entities/hbtom.md]], [[wiki/concepts/violation-of-expectation.md]] |
| **I7** | **Linear probing** — decode a hypothesised structure from internal activations | Ground-truth structure labels | Whether the structure is *there*, and by which layer: a whole maze at 0.83–0.99 per wall from one token at layer 2 | [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]], [[wiki/entities/maze-solving-transformers.md]] |
| **I8** | **Support/query split** — induce whatever structure the model infers from one observation pair, then **execute it on a second pair from the same latent mechanism** | A generator producing two instances of one mechanism | **The gap between self-fit and transfer**: 0.994 / 0.000 means the answer was encoded, not the mechanism. Paired with **action primitiveness** (does some single learned code reproduce each atomic transformation) it splits vocabulary coverage from search competence (T156) | [[wiki/entities/neo-neural-theorizer.md]] |
| **I9** | **Consistency score** — apply a transform the task's semantics says is meaning-preserving (synonym substitution; replace a sub-expression by its computed value) and compare the model's output to *its own* output, never to a target | A known invariance | No labels, so none of I7's circularity. **Its refinement is what makes it certification rather than robustness: restrict the score to items the model got *wrong*** — identical *incorrect* outputs cannot both be explained by being correct — which takes the best of three architectures from 0.90 to **0.34** | [[wiki/entities/pcfg-set.md]] |
| **I10** | **Concept-based evaluation** — enumerate the concepts the system should hold, select those it answers *correctly* i.i.d., hand-author items instantiating each through different attributes, layouts and roles, report the drop | A concept inventory and an author | MRNet 73% → 49%/44%; SCL 89% → 62%/68%; ARC-Kaggle2 19% → **29%** on *top/bottom*, **8%** on *boundary*. The **rise** is the methodological finding: a concept-variation set is a *decomposition*, not a harder test set | [[wiki/entities/raven.md]] |
| **I11** | **Declared held-out abstraction + context-blind baseline** — generate items from an explicit symbolic structure, then *declare* what is withheld (a `[relation, object, attribute]` triple, a pair of triples, an attribute pair, half a value range), and report every regime against a trained context-blind baseline (22.4% vs 12.5% chance) | A symbolic generator | Requirement 1 satisfied **by construction**, and a *dissociation* rather than a score: recombining seen triples costs ~20 points while a relation carried onto an unseen attribute lands at or below the blind baseline — one architecture passes and fails depending on which axis is withheld | [[wiki/entities/pgm.md]] |
| **I12** | **Adversarial per-item authoring** — pay a human to write an item the deployed model misclassifies, have two others verify the label, retrain, repeat | ~5 min annotator time per accepted item, forever | (i) A score that **cannot saturate** (18.33% → 8.07% → 6.92%); (ii) a **try count and a clock** per item (3.4 → 6.4 tries, 199 → 355 s), so F3's audit is a by-product of collection; (iii) the **item composition as a read-out** of which weaknesses closed (numerical inference 38% → 10% of items, and +18.5 points on an independent stress test) | [[wiki/entities/anli.md]] |
| **I13** | **Accuracy-profile correlation** — correlate the model's accuracy *profile across item types* with the human profile instead of comparing aggregates | A per-item human baseline | The dissociation that makes it necessary: a graph-encoder ToMnet at the human aggregate (.90 vs .91) correlates **0.06**, and is *below chance* (.05) on the one type authored to catch its heuristic; an inverse planner at .96 correlates 0.55. Valid only if item types are authored one-per-heuristic, not as difficulty tiers | [[wiki/entities/agent-benchmark.md]], [[wiki/concepts/human-baseline.md]] |
| **I14** | **Named-perturbation sweep + template re-instantiation** — rewrite each solved item along declared axes (value substitution, digit expansion, type change, added operation, *reversed* operation, rephrasing, distractor insertion, statement deletion) and read the per-axis drop; separately, variabilise the item and draw `n` instances of the identical reasoning graph | A parseable item format | Per-axis drops that **rank the shortcut channels**, and `(mean, SD)`. Free second measurement: **the published item's z-score inside its own distribution is a contamination test needing no corpus access** — right-tail, often beyond 1 SD, for 21 of 25 models | [[wiki/entities/gsm8k.md]] |
| **I15** | **Rule-level evaluation** — require the rule *and* the answer in one output, hand-classify the stated rule against the task's author-declared abstraction | An author-declared abstraction per task, and a hand-classifier | The only instrument that asks the solver *what it did*: a solver at or above human accuracy reaches ~27% of its correct answers by a rule the concept group is not about, against ~8% for humans | [[wiki/concepts/rule-level-evaluation.md]] |

**Two scaling notes.** I10 scaled and given its missing half is [[wiki/entities/conceptarc.md]] (16 concept groups × 10 variations × 3 test inputs, 415 participants) — which closed the instrument's standing "no human baseline" weakness and measured a 40-point gap on tasks authored to be *easy*. I5 built to spec is [[wiki/entities/arc-agi.md]] (private evaluation set, ~3 examples per task, no data generator, enumerated priors).

## Failure modes — shared across instruments, and the reason none is sufficient

| # | Failure mode | Evidence | Which instruments it hits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F1** | **Developer-blindness is a depleting stock, not a property** | ARC-AGI-1's 100 private tasks absorbed ~10,000 reported scores across four competitions, each leaking a little. The authors' fix is structural: separate the leaderboard set from the final-scoring set, enlarge both | Every instrument with a fixed held-out set — I1, I5, I10, I11 |
| **F2** | **Item-level blindness does not imply *distributional* blindness, and the two clauses conflict** | If the public training set is i.i.d. with the private test set, a model trained on the public data is already adapted to the private one — and the difficulty calibration that makes cross-subset scores comparable is precisely what guarantees it. Evidenced: an ARC harness never mentioning ARC elicits correct ARC colour naming from Gemini 3 Deep Think over raw integer arrays (T207) | I5, I10, I11 — and it is unfixable jointly with calibration |
| **F3** | **`GD ≈ 0` items are unaudited; difficulty is asserted per-*benchmark* and never measured per-*task*** | 49% of ARC-AGI-1's private set fell to blind brute-force DSL search in 2020, before LLMs, at low compute (T205) — invisible to the protocol | All of them except I12, where the try count makes it a by-product |
| **F4** | **Probe labels are two guesses deep** | The experimenter must know the answer *and* know it in the model's own coordinates: an Othello world model reads out at 75% under human colour labels and **99.6%** under mover-relative ones — same activations, same decoder class (T157) | I7, decisively; I2 partially |
| **F5** | **The framing axis** — an instrument supplying the same problem *representation* to every task measures search within that representation and cannot detect abstraction upstream of it | ARC-AGI-1's generalization difficulty is ≈0 on this axis by construction (G73) | All of them; it is upstream of leakage |
| **F6** | **Adversarial authoring drives toward the regime where the strong-baseline check stops discriminating** | By ANLI round 2 a context-blind hypothesis-only baseline is within **2.6 points** of the full state-of-the-art model (T216) | I12 specifically, and it is the price of F1-immunity |
| **F7** | **A benchmark hard enough to floor every solver destroys the ordering it exists to produce** | Two programs 2 points apart on ARC (21% / 19%) are **23 points** apart on ConceptARC. The diagnostic regime is **humans-at-ceiling** | Every instrument; it is a design constraint, not a defect |
| **F8** | **Constancy-under-perturbation is necessary and not sufficient** | I2 assumes an invariant failure profile indicts an installed prior — and a model with *no* prior exhibits one: V-JEPA's intuitive-physics partition is constant across 115M→ViT-H, three objectives, three corpora and a 100× data sweep, because what fixes it is a memory window and a framerate none of those levers move (T161). Repair: perturb the *resource* the failure could otherwise be attributed to | I2 |
| **F9** | **The scored statistic is a free parameter fitted on the results** | For I6, *which moment of the error trace* counts as surprise is unspecified: `max_t` vs `mean_t` swings the same model by 16 points one way on one subtask and 11 the other way on another, and rival groups justify opposite choices (T146). Repairs: pre-register the statistic; report the shortcut-solvable subtask subset separately | I6, and by extension any instrument reducing a trace to a scalar |
| **F10** | **The reference system is unspecified** | The human denominator is a protocol, not a number: 73% vs ~91% on the same items and participants; 60% vs 99% on the same benchmark (T213, T214, T210) | I10, I13, I15 — every instrument scored against a human |

**The control F8 forces, and it is cheap:** run `n = 20` **randomly-initialised** networks of the same architecture through the identical scoring pipeline and report the per-property effect size over that null rather than an accuracy — the machine analogue of habituation trials, one forward pass per seed, and the only thing that makes a benchmark's low-level bias subtractable. *Every VoE number in this wiki predating it quotes an unknown chance level.*

## What the record adds up to

Three conclusions the inventory supports that no single instrument states.

**1. An honest instrument returns a vector, not a number.** Reached independently from two sides. From the behaviour side (I9): a dataset *generated by* the intended compositional rule, with memorisation designed out, on which 0.92 i.i.d. accuracy coexists with 0.72 systematicity, 0.50 productivity and 0.54 localism. From the task side (I10): a concept-variation score that *rises* on one concept and falls 11 points on another, so every single-number benchmark score in this wiki is a mean over a concept mixture with unmeasured per-concept variance. Any single number, including the good ones, is under-determined.

**2. The certification target is the developer, not the system.** I5's developer-aware conditioning is not a refinement — it is the whole point, because skill on any task known in advance is purchasable outright with priors or with data. This is why F1 and F2 are the inventory's hardest failures: they are both statements about what the builder knew, and neither is measurable from the model.

**3. G17 may be a maintenance schedule rather than a protocol.** The only response anyone offers to F1 is to treat benchmark authorship as a [[wiki/concepts/refinement-loop.md]] run against the field, with the stopping criterion *"authoring tasks easy for humans and hard for AI becomes impossible"*. I12 is that loop with the period shortened to one item. If this is right, the instrument is not a thing you build once, and its cost is a standing expense rather than a capital one — which is a different kind of answer from the one G17 asks for, and the only one with five years of evidence behind it.

## Open problems

| # | Problem |
|---|---|
| C1 | **No instrument satisfies requirement 2 durably.** F1 and F2 are unfixed and, under difficulty calibration, jointly unfixable. I12 escapes F1 by paying forever and buys F6 |
| C2 | **`GD` has no approximation.** I5 is the only quantity here and it is defined through Kolmogorov complexity; ARC quantifies the generalization difficulty of exactly zero of its own tasks (G31) |
| C3 | **The concept-variation authoring bind** — variations cannot be generated automatically without a system that already understands the concept, so I10's sample size is permanently bounded by human authoring throughput. The escape ([[wiki/entities/arc-agi-3.md]]'s propose-then-human-gate pipeline) requires a computable "same concept, different instantiation" test that nothing in the wiki supplies |
| C4 | **No instrument certifies that several demonstrated abilities come from *one* model.** I3 and I4 are both gameable by multi-task training on the query set |
| C5 | **The facet vector has no agreed basis.** I9 supplies five facets, I11 four regimes, I10 a concept inventory, I14 eight perturbation axes. Nothing relates them, so two "vector" reports are not comparable |
