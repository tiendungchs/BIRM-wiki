# Synaptic Plasticity

**A weight update computed from quantities available *at the synapse* — pre-synaptic activity, post-synaptic activity, and at most one broadcast modulatory signal — with no error propagated backwards through the network.**

This is the brain's actual write mechanism. Where [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]] asks whether backpropagation can be *approximated* locally, this page covers the rules that were never trying to approximate it: they are dynamical equations on the weights, and what they compute is whatever their fixed points encode. For the wiki's target they are the candidate write rule for fast **M** — the only family that can modify a network *during deployment*, at the timescale a reasoning agent meets a new instance-graph (Schmidgall et al. 2023).

---

## The biological substrate

| Mechanism | Timescale | Computational content |
|---|---|---|
| **Short-term plasticity** | tens of ms → minutes | Adaptation to sensory statistics; short-lasting memory formation. A transient, automatically-decaying store — fast **M** with a built-in forget gate. Not a learning rule: it has no fixed point to converge to, and what it modulates is *gain*, not content (see below) |
| **Long-term plasticity** (long-term potentiation / depression) | minutes → longer | Long-term behavioural change and memory storage; the substrate slow **W** would be written into |
| **Neuromodulation** | ms → long | Chemical broadcast (acetylcholine, dopamine, serotonin) altering circuit excitability *and* synaptic strength. Supplies the third factor: a global scalar the local rule multiplies against |
| **Metaplasticity** | — | "Plasticity of plasticity": changes the *ability* of a synapse to change, by shifting the physiological state of the neuron or synapse. Protects the network from its own saturation |
| **Intrinsic excitability plasticity** | **hours**, then back to baseline | Not a weight at all: one cell-wide scalar, set by CREB-dependent transcription reducing K⁺ conductance (smaller after-hyperpolarization, less spike-frequency adaptation). Stores almost nothing — it decides *which cells* the next memory is written into, and is **anti**-homeostatic, so it is positive feedback braked only by synaptic saturation (Lisman et al. 2018; [[wiki/concepts/memory-allocation-excitability.md]], [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T68) |
| **Neurogenesis** | developmental, and adult (subventricular zone, amygdala, dentate gyrus) | Capacity added rather than reallocated; rate is environment-sensitive (enrichment, exercise, stress in rodents). Role in learning not established |
| **Glial modulation** | — | Astrocytes release, reuptake and metabolize neurotransmitters, regulating availability; structural changes in synaptic strength require glial involvement. Not represented in any learning rule below |
| **Behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity** (BTSP) | **seconds** | A dendritic plateau potential potentiates *every* input active in a ~seconds-wide window around it. One event, one new receptive field — single-shot learning at the timescale behaviour actually runs at (Bittner et al. 2017; Liao & Losonczy 2024) |
| **Inhibitory (GABAergic) plasticity** | minutes → longer | Long-term potentiation and depression at inhibitory synapses, with a symmetric spike-timing rule reported in auditory cortex (D'amour & Froemke 2015). Candidate mechanism for a *learned suppression mask* — see [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]] |

**The weight distribution any rule must reproduce is log-normal.** Measured CA3 recurrent EPSPs are heavy-tailed — median 0.66 mV [IQR 0.33–1.08], Gaussian in log amplitude — as in every other cortical region measured (Sammons et al. 2023). Most synapses are near-negligible and a small minority dominates the dynamics, which changes what a write *costs*: a ten-step sequence can be embedded by pushing **<1%** of synapses to the 99th percentile of the existing distribution, leaving the histogram essentially unchanged ([[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]). A learning rule with additive Gaussian noise and a hard weight ceiling — the default in nearly every machine implementation of the rules below — produces the wrong distribution, and therefore the wrong signal-to-noise ratio between stored items and background **(brainstorm)**. The measured spike-timing rule already has the right form for this: potentiation shrinks with the existing weight while depression does not (Bi & Poo 1998, rule 2), which is a multiplicative-up/additive-down process — the standard generator of a heavy-tailed stationary distribution and the reason no explicit ceiling is needed.

**The timescale objection.** Long-term potentiation is routinely cited as the biological warrant for Hebbian learning, and Gallistel & Matzel 2013 argue the cognitive significance of that warrant is unclear on three counts (reported in Lake et al. 2017): (i) LTP's critical interstimulus interval is *orders of magnitude smaller* than the intervals that matter behaviourally, and experiments varying interstimulus and intertrial intervals together show **no critical interval exists**; (ii) behaviour persists for weeks or months while LTP decays to baseline in days (Power et al. 1997); (iii) learned behaviour is rapidly reacquired after extinction (Bouton 2004) with no corresponding facilitation in LTP (de Jonge & Racine 1985). Their conclusion — that it would be "especially challenging" to implement structured model building with purely Hebbian mechanisms — is logged as [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T13. It does not touch the rules below as *engineering*; it removes the biological argument for preferring them.

**BTSP is the direct answer to objection (i).** The critical-interval complaint is that Hebbian plasticity's window is orders of magnitude shorter than behaviourally relevant intervals. Behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity has a window of **seconds**, set by an eligibility trace rather than by spike coincidence, and it produces a reproducible place field from a single traversal (Bittner et al. 2017; Liao & Losonczy 2024). So the family is no longer forced to explain behaviour with a 20 ms kernel: rule 6 below runs at the behavioural timescale by construction. Objections (ii) persistence and (iii) reacquisition-after-extinction are untouched.

**Short-term plasticity is the one row here that is a dynamical system rather than a rule.** The Tsodyks–Markram form — release probability `u` facilitating with presynaptic rate and recovering with `τ_f`, resources `x` depleting and recovering with `τ_r`, efficacy `u·x` — has no target and no fixed point that encodes anything:

```
du/dt = (U − u)/τ_f + U(1 − u)·m ,   dx/dt = (1 − x)/τ_r − u·x·m ,   efficacy = u·x
```

When `τ_f > τ_r` (e.g. 1.9 s vs 0.6 s in CA3), efficacy **overshoots after the presynaptic drive stops** — resources refill before facilitation decays — so a population that has just been active carries a transient *gain advantage* with no activity and no weight learning. Mark et al. 2017 make that rebound the whole memory: it is enough to reactivate an abandoned hippocampal map for seconds after its sensory support is gone ([[wiki/entities/stp-flickering-cann.md]]). Three consequences for the rules below: the substrate they write on has a fast state variable of its own; a "weight" is therefore `W · u · x`, only one factor of which is learned; and rebound amplitude is non-monotonic in `U`, so the same rule on the same weights behaves differently under neuromodulators that shift release probability.

**Trained end-to-end, the row's sign has to be flipped to be usable** (Kozachkov et al. 2022, [[wiki/entities/stsp-working-memory-rnn.md]]). Putting short-term plasticity inside a recurrent network trained by backpropagation through time on a working-memory task: purely *Hebbian* short-term plasticity was hard to train — rule 1's positive feedback, hit empirically — while an **excitatory anti-Hebbian / inhibitory Hebbian** form trained without any weight clipping, because its stability is a property of the parameterization (`K = BᵀB ∘ O + I`, positive and positive-definite, giving a contracting network) rather than a bound imposed afterwards. The presynaptic Tsodyks–Markram form needed `u, a` clamped to `[0,1]` to stay trainable, which the paper notes is the biologically real constraint (finite releasable resources) rather than a numerical patch. Two results carry beyond that model: the trained network survives **ablation of >50% of its synapses** where the fixed-weight control fails at 10–20% — a memory not held in the learned weights cannot be deleted by damaging them — and contracting modules compose into contracting systems, so provable stability is what lets a new module be added without retraining the whole network.

**Metaplasticity is the important one for this wiki.** It is elastic weight consolidation's biological ancestor stated as a *mechanism* rather than as a penalty term — the gate on writability is intrinsic to the synapse and stateful, not computed by an outer loop from a Fisher matrix ([[wiki/concepts/continual-learning.md]]). It is distinguishable from neuromodulation, though the two overlap in time at a modified synapse.

## The rule family, in order of what each fixes

**1. Hebbian.** Strengthen coactive pairs:

```
Δw_ij = η · x_i · x_j
```

Unsupervised; identifies structure in the input with no feedback. Stores large binary patterns in a fully-connected recurrent net (Hopfield). **Unstable by construction:** a weak positive correlation raises `w_ij`, which reinforces the correlation, which raises `w_ij` again. Needs bounding, or a rule tracking pre/post history or the activity of other neurons.

**What rule 1 computes — the one rule on this page whose objective is named.** For a linear unit `y = Σ_i w_i x_i` with zero-mean input, the rule integrates to `dw/dt = η⟨xxᵀ⟩w = ηCw`, `C` the input correlation matrix. In the eigenbasis of `C`, `w(t) = Σ_i k_i e^{ηα_i t} c_i`; all `α_i > 0` (correlation matrices are positive-definite), so the largest eigenvalue dominates and `w(t) → c*`, the **first principal component of the input**. Add lateral inhibition in the postsynaptic layer so that units cannot all capture the same direction, and the layer performs full PCA (Wikipedia, *Hebbian theory*; Oja 1982). This is the answer the vector-field section below demands of every rule: the quantity that improves under plain Hebb is **variance captured**, and the rule is a power iteration on `C` executed by the synapses.

**The instability is not an artefact of the linear case.** `α_i > 0` for every eigenvalue means the solution diverges exponentially for any input with a dominant direction; adding a saturating response function `f` does not fix it, and the divergence can be shown for **any** neuron model (Wikipedia, *Hebbian theory*). Hence the stabilised descendants, all of which keep the PCA fixed point and remove the blow-up: **Oja's rule** (normalise `w` to unit norm inside the update), **BCM theory** (a sliding modification threshold on postsynaptic activity, so the rule is metaplastic by construction), and the **generalized Hebbian algorithm** (deflation, extracting successive components).

**Oja's rule earns its keep as an *auxiliary* term, not as a standalone objective** (Shervani-Tabar & Rosenbaum 2023). Meta-learning over ten candidate local rules selected `F⁹ = y_ℓ y_{ℓ−1}ᵀ − (y_ℓ y_ℓᵀ) W_{ℓ−1,ℓ}` alongside a gradient-like term, and the measured mechanism is not variance capture but **orthonormalization**: weight rows converge toward an orthonormal basis of the presynaptic PCA subspace, a decorrelation measured directly on `W`, which decorrelates hidden features and improves what the readout layer can separate. Three consequences: (i) plain Hebb `y_ℓ y_{ℓ−1}ᵀ` had to be excluded from the candidate pool by hand because it blew the activations up — the instability above, hit empirically; (ii) because Oja's rule never touches the error, it is **not bounded by backpropagation** and accelerates learning even in symmetric-feedback networks with poor initialization; (iii) it is the only term in that pool that is local under *any* interpretation, since it uses no error at all. The lesson for a builder is that a PCA-objective rule is best used as a **representation conditioner running in parallel with** a credit-assignment rule, not as the thing that learns the task ([[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]]).

**(brainstorm) PCA is exactly the wrong objective for this wiki's target, and knowing that is the point.** A rule whose fixed point is the top eigenvector of `C` maximises retained variance — the single most shortcut-friendly objective available, because a high-variance nuisance direction beats a low-variance causal one every time ([[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]], [[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]]). Every later rule on this page can be read as replacing `C` with a different second-moment matrix: STDP with a *time-lagged* correlation `⟨x(t+Δ)x(t)ᵀ⟩` (directed edges instead of variance directions), rule 7 with a regression residual (transition structure instead of correlation). The family's progress is not "more stable Hebb" — it is **changing which matrix the synapse does power iteration on.**

**Hebb's own statement already required temporal precedence.** The canonical gloss "neurons that fire together, wire together" drops the causal clause: Hebb specified that cell *A* must "take part in firing" *B*, which is possible only if *A* fires *just before* *B* (Wikipedia, *Hebbian theory*, quoting Hebb 1949 p. 62). Read that way rule 2 is not an extension of rule 1 but a **recovery of the original claim**, and the symmetric coincidence form of rule 1 is the approximation.

**The cleanest behavioural demonstration that plain coincidence learning builds a cross-modal correspondence: mirror neurons.** Keysers & Perrett's account is that acting generates re-afferent sensory signals (seeing, hearing, feeling the action) that overlap in time with the motor activity that caused them, so Hebbian potentiation links the sensory and motor representations until the motor units fire to the *sight or sound alone*. The test case is quantitative: people who have never played piano show no motor activation to piano music, and **five hours of lessons** — in which each keypress is paired with its sound — suffice to produce it. Consistent with rule 2, the link is potentiated only when the stimulus is *contingent* on the motor program, not merely co-present (Wikipedia, *Hebbian theory*). **(brainstorm)** This is the wiki's cheapest existence proof that a purely local rule can install a **shared code across two systems with no common input** — the operation an agent needs to identify its own action space with an observed transition in [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]. What it does *not* supply is abstraction: the correspondence is learned per action from paired data, so it is a lookup between two instance-graphs, not a claim that they share a shape.

**What is definitionally *outside* the family, and why it matters.** Hebb proposed no rule for inhibitory synapses and no prediction for anti-causal order (post before pre), so all **heterosynaptic** and **homeostatic** plasticity is non-Hebbian by definition. The sharpest case is **volume learning**: retrograde signalling by nitric oxide, which is small, soluble and diffusive, modifies *neighbouring* synapses that were never part of the pre/post pair (Wikipedia, *Hebbian theory*). This breaks the assumption every rule on this page shares — that a weight update is indexed by a synapse. If a fraction of plasticity is addressed by **spatial neighbourhood** rather than by connectivity, the correct data structure is a field over the dendritic tree, not a matrix entry, and clustered synapse placement becomes a *learnable variable* ([[wiki/concepts/dendritic-computation.md]]) **(brainstorm)**.

**2. Spike-timing-dependent plasticity (STDP).** Replace coactivity with *order*:

```
Δw_ij =  A₊ · exp(−Δt/τ₊)   if Δt > 0   (pre before post → potentiate)
        −A₋ · exp( Δt/τ₋)   if Δt < 0   (post before pre → depress)
```

Observed in neocortex, hippocampus and cerebellum. The sign flip at `Δt = 0` is what makes it *causal* rather than correlational — the asymmetry is a cheap directional-edge detector, which is exactly the primitive latent graph discovery needs and Hebb's correlational form cannot supply **(brainstorm)**.

**The kernel's symmetry selects which error-driven rule the synapse implements** (Whittington & Bogacz 2019). Asymmetric STDP as written above computes a *derivative*: if the postsynaptic rate is rising, more post spikes land in the potentiating window than in the depressing one, so `ΔW ∝ ẋ_post · x_pre` — which is exactly the update the continuous-update approximation of backpropagation requires (Bengio et al. 2015, 2017). The **symmetric** form — near-coincident spikes potentiate regardless of order, plus a small depression per presynaptic spike (Vogels et al. 2011) — computes a *level* relative to a baseline `x₀`, which is the update predictive coding requires at equilibrium. So one substrate covers both error-representation schemes of [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]], and which one it covers is set by the window shape, not by the network. Note this is the *second* independent result on this page where kernel symmetry changes what is computed rather than how fast — rule 7's `β` selects successor vs. reversible transition structure (Keck et al. 2025).

**The kernel can also be *derived* rather than fitted** (Bengio et al. 2015). Posit the update `ΔW_ij ∝ V̇_j · S_i` — presynaptic spike times the temporal derivative of the postsynaptic voltage — and the exponential window above emerges from threshold-crossing geometry: with `V̇_j > 0` the postsynaptic spike arrives after the presynaptic one and sooner for larger `V̇_j`, giving positive `ΔW` decaying in `ΔT`; with `V̇_j < 0` the cell was above threshold *before*, giving negative `ΔW` at negative `ΔT`, shrinking in magnitude as `|ΔT|` grows. Simulation reproduces the Bi & Poo 1998 curve. Two things this changes for a builder:

- **`A₊, A₋, τ₊, τ₋` are not four free parameters.** They are consequences of membrane dynamics and firing threshold, so the "kernel shape as design parameter" claim above is really a claim about the *neuron model*, not about the synapse. What the derivation does *not* recover is the measured dependence of `A₊` on the existing weight and on the postsynaptic cell type (Bi & Poo 1998, below) — those are properties of the synapse and its target, so the neuron model fixes the kernel's *shape* and something else fixes its *gain*.
- **It makes the rule conditional in a useful way.** `ΔW ∝ V̇_j S_i` is approximate stochastic gradient descent on any objective `J` that the network's own dynamics happen to increase (Xie & Seung 2000 give the same rule as differential anti-Hebbian plasticity). The rule contributes no objective of its own — whatever the relaxation optimises is what STDP descends. See [[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]] for the variational bound proposed to fill that slot.

**The window was written down before it was measured, and its *peak location* is the design parameter** (Gerstner et al. 1996). Two years before Bi & Poo's curve, a model of the barn-owl laminar nucleus posited exactly this kernel — positive for pre-before-post, negative for post-before-pre, several milliseconds wide — plus a small nonspecific potentiation `γ` of every active synapse, and used it to tune sub-millisecond temporal structure. Three transferable results:

- **Efficient learning fixes the peak at `s ≈ −t_r/2`**, half the rise time of the postsynaptic potential. If the surviving synapses share a delay modulo the stimulus period, the postsynaptic spike falls ~`t_r/2` after coherent arrival, so peaking there gives the largest increment to the synapses that are already strongest — selection becomes self-reinforcing rather than diffusive. Nothing else about `W`'s shape matters; only its sign structure and this peak.
- **`γ` plus the depressing lobe self-normalizes total input strength.** Continuously strengthening every active synapse while `W` depresses the mistimed ones is a competitive self-organizing process that needs no explicit normalization step, and synapses driven to zero efficacy are simply removed. This is the wiki's cheapest answer to the saturation problem rule 1 has.
- **The kernel's width does not bound the resolution it can achieve.** `W(s)` spans several milliseconds while the structure it tunes has a 200 µs period — resolution comes from *competition among delays*, not from kernel sharpness. So the standard reading that a ~20 ms STDP window implies ~20 ms temporal resolution is wrong; see [[wiki/concepts/temporal-coding.md]].

**What rule 2 computes, given a circuit around it: online expectation-maximization** (Nessler et al. 2009, 2013, via Tavanaei et al. 2019). Put Poisson-firing input neurons in front of an STDP-trained layer and a stochastic **winner-take-all** circuit behind it — in the 2013 version the WTA is implemented by an inhibitory neuron, so it fits a cortical microcircuit — and the pair approximates stochastic online EM for the parameters of a **multinomial mixture distribution**:

| Spiking event | EM step |
|---|---|
| An output neuron fires | Draw a **sample from the posterior** over hidden causes given the current input (E-step) |
| STDP is applied to the synapses of the neuron that fired | **M-step** — update the mixture parameters toward that sample |

The rule used is the "LTP if pre fires within `ε ≈ 10 ms` before post, else LTD" simplification. Three things follow, and they are the most consequential single result the wiki has about local rules:

- **This is an answer to the demand below** ("name the quantity that improves"). The quantity is the **data log-likelihood under a latent-cause mixture model** — not retained variance, which is what plain Hebb ascends and what makes rule 1 shortcut-friendly. A rule that ascends likelihood under a model with hidden variables is doing exactly the estimation step [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] needs, and no other rule on this page names an objective at all.
- **The objective is a property of the circuit, not of the synapse.** Nothing changes in the STDP kernel; the WTA is what makes the output a *sample from a normalized posterior* rather than an unnormalized coactivity readout. So "what does this local rule optimize" is not well-posed without specifying the inhibition around it ([[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]) — a correction to how this whole page has been posing the question.
- **The construction composes upward.** With the WTA circuits as reservoir units, each spike train reads as a sample from the state space of a hidden Markov model, and STDP applied to *both* the lateral excitatory and the afferent synapses represents the statistical structure of spatio-temporal input (Klampfl & Maass 2013). The variant that fixes the model's negative-weight artefact — shift the weights positive with a constant in the LTP term — gives a Gaussian mixture embedded in HMM states, applied to spoken-word classification (Tavanaei & Maida 2017).

**Its residue.** The latent structure recovered is a **mixture** — a flat set of competing causes with no relations among them, exactly the limitation [[wiki/concepts/temporal-coding.md]] records for the interaural-time-difference detector. A WTA layer discovers *which cause*, never *which edge between causes*, so this result closes the objective question and leaves the structure question untouched.

**Supervised rules built on rule 2, and the one algebraic identity behind them** (via Tavanaei et al. 2019). Widrow–Hoff `Δw ∝ x·(y^d − y^o)` expands, when `x`, `y^d` and `y^o` are spike trains, into

```
Δw = Δw^STDP(pre, desired) + Δw^aSTDP(pre, observed)
```

— STDP against the *teacher's* spikes plus **anti**-STDP against the network's own. The teacher never connects to the trained synapse (hence ReSuMe, "remote supervised learning"). Two consequences: the delta rule and the spike-timing rule are the same object under a change of what the postsynaptic train is, and **anti-STDP is where supervision enters** — the sign flip against observed output is the entire difference between an unsupervised timing detector and a supervised one. The same pairing appears in classifier SNNs, where firing the target class neuron triggers STDP and firing a non-target neuron triggers anti-STDP, and it is reported to learn fast ([[wiki/entities/spiking-neural-networks.md]]).

**Rule 2 has a network-level effect the pairwise equation does not show: it walks the response earlier in time.** Presenting a repeated spatio-temporal pattern to an STDP-equipped neuron reduces the latency of the postsynaptic potential as learning proceeds, because the rule concentrates weight on afferents that consistently fire *early* (Guyonneau et al. 2005; Masquelier et al. 2008). The neuron ends up selective to the pattern *and* faster on it than on any other, and a single neuron suffices to detect and learn a repeating pattern embedded in stochastic background — with **no explicit time reference** available. **(brainstorm)** This is a free segmentation primitive: latency reduction converts "this pattern recurs" into "this unit fires sooner", so recurrence becomes readable by a downstream coincidence detector with no counter and no clock ([[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]]).

**The measured curve, and the three variables the kernel equation leaves out** (Bi & Poo 1998). Dual perforated-patch recordings from pairs of cultured hippocampal neurons (E18–20 rat, 8–14 days in vitro), induction 60 pulses at 1 Hz with the postsynaptic spike placed at a controlled `Δt`, read out 20–30 min later:

| Measured | Value |
|---|---|
| Potentiation window | postsynaptic spike peaking **0 → +20 ms** after excitatory postsynaptic potential onset; +48.4 ± 9.9% at short `Δt` (`n` = 14) |
| Depression window | postsynaptic spike **−20 → 0 ms** before onset; −18.0 ± 3.2% (`n` = 12) |
| Outside ±20 ms | no modification |
| Transition zone at `Δt ≈ 0` | **~5 ms wide**, with large fluctuation between maximal depression and maximal potentiation |
| Pharmacology | both branches abolished by d-AP-5 → **NMDA-receptor-dependent**. Nimodipine (L-type Ca²⁺ block) abolishes LTD entirely (−1.2 ± 1.3%) but only reduces LTP (48.4% → 27.3%, `p` = 0.068) |

The two branches therefore have **partly separate molecular gates**: `A₋` depends on L-type Ca²⁺ influx and `A₊` mostly does not, so a neuromodulator acting on those channels rescales the depressing lobe alone. That is a physiologically available handle on kernel *asymmetry* — the quantity the wiki has already shown selects between error-representation schemes (Whittington & Bogacz 2019) and between successor and reversible transition structure (rule 7) — and it is one channel, not a re-wiring.

Three dependencies the `Δw(Δt)` equation omits, each a design lever:

- **`A₊` is a function of the current weight; `A₋` is not.** Potentiation is confined to weak synapses — percentage change against log initial excitatory postsynaptic current gives `r = −0.72`, `p = 0.00017`, with significant LTP essentially only below 500 pA — while depression shows **no** strength dependence across 20–900 pA (`r = 0.037`, `p = 0.89`). So the real rule is `Δw = A₊(w)·exp(−Δt/τ₊)` with `A₊` decreasing in `w`, against a strength-independent `A₋`. **This is stabilization built into the measurement rather than patched on afterwards**: the positive-feedback blow-up of rule 1 cannot occur, because the increment vanishes exactly where runaway would start. **(brainstorm)** Multiplicative-LTP-with-additive-LTD is also the standard generator of a *unimodal heavy-tailed* stationary weight distribution — the log-normal histogram this page demands of any rule (Sammons et al. 2023) is a prediction of the measured `A₊(w)`, not an extra constraint to be imposed.
- **The postsynaptic cell type gates whether the synapse is writable at all.** Glutamatergic synapses onto **GABAergic** neurons showed no change under either protocol: −0.3 ± 3.4% for positively correlated spiking (`n` = 5) and −1.7 ± 2.0% for negatively correlated (`n` = 4), both `p` < 0.001 against the glutamatergic-target case. Plasticity is thus **target-specific**: the same presynaptic axon, the same spike pairing, and the write happens or does not depending on the identity of the receiving cell. For a builder this is a free, static write-gate that costs no instructive signal — a subpopulation whose incoming weights are structurally frozen ([[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]).
- **Order does not fix the sign — causal responsibility does.** At *suprathreshold* connections (synaptic drive alone triggers the postsynaptic spike), injecting a spike 10 ms **before** each excitatory postsynaptic potential — nominally the depression protocol — produced **potentiation** of +31.9 ± 9.3% (`n` = 3): the spike the synapse actually caused dominates the one it did not. So the "directional edge detector" reading above is stronger than the kernel suggests. The rule is not a lookup on `Δt` of the nearest spike; when the synapse is strong enough to be causal it reports its own contribution and ignores the anti-causal pairing — which is nearer to what [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] needs (a *caused* edge) than a timing coincidence is. It also means the effective kernel is not measurable at all on strong synapses, and that the sub-500 pA regime where the canonical curve was collected is the regime where the synapse is *not* causal **(brainstorm)**.

**3. Naive three-factor (reward-modulated Hebbian) — and why it fails.**

```
Δw_ij = η · x_i · x_j · R          ✗
```

Broken: if `w_ij` is already optimal, the rule still produces a net change and drives it away from the optimum. To track the actual covariance between inputs, outputs and rewards, **at least one term in the product must be zero-mean** (Frémaux et al., cited). Subtracting a reward baseline (`R − E[R]`) helps but is generally insufficient.

**4. Node perturbation — the fix that works.** Center the *output* instead, by perturbing activations with zero-mean noise `Δx_j` and using the perturbation in the product:

```
Δw_ij = η · x_i · Δx_j · R
```

The `x_i·Δx_j` increment pushes future `x_j` responses toward the perturbation; multiplying by `R` pushes toward it when reward was positive and away when negative. Net drift is up-reward *even when `R` is not zero-mean* (at higher variance). This rule implements REINFORCE and estimates the true gradient of `R` with respect to `w_ij`, biologically plausibly, and lets recurrent networks learn non-trivial cognitive and motor tasks **from sparse, delayed rewards**.

**5. Eligibility traces — the bridge across time.** Keep a per-synapse trace of which synapses contributed to recent activity; a dopamine-like signal arriving later converts the trace into an actual weight change. This is the mechanism that makes a *retroactive* third factor possible, and it is what e-prop and neuromodulated differentiable plasticity are both built on ([[wiki/concepts/meta-optimized-plasticity.md]]).

**6. Behavioral timescale synaptic plasticity (BTSP) — the single-shot rule.** A three-factor rule whose third factor is a **dendritic plateau potential** and whose eligibility window is seconds wide:

```
Δw_i = η · e_i(t) · P(t) ,   e_i(t) = ∫ K(t − s) · x_i(s) ds ,   K spanning ≈ ±2–4 s
```

`P(t)` is the instructive plateau, evoked by endogenous input — entorhinal cortex is the proposed source (Grienberger & Magee 2022) — or by optogenetic stimulation (Rolotti et al. 2022a). Every input whose trace `e_i` is non-zero when the plateau arrives is potentiated, so the cell acquires a whole receptive field in one event. Established in CA1 (Bittner et al. 2017), extended to CA3 (Li et al. 2023), more strongly expressed in **novel** environments (Priestley et al. 2022), and the plasticity kernel has been measured in vivo at synaptic resolution (Gonzalez et al. 2023).

| Property | Why it makes single-shot learning work |
|---|---|
| **Integration over seconds** | Averages over many noisy spikes, so one large weight change is driven by a reliable signal rather than by a coincidence |
| **Many synapses potentiated at once** | Robustness by the law of large numbers: even if some potentiated synapses are spuriously associated with the field, it is very unlikely that enough are to flip the output |
| **Population-driven, not pairwise** | Computationally it is the supervised fitting of a **binary classifier by one cell**, over a rich dendritic input basis, rather than a correlation detector (Milstein et al. 2021) |
| **Gated by an instructive signal** | The cell writes only when a plateau arrives — the first rule on this page whose writes are *licensed* rather than automatic |

**The rule reduces to an involution on binary weights, and the reduction is what makes it analysable** (Wu & Maass 2025). Approximate the measured kernel by: whenever presynaptic input arrives inside the ≈10 s window opened by a plateau, apply with probability 0.5

```
Δw_i = +1   if x_i = 1 and w_i = 0        (LTP for weak synapses)
Δw_i = −1   if x_i = 1 and w_i = 1        (LTD for strong synapses)
```

i.e. `w_i ← 1 − w_i` on every gated active input. Three consequences that the eligibility-trace framing above hides:

- **There is no `x_post` term anywhere.** BTSP is not "Hebb with a longer window" — it depends on presynaptic activity, the *current weight*, and the plateau, and on nothing the postsynaptic cell did. That places it outside the Hebbian family by definition, and it is the source of its main systems-level property: because the gate is independent of the input, the memory load spreads **uniformly** over the population instead of concentrating on the easily-driven cells, so earlier traces are not overwritten by later ones ([[wiki/entities/btsp-cam.md]]).
- **The depression branch is not just anti-saturation.** Two items sharing many 1's, both learned while the same cell was gated, leave that cell's shared weights at 0 — so it fires for neither and drops out of both traces. Similar items learned together are therefore **pushed apart**, which is the human repulsion effect and which no other rule on this page produces.
- **The plateau probability `f_q` is a rule parameter with no analogue anywhere else in this family.** It is the *memory-allocation rate*, and the seconds-long window is what sets it: a plateau rate of ≈0.0005 s⁻¹ times a 10 s window gives `f_q ≈ 0.005`. So the biophysical duration is not a detail of the eligibility trace — it is the gain control converting a rate into an allocation probability, and raising it trades recall-from-partial-cues away for larger traces.

**The dendritic basis is load-bearing.** Distinct dendrites of one CA1 pyramidal cell carry heterogeneous, partly decorrelated tuning; soma–dendrite coupling varies with brain state, so dendrites can act semi-independently (Rolotti et al. 2022b; O'Hare et al. 2022). Read functionally: **the dendrites are a high-dimensional random-feature basis and BTSP chooses a weighting on it.** A place field is then a readout direction, and "which field" is "which weights", which is why place-cell-like tuning also appears for sound frequency, faces, conspecifics and stimulus×choice conjunctions — the rule is not spatial, the basis is whatever the cell receives. The implication the review draws is strong: a tuned hippocampal cell may just be a **memory cell**, place coding a byproduct of compressing inputs under a sparsity penalty (Benna & Fusi 2021).

**7. Predictive/decorrelative rule with a symmetry dial — the rule whose fixed point is a named object.** Subtract the synapse's own predicted input from the post-synaptic activity, and run the same term on the time-reversed pair with weight `β`:

```
ΔW = α (p_post(t+1) − W p_pre(t)) p_pre(t)ᵀ  +  β (p_post(t) − W p_pre(t+1)) p_pre(t+1)ᵀ
```

The single-term version (`β = 0`) has `E[ΔW | p_pre] = 0` exactly when `W p_pre = E[p_post | p_pre]`, so the rule is a regression onto the *next* activity — which is why its fixed point is a successor representation rather than a correlation matrix. The `β` term is the same regression run backwards, and at convergence the network stores the SR of `P_{α,β} = (αP_forward + βP_backward)/(α+β)`: asymmetric → successor, symmetric → time-reversible (policy-insensitive) map, `β`-only → predecessor representation (Keck et al. 2025; see [[wiki/concepts/successor-representation.md]] for the functional consequences). Stability requires `|α| > |β|`; `α = −β` diverges.

**Why this rule matters here.** It is the wiki's clearest case of the demand made in the vector-field section below being *met*: the quantity that improves is named, and the coefficient controlling the shape of the plasticity kernel maps onto a defined change in the represented transition structure. The rule is also robust to the biological messiness the page's other rules assume away — heterogeneous per-synapse `α, β` drawn at random, and per-timestep noise on them, leave the behaviour qualitatively unchanged, so a symmetry *bias* rather than exact symmetry is sufficient. Empirically CA3 recurrent synapses show a temporally symmetric potentiation window while CA1 Schaffer-collateral synapses show classical asymmetric STDP, which under this rule assigns the two subfields different representations of the same experience.

**8. Structural growth — the rule that changes the connectivity rather than the weights** (Cazalets et al. 2025). Start from an *empty* recurrent matrix. Every `T` steps, measure one scalar per neuron (mean rate, or activity standard deviation) and compare it to a homeostatic set-point; for each neuron outside the band, grow one incoming synapse from the off-band neuron whose activity it correlates with most over the window, and for each over-active neuron delete a random existing one:

```
Δz_i = (s_i − ρ)/β ;   Δz_i < −1 → w_{i j*} += δw, j* = argmax_j r_ij   ;   Δz_i > +1 → w_ij −= δw, j ~ U{j : w_ij > 0}
```

Four things distinguish it from rules 1–7, and each is a design lesson rather than a detail:

- **The learned object is the graph, not the values on it.** This is [[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] G29's architecture lever pulled by an algorithm whose step is a single synapse. Biologically it is Homeostatic Structural Plasticity (Butz et al.), i.e. synaptogenesis and retraction, which sits *outside* the Hebbian family by the same definition that excludes homeostatic rules above — yet the pair selection is pure Hebb.
- **The correlation is computed over a long, randomly-resampled window rather than instantaneously,** and that is the measured source of the advantage: on five speech/audio classification benchmarks the grown reservoirs beat Intrinsic Plasticity and Anti-Oja — the moment-to-moment rules — on every task, and those rules seldom beat a static random reservoir at all. The wiki's usual framing (rule 1 does power iteration on `C = ⟨xxᵀ⟩`) survives, with `C` estimated over seconds of activity instead of one step.
- **The write gate is a per-neuron set-point deviation.** No error, no label, no reward, no plateau — the cheapest write-licensing signal on this page, and the one that needs no unexplained instructive pathway.
- **Growth is targeted and erasure is random**, because the authors have no local criterion for *which* synapse to remove that would increase global dimensionality. G19's complaint (no local rule is selective about what it writes) reappears on the erase side, where the wiki had not looked.

What it buys is decorrelation: mean pairwise `|r|` between neurons falls from 0.89 to 0.17 (Free Spoken Digit Dataset) and from 0.994 — near-total synchrony — to 0.48 on the hardest task, using **excitatory weights only**, matching what an explicitly signed excitatory–inhibitory network achieves. What it does not buy is a directed edge: `r_ij = r_ji`, so the grown graph is symmetric and expresses coactivity, never order — exactly the gap rule 2 exists to fill ([[wiki/entities/hag-reservoir.md]]).

### The two rules divide the graph between them

| | BTSP | STDP |
|---|---|---|
| Learns | **Nodes** — a new discrete concept / receptive field | **Edges** — ordered pairwise transitions between existing concepts |
| Shots | One | Many |
| Window | Seconds | Tens of ms (needs theta compression to see behavioural pairs — [[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]) |
| Weight change per event | Large | Small |
| Fails at | Order *within* the window is indiscriminable; sequences longer than the biophysical window are unlearnable | Single events; each pairing is dominated by noise |
| Why the failure is a feature | Discrete concepts have no internal order to preserve | Most observed pairings are spurious; requiring many exposures *is* the spuriousness filter |

**Speed–amplitude trade-off (the review's unifying proposal):** the magnitude of the weight change in a plasticity event is proportional to the **expected information gained** in that event. BTSP integrates seconds of population activity → large change → one shot. STDP integrates one spike pair → small change → many shots. This is one principle generating both rules from their integration windows, and it is directly implementable: make a learning rate a function of the evidence integrated by the update.

**And this pair is expressive enough for the wiki's whole target.** A system that can encode concepts *and* the pairwise sequential relationships between them can in principle represent **any graph** (Liao & Losonczy 2024) — which is [[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] reached from the synapse rather than from the task. The caveat the review adds is the one the wiki tracks as G27: unlike graph learning in the machine setting, **the concepts themselves are also learned, from a continuous input stream.**

**The distinction is not clean.** Place fields are also gradually refined and stabilised by Hebbian plasticity (Mehta et al. 1997), and BTSP may participate in that refinement too — a higher BTSP rate is associated with a more stable code (Vaidya et al. 2023; Madar et al. 2023). Read the table as two ends of a continuum indexed by integration window, not as two systems.

## Why this matters for a reasoning model

| Property | Consequence for the architecture |
|---|---|
| **Deployment-time weight change** | Backpropagation-trained networks cannot learn after training; the data they assume is i.i.d. and timeless, which physical reality is not (information is temporally and spatially correlated). Plasticity is the only family here that writes during the episode — the literal implementation of binding an instance-graph |
| **Locality** | Errors in fast **M** never propagate through slow **W**; the two levels stay decoupled, which is the property the `p = f(g, x)` factorization wants anyway (gap G1) |
| **Delayed sparse reward is reachable** | Node perturbation + eligibility traces learn from a reward arriving long after the responsible activity — the credit structure a multi-hop traversal actually has |
| **The rule is a hyperparameter** | `η`, `A₊`, `τ₊` are hand-set. This is the opening that [[wiki/concepts/meta-optimized-plasticity.md]] exploits: make them differentiable and the *rule* becomes learnable |
| **Metaplasticity is stateful gating** | A continual-learning mechanism that needs no task boundaries, unlike elastic weight consolidation — the synapse's own history sets its writability |
| **Single-shot writes are licensed, not automatic** | BTSP writes only when an instructive plateau arrives, so *when to allocate a new unit* is a separate signal carried on a separate anatomical pathway (entorhinal → CA1 distal dendrites). That is the allocate-vs-reuse decision of [[wiki/concepts/pattern-separation-completion.md]] implemented as a gate, and it is what gap G19 was asking for |
| **Learning rate scales with evidence integrated** | The speed–amplitude trade-off is a one-line change to any optimizer: weight the step by how much independent evidence the update averaged over, and single-shot and incremental learning become two operating points of one rule rather than two mechanisms |

**(brainstorm)** The rules above form a ladder where each rung buys one missing ingredient: order (STDP), reward (three-factor), an unbiased estimator (node perturbation), time (eligibility traces), and — with rule 6 — **a node**. BTSP is the first rule on this page with a claim on structure: one event creates one reusable discrete unit, and STDP then links the units. What is still missing is *identity across contexts*: neither rule says whether the field created in environment A is the same node as a field created in environment B, so the pair can build a graph per environment and cannot say two graphs share a shape. Plasticity now supplies the instance-graph write mechanism ([[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]) and still says nothing about the meta-graph.

## Does a non-gradient rule learn anything?

Richards et al. 2019 answer with a decomposition. Take a plasticity rule as a **vector field over weight space**; any vector field splits into a component along `∇_W F` and components orthogonal to it.

| Alignment with `∇_W F` | Trajectory | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Full | Direct ascent to the optimum | Learning |
| Partial | Indirect ascent — still arrives | Learning |
| Orthogonal | Never approaches the optimum | **Change, not learning** |

So the standing worry that Hebbian-family rules approximate no gradient is not disqualifying: a rule needs only a **positive projection** onto some objective's gradient. The price is a demand this page must now meet for each rule it lists — **name the quantity that improves**. If no metric gets better, the phenotypic change is not learning by any usable definition, which is also the wiki's sharpest statement of what gap G19 (no local rule is selective about what it writes) actually costs: an unselective rule may be writing orthogonally.

**Two entries now meet the demand outright, and they name opposite objectives.** Plain Hebb (rule 1) ascends *retained input variance* — it converges to the top eigenvector of `C`, so the family's founding rule does learn by Richards et al.'s criterion and simply learns the wrong objective for a reasoning system. Rule 2 ascends the **data log-likelihood of a latent-cause mixture**, but only when a stochastic winner-take-all circuit sits behind it (Nessler et al. 2009, 2013): the spike is the E-step sample, the STDP application is the M-step. The contrast is the useful part — **the same synapse-local rule has a different objective depending on the inhibitory circuit around it**, so "what does this rule optimize" is a question about the circuit, not the synapse. For rules 3–7 the objective is still named only informally.

## Open problems

- **No rule on this page is selective about *what* it writes.** Hebbian-family updates fire on any coactivity, including coactivity produced by a shortcut feature ([[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]]). A local rule cannot tell a causal edge from a correlational one — it is by construction a correlation detector. **One escape is now priced:** give up on making the *rule* selective and gate its writes with a population-level statistic instead — the fast pathway's recall of the proposed update, which separates recurring from one-off update patterns because the overlap distribution concentrates over `N` synapses even though no single synapse can distinguish them. This buys two-state synapses the retention scaling that otherwise demands a multi-timescale synapse, and beats *any* synapse-local consolidation mechanism when reinforcement arrives in bursts (Lindsey & Litwin-Kumar 2024, [[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]]). It filters noise, not shortcuts.
- **Erasure has no local criterion at all.** The selectivity complaint above is about writes; the structural rule (8) shows it is worse on the other side — growth can be aimed by correlation, but which synapse to *remove* is chosen at random for want of any local statistic that predicts the effect of removal on the population code (Cazalets et al. 2025). Every rule on this page with a decay or depression term inherits this silently.
- **Stabilization is unsolved in general**, only patched (bounding, normalization, history terms). The instability is intrinsic to the positive feedback. **One rule already carries its own patch as a measured fact rather than a modelling choice:** spike-timing potentiation is confined to weak synapses (`r = −0.72` against log initial strength; essentially absent above 500 pA) while depression is strength-independent, so `A₊(w)` decays exactly where runaway would start (Bi & Poo 1998). This is a bound, not an account — nothing says what sets the 500 pA scale, whether it is homeostatically re-set, or how it interacts with the depressing lobe to fix the stationary distribution.
- **Glial modulation and neurogenesis appear in no learning rule.** If astrocytes are required for structural synaptic change in biology, every rule here is modelling an incomplete mechanism.
- **What does metaplasticity compute?** It is described as protecting against saturation; no computational account states what objective its state machine is optimizing. And its trigger need not be local at all: in the hippocampus→prefrontal pathway the lock is set by activity in a **third region** (basolateral amygdala), so metaplasticity there is an addressed control signal on an edge rather than a synapse's own history — a mechanism with no counterpart in any rule on this page (Spedding & Jay 2012, gap G52).
- **The behavioural timescales do not line up.** If LTP has no critical interstimulus interval, decays in days where behaviour persists for months, and shows no reacquisition-after-extinction effect, then the mechanism cited as the biological warrant for the whole family is not obviously the mechanism behind behavioural learning (T13). No rule here is stated at a timescale that would fix this.
- **Sparse-reward credit still needs an exploration policy.** Node perturbation is unbiased but its variance grows with network size; nothing says where the perturbations should be injected.
- **Where does the instructive signal come from?** BTSP's whole selectivity rests on the plateau, and what generates a plateau at the right moment is unknown — entorhinal cortex is a proposal (Grienberger & Magee 2022), not a result. A single-shot rule with an unexplained teacher has relocated the credit-assignment problem, not solved it. **And the leading functional theory needs the teacher to be *uninformative*:** Wu & Maass 2025 model plateaus as an input-independent Poisson process, which is exactly what delivers the uniform memory load and the absence of retrograde degradation. If real plateaus turn out to be content-driven, the selectivity improves and the load-spreading argument collapses — the two desiderata pull in opposite directions.
- **The molecular substrate of the seconds-long eligibility trace is unidentified.** αCaMKII is the leading candidate (Xiao et al. 2023). Until it is pinned down, the seconds-wide kernel is a fitted phenomenology, and its shape — which sets what counts as "one event" — is not derived from anything.
- **Does plasticity run offline?** Whether weights change within hippocampus during rest is unstudied, though SWR-paired spiking potentiates in vitro and disrupting awake ripples impairs learning with online representations intact ([[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]).
- **A large fraction of measured plasticity is non-Hebbian *by definition*.** Hebb gave no rule for inhibitory synapses and no prediction for anti-causal order, so heterosynaptic and homeostatic plasticity fall outside the family entirely — and volume learning (diffuse retrograde nitric oxide) breaks the shared assumption that a weight update is indexed by a synapse rather than by a spatial neighbourhood.
- **Inhibitory plasticity has no agreed rule.** Reports are variously potentiating, depressing and rate-dependent; models both require it (Liao et al. 2022) and reproduce replay phenomena without it (Ecker et al. 2022; Nicola & Clopath 2017). The disagreement extends to the *excitatory* synapses that drive inhibitory cells: in hippocampal culture those synapses show **no** spike-timing plasticity in either direction (Bi & Poo 1998), while a symmetric timing rule is reported at inhibitory synapses in auditory cortex (D'amour & Froemke 2015) — so whether the inhibitory subnetwork is a learned or a fixed part of the circuit is unsettled at both of its interfaces.
- **Nothing says what sets target specificity.** If a write happens or not according to the *identity of the postsynaptic cell*, then which populations are writable is a property of the wiring diagram, decided developmentally, and no rule on this page has a term for it. A model that freezes a subpopulation's input weights is making a claim about cell type, and the wiki has no principle for choosing which subpopulation **(brainstorm)**.

## Connections

- **[[wiki/entities/rolls-treves-hippocampal-model.md]]** — states a requirement most Hebbian accounts omit: heterosynaptic LTD is *necessary* alongside LTP for efficient associative storage, and doubles as the forgetting mechanism that keeps a capacity-limited store from overloading; also names non-associative mossy-fibre plasticity as a signal-to-noise device favouring consistently-firing inputs.

- **[[wiki/concepts/biologically-plausible-credit-assignment.md]]** — the same locality constraint approached from the other end: that page asks how much of backpropagation survives locality, this one covers the rules that never approximated it and are dynamical systems in their own right. The bridge is the STDP window: its asymmetric form implements a rate-of-change rule and its symmetric form an activity-level rule, which are precisely the two error-representation classes that page splits on — and the asymmetric window is itself *derivable* from `ΔW ∝ V̇_j S_i`, which makes the rule a gradient descent on whatever objective the network's dynamics already increase (Bengio et al. 2015).
- **[[wiki/concepts/meta-optimized-plasticity.md]]** — takes the hand-set coefficients of these rules (`η`, `A₊`, `τ₊`) as the parameters of an outer loop, converting a fixed rule into a discovered one; run over ten candidate terms with an L1 penalty it keeps a pseudo-gradient, an error-based Hebbian term and Oja's rule, so this page's redundancy becomes measurable rather than argued.
- **[[wiki/concepts/continual-learning.md]]** — metaplasticity is the biological mechanism elastic weight consolidation reimplements as a loss penalty; the mechanism version needs no task boundaries.
- **[[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]]** — short- and long-term plasticity are the synaptic-level version of the fast/slow split that CLS states at the systems level; the timescale separation is present in a single synapse before any second anatomical system is invoked.
- **[[wiki/entities/spiking-tem.md]]** — STDP used as the *memory write of a generative model* rather than as a plasticity demonstration, and shown to be necessary for something upstream of memory: removing it drops entorhinal grid emergence from 59.6% to 10.1%, so the structural code cannot form until the associative store works.
- **[[wiki/entities/spiking-neural-networks.md]]** — STDP is defined on spike times, so it presupposes a spiking substrate; the rules on this page are what a spiking network is optimized *by* when gradients are unavailable.
- **[[wiki/concepts/temporal-coding.md]]** — where the measured kernel and the modelled one conflict: delay selection there needs potentiation proportional to the existing weight, while the measurement here confines potentiation to weak synapses ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T76). Otherwise: what this family's timing kernel is *for*, and the two parameters this page had left implicit: the window's peak belongs at `s ≈ −t_r/2` (half the postsynaptic rise time) because that is what makes delay selection self-reinforcing, and its width bounds nothing — a millisecond-wide window tunes 200 µs structure through competition among delays rather than through kernel sharpness (Gerstner et al. 1996).
- **[[wiki/concepts/event-segmentation.md]]** — a clock-free recurrence detector that falls out of the timing rule: repeated exposure to a spatio-temporal pattern walks the postsynaptic response *earlier* by concentrating weight on consistently-early afferents, so "this pattern recurred" is readable downstream as "this unit fired sooner", with no counter and no time reference (Guyonneau et al. 2005).
- **[[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]** — supplies a write rule for fast **M** that operates during the episode; with BTSP for nodes and STDP for edges the pair is expressive enough to build *any* instance-graph, and still has no notion of node identity *across* graphs, so the meta-level stays unwritten.
- **[[wiki/concepts/shortcut-learning.md]]** — a purely local correlation detector has no lever by which to prefer a causal edge, so plasticity rules inherit the shortcut problem rather than solving it.
- **[[wiki/concepts/universal-induction.md]]** — the ideal these rules approximate under locality and compute budgets: Bayesian conditioning of a universal mixture needs no update rule at all, so every local rule is a bounded stand-in for it.
- **[[wiki/concepts/predictive-coding-free-energy.md]]** — supplies an error-driven local write rule where this page's rules are coactivity-driven, and makes the precision gates `α, β, γ` a metaplasticity-like control over how much the fast level writes (Butz 2016).
- **[[wiki/concepts/three-component-framework.md]]** — rehabilitates non-gradient rules by the vector-field decomposition (only a rule *orthogonal* to `∇F` fails to learn), while imposing the counter-demand that every rule name the objective it improves, or be classed as change rather than learning.
- **[[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]** — supplies the temporal re-clocking these rules need (a theta cycle compresses a seconds-long trajectory into one STDP window) and the arena where the inhibitory and offline plasticity rows would do their work.
- **[[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]]** — BTSP is the synapse-level mechanism of the "fast, one exposure" column: a single plateau writes a new unit, which is what one-shot encoding means at the level of a rule.
- **[[wiki/concepts/pattern-separation-completion.md]]** — where this page's neurogenesis row acquires a demonstrated function: ablating adult dentate neurogenesis impairs discrimination only at *low* sample-target separation, so added capacity is spent specifically on pushing similar inputs apart (Yassa & Stark 2011).
- **[[wiki/concepts/successor-representation.md]]** — what rule 7's fixed point computes: the temporal symmetry of the plasticity kernel selects whether the stored matrix is the successor, the predecessor, or the policy-insensitive reversible map, so a plasticity coefficient becomes a representational design choice (Keck et al. 2025).
- **[[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]** — the correction this page needed to its central question: the objective a synapse-local rule ascends is set by the inhibition around it, since STDP plus a *stochastic winner-take-all* implements online expectation-maximization for a latent-cause mixture while the same kernel without it is an unnormalized coactivity detector (Nessler et al. 2009, 2013). Also receives from this page a constraint on what inhibitory circuitry can learn at all: excitatory synapses *onto* GABAergic cells show no spike-timing plasticity in either direction in hippocampal culture (Bi & Poo 1998), so the four control channels would be routed by wiring rather than written by correlated activity. Beyond that, the rival route to flexibility: remapping by rewriting excitatory weights versus remapping by re-mixing inhibitory gains, the second fast, reversible and weight-free. Plasticity re-enters one level up — short-term depression at the place-cell→*Pvalb* synapse and potentiation at the place-cell→*Sst* (OLM) synapse are what write the within-field schedule that hands excitatory control from entorhinal to CA3 input mid-traversal.
- **[[wiki/entities/sparse-distributed-memory.md]]** — the outer-product rule engineered rather than described: saturating up–down counters (bounded weights whose overflow discards the oldest evidence), a fixed non-learning addressing layer that is what keeps one-shot writes from interfering, and an error-gated variant (CMAC's `g(p̂_u − s_u)/K`, applied only when the retrieval error is too large) that is a write-selectivity rule of the kind G19 says no local rule has.
- **[[wiki/entities/stp-flickering-cann.md]]** — the short-term-plasticity row run as the *only* memory in a network: a `τ_f > τ_r` rebound gives the just-abandoned attractor a gain advantage that outlives its sensory support, which is a memory with no fixed point, no content and no write decision — the opposite end of the family from BTSP.
- **[[wiki/entities/stsp-working-memory-rnn.md]]** — the short-term-plasticity row trained rather than hand-set, and the sign result: Hebbian short-term plasticity is hard to train while the excitatory-anti-Hebbian form trains with no clipping because stability is built into its parameterization — plus the ablation result that a memory held in synaptic *state* survives destruction of half the learned weights.
- **[[wiki/entities/dense-sequence-memory.md]]** — the payoff and the ceiling of the temporally asymmetric rule: STDP writes exactly `J_ij ∝ Σ_μ ξ_i^{μ+1} ξ_j^μ`, and what that stores is a sequence only `O(N)` steps long unless a nonlinearity is applied to the overlap downstream — so the storable sequence length is set outside the plasticity rule entirely.
- **[[wiki/entities/adaptive-cann.md]]** — a case where the computational role is pinned down and the synaptic implementation is explicitly left open: the dynamics require only a slow negative feedback proportional to recent activity, which spike-frequency adaptation (intrinsic) and short-term depression (synaptic) both supply, so nothing in the behaviour distinguishes them.
- **[[wiki/entities/context-modular-memory-network.md]]** — the strongest challenge in the wiki to weights-as-memory: a symmetric Gaussian random matrix carrying no pattern information stores arbitrary chosen patterns as stable attractors once the right per-context binary mask is applied, so learning could sit in a sign-agreement gate rather than in weight change — and bounded or binary synapses then suffice ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T59).
- **[[wiki/entities/hopfield-network.md]]** — the Hebbian rule taken literally and applied once: an outer-product write that is local and correlation-based yet installs a *global* structure (an energy landscape), which is the clearest demonstration in the wiki that local plasticity can specify a network-level computation.
- **[[wiki/entities/boltzmann-machine.md]]** — Hebbian and anti-Hebbian terms inside one rule, with the sign decided by whether the network is currently driven by the world or by itself: the clearest case in the wiki that a rule's *phase context* carries as much information as its pre/post product.
- **[[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]** — where rule 1's PCA fixed point meets a measured code: adding a Hebbian term to a randomly connected prefrontal model is reported to be what raises mixed selectivity to observed levels, which puts a local coactivity rule in competition with two wiring-only accounts of the same phenomenon ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T67).
- **[[wiki/concepts/dendritic-computation.md]]** — this page's "the dendrites are a high-dimensional random-feature basis" made the primary object: each segment is an independent `θ`-of-`s` coincidence detector with a measured threshold of 8–20 synapses within 20–300 µm and 1–5 ms, so the plateau that licenses a BTSP write is itself a pattern detection, and clustered plasticity is the rule that chooses which `s` synapses a segment samples.

- **[[wiki/concepts/memory-allocation-excitability.md]]** — the write variable that is not a weight: a cell-wide, CREB-dependent excitability tag decaying over *hours* selects which neurons a memory is written into, while every rule on this page decides what is written at the synapses of the neurons so selected. It also supplies a selectivity gate of a shape absent here — a conjunction of dendritic plasticity (ERK diffusing to the soma) and somatic firing (CaMKIV), i.e. local evidence of change × global evidence that the change mattered (Lisman et al. 2018, gap G19).
- **[[wiki/concepts/recall-gated-consolidation.md]]** — takes this page's standing complaint (no local rule is selective about what it writes) as a premise rather than a defect, and moves selectivity to the population: a third factor whose value is the fast pathway's prediction accuracy, decision confidence or familiarity gates the slow store's write. The scaling result prices the trade — population gating gives two-state synapses the retention scaling that otherwise demands a multi-timescale synapse, and beats *any* synapse-local mechanism when reinforcement arrives in bursts (Lindsey & Litwin-Kumar 2024).
- **[[wiki/entities/hag-reservoir.md]]** — rule 8 as a system: the connectivity matrix itself grown from an empty start by homeostasis-gated long-window correlation, which decorrelates an excitatory-only recurrent network as effectively as adding inhibitory weights, and shows the family's usual moment-to-moment rules (Intrinsic Plasticity, Anti-Oja) losing to the same statistic estimated over a window.
- **[[wiki/entities/btsp-cam.md]]** — rule 6 evaluated as a memory *system* rather than as a field-creator, and the sharpest statement of what it costs to drop the postsynaptic term: with the plateau modelled as an input-independent Poisson gate, memory load spreads uniformly over the population, so sequentially learning 10,000 items leaves the first 100 as retrievable as the last 100 — a property no postsynaptically-gated one-shot rule has. It also gives the rule a second reading: `Δw_i = ±1` on every gated active input is an **involution**, so the same event potentiates a silent synapse and depresses a strong one, and the depression branch is what pulls traces for similar items apart (Wu & Maass 2025).
- **[[wiki/concepts/manifold-constrained-learning.md]]** — an upper bound on what any of these rules can do in hours, measured behaviourally: monkeys re-associate existing population co-modulation patterns to a new cursor readout within a session, but cannot learn a readout that requires co-modulation patterns the network does not already express, so the reach of local plasticity on a fast timescale is bounded by the current latent basis rather than by the size of the weight change required (Sadtler et al. 2014). The follow-up sharpens the target: because the activity repertoire is preserved across learning, the authors read the change as occurring in M1's *inputs* rather than in its recurrent connectivity — local weight change inside the area carrying the manifold would be expected to move the repertoire, and it does not (Golub et al. 2018, [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T79).
- **[[wiki/concepts/generalization-optimized-consolidation.md]]** — a division of labour between two rules on this page plus a process neither contains: one-shot Hebbian association writes the fast index, slow error-corrective gradient descent writes the generalization, and the whole content of the theory is that the second must be *halted* by a supervisor no local rule can supply.
- **[[wiki/concepts/engram.md]]** — the unit-level container for everything this page writes: cell selection is by excitability and is marked by the immediate-early gene program (*Fos*, *Arc*) that regulates receptor trafficking downstream, so IEG expression is an experimental read-out of *where* these rules were running, not a rule itself.
- **[[wiki/entities/pbwm.md]]** — a write rule that is licensed rather than coactivity-driven: a stripe's weights change in proportion to its own gating action times a broadcast dopamine scalar, which is the content selectivity gap G19 asks for — bought by requiring an external reward and an exploration mechanism (`Random Go`) to generate writes to evaluate.
- **[[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]** — supplies the return edge every rule on this page assumes away: deep layers of ventral prelimbic/infralimbic cortex reach the striatal patch compartment and medial accumbens shell, both of which contact ventral tegmental and nigral dopamine neurons directly, so the neuromodulatory term of a local rule is something the controller can *write to* rather than only consume (gap G50). It also supplies the wiki's only long-range synapse characterised as a *stateful* site rather than a weight: the hippocampus→prefrontal projection potentiates, depresses and depotentiates under NMDA/protein-kinase-A control with dopamine D1 as the regulator, is metaplastically **locked** by high-frequency activity in a third region (basolateral amygdala), and loses inducibility entirely after a single stress exposure — recoverable with antidepressants or glucocorticoid-receptor blockade (Spedding & Jay 2012, gap G52).
- **[[wiki/concepts/dynamic-network-connectivity.md]]** — the second register on the same spine, morphologically segregated from this page's: long-term potentiation stores content by enlarging a sessile spine into a mushroom, while a content-free gain is set in seconds by ion channels in a long thin spine's neck — so what a synapse *learned* and whether it is currently *expressed* are independent variables (Arnsten et al. 2010).
- **[[wiki/concepts/neuroscience-ai-transfer.md]]** — the largest single block of *learning-rule* transfers on the track record (Hebb, STDP, three-factor, eligibility traces), and the block with the weakest performance against engineered alternatives.
- **[[wiki/concepts/precision-weighting.md]]** — reads the learning rate as an inferred quantity: precision *is* the Rescorla–Wagner rate parameter, so a rule that estimates its own error variance sets its own step size instead of receiving a schedule — and the parameter update itself is second-order, driven by terms that accumulate gradients, which is where eligibility traces come from (Friston 2009).
- **[[wiki/entities/basal-ganglia.md]]** — the third factor with its *sign* supplied by the receptor on the target rather than by the signal: one diffuse, unaddressed dopamine transient yields long-term potentiation in D₁-expressing spiny projection neurons and endocannabinoid-dependent depression in D₂-expressing ones, so opponent credit assignment costs one broadcast wire and two cell types. It is also gap G19 in its barest form — the modulator carries the sign, the currently active cortical ensemble carries the address, and nothing anywhere in the circuit distinguishes a causal ensemble from a coincident one. Two riders a builder needs: the same transient simultaneously changes intrinsic excitability in the same direction, so learning rate and activation gain are not separable here; and the sign is not a property of the synapse, since 5 Hz cortical stimulation gives potentiation under anaesthesia and depression in the awake animal (Gerfen & Surmeier 2011).
- **[[wiki/concepts/neuromodulatory-metaparameters.md]]** — splits the third factor in two and puts the halves on different wires: dopamine carries *how much error*, acetylcholine carries *how fast to apply it*, so the rule's magnitude and its step size become independently addressable — and the step size has a proposed online estimator rather than a schedule (Doya 2002).
- **[[wiki/concepts/equilibrium-propagation.md]]** — where a rule from this page becomes a credit-assignment algorithm by integration rather than analogy: the contrastive two-phase update is the time-integral of a spike-timing rule along the nudged trajectory, so the global rule is not a separate postulate that merely resembles the local one.
- **[[wiki/concepts/reward-prediction-error.md]]** — the third factor these rules take, and the coupling none of them has: in striatum the same broadcast scalar scales the update *and* the activation function of the units it updates, oppositely signed on two populations.
- **[[wiki/concepts/test-time-training.md]]** — the same commitment (write weights during deployment) made with backpropagation and a supervised loss on the task's own demonstration pairs instead of a local rule; the machine-learning literature's strongest result for deployment-time plasticity, and it has no stopping criterion and no consolidation channel.
- **[[wiki/concepts/spike-frequency-adaptation.md]]** — presynaptic vesicle depletion is one of the three biological causes of spike-frequency adaptation, so short-term depression and adaptation are one functional variable measured at opposite ends of the synapse; which end carries it decides whether the adaptation is private to a cell or specific to a single incoming axon.
- **[[wiki/entities/liquid-state-machine.md]]** — the short-term-plasticity row's cleanest ablation, and a mechanism claim rather than a performance one: in a spiking circuit whose every time constant is 30 ms, replacing Tsodyks–Markram dynamic synapses with static ones at matched firing rate destroys the classification of every 250 ms input segment except the last, because without `u·x` the circuit's response to each incoming spike becomes stereotypical instead of conditioned on context set hundreds of milliseconds earlier. Dynamic synapses are also named there as a filter class with the pointwise separation property, i.e. sufficient on their own to make a liquid universal.
- **[[wiki/concepts/spike-train-error-metrics.md]]** — the supervised limit of this page's two-factor skeleton, with the modulator replaced by a teacher, and the one place the eligibility kernel stops being a fitted object: ascending the log-likelihood of a target spike train produces the postsynaptic **potential** `ε` as the presynaptic factor by derivation, settling a choice that ReSuMe, SPAN and PSD each made by hand (Gardner & Grüning 2016). It also shows that the *second* factor's temporal support is what decides stability — an instantaneous target-minus-actual error has its attractor at the threshold-crossing weight, independent of the target.
- **[[wiki/entities/spiking-hippocampal-cam.md]]** — STDP used as an access *protocol* rather than as a learning rule: the sign of `Δw(Δt)` alone implements writing, erasing and the separation between them, so the store's correctness rests on the rule's time constants (≥4 steps between activations) and on the platform's residual-decay term — a case where a plasticity rule is load-bearing for control, not for representation (Casanueva-Morato et al. 2024).
- **[[wiki/concepts/excitation-inhibition-balance.md]]** — inhibitory plasticity doing computational rather than stabilising work: `Δw = η(pre·post − ρ_0·pre)` pinning each node's excitatory population to 4 Hz is what makes the map from a node pair's excitation/inhibition ratio to their functional connectivity *monotone*; switch the rule off and the map is non-monotone and the coupling parameter cannot be fitted at all (Schirner et al. 2023).
- **[[wiki/concepts/encoding-retrieval-alternation.md]]** — a constraint no spike-timing kernel in this page carries: potentiation and depression assigned to opposite halves of a 4–8 Hz oscillation, so the sign of plasticity is set by an endogenous phase rather than by pre/post spike order alone — which is what lets one circuit run separation-favouring and completion-favouring plasticity without either cancelling the other.
