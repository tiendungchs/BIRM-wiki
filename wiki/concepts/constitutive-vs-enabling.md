# Constitutive vs Enabling — Sorting a Correlate Into Content, Background, Trigger or Readout

**A variable that correlates with a system's state is doing one of five jobs, and only one of them is *being* the state. The partition — content, background condition, modulator, trigger, output — is not philosophy: each cell is falsified by a different experimental design, and a measured correlate that has not been run through the designs licenses no architectural claim. Applied to the cortex, the partition moves most of the evidence for prefrontal involvement in conscious content into the modulator and output cells and leaves a posterior "hot zone" holding the content. Applied to a network, it is the reason an ablation sweep, a logit-attribution map and a probe table each measure a different cell and are routinely reported as if they measured the same one.**

> **Provenance.** Boly, Massimini, Tsuchiya, Postle, Koch & Tononi 2017, *Are the neural correlates of consciousness in the front or in the back of the cerebral cortex? Clinical and neuroimaging evidence*, J Neurosci 37(40):9603–9613 (`raw/boly-2017-ncc-front-or-back.md`). A *Dual Perspectives* review — **no new data**, paired with an opposing piece (Odegaard, Knight & Lau). Four of six authors are proponents of the integrated-information account, which sites content posteriorly; read the taxonomy and the lesion ledger as the contribution and the verdict as advocacy. The stimulation half of the same argument, with rates, is on [[wiki/concepts/perturbation-elicitability.md]].

## The partition

| Cell | Definition | Diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| **Content** (content-specific neural correlate of consciousness, NCC) | The minimal mechanism that *specifies* one particular content. Union over all contents = the **full NCC** | Lesion abolishes that content specifically; stimulation inserts or distorts it; the content is decodable there with no task and no report |
| **Global enabling factor** | Necessary for the whole system to run: blood flow, oxygen, temperature | Removing it kills everything at once and specifies nothing |
| **Activating system** | Neuromodulation (cholinergic, noradrenergic), diffuse thalamocortical projections, anterior forebrain mesocircuit — sets the *level*, not the content | Stimulation restores the level in an anaesthetised or seizing animal without inserting any content |
| **Modulator / processing loop** | Attention, working memory, expectation: changes the *probability* that a given content is selected, by biasing excitability of the content cell | Manipulate it **orthogonally** to the content and the two dissociate |
| **Trigger (input path)** | Sensory pathways. A reliable but indirect cause: the same content occurs imagined and dreamt with the pathway silent | Content survives with the pathway removed (dreams, imagery); pathway activity occurs without the content (masking) |
| **Output (readout path)** | Motor and language pathways used to *report*. Necessary to measure the content, contributes nothing to it | The correlate disappears in a **no-report** design |

**The load-bearing move is that the last two are usually the same tissue as the claim.** A task-based experiment ("press when you see it") activates attention, working memory, decision and motor circuits on exactly the trials where the content is present, so every one of them correlates with content at whatever significance the sample supports.

## The dissociation designs, and what each one removes

| Design | Confound removed | Result it produced |
|---|---|---|
| **Within-state, no-task** — contrast dreaming vs dreamless within NREM (~60% dream recall) or REM (~95%) sleep | Behavioural state, arousal, task, report | Posterior parieto-occipital hot zone; an online EEG marker over posterior cortex predicts dreaming vs not at **85%** accuracy |
| **Unresponsive but conscious** — ~20% of anaesthesia cases, ~35% of complex partial seizures; perturbational complexity of the EEG response to transcranial magnetic stimulation as the read-out | Unconsciousness confounded with unresponsiveness | Consciousness detectable with no behaviour at all — the instrument that makes the whole ledger scoreable in patients |
| **No-report** | The entire output cell | Posterior correlates survive; frontal activation tracks reporting |
| **Task-irrelevant but experienced** (inattentional blindness, backward masking) | Task relevance, which co-varies with content in every standard design | Content-specific correlate stays posterior; the frontal **P3** appears *only when the stimulus is task-relevant* |
| **Orthogonal manipulation** of consciousness × attention / working memory / expectation | The modulator cell, one factor at a time | The modulator's correlate and the content's separate |
| **Reverse inference** — `P(content \| activation)` rather than `P(activation \| content)` | Base rate; regions active in many tasks | See below |
| **Decoding, single-trial** | Group-average smearing; "activated" ≠ "predictive" | Best predictors of minimally-conscious vs vegetative state are temporo-parieto-occipital |

**Instrument ordering, asserted and defensible:** lesion > stimulation > imaging. Lesions and stimulation test *constitution* (does editing this change the content?); imaging tests *presence*. Intracranial stimulation predicts postoperative deficits better than fMRI or diffusion tensor imaging.

## The ledger it produces

| Instrument | Front (prerolandic: dorsolateral, medial prefrontal, anterior cingulate, orbitofrontal) | Back (parietal, occipital, lateral temporal) |
|---|---|---|
| **Lesion, full NCC** | Bilateral frontal lobectomy leaves consciousness intact — Patient A (Brickner 1936; BA 8–12, 16, 24, 32, 33, 45–47 removed) went unnoticed by two neurologists for over an hour; Patient KM's IQ *improved* after near-complete bilateral prefrontal resection; an iron spike through both frontal lobes left no perceptual complaint and a normal life. Medial lesions cause akinetic mutism, and recovered patients report having been conscious but unmotivated | Traumatic posterior corpus-callosum lesions in **98%** of patients still vegetative at 1 year; **214-fold** risk of permanent vegetative state; the same lesions predict permanent coma after cardiac arrest. Frontal traumatic lesions occur in ~half of acute traumatic vegetative state and **do not predict outcome** |
| **Lesion, content** | Little evidence of any specific content loss. Broca's-area lesions spare conscious speech *perception*; frontal injury slightly raises the threshold for 16 ms masked stimuli but the patients still experience them — a modulator signature | Prosopagnosia (fusiform), achromatopsia, visual form agnosia, simultanagnosia, loss of somatosensory percepts, loss of conscious speech/prosody comprehension (angular gyrus), loss of motor awareness (inferior parietal) |
| **Stimulation** | Largely silent for content; interferes with execution and produces movements. Rare positives: motivational states and intrusive thoughts from cingulate cortex | Phosphenes (V1), somatosensory percepts (postcentral), visual motion (temporoparietal), face-perception disruption (right fusiform), feeling of intention (parietal), out-of-body experience (angular gyrus) |
| **Forward inference** | Frontal activation ubiquitous in report-based bistable-perception and masking paradigms | Posterior activation likewise |
| **Reverse inference** | The frontal cluster **disappears** | Survives, sharply localised |
| **Decoding** | Poor predictor of consciousness under anaesthesia; working-memory *contents* decode worse from the frontoparietal delay region of interest than from the posterior sample region (Emrich et al. 2013) | Best single-trial predictor of state and of dream content; specific dream contents (faces, places, movement, speech) predictable from posterior and not anterior cortex |

**The reverse-inference numbers, because they are the cleanest transferable datum.** A Neurosynth forward meta-analysis on "conscious" puts frontal cortex on the map; the reverse map, `P(topic = consciousness | activation)`, erases it. At user-specified coordinates the posterior sites are diagnostic and the frontal one is not:

| Site | Best-predicted term | `p` |
|---|---|---|
| Fusiform face area `[41, −49, −20]` | faces | 0.88 |
| Temporo-occipital `[−46, −54, −12]` | visual words | 0.86 |
| Temporo-occipital `[46, −68, 0]` | visual motion | 0.90 |
| Superior temporal `[−58, −10, 0]` | speech sounds | 0.84 |
| Postcentral `[−54, −22, 20]` | touch | 0.88 |
| **Frontal peak of the consciousness forward map** `[−47, 6, 28]` | **"phonological" / "task"** | 0.76 / 0.63 |

The frontal peak of a consciousness map is, under inversion, a *task* region. That is the modulator/output cell being read as the content cell, with a number on it ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T102).

## The machine transfer

The partition is instrument-agnostic; a network has all five cells and the wiki's instruments are unevenly distributed over them.

| Cell in a network | The instrument that actually measures it | The instrument usually reported instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Content** | Intervention in the code's own basis — activation addition along a fitted direction, patching, causal tracing ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]) | A linear probe (presence, not constitution) |
| **Enabling** | Normalisation layers, residual scale, learning-rate schedule: remove and everything fails at once | Rarely separated; folded into "ablation hurt performance" |
| **Modulator** | Gates, attention weights, adapters — the components whose removal changes *which* representation wins, not what any representation says | Reported as content when the gate is task-correlated |
| **Trigger** | The encoder stack. Content should survive its replacement (imagery/dream analogue: generate the same latent from a different input path) | Conflated with content whenever probes are run on inputs only |
| **Output** | The unembedding, the decoder head, the answer-formatting circuit. **Direct logit attribution is an output-cell instrument by construction** — it decomposes the logit | Read as "which component computes the answer" |

Three designs the wiki should be running, all cheap, each the direct analogue of a row above:

- **The no-report control.** Score the internal correlate on trials where the model is *not asked to emit* the property — a forced-choice-free forward pass, or a probe trained on prompts that never query the feature. Any component whose signal vanishes is in the output cell. This is the single cheapest control in interpretability and almost nothing runs it.
- **The task-irrelevance control.** Present the feature while the task depends on something else. A component active only when the feature is task-relevant is a modulator; the biological version of exactly this contrast is what moved the P3 out of the content cell.
- **The generated-content control.** Elicit the same content with the input path silent (from a prompt that describes rather than displays; from sampling with the encoder detached). A content component must still carry it. **(brainstorm)** This is the machine form of the dream experiment, and it is the only listed design that can distinguish a stored concept from a re-encoding of the current input.

**What the partition costs.** It is a taxonomy of *nulls* as much as of positives, so it inherits the whole false-negative ledger: "no reportable change" and "no decodable signal" are conclusions about the instrument until a positive control on the same tissue at the same resolution says otherwise. Two of its verdicts here are already contested on exactly that ground — prefrontal content is decodable at 95% in tissue whose stimulation changes nothing ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T269), and a coarse decoding null over a mixed-selectivity code licenses nothing (T272).
