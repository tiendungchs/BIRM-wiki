# Structured Flows on Manifolds — Why a Low-Dimensional Trajectory Exists, and What Breaks the Symmetry That Shapes It

**Near an instability, a high-dimensional system separates into slow variables (*order parameters*) and fast ones; the fast ones are dragged onto a manifold whose flow the slow ones prescribe, so dimensionality reduction is a consequence of the operating point rather than an analysis choice. A *structured flow on a manifold* (SFM) is that pair — the low-dimensional surface plus the vector field on it. The load-bearing addition for this wiki is what supplies the structure: a translationally-invariant connectivity produces no non-trivial spatiotemporal pattern at all, so the flow is generated by the **symmetry breaking** that heterogeneous long-range connections introduce. And an SFM has two jobs, not one: it is the forward pattern generator *and* the object an inference process samples.**

> **Provenance.** Hashemi, Depannemaecker, Saggio, Triebkorn, Rabuffo, Fousek, Ziaeemehr, Sip, Athanasiadis, Breyton, Woodman, Wang, Petkoski, Sorrentino, Stefanovski, Schirner, Ritter, Bernard & Jirsa 2025, *Principles and Operation of Virtual Brain Twins*, IEEE Reviews in Biomedical Engineering (`raw/hashemi-2025-virtual-brain-twins.md`), Sections II and V-A. Traced back to Haken's synergetics, the local centre manifold theorem, and Jirsa & Sheheitli's derivation of SFMs by symmetry breaking in the low-dimensional space. Sits one level above [[wiki/concepts/dynamic-repertoire.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/metastability.md]] — those pages establish *that* whole-brain models must sit near an instability; this one says what being near an instability buys, and names the mathematical object.

## The reduction, as a mechanism rather than a method

| Step | Statement | What it supplies |
|---|---|---|
| **Operate near a bifurcation** | The system is close to a transition from one state to another | The precondition. Far inside a stable region nothing separates |
| **Local centre manifold theorem** | At an instability the spectrum splits: a few eigenvalues have near-zero real part, the rest are strongly negative | **Time-scale separation, derived rather than assumed** |
| **Adiabatic elimination** | Set the fast variables' derivatives to zero and solve for them in terms of the slow ones | Removes the fast dimensions; what remains is a surface, the *manifold* |
| **Enslaving** (Haken's term) | The slow variables — the **order parameters** — prescribe the flow the fast ones follow | The `≪` degrees of freedom that are left are not a summary of the system; they *govern* it |
| **Symmetry breaking** | Under a spatially-invariant (equivariant) interaction kernel the flow is trivial; heterogeneous long-range connections break the equivariance | The flow acquires *structure*. This is where the connectome enters |

The last row is what distinguishes this page from an ordinary "neural activity lies on a manifold" claim. **A dynamical system with symmetries is equivariant, and an equivariant system's reduced flow is degenerate.** The source's own demonstration is minimal: a one-dimensional sheet with a *single* long-range connection of length `d`; sweep `d`, and the system settles into a sequence of qualitatively different patterns. One heterogeneous edge is enough to change which SFM the system is on.

This is the same anatomical split [[wiki/concepts/mean-field-reduction.md]] uses for stability — homogeneous intracortical `g(|x − x′|)` versus heterogeneous corticocortical `G(x, x′) ≠ G(|x − x′|)` — read for a different consequence. There the split decided whether the resting state loses stability; here it decides whether the reduced dynamics have any structure to lose.

**The connectome is therefore not a parameter of the flow, it is the generator of the flow's structure** — and since the connectome changes across development, ageing and disease, the SFM changes with it. That gives a formal statement of what a structural change *does*: it re-shapes the manifold and the flow on it, rather than scaling a coupling.

## The four-way coincidence at the working point

Sweeping the global coupling `G` in a personalised whole-brain model, four independent quantities peak or minimise in the *same narrow band* (Rabuffo/Fousek results, reproduced in the source's Fig. 8):

| Quantity | Behaviour at the working point | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Fluidity** — variance of functional connectivity dynamics | maximal | The system keeps re-configuring; this is [[wiki/concepts/metastability.md]]'s `σ_R` in a different estimator |
| **Dimensionality** — variance accounted for by the first two principal components | **dimensionality falls / VAF rises** | The reduction is *empirically visible*: the flow collapses onto a low-dimensional surface exactly where the dynamics are richest |
| **Responsiveness** to perturbation, as driving noise is lowered | maximal | The system that wanders most is also the one a stimulus can steer most |
| **Fit to the individual's empirical fMRI** | maximal | The band is not a theoretical preference; it is where the model matches the person |

**Low dimensionality and high fluidity are not in tension — they co-occur, and they co-occur where the symmetry across nodes is broken.** That is the non-obvious content. A wiki reader's default intuition is that richer dynamics means higher effective dimension; here the opposite holds, because enslaving is what produces both: the order parameters are few *and* they are the ones that move.

**And it was validated against a perturbational measurement.** The model's prediction — that spontaneous fluidity and stimulus-evoked response complexity are set by the same parameter — was tested in humans under propofol, xenon and ketamine: fluidity of spontaneous activity separated cases of conscious report **as well as** the perturbational complexity index, which requires a TMS pulse. A cheap passive statistic matching an active probe is the strongest available argument that the working point is a real quantity and not a fitting artefact.

## The dual use: an SFM is also the inference target

The claim the wiki has no analogue of:

> The SFM serves, on the one hand, to describe mechanistically spatiotemporal pattern formation, and, on the other hand, **as a target for causal inference**. During inference, the SFM is the mathematical object sampled by the inference process.

Unpacked, this says the manifold does double duty:

- **Forward.** Given parameters, the flow on the manifold generates the observable time series. This is the ordinary generative-model reading ([[wiki/concepts/effective-connectivity.md]]).
- **Backward.** The manifold *constrains the posterior geometry*. Because the fast directions are enslaved, the parameters that move the system along the manifold are the ones the data can see, and the rest are degenerate. The source states the consequence directly: the manifold "naturally constrains the inference process".

**For a builder this is a design rule about where to put estimable parameters.** In [[wiki/entities/virtual-brain-twin.md]] only a handful of parameters are ever inferred (regional excitability, global coupling `G`); everything else is fixed from literature, and the stated reason is convergence. This page is why that works rather than being a shortcut: the enslaved directions carry no identifiable information, so a model that tries to infer them is spending sampling effort on a flat posterior. **Degeneracy is described here as "the geometrical counterpart of non-identifiability"** — the same statement as [[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]]'s many-to-one direction, but read off the *dynamics* rather than diagnosed after the fit.

## How the reduction happens — three candidates, none settled

The source is explicit that dimensionality reduction in the brain is observed and its mechanism is not established. The named candidates:

| Candidate | Statement | Status in the wiki |
|---|---|---|
| **Low-rank connectivity** | If the coupling matrix has rank `r`, the dynamics are confined to an `r`-dimensional subspace by construction | Nothing in the wiki builds one; the closest is the fixed rank of a retrieval embedding ([[wiki/concepts/retrieval-capacity.md]]) |
| **Averaging / decorrelation** | Population averaging over many weakly-correlated units suppresses all but a few collective modes | This is [[wiki/concepts/mean-field-reduction.md]]'s rung 3 — the eigenspectrum of the Fokker–Planck operator with a spectral gap |
| **Symmetry breaking in the low-dimensional space** | The reduced system is equivariant, and breaking the equivariance selects a structured flow (Jirsa & Sheheitli) | This page's own mechanism; requires heterogeneous connectivity |

These are not variants of one story. Low-rank connectivity fixes the manifold in the *weights*; averaging fixes it in the *statistics*; symmetry breaking fixes the *flow* given a manifold that already exists. A model can have all three, and no source in the wiki arbitrates which does the work in cortex.

## Relevance to a reasoning model

- **It gives "how many timescales" a derivation, not a hyperparameter** (gap `G67`). The split into slow order parameters and enslaved fast variables comes from the eigenvalue spectrum at the operating point, so the level count is a *consequence of being near a bifurcation*. Every hierarchy in the wiki fixes its levels by hand; this fixes them by where the spectral gap falls, and moves them when the operating point moves.
- **The manifold is the object to control, not the state.** If the order parameters govern, then a controller with access to a few slow variables has access to the system — which is a much smaller interface than the state vector, and is exactly the interface an SEEG electrode or a stimulation protocol has. **(brainstorm)** The machine analogue: a modular architecture near its coupling instability should be steerable by a control signal of dimension equal to the number of order parameters, and the wiki's usual assumption — that steering a network needs a signal of the network's own width — would be an artefact of running far from the instability.
- **Structural change has a formal image.** Pruning, demyelination and development all change the connectome; the SFM framework says what that *does* to computation (re-shapes the manifold and its flow) rather than only what it does to a graph statistic. For continual learning this is the statement that a weight change is a change of reachable dynamics, not only of stored content ([[wiki/concepts/continual-learning.md]]).
- **A caution for [[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]].** Every manifold that page reports is *measured* by a dimensionality-reduction algorithm applied post hoc. This page says a manifold with functional meaning must have a generator — a spectral gap, a low-rank coupling, a broken symmetry — and that nothing in the wiki exhibits one. A PCA plot is compatible with an SFM and does not evidence one (gap `G82`).
- **Symmetry is the enemy, and architectures are usually built symmetric.** Weight sharing, uniform module design, all-to-all or distance-only coupling — each is an equivariance. The result here is that an equivariant network's reduced dynamics are degenerate, so **heterogeneity in the coupling is a functional requirement rather than biological noise**, which is the same conclusion [[wiki/concepts/neuronal-parameter-heterogeneity.md]] reaches from the parameter side.

## Open problems

- **The reduction mechanism is unidentified in brains** (three candidates above), and unimplemented in any wiki architecture. Logged as `G82`.
- **Nothing here learns the manifold.** The connectome that breaks the symmetry is measured by tractography; the flow is a consequence. There is no plasticity rule that *shapes* an SFM toward a task — the same terminal limitation as [[wiki/concepts/mean-field-reduction.md]] and [[wiki/concepts/dynamic-repertoire.md]].
- **The "beyond visualization" claim is asserted.** The source states the argument that manifolds carry computational and functional meaning and immediately concedes that how the reduction occurs must still be established. The wiki should carry the SFM as a well-specified *hypothesis* with a demonstrated fitting utility, not as an established mechanism.
- **The space-time structure's practical importance is unproven.** Petkoski's resonance-body result — that the delay structure of the connectome makes graph theory reformulable and explains frequency-specific networks including the visual and default-mode networks (`N` = 100, Human Connectome Project) — is described by the source's own authors as "theoretically intriguing" with real-world importance "still to be shown".
- **The four-way coincidence is a coincidence of fitted quantities.** Fluidity, dimensionality, responsiveness and empirical fit all peak in the same `G` band, but three of the four are computed from the *simulated* signal; only the anaesthesia validation is independent of the model.

## Connections

- **[[wiki/concepts/mean-field-reduction.md]]** — supplies the equation this page reduces and the same homogeneous/heterogeneous connectivity split, read for a different consequence: there the split decides whether the resting state loses stability, here it decides whether the reduced flow has any structure at all, since an equivariant kernel yields a degenerate manifold.
- **[[wiki/concepts/dynamic-repertoire.md]]** — the same manifold approached empirically: that page says a connectome plus delays specifies a manifold of reachable configurations that noise samples, and this page supplies why a manifold exists (time-scale separation at an instability) and what gives its flow structure (symmetry breaking by long-range fibres).
- **[[wiki/concepts/metastability.md]]** — the working point measured two ways: `σ_R` there and functional-connectivity-dynamics variance ("fluidity") here, both maximal in the same narrow coupling band — and this page adds the non-obvious co-occurrence, that effective dimensionality *falls* exactly where fluidity peaks, because enslaving produces both.
- **[[wiki/concepts/population-geometry.md]]** — the measurement side of this page's object, and the caution it inherits: every manifold reported there is recovered by a dimensionality-reduction algorithm after the fact, whereas an SFM requires a named generator (spectral gap, low-rank coupling, broken symmetry), so a low-dimensional projection is compatible with an SFM without evidencing one.
- **[[wiki/concepts/attractor-dynamics.md]]** — the more general object: an SFM subsumes multistable states, limit cycles, travelling waves and any low-dimensional attractor as special cases of a flow on a manifold, so "which attractor" is a question about the flow's fixed points rather than about the system's architecture.
- **[[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]]** — the same non-identifiability derived from dynamics instead of diagnosed after fitting: degeneracy is "the geometrical counterpart of non-identifiability", so the enslaved fast directions are exactly the parameters a posterior cannot constrain, and which parameters are worth inferring is readable off the manifold before any data arrive.
- **[[wiki/entities/virtual-brain-twin.md]]** — the engineering system built on this page's dual-use claim: the SFM is simulated forward to generate SEEG/fMRI/MEG observables and simultaneously used as the object the Bayesian inversion samples, which is why only a handful of parameters are ever inferred and the rest fixed from literature.
- **[[wiki/concepts/effective-connectivity.md]]** — the estimation half stated in this page's vocabulary: inverting a generative model recovers the parameters that move the system *along* its manifold, and the observation model is what stands between the flow and the data.
- **[[wiki/concepts/neuronal-parameter-heterogeneity.md]]** — the same conclusion reached from the parameter side rather than the connectivity side: uniformity is an equivariance and equivariance costs dynamical structure, so heterogeneity is a functional requirement and not a manufacturing tolerance.
- **[[wiki/concepts/manifold-constrained-learning.md]]** — the behavioural counterpart: an animal can learn perturbations that stay within its existing intrinsic manifold and not those that leave it, which is what this page's enslaving predicts if the manifold is a property of the coupling rather than of the task.
- **[[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]** — a constraint on the navigation side: the state space to be navigated is not the full state vector but a low-dimensional surface whose shape is set by the graph's *heterogeneity*, so two graphs with the same degree statistics and different long-range structure give different navigable spaces.
- **[[wiki/concepts/learnable-synaptic-delays.md]]** — the temporal half of the symmetry breaking: each long-range edge carries a weight *and* a delay, and the delay structure spans a "resonance body" that gives the network frequency-specific preferences, so breaking spatial symmetry and breaking temporal symmetry are two separable design levers.
- **[[wiki/concepts/connectome-hubs-and-cores.md]]** — names the heterogeneous long-range fibres that do this page's symmetry breaking: a bilateral posterior medial core of connector hubs carrying >70% of between-module edge mass, with a bounded (exponential, ~10-fold) degree spread rather than a scale-free one.
- **[[wiki/concepts/anatomical-harmonic-modes.md]]** — this page's symmetry-breaking claim turned into a measurement and a basis: the heterogeneous long-range fibres are identified as >3 SD outliers to an exponential distance rule, and shuffling their positions degrades the low-frequency modes, so the structure of the reduced flow is traceable to specific edges (Vohryzek et al. 2024).
