# Population Geometry

**The level of description at which a population's activity is a point on a low-dimensional curved surface embedded in neuron-space, so that what a circuit represents is the *shape* of that surface and how task variables are arranged on it — not the tuning of any cell.**

[[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]] draws the wiki's sharpest methodological line: **population-level `g` is a legitimate prediction target; per-unit tuning is not**, because a task-forced low-dimensional latent can be projected onto a large population in many ways and nothing picks the projection. That line was stated and left without an instrument. This page is the instrument set, plus the wiki's strongest evidence that geometry is the thing conserved across brains while tuning is not (Nieh et al. 2021) — and the wiki's only measurement of an abstract geometry *forming*, in human single units, on the timescale of a session or of a four-minute verbal instruction (Courellis et al. 2024).

---

## Formal statement

`N` simultaneously recorded units give a state `s(t) = [s₁(t), …, s_N(t)] ∈ R^N`. The claim is that `s(t)` is confined to a manifold `M ⊂ R^N` of intrinsic dimension `d ≪ N`, and that the task variables are smooth functions on `M`.

| Quantity | Estimator | Value (mouse dorsal CA1, evidence-accumulation T-maze) |
|---|---|---|
| Intrinsic dimension `d` | Count neighbours within geodesic radius `r`; on a `d`-manifold `N(r) = c·r^d`, so fit the power law on a log-log plot over ~3 decades | `d = 5.4 [4.8, 6.0]` (95% bootstrap), in a **~450-dimensional** state space, `n = 7` animals |
| `d` by a second, independent route | Embed `M` in `d`-dimensional Euclidean space, reconstruct **held-out trials** from the `d` latents, correlate with real `ΔF/F` | Saturates at `d ≈ 5–6` — agrees with the power law |
| Curvature cost | Number of principal components needed to match the nonlinear embedding's held-out reconstruction | **29 / 40 / 47 PCs to match `d` = 4 / 5 / 6** — a linear count overstates the dimensionality by ~7–8× |

**The third row is the number to carry into every other page.** Wherever the wiki uses a *linear* dimensionality measure — participation ratio as the confound behind linear "neural predictivity" ([[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]]), PCA-based comparisons of representational richness — the quantity being measured is an artefact of how curved the manifold is, and can exceed the true latent count by nearly an order of magnitude. A model and a brain can differ by 8× in participation ratio while carrying the *same* 5-dimensional geometry.

## The instruments

| Instrument | Fitted object | What it reads out | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intrinsic dimensionality** | Power law `N(r) = c·r^d` on geodesic neighbour counts | How many latent variables the task forced | The fit range is chosen by hand; inherits the arbitrary-threshold criticism the wiki already applies to grid scores ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T37) |
| **Held-out reconstruction** | `d` latents → all `N` cells, cross-validated per trial | Whether `d` is sufficient, independent of the distance estimator | Rewards smoothing; a thresholding nonlinearity had to be fitted to match calcium optics |
| **Leave-one-neuron-out** | Fit `M` on `N−1` cells, then regress latents → the held-out cell | Whether `M` is a *population* property rather than a per-cell curve fit — a single neuron's activity is predictable from the rest | Only tested on the 25 most active cells |
| **Variable regression onto latents** | Gaussian-process regression `latents → position / evidence / velocity / view angle / previous choice / previous correct` | Which task variables are smooth functions on `M`, continuous and binary alike | Decodability, not use — the same caveat as [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]] |
| **Cross-condition generalization performance (CCGP)** | A linear decoder for a binary variable trained on *some* conditions, tested on held-out conditions defined by the values of the *other* variables | Whether a variable is coded in **abstract (generalizable) format** — not whether it is present. Decodability and CCGP dissociate: a variable can be fully decodable and score at chance on CCGP (Courellis et al. 2024) | Needs the condition grid enumerated in advance, so it inherits probing's label dependence; and the train/test split is a choice among many |
| **Parallelism score (PS)** | Cosine similarity between the coding vectors of the same variable computed at different values of the other variables | The *geometric reason* CCGP succeeds — coding directions being parallel. Cheap, and diagnosable neuron by neuron (consistency of modulation sign across conditions) | Undefined when the variable is not decodable at all; sensitive to how conditions are paired |
| **Shattering dimensionality** | Mean decoding accuracy over *all* balanced dichotomies of the conditions (35 for 8 conditions), including the parity-like ones no linear code supplies | How **expressive** the representation is — how many arbitrary readouts it supports. The counterweight to CCGP: geometry can gain abstraction and expressiveness at once | Grows with noise-free separability of anything; scales badly (`2^{n−1}−1` dichotomies) |
| **Cross-subject hyperalignment** | A single rotation `R ∈ SO(d)`: `e_B = GPR_A(R·x_B)` | Whether the geometry is **task-specific or subject-specific** — the only instrument here that needs no shared units, no shared cells and no probe trained per subject | The rotation group is a modelling choice; `SO(5)` has 10 free parameters, cross-validated on half the session |
| **Sequence prediction** | Reconstruct activity from `d` latents, re-run the sequence detector, sweep the threshold for an ROC | Whether the geometry accounts for the circuit's *temporal* structure and not only its state distribution | Sequence detector has its own shuffle-defined threshold |
| **Input-geometry control** | Run the identical pipeline on the raw sensory stream (here: the VR video the animal saw) | Whether the manifold is a representation or a re-description of the input | Run once, on a 17×-downsampled single colour channel |

The last row is the one the wiki should demand of every manifold claim. It is the population-geometry analogue of audit item 2 in [[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]] — *is the target already the phenomenon?* — moved from a supervised readout to a sensory input: a low-dimensional structure in the activity means nothing if the input stream has the same structure.

## What Nieh et al. 2021 establishes

Mouse dorsal CA1, two-photon calcium imaging, 3144 neurons (449 ± 64 simultaneous), 7 animals, running a virtual-reality T-maze where reward depends on which wall showed more towers. **Accumulated evidence** (`#right − #left`) is the abstract variable: not innate, not perceptually present, computable only after the task rules are learned.

| Result | Detail | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| **Single cells jointly code a physical and an abstract variable** | 917 cells with significant mutual information in the 2-D evidence × position (`E×Y`) space, exceeding both single-variable shuffle controls; **89.9%** beat both, 16% of the remainder were position-only, 6% evidence-only | The wiki's first **single-unit** demonstration that a learned, non-perceptual variable enters the hippocampal map. Every other "beyond space" entry on [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]] except the odour/time/frequency cells is fMRI |
| **Firing fields tile the abstract dimension** | 1.7 ± 0.3 fields per cell in `E×Y` space; 53% (490/917) multi-field | Evidence is coded like a place dimension — localised fields, not a monotonic ramp |
| **This is not splitter cells** | Choice-selective place sequences exist, but individual cells are *unreliable* trial-to-trial relative to a simpler alternation task — which is the prediction of joint coding (different trials traverse different evidence values), not of choice-conditioned place maps | Distinguishes a conjunctive map from a context-modulated one, using the *unreliability* as the signal |
| **~5-dimensional nonlinear manifold** | `d = 5.4 [4.8, 6.0]`; firing fields are localised *on* `M`; position and evidence appear as two **independent gradients** on `M` | The population claim, and the level at which the code is factorised (see below) |
| **Dimensionality is task-dependent** | A simpler one-side-cues version of the same maze yields a significantly *lower* `d` | `d` is a measurable readout of how many factors the task forced — see G40 below |
| **The geometry is shared across brains** | Hyperalignment by one `SO(5)` rotation: **69 ± 9%** (position) and **75 ± 10%** (evidence) of geometry shared between best pairs; cross-animal evidence decoding is **not significantly worse** than within-animal (`p = 0.81`), position slightly worse (`p = 0.016`) | The load-bearing result for this page — see below |
| **Sequences are trajectories on `M`** | 16 088 "doublets" (cell A reliably fires before cell B), each active in only 3.6 ± 0.01% of trials; reconstruction from 5 latents re-detects them at **TPR 0.87 / FPR 0.14**, and manifold path length predicts the inter-event *time*; choice-predictive doublets are more predictive than trial-ID-shuffled controls | Sequence structure is not a separate mechanism — it is the shadow of a path |
| **`M` is not the input's geometry** | MIND run on the visual stream the animal received gives a structure "fundamentally different" from `M` | The input-geometry control, passed |

## What Courellis et al. 2024 establishes — the geometry *forming*, in human single units

Same level of description, opposite experimental design: 2,694 single units in 17 neurosurgical patients (hippocampus 494, amygdala 889, ventromedial prefrontal 463, dorsal anterior cingulate 310, presupplementary motor 269, VTC (Ventral Temporal Cortex) 269), 36 sessions of an uncued serial reversal task. Four stimuli, two stimulus–response–outcome maps that are exact inversions of one another, latent context switching at random block boundaries. The behavioural signature is one-shot: after a single negative feedback the whole map should flip, so accuracy on the *first* encounter with each other stimulus in the new context splits sessions into **inference present** and **inference absent**. Both groups perform the non-inference trials well — the only difference is whether the latent variable is being used.

| Result | Number | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| **Abstract format appears only with inference, and only in hippocampus** | Context and stim-pair CCGP significantly above chance in inference-present sessions and at chance in inference-absent ones (`P_RS = 2.0 × 10⁻²⁸` both); no other recorded region changed shattering dimensionality, context or parity decodability, and **no other region held two variables in abstract format simultaneously** | The wiki's first measurement of an abstract geometry *appearing*, with a within-subject behavioural contrast rather than a cross-region comparison |
| **Format, not presence, is what tracks behaviour** | Stimulus decoding in hippocampus 0.73 → 0.76 (n.s., `P = 0.13`) while stimulus CCGP (`P = 0.041`) and PS (`P = 0.040`) rose; context decoding 0.63 → 0.67 (n.s., `P = 0.065`) while context CCGP (`P = 0.012`) and PS (`P = 0.015`) rose | Decodability was already there and bought nothing. The thing that changed with the ability to infer is the *arrangement* — which is the strongest argument in the wiki for scoring geometry rather than probe accuracy |
| **Abstraction and expressiveness rose together** | Shattering dimensionality 0.57 → 0.62 (`P_RS = 2.7 × 10⁻³`); the parity dichotomy — pure nonlinear interaction, no linear code supplies it — became decodable (`P_present = 0.0055`, `P_absent = 0.27`); decoding accuracy is *highest* for the hardest dichotomies | Kills the reflex that abstraction = low dimensionality. The code got both more disentangled and more expressive at once (see the tension below) |
| **Trial-by-trial, not session-by-session** | Within inference-present sessions, error trials show hippocampal decodability *and* format at inference-absent levels, in both stimulus and baseline periods | Correlational still, but the correlate moves at the timescale of single decisions — the tightest behaviour↔geometry coupling in the wiki (bears on [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T25) |
| **The upstream region does not do this** | VTC: 73% (195/269) of units stimulus-tuned vs 22% (109/494) in hippocampus; stimulus abstract in both; context **not decodable** in VTC in either group, context CCGP actually *decreased* (`P = 0.026`), and stimulus stayed decodable on error trials in VTC but not hippocampus | The reorganisation is not inherited from sensory input — an input-geometry control by anatomy rather than by re-running the pipeline on the stimulus stream |
| **It is a distributed code** | High context PS with no reliance on univariately context-tuned units; removing them does not remove the abstract context variable | Rules out the trivial reading (a "context cell" population), and matches the wiki's line that the object is population-level |

### The firing-rate mechanism — what actually moved

The paper's most exportable part, because it is a *recipe* rather than a description:

| Change | Measurement | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Global gain drop** | Hippocampal firing 3.37 ± 0.13 → 1.36 ± 0.03 Hz (60% reduction, `P_RS = 8.3 × 10⁻⁵`), baseline period likewise (58%); unique to hippocampus | Learning to infer made the region *quieter*, not busier |
| **One dichotomy exempted from the shrink** | Centroid distances fell for every dichotomy (5.77 → 4.17 Hz) **except context**, which rose (4.3 → 5.0 Hz, `P_ΔDist = 0.040`) and was the largest change of any dichotomy | Selective sparing under a global contraction — the separation of the behaviourally relevant latent is bought by shrinking everything else, not by amplifying it |
| **Variance drop is not a noise reduction** | Variance along coding directions fell for all dichotomies, but condition-wise Fano factor was unchanged (1.39 ± 0.22 vs 1.36 ± 0.14, `P_RS = 0.99`) | Poisson bookkeeping: the variance change is entirely explained by the rate change. Nothing was denoised |
| **Parallelism is a per-neuron consistency statistic** | Consistency of a unit's context modulation *across stimuli* rose 1.8 ± 0.2 → 2.9 ± 0.3 in hippocampus (`P_RS = 0.0049`) and **fell** in VTC (2.6 → 1.6, `P_RS = 0.0039`) | The population-level PS decomposes into a sign-consistency requirement on single units — a quantity a model can be regularised on directly |

**(brainstorm) This is a loss function, and it is an unusual one.** The observed transition is: *contract the whole representation toward the origin, hold one coding direction's separation fixed, and align that direction's sign across all values of the other variables.* Written as an objective on a population code that is `−Σ_d ‖Δ_d‖² + λ‖Δ_context‖² + μ·PS(context)`, with the first term a plain activity/energy penalty. That is a cheap regulariser: an L1/L2 activity cost supplies the contraction for free, and only the second and third terms need to know which variable matters. It predicts something testable in a model — that disentangling one variable should *reduce* mean activation and *reduce* the separability of everything else, which is the opposite of what an added-capacity account predicts.

### Instruction installs the same geometry in minutes

The result with no analogue anywhere else in the wiki. All patients received a ~4-minute verbal description of the latent structure after session 1, and each session used **new stimuli**, so the mappings had to be relearned from scratch either way.

| Group | `n` | Session 1 | Session 2 (post-instruction) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-instruction inference | 3 patients, 6 sessions | Context already decodable (`P = 0.014`) and PS already high | Geometry retained |
| **Post-instruction inference** | 5 patients, 10 sessions | Context not decodable, PS at chance (`P = 0.20`) | Context decodable (`P = 0.016`), CCGP and PS significant, **PS statistically indistinguishable from the pre-instruction group's** (`P = 0.0028` vs `0.0035`); units linearly tuned to context 8% → 18% (stim) and 7% → 16% (baseline) |
| Inference not exhibited | 4 patients, 8 sessions | Nothing | Nothing (all `P > 0.05`; context tuning 6% → 6%) |

Two claims fall out, and they are different in kind:

1. **Two learning channels converge on one geometry.** Trial-and-error discovery over a session and four minutes of language produce representations that are not distinguishable by PS. Whatever the wiki's meta-learner is, its output format is reachable without the outer loop.
2. **The instruction is not the mechanism — using it is.** A third of the patients received identical instructions and neither behaved nor represented differently. So language did not write the code; it changed what the learner attended to, and the code followed the behavioural change. (The paper's own framing: instructions "steer attention towards previously undiscovered latent structure", which is a curriculum intervention, not a knowledge transfer.)

**(brainstorm)** For a machine this is the cheapest available test of whether a system's structure is *addressable by description*: give one arm of a model family a natural-language statement of the latent variable and the other arm the same extra tokens of unrelated text, hold the data stream identical, and measure CCGP of the named variable in the residual stream. If the wiki's `g` is only reachable through experience, the two arms are the same. This is a distinct axis from in-context learning, because the instruction here is followed by *new* stimuli and a full re-learning of the surface mappings — nothing is copied.

## Entangled at the cell, factorised at the population — and this is gap G40's real knob

The two readings of this dataset are both true and they are at different levels:

| Level | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Single cell | **Entangled.** ~90% of informative cells carry a conjunction; position-only and evidence-only cells are a small minority. This is the `g̃ ⊙ x̃` outer-product regime of [[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]], not a factorised code |
| Population | **Factorised.** On `M`, a trial advances along a position direction and *splits* along an independent evidence direction. Two nearly-orthogonal gradients over one surface |

[[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]] G40 asks when a system should factorise and when it should entangle, and had no controller and no measurement. This dataset supplies the measurement and relocates the question: **factorisation is a property of the population geometry, not of tuning curves**, so a model can be scored on it without any cell-level correspondence — exactly the level [[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]] says is legitimate. And `d` itself is the candidate observable for the controller: a task with fewer independent factors produced a lower-dimensional manifold in the *same* circuit, so "how many things is this task making me track" is readable from the geometry rather than having to be declared.

**Courellis et al. 2024 splits the level question a second way, and the two datasets disagree about what the level *buys*.** In mouse CA1 the population is low-dimensional and factorised while the cells are conjunctive; in human hippocampus the population became *simultaneously* more disentangled (context and stim-pair CCGP up) and more expressive (shattering dimensionality up, parity decodable) as inference appeared. So "factorised at the population" is not a statement about dimensionality being low — it is a statement about which *directions* are parallel across conditions, and a code can add nonlinear mixing terms without losing them. The wiki should stop using low dimensionality as a proxy for abstraction ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T50).

**A third origin story for mixed selectivity, and it is the one that requires learning.** The wiki holds two accounts on which conjunctive tuning is free — fan-in restriction in a small dendritic network ([[wiki/entities/dendritic-ann.md]]) and patch/dendrite size matching in cortex ([[wiki/concepts/canonical-cortical-microcircuit.md]]), both of which get mixing from wiring alone. Against that: prefrontal models built on **random connectivity alone do not reach the measured levels of mixed selectivity, and do reach them once a Hebbian plasticity term is added** (Wikipedia, *Hebbian theory*) `(tentative)`. The three accounts are separable by a single prediction — under the wiring accounts mixed selectivity is present before training and stable through it; under the Hebbian account it *grows* with exposure and tracks input correlation structure. Nothing in the wiki measures this yet ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T67).

**A model-side result inverts the reflex the other way, and it is the one a builder can act on.** Cazalets et al. 2025 grow recurrent connectivity in ~500-unit reservoirs and measure, per architecture, both the linear effective dimensionality (fewest principal components reaching 90% of the variance) and the within-/between-class cluster geometry. The two do not co-vary: the best-performing networks occupy a region of *moderate* effective dimensionality with near-optimal within-class compactness, and architectures pushing dimensionality higher fail to convert it into accuracy — "beyond a dataset-dependent tipping point, extra dimensions mainly inject noise and erode class structure". The paper's own split of the term is the useful export: **task-agnostic expressivity** (how spread out the states are, irrespective of labels) versus **task-relevant effective dimensionality** (how many directions actually separate the classes), which come apart empirically. Read together with the Courellis result above, the wiki should stop using dimensionality as a proxy for abstraction in *either* direction — low does not mean abstract, high does not mean expressive-where-it-counts ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T50, T75).

**A third dataset puts the abstract format in a rodent, and adds the intervention.** Mishchanchuk et al. 2024 image 592 ventral-CA1 pyramidal neurons while mice run a reversing probabilistic bandit whose four trial types factor as choice × expected outcome, with the latent context being the pairing between them. The top three principal components separate the trial types by choice, expected outcome and context respectively, and decoders trained on one trial type per context transfer above chance to the other — the same cross-condition generalization logic as CCGP, run on a context that exists only as an inference over past outcomes. Two things it supplies that the human dataset does not: the abstraction is **not tied to a stimulus** (the animal stays in one place and the context has no sensory correlate at all), and the population is **causally necessary** — ablating it moves behaviour to a cached-value strategy ([[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]]).

**A fourth dataset puts the two structures in *different tissue*, which the level framing does not predict.** Zheng et al. 2024 map a learned transition graph and lifetime semantic taxonomy over the same objects and find them in non-overlapping hippocampal-formation clusters, each effect at zero in the other's region of interest ([[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]]). Here factorisation is not a geometric property of one population read at the right level — it is anatomical separation, which is the *strong* form and the one this page's "entangled at the cell, factorised at the population" resolution rules out for evidence × position. Either the two cases sit at opposite ends of one axis (mix in one population when the variables are sampled jointly; segregate when they are not) or the fMRI result is one population at a resolution that cannot see the mixing. The discriminating measurement is available and unrun: estimate whether a single manifold spans both structures ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T51).

**A fifth case makes the mixing *load-bearing*, and names the condition under which it is.** Lebedev et al. 2004 record dorsolateral prefrontal cells while an attended and a remembered location are dissociated within a trial — two variables of the **same type**, in the same population. Few cells are pure: the selectivity-index scatter is a continuum with attention and memory cells at the extremes and hybrids filling the middle, and attention cells decode the remembered location above chance and vice versa. The useful detail is a sign: in 73% of hybrids the preferred attended location and the preferred remembered location **differ** (one cell is inhibited by the location it is excited to remember), and those are precisely the cells that let an ensemble read out both variables — a hybrid whose two preferences *coincide* cannot distinguish the two by construction. Same-type variables also come with an anatomical hedge: ventrolateral of the principal sulcus the population is nearly single-variable (28% attention vs. 2% memory cells), dorsomedially it is balanced (8% / 9% / 10%).

**(brainstorm)** This sharpens the wiki's mixed-selectivity story into a design rule the wiring accounts do not supply. Random or fan-in-driven mixing gets conjunctions for free, but *aligned* conjunctions carry no more than one variable's worth of information about which variable is which. What separates two same-type variables is **anti-alignment** — a systematically negative relation between a unit's tuning for the two — which is a property of the learned readout, not of the fan-in. Testable directly in any model holding two like-typed slots: measure the sign correlation of per-unit tuning across the two, and predict that joint decodability tracks it.

**(brainstorm)** The clean experiment this suggests is a dimensionality ledger. Train one model on a family of tasks that share components, estimate `d` per task, and check whether `d` grows with the number of independently varying factors and *saturates* once components recombine (the factorised regime, where new tasks are new points on an old manifold) rather than growing linearly (the entangled regime, where each task gets its own bespoke surface). That is G40's "factorise when the task set is factorised" rule made into a curve that can be plotted, and it needs no labels beyond the task index.

## The shared geometry is the reusable code, and the rotation is the pose

The hyperalignment result is the strongest thing in the ingest for the wiki's central architecture.

- 69–75% of the manifold's geometry transfers between animals, and evidence decoding transfers with **no significant loss**.
- The entire cross-brain difference absorbed by one element of `SO(5)` — **10 parameters** — against a 5-dimensional geometry supporting the whole task.
- Single-cell fields, by contrast, have no cross-animal correspondence at all; nothing was matched cell to cell.

This is the *empirical* instance of the split [[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]] derived twice and had never seen measured: a large reusable structural code plus one small parameter that says how it is posed. There it was `φ_k`, an argmax over rotational offsets when orienting a stored map ([[wiki/entities/hidden-state-inference-remapping.md]]); in the fly it was the physically separable bump-to-landmark offset ([[wiki/entities/fly-central-complex.md]]). Here the same shape appears across *brains* rather than across environments, with the symmetry group named (`SO(d)`) because the space is a Euclidean embedding.

**(brainstorm) The cheapest export on this page is a model-comparison instrument.** To ask whether two systems learned the same structure, do not compare weights, units, or probe accuracies. Fit each system's manifold, search `SO(d)` for the rotation that best transfers a decoder from one to the other, and report the fraction of variance the rotation carries. It is label-free once the task variables are known, unit-count-free, and architecture-free — so it works model↔model, model↔brain and brain↔brain with one procedure. It is also the only instrument in the wiki that could compare a model to a brain without training a probe on ground-truth labels, which is the circularity [[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]] cannot escape. What it does not escape: it presupposes the group. Where the alignment is not a rotation — a permutation, a reparameterisation, a nonlinear warp — the instrument silently under-reports shared structure, which is the same residue as G39.

## The manifold's *topology* changes during learning, in a fixed order

The trajectory the two open problems below ask for now exists for a third dataset, and its content is topological rather than metric. A single UMAP embedding fitted across all sessions of mouse dorsal-CA1 imaging (100 neighbours, 3 components, correlation metric, longitudinally registered cells) turns weeks of 2ACDC learning into one sequence of shapes (Sun et al. 2025, [[wiki/entities/cscg.md]]):

| Stage | Shape | What has been learned |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Cue-clustered but unstructured cloud | Sensory identity only |
| 1 | **Hub-and-spoke** — hub = all four grey regions, spokes = trajectories out to each distinct cue | The grey regions are still one state; the track is not yet a sequence |
| 2 | **Ring**, closed by the 2 s dark teleportation period | The task is a cycle; within-track ambiguity resolved |
| 3 | **Split-shank ring** — a band splitting into two strands, plus an off-manifold "diamond" point cloud during reward consumption and immobility | The two trial types are distinct latent branches; short-term memory is carried as *which strand* |

Three things this adds that a metric dimensionality estimate cannot.

- **Merged states are visible as a hub.** Aliased observations that have not yet been split appear as a single high-degree vertex, and de-aliasing appears as that vertex fissioning. This is the graph framing's node-splitting operation observed directly ([[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]] hardness source 3).
- **The order is a model-discriminating statistic** — see [[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]]. Endpoint geometry does not separate three of the six models tested; the sequence of shapes does.
- **The off-manifold cloud is behavioural state, not task state.** It appears at every stage, sits beside the reward embeddings, and corresponds to periods when the animal is stationary — a candidate replay signature, and a reminder that a manifold estimated over all frames mixes the task's latent structure with the animal's postural regime ([[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]; the source marks this as speculation).

**The caveat is the page's standing one, sharpened.** UMAP is a visualisation with three hyperparameters and no significance test; the stages are read off by eye, and the quantitative claim that carries the argument is the population-vector correlation matrix, not the embedding.

## The manifold constrains what can be learned — the page's one causal result

Sadtler et al. 2014 is the only entry here in which a fitted manifold goes on to **predict the outcome of a manipulation it never saw**, and it is the closest thing the wiki has to the intervention the open problems below keep asking for. Two rhesus monkeys drive a cursor through a BCI (Brain–Computer Interface) whose decoder is deliberately factored as `units → 10 factors → velocity`, so each stage can be permuted separately: a **within-manifold** perturbation re-orients the control space inside the factor-analysis subspace (the *intrinsic manifold*), an **outside-manifold** perturbation moves it off. Within-manifold mappings were learned within a session and produced washout aftereffects; outside-manifold mappings were not learned, produced no aftereffect, and did not become easier over roughly two years of alternating exposure — with initial impairment, principal angles, required preferred-direction change, search-space size and hand movement all matched across the two types.

Golub et al. 2018, on the same experiments, tighten the boundary: for within-manifold perturbations the *set* of population activity patterns produced is statistically unchanged before and after learning (nearest-neighbour repertoire change ≈ 0, `p = 0.55`, `n` = 384; no change in covariability along either mapping), while the movement-specific clouds move a great deal. The manifold is not merely a subspace the activity stays in — on this timescale the population re-uses a fixed repertoire of points within it and only changes which behavioural intent evokes which point.

The consequence for this page is a change of status, not a new number: **a low-dimensional projection is not only a description of activity but a boundary on the reachable set.** Full treatment, including the exportable read that within-span re-association is cheap and basis change is not, on [[wiki/concepts/manifold-constrained-learning.md]].

Two caveats it leaves for the instruments above. The intrinsic manifold here is a *linear* 10-D subspace (estimated dimensionality 9.81 ± 0.31), so the curvature correction in the formal-statement table applies to it and nothing says whether the true constraint is the span or a curved surface inside it. And the intervention is on the **readout**, not the population — no activity was perturbed along a latent direction, so the "decodability again" problem below is narrowed rather than closed.

## Sequences are not primitives

The doublet analysis dissolves a distinction the wiki has been carrying. A "sequence" of cell activations is fully accounted for by a path through a 5-dimensional surface: identity at TPR 0.87 / FPR 0.14 from the latents alone, *and* timing, since manifold path length between the two firings predicts the elapsed time better than path lengths taken from other trials.

| Consequence | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Storage** | A system does not need to store sequences if it stores the geometry and a policy for moving on it. This is the same cost argument [[wiki/entities/vector-hash.md]] makes from capacity — reconstruct a tangent vector, let the manifold regenerate the state — arrived at from measurement instead of from theory |
| **Rare events come free** | Each doublet occurs in 3.6% of trials, yet the manifold predicts it. Sequence-level structure that is far too sparse to learn pairwise is implied by the geometry |
| **Prediction for replay** | The source proposes that non-spatial replay sequences in humans are organised by the same manifold geometry, by analogy with the online case measured here. **Now tested in the spatial case (tentative):** a UMAP manifold fitted to CA1 activity during figure-8 maze running admits ripple population vectors as points on it, decoding both maze position and *trial index*, and awake-ripple coordinates predict which trials the following sleep's ripples revisit (`raw/talk-nd-memory-gating.txt`). Caveat that cuts both ways — the manifold is defined by running data, so ripples expressing anything the animal never expressed while running fall *off* it and are simply undecodable, not absent ([[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]) |

## The channel, not the region, is the unit — and rate is blind to it

Lai et al. 2026 record prelimbic cells labelled by **where they project** (basolateral amygdala vs. nucleus accumbens), in the same tissue at the same depth, across three behavioural assays (`raw/lai-2026-mpfc-bla-nac-affective-pathways.md`, [[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]).

| Level | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Mean calcium transient rate | **Identical.** No region main effect and no Region × Behaviour interaction in the open field (`P = 0.14`, `P > 0.3`), none in the elevated plus maze (`p = 0.304`), none between social and non-social stimuli |
| Population geometry | **Divergent.** Both pathways place the animal's default state (corner) at nearly the same location (`r = 0.64`, `p < 1×10⁻⁹`; smallest cross-pathway canonical-correlation difference, 0.12) and disagree about which behaviours sit far from it — amygdala-projecting cells push centre and grooming out, accumbens-projecting cells push sniffing out |

Two things follow for this page. First, it is the wiki's cleanest case of geometry being the *only* level at which a real functional distinction exists — the rate comparison is not merely less sensitive, it is null under every condition tested. Second, the divergence is indexed by a variable no purely observational geometry analysis can recover: **which downstream structure reads the population**. A manifold fitted to all prelimbic cells would be the union of two differently-oriented codes, and nothing in the activity says to split it.

**(brainstorm) Shared origin, divergent axes is a cheap architecture.** The measured pattern — one anchor state agreed on by both readers, different directions maximized away from it — is what a shared recurrent state with multiple linear heads produces when each head is trained on a different contrast. It gives factorization *at the readout* without disentangling the shared code, and it predicts that adding a new downstream consumer costs a head rather than a renegotiation of the representation. The testable signature is exactly the one measured here: matched marginal statistics, matched reference state, divergent contrast directions.

**A caution the same dataset supplies.** A subensemble defined by one task's manipulation (cells excited by entry into the anxiogenic zone) and its parent population rank the two pathways in *opposite* orders for how well they separate social from non-social stimuli. Cluster-distance geometry is therefore not level-invariant: the answer depends on which cells were let into the projection ([[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]], [[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T116).

## Open problems

- **One manifold, one task.** Nothing here says how the manifolds for two tasks relate, whether they share dimensions, or what happens on the transition. That is G40's reuse question and this paper does not touch it.
- **~~No learning trajectory~~ — now supplied at three timescales, and the third is the one the wiki wanted.** Sun et al. 2025 track the same 3,000–5,000 CA1 cells across the entire multi-week acquisition of a task, so the intermediate geometries are measured rather than interpolated between endpoints. What it does *not* settle is the question below it: the ordered sequence is a population statistic averaged over trials, so it still cannot say whether an individual state splits abruptly or accretes.
- **~~No learning trajectory~~ — partly supplied, and at two timescales.** Nieh et al. measure the manifold once, after weeks of training. Courellis et al. contrast sessions in which the same participants did and did not perform inference, and sessions before and after a four-minute instruction, so the *endpoints* of the formation are now measured in the same brains. What is still missing is the trajectory between them: no continuous estimate of CCGP/PS across trials, so nothing says whether the geometry snaps into place at the first successful inference or accretes.
- **Decodability again — narrowed by one result.** Manifold inference is correlational. No intervention perturbed the population along a latent direction, so "the animal uses the geometry" is unlicensed exactly as it is for a linear probe (T25). The exception is Sadtler et al. 2014, where the manifold is fitted first and then predicts which of two matched decoder perturbations can be learned — a manipulation of the *readout* rather than of the activity, which licenses the geometry as a constraint on the reachable set without licensing "the animal reads out these directions". Courellis et al. push the correlation to the single-trial level — error trials lose the format — which is the strongest version of the correlational claim and still not an intervention. Mishchanchuk et al. 2024 supply the *coarsest* intervention — remove the population, lose the strategy and the downstream dopamine signature — which licenses necessity of the region but says nothing about the geometry, since a lesion cannot distinguish "the abstract format was used" from "any context signal was used". The missing experiment in all three is the same: perturb *along* the context coding direction and leave the rest of the population intact.
- **Pseudo-populations.** The human result pools units across patients and sessions into pseudo-populations, so every geometric quantity is computed over a population that never existed simultaneously. Noise correlations, which is what would make a geometry usable or not by a downstream reader, are destroyed by construction.
- **Where does the contraction come from?** The 60% firing-rate drop that accompanies abstraction is unexplained — the paper offers no controller for it, and it is hippocampus-specific, so it is not a global arousal or fatigue effect.
- **The abstract variable is scalar, continuous and metric.** `#right − #left` is a running count with a natural order. Nothing here says a manifold forms for a non-metric symbolic variable, which is the non-embeddable slice (G11) and the same limit [[wiki/concepts/abstract-structural-codes.md]] records for the concept-space grid result.
- **The estimator is threshold-bearing.** `d` comes from a hand-chosen power-law fit range, and the sequence detector from a shuffle-defined cutoff. Both are the instrument class the wiki distrusts elsewhere.
- **Why five?** No account is offered of why `d ≈ 5` for a task with two nominal variables. Velocity, view angle, previous choice and previous-trial correctness are all decodable from `M`, which plausibly consumes the remainder — but the decomposition is not done, so the dimension count is not yet an *inventory*.

## Connections

- **[[wiki/entities/medial-prefrontal-cortex.md]]** — the case where this page's level argument becomes the whole result: two prefrontal projection classes are indistinguishable in firing rate under every behavioural condition and differ only in which contrast directions leave a shared reference state, so the functional variable (which structure reads the population) is invisible to any analysis of the activity alone (Lai et al. 2026).
- **[[wiki/concepts/priority-map.md]]** — a mechanism left undetermined for want of this page's instrument: multi-unit recording cannot say whether object identity and location are conjunctive within cells or superposed across the population, and only the second lets a target–template similarity be read off a linear projection (Bichot et al. 2015).
- **[[wiki/concepts/objective-identifiability.md]]** — supplies this page's reason to exist (population structure is a legitimate prediction target, single-unit tuning is not) and receives its instrument, plus the number that bounds its own dimensionality confound: a linear PC count can exceed the true latent dimension by 7–8×, so two systems differing in participation ratio may carry identical geometry.
- **[[wiki/entities/cscg.md]]** — the model that predicts this page's one longitudinal result: the topological progression of the CA1 manifold *is* a state machine's states separating, and the clone model is the only one tested that reproduces the order in which they separate.
- **[[wiki/concepts/representation-probing.md]]** — the same inside-the-system ambition with the labels removed: a probe asks whether a *named* structure is decodable, manifold inference asks what shape the activity has before naming anything, and cross-subject rotation alignment is the one comparison here that does not need ground truth. Both stop at decodability without intervention.
- **[[wiki/concepts/cognitive-map.md]]** — supplies the single-unit case its "beyond space" table lacked (a learned, non-perceptual variable with firing fields, in mouse CA1) and the measured instance of its reusable-code-plus-pose decomposition, with the pose realised as a 10-parameter `SO(5)` rotation between brains.
- **[[wiki/concepts/abstract-structural-codes.md]]** — the opposite pole of the same architecture: `g` requires content-invariance, and this page's cells are ~90% conjunctive, so the invariance appears only after the population geometry is extracted — which says where in a model the factorisation is allowed to live.
- **[[wiki/architectural-gaps.md]]** — G40 gets its first measurement: entanglement is a cell-level fact and factorisation a population-level one, and intrinsic dimensionality responds to how many factors the task forces.
- **[[wiki/entities/tolman-eichenbaum-machine.md]]** — the model whose evidence-accumulation prediction this is: a 2-D map spanned by position × accumulated evidence, predicted from next-observation prediction and then recorded. It also predicts the conjunctive (`g̃ ⊙ x̃`) cell-level code this page measures at ~90%.
- **[[wiki/entities/vector-hash.md]]** — the theoretical version of this page's sequence result: store a tangent vector on a low-dimensional manifold and let the dynamics regenerate the state, here confirmed as a measurement (the geometry predicts sequence identity *and* timing).
- **[[wiki/concepts/offline-replay.md]]** — online sequences are paths on the manifold, and offline ones appear to be too: projecting sharp-wave-ripple population vectors onto a running-fitted manifold decodes their content as a (position, trial) coordinate, which makes replay's sampling distributions choices of trajectory on a fixed surface rather than choices among stored episodes, and supplies that page's evidence that awake ripples select the trials sleep later repeats (`raw/talk-nd-memory-gating.txt`, **(tentative)**).
- **[[wiki/entities/fly-central-complex.md]]** — the same reusable-code-plus-offset structure in a circuit small enough to record entirely, with the offset physically separable rather than fitted; that page's ring is `d = 1` and hand-identified, this page's surface is `d ≈ 5` and inferred.
- **[[wiki/concepts/path-integration.md]]** — the manifold's position gradient is what an integrator moves along, and the evidence gradient is a second integrator over a non-spatial quantity in the same population, which is the composition-of-integrators question made concrete in one circuit.
- **[[wiki/concepts/contextual-inference.md]]** — supplies the neural format of the context posterior this page measures: the latent context of a reversal task becomes an abstract coding direction in human hippocampus exactly when participants act on it, so "which context am I in" is not only a probability but a direction in population space that can be scored for parallelism (Courellis et al. 2024) — replicated in mouse ventral CA1 for a purely outcome-defined context, where the same population is also shown to be causally required (Mishchanchuk et al. 2024).
- **[[wiki/entities/hidden-state-inference-remapping.md]]** — the same latent-context variable read out a different way: remapping is the *identity* of the map expressing the posterior, CCGP/PS are the *format* of the context direction within one map, and neither instrument sees what the other does.
- **[[wiki/concepts/complementary-learning-systems.md]]** — the awkward datum for the fast-store story: the hippocampus here does not store more when it learns to infer, it fires 60% *less* and rearranges, so the region's contribution to a one-shot behavioural flip is geometric rather than a new memory trace.
- **[[wiki/concepts/latent-graph-discovery.md]]** — the geometry is the graph estimate seen as a surface rather than as nodes and edges: task variables are coordinates on it, sequences are paths, and the shared-across-brains fraction is the closest thing the wiki has to a measurement of how much of a recovered structure is the *task's* rather than the learner's.
- **[[wiki/concepts/inhibitory-control-of-coding.md]]** — the same population measured one level down: geometry asks what format the excitatory code is in, that page names the actuators that set the format (generalization ← *Sst*, selectivity ← *Pvalb*/*Id2*, information ← *Vip*), which makes format a manipulable variable rather than an observed one.
- **[[wiki/entities/adaptive-cann.md]]** — dimensionality reduction derived rather than measured: projecting a bump-carrying network onto its first two motion modes (height and position) turns `2N` neurons into a 4-D state space that preserves the bifurcation structure, which is what the empirical low-dimensional manifolds on this page would look like if their generating dynamics were known.
- **[[wiki/entities/fcann.md]]** — the case where the dynamics beat the description: attractor states of a connectome-derived Hopfield network explain more held-out fMRI variance (`R² = 0.396`) than the first two principal components fitted to the data itself (0.364), so the low-dimensional geometry is recovered from the *coupling matrix* rather than from the activity it produces.
- **[[wiki/concepts/sparse-distributed-representations.md]]** — the regime this page's low-dimensional manifolds trade against: recognition-by-subsampling needs error rates that fall superexponentially in dimension and fails outright on dense codes, so a population optimised for cheap abstraction is by construction not the one optimised for cheap identification (T50).
- **[[wiki/concepts/synaptic-plasticity.md]]** — the third origin story for mixed selectivity, and the only learned one: a Hebbian term added to random prefrontal connectivity is reported to reach measured mixing levels that random connectivity alone does not, which also imports Hebb's PCA fixed point — mixing directions chosen for variance rather than for task relevance ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T67).
- **[[wiki/entities/dendritic-ann.md]]** — a competing origin story for mixed selectivity: in a *small* dendritically wired network, conjunctive coding appears in both hidden layers purely because no unit sees enough of the input to be class-specific, which makes fan-in restriction rather than population expansion the sufficient condition (Chavlis & Poirazi 2025).
- **[[wiki/concepts/canonical-cortical-microcircuit.md]]** — a wiring-level origin for mixed selectivity that requires no learning: lateral patch diameter scales with basal dendritic spread across areas, so only a cell at a patch centre samples pure patch input and most superficial pyramids receive a patch/non-patch *mixture* by construction — Malach's argument that size matching maximises input diversity ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T65, Douglas & Martin 2004).
- **[[wiki/entities/hag-reservoir.md]]** — the same two quantities measured in a model where the connectivity is grown rather than given, and the result that constrains this page's use of dimensionality: raising the linear effective dimension past a dataset-dependent threshold degrades class separability, so participation-ratio-style expansion is not a proxy for a better code (T75).
- **[[wiki/concepts/manifold-constrained-learning.md]]** — takes this page's object and gives it causal force: the same factor-analysis subspace that summarises a population's activity predicts which new activity patterns it can be trained to produce, so a fitted manifold bounds the reachable set rather than merely describing the visited one (Sadtler et al. 2014) — and bounds it more tightly than the subspace implies, since within-session learning preserves the repertoire of patterns and re-associates them with new intents rather than visiting new points of the manifold (Golub et al. 2018). It also returns a caution to this page: the same M1 recording read through two different 2-D projections says opposite things about whether leftward and rightward movements share a code, so any "same population code" claim resting on one projection is underdetermined (talk-nd-brain-learning-limits, **(tentative)**).
- **[[wiki/concepts/attractor-dynamics.md]]** — supplies the dynamical mechanism whose stable states this page measures the shape of; the manifold's topology is imposed by the recurrence rather than learned (G47), and intrinsic dimensionality is the only quantity here that would notice.
- **[[wiki/entities/trnn.md]]** — uses this page's tools as the decisive test between two maintenance hypotheses: demixed PCA shows stimulus trajectories that keep moving at undiminished velocity through the delay, which is what a fixed-point account cannot produce (Liu et al. 2025).
- **[[wiki/concepts/attention.md]]** — the case where two *same-type* variables share one prefrontal population (an attended and a remembered location) and joint decodability rests on hybrid cells whose preferences for the two are anti-aligned, which makes the sign of the mixing, not its presence, the load-bearing property (Lebedev et al. 2004).
- **[[wiki/concepts/working-memory.md]]** — supplies the store whose contents this geometry is measuring, and inherits the caution: a decodable spatially tuned delay signal in prefrontal cortex is mostly the attentional pointer rather than the held item ([[wiki/empirical-tensions.md]] T88).
- **[[wiki/concepts/arbitrary-sensorimotor-mapping.md]]** — a conjunction result with a built-in control for the marginals: reversing a cue→action mapping holds cue identity and response direction fixed while flipping only the pairing, and ≈50% of prefrontal delay-period cells follow the pairing against ≈30% cue and ≈20% direction — high shattering dimension read out one cell at a time (Wise & Murray 2000).
- **[[wiki/concepts/cognitive-control.md]]** — a second prefrontal code read in this page's terms and a ready-made experiment: cat/dog morph categories produce sharp between-category separation with within-category collapse (the low-shattering, high-abstraction end), and retraining on two boundaries *orthogonal* to the original leaves the old dichotomy undecodable in the same cells — which is a cross-condition generalization test run by the animal's training history rather than by the analyst (Freedman et al. 2001, in Miller et al. 2002).
- **[[wiki/concepts/controller-knowledge-vs-process.md]]** — hands this page a decidable version of a theoretical dispute: whether a controller *stores* or merely *processes* becomes the question of whether its run-time states lie inside the manifold spanned by retrieval-evoked states, or visit an operating space no stored item can produce.
- **[[wiki/concepts/control-unity-and-diversity.md]]** — constrains adaptive coding geometrically: re-using one ensemble across tasks must still leave operation-specific variance, because updating and shifting are separable in behaviour, in pharmacology and in genetics — so a fully task-general control manifold is ruled out by the psychometrics (Friedman & Robbins 2021).
